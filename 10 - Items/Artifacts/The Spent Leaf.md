# The Spent Leaf

> **What people can see.** A small, dry leaf in a cedar box, kept by a First Seat yard-desk called [[The Remainder]]. It is not a Condition colour, one of the colours that name what a person becomes, and it does not Give. Towns that have heard of it call it a curiosity, a relic, or a bit of sweepings someone was too pious to throw away.

## Description

The leaf is smaller than a naming-fall, with the same vein as motherwood and no telling colour — not wine, not storm-grey, not copper-green, not the hug's ordinary summer. Held to daylight it looks *spent*: the green has gone to a tea-brown that still will not name. The box is cedar, unlabelled, with a catch a child could open. The cloth under it is a Road-hand's old wrapping, salt-stained.

[[The Remainder]] logs it as *uncounted fall, origin-yard, year unstated*. The First Seat's public line, when anyone bothers to ask, is that leftover tissue is horticulture: blight-cuttings, mixed-fall scraps, the sweep after Turning-Week. This is one more scrap.

A few Road-hands have carried the box with a scion, "so the earth would not travel empty." That is how the story leaks: a clerk on the Heskoren front who saw a colourless leaf packed like a tool.

## History & Provenance

No one agrees when it fell. The Remainder's oldest readable line says only *kept, not spoken*. The Watchers in the Sacred Core tell a story about a leaf like this. They say the old faith called such a leaf a *cost-leaf*, and the Mother-name later filed it as waste. Book-hands on Lestrand have offered to buy a viewing. The Seat has not sold one.

What anyone can check:

- It is motherwood. Set it against an ordinary oak and nothing happens; set it against a town Tree and the leaf lies a little less dry.
- The box-log claims the leaf is *lighter after a Giving week*. The scale in the side-yard is a kitchen scale, and skeptics have laughed at that sentence for a generation.
- It has never been Spoken as a colour. A warden once tried, said the word *Kept*, and then apologised, because no child was standing under a Tree to hear it.

## What people do with it

Most years nothing happens to it, and the box stays on the shelf above the blight-jars.

A remainder-clerk may take it out when a Cutting-leave is argued, or when a frontier Road-hand wants proof that the origin still *spends* something to send wood. The Seat prefers the leaf stay a yard-fact. The clerks who want it shown are not a church. They want the Seat to stop calling the slow graft queue bad luck.

Nobody makes a pilgrimage to it. The devout already have the Awakening Tree, and this is a scrap in a shed.

## GM Notes

This is the firing pin, the confirmable artifact for [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]]. The player-facing text stays deniable, as a cultivar, as drying, or as suggestion. The tests below are how a campaign *can* fire the keystone. They are not a sorter for the Kept, not a name for [[The Other Hands]], and not an answer to the nature of her limit.

When she Gives, she spends, and most of that spend is invisible. Once, or rarely, a remainder of that spend fell as tissue and was swept, and you should not make a second leaf. It is hers. It is not a Gift. It cannot be planted, and it cannot be Spoken into a Condition. A remnant Phoenix-fire is a different secret, on [[When the Fire Is Caught]], and you do not braid them.

Rungs 1–5 stay deniable. Continuity, the way it answers motherwood, is the same horticulture skeptics already grant. The weight-log is a bad scale. Warmth near a Tree is sap and hope. Play those as clues, and do not require the party to run the confirming beat in session three.

The confirming beat is rung 6. Hold the leaf under a healthy Tree *during a real Giving*, a colour falling on a child who is standing. The holder feels **warmth and a grief that is not theirs**: relief that someone was reached, and the ache of the spend. The canopy that just gave goes a shade thinner for a breath, then recovers. The next child in that week is not shorted. She pays, and she does not ration the next gift to balance a book. Land this as *warmth with an ache*, never as horror, and never as a voice from the wood.

What the leaf will not do is sort the Kept, chosen against past-her-edge. She does not tell them apart, and the leaf does not either. A party that wants it to is the wound, not a puzzle. It will not answer a demand to change a fall, rerun a week, or Give a second time, and the engine stays locked. It will not name a Hand, a door, or a Struck. Household confirmation is later, or never, and never first. It will not explain *why* she is bounded. It will not speak, glow for a crowd, or convert a square.

[[The Remainder]] is the desk that has it. The First Seat majority wants the box locked, because orthopraxy dies if a mind can be proved, and Threnmaieth would census the proof. A minority of remainder-clerks and some [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hands]] want it walked to a waiting town, not to found a church, but to make the scion-queue a moral fact. Methods stay ~5%: reassignment, a lost box, a refused Cutting-leave. Not a knife.

The consequences if it lands live on [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real#If confirmation lands]].
