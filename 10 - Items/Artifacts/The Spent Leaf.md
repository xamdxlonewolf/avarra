# The Spent Leaf

> **What people can see.** A small, dry leaf in a cedar box, kept by a First Seat yard-desk called [[The Remainder]]. It is not a Condition colour. It does not Give. Towns that have heard of it call it a curiosity, a relic, or a bit of sweepings someone was too pious to throw away.

## Description

The leaf is smaller than a naming-fall, with the same vein as motherwood and no telling colour — not wine, not storm-grey, not copper-green, not the hug's ordinary summer. Held to daylight it looks *spent*: the green has gone to a tea-brown that still will not name. The box is cedar, unlabelled, with a catch a child could open. The cloth under it is a Road-hand's old wrapping, salt-stained.

[[The Remainder]] logs it as *uncounted fall, origin-yard, year unstated*. The First Seat's public line, when anyone bothers to ask, is that leftover tissue is horticulture: blight-cuttings, mixed-fall scraps, the sweep after Turning-Week. This is one more scrap.

A few Road-hands have carried the box with a scion, "so the earth would not travel empty." That is how the story leaks: a clerk on the Heskoren front who saw a colourless leaf packed like a tool.

## History & Provenance

No one agrees when it fell. The Remainder's oldest readable line says only *kept, not spoken*. Watching households in the Sacred Core have a lintel-rumour that the Seeing once named a *cost-leaf* the Mother-name later filed as waste. Book-hands on Lestrand have offered to buy a viewing. The Seat has not sold one.

What can be checked without theology:

- It is motherwood. Set against an ordinary oak, nothing happens. Set against a town Tree, the leaf lies a little less dry.
- The box-log claims the leaf is *lighter after a Giving week*. The scale in the side-yard is a kitchen scale. Skeptics have laughed at that sentence for a generation.
- It has never been Speaked as a colour. A warden who tried, once, said the word *Kept* and then apologised, because no one was standing.

## What people do with it

Most years, nothing. The box stays on the shelf above the blight-jars.

A remainder-clerk may take it out when a Cutting-leave is argued, or when a frontier Road-hand wants proof that the origin still *spends* something to send wood. The Seat prefers the leaf stay a yard-fact. The clerks who want it shown are not a church. They want the queue to stop being called weather.

Do not expect a pilgrimage. The devout already have the Awakening Tree. This is a scrap in a shed.

## GM Notes

- **This is the firing pin.** The confirmable artifact for [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]]. Player-facing text stays deniable (cultivar, drying, suggestion). The tests below are how a campaign *can* fire the keystone. They are not a sorter for the Kept, not a name for [[The Other Hands]], and not an answer to the nature of her limit.
- **What it actually is.** When she Gives, she spends. Most of that spend is invisible. Once — or rarely; do not make a second leaf — a remainder of that spend fell as tissue and was swept. It is hers. It is not a Gift. It cannot be planted. It cannot be Speaked into a Condition. A remnant Phoenix-fire is a different secret ([[When the Fire Is Caught]]); do not braid them.
- **Rungs 1–5 stay deniable.** Continuity (it answers motherwood) is the same horticulture skeptics already grant. The weight-log is a bad scale. Warmth near a Tree is sap and hope. Play those as clues. Do not require the party to run the confirming beat in session three.
- **The confirming beat (rung 6).** Hold the leaf under a healthy Tree *during a real Giving* — a colour falling on a child who is standing. The holder feels **warmth and a grief that is not theirs**: relief that someone was reached, and the ache of the spend. The canopy that just gave goes a shade thinner for a breath, then recovers. The next child in that week is not shorted. She pays; she does not ration the next gift to balance a book. Land this as *warmth with an ache*, never horror, never a voice from the wood.
- **What the leaf will not do.**
  - Sort Kept (chosen vs past-her-edge). She does not tell them apart; the leaf does not either. A party that wants it to is the wound, not a puzzle.
  - Answer a demand to change a fall, rerun a week, or Give a second time. The engine stays locked.
  - Name a Hand, a door, or a Struck. Household confirmation is later, or never, and never first.
  - Explain *why* she is bounded.
  - Speak, glow for a crowd, or convert a square.
- **Who wants it shown or buried.** [[The Remainder]] is the desk that has it. The First Seat majority wants the box locked: orthopraxy dies if a mind can be proved, and Threnmaieth would census the proof. A minority of remainder-clerks and some [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hands]] want it walked to a waiting town — not to found a church, but to make the scion-queue a moral fact. Methods stay ~5%: reassignment, a lost box, a refused Cutting-leave. Not a knife.
- **Consequences if it lands** live on [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real#If confirmation lands]]. R2 still holds. Do not let the warmth launder the queue.
