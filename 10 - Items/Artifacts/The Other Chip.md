# The Other Chip

This one is true.

In Hale-month of Cut-year 381, Nurreith and three lodging-guild hands opened the bed under the blank name-stone at [[The Low Wall]]. Delamem, for [[The Walled Book]], had paid for a chip that could be filed as the Inner Close's quarry. The chip was the other quarry. The wall's stone and the Close's stone are not the same. They filled the bed the same week. The fill is still on top of the chip.

A person with an afternoon and a reason can open that bed and hold the chip. It is a piece of stone. It has no year on it. It does not Speak. It does not match the Close.

What people add, and what the stone does not do: a date for the wall, a date for the Tree, a first enemy, proof that the wall was the Close's outer ring. The chip does not date the wall or the Tree. It is the quarry fact under the fill.
