# The Other Chip

This one is true, which means the chip is really in the ground where they left it.

In Hale-month of Cut-year 381, Nurreith and three lodging-guild hands opened the bed under the blank name-stone at [[The Low Wall]]. Delamem, for [[The Walled Book]], had paid for a chip that could be filed as the Inner Close's quarry. The chip was the other quarry, because the wall's stone and the Close's stone are not the same. They filled the bed the same week, and the fill is still on top of the chip.

A person with an afternoon and a reason can open that bed and hold the chip. What they hold is a piece of stone with no year on it. It does not Speak, and it does not match the Close's stone.

People add a date for the wall, a date for the Tree, a first enemy, or proof that the wall was the Close's outer ring, and the stone does none of those things. The chip does not date the wall or the Tree, and under the fill it is only the quarry fact.

## GM Notes

The object is true. The 381 fill dates the quarrel, not the wall. Do not scratch a year onto the chip, and do not let it fire [[The Spent Leaf]] or prove a mother. The afternoon that opens the bed is played in [[The Name-Stone Bed]].
