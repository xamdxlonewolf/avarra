# The Ribboned Knife

This one is embellished.

A Near-Mile house hangs a kitchen knife on a ribbon beside a scorch on an old pot-ring. The people in the house know which summer that was. It was the Closing. They do not turn out for it. [[The Watch and the Cohort]] already says so. [[The Low Wall]] is another stone, from another dig. A knife-ribbon left at that wall was left in error.

Strangers call this knife the nameless knife. They want the first copy to have a handle they can buy. The house will not sell it as that. It is their kitchen knife. The summer is theirs. The scorch is on their pot.

A person can take it down and hold it. In a fight it is a small one-handed blade. Use the smallest one-handed physical weapon on the SRD list. The ribbon adds nothing.

The single blade people mean when they say *the* knife has not been produced. That search is [[The One Knife]]. Who held a knife at the first copy stays unwritten. This ribbon does not finish that sentence.
