# The Ribboned Knife

This one is embellished, which means the name strangers use is larger than the knife in the house.

A Near-Mile house hangs a kitchen knife on a ribbon beside a scorch on an old pot-ring. The people in the house know which summer that was. It was the Closing, and they do not turn out for it. [[The Low Wall]] is another stone, from another dig, and a knife-ribbon left at that wall was left in error.

Strangers call this knife the nameless knife, because they want the first copy to have a handle they can buy. The house will not sell it as that. It is their kitchen knife, the summer is theirs, and the scorch is on their pot.

A person can take it down and hold it. In a fight it is a small one-handed blade, so use the smallest one-handed physical weapon on the SRD list, and the ribbon adds nothing.

The single blade people mean when they say *the* knife has not been produced. That search is [[The One Knife]], and nobody has named who made the First Cut.
