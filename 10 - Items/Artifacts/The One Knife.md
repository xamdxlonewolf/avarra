# The One Knife

This one was never found.

People mean a single blade that made the first copy of living wood. Hearths keep a kitchen knife on a ribbon because they cannot say whether they are honouring a theft or a gift. [[The First Cut]] already says that, and it already says not to put a name on the handle. [[The Ribboned Knife]] is one of those kitchens. It marks a later summer.

No house has produced the one blade. No dig has turned it up. A buyer who offers coin for it is buying a story. The playable version of that offer is [[A Buyer for the Knife]]. Paying does not finish the search.

A person cannot hold this knife. It is not in a room. It has no SRD line. Who cut stays unwritten. People who are serious about the question will not name a house, because a house would pick a hand.
