# The One Knife

People mean a single blade that made the first copy of living wood, and this one was never found. Hearths keep a kitchen knife on a ribbon because they cannot say whether they are honouring a theft or a gift, and nobody has named who made [[The First Cut]]. [[The Ribboned Knife]] is one of those kitchens, and it marks a later summer, not this missing blade.

No house has produced the one blade, and no dig has turned it up. A buyer who offers coin for it is buying a story, and paying does not finish the search.

A person cannot hold this knife, because it is not in any room and it has no SRD line. People who are serious about the question will not name a house, because a house would pick a hand.

## GM Notes

It was never found. The player offer to buy it is played in [[A Buyer for the Knife]]. The First Cut note says not to put a name on the handle. Do not plant it in a wall, a grave, or a Close strongroom, and do not name the cutter to end the note. A later note must not name a house for this knife, and must not turn the refusal into a power, a relic, or a rite. This is not [[The Spent Leaf]], so do not fire the keystone with a knife.
