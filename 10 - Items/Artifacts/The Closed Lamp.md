# The Closed Lamp

> **What people can see.** A travelling-lamp, shut. Brass gone dull. The catch a ticket-hand could open. The coal inside should have gone cold days ago. It has not. Towns that have seen one call it a hospice-light, a leftover Turning-Week coal, or a keepsake from someone who would not let a room go dark.

It is not a leaf. It is not colourless tissue. It does not Give.

## Description

Palm-sized. A closed shutter, a short bail, a wrap that was a Road-hand's once and is a ticket-yard rag now. No Speaker-mark. Someone filed a hospice stamp off the base; the cut where it was is still bright in the brass.

Held still, it is only a lamp. Held near a person who runs hot, the coal *leans* toward them: not much, but enough that a table notices and then makes tea. If an old name is spoken in the same room, as a habit, a hymn, or a story the square still tells in the present tense, the coal brightens toward that name too. Then it is only a lamp again.

A remainder-clerk asked if it is a colourless leaf would laugh, or go quiet, depending on which desk they work at.

## History & Provenance

The public story is that the one [[Phoenix]] is in care, and the house that keeps them safe does not show them. Lamps like this travel with runners. Most never leave the inner court.

This one did. [[Rosire]] will not call it a relic. They will not talk about how it travelled. This Hale-month it is in an upper room at [[Harrow's Green]], shut, with water brought to the floor around it. If sand has been laid on that floor, Rosire will not sit in the room.

A warden cannot Speak a lamp. Speaking is for a person standing under a Tree, and a lamp is not a person.

What anyone can check:

- The coal is warm when the shutter is closed. Opened, it does not make a flame the way a wick does. If you wait, it shows a count, a room, a door, and a name.
- It leans toward new heat and brightens at an old name. People who say there is only one Phoenix, and that the Phoenix is in care, call that a trick of the wrap.
- It has never fallen from a Tree. Set against motherwood, nothing horticultural happens. It is metal and a banked coal.

## What people do with it

Most hours, nothing. It stays shut. Competence: you do not leave a travelling-coal unattended, and you do not open it for a crowd.

A ticket-hand may carry it because the room it came from should not go dark. A guest who runs hot may dislike it and not be able to say why. A child who is not in any hymn may watch it lean and ask for bread anyway.

Nobody makes a pilgrimage to it. The devout already have a legend in care, and this is only a lamp that left the inner court.

## GM Notes

- **Fire-plot object, not a cosmology pin.** Session-one moment 4 on [[The Opening]]. Narrate the lamp, the lean, the two names. Do **not** narrate harvest, bank, remnant, Protectors, or "this is not a real Phoenix." Facts, not meaning. Player body stays deniable (hospice-light, leftover coal, a wrap that leans). Remnant-vs-Gift confirmation lives on [[When the Fire Is Caught]] — this object does not close it.
- **What it actually is.** Banked Phoenix-fire from a prepared death, caught and shuttered. While a Gift lasts it can show a forced Rise's facts (count, layout, a name). After a Gift ends it is leftover fire in a tin. It cannot Give. It cannot be Given. It cannot Rise anyone. Immunity-warmth can linger because those are properties of the *fire*. Kindle and the Dwindling do not live in the lamp.
- **Not the Spent Leaf. Not harvested-fire-as-leaf.** Those misreadings are rejected. Colourless tissue is a Giving-remainder ([[The Spent Leaf]]). This is coal in brass. Do not braid the pins. Do not let a remainder-clerk "identify" it as uncounted fall. Do not let a Protector call it a second Phoenix.
- **What it will show if opened.** A count. A sanded room. A door that was not on the slate. A name the fire still answers, and a child it also knows. It will not assign a self, a forgiveness, a Gift's purpose, a second Gift, or her. If it tries to tell someone who they are, that is a keeper talking through stored fire — [[A Hidden Phoenix]].
- **The Care has more.** This is the one that walked. Do not make it unique cosmology. Do not invent a lamp-cult. ~5%: a closed shutter, a lean, tea afterward.
- **Do not fire clue 5 with this.** Remnant-to-ash and Tree-arbitrating two names stay later. The lamp can stay lit through session one.
