# The Closed Lamp

> **What people can see.** A travelling-lamp, shut, the brass gone dull and the catch something a ticket-hand could open. The coal inside should have gone cold days ago, and it has not. Towns that have seen one call it a hospice-light, a leftover Turning-Week coal, or a keepsake from someone who would not let a room go dark.

What the towns are looking at is not a leaf and not colourless tissue, and it does not Give.

## Description

It is palm-sized, with a closed shutter, a short bail, and a wrap that was a Road-hand's once and is a ticket-yard rag now. There is no Speaker-mark, and someone filed a hospice stamp off the base, so the cut where it was is still bright in the brass.

Held still, it is only a lamp. Held near a person who runs hot, the coal *leans* toward them: not much, but enough that a table notices and then makes tea. If an old name is spoken in the same room, as a habit, a hymn, or a story the square still tells in the present tense, the coal brightens toward that name too. Then it is only a lamp again.

Ask a remainder-clerk whether it is a colourless leaf, and they will laugh, or go quiet, depending on which desk they work at.

## History & Provenance

The public story is that the one [[Phoenix]] is in care, and the house that keeps them safe does not show them. Lamps like this travel with runners, and most of them never leave the inner court. This one did. [[Rosire]] will not call it a relic, and they will not talk about how it travelled. This Hale-month it is in an upper room at [[Harrow's Green]], shut, with water brought to the floor around it. If sand has been laid on that floor, Rosire will not sit in the room.

A warden cannot Speak a lamp. Speaking is for a person standing under a Tree, and a lamp is not a person.

What anyone can check:

- The coal stays warm while the shutter is closed. When you open it, it does not make a flame the way a wick does, and if you wait it shows a count, a room, a door, and a name.
- It leans toward new heat and brightens when an old name is spoken. People who say there is only one Phoenix, and that the Phoenix is in care, call that a trick of the wrap.
- It has never fallen from a Tree. Set it against motherwood, the wood a Turning Tree is made of, and nothing horticultural happens, because what you are holding is metal and a banked coal.

## What people do with it

Most hours it does nothing, and it stays shut. The competence around it is ordinary: you do not leave a travelling-coal unattended, and you do not open it for a crowd.

A ticket-hand may carry it because the room it came from should not go dark. A guest who runs hot may dislike it and not be able to say why. A child who is not in any hymn may watch it lean and ask for bread anyway.

Nobody makes a pilgrimage to it. The devout already have a legend in care, and this is only a lamp that left the inner court.

## GM Notes

This is a fire-plot object, and it is not a cosmology pin. Use it at session-one moment 4 on [[The Opening]]. Narrate the lamp, the lean, and the two names. Do **not** narrate harvest, bank, remnant, Protectors, or "this is not a real Phoenix." Give facts, and do not hand over the meaning. The player body stays deniable, as a hospice-light, as leftover coal, or as a wrap that leans. Remnant-versus-Gift confirmation lives on [[When the Fire Is Caught]], and this object does not close it.

What it actually is: banked Phoenix-fire from a prepared death, caught and shuttered. While a Gift lasts it can show a forced Rise's facts, the count, the layout, and a name. After a Gift ends it is leftover fire in a tin. It cannot Give, it cannot be Given, and it cannot Rise anyone. Immunity-warmth can linger because those are properties of the *fire*. Kindle and the Dwindling do not live in the lamp.

It is not [[The Spent Leaf]], and it is not harvested fire treated as a leaf. Those misreadings are rejected. Colourless tissue is a Giving-remainder, which is what [[The Spent Leaf]] is, and this is coal in brass. Do not braid the pins, do not let a remainder-clerk identify it as uncounted fall, and do not let a Protector call it a second Phoenix.

If it is opened it will show a count, a sanded room, a door that was not on the slate, a name the fire still answers, and a child it also knows. It will not assign a self, a forgiveness, a Gift's purpose, a second Gift, or her. If it tries to tell someone who they are, that is a keeper talking through stored fire, which is [[A Hidden Phoenix]].

The Care has more of these. This is the one that walked. Do not make it unique cosmology, and do not invent a lamp-cult. Keep it at ~5%: a closed shutter, a lean, and tea afterward.

Do not fire clue 5 with this. Remnant-to-ash, and a Tree arbitrating two names, stay later, and the lamp can stay lit through session one.
