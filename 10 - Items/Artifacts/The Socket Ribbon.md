# The Socket Ribbon

This one is embellished.

In Eolthael someone ties a cloth ribbon on the open socket of the old shrine-work at [[The Seeing-Ring]]. By Leddorn it is gone. Tithsael takes a colour-ribbon off. A hug-ribbon they leave. That kindness is already the night-keeper's habit.

Pilgrims say the wood sent the ribbon, or that it fell from the origin and landed on this hill. It is cloth. Someone on the hill ties it. They will not say why they chose a colour. The origin did not drop it. A person can untie it and hold it. It is a strip of cloth. It does not Speak, and it does not cast anything.

Two of the ring's four out-look gaps face nothing on a present map. Nobody says why.

## GM Notes

A later note must not explain why the ribbon is a colour. It must not turn the cloth into a power, a relic, or a rite. Do not let the ribbon prove a mother, a Hand, or a year for the ring. Tithsael taking a colour-ribbon off stays a kindness. It is not a detection spell.
