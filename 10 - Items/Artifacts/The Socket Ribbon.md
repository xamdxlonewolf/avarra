# The Socket Ribbon

This one is embellished, which means the story pilgrims tell is larger than the cloth.

In Eolthael someone ties a cloth ribbon on the open socket of the old shrine-work at [[The Seeing-Ring]], and by Leddorn it is gone. Tithsael takes a colour-ribbon off and leaves a hug-ribbon, and that kindness is already the night-keeper's habit.

Pilgrims say the wood sent the ribbon, or that it fell from the origin and landed on this hill. It is cloth that someone on the hill ties, and they will not say why they chose a colour, and the origin did not drop it. A person can untie it and hold it, and it is a strip of cloth that does not Speak and does not cast anything.

Two of the ring's four out-look gaps face nothing on a present map. Nobody says why.

## GM Notes

A later note must not explain why the ribbon is a colour, and it must not turn the cloth into a power, a relic, or a rite. Do not let the ribbon prove a mother, a Hand, or a year for the ring. Tithsael taking a colour-ribbon off stays a kindness, and it is not a detection spell.
