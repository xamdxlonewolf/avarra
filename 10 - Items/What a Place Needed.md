# What a Place Needed

These are ordinary things a place already needed. They are not a ladder of better kit. Nothing here is rarer than the next town's version of the same need. A coat from this list is clothing. It keeps weather off. It is not armor unless the wearer is also wearing armor from the SRD.

If a PC fights with one of the tools, use the smallest one-handed physical weapon on the SRD list. Add nothing because of the town that made it.

| Place | What they keep | Why it is there |
|---|---|---|
| Rain-Wall shelves, the passes above [[Lirorn]] | An oiled wool coat | The shelf is wet and cold. A quay shirt does not finish the walk. |
| [[Orentel]]'s quays | A short hook, and a coat waxed against brine | Lines, crates, and salt. The hook pulls. The coat is for the spray. |
| A young graft on the Heskoren live front | A pruning knife, and a wind cloak | The wood is new and the weather is not. The knife is a tender's tool. It is not [[The One Knife]]. |
| Rain-Shadow herds, toward [[The Dry Stair]] and [[Ornsael]] | A goat-hair blanket, and a water-skin | The days are dry and the well is shared. The blanket is the herd's hair, felted. |
| [[The Sundering Isle\|Kumbaan]] halls and shallows | A peat cloak, and a short knife for mussels | Smoke, rain, and the rocks. The cloak is for the hall and the terrace. It does not cross the storm-wall. |
| Mother-core orchards | Beeswax | Hives, and the halls that are owed wax when the blossom fails. [[Ecology of the Turning]] already has the bee. |

Motherwood is not on this list. A scion is a warden's cargo, not a material a PC buys. [[The Spent Leaf]] and [[The Closed Lamp]] are not craft stock.

## GM Notes

No prices, no tiers, no bonuses. Do not promote a coat into armor with a score, or a hook into a named magic weapon. The storm-wall stays weather. The pruning knife does not name the cutter.
