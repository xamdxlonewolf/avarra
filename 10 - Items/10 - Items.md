## GM Notes

This section is a pointer. The weapon, armor, equipment, and materials notes link to [[What a Place Needed]] and hold no lists of their own, and a later note must not add one.

[[The Spent Leaf]] is the keystone's firing pin. It is the confirmable artifact for [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]]. The confirmation sits in that leaf's GM Notes, and the player body stays deniable.

[[The Closed Lamp]] is a fire-plot object, and it is not a leaf. It belongs to [[When the Fire Is Caught]] and to the session opening, [[The Opening]].
