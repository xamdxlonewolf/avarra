# Weapons

Knives, hooks, and pruning blades that a place needed are on [[What a Place Needed]]. There is no ladder of better weapons.

If a fight needs a number, use the smallest one-handed physical weapon on the SRD list. Add nothing because of the town that made it. [[The Ribboned Knife]] is an argued object. It lives with the other artifacts. The ribbon adds nothing.
