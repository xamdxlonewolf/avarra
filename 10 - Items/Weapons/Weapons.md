# Weapons

Knives, hooks, and pruning blades that a place needed are on [[What a Place Needed]], and there is no ladder of better weapons above them. If a fight needs a number, use the smallest one-handed physical weapon on the SRD list, and add nothing because of the town that made it. [[The Ribboned Knife]] is an argued object, so it lives with the other artifacts, and the ribbon adds nothing.
