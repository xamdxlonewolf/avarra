# Weapons

Knives, hooks, and pruning blades that a place already needed are on [[What a Place Needed]]. There is no ladder of better weapons.

If a fight needs a number, that note already says which weapon on the SRD list to use. [[The Ribboned Knife]] is an argued object. It lives with the other artifacts. The ribbon adds nothing.

## GM Notes

This folder stays a pointer. A later note must not write a weapon list, a price, or a bonus here.
