# Conditions — Index

> **What a Condition is.** The *acquired* layer of the two-layer model (see [[The Premise]]): a recognizable "monster" reframed as an integrated, needed role in society. Every person has a **Kind** (born ancestry) and, for ~75% of people, a **Condition** (a becoming). Conditions arise two ways — **Given** at the [[The Premise#The engine — how Conditions arise (LOCKED)|Awakening / Leaf-Fall]] at age 10, or **Struck** by a later threshold. Each carries a **Tithe** (upkeep; the ~5%-scary edge).

## Mechanical frame

Conditions are built as Daggerheart **Transformation cards** (see the SRD Transformations WIP): one flavor line + **two features** — a **boon** and a **Tithe/drawback** — hooked to real resources (Hope, Stress, HP, Fear, tiers, Experiences). One transformation per PC; it sits in the loadout like a heritage card and doesn't count against domain-card limits. **Design rule:** a Tithe is satisfied by normal play/rest; the scary edge is opt-in or narrative — it must **never seize control of the PC**. Keep takes recognizable-but-fresh and reasonably system-portable.
