# Conditions — Index

A Condition is what a person becomes, not the ancestry they are born with. That born half is a Kind, and the features and the mixes are on [[Kind Heritage]]. For ~75% of people there is also a Condition: one of the old monsters, living in town as a neighbor with a job the town needs. The Tree gives most of them at the [[Turning Tree|Awakening / Leaf-Fall]], the ceremony at the Turning Tree at age 10, when the colour of a leaf names the Condition. The rest are Struck later, by a threshold the Tree does not hand out at that ceremony. Either way the Condition carries a Tithe, an upkeep you meet by ordinary play and ordinary rest, and the Tithe must never seize control of the person. The population shares, and the split between Given and Struck, are the tables below.

## Mechanical frame

Each Condition is built as a Daggerheart Transformation card (see the SRD Transformations WIP). The card opens with a line of flavor and then its features. At minimum there is a boon, the thing the Condition lets you do, and a Tithe, the drawback that keeps the boon honest. Both of them hook to resources the game already uses — Hope, Stress, Hit Points, Fear, weapon tiers, and Experiences — rather than to a private meter that only the Condition understands. A player character holds one transformation. It sits in the loadout the way a heritage card does, and it does not count against the limit on domain cards.

A Tithe is satisfied by normal play and normal rest. The frightening edge is something the player opts into, or something the story brings, and it must never seize control of the player character. Each take should stay recognizable and still feel fresh, and the card should be portable enough to carry to another table that uses the same kind of resources. How many features a card carries is allowed to vary, and that variation is deliberate. A spotlight Condition, or a complicated one, carries more features than a workhorse card. What stays constant is the shape of the card: a clean boon, and a Tithe with its name written on it.

### Advancement

A Condition card does not level, and it does not grow because the character does. A becoming is not a class. The card you hold at level 1 is the card you hold at level 10, while class features, domain cards, Experiences, weapon tier, and Hope scars are what change around it.

A few things look like the card is growing, and they are not level-scaling.

- [[Two-Bodied]] may attach an animal-form Experience when they would gain an Experience. A second signature capability is a once-ever story milestone, not a level pick.
- [[Returned]] and [[Phoenix]] spend Hope slots, and that spending is a countdown, not a power that climbs as they level.
- [[Bound]] Terms and the Pledge move when the story moves them, not when the character takes a level.
- Attacks that use **Proficiency** (Kindle, Reach, the Green Word, the Two-Bodied natural weapon) rise because Proficiency rises.

### Tithe clocks at a long rest

A Tithe clock drains or fills on a long rest, unless the card names a different trigger, and a day that holds two short rests does not tick that clock a second time. When the long rest comes you run the list once, and each line is a yes-or-no question for that rest alone.

| Card | At this long rest | Not a rest clock |
|---|---|---|
| [[Long-Lived]] | Spent Hope on Unfading Mind, used Blood-Read, or spent a downtime move in study or witness? **Refill.** Else **remove a Memory token.** | — |
| [[The Answered]] | Spent Hope on Reach since last long rest? **Refill.** Else **remove a token.** | Asking the element a question |
| [[The Taken-In]] | Can you touch a living plant where you rest? **Refill.** Else **remove a Green token.** | — |
| [[The Stilled]] | This long rest included a physical downtime move, a spar, a drill, or a fight? If not, **add a Grey token.** | Gaze still adds Grey when used |
| [[The Unbound]] | Rested with someone who knows your name and shares the meal or the watch? **Refill Quiet.** Else tokens stay. | Spending Quiet in play |
| [[Returned]] | Did anything toward your Purpose since last long rest? If not, settle (Stress, cold touch). | — |
| [[Far-Voiced]] | — | Scene: mark Stress from feeling, or the GM names grief, terror, rage, or joy |
| [[Two-Bodied]] | — | A day without the other self; next Fear roll shifts you |
| [[Bound]] | — | Terms and the Whisper |
| [[Phoenix]] | — | Death and the Dwindling |

## The roster — 9 selectable + 1 downstream

There are **9 Conditions a person can be Given or Struck with.** The **10th — [[The Unbound]] — is not a starting Condition:** it is the downstream state a [[Bound]] enters when a term-limited contract ends without another being signed. You can only become Unbound *through* Bound, in play or arranged at session zero. So there are **9 to choose from, and 10 in the world.**

### The 9 selectable Conditions
| Condition | Monster | Standing | Path | Share (of all people) | Given / Struck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Long-Lived]] | vampire | rare / revered | Given | ~2.5% | all Given |
| [[Two-Bodied]] | werewolf | common | Given | ~18% | all Given |
| [[Returned]] | zombie | normal | Struck | ~7% | all Struck |
| [[Bound]] | demon (by contract) | feared | Struck | ~5% contract-lane (about 3.5% currently Bound, 1.5% Unbound) | all Struck |
| [[Far-Voiced]] | siren | revered | Both | ~12% | ~11% Given / ~1% Struck |
| [[The Stilled]] | gorgon | feared / regulated | Both | ~7% | ~6% Given / ~1% Struck |
| [[The Answered]] | djinn | wildcard | Given | ~10% | all Given |
| [[The Taken-In]] | leshy | looked-down / frontier | Both | ~13% | ~12% Given / ~1% Struck |
| [[Phoenix]] | phoenix | legendary | Given | <0.1% (one at a time) | all Given |

### The downstream 10th
| Condition | Monster | Standing | Path | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[The Unbound]] *(colloq. the Hollow)* | the price of a contract | pitied / uneasy | Struck — **chains off [[Bound]] only** | ~1.5%, *inside* Bound's ~5% contract-lane |

**Over a lifetime,** about 60% of people are Given a Condition at the Tree, about 15% are Struck later, and about 25% are Kept. The Given and the Struck together are the ~75% who carry a Condition. At the ceremony itself about 40% of children are hugged and receive no colour, and a little over a third of those children are still Struck someday.

The Kept are the ~25% who carry no Condition for life, because the Tree hugged them at the ceremony and they are wanted in their own right.

The Given-Over is a title rather than a Condition: the world's name for a Bound who signs contract after contract until the being effectively owns them. It is an NPC-tier fate, not a card anyone plays, and the account of that fate is on [[Bound]].

## GM Notes

The cards and this hub are marked `visibility: player`, so an export for players keeps the mechanics. Design history and harvest rules stay behind walls or in `11 - Secrets`.

[[The Premise]], which is a GM note, is the only source for population shares. The share tables on this hub, and the population line on each card, quote it, and they have to change when it changes. Do not keep a second roster anywhere else. The figures were checked against The Premise on 2026-10-22, and every figure matched.

The rule that Condition cards do not level was locked on 2026-08-30. If a later pass wants a Condition to advance, it needs a new story. Do not quietly add level text to a card.

There is one Gift, and the account of it is [[When the Fire Is Caught]]. The harvesters are [[The Protectors]], and the hidden seat, where a player still has agency, is [[A Hidden Phoenix]].

The balance record is [[Condition Audit]], the working tracker is [[Build Plan]], and the longer tracker is [[Roadmap]].
