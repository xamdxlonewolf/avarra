# Conditions — Index

> **What a Condition is.** The *acquired* layer of the two-layer model (see [[The Premise]]): a recognizable "monster" reframed as an integrated, needed role in society. Every person has a **Kind** (born ancestry) and, for ~75% of people, a **Condition** (a becoming). Conditions arise two ways — **Given** at the [[The Premise#The engine — how Conditions arise (LOCKED)|Awakening / Leaf-Fall]] at age 10, or **Struck** by a later threshold. Each carries a **Tithe** (upkeep; the ~5%-scary edge).

## Mechanical frame

Conditions are built as Daggerheart **Transformation cards** (see the SRD Transformations WIP): a flavor line + **features** — at minimum a **boon** and a **Tithe/drawback**, hooked to real resources (Hope, Stress, HP, Fear, tiers, Experiences). One transformation per PC; it sits in the loadout like a heritage card and doesn't count against domain-card limits. **Design rule:** a Tithe is satisfied by normal play/rest; the scary edge is opt-in or narrative — it must **never seize control of the PC**. Keep takes recognizable-but-fresh and reasonably system-portable. *(Feature **count** varies card to card **by design** — spotlight/complex Conditions carry more than the workhorse cards; what's held constant is a clean boon + labeled Tithe per card. See [[Condition Audit]].)*

### Advancement (LOCKED 2026-08-30)

**Condition cards do not advance with character level.** A becoming is not a class. The card you hold at level 1 is the card you hold at level 10. Class features, domain cards, Experiences, weapon tier, and Hope scars are what change around it.

What looks like growth and is not level-scaling:

- [[Two-Bodied]] may attach an animal-form Experience when they would gain an Experience. A second signature capability is a once-ever story milestone, not a level pick.
- [[Returned]] and [[Phoenix]] spend Hope slots. That is a countdown, not a power-up.
- [[Bound]] Terms and Pledge move by story.
- Attacks that use **Proficiency** (Kindle, Reach, the Green Word, the Two-Bodied natural weapon) rise because Proficiency rises.

If a later pass wants Condition advancement, it needs a new story. Do not quietly add level text to a card.

### Tithe clocks at a long rest

Tithe clocks drain or fill on a **long rest** unless the card names a different trigger. A day with two short rests does not tick these clocks twice. At the rest, run this list once. Each line is a yes-or-no.

## The roster — 9 selectable + 1 downstream

There are **9 Conditions a person can be Given or Struck with.** The **10th — [[The Unbound]] — is not a starting Condition:** it is the downstream state a [[Bound]] enters when a term-limited contract ends without another being signed. You can only become Unbound *through* Bound (in play or arranged at session zero). So: **9 to choose from, 10 in the world.**

### The downstream 10th
| Condition | Monster | Standing | Path | ~% pop. | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[The Unbound]] *(colloq. the Hollow)* | the price of a contract | pitied / uneasy | Struck — **chains off [[Bound]] only** | ~1.5% | R.2 repaired (2026-08-30) |

**The Kept** — the normal ~25% who receive no Condition (the Tree hugs them; wanted in their own right).
**The Given-Over** *(title, not a Condition)* — the world's name for a Bound who signs contract after contract until the being effectively owns them; an NPC-tier fate. See [[Bound]].
