# The Unbound

The Unbound are what a term-limited [[Bound]] contract leaves behind when the term ends. The counterparty releases the person and collects the Pledge, the part of themselves that person named at the signing, and they walk away unbound: free of the contract at last, and left with a hole where that piece used to be, exactly as the contract agreed. The state is Struck only, and it chains off Bound, so there is no other door into it. It is rare, ~1.5% of all people, and that figure is counted inside Bound's ~5% contract-lane, because only the Bound whose contracts were term-limited, rather than open-ended, ever arrive here. People still call them the Hollow, in ordinary speech, for the absence the freedom cost.

The Condition takes its title from the release: the contract is over, and the person is unbound. Common speech fixes on the cost instead and calls them the Hollow. Both names are true, one of them a freedom and the other an absence, and this note uses the Unbound for the state and the Hollow for the name people live with day to day.

## The take

The Pledge was chosen at the signing, before any of the power had been spent, so you knew what you would owe. It might be an eye, or a leg, or your capacity for fear, or your memory of home. The loss is grief and gift at the same time, because the hole where that part was is also an immunity to whatever used to come in through it. People are not afraid of the Unbound so much as unsettled by the absence in them, and the standing that follows is pity mixed with unease. Where the [[Long-Lived]] have to keep learning if they want to stay whole, the Unbound have to keep connecting if they want to stay quiet. The fuel runs the other way.

## Away from the Tree

An Unbound has no colour, because the state chains off Bound and the Tree never named them, so there was never a leaf, and you cannot start a character here. The path is Struck only, and only by passing through Bound, which is why the cause lives on [[Bound]], while the palette, including the becomings that have no colour, is on [[Turning Tree#Reading the colours (colour → Condition)]] and the Tree's own giving is on [[Turning Tree]].

[[The Leaf-Mother|Motherfaith]] pities a person who paid with a part of themselves, while [[The Fair Hand]] hears a closed account, the debt paid in full so the person walks away. [[The Watching]] names a seeing of what is missing, and [[The Old Ways]] still count them as kin of the oath-door rather than as someone the Tree refused.

Nothing institutional comes next, because the chain runs off [[Bound]] and stops: there is no colour, no guild, and no school. Family keeps an Unbound person company through a long rest, or a hired companion does. The [[The Book-Hands|Book-Hands]] settled the contract in the year the person became Bound, and they do not help with the loss afterwards.

## Daggerheart — Transformation card

*You paid what you promised. Something is missing now — and the missing place is quiet, and strong.*

- **The Absence** — Name what the contract took, which is the Pledge from [[Bound]]. You lose it permanently, in the fiction and, where the loss matters, on the sheet. The hole is also a narrow immunity. You are immune to effects that would mark Stress, or that would grant the GM Fear, when those effects work through the capacity you gave up. You are not immune to every roll that sits in the same broad lane. Agree the details with the GM at the moment you become Hollow. The loss falls in one of three lanes, and the lanes do not borrow from each other.
  - A capacity of the mind, such as fear, grief, love, the ability to lie, or the memory of a person or a place, becomes a lack in the fiction, and the immunity covers only the Stress-marking or Fear-granting effects that use that particular capacity. If you gave up fear, a terror that would mark Stress does not mark it. You still have to treat a scared crowd as a crowd. If you gave up deceit, you cannot lie, and a lie that would have marked Stress on you if it landed does not mark Stress. You do not automatically know every lie you hear.
  - A sense of the body, an eye or hearing or taste, is a fictional loss with a sharp edge in play. Give up an eye and you have disadvantage on rolls that need depth or distance. You are immune to Stress-marking effects that require the sense you lost, including [[The Stilled|the Stilling Gaze]] aimed at you and a blinding light that would otherwise mark Stress. An illusion you have to see still works on you, unless the illusion would mark Stress through that missing sense.
  - A part of the body, a leg or a hand, is a fictional loss plus a standing penalty the group accepts, offset by immunity to Stress-marking pain and to other Stress-marking effects that work through that part. In the fiction there is often a prosthetic, or a Condition-crafted replacement.
- **The Quiet** *(Tithe)* — Place **3 Quiet tokens** on this card, and begin play with them filled. Spend a Quiet token to act through the hole. You take an action the missing thing would have stopped, or you gain advantage on a roll where the empty place makes the hard thing easy. You still mark Stress from effects that sit outside your Absence immunity. Quiet is not a second immunity to the same lane, and it is not the clause on [[Returned]] that leaves a Returned unshaken by corpses, gore, and deathly places. If you pledged fear, Quiet does not let you automatically pass a charnel house. At a long rest, refill all the tokens if you rest with at least one other person who knows your name and shares the meal or the watch. If you are alone, or among strangers who do not know you, the tokens stay where they are. At 0 tokens the pull turns loud. While the card is empty, whenever a bargain, a favor, or an offer of power is on the table, the GM may present that offer as unusually reasonable, and refusing to reach for it costs a Stress. The offer is a suggestion and never a compulsion, so you can always say no, and saying no aches. This ache is the road back to [[Bound]], and from there into the Given-Over spiral.

## Player guardrail (IMPORTANT)

A player cannot simply choose the Unbound at character creation, because it is a downstream state. You become Unbound only after you have played through an actual [[Bound]] contract, or arranged one with the GM at session zero: what the contract was, what you Pledged, and the moment the term came due and the Pledge was collected. The Absence has to be a specific loss that the table has agreed, with real weight. It is not a free immunity you pick off a list. Talk it through with the GM before anyone writes it on a sheet.

## GM Notes

The Quiet is a temptation, not a compulsion. Every beat in which the emptiness reaches for a bargain has to be refusable, and the refusal costs a Stress. The tragedy to play toward is an Unbound who stops connecting. The quiet inside them gets loud, they sign again to mute it, they pay another Pledge, and they slide toward the Given-Over, which is accounted on [[Bound]]. Warmth, another person who knows their name and stays through the rest, is the only thing that actually fills the hole. A new contract only mutes it.

Keep the three lanes apart when the table starts to blur them. Absence stops Stress, or Fear, that comes through the pledged hole. Quiet spends a token so the character can act anyway. The unshaken clause on [[Returned]] is corpses, gore, and deathly places, and only the Returned have it. If a table is automatically passing every fear roll on both cards, pull the ruling back to those lines.
