# 09 - Creatures

This folder holds the Condition cards, which are *becomings*, the changes a person comes to live with, and not a menagerie. It also holds a few adversaries you meet on the road, and the working animals people live beside. What the world would recognize as its monsters lives on [[Conditions]]. When the wilderness has a roll to make, that roll lives on [[Dangers of the Turning]].

## Conditions (the roster)

There are nine selectable becomings, and one downstream state that follows a Bound rather than being chosen beside the nine.

- [[Conditions]] — the index, the rest clocks, and the rule that these cards do not scale with level
- **Given only:** [[Long-Lived]] · [[Two-Bodied]] · [[The Answered]] · [[Phoenix]]
- **Struck only:** [[Returned]] · [[Bound]] → [[The Unbound]]
- **Both:** [[Far-Voiced]] · [[The Stilled]] · [[The Taken-In]]

The table procedures that sit on top of the cards are [[At the Table]], [[Kind Heritage]], and [[A Hidden Phoenix]].

## Adversaries (the road)

These are a small Daggerheart layer for the road, and they are not a second taxonomy of monsters.

- [[Dangers of the Turning]] — when to open this layer, and the environments and pairings that go with it
- [[Gap-Cat]] — a Tier 1 Skulk, the long-bodied cat of the high rock
- [[Chart-Crew]] — a Tier 1 Horde, the hands who wait for hulls that do not arrive
- [[Bed-Keeper]] — a Tier 1 Leader, the host whose bed will not empty
- [[Spent-Week Walker]] — a Tier 2 Standard, a person whose tenth-year week already closed
- [[Colourless Host]] — a Tier 2 Support, dead wood that still wants to Give

Settlement scene-dangers, the cohort and the crane and the well-mouth, stay on the settlement notes. The Pre-Walk sites are [[The Low Wall]], [[The Seeing-Ring]], and [[The Dry Stair]].

## Beasts (livelihoods)

These were promoted from the table on [[Ecology of the Turning]]. Each animal has a use and a fear, and a [[Two-Bodied]] other self stays a person.

- [[The Cart-Ox]] — plough and haul, and a ford the ox will not take once the water is over the axle
- [[The Terrace Goat]] — milk and scrub, and the house's fear of the wall and the well-share
- [[The Path Dog]] — the path and the door, and the floodwater the dog will not take

## A made thing

[[A Made Thing]] is the ruling for a thing left running. Condition labor and Answered craft already do this work, so if one of these things exists it has a maker and a problem, and it is not a people. Clank stay a Kind on [[Kinds of the Turning]].

## Spirits

[[Kin at the Door]] is a meal left at an old door, and a Returned person sent where the living die of the work. There is no other list.

## Monsters and unique creatures

[[Monsters]] says there is no bestiary, and the Conditions stay in this section. [[Unique]] says there is no quota of unique creatures. Neither folder holds a creature note.
