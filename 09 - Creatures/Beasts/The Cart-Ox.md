# The Cart-Ox

An ox ploughs, hauls timber, and pulls a cart. The herds are on the core farms, the Chart-run plain, and the broad vales of [[Heskoren]]. A breeding animal is worth more to a house than a week's wages. People are more afraid of losing that ox than of the ox itself.

The ox will not take a ford when the water is over the axle. It stops. A driver who knows the animal waits, or finds the shallow, or unloads. Whipping it through is how a house loses the season.

On a ford that is already shallow, the driver still gets down and leads. They will not say why.

A [[Two-Bodied]] person in an ox's body is a person. This animal is an ox. It is not on a guild roll, and it is not one of the five adversaries on [[Dangers of the Turning]].
