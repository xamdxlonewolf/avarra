# The Path Dog

A dog herds, guards a door, and keeps a quay or a hall honest about who is coming in. Farms, quays, halls, and road-houses keep them. The use is the path: the dog knows the way the house uses, and the house trusts the stop.

The dog is afraid of floodwater. It refuses a flooded path. Handlers who know the dog stop with it. A driver who drags it through is arguing with the animal about the water, and the animal has already read the path.

A granary cat, on the same livelihood table, marks spoilage. That is a different animal. This note is the dog.

A [[Two-Bodied]] person in a dog's body is a person. This animal is a dog. The five adversaries on [[Dangers of the Turning]] stay the list for a fight. A dog on a quay is not that list.
