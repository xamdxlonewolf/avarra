# A Made Thing

The work people expect from a construct is already someone's job.

An [[The Answered|Answered]] coaxes stone, water, fire, or air, and then goes home. A [[The Stilled|Stilled]] holds a body still for a knife. A mill-hand sets the stones. A hull-wright walks a hull. A road-mend leaves a shovel in the bank. Those are people, and the notes for their work are already written. [[How the Work Is Done]] is the craft. [[Conditions]] are the becomings. Neither one is a body you can own.

[[Kinds of the Turning|Clank]] are a Kind. They are people. They mend flood-walls, hulls, mills, and lofts with everyone else who does that work. A made thing is a thing. A Clank is a person.

## If one is in the scene

Most towns do not have one. If a scene has a thing that keeps working without a person in it — a crane left running, a mill someone swears turns with nobody at the stones, a figure of clay — it has a maker and a problem.

The maker is a person, or was. The problem is why it is still doing the thing: a stop that broke, a debt the maker did not finish, a story that got ahead of the mechanism. Name both before the scene goes on.

It has no Kind, no Condition, no hearth, and no domain card. It owes no Tithe. If the work needs doing, send the person whose job it is.

A town without that problem does not keep one on a shelf.
