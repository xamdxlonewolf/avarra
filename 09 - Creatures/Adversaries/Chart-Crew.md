# Chart-Crew

Tier 1 Horde. Night-Shore hands who wait for hulls that do not arrive, then take what the water gives. Grief with a hook. Not a pirate flag. Not [[The Standing Trade]] — they sell wreck-right, not a mouth.

**Motives & Tactics:** claim the wash · keep the slate empty · drive a stranger off a berth · fold when a known clerk stamps

**Difficulty:** 11 | **Thresholds:** 7/12 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** +1 | **Hooks and gaffs:** Very Close | 1d6+3 phy

**Experience:** Quay Work +2

## Features

**Horde (1d4+2) - Passive:** When the Horde has marked half or more of their HP, their standard attack deals 1d4+2 physical damage instead.

**We Were Here First - Action:** Attack a target within Very Close range who has claimed wreck-right, a berth, or a body. On a success, deal standard damage and the target must mark a Stress or drop what they were holding.

**Empty-Line Shout - Reaction:** When a PC fails a Presence roll at a night-berth, or when a Far-Voiced Cries without a hall-hour, you may mark a Stress to have the Horde make a standard attack against that PC as they close the quay.

## GM Notes

- They are people. Defeat can be a routed quay, a clerk's stamp, or a Bound word — not only corpses.
- Do not clone the Standing Trade. A sold vouch is a different house. This crew wants the hull, not a citizenship sentence.
- A late berth on [[Named Ground]] is enough excuse to spotlight them. They do not need a mastermind.
