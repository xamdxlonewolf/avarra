# Colourless Host

Tier 2 Support. A graft that never took, or wood that sickened, still dropping leaves with no colour. It wants to Give. It cannot. People who stand under it too long start to act as if a week were happening. Not a Tree. Not a third outcome. Dead wood is not a Tree.

**Motives & Tactics:** gather a circle · drop a leaf that names nothing · make a warden guess · thin a Tithe · fold when someone names it unsound and walks

**Difficulty:** 14 | **Thresholds:** 10/20 | **HP:** 5 | **Stress:** 5
**ATK:** +2 | **Lash of dry bough:** Close | 2d6+3 mag

**Experience:** Unsound Wood +3

## Features

**No Colour - Passive:** Leaves fall when a PC starts their turn within Close range. That PC must mark a Stress or make an Instinct roll (Difficulty 14). On a failure they are **Vulnerable** until they leave Close range — they stood as if they were ten. This is not a Gift and not a hug. A warden who Speaks a colour here is inventing.

**The Circle Forms - Action:** Once per scene, spend a Fear to pull each target within Far range who failed **No Colour** to Very Close range of this adversary. A [[Spent-Week Walker]] (if present) moves for free. Allies of this adversary within Close range clear a Stress.

**Wrong Green Adjacent - Reaction:** When a PC uses a Condition feature (Gaze, Cry, Reach, a Two-Bodied shift, Kindle) within Close range, you may mark a Stress to have that feature cost +1 Hope or +1 Stress (owner's choice). The wood is trying to *read* them. It cannot.

**Named Unsound - Passive:** If a PC spends an action to say, in the fiction, that this wood is unsound and the standing is over — and at least one other PC leaves Close range — this adversary marks 2 HP and cannot use **The Circle Forms** again this scene. Horticulture, not an exorcism.

## GM Notes

- **Not a Tree.** A taking never happened; a pot died; a scion sickened. The First Cut's "dead wood is not a Tree" stays. Do not let this block become a second-chance year or a third visible outcome.
- **Do not confirm her.** Believers will say a Hand that cannot close. Skeptics will say a plant that still tropisms. Both can be loud. The player body does not settle it. `reveals` stays empty.
- **Not the Wrong Green.** That year was a human name on a hug. This is wood that cannot Turn. Cite the year if a warden is present and wants a story; do not merge them.
- **Support, not Solo.** Pair with walkers or a cat. A Host alone is a scene about leaving, not a boss.
- **Tone.** 5%. Wrongness is a leaf with no colour and a circle that forms anyway. Do not make the bough whisper secrets or Speak a Condition.
