# Bed-Keeper

Tier 1 Leader. Someone who still makes the bed after the walk ended. Hospitality as a hold. They want the upper room occupied. They will feed you. They will not like the door.

**Motives & Tactics:** seat you · ask which walk · fill the slate · call a walker to the lintel · never start with a knife

**Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 8/14 | **HP:** 5 | **Stress:** 4
**ATK:** +1 | **Poker and pan:** Melee | 1d6+2 phy

**Experience:** Host-Right +3

## Features

**The House Stands for You - Passive:** While at least one PC has eaten in this house tonight, attacks against the Bed-Keeper from those PCs have Disadvantage unless the PC first succeeds on a Presence roll (Difficulty 12) to break guest-right in public.

**Stay the Night - Action:** Choose a target within Close range who has accepted food or a bed. The target must mark a Stress or stay in Very Close range of this adversary until they succeed on a Presence roll (Difficulty 12) as an action. On a failure with Fear, a [[Spent-Week Walker]] (if present) or a second guest arrives at the door.

**More Where You Came From - Action:** Spend a Fear to spotlight a Spent-Week Walker or a Chart-Crew at Far range — someone the slate still lists as due tonight.

**Don't Make Me Ask Twice - Reaction:** When a PC leaves Melee range without a word, mark a Stress to make a standard attack against them, or to increase the Difficulty of the next watch's three questions by 2 at the next town (the slight travelled).

## GM Notes

- Orthogonal to bandits: they want occupancy, not your purse. Thilim's Held bed is a *kind* version of this job; do not restock the Third Hearth as this block. This keeper is the road-house that did not become a deathless house or a hearth — it became a mouth that cannot stop hosting.
- Defeat can be a door closed, a slate washed, or a Bound word that the week is over. Killing the host is legal and ugly; the next shrine will hear it.
- Do not give them a Condition that is the plot. They may be Kept, Given, or Struck like anyone. The stat block is the hold, not a Tithe.
