# Gap-Cat

Tier 1 Skulk. A long-bodied cat of the high rock — tawny on the Rain-Wall, smoke-grey on Heskoren shale. Not a Condition. Not a story about a person who shifted and stayed. Two-Bodied range against these.

**Motives & Tactics:** take the straggler · keep the high rock · drag down, not duel · leave when the noise is a crowd

**Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 7/12 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** +1 | **Bite and drop:** Melee | 1d8+2 phy

**Experience:** High Rock +2

## Features

**From Above - Passive:** While this adversary is at a higher elevation than its target, its standard attack deals +2 damage and the target must mark a Stress on a success.

**Drag the Straggler - Action:** Move up to Close range and attack a target who is at least Very Close from every other PC. On a success, deal standard damage and place the target in Melee with this adversary at the cat's elevation if a path exists.

**Gone to Shale - Reaction:** When this adversary marks 2 or more HP from an attack, you may spend a Fear to have it move to Far range and hide. The next attack against it has Disadvantage unless the attacker can name where it went (Instinct, Difficulty 12).

## GM Notes

- Spotlight as an animal. It does not hate Trees and it does not know the party. It wants meat that lagged.
- A Two-Bodied in a form that can take a shelf may track or warn; that is ranging, not a second adversary type.
- Do not give it a Tithe or a leaf-colour. If you want a person in a cat-shape, that is a Two-Bodied NPC, and you should not use this block for them.
