# The Meaning of Avarra

> **GM ONLY.** The flagship reveal chain, decoded by the *players* through pattern recognition — never delivered by an NPC as exposition.

## The secret (GM truth)

*Avarra* is the true name of the unified reality that fractured into the [[The Fracture|Green Reach and the Kiln]]. It is canonically correct and known to almost no one. Both halves carry **unrecognized linguistic descendants** of the word (working: Green Reach *Avara* ≈ "the world before"; Kiln *Av'r* ≈ "the joining / the completed form").

## How it surfaces (clues, in order)

The word appears constantly and untranslated long before it means anything — a god, a dead empire, the antiquarian guild's name, a root on ruins scholars argue over. Then the ladder:

1. *"Avarra was whole."* — read at first as the name of the ancient people.
2. *"When Avarra divided, we remained."* — the word starts to feel strange.
3. *"We are Avarra."* — the horrifying resolution: not the ancients, not an empire, not a planet — the name reality had when it was one thing.

## Player misreadings (what they'll think first)

- "Avarra" = the lost ancient civilization / empire.
- The two forms *Avara* and *Av'r* are unrelated words in different languages.

## The set-piece (first contact)

An NPC on the other side asks what the party calls *their* world. The party answers. The NPC pauses — *"That's what we call ours."* The players understand before the NPC does.

## Do-not-violate

The title moves setting name → archaeological mystery → name of the lost whole → **what the players choose to become**. The ending creates a *new* Avarra that contains difference; it does **not** restore the old one.
