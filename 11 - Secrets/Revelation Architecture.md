# Revelation Architecture

> **What this is.** Table procedure for secrets that already exist. Not a new cosmology. Open this when a reveal is about to land, or when a session wants fire without proving a mother. Index of tags: [[Reveal Index]]. Home notes hold the truths.

The world still holds her as belief. Skeptics are sincere and wrong about the mind. Confirmation is optional. The campaign can finish without firing the keystone.

## Two tracks (do not braid)

| Track | What it is | Needs the keystone? | Session one |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire plot** | Remnant + new Gift on-screen; a house that will not produce the one; a lamp that knows two mouths | **No** | Already running at [[Harrow's Green]] ([[The Opening]]) |
| **Cosmology** | She is real, bounded, costly; household later or never; fire-as-*hers* only if you need it | **Yes, and slow** | Do not settle it. Neighbours may argue. You do not |

**Fire-plot can run without firing the keystone.** Do not require [[The Spent Leaf]] to play Harrow's Green. Do not hold session one until a Giving-week pin. The Isolated Fall is a hush, a walker, and a lamp — not a proof.

Fire *cosmology* (that she Gave the new leaf; that the slot is hers) stays behind the keystone. Fire *plot* does not. Narrate facts. Do not narrate her.

## Firing order

This is the only order that keeps the deniable rungs intact and the household late.

| Beat | What fires | Pin | May start | Must stay deniable until |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — Fire plot** | Apparent two: leftover fire next to one Gift | [[The Opening]] kit · [[The Closed Lamp]] | Session one | Cosmology (she Gave / remnant-as-theology). Clue 5 (remnant to ash / Tree arbitrating) stays later |
| **1–5 — Keystone texture** | Gifts-fit · Kept-cared · reach-edge · Tree-intent · Struck-received | Towns, maps, testimony. Not an object | Anytime as weather | Confirmation language. Do not name a Hand on rung 5 |
| **6 — Keystone confirm** | Mind, kindness, cost in one gesture | [[The Spent Leaf]] held under a healthy Tree during a real colour-fall | Late; optional; the square (or people who matter) must *believe* | Household. Limit's nature. Sorting the Kept |
| **After 6, or never** | Household: she Gives; they Strike | No object-pin. Testimony already split; then a late naming | Only after the pin is believed, or never | First reveal of a campaign. Same-session dump with the leaf |
| **Side — Unspent** | Hobby anytime; cupboard-already-there late and small | [[The Unspent]] · [[The Pourers]] · a cup | Anytime as a shelf | Sixth Hand. Give or Strike. Fire harvest |
| **Late — Fire cosmology** | Slot was empty; she (or the Tree, if still unconfirmed) Gave; remnant is leftover | Remnant to ash when the store is spent, *or* new Gift and old name in one room and the Tree does not argue | After clue 4 has been sat with. After keystone *if* you need her in the sentence | Harvest-god. Second Gift. Spent Leaf as harvested fire |

**Skip is legal.** A table can run 0 for a whole campaign and never touch 6. A table can fire 6 and never name Hands. A table can name Hands years later, as *the family was always in the next room*.

**Do not reverse 6 and household.** Household confirmation is late and never first.

## What must stay deniable

Rungs 1–5 of the keystone. Early Spent Leaf checks (answers motherwood; kitchen-scale weight-log; warmth near a Tree). Struck testimony that *felt received*. The Uncoloured Intake's same stamp on different mouths. The Closed Lamp's lean and two names. Kumbaan's moons. Graft horticulture. The Care's public sentence (*the one is in care*).

Player bodies never confirm her. Clue notes take `keystone-adjacent`. `leaf-mother-is-real` is the keystone's own `reveal_tag` — public only if the *body* (not `## GM Notes`) would prove a mind.

## What confirmation does not include

Authoritative list lives on [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real#What confirmation does not include]]. Do not recopy. In play, the leaf will not:

- Sort the Kept (chosen vs past-her-edge)
- Name a Hand, a door, or a Struck
- Explain the nature of her limit
- Rerun a week, change a fall, or Give a second time
- Launder injustice — [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real#Social guard: kindness does not launder injustice]]

Household naming, if it ever happens, will not make the Leaf-Fall a committee, will not make the Other Hands villains, and will not make Yumboes refused. Unspent confirmation will not add a door.

Fire confirmation (clue 5) will not add a second Gift, will not make the remnant a Phoenix, and will not make the Spent Leaf a banked coal.

## How the opening already uses fire

[[The Isolated Fall]] / [[The Opening]] at [[Harrow's Green]], Hale-month, C.Y. 387:

- On-screen: remnant-walker (Gift already ended) · [[Rosire]] · new Gift ([[Taeren]] *or* a PC in that seat)
- Object: [[The Closed Lamp]] (moment 4). Facts, not meaning
- Offstage: her. Hands. The Care as a place you can walk into. Clue 5

**Stop lines for that hour:** no Spent Leaf. No remnant-to-ash. No second Given-door. No harvest narrated. No Mother proved. Agency stays on [[A Hidden Phoenix]].

If the table later wants cosmology under the fire, wait until the keystone has landed *or* keep saying *the Tree* and let neighbours argue. You are not required to cash the slot as hers.

## How to land a beat (one page)

1. **Name the track.** Plot or cosmology. If you cannot say which, it is plot. Stay deniable.
2. **Check the pair table.** If this beat wants the Spent Leaf *and* a Hand-name, drop the Hand-name. If it wants the lamp *and* her, drop her.
3. **Offer facts.** Warmth, a thinner canopy, a lamp that leans, a mouth that will not agree. Do not assign meaning. `positional-revelation`: the clerk, the baker, the ticket-hand — not a prophet.
4. **Let misreadings live.** The useful ones are already on each secret. Do not correct them in narration.
5. **Leave an out.** The Seat can bury the leaf. The remnant can keep walking. The intake book can stay filed as one catch. The campaign still works.

Tone: warmth with an ache. ~5% scary, and only on purpose. No harvest-god twist.

## What this architecture does not do

- Lock the nature of her limit
- Add a sixth Hand ([[The Unspent]] is furniture)
- Date the Tree, name the cutter, graft Kumbaan, coin liturgy, or add a sixteenth power
- Reopen [[Contradictions]] C-01–C-05
- Confirm her in any player body
