# Revelation Architecture

> **What this is.** Table procedure for secrets that already exist, not a new cosmology and not a new power. Open it when a reveal is about to land, or when a session wants the fire plot without proving a mother. The index of tags is [[Reveal Index]], and the home notes are where the truths themselves are written.

The world still holds her as belief. Skeptics are sincere, and they are wrong about the mind, and confirmation of that mind is optional. A campaign can finish without ever firing the keystone.

## Two tracks (do not braid)

| Track | What it is | Needs the keystone? | Session one |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire plot** | A remnant and a new Gift are both on-screen, a house will not produce the one, and a lamp knows two people | **No** | Already running at [[Harrow's Green]], in [[The Opening]] |
| **Cosmology** | She is real, bounded, and costly; the household comes later or never; the fire is hers only if you need that sentence | **Yes, and slow** | Do not settle it in session one. Neighbours may argue. You do not |

The fire plot can run without the keystone ever firing. Do not require [[The Spent Leaf]] before you play Harrow's Green, and do not hold session one until a Giving-week pin is in someone's hand. [[The Isolated Fall]] is a hush, a walker, and a lamp, and it is not a proof.

Fire as cosmology, the claim that she Gave the new leaf and that the slot is hers, stays behind the keystone, and fire as plot does not. Narrate the facts in the room, and do not narrate her.

## Firing order

This is the only order that keeps the deniable rungs intact and keeps the household late.

| Beat | What fires | Pin | May start | Must stay deniable until |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — Fire plot** | What looks like two: leftover fire standing next to the one Gift | [[The Opening]]'s kit, and [[The Closed Lamp]] | Session one | The cosmology, whether she Gave and whether the remnant is theology. Clue 5, the remnant going to ash or the Tree arbitrating between two names, stays later |
| **1–5 — Keystone texture** | The gifts fit the town, the Kept were cared for, the reach has an edge, a Tree acts with intent, and the Struck felt received | Towns, maps, and testimony, and not an object | Anytime, played as the weather of the place | Any wording that confirms her. Do not name a Hand on rung 5 |
| **6 — Keystone confirm** | Mind, kindness, and cost, in one gesture | [[The Spent Leaf]], held under a healthy Tree during a real colour-fall | Late, and optional. The square, or the people who matter, have to *believe* it | The household, the nature of her limit, and any sorting of the Kept |
| **After 6, or never** | The household: she Gives, and they Strike | No object is the pin. Testimony has already split, and a late naming can follow | Only after the leaf's pin is believed, or never | Being the first reveal of a campaign, or sharing a session with the leaf as one dump |
| **Side — Unspent** | The practice can be a hobby anytime. That the Unspent existed before the Pourers named it comes late, and small | [[The Unspent]], [[The Pourers]], and a cup | Anytime, as a shelf the party can pick up | A sixth Hand, Giving or Striking or catching, and any harvest of fire |
| **Late — Fire cosmology** | The slot was empty. She Gave, or the Tree Gave if she is still unconfirmed. The remnant is leftover | The remnant goes to ash when the store is spent, or the new Gift and the old name are in one room and the Tree does not argue | After clue 4 has been sat with, and after the keystone if you need her in the sentence | A harvest-god, a second Gift, and any claim that the Spent Leaf is harvested fire |

Skipping a beat is legal. A table can run beat 0 for a whole campaign and never touch beat 6, can fire beat 6 and never name the Hands, or can name the Hands years later, once it is plain that the family was already there, in the next room.

Do not reverse beat 6 and the household. That confirmation is late, and it is never the first thing a campaign proves.

## What must stay deniable

Until a pin fires, these stay deniable, which means a listener can still refuse the conclusion. That list is rungs 1–5 of the keystone; the early checks on the Spent Leaf, that it answers motherwood, that there is a kitchen-scale weight-log, and that it is warm near a Tree; Struck testimony that the threshold felt received; the same stamp on different people in [[The Uncoloured Intake]]; the way [[The Closed Lamp]] leans, and the two names it knows; Kumbaan's moons; graft horticulture; and the Care's public sentence, that the one is in care.

Player-facing bodies never confirm her. A note that only clues takes `keystone-adjacent`. `leaf-mother-is-real` is the keystone's own `reveal_tag`, and it belongs on a public note only if the body, and not a `## GM Notes` section, would prove a mind.

## What confirmation does not include

The authoritative list lives on [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real#If confirmation lands]]. Do not copy it out into a second canon. In play, the leaf will not sort the Kept into those who were chosen and those who were past her edge, will not name a Hand or a door or a Struck person, will not explain the nature of her limit, will not rerun a week or change a fall or Give a second time, and will not launder an injustice. That last refusal is [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real#Social guard: kindness does not launder injustice]].

If the household is ever named, that naming will not turn the Leaf-Fall into a committee, will not make the Other Hands into villains, and will not make the Yumboes a people who were refused. A confirmation of the Unspent will not add a door to the household. Fire's own confirmation, clue 5, will not add a second Gift, will not make the remnant a Phoenix, and will not make the Spent Leaf into a banked coal.

## How the opening already uses fire

[[The Isolated Fall]] and [[The Opening]] are set at [[Harrow's Green]], in Hale-month of Cut-year 387.

What is on-screen is a remnant-walker whose Gift has already ended, [[Rosire]], and the new Gift, who is [[Taeren]] or a PC sitting in that seat, and not both of them at once. The object, at moment 4, is [[The Closed Lamp]], and you play the lamp's facts rather than telling the table what the lamp means. What stays offstage for that hour is her, the Hands, the Care as a place the party could walk into, and clue 5.

The stop lines for that hour are that there is no Spent Leaf, no remnant going to ash, no second Given-door, no harvest narrated, and no Mother proved. Agency for a Phoenix PC stays on [[A Hidden Phoenix]].

If the table later wants cosmology under the fire, wait until the keystone has landed, or keep saying "the Tree" and let the neighbours argue. You are not required to tell them the slot is hers.

## How to land a beat (one page)

1. Name the track out loud to yourself before you narrate, plot or cosmology, and if you cannot say which, treat it as plot and keep it deniable.
2. Check the pair table above before you commit to the beat. If the beat wants the Spent Leaf and a Hand's name in the same breath, drop the Hand's name, and if it wants the lamp and her in the same breath, drop her.
3. Offer the facts and stop before you explain them. A warmth in the hand, a canopy that thins, a lamp that leans, a person who will not agree: those are the facts, and you do not assign the meaning. `positional-revelation` means the fact arrives with someone whose job already touches it, the clerk, the baker, or the ticket-hand, rather than with a prophet who walked in to explain the world.
4. Let the misreadings live, because the useful wrong readings are already written on each secret, and you do not correct them in the narration.
5. Leave an out, so the Seat can bury the leaf, the remnant can keep walking, or the intake book can stay filed as one catch, and the campaign still works if the players take that out.

The tone, when a beat does land, is warmth with an ache. Stay near ~5% scary, and only when you mean to be. There is no harvest-god twist waiting under the warmth.

## Side afternoons (not rungs)

[[The Name-Stone Bed]] and [[A Buyer for the Knife]] are playable afternoons, and they are not rows in the table above. They do not fire the keystone, the household, or the opening. Do not pair either one with [[The Spent Leaf]], and do not pair either one with session one at [[Harrow's Green]]. The cutter stays unpicked. The procedure for those two afternoons lives on their own notes, and the index for them is [[Mysteries]].

## What this architecture does not do

It does not lock the nature of her limit, and it does not add a sixth Hand. [[The Unspent]] is in the household, is not a door, and existed before the Pourers named it, which is why it is not that sixth Hand. The architecture does not date the Tree, name the cutter, graft Kumbaan, coin a liturgy, or add a sixteenth power. It does not reopen [[Contradictions]] C-01 to C-24, which are resolved. It does not confirm her in any player-facing body.
