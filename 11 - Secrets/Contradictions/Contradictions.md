# Contradictions

> **What this is.** The pass-two log, where canon conflicts are written down, along with the residuals the completion gate would not silently close and the quality gaps that `story-sense` routes to a fix. Resolve a conflict here, or in the note that already owns it, and do not start a parallel bible to hold it. The philosophy comes from `shared-world`. The folders stay this vault's.

**Opened:** 2026-08-31, at the [[Epic R Completion Gate 2026-08-31|Epic R gate]]. Pass one is complete, P2.1 is done, and the world book was rebuilt because the user asked, which is C-02. Leftovers 1, 3, and 9 were closed on 2026-08-31. Do not resume the old Epic 8 plan.

**Diagnosis (`story-sense` / `worldbuilding`).** The setting is built, and what is left is hygiene and consistency, not a missing engine. Do not generate new powers, new faiths, or new liturgical names to fill a gap that is only a mismatched sentence. Hub collisions found while writing [[The Opening]] were logged and fixed here, and that passing sweep is the one this log means.
