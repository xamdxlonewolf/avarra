# The Other Hands

> **The household elaboration (2026-08-23 — structure locked; names open).** This sits under the locked keystone, and it does not replace it. [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real|She is real, benevolent, bounded, and costly]]. The door that keystone left open is the one this note answers: she is not the only such power. She is the first and greatest of a household, she Gives at a Turning Tree at the Leaf-Fall, and she does not Strike. The Struck, the people whose Condition arrived later, at a threshold, are the work of the Other Hands, lesser gods of her household, each with one restricted door and her allowance to reach through it.

> **Do not put this in a player-facing note.** The world still holds her as belief, and the other faiths already describe these powers as they themselves understand them. Confirmation is a late beat. When it lands, it should land as the household having always been there, not as a gotcha in which a pantheon is dumped on the table.

## The Secret (GM truth)

**The Leaf-Mother Gives and does not Strike, and the Struck are still hers, because the hands that catch at those doors are of her household and work under her allowance.**

That is how this note sits on the locked line from the keystone, that the Struck are still hers at the farthest reach of her arm. She does not walk those doors herself, she authorizes them, and the catch is real even when the hand is not hers. Narrow-Hand, the reading that she only Gives at the Tree, and Wide-Hand, the reading that the Struck are still hers, are each holding a true end of the same question.

A Condition is the becoming itself. Given means she gave it at the Leaf-Fall, and Struck means an Other Hand caught the person at that Hand's door. She cannot, or will not, Give a death or a contract to a ten-year-old, because those doors do not open at the Leaf-Fall, and that is why [[Returned]] and [[Bound]] are Struck-only. The sort rule in [[The Premise]] still holds, and this note is the cosmology underneath it.

### The division of labour

| Path | Who | Where | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Given** | the Leaf-Mother alone | a healthy [[Turning Tree]] at Leaf-Fall | She looks at the child, chooses, and Gives. Long-Lived, Two-Bodied, Answered, and Phoenix are hers only, and no Other Hand may grant them |
| **Struck** | one Other Hand, by her allowance | that Hand's own door (death, oath, drowning, stone, wild) | That Hand may grant only their own Condition, only at that door, and only within the allowance she has given |
| **Both** | Mother *or* the relevant Hand | Tree *or* door | Far-Voiced, the Stilled, and the Taken-In can be Given at ten, at that Leaf-Fall, or Struck later: the Condition is the same, and the hand and the person at the table are not |

### The household (names 🟡)

This is not a D&D twelve. There are five Hands, five doors, and five Conditions that can arrive without a leaf. Each Hand is restricted to one door, one grant, and her allowance. They are not a dark court, and they are not a rival pantheon set against her. They are family labour, the work she cannot do at the Tree without breaking what a gift is.

| Door | Condition | Everyday name | Liturgical (Maiethren) | What they want (positive) | The fresh angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| died with work unfinished | [[Returned]] | **the Quiet Tally** | *Threnvaeth* (THREN-vayth) — "the counted waiting" | **The last task done well.** Unfinished work finished, the book closed honestly. | Not Hades, but a clerk of leftover purpose, who will not close the book while work remains, warm and solemn in the way the Returned themselves are. |
| a contract sworn in extremity | [[Bound]] (and [[The Unbound]] when the term ends) | **the Counterparty** | *Orledd* (or-LED) — "the witnessed reckoning" | **An extremity that can still be answered, and the answer stay readable.** Fewer kept bargains, not more unread ones. | Not a devil, but the one who answers a sworn ask and keeps the term. The face the Bound chose is a mask that person gave, and Orledd wears it. |
| survived drowning | [[Far-Voiced]] (Struck path) | **the Second Breath** | *Eloren* (ay-LOR-en) — "the deep-witness" | **Voices that would have stopped, used.** Song, warning, comfort — breath spent, not stored. | Not Poseidon, but the deep that returns a voice. The Far-Voiced who was Given is hers, and the drowned one is Eloren's. |
| survived being turned to stone | [[The Stilled]] (Struck path) | **the Holding** | *Ornhael* (orn-HAYL) — "the whole-ground" | **A pause someone walks out of.** Stillness that saves, then lets go. | Not a curse-god, but the ground that holds a person still and then lets them walk out. |
| lost and dying in the deep wild | [[The Taken-In]] (Struck path) | **the Taking** | *Vaelorn* (VAYL-orn) — "the yield-land" | **That no one die unmade in the wild.** The lost taken in, not eaten. | Not a hungry forest, but the land that will not let you die unmade, *vael* in the Old Ways' older sense. |

**Name collisions.** *Eloren* (ay-LOR-en) sits one letter from *Eoloren*, the liturgical name of the First Cut. *Ornhael* (orn-HAYL) sits close to *Ornthael*, the liturgical name of the Years of Hands. Both stay as they are, and nothing new is coined to separate them. Say them with the stress given here, so a table does not hear one name and answer with the other.

The collective everyday name is **the Other Hands**. She is the First Hand, and they are the rest. They do not have temples of their own on the mainland as a set, because the Motherfaith grew over that memory, and the rival faiths kept pieces of it.

**Not a sixth Hand.** [[The Unspent]], whose everyday name is *the Poured God*, is in the household, and it is not a door. It existed before the Pourers named it. The Long-Lived fringe will dress it up and claim they made it. It cannot Give, it cannot Strike, and it cannot catch a person at a threshold. Do not add it to the table above, and do not let a circle pour someone into being Long-Lived.

### Allowance and purchase

Allowance is hers to give. An Other Hand cannot Strike a Condition outside their own door, cannot Give at a Tree, and cannot exceed what she has permitted. That restriction is what was asked for, and it is also why the Struck feel received and still unmet by ceremony: a lesser hand caught them, at the edge, by permission.

Purchase is not clerical magic, and it is not a matter of praying for a domain spell. An Other Hand reaches more easily where their door is kept: funerals and unfinished work for Threnvaeth, oaths and books for Orledd, drowning-water for Eloren, stone and stillness for Ornhael, wild and grove for Vaelorn. On the mainland those doors exist even for skeptics, because the world is full of them. Belief helps, and geography and practice matter more.

Her Giving does not need worship. Skeptics still Turn, which means their children still stand the Leaf-Fall and still receive what the Tree gives. The Trees are her hands, and they are not her collection plate. Do not make the Leaf-Fall something a person has to believe in before it works. That would break the locked engine, and it would break the ~5% tone.

### What Orledd receives (the Bound bargain)

A Bound PC named a want in extremity, and something answered. If the table never learns what the Counterparty receives, they will play Orledd as a devil. Do not let that happen.

**During the term, Orledd receives two things.**

1. **The ask remaining true.** The Granted Power works because the want was real and was named. Specificity is what feeds the bargain. A vague "save me" that was never turned into a clause is not a bargain they can hold.
2. **The Terms being kept in public.** Each kept Term is a witnessed reckoning, and that public keeping is how a Counterparty exists in a world whose ordinary hand Gives. Orledd cannot Give. A gift with no named return would be her work, so Orledd has to receive a readable exchange, or the household's labour at this door collapses.

**At the end of a term-limited contract, Orledd receives the Pledge**, which is already locked on [[Bound]]. They have been holding that Pledge through the term, not taking it as they go, and the collection at the end is the release of what they held, not a surprise theft.

**Orledd does not receive** a soul, worship, a second signing, or the Bound's personhood. A flood of desperate unread bargains starves them. The Given-Over spiral is the Bound's fear of paying, and it is not Orledd's appetite. Play a Counterparty who would rather have one kept Term than ten whispered extras.

The face chosen at creation, whether demon, vast, or unknown, is still a mask. What Orledd receives does not change with the mask. The Fair Hand is right that a Counterparty exists, and wrong that every becoming is this kind of bargain.

### What strains or breaks her allowance

Allowance is not a mood. It is a permission with a failure mode, so use strain often and quietly, and use a break almost never.

**Strain** means the door still works, and the catch feels thin, late, or off, so that Narrow-Hand testimony gets louder.

| Hand | A strain looks like |
|---|---|
| Threnvaeth | A [[Returned]] is kept from their Purpose and used as cheap deathless labour, so the book cannot close |
| Orledd | Terms are written to eat toward the Given-Over, or the ask was never extremity and was only appetite |
| Eloren | A Struck voice is silenced, or used only as a weapon, or a drowning is arranged so that a voice will come back |
| Ornhael | Someone is held as a tool and not allowed to walk out, or the Grey is treated as a prison |
| Vaelorn | The lost are warehoused as urban green-poverty, or land is used as a dump for people a city does not want |
| Any | Reaching on [[The Sundering Isle\|Kumbaan]], where the doors are barely a concept, or reaching in a year she has already spent |

At the table, strain looks like a threshold that should have caught and did not, so that the drowning just drowned; or a Strike that felt like a grab rather than a catch; or a Bound Whisper that lies more often, because the Terms are being kept as theatre.

**A break** is a household rupture. It is rare, it is campaign-grade, and it is not a war between gods. A Hand breaks allowance by doing any of these:

- Granting a Given-only Condition, which means Long-Lived, Two-Bodied, Answered, or Phoenix.
- Giving at a Tree, or during the Leaf-Fall.
- Striking where there is no true door: no unfinished death, no sworn ask, no drowning, no stone, and no deep wild.
- Taking another Hand's door.
- Answering an ask that is a Gift in disguise, such as "make my child Long-Lived" or "send a leaf."
- Seizing a will. The Inviolate Will is metaphysical bedrock, and a Hand that tried to seize one would be outside the household, not a scarier member of it.
- Persisting after she has refused a particular catch, or after she has narrowed a door for a year.

What a break looks like at the table is a door that sticks, so that the next three drownings stay dead; or a bargain that answered with a Gift-shaped clause, after which the next Leaf-Fall in that town went argumentative; or a Hand that keeps reaching, so the Struck who come through do not feel received. Keep it ~5% scary. This is a family argument, not a dark court declaring war.

She can narrow a door, which means fewer Struck that season, or refuse one catch, and neither of those is a break. That is her allowance still working. The Hands are not owed a quota.

### What this does to the schism (upgrade, not flatten)

Narrow-Hand is right that she Gives only at the Tree, and that the Struck were received by some other means. Wide-Hand is right that those means are still hers: her household, her allowance, and her catch at the edge. Neither reading holds the whole, which is the same design as the keystone, and it is fuel rather than a puzzle someone can finish.

The Watching remembered a household from before that household was named Mother, and that memory is half-right. The Fair Hand is right that a Counterparty exists for the Bound, and wrong that every becoming is a bargain, because the Mother's Gifts are still gifts. The Old Ways are right that the land-doors are real, Vaelorn and Ornhael and Eloren among them, and wrong that she is only a latecomer claiming credit, because she authorized those guests. The Open Table is not of this household, so do not absorb Kumbaan's staying-kin into Threnvaeth or into anyone else, because their table is their own.

## Kumbaan (the Yumboe lock)

The user's call is now locked in this note, and it is pointed at from [[Yumboe]] and from [[The Sundering Isle]].

Kumbaan has no Tree because a graft has not crossed the storm-wall, and not because the good people of the isle refused one. The isle is almost impossible to reach. The culture there is centred on something else, on [[The Open Table]], the moon, the hill, and hospitality, which are different ideals and a different sacred.

There are no Gifts on the isle. Her reach does not get there, there is no Leaf-Fall, and Yumboes do not have Given Conditions as a people. They do not worship her or her household, so the Other Hands have little purchase. The doors are not kept in their names, and the rites are not theirs. This is not a rule that gods starve without prayer, the way a video game drains a mana bar. A Hand reaches through a door that is used, and on Kumbaan those doors are barely a concept.

Struck becomings can still happen, at far smaller percentages than the mainland's ~15% lifetime Struck. A Yumboe who dies with work unfinished, or who drowns, or who is taken by the deep green of the Paps, can be caught, and the catching is rare. It is enough for a story, and it is not enough to make a social class. There are no Returned neighbourhoods on the isle, and no Bound guilds.

A Yumboe abroad, standing under a mainland Tree, is a live campaign beat, and it is not a population fact. The Tree is her hand, so she could Give, and it almost never arises. Do not write a racial immunity to cover that rarity.

The storm-wall is not a ward against the household, and that was settled on 2026-10-01. It is weather, current, and reef, a dial, as [[The Sundering Isle]] already has it. Do not set a larger power in the water to keep her, or the Hands, from interfering. That choice would pick the nature of her limit, which is still open on [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]], and it would turn a crossing into a seal. Unreached stays the word.

The isle remains the sharpest experiment on the map: a people outside her Giving, barely inside her household's reach, with a faith that was never hers.

## How It Surfaces (clues, in order)

Keep this ladder behind the keystone's own clues. Do not lead a campaign with a pantheon.

1. **Struck testimony splits.** Some people felt a mother. Some felt a clerk, a term, a deep, a holding, or a green. The Motherfaith files all of that testimony as "she caught them." The Old Ways and the Fair Hand file it as their own doors. Both filings are half-true. The handout is [[The Uncoloured Intake]], one stamp, and Struck people who will not sit under it. Do not complete the roster from that page.
2. **A Returned who settles** sometimes speaks of a book closing, and not of a lap. If you are listening, that is Threnvaeth.
3. **Bound faces disagree, and the terms do not.** Demon, vast, or unknown, the clause has the same shape, because Orledd is wearing masks.
4. **The Watching's saying,** "we kept the watch before the name," is about a household, and not only about a nameless Tree.
5. **Kumbaan works as a control.** There is almost no Struck life there, and no Given. If the household needed only doors, and not doors that people keep, the isle would be full of Struck people. Purchase matters. The storm-wall is a reach-edge and a name-edge together.
6. **The confirming beat is late, and it is the GM's to place.** If the campaign ever names the Other Hands, the fact in the room is that the family was already there, and it should feel like warmth with an ache, not like a lore dump. Pair it with the keystone's own confirming beat, or let it land after that beat. Never fire it first.

## Player Misreadings

**"She's just one god of many, so the gifts are politics."** Reject that. She is first, Giving is still love, and the Tithe is still the honest weight of a gift. A household does not turn the Leaf-Fall into a committee.

**"The Other Hands are a dark pantheon, the real villains."** Reject that. The tone stays near ~5% scary. Each Hand has a positive want: finished work, a readable answer, a voice that gets used, a pause someone walks out of, the lost taken in. Threnvaeth is why the Returned are respected, and not why they are a plague. A broken allowance is a stuck door, not a coup.

**"Narrow-Hand wins, and she never cared about the Struck."** That reading is incomplete. She authorized the catch. The stigma that follows a Struck person is social, and it is not a cosmological abandonment.

**"Yumboes are immune, or they were rejected."** They are unreached, and they were not refused. A graft that cannot travel is not a judgment on them.
