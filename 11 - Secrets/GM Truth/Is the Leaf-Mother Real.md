# Is the Leaf-Mother Real

> **The keystone GM secret (LOCKED 2026-08-19; edge added 2026-08-20).** This is the canon-layer answer, what is actually true, regardless of what anyone in the world believes. Epic 1, the Turning Tree and the Leaf-Mother religion, and the setting's theme all aim here. It is decided: the one-line truth below is load-bearing, the texture grows around it, and you do not re-litigate it.

## The Secret (GM truth)

**Yes. The Leaf-Mother is real, and she is benevolent — but her reach is not infinite, and giving costs her.**

She exists as a genuine, present power, not a metaphor and not a dead god whose name survived her, and the Turning Trees are genuinely her hands. The town scions, the Trees a town actually stands under, are not a natural phenomenon that people only read as divine. They really are how she reaches into the world, and when a Tree chooses, she is choosing. The Conditions are real gifts: at the Leaf-Fall she looks at a child and gives them a becoming the world will need and that they can carry, and that becoming is the Condition. The Tithe is the honest weight of the gift, not a hidden price and not a trap.

**She is bounded, not boundless (the constraint — locked 2026-08-20).** She is kind *and* finite. Her reach has an edge, and every gift she gives costs her something to give. She cannot lay a hand on every child, cannot reach every threshold in time, and cannot give without spending. This is not a flaw hidden under the warmth — it is *why* the warmth aches. A mother who could save everyone and didn't would be sinister; a mother who saves whom she can reach, at cost, and grieves the rest, is the one this world actually has. **The limit is real. She is doing her best against it.**

**The Kept are spared — and some are simply past her edge.** A hug and no colour is, for most, a deliberate and kind choice: the gift of being left whole, wanted in its own right, not a failure or an oversight. But the constraint means the Kept are *not all one thing* — some are chosen for wholeness, and some are children she could not reach far enough to give to this turning, held safe rather than gifted. She does not tell them apart for us, and neither can they. Both are held; not both are chosen. (This is the schism's sharpest question given a true, aching answer — see [[The Leaf-Mother]].) **A later week is not offered to sort them.** Player-facing procedure is settled on [[Turning Tree#Questions a warden gets asked]]: the Given-door is one week; a completed standing is once; a miss is not a retry. That honors the locked engine and keeps "this turning" an ache instead of a second appointment.

**The Struck are still hers — at the farthest reach of her arm.** A becoming that arrives through a later threshold (a drowning, an oath, a death survived) is not a Condition that "escaped her." She meets people at those doors too — but the threshold is the *edge* of her reach, not her ordinary hand. There she *catches* rather than gives: it is chancier, costlier, and not always in time. This is why the Struck feel "received" yet arrive without ceremony, and why they can feel a half-step outside the fold — they were reached for at the limit, not gifted at the centre. ***(Elaborated 2026-08-23 — see [[The Other Hands]].)*** She **Gives** at the Tree; she does **not** Strike. The catch at the door is enacted by **lesser gods of her household**, each restricted to one door, working under her allowance. The Struck are still hers because those hands are *hers to send*. Narrow-Hand, the reading that she only Gives at the Tree, and Wide-Hand, the reading that the Struck are still hers, are both half-right.

So: **the believers are right about the big things** — she is real, she is kind, the gifts are gifts. And the skeptics, though wrong that *no one is there*, are **not entirely wrong**: they have correctly felt the *edge* — the Kept who seem passed over, the Struck who arrive unmet by ceremony, the sense that the giving is not seamless. There **is** a seam; they have simply mislabelled a *limit* as an *absence*. No one in the world holds the whole shape: believers see the hand and miss the edge; skeptics see the edge and miss the hand. The faith is, underneath everything, **true — and incomplete in exactly the way its own doubters sense.**

> **Why this answer (design intent).** The user chose the warm reading on purpose, then (2026-08-20) added an *edge*: real and benevolent, but **bounded and costly**. This keeps the ~5%-scary tone — the floor is still *sound*, there is no harvest, no betrayal waiting to be uncovered — while giving the kindness an ache and the mystery a true seam. It rotates the secret off the most *available* "the goddess is simply real and kind" reading (which risked being generic) without ever reopening *is she kind* (locked): she is. She is just not omnipotent, and love that runs up against a limit is more moving, and more usable at the table, than love that can do anything. It also upgrades the schism from *poignant-but-lopsided* (believers right, skeptics merely wrong) to *genuinely two-sided*: both camps have hold of something real, neither has the whole, and that is inexhaustible fuel.

## What is deliberately still open (belief, not cosmology)

Locking "she is real and kind" does **not** flatten the mystery. The world keeps her as **belief, not confirmed cosmology** *in-fiction* — no one can *prove* her, revelation is quiet, and honest skeptics remain honest. These stay open on purpose, as live design space for later epics.

**What she is** stays undecided. A goddess, the Trees' shared soul, or something older wearing a mother's face for our comfort, are all still possible, and the note does not pick among them. The maternal, benevolent intent is what is locked, not her metaphysical species.

**What she wants,** past the giving of good gifts, is undecided. She is kind, and any larger purpose she might have is unwritten.

**Where she is, and whether anyone can reach her,** is undecided. Revelation is deliberately quiet, and there is no throne to visit.

**The nature of her limit** is locked in the fact that it exists, and open in the question of what it is. That her reach has an edge, and that giving costs her, is canon in the constraint above. Why — whether she is finite by nature, spent from some old wound, rationing a power that could run out, or holding something back in order to hold something else up — is deliberately unwritten, and it stays live design space. The history notes show the edge moving with wood, and they do not pick a cause. Lock the ache, not its cause.

**Whether she is the only such power** was closed on 2026-08-23 (structure 🔒; names 🟡). She is not exclusive. She is the first and greatest of a household: she Gives, and the Other Hands Strike by her allowance, each of them at one door. The full roster, and the Kumbaan lock, live on [[The Other Hands]] (`reveal_tag: the-other-hands`). Player-facing notes still do not confirm the household. The nature of her limit, why she is bounded and why Giving costs, remains open. The history notes show the edge moving with wood, and they do not pick a cause.

## How It Surfaces (clues, in order)

Because she is quiet and genuinely kind, revelation comes as a slow accretion of coherence, and then as one object that can close the question. Rungs 1–5 stay deniable on purpose, so a skeptic can still walk away from them. Rung 6 is no longer a beat you are told to reserve. It has a pin.

1. **The gifts fit too well.** Over a lifetime, a town notices that the Conditions Given tend to be the ones that town will need, as if something is looking ahead, and the pattern is easy to dismiss as hindsight and survivorship bias, which is what the skeptics do.
2. **The Kept are cared for, not merely skipped.** Many Kept report that the ceremony felt like a choice made for them, not like an absence, but not all of them report that, and the difference nags. The testimony is hard to falsify and easy to call sentiment. Under the constraint, both readings are true of different Kept — some chosen, some past her edge — which is why the testimony never resolves cleanly.
3. **The reach has an edge.** This is the quiet, best evidence of the constraint. Remote hamlets far from any mature Tree run higher on the Kept and higher on the Struck; a Tree strains and a child goes ungifted where a central town's Tree would not; the giving visibly thins where the Trees are thin. It reads, mundanely, as distance and biology, which is the skeptics' whole case, and it is in fact the shape of a bounded hand, not of an absent one. The old scholars know it was once far starker: before the Awakening Tree's grafts spread, only those who could make the pilgrimage were reached, and the far-flung stayed UnTurned, unreached, for want of the trip — see [[The Ages of the Turning]]. That the reach has grown with the grafts is itself the quiet argument that a hand, and not a climate, is behind it.
4. **A Tree does something a phenomenon should not.** Now and then, rarely and quietly, a scion acts with apparent intent. It withholds a colour that would have doomed a child, or it reaches, and sometimes visibly strains, for a specific person. The moment is never flashy, and it is always deniable.
5. **The Struck are met, barely.** Testimony from the Struck says the threshold felt received, as if someone reached for them from the far side of the door, but late, or thin, or at the very limit of arriving at all. It is the strongest experiential evidence, and the clearest felt sense of the edge, and it is still only testimony. Do not let this rung name [[The Other Hands]].
6. **The confirming beat is [[The Spent Leaf]].** A colourless remainder of a Giving, kept by [[The Remainder]] in the First Seat's side-yard, can be checked early, and those early checks stay as deniable as rungs 1–5: it answers motherwood, and the yard-log says it is lighter after a Giving week. The pin itself is to hold the leaf under a healthy Tree during a real colour-fall. The holder feels warmth, and a grief that is not theirs; the canopy thins for a breath and then recovers; the next child is not shorted. That gesture is mind, kindness, and cost together. Land it as warmth and relief with an ache under it, not as horror. The setting still works if a table never fires this, and it must remain possible to fire.

**What confirmation does not include.** The leaf will not sort the Kept into chosen and past-her-edge, it will not name a Hand or a door, it will not explain the nature of her limit, and it will not rerun a week or change a fall. The locked engine stays locked.

## If confirmation lands

Use this when the square, or the people who matter to the party, believe the pin. One clerk's private feeling is not that moment.

**Orthopraxy cracks.** Right practice was the peace. A proved mind makes the skeptics choose whether to convert, leave the festival, or become a named dissent, and Threnmaieth, which keeps the roll, already has a Grown-Over column and will want a Denied one.

**The scion-queue becomes a moral sentence.** If a bounded hand is real, then waiting under a Tree is the rationing of a person, not a run of weather. Road-hands get more leave and more blame. The hamlets past the ford do not get their twenty years back.

**Vaethorn.** The guilt-theology there gets its worst fear confirmed, because a thin reach is real. Some hearths stop bringing children rather than risk a hug they now read as a miss, the UnTurned spike, and you do not treat that spike as her will.

**Lestrand** sells viewings, copies, and "cost-insurance." The leaf does not become a product if the Seat keeps the box. The story of the leaf does.

**Threnmaieth** tries to write her into the roll as a counted fact. Speakers who refuse become a problem for them, and the college can still refuse the roll. Keep that refusal available.

**The Watching is not promoted.** They will say *we told you there was a cost*, and they still will not say Mother. The warmth lands through them, and not as them.

**The Kept** include people who will want the leaf to tell them which hug they were. It will not. The want is the wound, and she still does not tell them apart.

**The social guard below still holds.** Confirmation changes what people believe, and it does not change the justice of the queue, the Grown-Over column, the rope, or the week that will not end.

**The household stays behind the pin.** Do not let rung 6 dump [[The Other Hands]] into the same conversation. Pair that confirmation later, or never, and do not let it be first.

**The nature of the limit stays open.** They know that she spends, and they do not know why.

If the Seat buries the leaf instead, the square goes on as before. Rungs 1–5 still work, a remainder-clerk walks, and the yard-log still exists. That walk is how a party gets involved without a prophecy.

## Player Misreadings (what they'll think first)

**"It's just what the Trees do."** This is the skeptic's reading, biology and phenomenon with no mind behind it. It is sincere, it is common, and it is wrong about the mind, and the people who hold it have genuinely seen the edge, the thinning at the margins and the unmet Struck, and mislabelled a limit as an absence. It is safe to let them hold it. It is half-right, which is what makes it durable.

**"If she's real and kind, why the Tithes, why the Struck, why anyone Kept?"** This question is the schism's engine. The answers are true, and they ache: a gift has honest weight, she reaches the thresholds only at the edge of her arm, and some Kept are chosen for wholeness while some are simply past her reach. The answers are not provable, so the questions stay live.

**"A kind god would save everyone."** The constraint invites this misreading, the claim that a bounded mother is not really benevolent. In the world it is a real theological wound, the schism again, and at the design layer it is the point. She is kind and finite, and the gap between those two facts is where the grief lives. Do not let players solve the gap into omnipotence, and do not let them solve it into indifference.

**The dark reading** says a benevolent front is hiding a harvest or a betrayal. This setting rejects that reading, as the design intent above says. A GM who wants horror can house-rule it, and it is off-canon here. The locked answer exists so the floor stays sound: bounded, costly, and grieving, and still sound.

**"The Spent Leaf is harvested fire, a second Phoenix, or proof she is dying."** Reject that reading. The leaf is a remainder of a Giving, not the matter of [[When the Fire Is Caught]]. She spends, and she is not being farmed. Do not braid the pin to the harvesters.

**"The Closed Lamp is a leaf, an uncounted fall, or the same remainder."** Reject that reading. The lamp is metal and a banked coal, a different pin on a different track.

## Consequences for build (what this constrains downstream)

**The Turning Tree and Leaf-Mother faith notes** ([[Turning Tree]], [[The Leaf-Mother]]) are player-facing, and they have to stay reverent and unproven. Present the doctrine and the skepticism even-handedly, and never wink that she is confirmed. A player body that only clues her carries `reveals: [keystone-adjacent]`. The tag `leaf-mother-is-real` is this note's `reveal_tag`, and you use it on a public note only if the body, not a `## GM Notes` section, would confirm her. Household exposure is a separate tag, `the-other-hands`.

**The Tithes** are honest costs, and they are not divine leverage. That reinforces the rule already on [[The Premise]], that a Tithe never seizes the PC.

**The schism** stays a good-faith disagreement, and the constraint makes it two-sided. Believers hold the hand, skeptics hold the edge, and neither holds the whole. That is better long-term fuel: no faction is secretly correct and evil, and no faction is simply wrong.

**The reach-edge feeds the geography and the frontier.** The constraint predicts a real pattern for [[The World Frame]] and the settlement notes, that the giving thins at the margins, so remote and Tree-poor places run higher on the Kept and the Struck. Build with that pattern, and keep it consistent when you place the frontier. It is not a retcon waiting to happen.

### Social guard: kindness does not launder injustice

**Her benevolence is cosmological, not social.** The floor being sound at the *divine* layer does **not** make the *social* layer kind. People build genuinely unjust things *on top of* true gifts that she never authored and her kindness does not excuse: Struck stigma and legal suspicion, Tithe-infrastructure that becomes economic leverage over the Conditioned, guild conscription of the Given, communities that treat their Kept as spare labour or their feared Conditions as things to be managed. These are not misreadings that confirmation dissolves. The keystone answers *is anyone there and do they mean well* (yes); it does not answer *is the society people built around her just* (often no). This is the one authoritative statement of the social guard; other notes should show their particular injustice rather than repeat the formula.
