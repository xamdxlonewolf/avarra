# 11 - Secrets

**GM ONLY.** This folder is the revelation architecture, the shelf you open when a secret is about to matter at the table. It holds the mysteries a session can play, the revelations that are actually true, the clues that point toward them, the contradictions the log had to settle, and the GM truth the players are not handed. Each secret carries a `reveal_tag`. A player-facing note lists, in its `reveals:` front-matter, which of those tags it would expose if someone read past the wall.

The Leaf-Mother is the figure the keystone is about, and the tags below are how a note admits it would give some part of that truth, or of the household, or of the fire, away. When you need the firing order, what has to stay deniable, what a confirmation still does not include, and how to run the fire plot without proving the keystone, open [[Revelation Architecture]]. When you need a tag, the secret it names, and the notes keyed to it, open [[Reveal Index]].

[[Mysteries]], [[Revelations]], and [[Clues]] all point at those same two notes. Those folders do not hold a second copy, and a note stays in the folder that was already its home.

## Tag vocabulary

| Tag | Use it when | Do not use it when |
|---|---|---|
| `leaf-mother-is-real` | This note's own **`reveal_tag`** applies, or a player-facing body would confirm that she is real | The note is doctrine, Tree-talk, a reach-pattern, or a confirmation that lives only behind `## GM Notes` |
| `keystone-adjacent` | The note clues the keystone without confirming it, as graft horticulture, schism texts, the edge-on-the-map, and the Spent Leaf read as a yard-scrap all do | The body states that she is real |
| `the-other-hands` | GM Notes, or the body, expose the household: she Gives, they Strike, the Hands are named, or the Kumbaan purchase is on the page | A faith note only argues Struck against Given, and does it inside player-facing theology |
| `the-unspent` | The note exposes the lesser household presence, the Poured God | The note is only the Five Hands table |
| `when-the-fire-is-caught` | The note exposes the Phoenix slot: one Gift, and a remnant that is not a Phoenix | The note is about the keystone |

The notes that carry clues are listed on each secret's `foreshadowed_by`. The exact rule for stripping a note before a player sees it is [[Conventions#Player-safe export]].

## Contents

### Procedure
- [[Revelation Architecture]] is the GM hub. It keeps the two tracks, the fire plot and the cosmology, the firing order, and the retrieval table that runs from a secret to its pin and then to what you must not pair with that pin.
- [[Reveal Index]] has every `reveal_tag` keyed. [[Bound]] and [[Returned]] stay on `reveals: []`, and the Phoenix hub stays untagged.
- [[Mysteries]] points back at this page. It holds two playable afternoons that are not the keystone and not the opening.
- [[Revelations]] points back at this page. The truths themselves stay in GM Truth.
- [[Clues]] points back at this page. The clue objects stay in the folders that already own them.

### GM Truth
- [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]] is the **keystone secret**, tagged `reveal_tag: leaf-mother-is-real`. She **is** real and benevolent, the Trees genuinely are her hands, the gifts are real, and she is bounded, with the giving costing her. Skeptics are sincere, and they are wrong about the mind. The firing pin is [[The Spent Leaf]], held during a Giving, and the desk that keeps the box is [[The Remainder]]. Rungs 1–5 stay deniable, while rung 6 can fire, and the household stays behind that confirmation. Notes tagged `keystone-adjacent` expose the clue, not the confirmation.
- [[The Other Hands]] is the **household elaboration**, tagged `reveal_tag: the-other-hands`. She is the first of a real pantheon. She **Gives** only, and lesser gods Strike at restricted doors under her allowance. Each Hand has a positive want, and the note states what Orledd receives and how an allowance strains or breaks. On Kumbaan there are no Gifts, and a Struck becoming is rare. Do not confirm this in a player-facing note. It is never the first reveal, and it is never fired with the Spent Leaf pin. The handout is [[The Uncoloured Intake]].
- [[The Unspent]] lives in `07 - Religion/Deities`, and it is the **lesser household presence**, tagged `reveal_tag: the-unspent`. It stands outside the Five Hands. It cannot Give or Strike, and it accepts the mask the Long-Lived fringe invented for it, *the Poured God* and *the Cup We Made*. The congregation that keeps that mask is [[The Pourers]].
- [[When the Fire Is Caught]] is the **Phoenix slot**, tagged `reveal_tag: when-the-fire-is-caught`. There is one Gift: the original self ends at the death they cannot Rise from, harvested fire can keep a remnant walking after that death, and a new white-fire leaf may fall where no one sees it. The remnant is not a Phoenix. The harvesters are [[The Protectors]], and the hidden player character is [[A Hidden Phoenix]]. The plot pin is [[The Opening]] together with [[The Closed Lamp]]. The fire plot runs without the keystone. The cosmology, that she Gave the leaf, stays behind the keystone.

### Clue objects (player notes; confirmation stays in GM walls)
- [[The Spent Leaf]] is the colourless remainder of a Giving, and it is the keystone pin.
- [[The Remainder]] is the desk in the First Seat's side-yard, the one that keeps the box.
- [[The Closed Lamp]] is travelling-coal that answers two people. It is an object for the fire plot, and it is not a leaf.
- [[The Uncoloured Intake]] is a set of gift-hall slips. They carry one stamp, and they record people who will not agree, which is the household clue.
- Six foreshadowing texts sit upstream of the pin. [[The Leaf-Mother]] holds two of them, the Tender's blessing and the Kept-child's saying, and the other four are [[The Child at Four Doors]], [[The Child Who Counted Stones]], [[The Branch That Came Away]], and [[The Child Who Climbed the Stone]].

### Contradictions (pass-two log)

### Campaign (fires clues; does not rewrite the engine)
