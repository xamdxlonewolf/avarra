# The Name-Stone Bed

The afternoon is at [[The Low Wall]], in the bed under the blank name-stone. It is not the keystone, and it is not [[The Opening]].

## What the table can play

Someone wants that bed opened again, and the people already standing there do not want the same thing from the day. Delamem, for [[The Walled Book]], will pay for a chip that can be filed as the Close's quarry, while a Watcher will stand in the cut and keep a chisel off the panel. Nurreith wants the labour back on the guest-beds and wants the day not called a waste, and Thuda wants the person-gap empty by dark, with the garden's evening left alone.

The party can be hired by any of those people. They can open the bed, refuse the work, or stand where the Watcher stands and keep the chisel off the panel themselves.

## What is true

The chip under the Cut-year 381 fill is the wall's quarry, not the Close's. That answer is already public: [[The Low Wall]] and [[A History of the Turning]] both say the chip did not match, so the afternoon is not a hidden answer waiting under the stone. The play is the quarrel over what the mismatch means and who gets to file it. The fill dates that quarrel, and it does not date the wall, the Tree, or the Before. If they lift the chip, [[The Other Chip]] is the object in their hands, and [[The Low Wall]] already holds the discoveries and the solves that must not be handed over.

## Where to stop

Do not scratch a year, and do not name a first enemy. Do not confirm the wall as the Close's outer ring, do not prove a mother, and do not name a Hand. Do not put [[The Spent Leaf]] in the bed. A knife-ribbon was left at this wall in error, and it is not [[The One Knife]], and it is not the house's [[The Ribboned Knife]].
