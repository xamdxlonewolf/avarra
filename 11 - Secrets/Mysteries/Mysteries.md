# Mysteries

How a secret is allowed to fire lives on [[Revelation Architecture]], and the tags that mark a note as exposing one live on [[Reveal Index]].

This folder points at those two notes, and it does not replace them or keep a second copy. [[Revelation Architecture]] stays at the secrets root, where it is the firing order for the whole shelf, and [[Reveal Index]] stays beside it as the tag list. That root is their home, and they were not moved.

The keystone and the opening are not filed here. [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]] stays in GM Truth, [[The Opening]] stays with the campaign, and nothing in this folder confirms the Leaf-Mother or the Other Hands.

- [[The Name-Stone Bed]] is an afternoon in which the Cut-year 381 fill is opened again. The chip is the other quarry, and the bed yields no year.
