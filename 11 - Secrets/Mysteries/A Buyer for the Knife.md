# A Buyer for the Knife

A purse on the Near Mile. Not the keystone. Not [[The Opening]]. The cutter stays unpicked.

## What the table can play

A buyer with coin wants a Near-Mile house to say their ribboned kitchen knife is the nameless knife. The buyer will not give a house-name. They want a founder they can carry. The house knows which summer the scorch belongs to. It was the Closing. They do not turn out for it, and they will not sell the knife as the first copy.

The party can be asked to persuade the house, to expose the buyer, to forge a provenance, or to walk away. Forging a provenance is a lie the house can spot. The summer is local memory, not a cipher.

## What is true

[[The Ribboned Knife]] is a kitchen knife. [[The One Knife]] has never been produced. No sale finishes the search. [[The First Cut]] still has five attributions and no hand on the handle. [[Heroes and Villains]] still leaves the cutter unpicked.

## Where to stop

Do not let the coin name a cutter. Do not plant the one knife in the loft, the wall, or the Close. Do not braid this week with [[The Spent Leaf]] or with Harrow's Green. The reason a serious asker will not name a house is already on [[The One Knife]]: a house would pick a hand. Do not add a second reason.
