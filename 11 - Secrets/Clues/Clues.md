# Clues

The order in which a secret may fire lives on [[Revelation Architecture]], and the tag list lives on [[Reveal Index]]. This page is the map of the objects: which folder already owns each one, and which confirmation you must not pull from the index itself.

Clue objects stay in the folders that are already their homes, and none of them were moved into this folder. A clue is not a confirmation, so rungs 1–5, the early steps a table can still explain away, stay deniable, and the firing order that says when a rung is allowed to stop being deniable stays on the architecture note.

## Where they live

- [[The Spent Leaf]] is filed with the items, in `10 - Items`. It is the pin for the keystone, the object that can do the confirming, and you do not fire that pin from this index.
- [[The Closed Lamp]] is filed in `10 - Items` as well. It belongs to the fire plot, and it is not a leaf.
- [[The Remainder]] is the desk that keeps the box the leaf is stored in.
- [[The Uncoloured Intake]] is filed in `07 - Religion`. It is the handout for the household secret, and it is never the first thing you show, and never in the same sitting as the leaf.
- Six foreshadowing texts are already listed on [[11 - Secrets]]. [[The Leaf-Mother]] holds two of them, the Tender's blessing and the Kept-child's saying, and the other four are [[The Child at Four Doors]], [[The Child Who Counted Stones]], [[The Branch That Came Away]], and [[The Child Who Climbed the Stone]].

[[The Other Chip]], [[The Dry Slip]], [[The Ribboned Knife]], [[The Socket Ribbon]], and [[The One Knife]] are argued objects, and they are not pins for the keystone, the household, or the opening.
