# Clues

Procedure: [[Revelation Architecture]]. Tags: [[Reveal Index]].

Clue objects stay in the folders that are their real homes. None of them were moved into this folder. A clue is not a confirmation. Rungs 1–5 stay deniable. The firing order stays on the architecture note.

## Where they live

- [[The Spent Leaf]] — `10 - Items`. Keystone pin. Do not fire it from this index.
- [[The Closed Lamp]] — `10 - Items`. Fire-plot object. Not a leaf.
- [[The Remainder]] — the desk that keeps the leaf's box.
- [[The Uncoloured Intake]] — `07 - Religion`. Household handout. Never first, and never with the leaf.
- Six foreshadowing texts, already listed on [[11 - Secrets]]. [[The Leaf-Mother]] holds two of them, the Tender's blessing and the Kept-child's saying. The other four are [[The Child at Four Doors]] · [[The Child Who Counted Stones]] · [[The Branch That Came Away]] · [[The Child Who Climbed the Stone]].

[[The Other Chip]], [[The Dry Slip]], [[The Ribboned Knife]], [[The Socket Ribbon]], and [[The One Knife]] are argued objects. They are not pins for the keystone, the household, or the opening.
