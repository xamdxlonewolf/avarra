# Names & Descent

> **GM reference.** The canonical home of the world/people naming scheme. The *existence* of the two world-names is player-facing; their **shared descent from Avarra is the flagship secret** ([[The Meaning of Avarra]]). Resolved 2026-08-11 — supersedes the *Green Reach* / *the Kiln* placeholders.

## The three-tier descent

```
                    AVARRA          "that which is whole / the world entire"
                  (oldest tongue)    — the primordial unified reality; campaign title
                    /        \
              AVERA            VARA
        ah-VEH-rah         VAH-rah
        "the world /       "the world / the place
         our home"          that contains us"
        Averans · Averan   Varans · Varan
```

Both descendants mean, plainly, **"the world."** Neither people experiences its word as a *proper noun* — ask an Averan why the world is called Avera and they'd answer "why wouldn't it be?", exactly as we treat *world*, *earth*, *home*. That generic-feel is load-bearing: the reveal is not "these two words are related" but **"your word for *the world* was always a name."**

## The words

| | **Avera** | **Vara** | **Avarra** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Says** | ah-VEH-rah | VAH-rah | ah-VAR-rah |
| **Means** | the world / our home | the world / the place that contains us | that which is whole / the world entire |
| **People** | the Averans | the Varans | the Avarrans (if the term survives at all) |
| **Adjective** | Averan | Varan | Avarran |
| **Register** | warm, old, pastoral — *the land we live upon* | structural, enclosing — *the place that contains us* | archaic, almost sacred; the world before the fracture |
| **Visibility** | player-facing | GM until first contact | GM (the reveal) |

### Etymology (GM)

- **Avera** ← *Avarra* softened over millennia; the doubled *rr* eroded, the vowels warmed.
- **Vara** ← *Avarra* with the **initial vowel dropped** and the doubled consonant simplified (*Avarra → Av'ra → Vara*). A colder, more clipped evolution — the sound of the world mirrors the world.
- Same root, two independent erosions. Neither people recognizes the other's word as kin, and **neither recognizes its own word as a name.**

## Usage register (Avera, player-facing)

People say it the way we say "the world," not the way we say a country:

- "I was born in Avera."
- "The oldest roads in Avera…"
- "Across Avera, no two river-cities keep the same calendar."

An older, formal construction exists for inscriptions and liturgy — e.g. *Avera, the Land Beneath the Living Sky* — but ordinary speech never dignifies it as a proper name.

## The reveal (how the descent surfaces)

Decoded by the *players* through inscriptions, never NPC exposition. Full ladder in [[The Meaning of Avarra]]; in brief:

1. **AVARRA** on an old inscription → read as an archaic name for *Avera*.
2. **VARA** on a Varan inscription → dismissed as "their unrelated word for their world."
3. **AVARRA · *the world*** on an older inscription → *Avera* and *Vara* resolve into two survivors of **one** word. Neither civilization named its world; both inherited a broken piece of the name of the world before.

## Design notes

- The **near-twin forms are deliberate.** Legible enough at the table that no one asks "which side are we on?", yet close enough that *Averan* + *Varan* → *Avarran* is the phonetic payload of the reveal.
- **Neither name is hero- or villain-coded** (the hard naming constraint): both simply mean *home*, from the inside. Satisfied.
- **Green Reach / the Kiln** persist only as **GM design shorthand** for the organic-vs-vitreous aesthetic contrast. Never in-world.
- **The Wardens** kept as-is (means keeper/guard; non-villain-coded).
- Machine keys: `world: avarra | avera | vara`.

## Open (deferred)

- What the **languages themselves** are called (as opposed to the world-names).
- Capital / place-name registers for each half.
- Whether *Avarran* as a demonym survives anywhere in either tongue, or is wholly lost.
