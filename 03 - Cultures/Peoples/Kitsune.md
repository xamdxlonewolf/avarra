# Kitsune

> **A custom born-ancestry (Kind), not a Condition.** One of the homebrew ancestries kept for this world (with [[Selkie]] and [[Tengu]]), sitting alongside Daggerheart's stock roster. You are *born* Kitsune — it is your birth-shape, mundane in this world. **Refined 2026-08-16: fox-variety locked (Wilds / Snows / Sands), all features locked.** See "Open questions" for anything still open.

## Overview

Kitsune are the fox-born: slight, quick-eyed people with a fox's ears and a brush of tail, and the cold pale light of **foxfire** always a thought away at their fingertips. In folklore the fox is a shapeshifter and a liar. **Neither is true here.** A Kitsune keeps one shape their whole life like anyone else, and they are no more dishonest than their neighbours — the fox-born are simply **hard to pin down.** They redirect. They step out of the frame of a situation and leave you looking at where they were. The tail is real and worn openly; the foxfire is a cold flame that lights a room without warming it or catching the drapes.

**Not one fox but many.** Just as the world holds arctic, desert, and forest foxes, the fox-born come in kinds shaped by the country they were born to — the **Fox of the Wilds** (red-and-russet, the forest and hedgerow fox), the **Fox of the Snows** (white-coated, born to the cold north and the high snows), and the **Fox of the Sands** (pale and huge-eared, born to the deserts and dry country). All are Kitsune, share the same foxfire and the same slipperiness, and marry and mingle freely; a child takes after one parent's line or the other. Each carries a small knack bred into it by its home ground.

**The fresh angle:** in a world where three of every four people acquire a [[Conditions|Condition]], the Kitsune are a Kind defined not by *changing* but by *never quite being where you left them.* Their whole reputation is misdirection-without-deceit — the fox slips the trap, it doesn't lie about the trap being there.

## Values & Worldview

Kitsune prize **the graceful exit** and the **unfought fight** — the deal that dissolves before it comes to blows, the door found before the room fills with smoke. They tend to distrust cornered situations (their instinct in a corner is to *make a new corner*) and to admire cleverness over force. A common fox-born saying: *"Never the last one holding it."* They keep their word carefully precisely because, being so slippery, an untrustworthy Kitsune would be trusted by no one — so a Kitsune's given word tends to be *very* solid, and they know it's their most valuable possession.

## Customs & Daily Life

Because they defuse rather than escalate and can light a safe, cold lamp anywhere, Kitsune are the world's natural **mediators, brokers, and night-couriers**: they carry delicate news between rival houses, hold the neutral ground in a negotiation, walk the dark roads with foxfire lanterns that never risk a fire, and are trusted to be somewhere unnoticed and gone again without a scene. Foxfire is a small everyday grace — Kitsune children are taught to make it before they're taught to read, and a home with a fox-born in it never wants for a soft light. Older Kitsune, by tradition, carry themselves with more tails in the *telling* of it (the folklore of nine tails survives as reputation and honorific, not as literal biology or power).

## Daggerheart (ancestry features)

Every Kitsune has both shared features, **and** chooses one **Fox** at character creation.

- **Foxfire** — You can conjure **foxfire**: a cold, pale flame that sheds light like a torch but gives off no heat and cannot ignite anything. You can hold it, or send it to hover anywhere within **Close** range, and snuff it at will. Once per scene, you can spend a **Hope** to flare and split it into flickering shapes and sounds — a decoy that pulls attention — giving you **advantage** on a roll to distract, mislead, or slip past a creature within Close range.
- **Slip the Frame** — When you would take damage from an attack, you can mark a **Stress** to slip aside in a blink of foxfire: halve the damage, then immediately move up to **Very Close** range to a position out of the attacker's reach. If you end this movement out of line of sight, you are **Hidden**.
- **Your Fox** — Choose the fox you were born to. This grants an extra knack:
  - **Fox of the Wilds** *(red / forest fox)* — You are at home in tangled, broken, or overgrown country. You have **advantage** on Agility Rolls to climb, leap, tumble, or move quickly through difficult or wild terrain, and you ignore the movement penalty for such terrain.
  - **Fox of the Snows** *(arctic fox)* — You ignore the effects of cold, and your coat and stillness hide you where there's no cover to be had. You have **advantage** on rolls to **Hide** or move unseen in open, snowy, pale, or exposed terrain.
  - **Fox of the Sands** *(fennec)* — You ignore the effects of heat, and your great ears miss nothing. You have **advantage** on rolls to hear, notice, or locate hidden creatures, sounds, or movement, whether by day or in the dark.

## Open questions (to refine next)

- **Slip the Frame power band (noted, kept)** — halve-damage + reposition + conditional Hidden is deliberately a notch above the SRD ancestry band (cf. Faerie *Wings* = +2 Evasion for a Stress). Kept at full strength by choice; flag here in case a play-test wants it trimmed.
- **Fox knacks — parity check** — Wilds (traverse), Snows (hide/cold), Sands (perceive/heat) are each "one situational advantage + an environmental comfort." Confirm they feel evenly useful across typical campaigns, or if one home ground rarely comes up we can broaden it.
- Physical read: how fox is a Kitsune — full ears+tail, or subtler? Affects how "mundane, nobody blinks" reads at the table. (Fox *variety* now also colours this — a Snows fox reads white-furred, a Sands fox huge-eared and pale, etc.)
- Room for more than three foxes later (e.g. a grey/tree fox, a swift/plains fox) if the world wants them — the pattern scales.
