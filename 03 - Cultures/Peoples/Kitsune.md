# Kitsune

> **A custom born-ancestry (Kind), not a Condition.** Kitsune are one of four ancestries unique to this world, with the [[Selkie]], the [[Tengu]], and the [[Yumboe]], and they sit alongside Daggerheart's stock roster. They are born with the ears, the tail, and the foxfire already theirs, and a Condition is a change that comes later, so the fox-shape is not something they became. Three features come with that birth: **Slip the Frame**, **Fox's Glamour**, and **your Fox** (Wilds / Snows / Sands). Foxfire (*kitsunebi*, fox-flame) is what unifies the Fox moves, and *Fox's Glamour* is a seeming, another face, not another body.

## Overview

Kitsune are the fox-born: slight, quick-eyed people with a fox's ears and a brush of tail, and the cold pale light of **foxfire** — *kitsunebi*, fox-flame — always a thought away at their fingertips. They are born with that shape, a Kind rather than a Condition, so the neighbours treat the ears and the tail as ordinary. In folklore the fox is a shapeshifter and a liar, and **the truth here is gentler but real:** a Kitsune can throw a **glamour**, foxfire drawn into the shape of another face, a borrowed voice, or a trick of the eye, and that glamour is *seeming*, not flesh. It never touches what a person decides, only what they perceive, and a Kitsune is no more dishonest than their neighbours. They simply **cannot quite be pinned down:** they redirect, step out of the frame of a situation, and leave you looking at where they were, or at a face that isn't theirs. The tail is real and worn openly, and it is often the surest way to catch a glamoured fox, the one thing the fire won't hide.

Foxfire is the thread that runs through all of them — the ghost-light that leads travelers off the mountain path, the pale fire that hides the sacred white fox, the flame the old nine-tails were said to carry. Each kind of fox bends the same fire a different way.

**Not one fox but many.** Just as the world holds arctic, desert, and forest foxes, the fox-born come in kinds shaped by the country they were born to: the **Fox of the Wilds** (red-and-russet, the forest and hedgerow fox), the **Fox of the Snows** (white-coated, born to the cold north and the high snows), and the **Fox of the Sands** (pale and huge-eared, born to the dry country). All of them are Kitsune. They share the same slipperiness and the same foxfire, and they marry and mingle freely, a child taking after one parent's line or the other. Each carries a gift bred into it by its home ground, and turns its foxfire toward a different old fox-story: the will-o'-wisp that leads a hunter astray, the pale flame that swallows the white fox from sight, and the ward-fire of the fox that always knows what is coming.

A Kitsune is not changed into anything, and they are known for *never quite being where you left them*, a reputation for misdirection without deceit: the fox slips the trap, and it does not lie about the trap being there.

## Values & Worldview

Kitsune prize **the graceful exit** and the **unfought fight**, the deal that dissolves before it comes to blows, and the door found before the room fills with smoke. They tend to distrust a cornered situation, because their instinct in a corner is to *make a new corner*, and they admire cleverness over force. A common fox-born saying goes, *"Never the last one holding it."* They keep their word carefully, precisely because they are so slippery that an untrustworthy Kitsune would be trusted by no one, so a Kitsune's given word tends to be *very* solid, and they know it is their most valuable possession.

## Customs & Daily Life

Because they defuse rather than escalate, and because they can never quite be cornered, Kitsune are the world's natural **mediators, brokers, and night-couriers**. They carry delicate news between rival houses, hold the neutral ground in a negotiation, walk the dark roads by their own cold foxfire, and are trusted to be somewhere unnoticed and then gone again without a scene. Foxfire is a small everyday grace as well, so children are taught to make it before they are taught to read, and a home with a fox-born in it never wants for a soft, safe light. The summer before they Turn, the season when the town will gather to watch them, many spend a **fox-summer** at another Fox-ground, so the Wilds, the Snows, and the Sands stay one Kind ([[Kinds of the Turning]]). Older Kitsune, by tradition, carry themselves with more tails in the *telling* of it, and the folklore of nine tails survives as reputation and as an honorific, not as literal biology and not as a power.

## Daggerheart (ancestry features)

Every Kitsune has **Slip the Frame** and **Fox's Glamour**, **and** chooses one **Fox** at character creation. The Fox is a full feature — each grants a lasting knack (the real animal's gift, bred in by its home ground) and a **foxfire** signature move (the fox-*spirit* myth, each kind turning the same ghost-flame a different way). **SRD mix:** Top = Slip the Frame · Bottom = Fox's Glamour · Hearth (not mixed) = Your Fox. Full custom package takes no [[Kind Heritage|Hearth-Mark]]. In those published rules a mixed character takes the top feature of one ancestry and the bottom feature of another, so Your Fox, the hearth feature, stays with a full Kitsune. The Hearth-Mark is the mark a stock character takes from the place that raised them, and this package does not take one.

- **Slip the Frame** — When you would take damage from an attack, you can mark a **Stress** to slip aside: halve the damage, then immediately move up to **Very Close** range to a position out of the attacker's reach. If you end this movement out of line of sight, you are **Hidden**.
- **Fox's Glamour** — You can wrap yourself in foxfire-glamour to seem other than you are: veil your fox features to pass unremarked, cast your voice or a small sound a short way off, or throw a minor sensory illusion (a flicker of movement, a scent, a light) into a space within **Close** range. Once per scene, you can mark a **Stress** to **wear another face** — take on the convincing seeming of another person you've seen, holding it as long as you concentrate. The glamour is *illusion, not flesh*: it doesn't change your body or fool touch, and a creature that has strong reason to doubt what it's seeing can make an appropriate roll to catch the tell (a fox-shadow, a reflection that's a beat late, an animal's unease). *(This is disguise and misdirection — never mind-control; it changes what others perceive, not what they decide.)*
- **Your Fox** — Choose the fox you were born to. Each has a lasting knack and a foxfire move:
  - **Fox of the Wilds** *(red / forest fox — the will-o'-wisp that leads travelers off the path)* — *Knack:* you are at home in tangled, broken, or overgrown country — **advantage** on Agility Rolls to climb, leap, tumble, or move through difficult terrain, and you ignore its movement penalty. *Foxfire — Wisp-Lure:* mark a **Stress** to send dancing ghost-lights and false trails around a creature within **Close** range; until the end of its next turn it is drawn after the lights — its attacks against anyone but you are made with **disadvantage**, and you may immediately move up to **Very Close** range without drawing an attack. *(It's a lure, not a leash — the target isn't compelled to move, only baited to look the wrong way.)*
  - **Fox of the Snows** *(arctic fox — the sacred white fox that vanishes in its own pale fire)* — *Knack:* you leave no trail worth following — adversaries have **disadvantage** on rolls to track, trail, or find you, and you have **advantage** on rolls to **Hide** or move unseen in open, snowy, pale, or exposed terrain. *Foxfire — Cold Vanishing:* once per scene, mark a **Stress** to wrap yourself in cold white foxfire and let the eye slide off you — become **Hidden** even while observed and in the open, so long as you don't take an action that would obviously reveal you.
  - **Fox of the Sands** *(fennec — the huge-eared fox that hears what's coming)* — *Knack:* you see clearly in darkness and dim light as far as you would in daylight, and your great ears miss nothing — **advantage** on rolls to hear, notice, or locate hidden creatures, sounds, or movement. *Foxfire — Ward-Flame:* when you or an ally within **Close** range would be targeted by an attack, you can mark a **Stress** to throw up a sudden curtain of foxfire — the target gains a **+2 bonus to their Evasion** against that attack. *(Nine tails stay reputation and honorific — not this Fox's body.)*

## Naming & tongue

The hearth-register is **Kusawe** (koo-SAH-way), a light, slipping, sibilant tongue. The full inventory, the collision rules, and the two-name pattern — a hearth-given name, then the place's second name, whether that is a byname or a house-name — are in [[Naming People in the Turning]]. Working given names: Raki, Nenyu, Wishi, Kiraye, Suti, Hatu, Ranyu, Sinro, Foryo, Heka, Sofan. Daily speech is the local [[The Old Tongue|Maiethren drift]], the local wear on the old tongue, and the fox-name is what they answer to when someone is trying to pin them down.

Where they live is three hearths, not a fox-country, and that map is [[Kinds of the Turning]].

## GM Notes

- **Seed.** The Kusawe list was drawn at seed `20260823`.
- **Package locked.** The three features are Slip the Frame, Fox's Glamour, and your Fox (Wilds / Snows / Sands). Do not trim them. Stock characters take a [[Kind Heritage|Hearth-Mark]], and Mixed Ancestry never takes Your Fox.
- **Folklore check (2026-08-16).** The package hits 6 of 7 iconic kitsune traits. Fox's Glamour is illusion and disguise, not the flesh-change of the [[Two-Bodied]], and it fills the niche left when a doppelganger ancestry was cut.
- **Fox's Glamour scope.** A Kitsune can veil their features and cast small illusions at will. Once per scene they can wear another face, a person they have seen, for as long as they concentrate. The effect is illusion, not flesh, and it is never mind-control.
- **No damage option (intentional).** The package is control, stealth, and support. Do not add an offensive foxfire bolt unless a later pass asks for one.
- **Slip the Frame** sits a notch above the SRD band (compare Faerie *Wings*). It was kept at full strength by choice.
- **Fox parity.** Wilds is terrain plus Wisp-Lure, Snows is untrackable plus Cold Vanishing, and Sands is darkvision plus Ward-Flame. The ears and the tail are what gives a glamour away.
