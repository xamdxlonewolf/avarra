# Selkie

> **A custom born-ancestry (Kind), not a Condition.** Selkies are one of four ancestries unique to this world, with the [[Kitsune]], the [[Tengu]], and the [[Yumboe]]. They are born to a sealskin that is hereditary and mundane here, the body they arrived with and not a Condition they became. Three features come with that birth: **Second Skin**, **Slip the Hold**, and **Seal-Kin**.

## Overview

Selkies are the sea-born, the skin-kin: sleek, cool-skinned people who carry a **second self** — a **sealskin** they can wear or set aside. They are born to that skin, so it is a Kind, the ancestry they arrived with, and not a Condition, which would be a change that came later. The skin is not a costume and not a disguise; it is genuinely *them*, a spare body kept close, and slipping it on or off takes only a moment. Distinct from the frog-folk who simply live underwater, a Selkie's gift is not *breathing the deep* — it's that **you cannot keep hold of one.** A Selkie in the water, in a net, in a grip, in a locked room, has a way out; the sea taught them that being held is temporary.

Out of the skin, a Selkie reads as an ordinary person with a few quiet tells — **cool, faintly sleek skin, large dark eyes, and sometimes a little webbing at the fingers.** Nobody stares, because the seal-blood shows the way any other Kind's does, and the real change lives in the skin, not the face.

A Selkie belongs fully to **two homes at once**, shore and sea, and so Selkies move easily between those homes when the people on either side do not understand the other. The removable skin is a private, tender thing, not a leash. Loaning it is a real intimacy, and having it taken is a real violation. Both are matters of story and trust, and never a mechanical penalty.

## Values & Worldview

Selkies value **belonging without being bound**, a deep loyalty freely given and resented the instant it is *demanded.* They are famously hard to detain and famously hard to hold *onto*. A Selkie loves fiercely and will not be caged, and a relationship or a job that starts to feel like a net is one they will quietly, gracefully leave. They carry a comfortable double-homesickness their whole lives and consider it normal. To always miss the place you aren't is, to them, simply the price of getting to have two homes.

## Customs & Daily Life

Because they can't be kept down and can't be kept out, Selkies are the world's **rescue swimmers, cold-water divers and salvors, and go-betweens**, the ones sent into the surf after a drowning, down to a wreck, or across the water to a community the shore-folk can't talk to. That same talent for getting free makes them trusted couriers for anything that must not be seized in transit. The sealskin is treated with real ceremony. Where a Selkie keeps their skin, who they would ever let hold it, and the day a child is first given the run of their own skin, are all significant and private. Kin from **another strand** often come for those skin-days, or the child spends a summer on a second coast, which is how a quay-born Selkie still sounds like the sea ([[Kinds of the Turning]]). Surviving a drowning is the threshold that Strikes the [[Far-Voiced]], a Condition that can arrive by that drowning rather than by birth, and Selkies, being so at home in the water, have a complicated, storied relationship with that threshold.

## Daggerheart (ancestry features)

Every Selkie has all three. **SRD mix:** Top = Second Skin · Bottom = Slip the Hold · Hearth (not mixed) = Seal-Kin. Full custom package takes no [[Kind Heritage|Hearth-Mark]]. In the published rules a mixed character takes the top feature of one ancestry and the bottom of another, so Seal-Kin, the hearth feature, stays with a full Selkie. The Hearth-Mark is the mark a stock character takes from the place that raised them, and this package does not take one.

- **Second Skin** — You carry a **sealskin**, a second self you can don or shed in a moment. While you wear it, you ignore the effects of cold and deep water, you can hold your breath far longer than anyone should, and you always know which way is up and where the nearest surface or shore lies. You have **advantage** on Agility Rolls to swim, dive, or move through water.
  - *Anchored.* Your skin is your fixed point in the world. Wherever you leave it stashed, you always know the direction and distance to it and can find your way back to it unerringly, however far you've travelled or however turned-around you are. (Loaning your skin, or having it taken, is a matter of story and trust — never a mechanical penalty.)
- **Slip the Hold** — When a creature grabs or restrains you, or succeeds on an attack against you, you can mark a **Stress** to slip free like a seal from a net: you end any grab or **Restrained** effect on you, ignore any forced movement from that attack, and move up to **Very Close** range out of the creature's reach.
- **Seal-Kin** — Seals and the beasts of the sea treat you as kin, not as prey or stranger. You have **advantage** on rolls to calm, read, or call an animal of the sea. Once per scene, while you are in or near water, you can mark a **Stress** to let the sea itself warn you: the GM **names** what is approaching through the water, and until the end of your next turn the nearest seal-kin surfaces to aid you — a distraction that draws a foe's attention, a tow through the current, or a shove that carries you toward shore or safety. You may move up to **Close** range before the named approach arrives. *(A warning and a help — not an immunity.)*

## Naming & tongue

The hearth-register is **Sakoa** (sah-KOH-ah), a liquid, rounded, coastal tongue. The full inventory and the two-name pattern, a name given from the hearth and then the place's second name, are in [[Naming People in the Turning]]. Working given names: Lavo, Fesa, Moka, Dovo, Neson, Foaro, Veakea, Didel, Roadoa, Woanun, Laknea. The hearth-name is spoken *to the water* the day a child is first given the run of their skin. The second name, a byname or a house-name, comes from the town that watched them Turn.

Where they live is the coasts, not a sea-nation, and that map is [[Kinds of the Turning]].

## GM Notes

- **Seed.** The Sakoa list was drawn at seed `20260824`.
- **Package locked.** There are three features, and they should not be trimmed. Stock characters take a [[Kind Heritage|Hearth-Mark]] instead.
- **Skin.** *Anchored* is an upside: the Selkie can find the skin wherever it was stashed. There is no "skin stolen" penalty. Loaning the skin, or losing it, is a story beat, and it is not a Tithe.
- **Not Ribbet.** A Selkie is at home in the water and is not a true underwater breather. Ribbet are the frog-folk the overview sets them beside.
- **Slip the Hold** sits a notch above the stock band, matched to Kitsune's Slip the Frame. Do not add "cannot be caught off guard" back onto Seal-Kin.
- **Seal-Kin** calls seal-kin to aid, and it does not charm people and is not [[Far-Voiced]].
