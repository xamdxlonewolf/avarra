# Yumboe

> **A custom born-ancestry (Kind), not a Condition.** The Yumboe are the fourth born-ancestry of this world, with the [[Kitsune]], the [[Selkie]], and the [[Tengu]], and the first from beyond the mainland, the **hill-folk of [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]]**, born to that people rather than changed into them. Unlike the other three, a Yumboe is **mythic-rare** off that far isle, which means most people of [[Maiethorn]], [[Strandoren]], and [[Heskoren]] have only heard stories of the pearly, silver-haired good people from across the drowning sea. **A Yumboe character needs the GM's leave. If the table says yes, you take Hollow-Hill, Moon-Waked, and The Unseen Hands together, and you never take Mixed Ancestry,** because this Kind does not come in halves.

## Overview

Yumboes are the **good people** — *Bakhna Rakhna* in their own tongue — the small, moon-waked hill-folk of the far isle of [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]], born into that shape as a Kind, and not changed into it later by a Condition. They stand about waist-high to a mainlander, with **pearl-pale skin that catches the light, hair like spun silver, and wide dark eyes made for the night.** They live *within* the green hills of their home, a warren of hearths and long feast-halls hollowed under the turf, and they come up by moonlight, because they keep the night the way other folk keep the day. They are **famously, defiantly hospitable**: a Yumboe's table is open to native and stranger alike, laid with more than a small people should be able to carry, and waited on by **hands and feet you can plainly see and bodies you cannot** — the unseen servants that are the good people's oldest wonder.

The Yumboes are an ordinary, warm, whole people who live somewhere almost no one can reach, so their strangeness is a matter of **distance, not nature.** They are not strange to themselves, and they are only a legend to a world that has never had them to supper. The mainland files them, and the hands with no bodies, under fairy-tale.

## Values & Worldview

Yumboes prize **hospitality above nearly all else**. To feed a guest, and to be fed as one, is their deepest courtesy and their firmest bond, and a shared table binds them the way a sworn word binds the [[Bound]], people who carry a contract they swore. They are a people of the **threshold and the hearth**, comfortable underground and after dark, unhurried, and quietly certain that the bustle of the surface at noon misses most of what matters. Because they are small, and because their home is hidden, they have never learned to meet force with force, so they meet it with **welcome, with misdirection, and with a great many helping hands.** A Yumboe saying: *"No one is a stranger twice."* They find the mainland's daylight faiths loud, and they find its treatment of anyone left unwelcomed baffling.

## Customs & Daily Life

On Kumbaan, life runs by the moon. The good people wake at dusk, they work and feast and dance through the bright nights, and they sleep out the glare of day in the cool of the hills. Their halls are famous, in their own telling, for **feasts that outshine their means**, because the unseen hands fetch and pour and serve, so that even a poor household sets a table like a lord's. They keep **terrace-gardens and standing-stone marks** up on the surface, and they keep the real life below. They fish the storm-walled shallows and, the old accounts say, were not above **lifting a little corn** from anyone careless enough to leave it out, a small people's small mischief, told fondly.

Off the isle, among the very few who make the crossing, a Yumboe is a walking wonder and knows it. They tend to become **hosts, keepers, cooks, and go-betweens** wherever they land, turning the one thing they always have, a welcome and hands to lay a table, into a place in a world that first meets them as a myth. A Yumboe abroad is usually the only one anyone in that town has ever seen.

## Daggerheart (ancestry features)

Every Yumboe has all three: **Hollow-Hill**, **Moon-Waked**, and **The Unseen Hands**. This Kind is **not a Mixed Ancestry partner**. There is no split into a top feature and a bottom feature, and no pairing of a Yumboe feature with a stock feature or with a mainland custom feature. The package is the whole Kind, and it takes no [[Kind Heritage|Hearth-Mark]], the mark a stock character takes from the place that raised them. Playing one needs the GM's leave ([[Kind Heritage#Yumboe (not a mix partner)]]).

- **Hollow-Hill** — You are one of the good people: **small** (hill-folk stature), and born to the deep earth. You can move through gaps and spaces too tight for a larger creature, and you have **advantage** on Agility Rolls to squeeze, climb, or move through cramped, broken, or underground terrain and to **Hide** where there is cover to slip behind. Underground, or within stone and hill, you always know your depth and heading and **cannot become lost**. You feel movement through the ground: the **direction, number, and gait** (walk, crawl, or slide) of creatures within **Close** range who are on or under the earth. *(Tremor-sense — not an immunity.)*
- **Moon-Waked** — You keep the night. You see in darkness and dim light as clearly as others see by day, and you have **advantage** on rolls to notice, search, or act **at night or underground** — anywhere the sun doesn't reach. When you take rest **under the open night sky or moonlight**, you clear **1 additional Stress** (or your party gains a little of the good people's ease — GM's call on the fiction). The daylight doesn't harm you, but you are never quite at your best in its glare.
- **The Unseen Hands** — You can call the good people's unseen servants — **hands and feet plainly visible, but no body between them** — to attend you. At will, they perform small tasks within **Close** range: fetch and carry a light object, pour and serve, hold a door or a rope, steady a load, lay a table. Once per scene, you can mark a **Stress** to set them to **harry** instead: a flurry of unseen hands swarms a target within **Close** range, and until the end of its next turn choose one — the target has **disadvantage** on its next attack, **or** the next ally to act against it has **advantage**. *(The hands are a conjured seeming of many small helpers, not a force — they can distract, fumble, and fetch, but they can't deal damage, wield a weapon, or restrain a determined creature.)*

## Naming & tongue

The Yumboes speak **their own tongue**, unrelated to [[The Old Tongue|Maiethren]]. It is the one language in the setting that owes *nothing* to the Leaf-Mother's liturgy, the prayers spoken in the faith that grew up around the mainland's Turning Trees, because the [[Turning Tree|Trees]] never crossed the storm-wall. The sound is **open-voweled and warm, with soft pre-nasal clusters** (*mb, nd, nk, nj*) and a lilting, drum-like rhythm, kept deliberately distinct from the whole Maiethren world, so a Yumboe name *sounds* foreign the instant it is spoken.

- **Their name for themselves:** *Bakhna Rakhna* — "the good people."
- **Their homeland:** *Kumbaan* (koom-BAHN) — "the hidden home." (Mainlanders call it [[The Sundering Isle]].)
- **Sample given names:** Ambaa, Njeela, Kumbo, Sambanka, Ndeya, Mbaraan, Njunda, Rakhilo, Sonkaa, Mbindaa, Yumbaka, Ndoolu, **Sambiyo, Lunji, Yaaba, Saalo, Mbela, Soonke, Njili, Yendo, Waandi, Koriwaa.**
- The full rules for naming a person, the two-name pattern, what happens when names collide, and how they stay a *single* register even on the mainland, are in [[Naming People in the Turning]]. Where they live is [[Kinds of the Turning]].

## GM Notes

- **The isle is a reach-edge, not a racial immunity.** Read this with [[The Other Hands]]. Her Giving does not cross the storm-wall, and the Yumboe do not keep her household's doors, so her purchase on the isle is thin. The Struck are rare because those doors are barely a concept there, not because Yumboes are refused. A Yumboe under a mainland Tree *could* be Given, so do not write a ban that says the Kind cannot be Given, and do not confirm the household in the player-facing body of this note.
- **Seed and map tooling.** New Yumboe given names were drawn at seed `20260826`. The isle's Azgaar name base, which is place-names, lives in `14 - Assets/Maps/Map Generation Tooling.md` §③b. That file is a production aid, and it is not the terrain.
- **Keyed tags.** `keystone-adjacent` · `the-other-hands`.
- **Package locked.** There are three features, and they should not be trimmed. Stock characters take a [[Kind Heritage|Hearth-Mark]]. The tremor-sense on Hollow-Hill is not the phrase "cannot be caught off guard."
- **Folklore check (2026-08-22).** The source is the Wolof and Senegambian *Yumboe* in Keightley: pearly and silver, hill-dwellers, moon-waked, unseen hands, hospitality, and the name *Bakhna Rakhna*. The ancestral flavour of that tradition lives on [[The Open Table]], and it is not a claim that the unseen hands are literal ghosts.
- **Lanes.** Hollow-Hill is the earth and the small stature, Moon-Waked is seeing at night together with the extra clearing of Stress at rest, and the Unseen Hands are helpers who cannot restrain a determined creature and cannot deal damage.
- **Conditions on the isle.** There is no Tree on the isle, and nothing is Given there. The Struck are rare. A Yumboe who stands under a mainland Tree during a tenth-year week is a campaign beat, and Kind still stacks with whatever Condition comes of it.
- **Mixed Ancestry.** Yumboe do not mix. They need the GM's leave, and if the table says yes they are the complete Kind. A child of a Yumboe and a mainlander is a full Yumboe or the other parent's Kind.
