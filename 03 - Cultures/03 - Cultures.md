### Social Structures
- [[Law and Citizenship]] — the universal legal grammar of a world where 3 of 4 carry a Condition. Citizenship is *witnessed, not recorded* (your town saw you Turn; proof runs through vouching people, not papers); the Inviolate Will doctrine; guild safety-licences for the ~3 hazardous Conditions; polity variation on three independent axes (theology / reach / governance). *(Epic 2, Story 2.1.)*
- [[Economy and the Tithe]] — the universal economic grammar. The Tithe is the engine (upkeep for 75% = a whole sector); self-paying vs provided-for Tithes (a spectrum, with Far-Voiced & Returned on the seam); labor-by-Condition; the Kept as free generalist labor; deathless banking houses & the endow-not-inherit loop; center/periphery; shadow economy. Lived official faces → [[Tithe-Infrastructure]]. Lived illicit face → [[The Slide]]. *(Epic 2, Story 2.2; lived houses Epic 5.)*
- [[Daily Life]] — the universal grammar of everyday life. Marriage & family as a spectrum (mundane-mixed → the aching Long-Lived marriage you outlive); medicine (Stilled surgeons, Returned tending the dead — miraculous, unequally distributed); city design as principles (the Tree at the centre, Tithe-provision as civic utility). Where the ~5%-scary dial sits at eye level. *(Epic 2, Story 2.3.)*
- [[Polity Archetypes]] — three worked polities at *different corners* of the theology/reach/governance axes, proving the universal grammar above flexes: **Vaethorn** (the Waiting Lands — warm/poor/faithful pilgrim edge), **Lestrand** (the Ledger Coast — rich/cool secular merchant power), **Threnmaieth** (the Tallied Crown — sanctified surveillance, the darkest corner). Each pair shares exactly one axis, so the set proves the three are independent. *(Epic 2, Story 2.4.)*

### Languages & Naming
- [[The Old Tongue]] — *Maiethren*, the old liturgical root tongue of the [[Turning Tree|Awakening Tree]] (this world's "Latin"): warm/weighty phonology, pronunciation key, sacred lexicon, and **one root → three daughter drifts** (conserved core / worn frontier / eroded coast) — so *how a polity sounds reveals its stance*. Names the three polities. *(Epic 2 tail / Epic 4.)*
- [[Naming in the Turning]] — the naming-conventions companion: **the name a polity gives a shared institution reveals its stance** (venting-hall → *gift-hall* in devout Vaethorn / *release-house* on secular Lestrand / *counted hall* in surveillance-state Threnmaieth). The working dictionary of institution names + how to coin more. *(Epic 2 tail / Epic 4.)*
- [[Naming People in the Turning]] — person-names: named by *place*; custom Kinds keep a hearth-register (Kusawe / Sakoa / Gonan / Yumboe). Seeded lists, collision rules. *(Story 4.2.)*
