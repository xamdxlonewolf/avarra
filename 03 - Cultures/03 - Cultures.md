# 03 - Cultures

Peoples live here, and so do the languages they speak, the customs and traditions they keep, the social structures they build, and the naming conventions they use when they name a person or a hall.

### Custom Kinds (born-ancestries unique to this world)

A Kind is the ancestry a person is born with. These notes say where those peoples are densest, and how a child learns the ways of that Kind. A Condition, the change a person may come into later, has its own notes and is not repeated here.

- [[Kinds of the Turning]] — how Kinds sit on the map. A Kind has a hearth, the ground where that people is densest, and it does not have a nation of its own. The note also keeps the other kitchen, which is the summer a child spends with kin of the same Kind, the children of mixed Kinds, and a short sketch of where each stock ancestry is most often met.
- [[Kind Heritage]] — the rulings you need at the table: a stock character takes a Hearth-Mark from the place that raised them, Mixed Ancestry is how a character is drawn from two Kinds, and the one surprise keyword is kept on that same note.
- [[Kitsune]] — the fox-born, with three hearths, the Wilds, the Snows, and the Sands, and 3 ancestry features.
- [[Selkie]] — the sea-born, with a coastal hearth and 3 ancestry features.
- [[Tengu]] — the mountain-born crow-folk, with a ridge hearth and 3 ancestry features.
- [[Yumboe]] — the good people of [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]]. Off the isle they are mythic-rare, a story more often than a neighbour, and they have 3 ancestry features.
- These four sit alongside Daggerheart's 18 stock ancestries. Where a stock group is dense, what that kitchen cooks, and what the group does not own, live on [[Kinds of the Turning]]. A stock player character takes a Hearth-Mark, and that ruling is on [[Kind Heritage]].

### Social Structures

- [[Law and Citizenship]] — the law of a world where 3 of 4 people carry a Condition, the change that comes to sit on the Kind they were born. Citizenship is witnessed, not recorded: a hearth saw you Turn, and when you have to prove it you go through people who will vouch for you, not through a paper. In a city the witnessing is by hearth-stand, the hearth that stood and can say it saw you. On the road there is a road-word, and the watch has three questions. Crime walks a ladder under the Inviolate Will, the doctrine that a person's will is their own. Guilds issue safety-tickets for hazardous *uses* of a Condition, and the pool of people who can be ticketed is not a census of who carries one (ticketable pool ≠ census). Polities, the countries of this world, differ on three things: theology, which is how devout the place is; reach, which is how the Trees stand there; and governance, which is how far the state reaches onto a person.
- [[Economy and the Tithe]] — the economy of that same world. The Tithe, the upkeep a Condition asks, organizes the work a person is trusted to do and a civic utility the town keeps. The picture for that utility is wells, not granaries: the upkeep is kept available the way a well is, and it is not a warehouse of the Condition. Some Tithes pay for themselves in the work, and some have to be provided, and that difference is a spectrum, with the Far-Voiced and the Returned sitting on the seam between the two. The note says why the Taken-In live in stone cities, how labor sorts by the Condition a person carries, and how the Kept, the people who carry no Condition, are the free generalist labor. It follows the deathless banking houses, whose bankers do not die, and the loop in which a fortune is endowed into an institution rather than inherited. It follows the center of the world and its edge, and the shadow economy that moves beside the official one. The lived official faces of that economy are on [[Tithe-Infrastructure]]. The lived illicit faces are [[The Slide]], [[The Holding Desk]], and [[The Standing Trade]].
- [[Daily Life]] — the ordinary day of that world. Marriage and family run a spectrum, from an ordinary mixed household to the aching Long-Lived marriage you outlive. Medicine in the same note is miraculous and unequally distributed: Stilled surgeons, and the Returned tending the dead. A city is built on a few principles rather than on one map. The Turning Tree stands at the centre, the city Turns by hearth, Tithe-provision is a civic utility, and the watch is on the street.
- [[Polity Archetypes]] — three worked polities, set at different corners of theology, reach, and governance, each one bending the law, the economy, and the daily life above. **Vaethorn**, the Waiting Lands, is a warm, poor, faithful pilgrim edge; **Lestrand**, the Ledger Coast, is a rich, cool, secular merchant power; and **Threnmaieth**, the Tallied Crown, keeps a sacred roll of persons. Each pair of the three shares exactly one of those settings and differs on the other two, and the other twelve powers are on [[Powers of the Turning]].

### Customs and traditions
- [[What the Year Feels Like]] — the ordinary year, told by stance and by faith. One habit in each stance is left in the place that already keeps it.
- [[How the Week Is Kept]] — Turning-Week, the week a town holds its Turning, in a devout square, on a dock, and in a waiting town, and the night a hill-hall does not keep a Leaf-Fall. A Leaf-Fall is the rite at which a child may be Given a Condition.
- Burials, a stranger's bowl, and the hour someone changes are the rituals [[When the Town Buries]], [[The Guest-Meal]], and [[When Someone Is Struck]], and they live in `07 - Religion`. The household stays on [[Daily Life]].

### Languages & Naming
- [[The Old Tongue]] — *Maiethren*, the old liturgical root tongue of the [[Turning Tree|Awakening Tree]], used in this world the way Latin is used in ours. The note keeps a warm, weighty sound, a pronunciation key, a sacred lexicon, and one root that became three daughter drifts: a conserved core, a worn frontier, and an eroded coast. How a polity sounds is how you hear its stance, and the tongue names the fifteen powers.
- [[How a Place Speaks]] — the handles people actually say, and four tones a place can speak in: a devout core, a dock, a waiting town or a live front, and a hill-hall. Kumbaan stays outside the Old Tongue.
- [[Naming in the Turning]] — how a shared institution gets its name, and how that name reveals the polity's stance. The building these notes call a venting-hall is a *gift-hall* in devout Vaethorn, a *release-house* on secular Lestrand, and a *counted hall* in Threnmaieth. The note is the dictionary of institution names.
- [[Naming People in the Turning]] — how a person is named. You are named by the place that raised you. The custom Kinds keep a hearth-register, Kusawe, Sakoa, Gonan, and the Yumboe tongue. The name lists and the collision rules live in that note.

### Conditions (acquired becomings — the "monster" layer)
- All 10 Conditions have their full mechanics on [[Conditions]], in `09 - Creatures`. A Condition is the acquired becoming, the "monster" of an old story, sitting on top of the Kind a person was born.
