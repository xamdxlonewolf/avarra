# 02 - History

This folder is the past of the Turning as people believe it and as clerks wrote it down: the eras, the events, the ways a year is counted, and the people those years still name. The stones people argue over are written on the site notes, not collected here. One summer's dig is on [[The Low Wall]]. That dig did not found a discipline.

## Contents

### Start here
- [[A History of the Turning]] tells the whole history in order, from before anyone counted years to Cut-year 387.
- [[The Library Pages]] names six short pages a party can find on shelves in Rothallo and Lestrand. Each was written by someone in the world, and the note says what each page gets right and what it gets wrong.

### The spine, the lived road, the hinge, and the residues
- [[The Ages of the Turning]] keeps two clocks rather than four ages stacked on one another, says how Cut-years are numbered, and treats the Grafting, the carrying of living wood out from the first Tree, as a spread that is still moving.
- [[Before the Walk]] is the unnamed preface. The Tree was already standing, and no count begins with it.
- [[The Walking Years]] had only Thaeloren, the one Tree, to Turn under. A child walked to that Tree, or stayed UnTurned because the trip was never made. The road was a life of its own: three walks, the inns along them, and the graves.
- [[The Child Who Counted Stones]] is the road's paradox-fable. The note lives in `07 - Religion`.
- [[The First Cut]] is the hinge, and the spread of wood that followed. Who cut is contested. Cutting-leave is the right to copy that wood, captured after people had already begun to cut.
- People named in the years of the Walk and the Cut, and none of them is the cutter: [[Monseoth]] · [[Rithnali]] · [[Sedrad]]
- [[The Branch That Came Away]] is the Cut's paradox-fable. The note lives in `07 - Religion`.
- [[The Years of Hands]] are the years when a town Tree is the ordinary way to Turn. The old walk still has three jobs, which that note names. Heskoren is still the front of the spread, the place the wood has not finished reaching.
- [[The Child Who Climbed the Stone]] is the paradox-fable of what the Walk left behind. The note lives in `07 - Religion`.
- [[The Wrong Green]] is Cut-year 248, when a hug was announced as copper-green at [[Harrow's Green]].
- [[Settlement Seeds]] are the places the old roads left behind. The notes live in `04 - Settlements`.

### The Other Count — dated years that were not wood
- [[The Other Count]] is the chronicle of the dated years that were not the spread of wood. The two clocks still stand beside it.
- The faces on those years are [[Hildal]] · [[Limrae]] · [[Dirrol]] · [[Narol of the Pass]] · [[Taerso]], and their notes live in `08 - People`. [[Historical Figures]] is the pointer in this section, and it does not copy the notes.
- [[The Closing]] runs from Cut-year 19 to Cut-year 38. It is the Closed Seat's war, then the collapse, then the Retreat.
- [[The Two Papers]] is Cut-year 67, the split into Maiethvael and Threnmaieth. The crown-count starts in that year.
- [[The Grey Summer]] is Cut-year 171, a fever on the quays, and the cause behind Trenledd, Brenledd, and Leddvael as they stand now.
- [[The Thaw-Break]] is Cut-year 233, a melt that came in one week.
- [[The Hinge Hush]] is Cut-year 304, the peace that still governs the Old Crossing.
- Three old sites, [[The Low Wall]] · [[The Seeing-Ring]] · [[The Dry Stair]], live in `04 - Settlements`. The Low Wall's opening in Cut-year 381 was a fight over a chip of stone, not a school of diggers. The other two stay as they are. [[Archaeology]] points at the three sites, and it is not a discipline.
