# Before the Walk

> **The unnamed preface.** The [[Turning Tree|Awakening Tree]] was already standing before anyone had a year to set it in, and people already had [[Kinds of the Turning|Kinds]], what each person already was, before any of them *became* something else. Some had already become things at doors the later faith would struggle to place. This stretch of time has no honest start, and the ages that follow it, in the order they come, are the spine in [[The Ages of the Turning]].


## Overview

Most of the world does not have a name for this time. The devout skip it, and skeptics call it "before anyone bothered to walk," which is a shrug, not a chronicle. The one name that is still lived, rather than shrugged off, is kept by a single faith.

[[The Watching]] keep the one lived name: **the Before.** In their telling the world was Kind and Kind alone — what people already were, and not yet anything they became — and then the Tree was already there, *watching*. The Giving, their name for the later story in which people began to be Given, came after the Watching. The Mother-name, the later story that calls the Tree a mother, came after that. Each of those is a later layer, and each layer is a story laid over a seeing. The full doctrine is in [[The Watching]].

[[The Old Ways]] do not grant that becoming waited on a road. Heskoren's people already crossed doors — a drowning, a grove, a stone — before any pilgrim mile. The Walking Years, when they arrived as a foreign traffic, were not the beginning of history. They were the beginning of *company*. The full doctrine is in [[The Old Ways]].

[[The Open Table]] is not part of this history. [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]]'s hill-history does not owe Thaeloren, the Awakening Tree, a preface.

What can be said without inventing kings is only what follows.

The origin Tree is the one Tree no one grafted from another. Nobody grew it from a cutting of some earlier tree, and it was not planted in a remembered summer. Far from it, people lived as they lived. Some were Struck at thresholds because thresholds exist: a door was already there, and a person became something at it, with no journey organized to bring that about. Some were never either Given or Struck, because there was no local fall to miss. Where nothing nearby offered a fall, they were not people who had failed to attend one. When people *organized the tenth-year trip*, and a child's tenth year became a journey households could plan, the Walking Years began. That organization is the start of the walk, not the start of the world.

## Key Events

None of it is dated. That is the point. A later chronicle that "discovers" a first king, a first war, or a first sermon in this era is doing the overlay [[The Watching]] warned about, laying a story that has a beginning over a seeing that was never given one.

The physical remains are uncounted, and a party can still walk into them. [[The Low Wall]], [[The Seeing-Ring]], and [[The Dry Stair]] support archaeology and play through entrances, pressures, and current actors. They do not start a year.

## GM Notes

### Production notes (P2.1)

These lines were moved out of the player-facing body during residual export polish. They stay here so a later draft does not fill the era back in.

> **Canon status.** 🔒 **There is no date.** 🔒 The Tree is old beyond reliable dating ([[Turning Tree]]). 🔒 **Kept thin** — no creation myth, no first king. [[The Watching]] keep **the Before**.

**Do not fill this era in.** [[The Watching]] and [[The Old Ways]] already hold the two memories people can use, seeing without a mother, and doors without a graft. A third account, offered as the true prehistory, would flatten both. This note will not write one.

**Keystone-adjacent, not keystone-confirming.** The Tree standing before it was called Hers is compatible with [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real|she is real]] and with "the Mother-name is later." Do not use this note to decide what she is. The *maternal intent* is locked, and her metaphysical species is not.

**Nature of the limit.** A wound-in-the-deep-past is *one* available cause among several, and Story 6.1 does not pick it. If a later epic wants "she is spent from something Before," that is a taste call, not a smuggled fact.

**The count is not the birthday of Conditions (settled 2026-10-01).** Cut-year 0 is a copy of wood, not the day Conditions began. The Giving, and the organized walk, are older than that shelf and have no start year. Becoming at a door is older than the walk, and Kinds are older than both, so do not build a second official calendar of reliable pre-Cut years. Pages older than the Cut may exist, and they are local and lossy — a grove-leave, a shrine name, a house note — while person-rolls are late. Past the oldest page a living keeper will stand behind, the record blurs into stones, the three physical remains, Kumbaan memory, and Watchers who will not chisel a year. [[Long-Lived]] are Given only: they can remember a walk they witnessed, and they are not a library of centuries from before the Giving. A clean switch at Cut-year 0 — she appeared when the chronicle starts, and before that there were no Conditions — is the overlay this note refuses.

**Named figures.** Put named figures in the Hands and the Other Count, and only if they can survive a Watcher asking "seen by whom?" This era is kept thin on purpose. A name that cannot answer that question does not belong here.

**Do not date the Tree** from the three physical remains. They can be walked into, and they still do not give the Tree a year.
