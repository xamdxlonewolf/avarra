# The First Cut

> **The hinge.** Someone cut living wood from Thaeloren (or from a branch that came away in a hand), carried it, married it to new ground, and it *took*. A town somewhere on [[Maiethorn]] Turned under a canopy that was not the origin. Everyday name: **the First Cut.** Liturgical (🟡): **Eoloren** *(ay-ohl-OR-en)* — "the witnessed hinge." The clerical count of years starts here ([[The Ages of the Turning]]). **Who cut, and how the Seat captured the copy-right, is Story 6.3.** This note holds the event's job in the spine.

> **Canon status.** 🔒 **That it happened, and that every later town Tree descends from a cut, is locked** (graft topology, [[Turning Tree]]). 🔒 **Who did it is not locked.** 🟡 Name and C.Y. 0 as the clerical epoch.

## What Happened (as believed)

Everyone agrees *a first successful town-graft occurred.* No two houses agree whose story that is.

**Folk (the stolen branch).** A parent who would not watch another child stay Kept for want of a trip, or would not bury another child on the road, took wood without leave. Mercy as theft. The later Cutting-leave fee is, in this telling, a fine that forgot it was a fine.

**Clergy (the warden ahead of the college).** A tree-warden cut with a leave that did not yet exist, the wood took, and the First Seat spent a generation arguing before it regularized the practice as rite. The [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hands]] are this story in an office: the people who walk what used to be a scandal.

**Devout (the Tree offered).** A branch came away. No theft, no genius — a Hand giving a Hand. The Mother (or the Tree, if you will not say Mother) *meant* the wood to travel. This is the version the Seat prefers in sermons, because it makes the queue sound like stewardship rather than rationing.

**[[The Watching]].** Grafting is horticulture. People had always been able to cut living wood. The Motherfaith later *named* one cut the First, and started a count, which is what overlays do. The Tree was already seeing. A date does not make a mother.

**[[The Old Ways]].** The land received a guest. No one "cut" in the Seat's sense; a grove took in kin. The Hands are when guests began arriving from the east. Host-rights start here, not a college's warrant.

All five are playable. The spine does not pick a winner. Skeptics grant the horticulture (observable) and deny the conclusion (that a mind chose the travelling). Same split as every keystone-adjacent fact.

## Consequences

- **Clock 2 starts.** The Grafting as a *wave* — wood carried outward, a generation to mature, [[Maiethorn]] first, [[Strandoren]] by sea, [[Heskoren]] last and live, [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] never. Working spread in [[The Ages of the Turning]].
- **Clock 1 flips where the wood has taken.** Turning under a town canopy becomes the default. The pilgrimage remains, with a new job (devotion, prestige, or necessity when a graft fails).
- **The Seat gains a bottleneck.** [[The Tree-Wardens|Cutting-leave]] and the [[The Tree-Wardens|scion-queue]] are how an institution absorbed a heresy and called it horticulture. R2: she did not write the queue.
- **The clerical epoch.** Cut-years begin. Other people do not have to care ([[The Ages of the Turning#How years are numbered (the name-reveals-stance lever, on history itself)|dating reveals stance]]).

## GM Notes (what actually happened)

- **Do not confirm a cutter in this note.** A named first Road-hand, a named parent, a named saint, is Story 6.3 *and* a taste call. A face makes a great-man history; the setting's engine is wood and waiting. If play later needs a tomb, pick then.
- **Keystone:** every scion *is* continuous tissue of the one Tree, so the First Cut really did move a Hand. Player-facing, that is doctrine. The growth of reach with grafts is the quiet argument for a mind; it is not a licence to narrate her standing over the first knife.
- **Nature of the limit stays open.** That she *could* reach further once a Hand stood nearer is already locked as pattern. *Why she could not simply reach without the wood* is still unwritten.
- **Colonial trap.** The devout version ("she meant the wood to travel") is one belief among five. Do not let GM voice agree that the far needed saving. The thing saved, when anything was, was a walk.
