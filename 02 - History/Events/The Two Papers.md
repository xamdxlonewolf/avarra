# The Two Papers

> **A hymn-country that would not share a page.** Twenty-nine summers after the Retreat, the old gift-country of the Sacred Core tried to write its people down and half of it refused. Everyday name: **the Two Papers.** [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] calls it **the year the reckoning stood.** [[Maiethvael]] calls it **the year they would not be named.** It is a succession of *desks*, not of blood. Chronicle: [[The Other Count]].


## What happened (as believed)

After [[The Closing]] the grove had a college beside it and lodging-hearths around it. The wider hymn-country — Tree-dense, gift-talk, no Closed Seat on the lintel — still sang the same hymns. It did not yet have two flags.

A reckoning-desk opened in a river-town that would later be counted as [[Maiethlir]]'s country. **Limrae** *(lim-RAY)* wrote persons because, they said, a gift you cannot find again is a gift you have already failed to tend. The Closed Seat had ranked. Limrae wanted to *keep*. Same ink-hunger, opposite kindness, if you believe the recto.

**Manril** *(MAN-ril)* kept the older proof in the plenty-country that would not take the name: you belong because you were seen to Turn, and you stay whole because your neighbours furnish what you were Given. To write a person is to say the three pillars failed. Manril would not sign the roll. They would not be named to it.

There was no battle worth a banner. There were two desks, two refusals, and a summer after which the same hymn was sung over two papers. Households chose a desk the way they now choose a stance. Some cousins still share a Turning-Week and will not share a clerk.

Threnmaieth dates a **crown-count** from that summer. The second number the Ages promised is this one. A devout clerk may write Cut-year 387 and Crown-year 320 on the same slate. The difference is sixty-seven. That difference *is* the split.

Maiethvael dates nothing of the kind. They will tell you the year is a river-habit.

## What each paper inherited

| | [[Polity Archetypes\|Threnmaieth]] | [[Maiethvael]] |
|---|---|---|
| The hymn | kept | kept |
| The people | written | witnessed, not written |
| The Closed Seat's hunger | care-count (everyone kept, none ranked out for a gift) | refusal (no list at all) |
| The injustice | sanctified surveillance | piety as a social tax |
| The claim | we are the older tending | we are the older proof |

Both claims are true of the speaker. Neither is a blood-dynasty. A later crown that wants a founding-ancestor will try to make Limrae a first king. The river-town knows they were a clerk.

[[Orenbren]] was not in the room. They house. They do not sing this argument. The [[The Walled Book|Inner Close]] watched two lists be born that were not the Book, and filed both as *other people's ink*.

[[The Watching]] already refused the Cut-count. A second count did not tempt them.

## Visibility markers

- Two numbers on a Threnmaieth debt: Eoloren and crown. A Maiethvael neighbour will convert neither.
- Cousins who share a Tree and will not share a clerk — the split household's Hands-era cousin, done with paper instead of a walk.
- Limrae's name on a roll-preface; Manril's name on no roll, repeated in guild-minutes as *the one who would not be named.*
- A Close heir who says, correctly, that Threnmaieth's list is not their Book, and is still insulted when Menirein writes them unmarked.

## In-world text

*A guild-minute from the plenty-country, Manril's summer. Later Threnmaieth clerks file it as Crown-year 0 and will not read the last line aloud. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *Limrae will write the colour and the person in one breath. We will furnish the colour and leave the person to the square that saw them. If this is a slight, it is a slight against a desk, not against the Mother. Do not send their tablets. We are not unmarked. We are unlisted. Those are not the same hunger, and we will not feed theirs.*

Manril cannot afford to see that "we will furnish" is already a bill. Limrae cannot afford to see that "tend" and "find again" are how a river learns a census. Both can hold the same hymn.

## Consequences

- **Maiethorn's hymn-country is two powers.** Same *maieth*, gift vs reckoning — the name-pair [[Powers of the Turning]] already taught. Now it has a year.
- **Threnmaieth's crown-count has a start.** Dating reveals stance *inside* the darkest corner: a clerk who omits the crown-year is doing a quiet Maiethvael.
- **The First Seat stays uncaptured.** Neither paper owns Thaeloren. The college would not be named to a roll either, and would not refuse in Manril's voice.
- **Dynastic flavour without a dynasty.** Play can meet a house that claims Limrae or Manril. The claim is paper-kin, not blood-right, unless a later story wants a tomb.
