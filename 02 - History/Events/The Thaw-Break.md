# The Thaw-Break

> **Snowmelt in one week.** In C.Y. 233 the central range did not thaw as it usually thaws. The water that should have taken a month took seven days. Valleys drank it as flood. Passes lost their old stones. Everyday name: **the Thaw-Break.** Ridge-cant: **the one-week melt.** Chronicle: [[The Other Count]].

> **Canon status.** 🟡 **Lived disaster (Story R.9).** 🔒 **Natural first.** Theology arrives as interpretation. 🔒 **Does not name the range or the rivers** — those wait on [[Roadmap|Story R.10]]. 🔒 **Does not invert Clock 2.** [[Heskoren]] was already on the wave (C.Y. 200–). The flood delayed carts. It did not start the wait.

## What happened (as believed)

[[Lirorn]] lives by water that runs again. Lowlands drink the spring. Ridges notice. In an ordinary Liren the melt is a season: shelves drip, then run, then the high road is mud and then it is a road.

In 233 the high snow went together. Sky-couriers saw the white break in a week. Valley charters saw the rest. A mile-shrine in a thaw-square stood in water to the ribbon-notch. The name on the stone survived. The road to it did not, for two summers.

People died. So did animals, stores, and one well-town on the leeward side whose cistern took silt instead of snowmelt. [[Saelthael]]'s west-road — the leftover walk after the core stopped — was cut. Children who would have gone west in 233 and 234 went later, or went to a neighbour, or missed. The Given-door is still one week. A flood is not a second-chance year.

**Narol of the Pass** *(NAH-rol)* took the thaw-toll when the old pass-holder's stone was under the water-line. Lirorn's mid-state is this fact you can levy: you tax the thaw, the pass, the high road. After the Break, *which* pass was the argument. Narol's house still levies. The valley still drinks. The ridge still pays in isolation. The injustice did not start in 233. The year made it visible as a water-line over a ribbon.

## What the water moved (not a sermon)

**[[Saelthael]] kept walking, two years late.** The core called it soil. The west-road called it mud. [[Ornsael]]'s type — a well-town that still sends — learned that Clock 2 can un-Hands a Motherland square without a sick Tree. Weather is a clock.

**A waited graft in [[Ornled]] died in the pot.** The cart from the hinge-shore was late because the range was a lake. **Vathne** *(VATH-neh)* wrote a slate on the wall: *we will not wait with a hymn for a stick that drowned in someone else's thaw.* Vaethorn heard godlessness. Ornled heard a luxury they had just watched die. The secular frontier is older than one pot — the stub already said they declined to make scarcity a soul-problem — but this is the year a specific cluster can point at. Play that pot. Do not make it the only origin of Ornled.

**[[Saelvaeth]]'s neighbour's week climbed worse mud.** The live front was fifteen years into speaking, in the luckiest squares. A delayed pot is a delayed childhood of wood. The queue did not become fair. The Harrow-sentence had more weather in it.

**[[Vaelhesk]] fed first meals to people the water had moved.** Host-rights do not care what Cut-year a flood is. A guest who arrives dripping is still a guest, or a claiming, depending on whether they sat down. Some Road-hands sat. Some did not.

**[[Threnmaieth]] wanted the high road counted.** A pass that moves is a row that moved without a clerk. Lirorn would not be named as a people. They would name the passes if they could. After 233 they named the new one, and Lirorn charged them for the water anyway.

## What it was not

- Not hers, not a watching, not a bargain, not a Closed Seat. A later preacher who needs a moral will find one. The melt does not owe it.
- Not the reason Heskoren is Tree-poor. Thin reach was already the wave's front.
- Not a Kind-disaster. Tengu shelves and Snows-Fox dens flooded with everyone else. Do not hang ears or wings on the water-line.

## Visibility markers

- A pass-stone with a water-line over the old ribbon-notch. Children rub it for luck. Ridge-folk rub it because the water was real.
- West-road mile-shrines that skip two summers of names and then resume.
- An Ornled slate whose first line is still Vathne's, recopied, the wood of the wall newer than the sentence.
- A Lirorn toll that quotes "Break-rate" in a kind year and will not explain it to a lowlander.
- A first-meal bowl at a high door with silt in the glaze.

## In-world text

*A sky-courier's strip, Lirorn high shelf, the third day of the melt. Later filed as a weather-note. The verso is a list of passes that were roads on the first day and water on the seventh. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *The white is going together. Tell the valley to move the grain up, not the people down. The old stone at the noon-pass will not be a stone by dark. I can see three roofs that were shelves. I cannot see the well-town. If you are a warden, this is not a colour. If you are a clerk, this is not a row. If you are Narol, the toll is going to be a different notch. Water the wood if you can reach it. I am turning back. The air is too heavy to be a road.*

The courier cannot afford to see that "this is not a row" is exactly why Threnmaieth will later want the pass written. Narol cannot afford to see that a different notch is a house. Vathne, far east of this strip, will read a late cart as a gospel they do not owe.

## Consequences

- **387 years contained a flood.** The wave does not get to be the only weather.
- **Lirorn's watershed power has a year.** Tolls that feel like geography feel like 233 if you were on the shelf.
- **Saelthael / Ornled / Saelvaeth inherit delays, not destinies.** Pair the west-road with the live front. Same sowing, two clocks, one melt.
- **Named rivers still wait.** Write *the thaw*, *the pass*, *the well-town*. Do not baptize them here.

## GM Notes

- **Geography is the actor.** If play needs a hazard, it is snowmelt, mud, a stone that was a road. Dangers-layer adversaries stay [[Roadmap|Story R.13]].
- **Narol and Vathne are working mouths** (seed `20260830`). Do not make Narol a mountain-king or Vathne the prophet of secularism.
- **Hooks.** A toll-right dated from the Break; a west-road cohort whose grandparents missed; Vathne's slate used as a charter; a pot-ring in Ornled with no wood in it; Threnmaieth tablets at a pass that moved; a Door-Keeper who sat down in the wet and a Road-hand who did not.
