# The Wrong Green

> **The shadow the rite still carries.** In C.Y. 248, at [[Harrow's Green]], a warden named a hug as copper-green. The child was sent to the garden-common. The green did not take, because there was no Tithe to pay. Speakers still cite the year when they teach a town-hearth not to Speak what the town wishes it had. Full Leaf-Fall procedure: [[Turning Tree#Questions a warden gets asked]].

> **Canon status.** 🟡 **Lived case-law** (the year, the square, the warden's name). 🔒 **Does not change the engine:** the Tree still only Gives or Keeps; a completed standing is once; the colour is the Gift, the Speaking is civic. This is a human wound on a warm rite, not a sinister Tree.

## What happened (as the square tells it)

[[Harrow's Green]] was young. The scion had only spoken a few summers. The neighbour's week already climbed the rise. The greens around the new Hand were thin, and the town wanted a [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] child the way a new mill wants a miller.

**Nathdo of Harrow's** *(NATH-doh)* Speaks that Eolthael. A child stands. The leaves gather. What falls is green. Nathdo says *copper-green* aloud, and the square hears a vocation.

Two summers later a visiting Speaker — sent because a garden-common cannot teach a child who will not root — walks the same boughs, asks who saw the fall, and says the green was the Tree's own. Ordinary summer-green. A hug. One of the [[The Premise|Kept]]. There had never been a Tithe.

Nathdo does not recant the first summer. Recanting a Speaking is a civic tear: guilds opened, a neighbour's week already told, a child already called *green-hand* at the cistern. The Seat letter that arrives in C.Y. 250 does not ask for a second Leaf-Fall. It asks for a second *word*.

The child lives. They are not a ghost story. They are a person who spent two years trying to tend a gift they had not been given, in a town that needed the gift more than it needed to be careful.

## What a warden does with it now

- **The colour is the Gift. The Speaking is the town.** If they part, the Gift does not move. The town does. Training, pity, a ticket, a slight — those move.
- **You may Speak again to correct a harm. You may not stand the child again.** The Given-door is one week. The Wrong Green is why Speakers exist as a civic office, not only a devout one.
- **Copper-green is the easy mistake.** Folk already know not to confuse it with the hug ([[Turning Tree#Reading the colours (colour → Condition)]]). Harrow's is the year someone did it on purpose, or near enough: they named what the square could use.
- **White-fire is the loud cousin.** A warden who names [[Phoenix]] has made history, and a problem. The Wrong Green is the quiet cousin. Same office, smaller town, no legend to hide in.

Vaethorn tells it as pride. Lestrand tells it as a bad ticket. The Watching, if they kept that eve, will say the fall was seen and the name was added. Harrow's tells it as a cistern-year: the pot that would not take, and the child who still drinks there.

## In-world text

*A First Seat correction, carried north in C.Y. 250 with a Road-hand who had other wood to plant. The verso is a packing list. The recto is the whole case. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *Harrow's green, Eolthael 248. The fall was gathered leaves, no naming colour. The Speaking said copper-green. The child was set to the common. The common could not teach them. Do not stand them again. Speak the hug, and let the neighbour's week hear the second word as well as the first. A town may want a green-hand. A Tree does not take instruction from a want. If the soil is thin, write for carrying-earth. Do not write a colour onto a child to thicken it.*

The clerk who wrote it can see horticulture and a queue. They cannot see the two summers. Nathdo kept the letter in the warden-hearth. Haelin still has it. The packing list on the verso is for a pot bound further on.

## GM Notes

- **The floor stays sound.** The Tree hugged. The wound is the name. Do not make the wood eerie, and do not make Nathdo a villain-priest. They wanted a Taken-In for a young square that was already feeding three hamlets' week. The orthogonality: they were in a horticulture story. The child was in a life.
- **Once-only is the ache, not a retry hook.** Correct the Speaking. Do not invent a second Given-door. Per [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]], some hugs are chosen wholeness and some are reach-failure; this child was hugged. Whether that hug was choice or edge is not for the square to know, and not for you to solve with a later leaf.
- **~5% scary.** Two wasted summers, a wrong vocation, a town that has to say a second word. No harvest, no cursed Tree, no child-eating rite. If you need a louder cousin, the white-fire row on [[Turning Tree]] is already a campaign. Do not grow the Protectors here.
- **Haelin inherited the letter.** They want the stone moved because the crowd needs the square. They also Speak more slowly than a front-town has patience for. That is not a second theme. It is why a careful warden looks like a hindrance in Hale-month.
