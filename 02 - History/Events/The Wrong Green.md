# The Wrong Green

> The Wrong Green is the shadow the rite still carries. In Cut-year 248, at [[Harrow's Green]], a warden named a hug as copper-green. The child was sent to the garden-common to learn a Taken-In's work, and the green did not take, because there had been no Tithe to pay and no Gift to tend. Speakers still cite the year when they teach a town's hearth not to Speak, not to name aloud, the thing the town wishes it had seen. The full way a Leaf-Fall is done sits in [[Turning Tree#Questions a warden gets asked]].


## What happened (as the square tells it)

[[Harrow's Green]] was young. The scion, the young tree cut from an older one, had only been speaking for a few summers, and the neighbour's week, the week a town's children spend under someone else's Tree, already climbed the rise to it. The greens around the new Hand were thin. The town wanted a [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] child the way a new mill wants a miller, someone the copper-green would set to the care of living ground.

**Nathdo of Harrow's** *(NATH-doh)* is the one who Speaks that Eolthael, the month the town stands its children. A child stands under the boughs, the leaves gather, and what falls is green. Nathdo says *copper-green* aloud, and the square hears a vocation in the name.

Two summers later a visiting Speaker comes, sent because a garden-common cannot teach a child who will not root in that work. The Speaker walks the same boughs, asks who saw the fall, and says the green was the Tree's own green, ordinary summer-green, a hug, so the child was one of the [[The Premise|Kept]] and there had never been a Tithe.

Nathdo does not take back the first summer. Taking back a Speaking tears the civic life that has already been built on it: guilds opened for the child, a neighbour's week already told the news, a child already called *green-hand* at the cistern. The letter from the Seat that arrives in Cut-year 250 does not ask for a second Leaf-Fall. It asks for a second *word*.

The child lives, and they are not a ghost story. They are a person who spent two years trying to tend a gift they had not been given, in a town that needed the gift more than it needed to be careful.

## What a warden does with it now

The colour is the Gift, and the Speaking is the town. If the two part company, the Gift does not move to match the name, and the town does. Training, pity, a ticket, and a slight are what move, and the leaf does not.

You may Speak again, and give the second word, to correct a harm. You may not stand the child under the Tree again. The Given-door is one week, and it does not reopen. The Wrong Green is why Speakers exist as a civic office, a town's correction, and not only as a devout one.

Copper-green is the easy mistake, and folk already know not to confuse that colour with the hug ([[Turning Tree#Reading the colours (colour → Condition)]]). Harrow's is the year someone did confuse them on purpose, or near enough to purpose. They named the thing the square could use.

White-fire is the loud cousin of the same office. A warden who names [[Phoenix]] has made history, and has made a problem. The Wrong Green is the quiet cousin. It is the same office in a smaller town, with no legend to hide the mistake in.

Vaethorn tells the year as pride. Lestrand tells it as a bad ticket. The Watching, if they kept that evening, will say the fall was seen and the name was added afterward. Harrow's tells it as a cistern-year: the green that would not take, and the child who still drinks there.

## In-world text

*This is a correction from the First Seat, carried north in Cut-year 250 with a Road-hand who had other wood to plant. The back of the page is a packing list. The front of the page is the whole case.*

> *Harrow's green, Eolthael 248. The fall was gathered leaves, no naming colour. The Speaking said copper-green. The child was set to the common. The common could not teach them. Do not stand them again. Speak the hug, and let the neighbour's week hear the second word as well as the first. A town may want a green-hand. A Tree does not take instruction from a want. If the soil is thin, write for carrying-earth. Do not write a colour onto a child to thicken it.*

The clerk who wrote the letter can see horticulture, and can see a queue. They cannot see the two summers. Nathdo kept the letter in the warden-hearth, and Haelin still has it. The packing list on the back is for a pot bound further on.

## GM Notes

### Production notes (P2.1)

Moved from the player-facing body during residual export polish:

- > **Canon status.** 🟡 **Lived case-law** (the year, the square, the warden's name). 🔒 **Does not change the engine:** the Tree still only Gives or Keeps; a completed standing is once; the colour is the Gift, the Speaking is civic. This is a human wound on a warm rite, not a sinister Tree.

The floor stays sound. The Tree hugged, and the wound is the name that was put on the hug. Do not make the wood eerie, and do not make Nathdo a villain-priest. They wanted a Taken-In for a young square that was already feeding the week of three hamlets. Nathdo was in a story about horticulture, and the child was in a life.

Once is the ache, and it is not a hook for a retry. Correct the Speaking. Do not invent a second Given-door. As [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]] has it, some hugs are a chosen wholeness and some are a failure of reach, and this child was hugged. Whether that particular hug was choice or edge is not for the square to know, and it is not for you to solve by producing a later leaf.

Keep it ~5% scary. What the year costs is two wasted summers, a wrong vocation, and a town that has to say a second word. There is no harvest, no cursed Tree, and no rite that eats children. If you need a louder cousin, the white-fire row on [[Turning Tree]] is already a campaign. Do not grow the Protectors here.

Haelin inherited the letter. They want the stone moved because the crowd needs the square, and they also Speak more slowly than a town on the front has patience for. That slowness is not a second theme. It is why a careful warden looks like a hindrance when Hale-month comes.
