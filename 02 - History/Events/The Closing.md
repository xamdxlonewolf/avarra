# The Closing

> **The war that followed the copy.** Someone had already cut living wood. A Near-Mile town had already Turned under a canopy that was not Thaeloren. The class that sat on the grove tried to make that untrue. They could not. Everyday name: **the Closing** — they closed the walls. Folk: **the Uprooting.** The remnant still says **the Retreat.** The people who sat the grove: **the Closed Seat** (folk **Grove-Sitters**). The hinge in the wood remains [[The First Cut]]. Who held the knife is still unwritten. Chronicle: [[The Other Count]].

> **Canon status.** 🟡 **Lived war (Story R.9).** 🔒 **Does not name the cutter.** 🔒 **Does not add a sixteenth power.** The Inner Close stays a town inside [[Orenbren]] (**placement 🔒 Story R.10** — not a shore-flag). 🔒 **The Closed Seat was an origin-gate, not a world-empire.**

## What happened (as believed)

In the [[The Walking Years]] the origin was a gate. The Closed Seat — untithed houses, a wall-right older than any Cutting-leave — decided who might stand under Thaeloren. A family could have shoes and a note and still be turned at the lintel. Distance was not the only sorter. **Leave** was.

C.Y. 0 is a wound in wood. C.Y. 19 is a ceremony the gate did not authorize: the nameless green held a Turning-Week, and a town that was not the origin was *seen seeing* ([[The First Cut#The generation of argument (how a heresy grew a desk)|the generation of argument]]). Clock 1 flipped for that hearth. The Closed Seat's monopoly was no longer a fact. It was a claim.

They did what a gate does when the road grows a second door. They tried to **uproot**.

They rode a day's earth with carrying-sacks and a sentence: the copy is a theft of the one seeing; bring the wood home or burn the pot. Some pots died. Some towns hid a slip in a well. Some lodging-hearths — the squares that would become [[Orenbren]] — would not starve their own beds to save a lintel. The early [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]] wanted *authorization*, not ash. Those are three different refusals. Together they are why an origin-empire cannot police a day's walk in a pre-print world.

By C.Y. 38 the sitting was over. **Hildal** *(HIL-dal)* — last mouth of the Closed Seat that anyone will still name — ordered the Retreat. They did not save the grove. They saved a **book**. The walls they closed are the [[The Walled Book|Inner Close]]. The grove stayed. The college sat *beside* it and was not a throne. The beds stayed civic.

No one agrees which pot was the first they failed to pull. Naming that town would pick a hearth, and a hearth would pick a hand. History does not.

## The Closed Seat (what they thought they were)

They were not a dark lord and not a first king. They were a class that had grown a country around a job: **the one Tree cannot stand a world at once.** Someone must choose the road. A hug you never received because you were not let through is a Kept child with a different cause than want-of-a-trip. Both causes filled the far lands. [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]]'s later guilt wants the denied to have been unworthy. The songs do not agree.

Lived:

- **Grove-leave** was paper you carried to the lintel. Salt-Walk wealth could buy a berth and still fail the gate. Long-Mile columns learned to arrive in numbers the gate could not turn without a riot, and still some were turned.
- **The untithed sat.** That is folk memory and it is also how the remnant still ranks. A Given Closed child loses the heir-line *now* ([[The Walled Book]]). That rule is older than the Retreat; the war made it a wall instead of a grove.
- **They fought the copy, not the knife.** A named cutter would have been a gift. They never received one. The five attributions stayed five. The Closed Seat's sermons called the nameless green a blight. The folk called the riders thieves of a theft.

What this mouth cannot afford to see: that rationing the one seeing is the same hunger as rationing the next scion. The sentence changed owner. The hunger did not.

## Who would not help them

| Mouth | What they did | What they were not doing |
|---|---|---|
| Near-Mile towns that had planted | Hid slips. Watered at night. Sang thief-songs with *we* in them. | A rebel army with a banner. Horticulture under a rider. |
| Lodging-hearths | Kept the extra beds open. Refused to starve a pilgrim-season to please a lintel. | A new crown. They federated later, as [[Orenbren]]. |
| The origin-college | Forbade unsanctioned cuts *and* forbade burning a Hand that had already spoken. | The Closed Seat's clergy. The Seat captured *how*, not *who*, and not the grove. |
| Salt-Walk houses | Kept writing notes. A war at the origin is a delay, not a theology. | Provinces in revolt. They had never been a province. |
| Far hearths on the Long Mile | Walked anyway, or did not. The gate's collapse did not shorten the road. | Liberated subjects. The Closed Seat had never held them. |

[[The Watching]] grafted pears and refused to start a war-year. [[The Old Ways]] were not in this fight; the guests had not yet reached [[Heskoren]]. [[The Fair Hand]] filed cargo. [[The Open Table]] owes the Closing nothing.

## Four monopolies from one fall

The Closed Seat ended as a territorial sitting. The job split. Do not narrate this as freedom.

| What they had held | Who holds the piece now |
|---|---|
| Who may *stand under the origin* | No one, as a gate. The extra mile is devotion, prestige, or necessity ([[The Years of Hands]]). [[Orenbren]] houses it. |
| Who may *cut next* | [[The Tree-Wardens\|Cutting-leave]] and the scion-queue. The college absorbed a heresy and called it horticulture. |
| Who *slept near the grove* | [[Orenbren]]'s lodging-compact. Hospitality as rent. |
| Who *owes* besides the Tithe they already carry | The [[The Walled Book\|Book of Tithes]], inside the walls. Classification. Not a church. |

[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]]'s list is a later paper ([[The Two Papers]], C.Y. 67). It is not the Closing's child in a straight line. It is the same hunger in a hymn-country that decided reckoning was care.

`world-fates`: this is a fall already in the past. Peak sitting → overextension (you cannot uproot a day's walk in every direction) → remnant. Do not collapse the Close, the Seat, or Orenbren here.

A [[Long-Lived]] who walked as a child in C.Y. 10 and a Closed Heir who keeps Hildal's last page will fight about the same summer. Memory is perfect. Meaning is not.

## Visibility markers

- A Near-Mile kitchen-knife on a ribbon *and* a scorch-mark on an old pot-ring in the same house.
- The Inner Close's stone is one colour. [[The Low Wall]] is another. People who say they are the same wall are doing a claim.
- Hildal's name in the Book, and nowhere on a grove-stone. The college will not write it. The Close will not loan the page.
- A Salt-Walk house-year that skips 19–38 as "origin delay" and still collects.
- A child in Eolvaeth told that the old untithed sat on the grove — true as folk, incomplete as a map.

## In-world text

*Hildal's last leaf, Inner Close, filed as the Retreat. Later clerks of the Book keep it under the heir-roll. The verso is a list of pots they failed to bring home. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *We will not write the knife. We will write the house. The copy has spoken and we cannot unspeak a colour. Carry the Book behind the wall. Rank who owes. The grove will keep its college; the college was never ours to sit. A gift is a gift. A gift is not a seat. If the next fall is a colour, strike heir. We have always struck heir. We are only closing the door we used to open.*

Hildal cannot afford to see that "we have always struck heir" is the sitting, continued. Folk who keep the nameless knife think this page is a confession. The Close thinks it is the same housekeeping as Talnin's line. Both can hold the same copy.

## Consequences

- **Clock 1 is no longer a gate's to grant.** Local witness is born as weather in the squares that kept their wood. The walk remains, with new jobs.
- **[[Orenbren]] federates from hearths that would not starve.** The guest-compact with the Close starts here: beds outside, Book inside. They share a country. They do not share a page.
- **The First Seat does not capture the grove as a throne.** It captures the copy-right. That was already traced. The Closing is why a college could sit *beside* a sitting that had left.
- **Maiethorn's later six are successors, not ancient kingdoms.** Gift, reckoning, hinge, thaw, dry sowing, and lodging inherit a wreck. [[The Two Papers]] splits the hymn-country twenty-nine summers later.
- **R2, in the past tense.** Spreading Trees did not make the origin kind. They relocated who decides the next door.

## GM Notes (what actually happened)

- **Do not name the cutter to explain the war.** The Closed Seat never learned a face. Play that ignorance. A tomb for a cutter is still a later taste call ([[The First Cut]]).
- **Hildal is a clerk, not a villain-king.** Seed `20260830`. They chose a wall over a grove they could no longer hold. The injustice is the Book, then and now.
- **Not a world-empire.** Strandoren paid; Heskoren waited; Kumbaan is off the clocks. A campaign that "liberates the continents from the Grove-Sitters" is writing the wrong map.
- **Colonial trap, again.** Do not let GM voice agree that the Closed Seat *deserved* to sit, or that the copies *civilized* the origin. The thing saved, when anything was, was a walk some children could now skip — and a rank some children still cannot.
- **The Low Wall is not the Close.** Different stone, uncounted. The Close will claim it. Watchers will not date it. Let the party walk both.
- **Hooks.** Hildal's verso of failed pots; a Near-Mile slip that survived a rider; a Salt-Walk note that still charges "origin delay"; Threnmaieth clerks at a gate that was a Retreat, not a census; a pilgrim ranked as labour because the Book does not know their hug.
