# The Ages of the Turning

> **What this is.** How **The Turning** divides the past — the setting’s era spine. This is not a chronicle of kings and not a four-age cosmology dump. The starting fact is this: once there was only the [[Turning Tree|Awakening Tree]], and a child's tenth-year Turning meant a *walk* to it; then grafts travelled, and the devout say the Mother's reach travelled with them. This note names the ages, how years are counted, and what that fact *did* to law, money, language, and the map. Life on the road → [[The Walking Years]]. The hinge event and the spread → [[The First Cut]]. The present as leftover → [[The Years of Hands]] · [[Settlement Seeds]]. Dated years that were not wood → [[The Other Count]]. The whole history told in order, in one page → [[A History of the Turning]].


## The organizing idea: two clocks, not four stacked ages

People in this world do not live in "the Fourth Age." They keep **two overlapping clocks**, and which one they glance at tells you where they stand.

**Clock 1 — How you Turned** (personal, generational). Did you walk to [[Maiethorn|Thaeloren]] (or to a neighbour who had already walked), or did you stand under a town graft? Ordinary families date themselves this way: *we are a Hands-house; grandmother still talks of the road.* A [[Long-Lived]] who says *"I walked"* is saying when they were young.

**Clock 2 — Where the wood has reached** (geographic, unfinished). The **Grafting** is not a closed golden age. It is a *wave of living wood* that started at the origin, filled [[Maiethorn]], crossed to [[Strandoren]] by sea, and is **still arriving** on [[Heskoren]]. [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] is off the wave entirely. Present-day thin reach is not "history's scar." It is the front of a process that has not finished.

Both clocks sit on an unnamed preface: [[Before the Walk]]. The Tree was already there. No honest count starts it.

> **What this is not.** It is not a civilizing mission and not a golden age of pilgrimage that grafts then "improved." The Walking Years were a *logistics problem that killed children on the road.* The Grafting was *horticulture that people later sanctified.* The Seat now tells both as the Mother's will travelling. Folk memory is less tidy, and holds the romance and the graves in the same sentence.

## The ages at a glance

| | Everyday | Liturgical | Says | What it *is* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Preface | **[[Before the Walk]]** | *(mostly unnamed)* · [[The Watching]] keep **the Before** | — | The Tree already stood. Kinds, doors, and seeing without a mother's name. Uncounted on purpose. |
| Clock 1, old default | **[[The Walking Years]]** | **Brenvaeth** *(BREN-vaeth)* | "the hearth gone to waiting" | You walked to Thaeloren or you stayed [[Law and Citizenship\|Kept]] for want of the trip. Far lands ran heavy on the Kept and the [[The Premise\|Struck]]. |
| The hinge | **[[The First Cut]]** | **Eoloren** *(EOL-or-en)* | "the witnessed hinge" | Someone cut living wood and it *took*. Who, is argued. That it happened, is not. |
| Clock 2 | **the Grafting** | *(process, not an age you exit)* | — | The carrying of hands. Still unfinished at the [[Heskoren\|Sundered Reach]]. Never crossed the storm-wall. |
| Clock 1, new default | **[[The Years of Hands]]** | **Ornthael** *(ORN-thael)* | "Tree-land" | A town Tree is the ordinary way to Turn. Pilgrimage remains — as devotion, prestige, or necessity where a graft has failed. |

The liturgical pair is a deliberate inversion of the [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hands']] own name. **[[The Old Tongue|Thaelvaeth]]** is "the Tree gone to the waiting" — wood carried *out*. **Brenvaeth** is "the hearth gone to the waiting" — people walking *in*. The Grafting is the moment the world stopped sending hearths to the Tree and started sending the Tree to hearths.

## How years are numbered (the name-reveals-stance lever, on history itself)

There is **no universal year-zero.** Same twelve months; three new-year's days ([[The Reckoning of the Year]]). Same past; **seven honest ways to count it.** The number you write on a debt is a theology.

| Who | What they count from | What it sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| [[The Tree-Wardens\|First Seat]] / devout clerks | **the First Cut** — **Cut-years** (liturg. the *Eoloren-count*) | "the 387th Cut-year" |
| [[Polity Archetypes\|Threnmaieth]] | Cut-years *and* a **crown-count** from [[The Two Papers]] (Cut-year 67; present **Crown-year 320**) | a number that wants to be a roll |
| [[Polity Archetypes\|Lestrand]] / [[Economy and the Tithe\|deathless houses]] | a **house-founding**, a note-issue, a contract-term | "the 307th summer of the White Note" (present); the archival conversion is the **200th** (Ledan's query, Cut-year 280) |
| [[Polity Archetypes\|Vaethorn]] | **the year our graft took** — or the last *actual* Turning-Week | a child "turns the year" when the local Tree last managed a fall (already how they start the calendar) |
| [[The Watching]] | they **refuse the Cut-count** | the overlay is not an epoch; the Tree was already seeing |
| [[The Old Ways]] | a closed door, a drowning-year, a guest that took | the Hands are when *guests* arrived, not when history began |
| [[The Sundering Isle\|Kumbaan]] | **moons** | the mainland ages are a sailor's chart they do not owe |

**Present:** **Cut-year 387** — the 387th summer since the First Cut.

**What nobody can date.** The [[Turning Tree|Awakening Tree]] is **older than any reliable dating.** [[Before the Walk]] has no start. Who made the First Cut is contested ([[The First Cut]]). Believers do not agree on what limits [[The Leaf-Mother|the Leaf-Mother]], and skeptics say there is no Leaf-Mother.

**Cut-year 0 is a graft.** It is the first witnessed copy of living wood. It is not the first Condition, not the first Giving, and not the Tree's birthday. The Giving and the walk sit in [[Before the Walk]] and [[The Walking Years]] with no start year on this shelf. Becoming at a door is older than the walk. Being born a Kind is older than both.

### Spread of the wave

Enough to keep geography honest. Not a campaign almanac.

| Cut-years | Where the wood went |
|---|---|
| **Cut-year 0** | [[The First Cut]] — near Thaeloren, [[Maiethorn]] |
| **Cut-year 0–80** | Maiethorn filling. Origin-towns first; the Rain-Shadow slower. |
| **Cut-year 40–160** | [[Strandoren]] by sea — wealthy coasts first, deep interior later. |
| **Cut-year 200–387** | [[Heskoren]] — still incomplete. Luckiest towns have had a local Turning for a few generations; whole districts still wait. |
| **never** | [[The Sundering Isle\|Kumbaan]] |

A freshly planted scion still takes **a generation** to hold a Leaf-Fall ([[Turning Tree]]). So "a graft arrived" and "this town Turns under its own canopy" are twenty years apart. Heskoren's waiting is not only the queue. It is the queue *plus the childhood of the wood.*

## What the two clocks did (spine consequences)

These are the lasting effects of the two clocks on law, money, clergy, language, and the map.

**Law.** [[Law and Citizenship|Citizenship-by-local-witness]] is a **Hands-era default.** In the Walking Years the town that *saw you Turn* was the crowd under Thaeloren — or no one, if you never arrived. Far children who stayed Kept were not "unmarked on a roll"; they were **unwitnessed as Given because they did not make the trip.** Vouching-at-a-distance (a [[Long-Lived]] who remembers the origin-fall, a [[Bound]] word, a [[Far-Voiced]] feeling) was invented to *carry the origin's witness home.* The unvouched Struck are older than the grafts: a becoming on a far threshold was always unwitnessed. The Grafting did not invent that unease. It *localized* who is expected to have been seen.

**Money.** Many [[Economy and the Tithe|deathless houses]] began as **road-houses** — inns, endowments, and century-credit for families who had to walk. When the walk stopped being required in the core, the houses still had Long-Lived memory of every debtor on the road, and paper that outlived the pilgrimage-season. They became banks. The endow-not-inherit loop is older than the Hands; the *trusted note* is a Walking-era instrument that outlived its road. The same houses survive with a different business, and present banking is old.

**Clergy.** [[The Tree-Wardens|Cutting-leave]] is how the First Seat **captured a practice that began without it.** The first successful cut was argued as theft, as mercy, as the Tree offering a branch, as old horticulture the Motherfaith later named. The Seat won the *authorization*. The [[The Tree-Wardens|scion-queue]] is the Grafting's child — the bottleneck that appears when someone decides *which town next.*

**Language.** The pilgrimage was a **conserving pull.** Once a year the far-flung walked to the same grove and heard the same liturgy, so [[The Old Tongue]] stayed nearer itself along the road. Local Trees let daughters grow *in place.* This is why drift tracks Tree-thinness *and* why [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] eroded so far so fast: trade, a local canopy, and no need to walk to the Motherwood. The three daughter-drifts are Hands-era weather sitting on a Walking-era root.

**Geography.** The [[The World Frame|reach-gradient]] *is* the wave, frozen at Cut-year 387. [[Maiethorn]] is old Hands. [[Strandoren]] is mature Hands with a commercial aftertaste. [[Heskoren]] is the Grafting *this decade.* [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] never entered the clocks. In the world this reads as distance and history, true as far as it goes.

**Faith.** [[The Watching]] remember [[Before the Walk]] as *the Before* — seeing without a mother's name — and will not start history at a Cut. [[The Old Ways]] were already becoming before any pilgrim road; the Hands are when *guests* arrived late. [[The Fair Hand]] dates contracts from a signing, not from a wound in wood; the Grafting is a supply-chain fact. [[The Open Table]] owes the ages nothing.

## Visibility markers (what a character notices)

- A [[Long-Lived]] who says *I walked*, and a room that goes quiet — Clock 1, spoken as biography.
- A **mile-shrine** on a road that now ends at a town Tree: a stone for a Turning that no longer has to happen there. The devout still leave a ribbon. The town children climb it.
- **Empty pilgrim-beds** in an old inn's upper room, let now to merchants. The deathless house that owns the inn still dates its notes from the founding-summer the beds were full.
- Three dates for one summer, written on one debt: a Cut-year, a house-year, a "year the graft took." A fourth if the note survived [[The Grey Summer]]. See the clerk's query below — it is **archival** (Cut-year 280), and Ledan still writes for the house.
- A [[Heskoren]] hamlet whose ten-year-olds still walk to a neighbour. They are not doing history. They are doing *this year.*
- A [[Phoenix]] named as an era-marker — *the Phoenix of the last Walking summer* — without anyone agreeing which summer that was.

Ledan is [[Long-Lived]]; Mataero, in Cut-year 387, is still junior to Ledan. The verso has been folded around a second number.*

>
> *The house writes a century-loan from our founding-summer, which we call the 200th of the White Note. The debtor in Wetorn writes the same summer as the year their graft took. Your college will write it as the 280th Cut-year if I have the conversion right, and as the 279th if I have it wrong.*
>
> *Which number should stand on the note so that in fifty years the same person still owes us? We do not ask which summer was holy. We ask which summer a court will still be able to find.*

In Cut-year 387 the house is in its **307th** summer, and Ledan still asks which number a court can find.

## What this history does not say

- **Who made the First Cut.** Each telling has its own story: folk, clergy, the devout, the Watching, and the Old Ways ([[The First Cut]]). This note picks none.
- **When the Tree first stood.** Nobody has counted it. [[The Watching]] will fight any date.

## GM Notes

### What the player body leaves out

- **The nature of the Leaf-Mother's limit.** It is locked that she exists and that her reach has a limit ([[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]]). What the limit is stays open. That her reach grew with the grafts is the quiet evidence of a hand. It does not explain the wound, the finiteness, or the rationing. Do not put a cause into the history.
- **Where the ages came from.** The two-clock spine, the unnamed preface, and the names *Brenvaeth*, *Eoloren*, and *Ornthael* were locked on 2026-08-24, with the present at Cut-year 387. The spread (Maiethorn first, Strandoren by sea, Heskoren last and still going, Kumbaan never) was locked the same day. Still open: who made the First Cut, and what limits the Leaf-Mother.
- **Pick the cutter only if play needs a tomb.** The spine does not pick a face.
- **Do not date the Tree, and do not slide the present year.** Cut-year 387 stays the present.
- **The clerk's query.** Ledan cannot see that the three numbers are three theologies. The Speaker who answers, if they answer, will name a wood. The Vaethorn debtor will name a taking. The loan still comes due. The unanswered query is still on the desk in Cut-year 387.
- **Reading the consequences.** The law, money, clergy, language, and geography paragraphs were traced from the two clocks with `systemic-worldbuilding`. The three daughter-drifts of the Old Tongue are Hands-era weather on a Walking-era root. Deep grammar is not decided.
- **Reformations already in the past.** Road-houses became deathless banks. Unsanctioned cutting became Cutting-leave and the queue. Required pilgrimage became optional. The Closed Seat became the Inner Close. The hymn-country became two papers. Quay-fever became Trenledd's roll and Brenledd's compact. The Hinge War became the Hush-rate. Present-day fate-pressure is in [[The Years of Hands]] (noted, not rolled). Do not collapse any of these without a later call.
- **A creation myth, a first king, a war of the ages.** None is written. The Closed Seat was a gate that grew a country, not a first king ([[The Closing]]). Dated non-Tree events are in [[The Other Count]] and do not become a fourth age.

### The two clocks protect the keystone without confirming it

- Clock 2 (the wave) is the player-facing face of "reach grew with the grafts," the quiet argument that a hand and not a climate is behind the thin reach. Skeptics still win the ordinary reading: young Trees, sick Trees, few grafts, distance, history. Both readings share the same map. Do not let a player note assert the mind.
- **The Grafting is not a redemption arc.** Local Trees changed who gets a local witness and who waits on a queue. The Seat's phrase *the Mother's will travelling* is a claim about the wood, not a verdict on the list.
- **Do not run the colonial version.** "We brought Trees to the benighted far" is the Seat's sermon after the fact and Lestrand's supply-chain story. What happened is shorter and worse: children died on the road, someone cut a branch, it took, people copied it, and the Seat claimed the right to authorize copies. [[Heskoren]] still lives the wave as waiting, not as being given civilization. [[The Old Ways]] live it as guests arriving late. Keep all three.
- **Long-Lived memory is the era-marker the calendar refused to be.** At Cut-year 387, many Long-Lived remember the late Walking Years as their own lives. They do not agree on what it meant. A Long-Lived who walked as a child and a Seat chronicle that starts at the Cut will fight about the same summer. That fight is play. Do not give the table "the" Long-Lived account.
- **Kumbaan is the control.** If a later note finds an old graft on the Isle, it breaks the isolation and the Open Table. Road-hands do not bring the Mother across the storm-wall.
- **Hooks.** A debt with three dates, or four if the note survived the Grey Summer. A Long-Lived who walked, and a town that wants that memory as citizenship. A mile-shrine a Hands-town wants to pull down. A Speaker asked to convert a house-year to a Cut-year for a court. A Heskoren cohort still walking while a neighbouring canopy is one summer from its first fall. Ledan's unanswered Cut-year 280 query.
