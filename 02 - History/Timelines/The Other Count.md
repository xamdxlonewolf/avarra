# The Other Count

> **What this is.** The dated years inside C.Y. 0–387 that are **not** the spread of grafts. Two clocks still organize how you Turned and where the wood has reached ([[The Ages of the Turning]]). This note is the proof that three hundred and eighty-seven summers also held a war, a split of paper, a fever, a flood, and a hush that is still the Old Crossing's peace.


## The two clocks still stand

People still date a life by whether they walked or stood under a town Hand. Geography is still a wave: [[Maiethorn]] old Hands, [[Strandoren]] mature Hands, [[Heskoren]] the front, [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] never.

The Other Count does not replace that. It sits *beside* it. A clerk who only writes Eoloren-years can still be asked what the Grey Summer did to a note. A Vaethorn hearth that dates from "the year our graft took" can still owe a Hinge-Hush hull-fee. Dating still reveals stance. The years just have more than wood in them.

**What you still may not date.** The [[Turning Tree|Awakening Tree]]. [[Before the Walk]] has no start. The three pre-Walk leftovers ([[The Low Wall]] · [[The Seeing-Ring]] · [[The Dry Stair]]) are *found*, not founded. A chronicle that writes a first king onto the Before is doing the overlay [[The Watching]] warned about.

## What the Closed Seat was (one paragraph, then the war)

In the [[The Walking Years]] the origin was not an open grove. A class of the untithed **sat on Thaeloren** and decided who might walk to it. Everyday historical name: **the Closed Seat.** Folk: **the Grove-Sitters.** They were an *origin-empire* — a gate that grew a country around the one seeing — not a flag on all three continents. [[Strandoren]] paid the Salt Walk; it was never a province. [[Heskoren]] was beyond a gate's ride. [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] was not in the story.

They thought they were doing horticulture and mercy: the one Tree cannot stand a world at once, so someone must choose the road. That is the same hunger the [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]] later named Cutting-leave, and the same hunger the Inner Close still files as rank. Four monopolies grew from one fall. The war is [[The Closing]]. Who held the nameless knife is still unwritten.

## How the fifteen inherited a map

Full table: [[Powers of the Turning#What they remember (the Other Count)]]. The short inheritance:

- **[[Maiethorn]]** is post-imperial lodging, gift, reckoning, hinge, thaw, and dry sowing — six successors of a grove-gate, not six ancient kingdoms.
- **[[Strandoren]]** is what the Salt Walk became when the gate died and the fever wrote new paper: a premier coast, a compact that refused one house, a signing-coast, a worn count, a night shore.
- **[[Heskoren]]** entered the clocks late. Host-rights were already there. Waiting, sowing, and slate are three answers to the same late cart, not three eternal races.

Borders, guest-compacts, crown-counts, and hull-clauses have *causes* now. They do not have a first king.

- A Watching night on a ring of stones that is older than any Cut-year anyone will write.

## In-world text

*A conversion-table, unsigned, filed in the White Note's founding-book after Ledan's query. The recto is Eoloren-years. The verso is house-years. A third hand has added, in dock-ink, "Grey + 1" beside 172. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *280 = White Note 200. 304 = the Hush, do not discount. 171 is not a Cut we honor and is not a founding. If the debtor's grandparent died in the Grey, the note still stands. If the debtor's town dates from a taking, ask which taking. Do not ask the Close for a year. They will rank you.*

The clerk cannot afford to see that "171 is not a Cut we honor" is how a fever becomes a hole in a theology. The Close cannot afford to see that a year they will not give is still a year a court can find.

## What this pass does not lock

- **Who cut.** Five attributions stand ([[The First Cut]]). The Closing is about the *copy*, not the knife.
- **The nature of her limit.** A war over access is people. It is not a wound-theory.
- **A sixteenth power.** The Inner Close is a town inside [[Orenbren]].
The three sites remain undated.
