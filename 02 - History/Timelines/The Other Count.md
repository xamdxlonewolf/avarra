# The Other Count

The years on this page are the dated ones inside Cut-year 0 to Cut-year 387 that are not the spread of grafts. Two clocks still organize that past, and [[The Ages of the Turning]] is where the clocks are set out. One counts how you were Turned, by a walk or under a town Tree. The other counts where the wood has reached. This note is the proof that those three hundred and eighty-seven summers also held a war, a split over paper, a fever, a flood, and a hush that is still the peace on the Old Crossing.

## The two clocks still stand

People still date a life by whether they walked to the Tree or stood under a town Hand, a town Tree grown from a cutting. The map is still that spread, as far as the wood has reached. [[Maiethorn]] is old in the Years of Hands, [[Strandoren]] is mature in them, [[Heskoren]] is the front where the wood is still arriving, and [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] was never on the spread at all.

The Other Count does not take the place of those clocks. It sits beside them. A clerk who writes only Eoloren-years, the count that runs from the First Cut, can still be asked what the Grey Summer did to a note. A hearth in Vaethorn that dates itself from "the year our graft took" can still owe a hull-fee under the Hinge Hush. The year someone writes down still shows what they are willing to start from. The years simply hold more than wood.

What stays without a date is the [[Turning Tree|Awakening Tree]]. [[Before the Walk]] has no year it begins. The three places from before that walk, [[The Low Wall]], [[The Seeing-Ring]], and [[The Dry Stair]], were found, not founded, and this chronicle does not give them a year. A chronicle that writes a first king onto the Before is doing the overlay [[The Watching]] warned about, which is a later story laid on a time that was left uncounted.

## What the Closed Seat was (one paragraph, then the war)

In [[The Walking Years]] the grove at the origin was not open to everyone who arrived. A class of the untithed, people of whom no Tithe was asked, sat on Thaeloren, the Awakening Tree, and decided who might walk up to it. The everyday historical name is the Closed Seat, and in ordinary speech they are the Grove-Sitters. They were a gate that grew a country around the one Tree, and not a banner planted on all three continents. [[Strandoren]] paid for the Salt Walk and was never a province of that gate. [[Heskoren]] lay beyond a ride from that gate, and [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] was not in the story.

They believed they were doing horticulture and mercy. One Tree, as they told it, cannot bear the whole world coming at once, so someone has to choose who takes the road. That is the same wish to ration the Tree that the [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]] later named Cutting-leave, and the same wish the Inner Close still files as rank. Four monopolies grew from that one fall, and the war that followed is [[The Closing]]. Who held the nameless knife, the person who made the first cut, is still unwritten.

## How the fifteen inherited a map

The full table of what the fifteen powers remember is on [[Powers of the Turning#What they remember (the Other Count)]]. What follows is the short inheritance, and it is how the present map got its causes.

[[Maiethorn]] is what followed the grove-gate: lodging, gift, reckoning, hinge, thaw, and dry sowing. Those are six successors of one gate, not six ancient kingdoms. [[Strandoren]] is what the Salt Walk became once the gate was dead and the fever had made people write new paper. It left a premier coast, a compact that refused one house, a signing-coast, a worn count, and a night shore. [[Heskoren]] came into the clocks late, and host-rights were already there. Waiting, sowing, and slate are three answers to the same cart that arrived late, not three eternal races.

Borders, guest-compacts, crown-counts, and hull-clauses have causes now. They do not have a first king. [[Rothallo]], still called the Inner Close, is a walled city inside [[Orenbren]], and it is Orenbren's capital. It is not a sixteenth power.

## Visibility markers

- A kitchen that still will not send a child through the Inner Close's gate, and cannot say whether the fear is the Book or the older Closed Seat.
- In [[Lirorn]], a stone on the pass has a water-line over the old ribbon-notch, above the mark where a ribbon used to be tied.
- A quay on the Hinge Shore charges the Hush-rate and will not call it a victory.
- [[The Watching]] keep a night on a ring of stones that is older than any Cut-year anyone will write down.

### Production notes (P2.1)

The dated years inside Cut-year 0–387 are not the spread of grafts. Those three hundred and eighty-seven summers are the span [[The Ages of the Turning]] already organizes, and the production line that used to sit in the player-facing opening lives here now. The note was built for [[Roadmap|Story R.9]] with `systemic-worldbuilding`, `world-fates` (the shifts are already in the past), `oblique-worldbuilding`, and `cliche-transcendence`. The Closed Seat is a gate that grew a country, not a dark lord.

🟡 **Lived chronicle (2026-08-30).** 🔒 **Does not move the two clocks, the present year, the spread-bands, or the undated Tree.** 🔒 **Who cut remains unpicked.** The names of the five years and of the Closed Seat are working common speech, and they are not a new liturgy. The world book was left untouched.

[[Rothallo]], still called the Inner Close, is a walled city inside [[Orenbren]] and that power's capital. The placement was locked in Story R.10, and Rothallo is not a replacement of [[The Hinge Shore]]. The regional painting still shows the small walled square. It was not redrawn to city scale.

The named terrain was settled in Story R.10, on [[Named Ground]]: the Old Crossing, the Rain-Wall, the Core-thaw, the Rise-water, the Chart-run, and the Well-wash.

Story R.11 gave [[The Low Wall]], [[The Seeing-Ring]], and [[The Dry Stair]] their entrances, their pressures, their discoveries, and the people acting there now. A party may walk into them. The sites still do not receive a date.

The index for this work is [[02 - History]], and the tracker is [[Roadmap]] (Story R.9, and R.11).

If play starts treating these Cut-year numbers as a fourth age, pull back to Clock 1 / Clock 2. The Other Count is what happened while the wood travelled. It is not a spine that replaces them.

The Closed Seat is not a dark lord and not a first king. They rationed who could be seen at the one Tree. The Seat later rationed the wood. The Close still ranks. Four different papers preserve four human choices.

Do not date the remains. A party may dig. They may not be handed a year that starts the Tree.

Under Faces (L.2), [[Hildal]], [[Limrae]], [[Dirrol]], [[Narol of the Pass]], and [[Taerso]] are the persons the years remember. Manril stays the argument. Nidtol stays the later overlay. Vathne stays the slate. Sirtol stays the other shore. Tithsael stays the present night on [[The Seeing-Ring]]. Do not promote any of them to a court. Do not write the unnamed speakers as second celebrities.

The fate-shifts on this page are already in the past, and they are not proposals. The Closed Seat fell into the remnant Close. The hymn-country split into two papers. The quay-fever became Trenledd's roll and Brenledd's compact, institutions adapting to the fever. The thaw's tolls passed to a new holder of the pass. The Hinge War was absorbed into the Hush, a fight turned into a rate. Do not collapse any present power from this note.

What this note does not lock: who cut. Five attributions stand on [[The First Cut]], and the Closing is about the copy, not the knife. The nature of the Leaf-Mother's limit stays open. A war over who could reach the Tree is people, and it is not a theory of her wound. A sixteenth power stays unmade. [[Rothallo]] is this power's capital and not a second flag. The three sites stay undated. The present year does not slide.

The clerk who wrote the conversion table cannot see that "171 is not a Cut we honor" is how a fever becomes a hole in a theology. The Close cannot see that a year they will not give is still a year a court can find.

There is no second Harrow. [[The Wrong Green]] sits on the table only because it is already written. Do not invent a second case like it.

Hooks a table can use: a debt with a Grey-summer verso; a gate of the Close that is not the Low Wall; a crown-count and a Cut-year that disagree by 67; a Hush-rate charged to a necessity-family, a family making the crossing because it has to; a night on the Seeing-Ring that a warden wants blessed.
