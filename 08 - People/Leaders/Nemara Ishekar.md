# Nemara Ishekar

> Heir of a water-judge house and the presiding voice of the Phantom Assizes — [[The Sworn|the Sworn's]] conscience and its unwilling figurehead. She keeps a dead court alive out of grief, and discovered too late that grief makes an excellent flag. A woman of real judicial fairness presiding over a movement she can legitimize but cannot control.

## The person

Middle-aged, precise, unshowy; she would have been a fine judge in any system, and knows it, and that is her wound. She hears the annual Phantom Assizes cases with genuine care, in a law no one will enforce, because to let the bench die is to let the [[The Kessadi|Karesh]] die. She did not build [[The Sworn|the Sworn]]; it grew up around her convening like ivy around a post, and by the time she saw it she was already the thing holding it upright.

## The arc (flat, testing to breaking)

- **Truth she holds:** *A people that cannot judge itself is not a people.* Self-judgment, not the Empire's charity, is the thing worth any price.
- **The pressure:** the delta's material comfort keeps proving her cause a luxury, and her own hardliners keep proving her cause a lie by killing in its name. She is tested from both sides — by the Empire's fairness and by her allies' blood.
- **The break she fears:** the day she must choose between denouncing the violence her legitimacy shelters (and shattering the movement) or blessing it with her silence (and becoming the thing she judged). She is a flat-arc character whose steadfastness is being turned into complicity — the moral-parallax engine aimed at a single soul.

## Table use

- The party's most reasonable point of contact inside the Sworn — she will *argue* the case, in a court's cadence, and she is not wrong. Ideal for the PCs' believer/skeptic argument to go live against a worthy voice.
- She can hand a fair verdict *up* to the [[The Assize|Assize]] as a challenge: hear this petition properly, and see what your own law says about what was done to us.

## GM Notes

`visibility: gm` — player-safe above.

- She knows **nothing** of [[The Cost]] and would be horrified by it; her cause is sovereignty and the bench, full stop. Keep her clean of the secret.
- Design-contract anchor for the *rebellion's* right-ness: as long as Nemara is on stage, the Sworn cannot read as villains. If the table drifts to "rebels = terrorists," put her in the room.
