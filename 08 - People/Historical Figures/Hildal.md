# Hildal

> Last mouth of the Closed Seat that anyone will still name. Clerk of the Retreat. Dead since the walls closed.

*(HIL-dal.)* The name in the Book is Hildal. **Memory: still argued.**

## Summary

In C.Y. 38 the sitting was over. Hildal ordered the Retreat. They did not save the grove. They saved a book. The walls that order closed are the [[The Walled Book|Inner Close]]. The war is [[The Closing]]. The knife is still unwritten.

They wanted the copy, which had already been seen to Turn, to stop being a fight the gate could not win. The leaf says to carry the Book behind the wall, to rank who owes, and to leave the college on the grove. A gift is a gift. A gift is not a seat. They wrote the house. They left the knife off the page.

The Close still calls that stewardship. Folk who hid a slip and kept it still call it a gate that chose a wall. A [[Long-Lived]] who walked as a child in the sitting and a Closed Heir who keeps the page will not agree about that summer. Memory is perfect. The meaning is the argument.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Remembered as a hand, not a height. Ink. A leaf. No crown in any telling that has held. People who arrive hoping for a king find a clerk's page and a list of pots the riders did not bring home.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[The Closing]]** — the war. Hildal is its last named mouth, not its map.
- **[[The Walled Book]]** — the remnant. The present heir-roll keeps the Retreat leaf. The living line calls the page housekeeping. This note does not make Hildal their parent.
- **[[The First Cut]]** — the copy they could not unspeak. They fought the copy. They never received a face for the knife.
- **[[Orenbren]]** — the beds that would not starve a pilgrim-season to save a lintel. Outside the wall. Not this clerk's country to sit.

The college will not write the name on the grove. The Close will not loan the page.

## GM Notes

- **Memory: still argued.** Past layer only. Dead. Not a present antagonist. Not a villain-king. A clerk who chose a wall over a grove the sitting could no longer hold.
- **Positional.** Threshold guardian at the end of a gate. The standard they kept (who owes, who sits) became the remnant's constitution because they were the last mouth the Book would name. They could not put the grove back.
- **Arc, if you need one.** *Want:* the house saved as rank. *Lie:* closing the door is the same tending the grove was. *Need:* to see that striking heir behind a wall is the sitting, continued. They did not take it. The Book outlived them. Do not give them a redemption in play, or a throne.
- **Do not** name the cutter to explain them. Do not add a house-line. The name the year uses is Hildal. *Closed* on a roster is a rank, not a second name. Do not make the Inner Close a sixteenth power. Do not date the Tree. Do not loan the page to the college to tidy the argument.
- **The other mouths of the war stay a class.** One remembered clerk. Not a court.
