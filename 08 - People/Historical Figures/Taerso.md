# Taerso

> Spoke for the Hinge Shore's houses, and could stop a berth. [[The Hinge Hush]], C.Y. 304. Dead. One shore's remembered mouth. The other shore stayed a rate.

*(TARE-so.)* A neighbour said Taerso. A stranger who asks *of where?* gets Taerso of the Hinge Shore. **Memory: still argued.**

## Summary

From C.Y. 299 to 304 the [[The Hinge Shore]] and the Ledger Coast tied each other's hulls over who set the hinge-toll. Taerso spoke for the Shore's houses. They did not sit a throne. They could stop a berth, which was enough to make a war of logistics.

In C.Y. 304 they hushed. The Shore kept the blessing. The Coast kept the paper. The crossing kept moving. The hush says the Shore will say the blessing if a mouth wants one. Taerso had wanted the blessing and the tariff to stay the same fact. The rate split them. People still argue whether that sentence saved a Motherland crossing or taught a pious shore how to step around a fade.

A younger clerk writes **Hush** on a berth-slate and will not add a hero. Older mouths, in the fifth-summer pause in Hale-month, still say Taerso before the bells return to cargo. [[Orentel]]'s first quay holds, and will not call the name a founder of the city.

Sirtol's name sits on the Coast copy, over a rate-table. One throat among a charter-pile. Orentel will not found a city on that name either. The other shore stayed a paper. The bells do not know it as a person.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Remembered as a berth that did not cast off. No admiral's coat has stuck to the telling. The Shore, when it is being devout, gives the name two sentences and puts the blessing back in the second. The quay, when it is working, says the name once and rings the bell.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[The Hinge Hush]]** — the peace the crossing still charges. Taerso is the Shore's mouth on it. Not the winner the hush refused to write.
- **[[The Hinge Shore]]** — the houses they could speak for. The living shore is not this voice, returned.
- **[[The Grey Summer]]** — the quiet sea both shores had already learned to fear. Taerso did not cause it.
- **[[Orentel]]** — will not put the name on the city. [[The White Note House]] did not sign the hush.
- **Sirtol** — the other shore. A desk on a rate-table. Not a second note, and not an admiral.

There is no house-name. Both shores refused to let the hush be written as persons.

## GM Notes

- **Memory: still argued.** Past layer only. Dead. Not a present antagonist. A desk that could stop a berth. Not a first admiral. Do not seat them.
- **Positional.** Bridge. They stood where blessing and tariff still had to be said by one mouth. Neither shore fully released the berth to the other. Their say-so was the lock. The hush kept the crossing and split the fact they had wanted whole.
- **Arc, if you need one.** *Want:* one Motherland crossing. *Lie:* saying the blessing, even optionally, keeps the tariff inside the hymn. *Need:* to see that "if a mouth wants one" is how a shore learns to step around a fade. They did not take it. The rate still collects.
- **Sirtol stays the other shore.** Do not write them a life, a flag, or a matching hero's note. One remembered person. The Coast's paper is already on [[The Hinge Hush]].
- **Do not** restart the war by bringing Taerso back. Do not crown Orentel. Do not book Kumbaan onto the clause. The west-water clause can still be a live wire without this mouth in the room.
