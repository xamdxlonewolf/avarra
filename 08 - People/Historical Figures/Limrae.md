# Limrae

> The clerk a crown-count still starts from. One remembered person of [[The Two Papers]]. Dead. The other paper stayed a refusal.

*(lim-RAY.)* The name on the roll-preface is Limrae. **Memory: still argued.**

## Summary

In C.Y. 67 a reckoning-desk opened in the river-town that would be counted as [[Maiethlir]]'s country. Limrae wrote the colour and the person in one breath. They said they were keeping. The Closed Seat had ranked. This desk wanted the opposite kindness, if you believe the preface.

[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] still dates a crown-count from that summer. Present Crown-year 320 is sixty-seven behind the Cut-year, and that gap is the split. A devout clerk may write both numbers. [[Maiethvael]] will convert neither. They will tell you the year is a river-habit, and that Limrae was a clerk.

A later crown that wants a founding-ancestor tries to make this name a first king. The river-town knows the hand was a desk. Both tellings can hold the same hymn. The injustice of a list that can find you, and the care of a list that will not lose you, are the argument. Limrae does not settle it by being dead.

Manril would not be named to the roll. The guild-minute from that summer still says so, and still will not say who they went home to. The country that refused the list kept the refusal. It did not keep a second life beside this one.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Remembered as tablets and a preface, not a tomb. People who want a dynasty are shown a clerk's hand and a river. The voice, where anyone imitates it, finishes its sentence and puts the tending back in.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[The Two Papers]]** — the split. Limrae is the paper that writes. The year is C.Y. 67.
- **[[Maiethlir]]** — the river-town's country. They will not crown the preface.
- **[[The Reckoned Offices]]** — the living desks. They inherited a count. They did not inherit this clerk in the room.
- **[[The Walled Book]]** — filed both papers as other people's ink. Not Limrae's employer.
- **Manril** — the argument. On no roll. Repeated as the one who would not be named. Not a second note.

## GM Notes

- **Memory: still argued.** Past layer only. Dead. Not a present antagonist. Not a first king. A clerk whose column became a year.
- **Positional.** Accidental historian. The job was to find a gift again. The system made the finding into a crown-count. They were out of their depth the moment a country dated itself from the preface.
- **Arc, if you need one.** *Want:* tend by finding. *Lie:* a person written down is a person kept, and finding is not a cage. *Need:* the square that had already seen the colour, which is the proof they would not leave unlisted. They did not take it. Do not redeem the list by liking the clerk.
- **Manril stays the argument.** Do not write them a childhood, a house, or a hero's note. One remembered person. The refusal is already on [[The Two Papers]].
- **Do not** give Limrae a house-name. A line would be the dynasty the river-town refused. The name the year uses is Limrae. Do not capture the First Seat. Do not make Maiethvael the kind foil.
