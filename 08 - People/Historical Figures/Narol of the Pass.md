# Narol of the Pass

> Narol of the Pass took the thaw-toll when the old pass-holder's stone was under the water-line, in [[The Thaw-Break]] of Cut-year 233. They are dead. The house still levies. The person does not.

*(NAH-rol.)* Of the Pass. A neighbour said Narol, and a stranger on the shelf is asked which notch. The memory of that taking is **still argued**.

## Summary

In Cut-year 233 the high snow on [[Named Ground|the Rain-Wall]] went in one week, and that melt is [[The Thaw-Break]]. Noon Pass stopped being a road. Someone still had to say which notch a cart could use, a notch being the cut in the ridge that a road can still follow. Narol of the Pass took the thaw-toll when the old holder's stone was under the water. [[Lirorn]] levies the thaw, the pass, and the high road. After the Break, the argument was which pass. Narol's house still levies, and Narol is the name on that taking.

The house says a drowned stone cannot collect, and a road that is not levied is a road that closes. The ridge says a house put its notch on a flood. The valley still drinks, and the ridge still pays in isolation. The injustice did not start in 233. The year made it visible as a water-line over a ribbon. Children rub the line for luck, and ridge-folk rub it because the water was real. The toll does not tell a lowlander which rubbing is the prayer.

They are not a mountain-king. A sky-courier's strip from the third day already speaks to them as the person the notch will belong to, and that strip is a desk of weather, not a crown.

Vathne's slate is in [[Ornled]], on a wall, about a stick that drowned in someone else's thaw. That sentence stays on the flood, and it is not Narol's biography.

## Appearance & Demeanor

People remember them wet: a hem, and a notch cut where the old ribbon was not. No throne has survived the telling, and no beard of office has either. The house sounds like a rate. The ridge sounds like a name said once, and then the water.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[The Thaw-Break]]** is the melt. Narol is the person who took the new notch, not the snow.
- **[[Lirorn]]** is the watershed that lives by that water. The living toll is the house. The dead toll-keeper is this name.
- **[[Named Ground|the Shelf-gate]]** is the road that replaced Noon Pass. The name is said here, as Break-rate.
- **[[Named Ground|the Noon Pass]]** is the stone that went under. It is not a seat they founded.
- **[[Saelthael]]** is the west-road, and it was two summers late. Narol did not close it. The water did.

There is no house-name to put first. Lirorn would not be written as a people, and the byname is the pass.

## GM Notes

- **Memory: still argued.** This is a past layer only. Narol is dead, not a present antagonist, and not a mountain-king. The house that still levies is not a villain you can arrest in Narol's shape.
- **Positional.** They were the threshold guardian of a pass. The old standard was under water, and their reading of which notch was still a road became the rate. They were the person on the shelf when the category broke. They could not put the old stone back.
- **Arc, if you need one.** They wanted a notch that still collects, so the pass stays a pass. The lie was that a different notch is only a road. What they needed was to see that the water-line is a grave as well as a rate. They did not take it. The house still does not.
- **Vathne stays the slate** on [[The Thaw-Break]]. Do not make them the prophet of secularism, and do not give them a note beside this one.
- **Do not** date the Tree from the pass-stone. Do not hang a Kind on the water-line. Do not give the ridge a surname.
