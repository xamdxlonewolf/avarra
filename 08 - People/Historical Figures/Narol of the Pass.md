# Narol of the Pass

> Took the thaw-toll when the old pass-holder's stone was under the water-line. [[The Thaw-Break]], C.Y. 233. Dead. The house still levies. The person does not.

*(NAH-rol.)* Of the Pass. A neighbour said Narol. A stranger on the shelf is asked which notch. **Memory: still argued.**

## Summary

In C.Y. 233 the high snow on [[Named Ground|the Rain-Wall]] went in one week. Noon Pass stopped being a road. Someone still had to say which notch a cart could use. Narol of the Pass took the thaw-toll when the old holder's stone was under the water. [[Lirorn]] levies the thaw, the pass, the high road. After the Break, the argument was which pass. Narol's house still levies. Narol is the name on that taking.

The house says a drowned stone cannot collect, and a road that is not levied is a road that closes. The ridge says a house put its notch on a flood. The valley still drinks. The ridge still pays in isolation. The injustice did not start in 233. The year made it visible as a water-line over a ribbon. Children rub the line for luck. Ridge-folk rub it because the water was real. The toll does not tell a lowlander which rubbing is the prayer.

They are not a mountain-king. A sky-courier's strip from the third day already speaks to them as the person the notch will belong to. That is a desk of weather, not a crown.

Vathne's slate is in [[Ornled]], on a wall, about a stick that drowned in someone else's thaw. That sentence stays on the flood. It is not Narol's biography.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Remembered wet. A hem. A notch cut where the old ribbon was not. No throne, no beard of office that has survived the telling. The house sounds like a rate. The ridge sounds like a name said once, and then the water.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[The Thaw-Break]]** — the melt. Narol is the mouth that took the new notch. Not the snow.
- **[[Lirorn]]** — the watershed. The living toll is the house. The dead toll-keeper is this name.
- **[[Named Ground|the Shelf-gate]]** — the road that replaced Noon Pass. The name is said here, as Break-rate.
- **[[Named Ground|the Noon Pass]]** — the stone that went under. Not a seat they founded.
- **[[Saelthael]]** — the west-road, two summers late. Narol did not close it. The water did.

There is no house-name to put first. Lirorn would not be written as a people. The byname is the pass.

## GM Notes

- **Memory: still argued.** Past layer only. Dead. Not a present antagonist. Not a mountain-king. The house that still levies is not a villain you can arrest in Narol's shape.
- **Positional.** Threshold guardian of a pass. The old standard was under water. Their reading of which notch was a road became the rate. They were the person on the shelf when the category broke. They could not put the old stone back.
- **Arc, if you need one.** *Want:* a notch that still collects, so the pass stays a pass. *Lie:* a different notch is only a road. *Need:* to see that the water-line is a grave as well as a rate. They did not take it. The house still does not.
- **Vathne stays the slate** on [[The Thaw-Break]]. Do not make them the prophet of secularism, and do not give them a note beside this one.
- **Do not** date the Tree from the pass-stone. Do not hang a Kind on the water-line. Do not give the ridge a surname.
