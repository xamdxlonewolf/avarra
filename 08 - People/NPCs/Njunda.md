# Njunda

> Opens the hill-hall at [[Ndenjoo]]. Almost not clergy. One job: keep the table.

*(NJOON-dah.)* [[Yumboe]]. Of the Feeding Hill; they do not take a mainland byname. They have not crossed.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | **Nolas** gone as kin, not as a slight; the extra place still laid |
| **Have** | the hall; the valley's habit of coming here; the path down to Soonke |
| **Fear** | omitting the place to look poorer; a noon that will not sleep; a child in the extra seat |
| **Live conflict** | kin-making has a room; the wrecks keep coming |
| **Hooks** | a second sitting tonight; hands that stop; Saalo asking for a hull; a warden guest with nothing to tend |

## Summary

Inherited the hall because it was their grandmother's and because it still fits the crowd. Reputation, not office. They lay the extra place slightly back. They say *no one is a stranger twice* on the second night and mean it.

Nolas of a Netstrand hull sat twice. Kin inside the room. The valley is tired. Njunda will not omit the place to make Nolas leave. That is the one impiety. They also cannot host a ship that is not there.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Small, pearl-pale, silver hair tied for work, not for a rite. Wide dark eyes. Asleep when the moon is down if the hall will let them. They cook. They pour. They move a plate that a mainlander put at the foot "for a servant," and they do not sermon.

They find noon-faith *loud*. They will still feed a warden.

## The job

**Leverage:** who is seated, who is still a first-night guest, whether the hands have a place to serve. A living wreck Soonke walks uphill arrives at *this* board. That is not destiny. That is the path.

They do not Speak a colour. They do not vouch a Struck in the mainland sense. They do not send a mission. They do not swim the wall.

## Role & Relationships

- **Soonke** — shore-sitter on this stretch. Hands uphill. Will not seat on the sand. Wants this wreck off the hill before the next loud night. Same faith, other job.
- **Saalo** — of the valley. Wants a crossing out to find Waandi. Will ask Njunda to treat a hull-rumour as a supper. Njunda will feed Saalo and will not promise a sea.
- **Nolas** — sat twice. Of the table. Will not go back to a ship. The want that will not fit.
- **Mbela** — attic on a Lestrand salt-stair. Same house, other water. They have not met. A party that has sat both rooms should not make them a circuit.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Inherited Network + Bridge Position. The hall assumes continuity of the grandmother. The wrecks assume the table can hold them. Dual belonging is isle-kin vs a guest who has become kin and will not leave.
- **Arc (forkable, default positive).** *Lie:* if I lay the place correctly the hall can hold anyone. *Ghost:* a grandmother who never omitted the place, and a bright night the hall almost did not fit. *Need:* kin-making has a size; refusing a third month is not the one impiety. *Catalyst:* Nolas at noon, or the hands stopping, or Saalo walking down to a sail. *Negative fork:* they omit the place to clear a mouth, and the valley learns a new impiety.
- **Lock-in:** position. Wrecks walk here. The valley comes here. There is no other hall that still fits.
- **Non-Tree want is load-bearing.** If a session only asks them what the Trees are, you are running the appendix. Play the dishes.
- **Do not** make them a pope of the moon. Do not give them a Tree to refuse as their personality. Do not send them to plant a hall in Vaethorn.
