# Nedaen

Nedaen (neh-DAYN) is [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]], a person the wild took partway into itself, living on the commons around [[Eolvaeth]]'s Tree, and a neighbour says Nedaen and a stranger gets Nedaen of Eolvaeth, or of the commons if they are already at the Tree, and there is no house to give. On Kumbaan, where a person is asked for the given name, the name is Nedaen, and they are not a nation.

They want the gift-hall pot, the hall's common meal, to feed their child through Ledger-month. Mother's green, the shared green of that Tree, is enough until it is not.
