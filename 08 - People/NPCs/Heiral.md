# Heiral

*(HAY-ral.)* Kept. Camp-street, [[Eolvaeth]]. A neighbour says Heiral. A stranger gets Heiral of Eolvaeth, or of the camp-street if that is the street they mean. There is no house. On Kumbaan the name is Heiral.

**Want:** a marriage witnessed without being pitied as Mother-kept. A neighbour's word. Not a sermon.
