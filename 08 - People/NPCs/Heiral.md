# Heiral

Heiral (HAY-ral) is Kept, witnessed at the Turning and given no colour to carry, on the camp-street in [[Eolvaeth]], and a neighbour says Heiral and a stranger gets Heiral of Eolvaeth, or of the camp-street if that is the street they mean, and there is no house to give. On Kumbaan, where a person is asked for the given name, the name is Heiral.

They want a marriage witnessed without being pitied as Mother-kept, with a neighbour's word standing for them, and not a sermon.
