# Ledan

> Ledan writes the quay-desk of [[The White Note House]], the deathless road-house that kept a counter when the beds became paper. They are [[Long-Lived]], aging stopped, and the memory of every debtor still with them. The Cut-year 280 conversion-query, the archival question of how to turn one count of years into another, is theirs.

*(LED-an.)* The names on this quay have eroded. They are of the third quay, north side. A neighbour on the quay says Ledan, and a stranger gets Ledan of Orentel. While the paper is open a clerk may write White Note, Ledan, and it is the desk. Shut the paper and the name is Ledan of the third quay. There is no family. On Kumbaan the name is Ledan, and they are a clerk at the desk.

## Summary

They kept the inn when the walk was required, and they kept the paper when it was not. They remember the ford on the verso. They will convert a house-year, the house's own count of summers, or a Cut-year, the wardens' count from the First Cut, or a court's third date, if you say which. They will not invent a berth, and they will not invent a hull.

When they are in the room, what they want is the founding-name spoken, so they can stop being the only memory of it, and they will not speak it first.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They are not young, and they are not performing age for the room. There is ink on the hands, and the door is left open to the quay. They answer in conversions even when you asked about a bed, and they stay until the tide-bell. They do not like to be asked if they *walked*, and they will say the house did.

## The job

**Leverage:** which count a debt wears, whether a necessity-note still says *eat now*, and whether the founding-book stays shut. A necessity-note is the old paper that lets a family eat now and pay on the walk home. A party can get a winter written in an hour. They cannot get Sorim's hold, the first-quay berth, from this stool.

They are junior to no one in the house. Mataero is junior to them, and that is not a kindness.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Mataero]]** keeps occupancy, the loft's account of who sleeps, and wants Sorel off the paper by filing beds correctly. Ledan has not moved the number.
- **[[Sorel]]** is still on a century-instrument, a paper written to run a hundred years, and still alive. Ledan remembers the walk Sorel cannot point to.
- **[[Sorim]]** is the first-quay factor, and will not convert house-years. Ledan will not send a conversion down to the crane.
- **[[Thilim]]** is the other fate of the same kind of house, inland, still a hearth. A far-cape name this house does not hold is still held there. Ledan will not tidy that by taking the name.

## GM Notes

- **Not a seventh pivot.** The six pivots remain the six. Ledan is the desk a session can sit at when the paper has to be in the room.
- **Do not** invent a second Ledan and call the name a title. Do not explain the north bed in the first scene. Do not make this house Orentel's government.
- **Walked.** They walked, in the house's sense. The inn did. They will not pick which of Thilim's four *I walked* jobs that was, the four things a deathless person can mean by saying they walked when they were young.
- **Arc if you need one (flat, default).** *Truth:* the house is still an inn when it is kind, and a desk when it is not. The world wants only the desk. They keep the north bed empty anyway. *Negative fork:* they let the name be read as a performance, and the house becomes only paper.
