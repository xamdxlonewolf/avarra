# Turvo

> Keeps the shared notes in the council hall at [[Raitin]]. Calls the next hearth. Does not become the league's one throat.

*(TUR-vo.)* Of Raitin. A neighbour says Turvo. A stranger hears Turvo of Raitin. There is no house. The Hearth-League will not write one.

## Summary

Turvo sits the council hall where [[Brenledd]]'s shared notes clear. Seven hearths are on the list. Raitin is one. The other six keep their names in their own squares. Turvo calls them as hearths. They do not merge them into a house.

They turn a cleared note face-down before the next hearth is called. They will not say why.

Nidtol is the hearth-name people recite when someone offers a simpler throat. Turvo can say that name. It is not a chair in the hall, and it is not Turvo's office.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Ink on the heel of the hand that turns the page. They ask which hearth stood the person, and they wait for the answer before they write. A neighbour gets the given name. A ledger from [[Orentel]] that wants one signature for the whole league is sent back down the stair.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Raitin]]** — the hall is the work. The city is the throne-city. Turvo is one mouth in it.
- **[[Nubo]]** — vouched here. The next hearth did not stand them. Turvo's page cannot repair that.
- **[[Sulnu]]** — takes the cleared ticket to the river. Turvo does not walk it down.
- **[[Orentel]]** — the premier coast. A factor who offers to simplify the league is a guest, not a member of the list.
