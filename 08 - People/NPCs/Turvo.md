# Turvo

> Turvo keeps the shared notes in the council hall at [[Raitin]], calls the next hearth, and does not become the one person who speaks for the league.

People in the hall say Turvo (TUR-vo), and a stranger hears Turvo of Raitin. The Hearth-League will not write a house, so there is no house-name to give them.

## Summary

Turvo sits the council hall where [[Brenledd]]'s shared notes clear, with seven hearths on the list, Raitin one of them and the other six keeping their names in their own squares. Turvo calls those hearths as hearths, and does not merge them into a single house.

They turn a cleared note face-down before the next hearth is called, and they will not say why.

Nidtol is the hearth-name people recite when someone offers one speaker for all the hearths, and Turvo can say that name, though it is not a chair in the hall and it is not Turvo's office.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Ink stains the heel of the hand that turns the page. They ask which hearth stood the person, and they wait for the answer before they write, giving a neighbour the given name, while a ledger from [[Orentel]] that wants one signature for the whole league is sent back down the stair.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Raitin]]** — The hall is the work in the throne-city of the league, and Turvo is one person in that city.
- **[[Nubo]]** — Nubo was vouched in this hall, the next hearth did not stand them, and Turvo's page cannot repair that refusal.
- **[[Sulnu]]** — Sulnu takes the cleared ticket down to the river, and Turvo does not walk it down.
- **[[Orentel]]** — Orentel is the premier coast, and a factor who offers to simplify the league is a guest rather than a member of the list.
