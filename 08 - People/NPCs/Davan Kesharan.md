# Davan Kesharan

> The firebrand — and the one who built the thing he now destroys. A Kessadi who took the imperial ladder, trained as a surveyor, and helped map the water-reallocation that dispossessed his own neighbours before he understood what the lines meant. Now he runs the cells that cut the network he charted. His competence is the Empire's gift, and he is turning it against the Empire with a convert's fury.

## The person

Young-ish, brilliant, exact — an engineer's mind on a rebel's errand. He speaks Imperial Common better than the officers he ambushes and knows the road, the locks, and the relay-schedule from the inside because he *drew* them. Where [[Nemara Ishekar]] argues law, Davan argues arithmetic: here is what they took, here is the schedule, here is where it breaks. He is the most effective and most dangerous person in [[The Sworn]], and the least willing to stop at sabotage.

## The arc (negative, descending)

- **Ghost:** the day he realized his own survey lines had erased his neighbours' ancestral water — that his competence had been the instrument. Complicity, not conquest, radicalized him.
- **Lie he's building toward:** *the only clean thing left is to destroy it.* Guilt turned outward into righteousness; the surveyor who cannot forgive himself deciding the network must pay.
- **Descent:** non-lethal sabotage → "warnings" → the first body → timing an open rising to the closing road-window. Each step is justified by the last. His point-of-no-return is a killing he can no longer call an accident.
- **The redemption he'll reject:** Nemara, or a PC, offering him the slower, survivable path — and him choosing the fire because slow means living with the guilt. His tragedy is that he is *right about the wrong* and becoming monstrous in answering it.

## Table use

- The face of the question *"when does a legitimate grievance become terrorism, and who decided?"* — put him against a PC who took the same imperial ladder and made peace with it.
- His inside knowledge of the network is a live plot engine: he knows exactly how to hurt the Empire, and the players may have to decide whether stopping him means defending something they've started to doubt.

## GM Notes

`visibility: gm` — player-safe above.

- Knows **nothing** of [[The Cost]]. His "arithmetic" is about *water and rolls*, not the secret — though a paranoid Davan is the natural mouth for the fringe **ledger-seed** ([[The Sworn]] GM Notes): he's the one who'd notice the reconciliation lists don't balance and call it a hidden levy. Keep him *wrong in a rhyming way* — never let him touch the real mechanism.
- He must never become a simple villain. Keep his math correct and his grief legible; the horror is that a decent, wronged, capable man is choosing the fire in full view. If he curdles into a stock terrorist, the parallax is broken.
