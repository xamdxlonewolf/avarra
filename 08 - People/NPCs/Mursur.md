# Mursur

> Mursur quotes a far crossing at [[Braetu]] and keeps a lamp on a name, and the unlit berth is not their house.

People on the quay say Mursur (MUR-sur), and a stranger hears Mursur of Braetu. The Night Shore does not keep a list of persons, so there is no house-name to give them.

## Summary

Mursur sits the quote-desk, where a crossing toward the Heskoren frontier, or a hull chartered onward to the Old Crossing, becomes a figure on their page, and houses pay them to keep a lamp burning beside a name in the dark book, which is a quoted crossing that did not come back. That book is not a roll of people, and it is not the [[The White Note House|White Note]]. The White Note is where the terms are written, [[Orentel]] is where the berth is held, and Mursur's work is the quote and the fee for the watch.

They light a name's lamp with a spill from the cook-fire, not from another lamp, and they will not say why.

One berth in the harbour stays unlit, with no house attached to it, and Mursur does not attach theirs. [[Selkie]] families are common on this water, sea-born, and Mursur's name is not a Kind-name from that birth, nor is the shore their flag.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Salt stays on the cuff nearest the book, they say the figure, blot it, and say it again the same, and they will not say why. A neighbour gets the given name, while a stranger who offers a house is treated as someone who wants the dark berth tidied, and Mursur will not tidy it.
