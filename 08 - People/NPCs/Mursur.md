# Mursur

> Quotes a far crossing at [[Braetu]], and keeps a lamp on a name. The unlit berth is not their house.

*(MUR-sur.)* Of Braetu. A neighbour says Mursur. A stranger hears Mursur of Braetu. There is no house. The Night Shore does not keep a person-list.

## Summary

Mursur sits the quote-desk. A crossing toward the Heskoren frontier, or a hull chartered onward to the Old Crossing, is a figure on their page. Houses pay them to keep a lamp on a name in the dark book: a quoted crossing that did not come back. The book is not a roll and not the [[The White Note House|White Note]]. The White Note writes terms. [[Orentel]] holds the berth. Mursur quotes, and charges the watch.

They light a name's lamp with a spill from the cook-fire, not from another lamp. They will not say why.

One berth in the harbour stays unlit. No house is attached to it. Mursur does not attach theirs. [[Selkie]] families are common on this water. Mursur's name is not a Kind-name, and the shore is not their flag.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Salt on the cuff that is nearest the book. They say the figure, blot it, and say it again the same. They will not say why. A neighbour gets the given name. A stranger who offers a house is treated as someone who wants the dark berth tidied, and Mursur will not tidy it.
