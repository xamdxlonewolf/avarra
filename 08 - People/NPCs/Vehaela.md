# Vehaela

Vehaela (veh-HAY-lah) came down from [[Lirorn]]'s thin snow with two children, a necessity-family that moved because it had to, and a neighbour who has already fed them says Vehaela and a stranger gets Vehaela of Lirorn, and there is no house to give. They have asked [[Maiethlir]] for a room the roll has not found, and they have not taken a house the roll might invent. On Kumbaan, where a person is asked for the given name, the name is Vehaela.

They want an uncounted loft for one winter, and the extra-mile street in [[Maiethlir]] still has rooms the roll has not found.
