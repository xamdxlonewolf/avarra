# Laevila

> Bakes on the old extra-mile. Keeps the room under the chapel the wardens recut. Has the only copy of the beam's hidden face. The roll files them as the Grown-Over. They bake for a living.

*(lay-VEE-lah.)* House **Thalonve** *(tha-LON-ve)*. Conservative drift. A neighbour says Laevila. A clerk says Thalonve, Laevila. A stranger gets Laevila of Maiethlir. On Kumbaan the name is Laevila. [[Long-Lived]]. They walked as a child, under Thaeloren, before this river had to count them.

## Summary

A Watcher looks like a neighbour. Laevila looks like a baker who stayed. Flour on the cuff. No mark of any office. You know them because someone said *ask the old room*, or because you saw them under the Tree the night before Turning-Week with no lamp.

They remember a cost. They have no institution that can keep the beam from being turned to face the stone. What they have is paper they made before the last recut, and a grandchild whose household line the roll wrote wrong.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Deathless the way a street gets used to: the same face in the same doorway, a little emptier some decades, always asked in. They describe before they conclude. A sloppy sentence offends them more than a wrong theology.

They will not Speak. They will not say *Two-Bodied* if they saw two colours. They will say the two colours.

## The job

**Leverage:** the hidden face of the recut beam, copied in their own hand, is the only text that still has [[Senithi]]'s grandmother under her own name. A clerk who will take a second reading as *alongside* might take this as a witness for a marriage. A clerk who wants the night scheduled will call it a column being difficult.

They cannot confirm a colour. They cannot unsay the recut. They can sit beside the Speaker and say the second colour aloud, so it stays spoken in the square and the clerk cannot quietly drop it.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Senithi]]** *(seh-NITH-ee)* — grandchild, Given, ordinary, the same house. A marriage is priced on a line that lists the wrong grandmother. The house-word is Thalonve either way. Laevila wants the hidden face read once, not as theology — as a name a tablet will swallow.
- **[[Rithim]]** — Speaks in the same square. They eat after the Night Watch. Rithim will not say the line. Laevila will not say the Mother. That is as close as peace gets on this street.
- **The chapel wardens** — recut the beam. File the room as vestry. Leave a plate during Turning-Week. Courtesy, not a wage.
- **Bare Tree kin** — will not eat with a Watcher who takes crown-coin for a copy of the beam. Laevila has not taken it. They are running out of reasons not to.
- **[[Baerith]]** — sings the counted hour two streets over. Has asked Laevila to describe a fall the channel already named.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Accidental Historian. Routine copy became the only source. Their interpretation is the battleground.
- **Arc (positive).** *Lie:* if I only describe, I have not chosen sides. *Ghost:* they held the lamp the night the beam was recut, because someone had to see the work. *Need:* accept that description in Threnmaieth is already a political act — and do it for Senithi anyway, or refuse the marriage-price and keep the night unpaid. *Catalyst:* a clerk offers coin for the copy, or Senithi's match walks.
- **Lock-in:** knowledge + trust. They are the second voice a mixed fall still asks for. The copy is unique. Leaving the room lets the vestry finish the rot.
- **Competing interests:** the roll (one colour, already Spoken); Rithim (hymn first, Valein unfiled — not the same fight); Senithi (a wedding, not an inscription); Bare Tree (no crown-coin); the chapel (the room stays a room if it stays quiet).
- **Walked** as a child under Thaeloren. Different *I walked* than [[Thilim]] (who walked as the inn). Play the disagreement if they are ever in the same week.
- **Do not** clone the Orenhael onto this person. No circuit, no Speaking, no Cutting-leave. Constraint is *no mandate anyone will fund.*
- **About 5% frightening:** the recut beam and the roll, not a hunt.
