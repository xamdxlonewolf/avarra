# Laevila

> Laevila bakes for a living on the old extra-mile, the street where people still walk past what the local rite already gave them, and they keep the room under the chapel the wardens recut. Before that recut they copied the hidden face of the beam, the side turned away from the room, and the copy is the only one. The roll files a Watcher of that room as the Grown-Over, its name for people whose old place sits under a chapel that was built over it.

*(lay-VEE-lah.)* House **Thalonve** *(tha-LON-ve)*, on the conservative side of the river, where a clerk still has a house to put first. A neighbour says Laevila, a clerk says Thalonve, Laevila, and a stranger gets Laevila of Maiethlir. On Kumbaan the name is Laevila. They are [[Long-Lived]], deathless, with the aging stopped and the memory of what they witnessed still in them, and they walked as a child, under Thaeloren, the origin Tree in the Motherwood, before this river had to count them.

## Summary

A Watcher is the neighbour the Watching sends when a leaf comes down strange. They describe what they saw, and they do not confirm it, because confirmation is the warden's Speaking. Laevila looks like that neighbour who stayed: flour on the cuff, no mark of any office. You know them because someone said *ask the old room*, or because you saw them under the Tree the night before Turning-Week, the solstice week when the ten-year-olds stand for the leaf, with no lamp.

They remember what the recut cost, and they have no institution that can keep the beam from being turned to face the stone. What they have is paper they made before the last recut, and a grandchild whose household line the roll wrote wrong.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They are deathless the way a street gets used to a person: the same face in the same doorway, a little emptier some decades, always asked in. They describe a thing before they will conclude about it, and a sloppy sentence offends them more than a wrong theology.

They will not Speak, which is the act of naming the colour aloud so the town can file it. They will not say *Two-Bodied*, a Condition's own name, if what they saw was two colours. They will say the two colours.

## The job

**Leverage:** the hidden face of the recut beam, copied in their own hand, is the only text that still has [[Senithi]]'s grandmother under her own name. A second reading is a description set *alongside* the warden's word, not over it. A clerk who will take that kind of reading might take this copy as a witness for a marriage. A clerk who wants the night put on a festival schedule will call it a column being difficult.

They cannot confirm a colour, and they cannot unsay the recut. They can sit beside the Speaker, the person whose word makes a colour civic, and say the second colour aloud, so it stays spoken in the square and the clerk cannot quietly drop it.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Senithi]]** *(seh-NITH-ee)* is their grandchild, Given a colour at the Leaf-Fall, ordinary, and of the same house. A marriage is priced on a line that lists the wrong grandmother. The house-word is Thalonve either way. Laevila wants the hidden face read once, not as theology, but as a name a tablet will swallow.
- **[[Rithim]]** Speaks in the same square. They eat together after the Night Watch, the silent sitting under the Tree the night before Turning-Week. Rithim will not say the line the clerk is waiting to copy. Laevila will not say the Mother, the chooser's name a Watcher leaves out of the blessing. That is as close as peace gets on this street.
- **The chapel wardens** recut the beam and file the room as a vestry. They leave a plate during Turning-Week. That plate is courtesy, not a wage.
- **Bare Tree kin**, the branch of the Watching that will not say *she* at all, will not eat with a Watcher who takes crown-coin for a copy of the beam. Laevila has not taken it. They are running out of reasons not to.
- **[[Baerith]]** sings the counted hour two streets over, the hour the crown treats as matched to a name, and has asked Laevila to describe a fall the channel has already named.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Accidental Historian. A routine copy became the only source, and what they say the copy means is the fight.
- **Arc (positive).** *Lie:* if I only describe, I have not chosen sides. *Ghost:* they held the lamp the night the beam was recut, because someone had to see the work. *Need:* accept that a description in Threnmaieth is already a political act, and do it for Senithi anyway, or refuse the marriage-price and keep the night unpaid. *Catalyst:* a clerk offers coin for the copy, or Senithi's match walks.
- **Lock-in:** knowledge and trust together. They are the second voice a mixed fall still asks for, and the copy is unique. If they leave the room, the vestry can finish the rot.
- **Competing interests:** the roll already has one colour, and it has been Spoken; Rithim wants the hymn first and Valein left unfiled, which is not the same fight; Senithi wants a wedding, not an inscription; the Bare Tree wants no crown-coin on this copy; the chapel wants the room to stay a room, which it will if the room stays quiet.
- **Walked** as a child under Thaeloren. That is a different *I walked* from [[Thilim]], who walked as the inn, the road-house keeping the mile rather than the child who stood under the Tree. Play the disagreement if they are ever in the same week.
- **Do not** clone the Orenhael, the Tree-Wardens' order, onto this person. They have no circuit, no Speaking, and no Cutting-leave, the Seat's paper for who may carry living wood. The constraint is that nobody will fund a mandate for them.
- **About 5% frightening:** what frightens here is the recut beam and the roll, not a hunt.
