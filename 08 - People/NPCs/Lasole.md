# Lasole

Lasole (lah-SO-leh), of the rise at [[Orentel]], rents a leaf-lot, a plot of green let for the season, and is Tithe-poor in a rich city, short of the upkeep a Condition, an acquired change, asks of them. A neighbour says Lasole and a stranger gets Lasole of Orentel, the lot is not a house, and it will not follow them. On Kumbaan, where a person is asked for the given name, the name is Lasole.

They want the credit extended one season. The lot will not follow them home, and if it did, home would be a loft. They have asked [[Mataero]] to stand for a season, and that ask is not a White Note term.
