# Lasole

*(lah-SO-leh.)* Of the rise, [[Orentel]]. Rents a leaf-lot. Tithe-poor in a rich city. A neighbour says Lasole. A stranger gets Lasole of Orentel. The lot is not a house, and it will not follow them. On Kumbaan the name is Lasole.

**Want:** the credit extended one season. The lot will not follow them home. Home is a loft if it did.

Has asked [[Mataero]] to stand for a season. That is not a White Note term.
