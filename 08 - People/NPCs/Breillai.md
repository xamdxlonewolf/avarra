# Breillai

> Breillai left the hymn in [[Seinbrun]], and the green, the hall, and the medicine went with it.

People who still know the lane say Breillai (BRAY-lye), and a stranger hears Breillai of Seinbrun. There is no house to appeal to, and no crown either.

## Summary

Breillai was inside the congregation, and they left it, which in Seinbrun is not a line on a roll but the net gone: the tended green, the hall's pot, and the guild's medicine. People are kind about the loss in the way the Gift-Realm is kind, and the kindness remembers what Breillai no longer receives.

They are still in the city, the smaller towns around Seinbrun are not a refuge this note is going to name, and the argument stays in the lane.

On the day a household leaves, someone stacks the green pots in the lane, still planted, Breillai's were stacked that way, and the city will not say why.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Their hands still look like hall-work, and the coat has been mended past the thread the congregation used. They stand where the lane meets the green and do not go in.

- **[[Seinbrun]]** — The tension of Seinbrun, the hymn and what leaves with it, is lived here by one household.
