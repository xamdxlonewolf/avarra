# Breillai

> Left the hymn in [[Seinbrun]]. The green, the hall, and the medicine went with it.

*(BRAY-lye.)* Of Seinbrun. A neighbour says Breillai. A stranger hears Breillai of Seinbrun. There is no house to appeal to, and no crown either.

## Summary

Breillai was inside the congregation. They left it. In Seinbrun that is not a line on a roll. It is the net, gone: the tended green, the hall's pot, the guild's medicine. People are kind about the loss in the way the Gift-Realm is kind. The kindness remembers what Breillai no longer receives.

They are still in the city. The smaller towns around Seinbrun are not a refuge this note is going to name. The argument is in the lane.

On the day a household leaves, someone stacks the green pots in the lane, still planted. Breillai's were stacked. The city will not say why.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Hands that still look like hall-work. A coat mended past the congregation's thread. They stand where the lane meets the green and do not go in.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Vuthbraen]]** — can set a bowl. Cannot put Breillai back inside the hymn by setting it.
- **[[Raermu]]** — the basket opens for the congregation's sick. Breillai's household is the question on the step.
- **[[Seinbrun]]** — the tension, lived by one house. Not a faction of the exited. Do not grow one.
