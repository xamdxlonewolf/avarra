# Derdil

> Stands the signing-watch at [[Naenor]]. A term can close in their hearing. They do not rewrite a Bound Term, and they do not hold Orentel's berth.

*(DER-dil.)* Of Naenor. A neighbour says Derdil. A stranger hears Derdil of Naenor. There is no house. The Reckoned Gift does not keep a person-list.

## At a glance

| | |

| **Want** | A term that closes without making a gift into a debt |
| **Have** | The book-table, the wax, the left hand on the page |

| **Live conflict** | The signing is won here. The berth is not. |

## Summary

Derdil watches the signing on Naenor's square. Leaf-Fall is a festival and a watch, and the ordinary week is the same table with fewer hymns. A [[Bound]] word can close a contract in this wind. [[The Book-Hands|Book-Hands]] stamp and recite beside that work. They have no seat. They do not rewrite Terms. Derdil is the city's watch, not their chair.

They stamp the wax, then warm the stamp in a closed hand before the next page. They will not say why.

A household that calls a colour a gift is still, in this statute, a credit the city does not like. Derdil can read the term aloud. They cannot make the Ledger Coast hold the berth, and they cannot make the statute love a gift.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Wax under a nail. They face the speaker, not the Tree, when the page is open. They use a given name. A house, offered to sound important, is sent back as the wrong mouth.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Naenor]]** — the watch is the work. The port is slightly smaller than Orentel, and the difference is the job.
- **[[The Book-Hands]]** — in the same wind. Not Derdil's seat. Terms stay as written.
- **[[Orentel]]** — wins the berth this city does not hold. A First-Hand year is not Derdil's product.
- **[[The Fair Hand]]** — the reading the city made civic. Derdil services the term. They are not a prophet.
