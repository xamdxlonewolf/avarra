# Derdil

> Derdil stands the signing-watch at [[Naenor]], where a term can close in their hearing, and they do not rewrite a Bound Term or hold [[Orentel]]'s berth.

People on the square say Derdil (DER-dil), and a stranger hears Derdil of Naenor. The Reckoned Gift, which is what people call this country, does not keep a list of persons, so there is no house-name to give them.

## Summary

Derdil watches the signing on Naenor's square, where Leaf-Fall, the festival when a child's leaf falls, is a watch as well as a festival, and the ordinary week is the same table with fewer hymns. A [[Bound]] word, the sworn word of a person under a contract, can close a contract in this wind, and the [[The Book-Hands|Book-Hands]] stamp the page and recite beside that work without a seat of their own and without rewriting Terms. Derdil is the city's watch on the signing, not their chair.

They stamp the wax, then warm the stamp in a closed hand before the next page, and they will not say why.

A household that calls a colour a gift is still, in this statute, a credit the city does not like. Derdil can read the term aloud, and cannot make the Ledger Coast hold the berth or make the statute love a gift.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Wax stays under a nail, they face the speaker rather than the Tree when the page is open, and they use a given name, so a house offered to sound important is sent back as the wrong name.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Naenor]]** — The watch is the work of this port, which is slightly smaller than Orentel, and that difference is the job.
- **[[The Book-Hands]]** — They work in the same wind, the seat is not Derdil's, and the Terms stay as written.
- **[[Orentel]]** — Orentel wins the berth this city does not hold, and a First-Hand year is not Derdil's product.
- **[[The Fair Hand]]** — This is the reading the city made into civic law, Derdil services the term, and they are not a prophet of that reading.
