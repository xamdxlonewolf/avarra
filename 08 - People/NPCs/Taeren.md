# Taeren

> Taeren is a hush, the filing word for a white-fire the clerks will not let become a civic fact, and a child of Brenod who stood at the neighbour's canopy when something white happened. Nobody important wrote it down. They do not know why a fire answers them when someone says an old name.

*(TAY-ren.)* The naming here is worn, with no house to put first. They are of [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford|Brenod]], not of Harrow's, and not of a capital. A neighbour says Taeren, a stranger gets Taeren of Brenod, and there is no house. On Kumbaan the name is Taeren. The fall was the neighbour's week, not a week Brenod was holding.

## Summary

The wider world knows there should be a [[Phoenix]], the one person the stories are about, a life that burns and rises until it cannot, and it has not seen one in a generation. Doctrine says there cannot be two. If a white-fire is Spoken while a sanctuary still "holds the Phoenix," the clerks file it as a mis-Speaking or as a hush, a colour that is not allowed to become the town's fact.

Last Eolthael, the Turning-month, Taeren stood at [[Harrow's Green]]. Something white happened, and Haelin, who keeps that square, did not Speak it. A colour that was never named aloud cannot be walked home as a witnessing, so Taeren is still in the square. The tenth year, the year a child stands for the leaf, is just behind them, and there is heat in the hands they do not show. They ask ordinary questions: is there bread, is the week over, why does that old hymn use a name I do not own.

What a player can read on [[Phoenix]] is still the public sentence: there is one, they Rise until they cannot, then a leaf falls. This note does not change that sentence.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They are quiet in the way of a person who has been told not to be a story. The heat stays in the hands, and they do not show it in a gift-hall. The questions they ask are ordinary ones, and they are not cryptic about them.

## How they reach a table

- At [[Harrow's Green]], this Hale-month, the spring month, they are still in the square because the witness-walk never happened. Tora of Brenod wants someone to say what the colour was. Haelin will not name it.
- [[Rosire]], if the party has already met them. It is not a reunion scripted on this page. It is a door that was opened.

Taeren is far from anyone who knows them, and that costs them. The cost does not mean a town is missing from the map.
