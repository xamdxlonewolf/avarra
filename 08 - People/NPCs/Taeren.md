# Taeren

> A far warden's letter that did not arrive. A hush. A young person at a thin Tree who does not know why a fire answers them when someone says an old name.

*(TAY-ren.)* Worn drift. Not of Eolvaeth, not of Harrow's. Not a capital. Seed, not a plot.

## Summary

The wider world knows there should be a Phoenix. It has not seen one in a generation. Doctrine says there cannot be two. A Spoken white-fire while a sanctuary still "holds the Phoenix" is filed as a mis-Speaking or a hush. A far graft with no Speaker never enters the record.

Taeren Turned. Something white happened. Nobody important wrote it. They live where the wood is a maybe and the stories are about someone else.

Player-facing [[Phoenix]] still reads: there is one, they Rise until they cannot, then a leaf falls. This note does not change that sentence.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Tenth-year just behind them, or a little older if you need them able to walk. Quiet in the way of a person who has been told not to be a story. Heat in the hands they do not show in a gift-hall. They ask ordinary questions: is there bread, is the week over, why does that old hymn use a name I do not own.

## How they reach a table

- A Road-hand sleeping with a pot for someone else, who heard a hush and did not file it.
- [[Rosire]], if the party already has that mouth — not as a reunion scripted on this page, as a door that was opened.
- A letter Haelin or Vaethod did not ask for, about a colour nobody will Speak.

Do not walk them into a seat in the first session and wait for a crowd. The point of the seed is isolation.

## What this note does not do

Does not add a second Gift. Does not plot the sanctuary, the remnant, the stored fire, or the opening. Does not write a hidden-Phoenix PC sheet. Does not confirm the Leaf-Mother in player text to explain the slot.

## GM Notes

- **Engine is already locked.** [[When the Fire Is Caught]]: never two Gifts. What can look like two is leftover fire next to a new Gift. Taeren is the new leaf if you need the child. The walker from the sanctuary is the remnant. Two Gifts at once is not legal.
- **Seed only.** The harvesters are [[The Protectors]]. R.13 writes player-agency rules for a hidden Phoenix PC. Epic 10 picks the opening. This page puts a mouth on a thin Tree.
- **Player body stays deniable.** A hush, a letter, a child who burns. Confirmation that the slot was empty and the remnant is not a Phoenix stays behind the secret note.
- **Do not** lead with a second bird. Do not sit them next to [[Reimaethe]] or [[Hithaen]] to make a crew.
- **Hope scars** belong to the captive / remnant, not automatically to Taeren. If this child is the new Gift they may have few or none yet.
