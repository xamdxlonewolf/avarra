# Taeren

> A hush. A Brenod child who stood at the neighbour's canopy. Something white. Nobody important wrote it. They do not know why a fire answers them when someone says an old name.

*(TAY-ren.)* Worn drift. Of [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford|Brenod]], not of Harrow's, not of a capital. The fall was the neighbour's week.

## Summary

The wider world knows there should be a Phoenix. It has not seen one in a generation. Doctrine says there cannot be two. A Spoken white-fire while a sanctuary still "holds the Phoenix" is filed as a mis-Speaking or a hush.

Last Eolthael Taeren stood at [[Harrow's Green]]. Something white happened. Haelin did not Speak it. A colour that is not civic cannot be walked home as a witnessing, so they are still in the square. Tenth-year just behind them. Heat in the hands they do not show. They ask ordinary questions: is there bread, is the week over, why does that old hymn use a name I do not own.

Player-facing [[Phoenix]] still reads: there is one, they Rise until they cannot, then a leaf falls. This note does not change that sentence.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Quiet in the way of a person who has been told not to be a story. Heat in the hands they do not show in a gift-hall. Ordinary questions. Not cryptic.

## How they reach a table

- [[Harrow's Green]], Hale-month: still in the square because the witness-walk never happened. Tora of Brenod wants a mouth. Haelin will not name the colour.
- [[Rosire]], if the party already has that mouth — not as a reunion scripted on this page, as a door that was opened.
- If a PC occupies this seat, do not run this person as an NPC beside them.

Do not walk them into a capital and wait for a crowd. Isolation is the leftover square, not a missing town.

## What this note does not do

Does not add a second Gift. Does not write a hidden-Phoenix PC sheet. Does not confirm the Leaf-Mother in player text to explain the slot. Does not make them of Harrow's.
