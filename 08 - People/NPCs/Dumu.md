# Dumu

> Stands the march-voice at [[Natai]]. A complaint can land on them. They do not keep [[Harrow's Green]], and they do not walk the ford.

*(DOO-moo.)* Of Natai. A neighbour says Dumu. A stranger hears Dumu of Natai. There is no house. The Sown Waiting does not keep a person-list.

## Summary

Dumu stands where [[Saelvaeth]] can be blamed. The march-voice did not exist when the first pots came. The town exists so the queue has a person. [[Harrow's Green]] has the canopy. Haelin keeps that square. Tora of Brenod walks a witness home from the ford when the week needs a mouth that was there. Dumu is the third fact: someone in a town on the road between grafts, who did not write the Seat's sentence and still has to hear it said.

They leave the gate-latch on the post while a complaint is said, and put it back in the catch when the speaker turns. They will not say why.

A pot may come in without a Vaethorn waiting-due. The Hush allows it. The shout about whose pocket is next is still Dumu's afternoon. They can answer. They cannot move the wood.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Latch-grime on the thumb. They face the speaker. They do not face a canopy to borrow authority from it. They use a given name. A house, offered to sound like a list, is sent back as the wrong mouth.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Natai]]** — the voice is the work. One town. Other graft-towns stay unnamed.
- **[[Harrow's Green]]** — Haelin's square. Dumu does not stand that stone, and does not want the office.
- **[[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]]** — Tora, Nethiro, and Baelon stay their kitchens. Dumu does not become a fourth.
- **[[The Tree-Wardens|The Seat]]** — wrote the queue. Dumu lives in the sentence and does not edit it.
