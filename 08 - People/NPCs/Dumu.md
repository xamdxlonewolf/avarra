# Dumu

> Dumu stands the march-voice at [[Natai]], the person a complaint about the queue of young wood can land on. They do not keep [[Harrow's Green]], and they do not walk the ford.

*(DOO-moo.)* They are of Natai. A neighbour says Dumu, a stranger hears Dumu of Natai, and there is no house. The Sown Waiting, which is what the people of this march call themselves, does not keep a person-list.

## Summary

Dumu stands where [[Saelvaeth]] can be blamed. Saelvaeth is the march where grafts, young Trees planted from living wood, are taking, and the march-voice is the office that hears the queue. The office did not exist when the first pots came. A pot, here, is young wood carried for a town that is waiting. The town exists so the queue has a person.

[[Harrow's Green]] has the canopy, the Tree the hamlets can see. Haelin keeps that square. Tora of Brenod walks a witness home from the ford when the week needs someone who was there. Dumu is the third fact: someone in a town on the road between grafts, who did not write the Seat's sentence and still has to hear it said. The Seat is the college that wrote which town gets wood, and when.

They leave the gate-latch on the post while a complaint is said, and put it back in the catch when the speaker turns. They will not say why.

A pot may come in without a Vaethorn waiting-due, the charge Vaethorn would have written before the wood could move. The Hush allows it. The Hush is the peace that lets this march take a pot without that due. The shout about whose pocket is next, which waiting town the wood should have gone to, is still Dumu's afternoon. They can answer the shout, and they cannot move the wood.

## Appearance & Demeanor

There is latch-grime on the thumb. They face the speaker, and they do not turn to a canopy to borrow authority from a Tree that is not this gate. They use a given name. If someone offers a house to make the name sound like a list, they send that house back as the wrong name.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Natai]]** is the work as well as the town. The voice is one town, and the other graft-towns, the other places a young Tree has taken, stay unnamed.
- **[[Harrow's Green]]** is Haelin's square. Dumu does not stand that stone, and does not want the office.
- **[[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]]** keep Tora, Nethiro, and Baelon in their kitchens. Dumu does not become a fourth kitchen.
- **[[The Tree-Wardens|The Seat]]** wrote the queue. Dumu lives inside the sentence and does not edit it.
