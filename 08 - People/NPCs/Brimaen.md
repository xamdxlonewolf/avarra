# Brimaen

> Keeps a stall inside the walls of [[Rothallo]]. The licence is written. The loaf is sold after the board is wiped.

*(brih-MAYN.)* House **Lanbru** *(LAN-broo).* A neighbour says Brimaen. A clerk says Lanbru, Brimaen. A stranger who uses the old name hears Brimaen of the Inner Close. The seat's name is Rothallo. The house is not a town.

## Summary

Brimaen sells from a stall inside Rothallo's walls. The [[The Walled Book|Book of Tithes]] allows a Conditioned person to keep a stall as a licensed exception. Brimaen is that exception, written. Neighbours treat them as the person at the board. The page treats them as Lanbru, Brimaen, and as a line that can be closed.

They wipe the stall-board with the cut edge of a loaf and then sell the loaf. They will not say why.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Apron, chalk, a key that is not the gate's key. They answer to Brimaen unless the speaker is holding a page. Then they wait to hear the house, because an incomplete row is how a licence starts to fail.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Rothallo]]** — the stall is inside. The beds are outside. Brimaen does not keep the Book.
- **[[The Walled Book|Delamem]]** — keeps the page the licence lives on. Brimaen is not their deputy.
- **[[Breolnir]]** — the gate. A sleep-line and a stall-licence are different ink.
- **[[Methei]]** — assigned outside. Brimaen can see the wall-stone from the stall and does not trade places.

## GM Notes
