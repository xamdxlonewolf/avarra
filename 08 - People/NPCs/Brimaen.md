# Brimaen

> Brimaen keeps a stall inside the walls of [[Rothallo]]. The licence is written, and the loaf is sold after the board is wiped.

The name is Brimaen (brih-MAYN), of house **Lanbru** (LAN-broo), and a neighbour says Brimaen, a clerk says Lanbru, Brimaen, house first, and a stranger who uses the old name hears Brimaen of the Inner Close. The seat's name is Rothallo, and the house is not a town.

## Summary

Brimaen sells from a stall inside Rothallo's walls, where the [[The Walled Book|Book of Tithes]] allows a Conditioned person, someone carrying a Condition, an acquired change, to keep a stall as a licensed exception. Brimaen is that exception, written down, and neighbours treat them as the person at the board while the page treats them as Lanbru, Brimaen, a line that can be closed.

They wipe the stall-board with the cut edge of a loaf and then sell the loaf, and they will not say why.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They work in an apron, with chalk on the board and a key that is not the gate's key. They answer to Brimaen unless the speaker is holding a page. Then they wait to hear the house, because an incomplete row is how a licence starts to fail.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Rothallo]]** — The stall is inside the walls, the beds are outside them, and Brimaen does not keep the Book.
- **[[The Walled Book|Delamem]]** — Delamem keeps the page the licence lives on, and Brimaen is not their deputy.
- **[[Breolnir]]** — Breolnir keeps the gate, and a sleep-line and a stall-licence are different ink.
- **[[Methei]]** — Methei is assigned outside, where Brimaen can see the wall-stone from the stall and does not trade places.
