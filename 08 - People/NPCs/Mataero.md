# Mataero

> Mataero lets the inland loft of [[The White Note House]], the deathless road-house on the quay that kept its beds when it became a desk. There are twelve beds. Eight are let. Four stand open, and the north bed is one of the four. Three of them are for Eolthael, the Turning-month, and the north bed is not to let. Mataero files all of that as occupancy, the house's word for who sleeps.

*(mah-TAY-ro.)* The names here have eroded, and there is no family to put first. They are of the rise behind the third quay. A neighbour says Mataero, and a stranger, or a clerk with the paper shut, gets Mataero of Orentel. While a White Note paper is open the line may be headed White Note, Mataero, and it is the desk. Shut the paper and the quay says Mataero of the rise. On Kumbaan the name is Mataero.

## Summary

They are junior in a deathless house, a house kept by people whose aging has stopped and whose memory of a debt does not fade. The job requires them to believe the house is a bank that happens to have rooms. They did not walk the old mile. They Turned on the rise, stood for the leaf at the local Tree, for free, in a city that still does that and does not like to notice.

If the loft clears and the open beds earn, they think Ledan will see them. They think that seeing will move a number on their mother's paper.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They are young for the house, which is a joke the house can afford, because the house does not age. There is ink on the fingers, and a key to the inland door rather than the quay-desk. They answer in occupancy even when you asked about a name.

They will not copy the north bed onto a term. If you press them, they will tell you that empty is a bed they are not charging for.

## The job

**Leverage:** who gets a loft-term this Hale-month, the spring month when wool and walkers both want a bed. A First-Hand year, which is credit for a walk the buyer does not need to make, and a neighbour's ordinary week can be booked into the same upper room if the junior files both as occupancy. A person whose slip [[Tesara]] did not burn may need a bed. A household [[Vaethod]] sent toward another Tree may already owe one.

They cannot rewrite Ledan's count, and they cannot let the north bed. What they can do is lose a name in a slate that only says *let* or *open*.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Sorel]]** is their mother, still alive, still on a White Note century-instrument from a walk Sorel cannot point to. Mataero's cut of the occupancy money is supposed to be how that paper moves. It has not moved.
- **[[Ledan]]** is [[Long-Lived]], and writes the quay-desk. They are the same house and older, and the Cut-year 280 conversion-query, the old question of how to write one count of years as another, is theirs. Ledan will not explain house custom unless play makes them. If asked, Ledan wants the founding-name spoken once.
- **[[Sorim]]** is factor on the first quay. Sorim will not convert house-years, the White Note's own count of summers, into anybody else's calendar. Sorim treats a pot as cargo and a First-Hand year as not-cargo. Mataero hears a neighbour who cannot file beds.
- **[[Thilim]]** is inland and still making the bed. Mataero cannot see that house except as a warning: a loft that failed to become a slate.
- **[[Reimaethe]]** lets a rise-room by the season. They pay, and they pour, and Mataero has written *let*.
- **[[Lasole]]** holds leaf-lot credit on the same rise, and it is not a White Note term. They have asked Mataero to stand for a season anyway.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Competence Trap, with a Structural Innocent's blindness. The job requires believing that a bed converts cleanly into a number.
- **Arc (forkable, default positive).** *Lie:* the house is a bank that happens to have rooms; if I file occupancy correctly I will be seen. *Ghost:* Sorel's paper, and a childhood of being told the north bed was "not our business." *Need:* see that house custom *is* the credit. *Catalyst:* a court, or Ledan, or a necessity-family who remembers a ford Mataero filed as a term. *Negative fork:* they get the quay-desk by letting the north bed, and become the bank.
- **Lock-in:** knowledge and skill. They have seen the founding-book's Held names, the names the house keeps as credit-tokens, treated as tokens. They are the only junior who can find a bed this Eolthael without asking the desk.
- **Competing interests:** Ledan wants the house to answer as one desk; Sorim holds berths, not beds; Sorel wants to die off the paper, or to understand it; Thilim will ask who is here at dark; the grandchild of a household Vaethod sent toward another Tree wants to eat now and pay a walk they cannot point to.
- **Did not walk.** They think the wave is over because the bed-house collected. That belief is the blindness.
- **Do not** have Ledan explain the north bed in the first scene. Do not move Thilim here. Do not make this house Orentel's government.
