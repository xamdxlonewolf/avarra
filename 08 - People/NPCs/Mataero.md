# Mataero

> Lets the inland loft of [[The White Note House]]. Twelve beds. Eight let. Four open for Eolthael. North bed not to let. They file all of that as occupancy.

*(mah-TAY-ro.)* Eroded drift. Of the rise behind the third quay; a clerk will write *of Orentel*.

## Summary

Junior of a deathless house. They must believe the house is a bank that happens to have rooms. They did not walk. They Turned on the rise, for free, in a city that still does that and does not like to notice.

If the loft clears and the open beds earn, they think Ledan will see them. They think that seeing will move a number on their mother's paper.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Young for the house, which is a joke the house can afford. Ink on the fingers. A key to the inland door, not the quay-desk. They answer in occupancy even when you asked about a name.

They will not copy the north bed onto a term. They will tell you empty is a bed they are not charging for.

## The job

**Leverage:** who gets a loft-term this Hale-month. A First-Hand year and a neighbour's week can be booked into the same upper room if the junior files both as occupancy. A person [[Tesara]] did not burn may need a bed. A household [[Vaethod]] sent may already owe one.

They cannot rewrite Ledan's count. They cannot let the north bed. They can lose a name in a slate that only says *let* or *open*.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Sorel]]** — mother, still alive, still on a White Note century-instrument from a walk Sorel cannot point to. Mataero's cut of occupancy is supposed to be how that paper moves. It has not moved.
- **Ledan** — writes the quay-desk. Same house, older mouth. Will not explain house custom unless play makes them. Wants, if asked, the founding-name spoken once.
- **[[Sorim]]** — factor on the first quay. Will not convert house-years. Treats a pot as cargo and a First-Hand year as not-cargo. Mataero hears a neighbour who cannot file beds.
- **[[Thilim]]** — inland, still making the bed. Mataero cannot see that house except as a warning: a loft that failed to become a slate.
- **[[Reimaethe]]** — lets a rise-room by the season. Pays. Pours. Mataero has written *let*.
- **[[Lasole]]** — leaf-lot credit, same rise. Not a White Note term. They have asked Mataero to stand for a season anyway.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Competence Trap, with a Structural Innocent's blindness. The job requires believing the conversion.
- **Arc (forkable, default positive).** *Lie:* the house is a bank that happens to have rooms; if I file occupancy correctly I will be seen. *Ghost:* Sorel's paper, and a childhood of being told the north bed was "not our business." *Need:* see that house custom *is* the credit. *Catalyst:* a court, or Ledan, or a necessity-family who remembers a ford Mataero filed as a term. *Negative fork:* they get the quay-desk by letting the north bed, and become the bank.
- **Lock-in:** knowledge + skill. They have seen the founding-book's Held names as tokens. They are the only junior who can find a bed this Eolthael without asking the desk.
- **Competing interests:** Ledan (the house is one mouth); Sorim (holds, not beds); Sorel (to die off the paper, or to understand it); Thilim (mouths at dark); a sent household's grandchild (eat now, pay a walk they cannot point to).
- **Clock:** did not walk. The wave is over because the bed-house collected. That is the blindness.
- **Do not** have Ledan explain the north bed in the first scene. Do not move Thilim here. Do not make this house Orentel's government.
