# Rosire

> Brought water at [[Tesara]]'s shed. This Hale-month they are not on that floor. An upper room at the live front. Still wants to be forgotten.

*(ro-SEER-eh.)* Eroded drift. Of [[Orentel]]. Sleeping at [[Harrow's Green]].

## Summary

They had a key to a prepared room in a house that keeps a fire safe and does not show it. They opened a door they were not scheduled to open. Someone walked. Rosire walked the other way, took a ticket-yard job in a city that prices competence, and does not use their old byname.

They are not on Tesara's water this week. They walked inland with the person who walked out. They want that person — and any child a fire might answer — not to become a story, and themselves not sent for.

The house is [[The Protectors]]. This is one hand with a key they no longer have. Do not write Vimein's Care on this page.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Infirmary-tired. Grey under the nails from other people's stone. They do not talk about travel. They flinch at a room that has been cleared and laid with sand. They are good at the first drill and bad at being asked *where did you sit before*.

## How they reach a table

- [[Harrow's Green]] upper room. Water. A closed travelling-lamp they will not call a relic. A question they will not answer.
- A prepared-room habit they cannot hide from someone who has seen one. Sand. A flinch.
- A runner from the house they left. Not a hunt in the first scene. A disappointment. A job offer that is not a job.

## What this note does not do

Does not write how fire is caught or stored. Does not write Vimein's inner court. Does not make them a chosen defector-hero. Does not script a reunion with [[Taeren]].

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Competence Trap. The skill that made them useful in the sanctuary (preparing a death-room, banking a coal, sitting a body that would burn) is why they kept the lamp. It is also the tell.
- **Opening sits at [[Harrow's Green]].** Kit: [[The Opening]]. This opening picks the walker as remnant and the new Gift as Taeren *or* a PC in that seat. Collision with "Tesara's shed" as current floor: [[Contradictions|C-05]].
- **Arc.** *Lie:* if I am quiet here, the house will treat me as weather. *Need:* pick whether the opened door was mercy or theft — and live with the house answering. *Want:* forgotten. Gratitude, rage, or nothing from the party stays player choice.
- **Engine:** [[When the Fire Is Caught]]. Do not invent a second Gift to make their guilt tidier. The person they walked out is leftover fire. The child at the canopy is the new leaf (or the PC). Two Gifts at once is not legal.
- **Intersection:** same square as Taeren this Hale-month, different rooms. Not a crew with [[Reimaethe]] or [[Hithaen]]. Tesara stays in Orentel. Play can return to the shed later; do not start there.
- **~5%:** a cleared room, a flinch, a lamp that should be cold. Not a confession in scene one.
