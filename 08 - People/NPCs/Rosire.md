# Rosire

> Works the ticket-infirmary downwind of [[Tesara]]'s shed. Brings water. Flies a Gaze when asked to sit a drill. Wants to be forgotten.

*(ro-SEER-eh.)* Eroded drift. Of Orentel, now. Seed, not a plot.

## Summary

They had a key to a prepared room in a house that keeps a fire safe and does not show it. They opened a door they were not scheduled to open. Someone walked. Rosire walked the other way, took a ticket-yard job in a city that prices competence, and does not use their old byname.

They want the child gone and themselves not sent for. They will sit Tesara's week if the floor can spare them, because a raw Gaze is a problem they understand and a prepared death is one they are trying not to.

The house is [[The Protectors]]. This is one hand with a key they no longer have. Do not write Vimein's Care on this page.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Infirmary-tired. Grey under the nails from other people's stone. They do not talk about travel. They flinch at a room that has been cleared and laid with sand. They are good at the first drill and bad at being asked *where did you sit before*.

## How they reach a table

- Tesara's shed. Water. A spare pair of hands. A question they will not answer.
- A stiller's ticket they can stamp, and a prepared-room habit they cannot hide from someone who has seen one.
- A runner from the house they left. Not a hunt in the first scene. A disappointment. A job offer that is not a job.

## What this note does not do

Does not name the Protectors. Does not write how fire is caught or stored. Does not decide whether the walker was remnant or Gift. Does not pair them with [[Taeren]] on a map as if the road were already walked. Does not make them a chosen defector-hero.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern (if play picks them up):** Competence Trap. The skill that made them useful in the sanctuary (preparing a death-room, sitting a body that would burn) is the skill Tesara needs. It is also the tell.
- **Seed only.** The house is [[The Protectors]]: secret Phoenix worship, removal from public life, controlled deaths, harvested fire, erased memory, institutional power. This person helped someone leave. That is all this page locks.
- **Arc (if picked up).** *Lie:* if I am quiet here, the house will treat me as weather. *Need:* pick whether the opened door was mercy or theft — and live with the house answering. *Want:* forgotten.
- **Engine:** [[When the Fire Is Caught]]. Do not invent a second Gift to make their guilt tidier. The child they helped may be the remnant, or may be a captive who still had a Rise. Taeren may be a different leaf. Epic 10 may pick. Do not pick here.
- **Intersection:** Tesara's shed, not a crew with the other seeds.
- **~5%:** a cleared room and a flinch. Not a confession in scene one.
