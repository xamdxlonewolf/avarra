# Rosire

> Rosire brought water at [[Tesara]]'s shed, the catching-week where a newly Struck person is sat off the street. This Hale-month, the spring month, they are not on that floor. They are in an upper room at the live front, the square where the wood is being argued now, and they still want to be forgotten.

*(ro-SEER-eh.)* The names on this coast have eroded, and there is no house. They are of [[Orentel]]. A neighbour says Rosire, and a stranger gets Rosire of Orentel. They do not use the byname from before this floor, the name that would say where they sat before the ticket-yard. On Kumbaan the name is Rosire, and they are sleeping at [[Harrow's Green]].

## Summary

Rosire once held a post in a house they do not name. They opened a door they were not scheduled to open, and someone walked out through it. Rosire walked the other way, took a ticket-yard job, the infirmary work of the Stillers in a city that prices competence, and does not use their old byname.

They are not on Tesara's water this week. They walked inland with the person who walked out. They want that person, and any child a fire might answer, not to become a story, and they want themselves not sent for.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They look infirmary-tired, with grey under the nails from other people's stone. They do not talk about travel. They are good at the first drill, the practice that works a stillness back out of a body before it settles, and bad at being asked *where did you sit before*.

## How they reach a table

- The upper room at [[Harrow's Green]] is where they are. There is water, and a question they will not answer.
- A runner may come from the house they left. It is not a hunt in the first scene. It is a disappointment, and a job offer that is not a job.

## GM Notes

- **Links:** [[The Protectors]] · [[Phoenix]] · [[The Opening]]
- **The house is [[The Protectors]].** Rosire had a key to a prepared room in the house that catches Phoenix fire and keeps it hidden. The door they opened let a person walk out of that room. This is one hand with a key they no longer have.
- **Keep out of the player body:** how fire is caught or stored, Vimein's inner court, and any chosen defector-hero. Do not script a reunion with [[Taeren]].
- **Tells.** They carry a closed travelling-lamp they will not call a relic. They flinch at a room that has been cleared and laid with sand, a prepared-room habit they cannot hide from someone who has seen one. The tells are small, and they are not a confession in scene one.
- **Pattern:** the skill that made them useful in the sanctuary, preparing a death-room, banking a coal, sitting a body that would burn, is why they kept the lamp. That same skill is the tell.
- **Opening sits at [[Harrow's Green]].** The kit is [[The Opening]]. This opening picks the walker as the remnant and the new Gift as Taeren, or as a PC in that seat. The collision with "Tesara's shed" as their current floor is [[Contradictions|C-05]].
- **Arc.** *Lie:* if I am quiet here, the house will treat me as weather. *Need:* pick whether the opened door was mercy or theft, and live with the house's answer. *Want:* to be forgotten. Gratitude, rage, or nothing from the party stays the players' choice.
- **Engine:** [[When the Fire Is Caught]]. Do not invent a second Gift to make their guilt tidier. The person they walked out is leftover fire. The child at the canopy is the new leaf, or the PC. Two Gifts at once is not legal.
- **Intersection:** they are in the same square as Taeren this Hale-month, in different rooms. They are not a crew with [[Reimaethe]] or [[Hithaen]]. Tesara stays in Orentel. Play can return to the shed later, and it should not start there.
- **About 5% frightening:** a cleared room, a flinch, and a lamp that should be cold.
