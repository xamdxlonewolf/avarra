# Methei

> Given. The house is still on them. They live inside [[Rothallo]]'s walls, and the [[The Walled Book|Book]] has set their turn of work at the beds outside the wall.

*(METH-ay.)* House **Sithlaen** *(sith-LAYN).* A neighbour says Methei. The Book says Sithlaen, Methei. The line cast them out of the heir's rank and kept the house. *Closed* is no longer their rank. The name stayed.

## Summary

Methei took a colour. In the Close that is a disinheritance. They are a person. They are not a Closed Heir. Everyone who lives inside the walls owes the Close turns of work, and the Book picks a turn by the Condition. The list was already drafted for what Methei became, and it puts them on the beds outside the wall, which is the lodging-country's work done as a turn. Nobody holds them. A person with a Condition can walk out of the gate on any day it is open and live somewhere else, and the Book has no claim on anyone who has left. Methei has stayed.

They are not Talnin, whose tenth year is unfinished. They are not [[Hithaen]], who eats in [[Eolvaeth]] with a letter unopened. Methei is at the wall this year, on the outer side.

They turn the blanket so the stripe faces the wall, not the door. They will not say why.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Outer-side clothes, a house-name they flinch at and answer to when a page is in the room. They make a bed the way an inn makes a bed, and when the turn is done at dark they stop.
