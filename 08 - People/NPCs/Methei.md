# Methei

> Given. The house is still on them. The [[The Walled Book|Book]] assigned them to the beds outside [[Rothallo]]'s wall.

*(METH-ay.)* House **Sithlaen** *(sith-LAYN).* A neighbour says Methei. The Book says Sithlaen, Methei. The line cast them out of the heir's rank and kept the house. *Closed* is no longer their rank. The name stayed.

## Summary

Methei took a colour. In the Close that is a disinheritance. They are a person. They are not a Closed Heir. The labour-line was already drafted for what they became, and it puts them on the beds outside the wall: the lodging-country's work, done as an assignment.

They are not Talnin, whose tenth year is unfinished. They are not [[Hithaen]], who eats in [[Eolvaeth]] with a letter unopened. Methei is at the wall, this year, on the outer side.

They turn the blanket so the stripe faces the wall, not the door. They will not say why.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Outer-side clothes, a house-name they flinch at and answer to when a page is in the room. They make a bed the way an inn makes a bed, and they stop at dark if they are allowed to stop.
