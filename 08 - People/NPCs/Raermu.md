# Raermu

> Raermu carries the medicine [[Seinbrun]]'s guild furnishes. The basket is the job, and the latch comes first.

People in the lane say Raermu (RAYR-moo), and a stranger hears Raermu of Seinbrun. There is no house-name to give them.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | A stiller's hour for a Kept child the city pities |
| **Have** | The basket, the guild's stock, the doors that open to the hymn |
| **Fear** | A house on the step that the stock is not for, and no other door to send them to |
| **Live conflict** | Medicine is public tending, and the public is the congregation |

## Summary

Raermu carries what the hall's guild has put up for the sick: salves, a stiller's hour when [[The Stillers|a stiller]] is in the city, and the ordinary work of a place that furnishes care instead of filing it. A stiller is someone ticketed to hold a body still, Raermu is not a stiller by that ticket, and they are the person the basket is handed to.

They open the latch before they knock, and they will not say why.

The [[Law and Citizenship|Kept]] in Seinbrun, people the Turning hugged and left without a colour, are pitied in plenty, and Raermu sees the child that pity is about, though the basket's rule and Raermu's want are not always the same hour.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They carry a covered basket, the latch thumbed open while they are still in the lane, and they are quiet once they are in the room. They look at the bed before they look at the person who called them, and they are embarrassed when they are seen doing it.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Vuthbraen]]** — The hall sends Raermu out with the basket, and the hall does not follow them into the house.
- **[[Breillai]]** — Breillai's step is one the stock may not cross, and Raermu has not decided what their hands will do if they are already holding the basket when they arrive.
- **[[Seinbrun]]** — The medicine is the work, beside the hall, and it is not a second office.
