# Raermu

> Carries the medicine [[Seinbrun]]'s guild furnishes. The basket is the job. The latch comes first.

*(RAYR-moo.)* Of Seinbrun. A neighbour says Raermu. A stranger hears Raermu of Seinbrun. There is no house.

## Summary

Raermu carries what the hall's guild has put up for the sick: salves, a stiller's hour when one is in the city, the ordinary work of a place that furnishes instead of filing. They are not [[The Stillers|a stiller]] by ticket. They are the person the basket is handed to.

They open the latch before they knock. They will not say why.

The [[Law and Citizenship|Kept]] in Seinbrun are pitied in plenty. Raermu sees the child the pity is about. The basket's rule and Raermu's want are not always the same hour.

## Appearance & Demeanor

A covered basket, the latch thumbed open while they are still in the lane. Quiet in the room. They look at the bed before they look at the person who called them, and they are embarrassed when they are seen doing it.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Vuthbraen]]** — the hall sends them. The hall does not follow them into the house.
- **[[Breillai]]** — a step the stock may not cross. Raermu has not decided what their hands will do if they are already holding the basket.
- **[[Seinbrun]]** — the work of medicine, beside the hall. Not a second office.
