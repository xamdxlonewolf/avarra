# Rithim

> Speaker of [[Maiethlir]]. Names the leaf-colour at Leaf-Fall. A clerk waits to copy it. Some summers they let that happen in the same breath. Some they do not.

*(RITH-im.)* Conservative drift. Of Maiethlir. The crown has not replaced them. The Seat has not recalled them.

## Summary

In a small town the warden Speaks. Here the office has its own mouth. Rithim is that mouth: Reckoned Hands pressure, practice-first, not Kind. They Speak truly. They treat the copy as a second, lesser seeing.

They are a Hands-child of this river. The extra mile is a civic season. They do not narrate the age as a pilgrimage they missed. They narrate it as a hymn the clerks moved into.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Elder. Grey pinned back with a wooden pin that is not a lintel-mark. Voice built for a square, used shorter than it used to be. They stand closer to the Tree than to the tablet-hall, and the clerks have filed that as habit.

They will point a pilgrim at the Motherwood if asked where the college is. They will not walk them there.

## The job

**Leverage:** until they Speak, the colour is weather. After they Speak, it is a civic fact. The Threnhael still needs a line. The delay between those two things is the only refusal the roll has to metabolize without replacing the Speaker.

They cannot unsay a colour. They cannot keep a clerk from writing *delay noted*. They can make the hymn happen first, and they can refuse to read a household aloud.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Valein]]** *(vah-LAYN)* — Kept, same household, unmarked. A partner, not a child; the square knows them as the person who brings Rithim a cup after a Speaking. The verso of last Eolthael's copy-slip already noted an unmarked in the household ([[Maiethlir#In-world text]]). Rithim has not said the name into a hall.
- **[[Laevila]]** — keeps the old room under the recut chapel. Same morning, some years: one mouth Speaks, one describes. They eat together after Night Watch and do not agree what that means.
- **The tablet-clerks** — Long-Lived among them. They want the copy in the same breath. They have not yet asked for Valein by name in front of the Tree.
- **[[Vehaela]] · [[Baerith]]** — other wants on this square. See [[People of the Turning]].
- **The First Seat** — beside, in the wood. Not a master they report to by the week. Proximity is pressure.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Threshold Guardian, with a private Bridge (household vs roll).
- **Arc (positive).** *Lie:* delaying the copy is the same as refusing. *Ghost:* a summer they let the copy happen in one breath and a Kept neighbour was written as a lapse. *Need:* a refusal that costs the office, or honesty that the delay is already a kind of filing. *Catalyst:* the clerks ask for Valein on the same page as a colour.
- **Lock-in:** legal + moral. They are the Speaking this city will take. Replacing them would be a Reckoned-Hand fight the Seat has not chosen. Walking away leaves Valein to the verso.
- **Competing interests:** the roll (one name, one colour, one breath); the Seat (the Tree witnessed); Valein (to stay a person who is not a column); Laevila (the other colour spoken as seen); a necessity-family in an uncounted loft (the leftover the roll has not found).
- **Clock:** Hands-child, origin-town. The wave is "over" as a requirement and not over as a street.
- **Do not** capture the First Seat through this mouth. Incomplete refusal is load-bearing.
- **Do not** confirm the loud river as her voice.
