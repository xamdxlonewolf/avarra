# Rithim

> Rithim is Speaker of [[Maiethlir]], the elder who names the leaf-colour at Leaf-Fall so the town can treat it as a fact. A clerk waits to copy that colour onto the roll. Some summers Rithim lets the Speaking and the copy happen in the same breath. Some summers they do not.

*(RITH-im.)* House **Nirsei** *(nir-SAY)*, on the conservative side of the river, where a clerk still puts the house first. A neighbour says Rithim, a clerk says Nirsei, Rithim, and a stranger gets Rithim of Maiethlir. On Kumbaan the name is Rithim. The crown has not replaced them, and the Seat, the college of wardens in the Motherwood, has not recalled them.

## Summary

In a small town the warden Speaks, and the Speaking is the act of naming the colour aloud. Here the Speaker is a separate office, and Rithim holds it under Reckoned Hands pressure, the crown's tablet offices pushing on the rite. The office is taken by practice, by doing the work, and not by Kind, the ancestry a person is born. They Speak truly. They treat the copy as a second, lesser seeing.

They are a Hands-child of this river, raised where a town's own Tree is the ordinary way a child Turns, so the extra mile is a civic season. They do not narrate the age as a pilgrimage they missed. They narrate it as a hymn the clerks moved into.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They are elder, with the grey pinned back by a wooden pin that marks no office. The voice was built for a square, and they use it shorter than they used to. They stand closer to the Tree than to the tablet-hall, and the clerks have filed that stance as a habit.

If a pilgrim asks where the college is, they will point at the Motherwood. They will not walk the pilgrim there.

## The job

**Leverage:** until they Speak, the colour is weather, and after they Speak, it is a civic fact. The Threnhael, Threnmaieth's roll of names and colours, still needs a line. The delay between the Spoken colour and the copied line is the only refusal the roll has to absorb without replacing the Speaker.

They cannot unsay a colour, and they cannot keep a clerk from writing *delay noted*. They can make the hymn happen first, and they can refuse to read a household aloud.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Valein]]** *(vah-LAYN)* is Kept, which means the Tree closed around them in a hug and gave no colour, so the roll's word is unmarked. They are the same household. The house is Nirsei, if a clerk is allowed the second name. Valein is a partner, not a child, and the square knows them as the person who brings Rithim a cup after a Speaking. The verso of last Eolthael's copy-slip, Eolthael being the Turning-month, already noted an unmarked person in the household ([[Maiethlir#In-world text]]). Rithim has not said the name into a hall.
- **[[Laevila]]** keeps the old room under the recut chapel. Some years the same morning holds both of them: one Speaks, and one describes. They eat together after the Night Watch, the silent vigil the night before Turning-Week, and they do not agree what that meal means.
- **The tablet-clerks** include Long-Lived among them, deathless people whose memory of a fall does not fade, and they want the copy in the same breath as the Speaking. They have not yet asked for Valein by name in front of the Tree.
- **[[Vehaela]] · [[Baerith]]** carry other wants on this square. See [[People of the Turning]].
- **The First Seat** sits beside this city, in the wood. It is not a master they report to by the week. The nearness is the pressure.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Threshold Guardian, with a private Bridge between the household and the roll.
- **Arc (positive).** *Lie:* delaying the copy is the same thing as refusing it. *Ghost:* one summer they let the copy happen in a single breath, and a Kept neighbour was written down as a lapse. *Need:* a refusal that costs the office, or else an honesty that the delay is already a kind of filing. *Catalyst:* the clerks ask for Valein on the same page as a colour.
- **Lock-in:** the law of the office and a moral at once. They are the Speaking this city will take. Replacing them would be a Reckoned-Hand fight the Seat has not chosen. Walking away leaves Valein to the verso.
- **Competing interests:** the roll wants one name, one colour, and one breath; the Seat wants the Tree witnessed; Valein wants to stay a person who is not a column on a tablet; Laevila wants the other colour spoken as it was seen; a necessity-family in an uncounted loft is the household the roll has not found.
- **Where they come from.** They are a Hands-child, and this is the origin-town side of the river. The wave of required walking is over as a requirement, and it is not over as a street.
- **Do not** capture the First Seat through this Speaker. The refusal has to stay incomplete. That incompleteness is load-bearing.
- **Do not** confirm the loud river as her voice.
