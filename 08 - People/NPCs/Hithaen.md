# Hithaen

> Hithaen arrived in [[Eolvaeth]]'s gift-hall this Hale-month, the spring month, with no list behind them and a name they will not put on a wall. They are Given, a colour fallen for them at the Leaf-Fall. They used to be an heir of a house that counts the Kept, the people the Tree hugged and left without a Condition, as the line that may rule. The leaf took that.

*(hith-AYN.)* The given name is conservative, and the house is **Brethlumal** *(breth-LU-mal)*. The line cast them out and kept the name on them. They will not say it, and they will not take *of Eolvaeth*. A neighbour in the vale says Hithaen, while a clerk behind the wall would say Brethlumal, Hithaen, and a letter that still uses the house has not been opened. On Kumbaan the name is Hithaen.

## Summary

Somewhere behind the walls that are still standing, families that have stayed Kept for generations are still a class. A Book of Tithes, a roster of taxes, restrictions, and labour, is how that class keeps itself. A child Given at Leaf-Fall, the tenth-year week when a leaf can fall, can fall out of the line. Hithaen fell.

They want a life that is not a wall-pass, not a life of being let through the wall. They also want, on worse nights, the name back. Those are not the same want. [[Vaethod]], the town-warden, has not decided whether Hithaen came to wait or to be counted.

What is left of the arrangement they walked out of is [[The Walled Book]], the Inner Close inside [[Orenbren]], political rank and not a church. This is one person eating alms in a vale that has no list, which is why they walked here.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They are young, and the clothes were good last year and are wrong for a camp-street. They flinch when someone says *unmarked* as pity. Unmarked is the roll's word for a person who carries no Condition, and they were unmarked, and it was power, and now they are marked and it is not. They do not sermon, and they ask where bread is and who has to see them eat it.

## How they reach a table

- The gift-hall is the door. Vaethod can tell a clerk from a person who came to wait for the Tree. Hithaen is neither yet.
- They have a vouching problem. Vouching is someone standing up to say what a person is now, so the town will keep them, and they need that standing for the person they have become. Sold vouching is an Orentel alley rather than this vale's custom, so a party that will stand for them is a session.
- A letter from behind a wall still uses their old name. They have not opened the last one.
