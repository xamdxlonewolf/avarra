# Hithaen

> Arrived in [[Eolvaeth]]'s gift-hall this Hale-month with no list behind them and a name they will not put on a wall. Given. Used to be an heir of a house that counts the Kept as the line that may rule. The leaf took that.

*(hith-AYN.)* Conservative given-name. House **Brethlumal** *(breth-LU-mal)*. The line cast them out and kept the name on them. They will not say it. They will not take *of Eolvaeth*. A neighbour in the vale says Hithaen. A clerk behind the wall would say Brethlumal, Hithaen. A letter that still uses the house has not been opened. On Kumbaan the name is Hithaen. Seed, not a plot.

## Summary

Somewhere behind surviving walls, Kept blood is still a class. A Book of Tithes — taxes, restrictions, labour — is how that class keeps itself. A child Given at Leaf-Fall can fall out of the line. Hithaen fell.

They want a life that is not a wall-pass. They also want, on worse nights, the name back. Those are not the same want. [[Vaethod]] has not decided if this mouth came to wait or to be counted.

The remnant is [[The Walled Book]] — Inner Close inside [[Orenbren]], not a sixteenth flag. This is one person eating alms in a vale that has no list, which is why they walked here. Do not write the Book on this page.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Young. Clothes that were good last year and are wrong for a camp-street. They flinch when someone says *unmarked* as pity — they were unmarked, and it was power, and now they are marked and it is not. They do not sermon. They ask where bread is and who has to see them eat it.

## How they reach a table

- The gift-hall. Vaethod can tell a clerk from a mouth. Hithaen is neither yet.
- A vouching problem: they need someone to stand for what they are *now*. Sold vouching is an Orentel alley, not this vale's custom. A party that will stand is a session.
- A letter from behind a wall that still uses their old name. They have not opened the last one.

## What this note does not do

Does not name the surviving power. Does not decide whether that regime replaces a weak existing polity or lives inside a successor — that is a later geography story. Does not add a sixteenth great power. Does not write the Book of Tithes. Does not make Hithaen a chosen exile.

- **Arc (if play picks them up).** *Lie:* if I get the name back I get myself back. *Need:* a standing that is witnessed for the person who Turned, not the line that lost them. *Want:* bread, a stall, or a wall-pass — they will not admit which.
- **House.** Brethlumal is the line that kept the name after the leaf. They will not say it in the vale. Do not turn it into *of Eolvaeth*. Do not write the Book on this page. The civic slot is the house, not a Kind.
- **Kind is incidental.** Do not make them a Kind-champion of the old walls.
- **Intersection:** [[Vaethod]]'s hall. A later road to [[Orentel]] if they need a warrant. Not a party with [[Reimaethe]] or [[Taeren]].
- **The leaf did not disinherit them; the class rule did.**
