# Reimaethe

> A [[Long-Lived]] who poured four years into a cup and wants to stop before the fifth. Pays a rise-loft on time. Neighbours call it a rich uncle's hobby. They do not look like a hobby.

*(ray-MY-eth-eh.)* Given-name from the conservative working set. Of the rise, [[Orentel]]. Seed, not a plot.

## Summary

They outlived a family and did the thing a few of the deathless have started doing: poured what they could not keep carrying into a vessel they named themselves. [[The Unspent|The Poured God]]. A shelf. A drop of blood as record. Four years gone into the cupboard. The fifth is the one the circle treats as the real gift.

They want out. They want the years back thin if that is all that is offered. They want [[Mataero]] to keep writing *let* and not *why*.

The congregation that will wear this practice — volunteered five-year sacrifice, shared blood-memory, a god they believe they created — is a later story. This is one person, one shelf, one loft.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Deathless and recently emptied. They forget the ordinary way a person forgets a decade they sold. They wait to be asked in. They smile when someone says *Poured God* the quay-way, and the smile does not reach the year they are trying not to start.

A second cup sits next to the first. They have not asked to drink. Asking is already a social cost.

## How they reach a table

- The loft. Mataero files occupancy. A party sleeping the rise may hear a cup set down every night and no toast.
- A novelty-house or endowed library that still has their name on a chair they no longer sit. The Tithe to learn is harder with four years missing.
- Someone from the circle coming to collect the fifth year. Not a hunt. A visit. A disappointment.

## What this note does not do

Does not name a sect. Does not write the withdrawal-price as law. Does not make the cup a sixth Turning. Does not fold this person into Phoenix-keepers or a walled Kept house.

## GM Notes

- **Seed only.** Story R.7 builds the fringe house: volunteered five-year sacrifice, shared blood-memory, withdrawal possible but socially costly. Use [[The Unspent]] for the presence. Reimaethe is the mouth that wants out *before* that house is finished.
- **Arc (if play picks them up).** *Lie:* I invented the listener, so I can uninvent the debt. *Need:* accept that the circle will not call stopping a refund — and stop anyway. *Want:* the fifth year kept in their own head.
- **Tone.** ~5%. Sad and a little vain, not a blood cult. The scary edge is a circle that will not let someone stop. That edge is later; hint, do not staff.
- **Do not** confirm the cupboard was already in the household in player text. The hobby and the claim are public. The older furniture is behind [[The Unspent]]'s wall.
- **Intersection:** [[Mataero]]'s slate. Not a party with [[Hithaen]] or [[Taeren]].
