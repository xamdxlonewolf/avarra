# Reimaethe

> Reimaethe is [[Long-Lived]], deathless, aging stopped, and has given four years into the cup, one year at a time, and wants to stop before the fifth. They pay a rise-loft on time. Neighbours call the practice a rich uncle's hobby, and they do not look like a hobby.

*(RYE-may-theh.)* The given name comes from the conservative working set. They are of the rise, [[Orentel]]. A neighbour says Reimaethe, a stranger gets Reimaethe of Orentel, and there is no house. On Kumbaan the name is Reimaethe.

## Summary

They outlived a family, and they did what a few of the deathless have started doing. Every Long-Lived person is asked once to join [[The Pourers]], and Reimaethe said yes. They gave away years they could no longer keep carrying. A member gives one year at a time, each on its own night, with a drop of blood as the record, because a Long-Lived already reads memory in blood. The year goes into the cup and is gone from the member's memory. The papers from that year go onto the shelf, and the circle counts the member one year younger. The circle says the cup holds a god it made. [[The Unspent]] is the presence in it, and it stores what it is given. The circle asks five years of a member in all, and Reimaethe has given four. A member may stop before any year, and the years already given stay in the cup.

They want to stop. They want their papers back, thin as that copy is, because the papers are all the circle returns. They want [[Mataero]], who lets the loft and files who sleeps, to keep writing *let* and not *why*.

This is one person, one shelf, and one loft, and the circle around them is [[The Pourers]].

## Appearance & Demeanor

They are deathless and recently emptied, and they forget the ordinary way a person forgets the four years they gave. They wait to be asked in, which is the courtesy of the deathless at a threshold. They smile when someone says *Poured God* the quay-way, the neighbours' name for the presence in the cup, and the smile does not reach the year they are trying not to start.

A second cup sits next to the first. They have not asked to drink from it, because asking would already cost them something with the circle.

## How they reach a table

- The loft is the ordinary way in. Mataero files occupancy, the word for who sleeps, and a party sleeping the rise may hear a cup set down every night and no toast.
- A novelty-house or an endowed library may still have their name on a chair they no longer sit. The Tithe, the upkeep a Long-Lived pays by continuing to learn, is harder with four years missing.
- A member of the circle may come to ask whether they will give the fifth year. It is a visit and not a hunt, and the visitor will be disappointed.

## GM Notes

- **Reimaethe is right about how the Pourers give.** A member gives one year at a time, may stop before any year, and the years already given stay in the cup. The papers are what come back, as a thinner copy. The ask, the refusal, and the founder's taking under [[The Pourers]] do not change.
- **Use as a hook, not a plot.** They stay offstage in [[The Isolated Fall]]. Do not plot Molevad's circle around them. Use [[The Unspent]] for the presence.
- **Arc (if play picks them up).** *Lie:* I invented the listener, so I can uninvent the debt. *Need:* accept that the circle will not call stopping a refund, and stop anyway. *Want:* the fifth year kept in their own head.
- **Tone.** About 5% frightening. Play them sad and a little vain, not as a blood cult. The hard edge is that stopping costs them their friends and their seat in the long-house. Hint at that cost, and do not stage it.
- **Do not say in player text that the Unspent was in the household before the circle named it.** The hobby and the claim are public. The older fact stays behind [[The Unspent]]'s wall.
- **Intersection:** [[Mataero]]'s slate. They are not a party with [[Hithaen]] or [[Taeren]].
