# Reimaethe

> A [[Long-Lived]] who has given four years into the cup, one year at a time, and wants to stop before the fifth. Pays a rise-loft on time. Neighbours call it a rich uncle's hobby. They do not look like a hobby.

*(RYE-may-theh.)* Given-name from the conservative working set. Of the rise, [[Orentel]]. A neighbour says Reimaethe. A stranger gets Reimaethe of Orentel. There is no house. On Kumbaan the name is Reimaethe.

## Summary

They outlived a family and did what a few of the deathless have started doing. They joined [[The Pourers]] and gave away years they could not keep carrying. A member gives one year at a time, each on its own night, with a drop of blood as the record. The year goes into the cup and is gone from the member's memory. The member's papers from that year go onto the shelf, and the circle counts the member one year younger. The circle says the cup holds a god it made. [[The Unspent]] is the presence in it, and it stores what it is given. The circle asks five years of a member in all, and Reimaethe has given four. A member may stop before any year, and the years already given stay in the cup.

They want to stop. They want their papers back, thin as that copy is, because the papers are all the circle returns. They want [[Mataero]] to keep writing *let* and not *why*.

This is one person, one shelf, one loft. The circle around them is [[The Pourers]].

## Appearance & Demeanor

Deathless and recently emptied. They forget the ordinary way a person forgets a decade they sold. They wait to be asked in. They smile when someone says *Poured God* the quay-way, and the smile does not reach the year they are trying not to start.

A second cup sits next to the first. They have not asked to drink from it. Asking would already cost them something with the circle.

## How they reach a table

- The loft. Mataero files occupancy. A party sleeping the rise may hear a cup set down every night and no toast.
- A novelty-house or endowed library that still has their name on a chair they no longer sit. The Tithe to learn is harder with four years missing.
- A member of the circle coming to ask whether they will give the fifth year. It is a visit and not a hunt, and the visitor will be disappointed.

## GM Notes

- **Reimaethe is right about how the Pourers give.** A member gives one year at a time, may stop before any year, and the years already given stay in the cup. The papers are what come back, as a thinner copy. The ask, the refusal, and the founder's taking under [[The Pourers]] do not change.
- **Use as a hook, not a plot.** They stay offstage in [[The Isolated Fall]]. Do not plot Molevad's circle around them. Use [[The Unspent]] for the presence.
- **Arc (if play picks them up).** *Lie:* I invented the listener, so I can uninvent the debt. *Need:* accept that the circle will not call stopping a refund, and stop anyway. *Want:* the fifth year kept in their own head.
- **Tone.** About 5% frightening. Sad and a little vain, not a blood cult. The hard edge is that stopping costs them their friends and their seat in the long-house. Hint at it and do not stage it.
- **Do not say in player text that the Unspent was in the household before the circle named it.** The hobby and the claim are public. The older fact is behind [[The Unspent]]'s wall.
- **Intersection:** [[Mataero]]'s slate. Not a party with [[Hithaen]] or [[Taeren]].
