# Brormei

> Stands where the west road meets the Well-wash, at [[Larbril]]. Says what the water is this week.

*(bror-MAY.)* Of Larbril. A neighbour says Brormei. A stranger hears Brormei of Larbril. Saelthael does not keep a person-list. There is no house.

## Summary

Brormei works the meeting at Larbril. In a kind Liren the [[Named Ground|Well-wash]] is a river and the ford is obvious. In a cruel one it is a silt-line, and the carts still come, because the walk west did not stop when the core's did. Brormei says which week it is, and whether a column goes up toward the [[Named Ground|Shelf-gate]].

When the wash is dry they still wet the meeting-stone from a jar. They will not say why.

They are not Theisva, and they do not chalk Ornsael's rope. The well-town is farther into the dry. The [[The Dry Stair|Dry Stair]] is a different rise. People may leave Larbril to climb it. Brormei's job is the road and the wash.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Dust at the hem even in a river year. A jar that is not a relic. They talk like a person who has already looked at the line this morning, and they are short with travellers who want the line to be a hymn.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Larbril]]** — the meeting is the work. One mouth. Not a warden who invents a colour. The Hand Speaks most years. That is ordinary.
- **[[Ornsael]]** — Theisva, Lesna, Bovaer. Their share is the well. They are not reassigned here.
- **[[Tasain]]** — the levy, on the far side of the pass. Brormei does not collect it.
- **[[Lirorn]]** — the Shelf-gate is ahead or behind, depending on the day. The pass is not Brormei's gate.
