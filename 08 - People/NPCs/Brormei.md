# Brormei

> Brormei stands where the west road meets the [[Named Ground|Well-wash]] at [[Larbril]], and each week they are the person who says what that water is.

People on this road say Brormei (bror-MAY), and a stranger who needs the place attached hears Brormei of Larbril. [[Saelthael]] does not keep a list of persons, so there is no house-name to set in front of the given one.

## Summary

Brormei works the meeting at [[Larbril]], where the west road comes down onto the [[Named Ground|Well-wash]]. In a kind Liren, the thaw when this wash still runs, the water is a river and the ford is obvious, and in a cruel Liren that same thaw leaves only a silt-line, though the carts still come, because the walk west did not stop when the core's did. Brormei is who says which week this one is, and whether a column goes on up toward the [[Named Ground|Shelf-gate]].

When the wash is dry they still wet the meeting-stone from a jar, and they will not say why.

They are not Theisva, and they do not chalk [[Ornsael]]'s rope, the well-town lying farther into the dry. That gang's share is the well, which is why they are not reassigned to this meeting. The [[The Dry Stair|Dry Stair]] is a different rise, which people may leave Larbril to climb, and Brormei's job stays the road and the wash.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Dust sits at the hem even in a river year, and the jar they carry is not a relic. They talk like a person who has already looked at the line this morning, and they are short with travellers who want the line to be a hymn.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Larbril]]** — The meeting is one person's work, Brormei is not a warden who invents a colour, and the Hand at the meeting Speaks most years, which is ordinary.
- **[[Ornsael]]** — Theisva, Lesna, and Bovaer keep the well there, their share of the work stays the well, and they are not moved onto this road.
- **[[Tasain]]** — The levy is taken on the far side of the pass, and Brormei does not collect it.
- **[[Lirorn]]** — The Shelf-gate lies ahead or behind depending on the day you ask, and the pass is not Brormei's gate.
