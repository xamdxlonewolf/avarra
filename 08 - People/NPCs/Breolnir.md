# Breolnir

> Breolnir writes who slept at the gate of [[Rothallo]], and will not copy the Book's rank onto that line.

The name is Breolnir (bree-OL-neer), of house **Tethnae** (TETH-nay), and the file reads Tethnae, Breolnir, because a clerk says the house first, while a neighbour says Breolnir. A stranger hears Breolnir of the Inner Close, which is the wall, or of Rothallo, which is the seat, and both of those names are this same gate.

## Summary

Breolnir sits the gate and writes the sleep-line, which is who was outside, who was let through to a bed, and who was turned away. [[Orenbren]] is lodging-country, so what it witnesses is sleep, while the [[The Walled Book|Book of Tithes]] ranks debt and labour, and Breolnir's page stays the sleep. They will not copy the Book's rank onto it. [[The Walled Book|Delamem]] keeps the Book, and does not need Breolnir's permission to write a different page about the same person.

They sand a sleep-line and write it again before the ink would have needed sand, and they will not say why.

In Turning-Week, the week of the Turning, the wall will not lodge a Given pilgrim, someone already given a Condition, an acquired change, who has come for the week. The beds are the country's business on the outer side of the wall, and Breolnir is where the shut gate and those beds meet.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Ink stains the side of the hand that holds the sand. They ask for a house when the speaker is being filed, and they use a given name when they are buying bread. The difference is the job, and they are tired of people who perform the wrong one on purpose.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[The Walled Book|Delamem]]** — Delamem keeps the Book and is not Breolnir's master on the sleep-line, though they are not absent from the gate either.
- **[[Methei]]** — Methei stands on the labour-line outside, where Breolnir can write that they slept and still cannot end the assignment.
- **[[Brimaen]]** — Brimaen is inside on a licence, which is a different page from the sleep-line Breolnir writes at the gate.
- **[[Maiethlir]]** — Clerks come up from Maiethlir with empty tablets, and Breolnir does not hand them the Book, or the sleep-line if that line would become a roll.
