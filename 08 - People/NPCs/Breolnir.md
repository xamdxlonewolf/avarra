# Breolnir

> Writes who slept at the gate of [[Rothallo]]. Will not copy the Book's rank onto that line.

*(bree-OL-neer.)* House **Tethnae** *(TETH-nay).* The file is Tethnae, Breolnir. A neighbour says Breolnir. A stranger hears Breolnir of the Inner Close, which is the wall, or of Rothallo, which is the seat. Same gate.

## Summary

Breolnir sits the gate and writes the sleep-line: who was outside, who was let through to a bed, who was turned. Orenbren's lodging-country witnesses sleep. The [[The Walled Book|Book of Tithes]] ranks debt and labour. Breolnir's page is the first. They will not copy the second onto it. [[The Walled Book|Delamem]] keeps the Book and does not need Breolnir's permission to write a different page about the same person.

They sand a sleep-line and write it again before the ink would have needed sand. They will not say why.

In Turning-Week the wall will not lodge a Given pilgrim. The beds are the country's business, on the outer side. Breolnir is where those two sentences meet.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Ink on the side of the hand that holds the sand. They ask for a house when the speaker is being filed, and they use a given name when they are buying bread. The difference is the job, and they are tired of people who perform the wrong one on purpose.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[The Walled Book|Delamem]]** — the Book. Not Breolnir's master in the sleep-line, and not absent either.
- **[[Methei]]** — on the labour-line, outside. Breolnir can write that they slept. They cannot end the assignment.
- **[[Brimaen]]** — inside, on a licence. A different page.
- **[[Maiethlir]]** — clerks with empty tablets. Breolnir does not hand them the Book, and does not hand them the sleep-line either if the sleep-line would become a roll.

## GM Notes

- **Not Delamem.** Do not merge the sleep-line and the Book into one clerk. The disagreement is the tension.
- **Not the First Seat.** The college is in the wood. Breolnir does not Speak.
- **Name.** Algorithm 1. `python3 "14 - Assets/Names/generate_names.py" --seed 20261015 --register conservative --count 80` — given **Breolnir** position 30, house **Tethnae** position 63. Clerk: house first.
- **The sanded line is a habit.** A later note must not explain it, and must not turn it into a power, a relic, or a rite.
