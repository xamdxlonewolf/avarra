# Tumair

> Tumair writes what a hull is on the quay at [[Votaer]]: pilgrim, pot, person, or cargo.

People on the quay say Tumair (too-MAIR), and a stranger hears Tumair of Votaer. The quay does not file a family, so there is no house-name to give them.

## Summary

Tumair stands on Votaer's classification quay and writes the docket, before [[Orentel]] prices the crossing, and Tumair's sentence is the earlier one: what the hull is. A child, a pot of living earth, a Given-Over whom a contract has come to hold, a recanter, and a family on a neighbour's week can all share one bell, and the docket is how the quay tells them apart as pilgrim, pot, person, or cargo.

They chalk the class under the waterline, where the next tide takes the mark, and they will not say why.

They are not a factor, the [[The White Note House|White Note]] is not their desk, and [[Taerso]] is the name on the old Hush rather than a colleague on this quay.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Chalk rides in a pocket that the spray finds. They look at the hold before they look at the person who wants to explain the hold. The explanation can be true and still not be the docket.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Lertho]]** — Lertho takes the Hush-rate on the same water, and the class and the coin are one quay worked by two hands.
- **[[Votaer]]** — The work is the classification on this quay, and the city is not a second Orentel.
- **[[The Standing Trade]]** — The trade uses this face because a standing has to travel. Tumair can write a person onto the docket, and they do not sell the standing.
- **[[Orentel]]** — Orentel lies across the Crossing, three to five days in fair weather, and the price is theirs.
