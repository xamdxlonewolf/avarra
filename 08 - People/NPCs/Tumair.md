# Tumair

> Writes what a hull is, on the quay at [[Votaer]]. Pilgrim, pot, person, or cargo.

*(too-MAIR.)* Of Votaer. A neighbour says Tumair. A stranger hears Tumair of Votaer. The quay does not file a family. There is no house.

## Summary

Tumair stands on Votaer's classification quay and writes the docket. [[Orentel]] will price the crossing after. Tumair's sentence is earlier: what the hull is. A child, a pot of living earth, a Given-Over, a recanter, and a family on a neighbour's week can share a bell. The docket is how the quay tells them apart.

They chalk the class under the waterline, where the next tide takes the mark. They will not say why.

They are not a factor. The [[The White Note House|White Note]] is not their desk. [[Taerso]] is the name on the old Hush, not a colleague.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Chalk in a pocket that the spray finds. They look at the hold before they look at the person who wants to explain the hold. The explanation can be true and still not be the docket.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Lertho]]** — takes the Hush-rate on the same water. The class and the coin are one quay and two hands.
- **[[Votaer]]** — the work. Not a second Orentel.
- **[[The Standing Trade]]** — uses this face because a standing travels. Tumair can write a person. They do not sell the standing.
- **[[Orentel]]** — across the Crossing, three to five days in fair weather. The price is theirs.
