# Vaethod

> Vaethod is town-warden of [[Eolvaeth]], the person who tends the Tree and says, or refuses to say, what the week did. They are Hands-folk, raised where a town Tree is the ordinary way to Turn. They Speak the fall, the colour coming down, or they refuse to invent one. Some years they send a cohort toward [[Saelvaeth]]'s luck, the march where young wood takes more readily. They have not resigned.

*(VAYTH-od.)* The naming here is worn down, and there is no house to give a clerk. A neighbour says Vaethod, and a stranger gets Vaethod of Eolvaeth; folk will also say *of Elvaeth*. On Kumbaan the name is Vaethod.

## Summary

Vaethod is the person who has to say whether the hinge happened, whether this Tree's week actually turned. They will not write a colour that did not fall. They have sent people away before, and the town remembers which summers.

They were Given at this Tree, a colour at their own Leaf-Fall, and they are a Hands-child rather than a walker of the old mile. The office is practice-first, taken by doing the work, and not by Kind, the ancestry a person is born. They are believer-heavy the way a thin canopy still makes you, and tired of being asked to make the wait look like a certainty.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They are fortyish, with salt in the hair already. The hands are cracked from spring-water and from the pots in the gift-hall. There is chalk on a cuff more weeks than not. They speak short, and they look at faces before they look at papers.

They will feed you, and they will not let you name the spring as a clerk's list.

## The job

**Leverage:** the Speaking is the civic fact in a town with almost no other office. A visiting clerk who wants the hinge tidy has to go through them. A cohort that goes toward Saelvaeth goes because Vaethod said the week was over and the wood had not done its work.

They cannot stamp a Cutting-leave, the First Seat's paper for who may take living wood and where it may be planted, and they cannot make the Tree speak. They can refuse to write a colour that did not happen, and they can tell a hundred people to walk toward a luckier Hand.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Saelid]]** *(SAY-lid)* is the younger sibling, a baker on the camp-streets. The stall-pots carry Taken-In green, the green of a person the wild took partway into itself, and the rest of the baking is ordinary flour. The winter customers who buy bread are the same households a sent cohort takes away. Vaethod has not told the square that this is why they wait until the week is over.
- **The camp-streets** sometimes hold a local witness, and some years the witness has to walk home from a luckier Hand. Vaethod sleeps badly those years.
- **[[Harrow's Green|Haelin]]** keeps the live front, the square where the wood is actually being argued this season. Vaethod has sent people into that orbit, and they have not met. A pot going that way is a Road-hand's job, and the Road-hands are the wardens who carry the wood.
- **[[Nedaen]] · [[Heiral]] · [[Daron]]** carry other wants on this square. See [[People of the Turning]].
- **[[Hithaen]]** arrived this Hale-month, the spring month, with no list behind them. Vaethod has not decided whether they came to wait.

The gift-hall notice from Eolvaeth in Cut-year 387, the wardens' count of years from the First Cut, is still chalked on the gift-hall door-beam ([[Eolvaeth#In-world text]]). It does not mention Saelid.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Threshold Guardian. A technical call, whether a colour happened and whether the week is over, becomes the gospel's politics without their consent.
- **Arc (positive).** *Lie:* if I speak truly and never invent a colour, I have not chosen. *Ghost:* a year they sent a cohort away, and the sending took a household Saelid still asks after. *Need:* admit that sending is a choice with a cost, and that the cost lands on people they love. *Catalyst:* another thin week, a letter from the Seat, and the stall's flour counted in days.
- **Lock-in:** skill and a moral together. They are the only Speaking the vale will take this year. If they stop, the hinge falls to a clerk.
- **Competing interests:** the gospel says stay; the Seat's horticulture says send; Saelid wants the customers kept; a visiting Reckoning clerk wants the spring named on a list; the unvouched Struck person in the gift-hall may be here from faith or from fear.
- **Where they stand.** They are a Hands-child, and they did not walk the old mile. They are not the live front. They point at it.
- **Do not** let them assert the mind of the Tree. "The soil was never quite right here" is horticulture, a sentence about wood and ground. Confirming that she is kind does not make the wait into a virtue.
- **Do not** make them the First Seat's local pope.
