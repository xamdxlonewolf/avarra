# Thilim

> Thilim keeps the third lodging out from the Motherwood on the Near Mile, the old short walk in toward the origin Tree. They are [[Long-Lived]], deathless, and inland. The notes they write are smaller than the White Note's, and the shrine is larger. Seine's bed is still made.

*(THIL-im.)* Of the Held bed, a bed kept for a name that has not come. A neighbour says Thilim, a stranger gets Thilim of the Third Hearth or of the Held bed, and a list gets of the Held bed. There is no house, and they will not borrow one. On Kumbaan the name is Thilim. A pilgrim who asks where the college is will be pointed at the wood, not the inn.

## Summary

They have kept this house since before [[The First Cut]], as later clerks would say. The First Cut is the hinge the wardens count from, the year living wood began to leave the one Tree and the required walk stopped being how everyone Turned. Thilim walked the mile as the inn. It was the same stretch, every summer, until the stretch was optional, and they kept making the bed anyway.

Lestrand, which prices a road-house by whether it became a bank, calls this a bed-house that failed to become one. Thilim asks who will be here at dark.

A [[Long-Lived]] person who says *I walked* is saying when they were young, and the same sentence does four jobs that the people who say it do not agree on. They will not always tell you which of the four *I walked* jobs they are doing when they pour.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They are deathless and inland-tired, and they look like a person who has carried water a long time. They wait to be asked across a threshold, even their own, if they have been out to the stone. It is courtesy, and they will not explain it.

They remember every debtor, and they will not always tell you which children came back.

## The job

**Leverage:** who sleeps, and whether a name stays on the door-beam. The stone by the road is the inn's water and the Road-hands' mile. Brenthael, the neighbour town, wants the stone moved so the extra mile does not look like a requirement on their approach. Thilim will not move a name that is also a well.

They cannot make Seine arrive, and they cannot make the White Note, the quay-house that did become a bank, fail. They can feed a necessity-family, people eating now against a promise to pay when they walk home, and a devotion-column, people still walking the mile as the devotion, in the same loft in the same week, and know which is which without saying.

## Role & Relationships

- **Seine** of the far cape, tenth summer, the summer a child is brought to the leaf, has not come. The mother said next year, and next year was last year, and has been. It is not a shrine Thilim will admit.
- **Brenthael's warden** is unnamed, and wants the stone. The chalked complaint is still under the lip ([[The Third Hearth#In-world text]]). Thilim has not answered on paper.
- **[[Mataero]]** will hear this house as a warning, a loft that never became a slate. They have not been here. A court letter may reach both houses.
- **[[Vaethod]]** sent a household toward another Tree, and that household has eaten here, on the way toward a luck they did not have. Thilim did not take a White Note's rate.
- **Orenbren lodging-custom** treats hospitality as rent in Eolthael, the Turning-month, and Thilim charges less and is called a failure for it. The compact does not own this custom.

There is no court of staff, only a pot-boy in Hale-month, the spring month when the road fills, a shed, and a cutting in the pot that is still not a Tree.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Caretaker, carrying an Inherited Network's expectations. Every pilgrim-family thinks the house remembers *their* walk.
- **Arc (flat).** *Truth:* who is fed at dark matters more than paper. The world, meaning Brenthael, the White Note, and a court, will call that failure. Thilim holds. A party that sleeps here has to pick whether they came as a family in need or as a devotion. *Do not* turn this into a tragedy in which they finally let the bed that corresponds to Seine's. Seine's bed stays made.
- **The four jobs stay disagreed.** Blood-Read gives a true fragment of a walk. It will not tell you which sermon they are in when they say *I walked*.
- **Lock-in:** a moral and a network at once. Stopping would unmake the Held bed and the water. The network assumes they know rules they never wrote down.
- **Non-Tree want is the well.** Seine is grief. The playable pressure this year is Brenthael's crowd and the pot at dark. Do not let the session only ask about the Tree.
- **Walked** as the inn. They do not narrate Ornthael, the Years of Hands, in which a town Tree is the ordinary way to Turn, as if it were an age that already ended and left them behind.
- **Do not** kill them to close the Held bed. Do not move them to the quay. Do not pick which of the four *I walked* jobs they are doing.
