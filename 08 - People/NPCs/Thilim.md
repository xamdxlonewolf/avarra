# Thilim

> Keeps the third lodging out from the Motherwood on the Near Mile. [[Long-Lived]]. Inland. The notes are smaller than the White Note's. The shrine is larger. Seine's bed is still made.

*(THIL-im.)* Of the Third Hearth. A pilgrim who asks where the college is will be pointed at the wood, not the inn.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | the stone's water still feeds the pot when it is dark |
| **Have** | beds, porridge, a slate whose verso does not match, a Held name |
| **Fear** | Brenthael pulling the stone and calling it a ribbon in a square |
| **Live conflict** | mouths at dark, or a leftover the town no longer owes |
| **Hooks** | Seine's lintel; a court letter asking for a Cut-year; a First-Hand year in the loft; Blood-Read |

## Summary

They have kept this house since before [[The First Cut]], as later clerks would say. They walked the mile as the inn: the same stretch, every summer, until the stretch was optional and they kept making the bed anyway.

Lestrand calls this a bed-house that failed to become a bank. Thilim asks which mouths will be here at dark.

They will not convert a house-year into a Cut-year for a court if the court is only tidying. They will not always tell you which of the four *I walked* jobs they are doing when they pour.

## Appearance & Demeanor

Deathless and inland-tired. They look like a person who has carried water a long time. They wait to be asked across a threshold, even their own, if they have been out to the stone. Courtesy. They will not explain it.

They remember every debtor. They will not always tell you which children came back.

## The job

**Leverage:** who sleeps, and whether a name stays on a lintel. The stone at the lintel-road is the inn's drink and the Road-hands' mile. Brenthael wants it moved so the extra mile does not look like a requirement on their approach. Thilim will not move a name that is also a well.

They cannot make Seine arrive. They cannot make the White Note fail. They can feed a necessity-family and a devotion-column in the same loft in the same week, and know which is which without saying.

## Role & Relationships

- **Seine** of the far cape — tenth summer, not come. The mother said next year. Next year was last year, and has been. Not a shrine Thilim will admit.
- **Brenthael's warden** — unnamed, wants the stone. The chalked complaint is still under the lip ([[The Third Hearth#In-world text]]). Thilim has not answered on paper.
- **[[Mataero]]** — will hear this house as a warning. Has not been here. A court letter may reach both.
- **[[Vaethod]]** — a sent household has eaten here on the way toward a luck they did not have. Thilim did not take a White Note's rate.
- **Orenbren lodging-custom** — hospitality as rent in Eolthael. Thilim charges less and is called a failure for it. The compact does not own this mouth.

No court of staff. A pot-boy in Hale-month. A shed. A cutting in the pot that is still not a Tree.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Caretaker, with an Inherited Network's expectations (every pilgrim-family thinks the house remembers *their* walk).
- **Arc (flat).** *Truth:* mouths at dark matter more than paper. The world (Brenthael, the White Note, a court) will call that failure. They hold. Influence: a party that sleeps here has to pick which leftover they are. *Do not* turn this into a tragedy where they finally let the north-equivalent bed. Seine stays made.
- **The four jobs stay disagreed.** Blood-Read gives a true fragment. It will not tell you which sermon they are.
- **Lock-in:** moral + network. Stopping would unmake the Held bed and the water. The network assumes they know rules they never wrote down.
- **Non-Tree want is the well.** Seine is grief. The playable pressure this year is Brenthael's crowd and the pot at dark. Do not let the session only ask about the Tree.
- **Clock:** walked — as the inn. Does not narrate Ornthael as post-history.
- **Do not** kill them to close the Held bed. Do not move them to the quay. Do not pick which *I walked* they are.
