## Summary

Nubo lives in [[Raitin]], and the council's note has their name on it. Another hearth on the shared list did not stand them, and the league will not make that hearth's vouching into a city matter. Tithe-provision, the upkeep a town furnishes for a changed person, stays with the town. Nubo can buy bread in the throne-city and still fail a day's walk away, in a charter-town this note does not name.

They tie a dry thread on the left wrist and move it to the right before they leave the hall, and they will not say why.

## Appearance & Demeanor

The thread is the only mark on them. They speak the given name, they flinch when a clerk from outside the league asks for a house, and they are tired of explaining that the league is not one.
