> Vouched in [[Raitin]]. Unknown where another hearth of the Hearth-League did not stand them. Still in the city.

## Summary

Nubo lives in Raitin. The council's note has their name on it. Another hearth on the shared list did not stand them, and the league will not make that hearth's vouching a city matter. Tithe-provision stays with the town. Nubo can buy bread in the throne-city and still fail a day's walk away, in a charter-town this note does not name.

They tie a dry thread on the left wrist and move it to the right before they leave the hall. They will not say why.

## Appearance & Demeanor

The thread is the only mark. They speak the given name, and they flinch when a clerk from outside the league asks for a house. They are tired of explaining that the league is not one.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Raitin]]** — home, and the hall that cleared them. Not a guarantee.
- **[[Turvo]]** — wrote the note. Cannot carry it.
- **[[Sulnu]]** — can take a ticket to the stair. The next hearth is not the stair.
- **The next hearth** — did not stand them. Nubo will not say which town. The hall will not send a mouth to settle it.
