# Senithi

Senithi (seh-NITH-ee) is of house **Thalonve** (tha-LON-ve), [[Laevila]]'s line, Given a Condition, an acquired change, at the Turning and otherwise ordinary, and a neighbour says Senithi and a clerk says Thalonve, Senithi. The line still lists the wrong grandmother, a stranger gets Senithi of Maiethlir, and on Kumbaan, where a person is asked for the given name, the name is Senithi.

They want a wedding rather than an old inscription read aloud, and they will take the house if taking it gets them the match.
