# Senithi

*(seh-NITH-ee.)* House **Thalonve** *(tha-LON-ve)*, [[Laevila]]'s line. Given. Ordinary. A neighbour says Senithi. A clerk says Thalonve, Senithi. The line still lists the wrong grandmother. A stranger gets Senithi of Maiethlir. On Kumbaan the name is Senithi.

**Want:** a wedding, not a lintel. They will take the house if it gets them the match.
