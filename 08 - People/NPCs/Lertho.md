# Lertho

> Lertho charges the Hush-rate at [[Votaer]], counts it aloud, and will not call the taking a victory.

People on the quay say Lertho (LER-tho), and a stranger hears Lertho of Votaer. The rate is not a house, and the quay does not file one.

## Summary

Lertho charges the Hush-rate on Votaer's water, the peace from [[The Hinge Hush]] still collected as custom, and Lertho will not call that taking a victory. [[Taerso]] spoke that hush for the hinge-houses, Taerso is dead to the desk, and Lertho is the living hand on the box.

They count the rate aloud, set the last coin in, and take it out once before it stays, and they will not say why.

[[Tumair]] has usually already written what the hull is, the blessing, if someone wants one, happens where Lertho can hear it, and a household can be classed, charged, and blessed before anyone has asked whether they can pay for the week inland. That is the quay's injustice, and it is Lertho's hour as much as Tumair's.

## Appearance & Demeanor

The box has a worn lip where the coins go over. They speak the count the way a person speaks a measure, not a prayer, and they go quiet if someone thanks them as if the rate were a mercy.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Tumair]]** — Tumair's docket comes first, or beside the box, and the two of them work side by side, neither the other's superior.
- **[[Votaer]]** — The rate's home is this quay, other shore towns are not named here, and those towns do not collect a second Hush.
- **[[Taerso]]** — Taerso is the historical figure on the year of the hush, not a partner at the box and not a ghost in it.
- **[[Orentel]]** — Orentel will price what this rate has already sorted, and Lertho does not set that price.
