# Valein

Valein (vah-LAYN) is of house **Nirsei** (nir-SAY), the same household as [[Rithim]], though that house is not yet written into a hall. Valein is Kept and unmarked, which means the Turning left no colour on them, and after a Speaking they bring a cup. A neighbour says Valein, a clerk who is allowed the second name says Nirsei, Valein, Rithim has not said that second name into a hall, and a stranger gets Valein of Maiethlir. On Kumbaan, where a person is asked for the given name, the name is Valein.

They want to stay a person who is not a column.
