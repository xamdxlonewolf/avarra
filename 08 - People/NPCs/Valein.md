# Valein

*(vah-LAYN.)* House **Nirsei** *(nir-SAY)*, the same household as [[Rithim]], and not yet written. Kept. Unmarked. Brings a cup after a Speaking. A neighbour says Valein. A clerk who is allowed the second name says Nirsei, Valein. Rithim has not said it into a hall. A stranger gets Valein of Maiethlir. On Kumbaan the name is Valein.

**Want:** to stay a person who is not a column.
