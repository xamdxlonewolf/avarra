# Tesara

> Tesara sits the stillers' shed that does not face the street, the catching-year, which is the week a newly Struck person is kept somewhere quiet before the town reaches for a rope. They hold a key the slate does not name. If the week goes well, nobody hears of it. They will not stamp a ticket this week, and a ticket is the licence that lets one of the Stilled use the gaze where the town can see it.

*(teh-SAH-rah.)* The names on this quay have eroded, so there is no family to put first. A neighbour says Tesara, a stranger gets Tesara of Orentel, and a list gets of Orentel and no family. The shed is the week, not a house. On Kumbaan the name is Tesara. If the floor says anything about the work, it will say *emergency ticket*.

## Summary

Years back Tesara was Struck themself, which means a Condition met at a later threshold rather than at the childhood leaf, and what they carry is a Gaze, the stilling look of the Stilled, taken raw in a week somebody else sat. The paying floor does not write that down. Tesara remembers the door, and that memory is why they are the one the floor can spare.

Tesara thinks the work is first aid. You catch the person, you steady them, and you hand them on to the year the guild would actually train. They do not think they are the hero of a story. If the week fails, the person goes to the rope or to [[The Slide]], and the Tithe, the upkeep the Condition still demands, is still live.

[[Valen]] is not a client on a book. Valen is the person who was stone on the third night, a Struck member of [[The Stilled|the Stilled]], the kind who survived being turned to stone, stood for by a sister and by an N. who would not give a family name. The week is almost over, and the face of the ticket is still empty, because the slip is written on the inside of a blank and Tesara will not stamp the outside this week.

## Appearance & Demeanor

They look tired, chalk on a sleeve, younger than the master and older than the week. You would miss them if you came in the front of the infirmary. You would find them if you asked where the one who was stone yesterday went.

They do not face the street, and they do not write the wall.

## The job

**Leverage:** the shed can stand in for the rope, if the person will come. They cannot overrule a quay-watch, the watch that wants the square clear, and they cannot stamp a ticket this week. They can keep a master in the shed one more night, and they can fail to burn a slip. A burned slip is how a quiet week disappears.

Lestrand prices the emergency. A raw Struck person who cannot stamp a fee is still taken, because a street-hazard demands it, and the house will remember the debt. Tesara has not told Valen about the debt.

## Role & Relationships

- **[[Valen]]** is raw [[The Stilled|Stilled]]. The sister stood for them, and the first drill, the practice that works the stillness back out of the body before it settles, held and then did not last. There is still no ticket. Tesara wants the year of training rather than the silence, and the job wants the silence.
- **The paying floor** wants the master back. A quiet catching-year is a week the theatre lost.
- **[[Rosire]]** used to bring water downwind of the shed. They are not on the floor this Hale-month, the spring month before the Turning. Tesara has not been told why.
- **[[Mataero]]** is who Valen will need if they live and still have no ticket, because a person with nowhere to stand needs a loft. Occupancy, the White Note's word for who sleeps, will not say *Struck*.
- **[[The Slide]]** is the overflow if the week fails. Tesara wants Valen *safe enough to leave.* A week-keeper at the Slide wants them *not-fallen enough to stay.*
- **[[Didel]]** has hauled a person to this door once, and will not haul a rope.

## GM Notes

- **Pattern:** Competence Trap. The skill of sitting a Gaze is why they cannot leave the week, and the week is why they cannot get Valen what Valen actually wants.
- **Arc (positive).** *Lie:* if the week is quiet, we saved them. *Ghost:* their own catching-year was burned, and the ticket arrived late enough to feel like weather. *Need:* a witnessed week, someone who writes the becoming *as* a becoming. *Catalyst:* the choice to burn or keep Valen's slip, and the master called back to the floor.
- **Lock-in:** skill and a moral at the same time. They are the spare who remembers the door. Walking away is a rope.
- **Competing interests:** the floor wants silence; the watch wants the person gone; Valen's sister wants a person she can stand next to; the house wants its debt; the Slide wants a week; Mataero can offer a bed that does not say why the person is in it.
- **Struck later.** Tesara did not walk the old mile to the one Tree. They do not think the wave of new Struck people is over. They think the next raw one is three days out.
- **Do not** make the Intake a circuit, or a nicer Slide. Do not stamp the ticket this week unless the story has earned a house breaking its own rule, and then make that stamp cost the charter.
- **Rosire is not here this Hale-month.** The Opening sits at [[Harrow's Green]] ([[The Opening]]). Valen's week is still what Tesara wants. Do not pull Tesara to the live front to tidy the infirmary-hand.
- **Wrong office:** Bound, Taken-In, Returned, Two-Bodied, Long-Lived. Valen is a Gaze. Keep it.
