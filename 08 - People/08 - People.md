# 08 - People

This folder is the persistent cast of the setting: the people a campaign can meet, the historical figures the years still say, and the indexes for who holds an office and for what a street praises, condemns, or still argues. It is the canonical home for people. A person who exists only because a campaign invented them lives under 12 - Campaigns, not here.

## Contents

### Hub
- [[People of the Turning]] holds the six pivots, the named wants in the three seats, and four people a campaign can start from.

### Indexes
- [[Leaders]] is the list of offices that already have a holder.
- [[Heroes and Villains]] is the one index for the praised, the condemned, and the still argued. [[Heroes]] and [[Villains]] point at that index, and the people are not split across the two folders.
