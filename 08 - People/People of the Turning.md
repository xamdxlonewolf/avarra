# People of the Turning

These are offices that already had to exist, made into people, and the job is why they matter. None of them were chosen, and a party can meet two in a week and never meet the rest.

They were not recruited by Kind, the ancestry a person is born with. The names below are what a neighbour says. The second name, and what a clerk or a stranger gets instead, is on each note, under the rule in [[Naming People in the Turning]].

Read the last column of a table as an ordinary want, a stall or a bed or a name, and not as a want about the Tree. The job beside it is why a party meets the person at all.

## The six pivots

[[Vaethod]] stands in a square, the town ground of [[Eolvaeth]], and some years sends a cohort on toward another Turning Tree, a group of that town. [[Rithim]] is Speaker in [[Maiethlir]], the person who names the colour a child was given, and will not say the line a clerk is waiting to copy. [[Mataero]] keeps occupancy for the White Note, the house of old papers, and occupancy is the count of who is in a room. [[Laevila]] is a Watcher, someone who describes what was seen and does not confirm a colour. [[Tesara]] sits the catching-year, the week [[The Intake]] keeps in the stillers' shed with a person just Struck, someone changed later than the childhood Turning. [[Thilim]] keeps the Held bed at [[The Third Hearth]], and the table below sets those six out again, with the ordinary want written beside the job.

[[Vaethod]] sends people toward a luck they cannot see from [[Eolvaeth]]. The live front is [[Harrow's Green|Haelin]]'s square, not Vaethod's.

## Named wants in the three seats

Wants sit on people. An institution can furnish a want or refuse it, and it cannot *want*.

### [[Eolvaeth]]

1. **[[Vaethod]]** wants [[Saelid]]'s stall to keep its winter customers. In a year when Vaethod sends a cohort away, the camp-streets that buy the bread go empty.
2. **[[Nedaen]]** *(neh-DAYN)*, one of [[The Taken-In|the Taken-In]], people the wild took partway into itself and gave back, keeps the commons around the Tree and wants the gift-hall pot to feed their child through Ledger-month, the late-summer month when contracts come due. Mother's green is enough until it is not.
3. **[[Heiral]]** *(HAY-ral)* is Kept, which is what a square calls a person the Tree hugged and did not give a colour, and they live on the camp-street. They want a marriage witnessed without being pitied as Mother-kept. They will take a neighbour's word, and they will not take a sermon.
4. **[[Daron]]** *(DAR-on)* is [[Returned]], a person who died with something unfinished and is still walking because of it, and they keep the far shed. They want a specific corpse named so a grain-debt dies with it. The family will not come downwind to do it.

### [[Orentel]]

1. **[[Mataero]]** wants [[Sorel]] off the White Note's century-paper, the long note that still carries Sorel, and they think a clean loft-slate will buy that.
2. **[[Sorim]]** wants berths 1–4 to stay held through Hale-month, the month when the land is counted sound. Their house-share is the hold-fee, the fee for keeping the berth, and not the wool.
3. **[[Tesara]]** wants [[Valen]] stamped with a real ticket before the paying floor takes the master back.
4. **[[Lasole]]** *(lah-SO-leh)* rents a leaf-lot on the rise and wants the credit extended one season. The lot will not follow them home, and home is a loft if it did.
5. **[[Didel]]** *(dih-DEL)* of Hallowquay is [[Selkie]], sea-born, one of the skin-kin, and works haulage. They want a kin-berth for a skin-kin wedding in Eolthael, the month that holds Turning-Week. [[Sorim]] has held berths 1–4 for a First-Hand year, a berth kept for the prestige walk to the origin. Didel cannot hold those.

### [[Maiethlir]]

1. **[[Rithim]]** wants [[Valein]] unfiled, left off the tablet. The verso, the back of the copy, already knows how to write *unmarked*, the clerk's word for a person with no colour set down.
2. **[[Laevila]]** wants [[Senithi]]'s name read off the hidden face of a recut beam, once, in a room a clerk will accept as a witness.
3. **[[Vehaela]]** *(veh-HAY-lah)* came down from [[Lirorn]]'s thin snow with two children and wants an uncounted loft for one winter. The extra-mile street still has rooms the roll has not found.
4. **[[Baerith]]** *(BAY-rith)* is [[Far-Voiced]], a voice that carries exactly as far and as true as the singer means it to, and they sing the counted hour. They want the crown to stop cutting green on the street where their sister keeps a pot. To be cut off is framed as a lapse, and what it is, is a household going hungry.

## How the squares touch

These crossings are not a party. They are paper, beds, pots, and one week in Eolthael, the month that holds Turning-Week.

- A household [[Vaethod]] sent last thin year sometimes eats now on a necessity-note from the White Note, a paper for a household in need. [[Mataero]] files the loft, and [[Vaethod]] does not know the house-year, the count that house keeps for itself.
- [[Sorim]]'s berth 5 is earth. The pot, a young tree in the container it travels in, is going toward a luckier Hand, a Tree. [[Harrow's Green|Haelin]] will sleep the Road-hand, the warden walking with that wood, if the wood arrives. That is the live front, not a reunion.
- [[Rithim]] Speaks the colour, and [[Laevila]] describes the other colour in the same square, the same morning. They already share a street, and they do not say the same thing.
- [[Tesara]]'s week, if it is not burned at the end of it, becomes a person who later needs a loft or a berth. [[Mataero]] will hear occupancy, and [[Sorim]] will hear a hold they cannot invent.
- [[Thilim]] still holds a far-cape name the White Note does not have. A court that wants a Cut-year, the count that runs from the first cutting, set beside a house-year writes inland and to the quay. The two answers will not match, and the two desks do not travel to deliver them.
- [[Reimaethe]] lives in a rise-room [[Mataero]] lets. They have given four years of memory into [[The Pourers]]' cup, one year at a time, and want to stop before the fifth. The Pourers are [[Long-Lived]] who set a year of memory into that cup, and the cup keeps what they gave.
- [[Hithaen]] arrived in the gift-hall with no list behind them. [[Vaethod]] can tell a person who came to wait from a clerk who came to write, and has not yet decided which this is.
- [[Rosire]] works a ticket-infirmary, an infirmary that runs on a ticket, downwind of [[Tesara]]'s shed when they are in [[Orentel]]. This Hale-month they are not there, and Tesara has not been told why.

[[Ledan]] still writes the quay-desk, and they are a clerk whose want is the founding-name spoken once. [[Haelin]] still wants the stone moved so the crowd can stand, and Haelin is the live front this cast points at, not a capital. **Tora** of Brenod is the one who walks the witness home.

## Names in the room

A neighbour still gets the given name, the name said in the street. Raetoth, Lithsur, Lonteith, Relmun, Huval, and Nurreith are given names on the place notes. A stranger hears *of Nelath*, *of Raillath*, *of Denlad*, *of Tunral*, or *of the garden-end*. None of them has a house.

[[Maiethlir]] writes a house before the given name: [[Laevila]] and [[Senithi]] are Thalonve, [[Rithim]] is Nirsei, [[Valein]] is that same house left unwritten, and [[Baerith]] is Rairtei. [[Vehaela]] is of Lirorn, and has not taken a house.

[[Eolvaeth]] asks *of where?* [[Vaethod]], [[Saelid]], [[Heiral]], [[Nedaen]], and [[Daron]] are of the town, or of the street they already stand on. [[Hithaen]] will not take *of Eolvaeth*. The line they come from still has a house on them, and they will not say it in this hall.

[[Orentel]] asks *of where?* The quay does not file a family. [[The White Note House|The White Note]] is a house only while the paper is open, and then it is the desk. [[Valen]] is of Orentel, and [[Didel]] is of Hallowquay.

[[Thilim]] is of the Held bed, [[Taeren]] is of Brenod, and [[Njunda]] is the given name, which is the whole of the name.

## GM Notes

- **Links:** [[The Opening]] · [[The Isolated Fall]] · [[Roadmap]]
- **Taeren's row.** The row used to say "hidden second fire." Taeren is the new leaf if no player character holds the seat, and the walker from the sanctuary is the remnant. That stays in [[Taeren]]'s GM Notes and in [[When the Fire Is Caught]]. Do not put it back in the table.
- **Names.** The draw is seed `20260830`, middle of the list, plus unused names already on that working set. Do not re-roll the six.
- **Positional, not destined.** Involvement is the job. Do not give any of the six a prophecy, a Kind-champion speech, or a First Seat that answers mail.
- **Constellation, not a party.** The intersections are structural, the same week or the same paper or the same square. If the table has them all sit down together, you have built a crew. Don't.
- **Non-Tree wants are load-bearing.** The audit's hole was a world where nobody wanted money, love, revenge, or a stall. If a session only asks them about the wood, you are running the old furniture.
- **Two walks.** Someone walked: [[Thilim]] as the inn, and [[Laevila]] as a child under Thaeloren. Someone did not. Someone thinks the wave is over, and that someone is [[Mataero]]. Someone is still sending, and that someone is [[Vaethod]]. The live front is Haelin's, pointed at, and not cloned into a seventh pivot.
- **Seeds stay seeds.** [[Reimaethe]] is a member of [[The Pourers]], not the circle. [[Hithaen]] is a Given heir of [[The Walled Book]]'s Close, not the Book. [[Taeren]] and [[Rosire]] sit under [[The Protectors]] and [[When the Fire Is Caught]]. The opening kit is [[The Opening]], with those two on-screen at Harrow's, while Reimaethe and Hithaen stay in their squares. Hidden-Phoenix PC agency is [[A Hidden Phoenix]]. This page does not turn the four into a crew.
- **Do not add a Given-Over broker here.** That face is [[The Holding Desk]]. Sold vouching is [[The Standing Trade]], an alley [[Didel]] has heard of, and not a seventh pivot.
- **Threnmaieth instruments** live on [[The Reckoned Offices]] (Menirein · Tarvae · Videm · Sirtal). [[Rithim]] is already the local Speaker. Do not invent a second registrar to tidy them. Do not give those four a person-note or a house from this page.
