# 01 - World

This folder is the physical setting as the people who live in it understand the ground under them. The notes here are the shape of the lands, the weather, what grows, the sky they reckon a year by, and a few strange things that the climate and the reach of the Trees already do. There are no other planes. A reader who is new to the world should start with the frame, learn the names of the water and the roads next, and only then trust a picture.

## Contents
<!-- Link notes in this section here as they are created. Aggregate; do not duplicate. -->
- **[[The World Frame]]** is the top-level geography: four continents set on a reach-gradient, from the old Tree-dense heart out through the trading coast and the thin frontier to the storm-walled isle. Start here.
- **[[Named Ground]]** is the water, the Rain-Wall, the four rivers, and the travel times, in the words people actually say.
- **[[The Known Map]]** is the labelled picture of that same ground.
- The continents are **[[Maiethorn]]**, the Motherland and the old heart; **[[Strandoren]]**, the Shore-lands and the trade coast; **[[Heskoren]]**, the Sundered Reach and the frontier; and **[[The Sundering Isle]]**, the storm-walled homeland of the Yumboes.
- The climate notes begin with **[[Climate of the Turning]]**, which holds the seasons and the eight weather bands, and continue in **[[Climate of Maiethorn]]**, **[[Climate of Strandoren]]**, **[[Climate of Heskoren]]**, and **[[Climate of Kumbaan]]**.
- **[[Ecology of the Turning]]** is what the ground gives and what it costs: forage, crops, disease, Tithe-ecology, and the beasts people work.
- **[[Powers of the Turning]]** names the fifteen great powers.
- Sky and time live in **[[The Reckoning of the Year]]**, which is the calendar, the solstice, Turning-Week (the week a child of ten stands under a Tree), the twelve months, and Kumbaan's moons. What a person actually sees overhead is **[[The Sky from the Ground]]**. Cut-years, the count kept from the first cutting of living wood, are in **[[The Ages of the Turning]]**.
- The phenomena are what the climate and the reach of the Trees already do: **[[The Green Across the Gap]]**, **[[The Day-Wash]]**, and **[[Brine and Rain]]**.
- **[[Planes]]** is the note that says this world does not have other planes.

## Related
- **[[The Premise]]** holds the world's scale and register. **[[Turning Tree]]** is what a town gathers under, and how a graft is carried out from the one Awakening Tree. **[[The Old Tongue]]** is the liturgical speech and the way it has worn. **[[Polity Archetypes]]** and **[[Powers of the Turning]]** are the powers. **[[Kinds of the Turning]]** is where the born peoples live.
- **[[The Ages of the Turning]]** is the era spine. The map is one date on that spine.
