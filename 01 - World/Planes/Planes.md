# Planes

This world does not have them.

There is one ground, one sun, and one mainland moon. [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]]'s twelve moons are the same sky, kept on a different count. The storm-wall is weather, current, and reef. It is not a door. The hills at [[Ndenjoo]] are hills people live in, not a country underneath the world. A [[Turning Tree|Tree]] is a tree. A Condition is a person who has become, still standing on this ground. The Before is older stone — [[The Low Wall]], [[The Seeing-Ring]], [[The Dry Stair]] — not a ruined heaven you can walk into.

No spirit-world opens beside the gift-hall. No second map waits under a square. Travel is walks, sails, and the storm-wall's refusal. What a faith calls a door is a practice, a meal, a return, or a hymn. It is not another plane with its own geography.

The empty folder was a hole in the shelf. It is not a hole in the world.


## GM Notes

- **Decision, L.5 (2026-10-02).** Do not add planes later to park a secret. The household is not a place. The keystone is not a realm. The limit's nature stays open and is not "she cannot cross planes."
- **The storm-wall stays weather.** Do not set a power in it. Do not put a graft on Kumbaan. Do not make the bright night a portal.
- **Conditions are not extradimensional.** A becoming is this world's person. Do not invent a fourth body-licence or a plane-touched Kind to explain one.
- **Archaeology is not a plane either.** The three stones are arguments on this ground. They are not layers of another world.
