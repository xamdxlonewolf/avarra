# The Green Across the Gap

On [[Heskoren]], in clear weather, a canopy shows on the next ridge, and the road does not. A bog, a flood, or a fold that was a path last month has the miles. [[Climate of the Turning|Sundered upland]] weather already turns this way, one vale standing in sun while the next loses its road, and thin reach already means the next Hand is a journey rather than a neighbour across a street.

From the ground the canopy is a green the size of a thumbnail, then a colour if the light is right, and then an argument. Someone says it Spoke this week, someone else says the colour is last year's leaves still hanging, and neither of them has walked it. [[Tunral]] lives in this kind of gap and will not draw the green on a post. [[Harrow's Green]] is what the thumbnail is, on the weeks the road allows, and the hamlets are the rare case where the canopy is a sightline and a ford, not a rumour.

The phenomenon is that mismatch, a green you can see and cannot reach before the week moves. It does not create a second Leaf-Fall, and it does not mean the wood is calling. A [[Far-Voiced]] can carry the news that the road is shut, and still cannot carry the child across the bog by being heard.

On some clear days the green is there at dawn and gone by noon, with no cloud. Travelers blame haze, ridge-folk do not bother to blame it, and nobody measures it.
