# The Green Across the Gap

On [[Heskoren]], in clear weather, a canopy shows on the next ridge. The road does not. A bog, a flood, or a fold that was a path last month has the miles. [[Climate of the Turning|Sundered upland]] weather already turns this way: one vale in sun, the next losing its road. Thin reach already means the next Hand is a journey, not a neighbour across a street.

From the ground it is a green the size of a thumbnail, then a colour if the light is right, then an argument. Someone says it Spoke this week. Someone says the colour is last year's leaves still hanging. Neither has walked it. [[Tunral]] lives in this kind of gap and will not draw the green on a post. [[Harrow's Green]] is what the thumbnail is, on the weeks the road allows. The hamlets are the rare case where the canopy is a sightline and a ford, not a rumour.

The phenomenon is the mismatch: seen, and not reachable before the week moves. It does not create a second Leaf-Fall. It does not mean the wood is calling. A [[Far-Voiced]] can carry the news that the road is shut. They cannot carry the child across the bog by being heard.

On some clear days the green is there at dawn and gone by noon, with no cloud. Travelers blame haze. Ridge-folk do not bother to blame it. Nobody measures it.
