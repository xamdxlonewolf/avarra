# Maiethorn

> **What this is.** People call it the **Motherland**. It is the sacred origin of the Turning, and the eastern anchor of [[The World Frame|the world]]. The [[Turning Tree|Awakening Tree]], whose own name is Thaeloren, still stands here, and every graft in the world, every living piece of that Tree planted in some other town, was carried out from this continent. It is the oldest of the four, the most densely treed, and the most devout, and the reach here is full. It is the baseline the [[Polity Archetypes|universal social physics]] was written against, the ordinary rules of this society set down as if Maiethorn's fullness were the normal case, and it is the home of **[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]]**, the Tallied Crown.

*(The name is said **MAI-eth-orn**. [[The Old Tongue|maieth]] is "Mother," and [[The Old Tongue|orn]] is "land," so the name is "the Mother's Land." This is the conservative drift, spoken almost unchanged from the liturgy.)*

## Overview

Maiethorn is the world's old heart, the continent people mean when they say "the center." It is old the way a cathedral town is old: long-settled, deeply grafted, every hill with its Tree and every Tree with its centuries. This is where the [[The Leaf-Mother|Motherfaith]] began, where the [[Turning Tree|Awakening Tree]] grows, and where a child's [[Turning Tree|Turning]] is least remarkable precisely because it is most certain. The Trees here are so healthy that more children are Given here, a coloured leaf falling on them at the solstice, than in the world as a whole, so a hug at the Tree, which is what makes a child one of the [[Law and Citizenship|Kept]], is less common here than elsewhere. The hug is not rare. Every Maiethorn school has Kept children, and the shares that say so are kept on [[Conditions]].

The whole [[Economy and the Tithe|Condition-economy]], the work and trade that runs on what people became, goes to its full depth here. Deathless houses, the households of the [[Long-Lived]], stand centuries old. [[The Stilled|Stilled]] infirmaries, kept by people who can hold a wound or a patient still, are part of ordinary care. The [[Far-Voiced]], whose feeling carries in the voice, are on every court and every quay. If the rest of the world is that fullness thinning as you travel outward, Maiethorn is the thick end the thinning is measured from.

Maiethorn is also the place where devotion is heaviest, most institutional, and most political. The faith here is old, layered, and contested. A sanctified surveillance-crown, [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]], and a stubborn [[The Watching|heartland heresy]] both claim the sacred center. Living close to the holy brings pressure, not peace.

## Geography & Climate

At the heart of the continent stands **Thaeloren, the [[Turning Tree|Awakening Tree]]**, called the First Hand, in a deep old grove that is the destination of the world's great pilgrimage. The land around that grove is the most densely grafted on earth: an old, gentle country of rounded hills, river valleys, and towns crowned with Trees, worn smooth by long settlement.

A vast old-growth forest rings that core. It is [[Turning Tree|motherwood]], the wood of the Turning Trees, together with its wild kin, and it holds the healthiest Trees in the world, the graft-nurseries from which scions are carried out to every other land. The [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]] and the faith's institutions cluster in the clearings. [[Rothallo]] sits a day's walk out from the wood. People still call it the Inner Close, and it stands inside [[Orenbren]], not as a sixteenth flag.

[[Named Ground|the Rain-Wall]], which [[Lirorn]] calls the Thaw-Wall, divides the continent's watersheds. The climate on the settled side is temperate and seasonal, with four true seasons, guild towns, mechanical clocks, and no printing press, and the winters are real enough that a Leaf-Fall timed to the solstice means something. [[Tengu]] hearths and the country of the Fox-of-the-Snows sit on the high snows. Two passes matter on that range. The **Noon Pass** is the water-line over the ribbon of the old high road, and the **Shelf-gate** is Narol's road after [[The Thaw-Break]]. The river that runs west off the range is **[[Named Ground|the Core-thaw]]**, and [[Maiethlir]] sits where that water slows.

East of the Rain-Wall, on the leeward side, away from the Old Crossing, the land falls into dry hills and sand-country. This is the one true dry reach in the known world. **[[Named Ground|the Well-wash]]** is a river in a kind Liren, the thaw when the water is gentle, and a line of silt in a cruel one. This dry country is the hearth of the [[Kitsune|Fox of the Sands]], and it is still Maiethorn, with the same Trees, the same conservative tongue, and the same pull of pilgrimage westward toward Thaeloren. It is simply the Motherland's other weather. Towns here cluster at wells, and a town of that kind is [[Ornsael]], the sown-ground, set on the west road after the core had already stopped walking.

The western coast faces **[[Named Ground|the Old Crossing]]** toward [[Strandoren]], the oldest trade route in the world. The ports here are ancient and rich, if less frantic than Strandoren's, and [[The Hinge Shore]] classifies the crossing. [[Selkie]] families are common on these quays, as they are on any old water.

Week-by-week weather, crops, sickness, and livelihood ecology are in [[Climate of Maiethorn]].

## Reach

The reach is full, the densest and healthiest grafting anywhere. The core runs higher on the Given than the world as a whole. The [[Law and Citizenship|three pillars]] stand thick on the ground: [[Long-Lived]] memory, the word of the [[Bound]], and the feeling carried by the [[Far-Voiced]]. The [[Law and Citizenship|Kept]] are fewer than elsewhere, but they are present in every town, and in the more fervent districts they are quietly pitied as passed-over. [[The Walled Book]] teaches that a hug is rare, and that is the Close's lesson, not the count. This is the world's ceiling for how dense the Conditions become, and everything the [[Polity Archetypes|archetype grammars]] assume will be true "normally" is most true here.

How the wood got here is [[The First Cut]]. The years are Cut-year 0–80, origin-towns first, and the wood moved a day's living-earth at a time, no farther than a cutting could be carried in a day and still meet new ground alive. The Rain-Shadow was slower, on the same continent, with thin soil and wells, so the core squares stopped walking to the Awakening Tree while the sand-country was still going west. That difference is dirt, not a different gospel.

## Powers & Polities

**[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]], the Tallied Crown** *(the darkest corner: theology high · reach high · governance high)*, is the wealthy, pious, surveilled kingdom coiled near the sacred center. Its census, the **[[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]]**, treats the writing-down of people as reverent stewardship of the Mother's gifts. Maiethorn's full reach is why Threnmaieth has so much to count. The seat is [[Maiethlir]], a Hand on the Core-thaw, a town whose Tree stands on that river, and Maiethlir does not own the grove.

Five other powers share the continent, and [[Powers of the Turning]] names the whole set. They are hearths, not nations of one born people. [[Maiethvael|The Gift-Realm]] is devout and rich and keeps no list, and its capital is [[Seinbrun]]. [[Orenbren|The Witness-Hearths]] holds lodging as its power and does not own the Tree. Its capital is [[Rothallo]], still called the Inner Close, and its squares are [[The Third Hearth]], [[The Mill-hold]], and [[Nelath]]. [[Saelthael|The Sown Tree]] is the Rain-Shadow still walking, its city [[Larbril]] and its square [[Ornsael]]. [[The Hinge Shore]] is the face toward the Old Crossing, and its port is [[Votaer]]. [[Lirorn|The Thaw-Land]] is the Rain-Wall and the snowmelt, and its town is [[Tasain]].

Threnmaieth's capital is [[Maiethlir]]. For a reader who has only this page, Strandoren's seats are [[Raitin]], [[Naenor]], [[Lunbra]], and [[Braetu]], and Heskoren's other seats are [[Eolvaeth]], [[Sanbreo]], the land of [[Vaelhesk]], and [[Natai]].

## Faith

This continent is the birthplace and the stronghold of the **[[The Leaf-Mother|Motherfaith]]**. Its institutions, its pilgrimage, and its authoritative [[The Tree-Wardens|tree-wardens]] all seat here. The sacred center is contested, not serene.

### The heartland heresy — [[The Watching]]

An older Tree-faith, one the Motherfaith grew over, persists in Maiethorn's oldest districts. The friction is at the center, not out on the frontier. They keep the Tree as witness, and their own name for that keeping is *Haelthael*. They refuse the Mother-name. Because the faith is old, it claims to remember what the world believed before the Trees were understood as the Mother's hands. The full account is [[The Watching]], and the map of the faiths is [[Faiths of the Turning]].

## Story hooks

The roads into this continent are already stories.

- The pilgrimage to Thaeloren is a road-story in its own right, because everyone comes here eventually. In [[The Walking Years]] they had to come. The Near Mile, the inland walk to the Awakening Tree, was how Maiethorn Turned its children.
- Threnmaieth's [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]] census is a thing to steal, to forge, to escape, or to audit against what the old heresy remembers.
- Clergy of the heartland heresy "remember" something the Motherfaith teaches otherwise, and a scholar can follow the question of what the world believed before the Trees were called the Mother's hands.
- A Kept child in a fervent full-reach district is treated as spiritually deficient in a place where most of the children around them were Given.

**Status and build.** This note was built with `systemic-worldbuilding` for Epic 3, tracked on [[Roadmap]]. It is proposed, and these bones are locked. Maiethorn holds the one Awakening Tree, as [[The Premise]] and [[Turning Tree]] have it. The reach is full. Threnmaieth sits here, as [[Polity Archetypes]] drew it. The conservative [[The Old Tongue|Old-Tongue drift]] is the daily speech.

**Heresy thread.** [[The Watching]] is a quiet keystone thread. The heartland-heresy note below is the place to keep it.

**The scholar hook.** The scholar who follows the old faith's memory is walking toward [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real|the keystone question]].

**Full reach here is the control end of the natural experiment.** Maiethorn is where the Trees are healthiest and the Given are densest, the baseline against which the frontier's thin reach and the Isle's no-reach are read. Keep the cause on the GM side. [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]] holds that her reach is fullest at the source. In the world, what people say is only that these are the oldest, best-tended Trees. Do not let a region note assert why proximity to Thaeloren means a fuller reach.

**The heartland heresy is a keystone keeper.** [[The Watching]] sits at the sacred center so that an old faith can hold an old memory, what was believed before the Trees were called hers. It is the natural in-world source that could corroborate the keystone from an unexpected direction: not the skeptics, and not the devout, but the heretics. The faith is built, and it still points at [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]].

**Threnmaieth's tell lives here.** Per [[Polity Archetypes]], the Tallied Crown distrusts the [[Law and Citizenship|three pillars]] and keeps a ledger instead, and its Long-Lived crown-archivists may quietly remember what the [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]] was made to overwrite. On Maiethorn that archivist is near the sacred center and near the old heresy, which makes this a rich place to seat that character.
