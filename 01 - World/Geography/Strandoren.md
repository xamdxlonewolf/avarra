# Strandoren

> **What this is.** People call it the **Shore-lands**. It is the great trade continent of [[The World Frame|the world]], lying west of [[Maiethorn]] across **[[Named Ground|the Old Crossing]]**, the narrow and ancient water between them. The coasts are long and deeply indented, the [[Turning Tree|Trees]] are mature and abundant, and the reach is high, while the faith has been worn thin by commerce. This is the crossroads where the [[Economy and the Tithe|Condition-economy]], the work and trade that runs on what people became, comes into full commercial flower. It is the home of **[[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]]**, the Ledger Coast.

The speech here is the *eroded* [[The Old Tongue|Old-Tongue drift]], with the *th* dropped and the ends of words clipped. It is the most-drifted speech in the world, and this is the least devout of the lands.

*(In daily speech the name is worn down to **Strandor**. [[The Old Tongue|strand]] is "shore," and [[The Old Tongue|oren]] makes the whole "the Witnessed Shore," eroded smooth. The name itself shows the drift: a sacred root worn down by the docks.)*

## Overview

Strandoren is the crossroads of the known world, a continent of harbours. The coastline is so deeply indented with bays, sounds, and peninsulas that no point of it sits far from saltwater. If [[Maiethorn]] is the old cathedral heart, Strandoren is the market that grew up facing it: charter-towns, guild halls, banking houses, and quays. The [[Turning Tree|Trees]] here are mature and many. The reach is high, and the Given, the people a coloured leaf fell on in childhood, are dense. The faith is worn light, folded into commerce, until a child's [[Turning Tree|Turning]] is watched less as a sacrament than as the question of which guild the colour opens. This is where [[Economy and the Tithe|deathless banking]], the finance of houses that outlive an ordinary span of years, reaches its full flower, and where Condition-labor buys the world's most advanced public works.

Strandoren is free, prosperous, and cool, and nothing here is policed: your faith, your gift, and your marriage are your own. Everything is priced, and the injustice is the market's. A person who cannot pay fades while a rich continent steps politely around them. Warmth is not its virtue; liberty is.

## Geography & Climate

The indented coast is the fact the whole continent is built on. The shoreline is a run of natural harbours, tidal estuaries, and sheltered sounds. Every notable town is a port, and the sea is the continent's highway. This is the densest [[Selkie]] hearth in the world, not a Selkie nation but a lot of shoreline, which is the distinction [[Kinds of the Turning]] is careful about. Selkies are the sea-born, and a coast this broken simply holds more of them.

**[[Named Ground|the Old Crossing]]** lies to the east, the narrow, crowded, ancient water between Strandoren and [[Maiethorn]], and it is the busiest crossing in the world. [[Orentel]] faces it, and the Hush-rate, the charge left over from [[The Hinge Hush]], is still taken on this water.

**[[Named Ground|the West Water]]** lies to the west and south, the wider sea toward [[Heskoren]] and, far beyond the last capes, [[The Sundering Isle]]. [[Netstrand]] faces that water. Strandoren's boldest houses run the frontier trade, and almost none of them have crossed the storm-wall, the band of weather, current, and reef that closes the far ocean. The people who claim they have crossed are the ones who tell the Yumboe stories, the tales of the people of that isle.

Inland, fertile lowlands follow **[[Named Ground|the Chart-run]]**, the river that feeds the port-cities. The climate is mild and maritime, wet and temperate. Mature grafted Trees stand thick along the wealthy coasts. [[Trenledd]] sits on the stretch of river that got filed, the part rich enough to be written into the count. The "river-days" in [[The First Cut]], the inland stretches that waited while the coasts were already served, are this run.

Week-by-week weather, crops, sickness, and livelihood ecology are in [[Climate of Strandoren]].

## Reach

The reach is high. The grafts are mature and abundant, especially on the coast, the Given are dense, and the [[Law and Citizenship|three pillars]] are pure infrastructure here, not reverence but the machinery a huge commercial society runs on. [[Long-Lived]] notaries remember what a contract said, the [[Bound]], whose sworn word closes a bargain, are how a deal is finished, and the [[Far-Voiced]], whose feeling carries in the voice, are on every dock and in every court. The [[Law and Citizenship|Struck]], people who changed later and alone, are vouched in briskly and efficiently, and an unvouched person is a transaction that cannot clear.

How the wood got here is [[The First Cut]]. The years are Cut-year 40–160, and the wood came by sea, wealthy coasts first. A ship is faster than filling every inland hamlet, and a coast could pay a Cutting-leave, the permission to carry a living cutting, while a village on the Near Mile, the old inland walk on Maiethorn, was still waiting on the list. The interior river-days came later. [[The Fair Hand]] files this history as *the carrying*, a bill of lading, not a wound in wood.

## Powers & Polities

**[[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]], the Ledger Coast** *(theology low · reach high · governance low)*, is the premier merchant power. Guild self-rule is carried as far as it will go, deathless-house finance runs at scale, and the provision of a Tithe, the upkeep a Condition needs, is a private market. You buy your green, your novelty, your outlet, whatever the gift requires if it is not to fade, and you fade if you cannot pay. This is the richest and coolest corner of the world. The seat is [[Orentel]], the Salt Quay. [[The White Note House]] is placed in that city, and it is not crowned there.

Four other powers share the continent. [[Brenledd]], the Hearth-League, keeps its council at [[Raitin]], and the league stays a league of hearths rather than becoming one state. [[Leddvael]], the Reckoned Gift, keeps its signing-watch at [[Naenor]], and the [[The Book-Hands|Book-Hands]] still have no seat of their own. [[Trenledd]], the Worn Count, keeps the roll at [[Lunbra]], and [[Netstrand]], the Night Shore, quotes the far crossing at [[Braetu]]. Lestrand's capital is [[Orentel]].

## Faith

The faith is worn light, but it is not absent, and here it has a rival with real doctrine.

### The fortune-and-contract cult — [[The Fair Hand]]

[[The Fair Hand]] is a mercantile theology. It does not read a Condition as a gift given, which is the [[The Leaf-Mother|Motherfaith]]'s line. It reads a Condition as a bargain struck. The world, in this telling, is a ledger of deals, and what you became is what you, or your line, bargained for. That is a direct doctrinal rival to the doctrine of the gift, and it is at home among the [[Bound]]. It reframes the Tithe as a term to be serviced, rather than an upkeep to be tended. The full account is [[The Fair Hand]], whose own name is *Leddoren*, and the map of the faiths is [[Faiths of the Turning]].

## Story hooks

The market gives a party something to walk into on the first day.

- A [[Economy and the Tithe|deathless house]] calls a century-old debt, or a family too poor to meet a Tithe fades while the rich city steps politely around them.
- The [[Economy and the Tithe|shadow economy]] is at its biggest here: smuggled green, black-market novelty, vouching sold for coin, and off-book [[Bound|contracts]] that feed the [[Bound|Given-Over]], people signed so far into a bargain that someone else effectively holds them.
- The fortune-and-contract cult and the Motherfaith feud in the open, in a port city, two doctrines competing for the same [[Bound]] souls.
- A rare merchant-adventurer claims to have crossed to [[The Sundering Isle]] and come back. The claim may be true, embellished, or a fraud, and the city will price all three.

## GM Notes
*(Stripped on player-safe export. Keyed to the keystone via `reveals: [keystone-adjacent]`.)*

**Status and build.** This note was built with `systemic-worldbuilding` for Epic 3, tracked on [[Roadmap]]. It is proposed, and these bones are locked. The reach is high. Lestrand sits here, as [[Polity Archetypes]] drew it. The eroded Old-Tongue drift dominates the speech.

**High reach and low faith are the pairing that shows theology and reach are independent.** Strandoren has Trees as healthy as [[Maiethorn]]'s and wears the faith lightly. The Given are dense here for the same reason they are dense anywhere, which is healthy grafts, and the faith is thin for a purely social reason, which is that commerce erodes devotion. Keep those two facts separate in the world. Do not let the cool faith be read as thin reach, or the thin faith be read backward as sick Trees. The [[The Old Tongue|eroded language drift]] is the tell. The speech is smooth because the coast is secular, not because it is Tree-poor.

**The fortune-and-contract cult is a rival reading of the true gifts, not a false gift.** Per [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real|the keystone]], the Conditions are real gifts. The cult calls them bargains, the Motherfaith calls them blessings, and both are interpretations laid over the same real thing. Neither reading is confirmed by any reveal. The cult is wrong about who, because no bargain was struck, but its adherents are as sincere as the Motherfaith's. Do not play the cult as villain-theology.

**Strandoren is the natural staging ground for an expedition to the Isle.** If a campaign ever reaches for [[The Sundering Isle]], the ships, the money, and the tall tales all originate here. The merchant who "crossed the storm-wall" is a Strandor archetype. Hold the question of whether the claim is true as a live question tied to the Isle's reveal.
