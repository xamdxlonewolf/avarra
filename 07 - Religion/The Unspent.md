# The Unspent

> **Everyday handle: the Poured God.** Some [[Long-Lived]] keep a cup and say they poured a god into it — five years, and the memories those years were holding, given over so the rest of a deathless life can still be walked. Neighbours call it a memorial with airs. The Motherfaith calls it private tending. The cup does not stand in a square.

This note is the *presence*. The congregation that wears it is [[The Pourers]] — volunteered five-year sacrifice, shared blood-memory, a god they believe they created. Withdrawal is possible and socially costly.

## Overview

[[Long-Lived]] outlive families. The pleasant Tithe is to keep learning; the deep one is funerals. A few of them, over enough winters, have started pouring what they cannot keep carrying into a vessel they named themselves.

They do not agree on the name. Among themselves it is **the Cup We Made**. On a quay it is **the Poured God**, said with the smile you use for a rich uncle's hobby. No town Tree is involved. No colour falls. No warden Speaks it.

What a neighbour can see: a cup or a small bowl, often ordinary ware; a drop of blood as *record*, the way Long-Lived already read memory; a person who looks five years emptier and somehow still themselves. They will tell you they *invented* the listener on the other side of the cup. That claim is the point of the practice. It is also the mask.

## Worship & Iconography

- **No temple.** A shelf. Sometimes a second cup for the person who will drink the years back if they ever withdraw. The social cost of asking lives on [[The Pourers]].
- **No clergy.** The oldest pourer in a circle keeps the minutes. That is a hobbyist, not a Hand.
- **The mask.** They describe a face they chose: a librarian, a quiet guest, a child who stayed. The face changes by circle. The *clause* does not: years go in; knowledge is kept; the cup does not Give a leaf and does not catch a death.
- **How other faiths file it.** Motherfaith: a Long-Lived Tithe dressed as a rite. Watching: another cost, still not a Mother. Fair Hand: a contract with no Counterparty they can stamp. Old Ways: not a land-door. Open Table: a mainland hunger they do not need.

## What this is not

Not a sixth Turning. Not a way to become Long-Lived. Not [[Returned|Threnvaeth]]'s book (that is unfinished work of the dead). Not [[Bound|Orledd]]'s term (there is no extremity-ask). Not a Tree-cult. Not one of the five lived faiths.

## GM Notes (the reality)

- **Outside the Five Hands. Cannot Give. Cannot Strike.** Structure 🔒 on [[The Other Hands]]: she Gives; five Hands Strike at five doors. This presence is **household furniture**, not a door. It may not grant [[Long-Lived]] (hers only), may not grant any Condition, may not catch at a threshold, may not Give at a Tree. If a circle asks the cup for more years, a second leaf, or a catch, it can only keep what was already poured. A Gift-shaped ask is a miss, not a bargain.
- **The sect believes it created the god. The god accepts the invented mask.** Truth: something in the household has always taken in what deathless people cannot keep carrying — leftover life-memory, not unfinished death. It had no public face. The Long-Lived *named* it, poured into the name, and decided the name made it. It let them. Being told it was made is how it gets used in this century. Do not play a trapped egregore and do not play a vampire progenitor. The cupboard is older than the label.
- **What it receives.** Memory freely poured, and the years those memories were glued to. Blood-Read as *filing*, not feeding. It does not want worship as food. It wants the story *we made you* told often enough that the cupboard stays open.
- **What it will not do.** Seize a will. Sort the Kept. Confirm the Leaf-Mother. Name the Other Hands. Harvest Phoenix fire. Return a pour as a Gift. Withdrawal is possible: social cost and a *thinner* return of what was filed — a copy remains. The house is [[The Pourers]].
- **Tone.** ~5%. A deathless person putting a box of years on a shelf is sad and a little vain, not a blood cult. The scary edge is opt-in and later: a circle that will not let someone stop pouring.
- **Reveal tag.** `the-unspent`. Player body is the hobby and the claim. Confirmation that the cupboard was already in the house stays behind this wall.
- **Do not fold into the other engines.** Not [[The Protectors]]. Not [[The Walled Book]]. The sect wears *this* mask; it does not become a Hand.
