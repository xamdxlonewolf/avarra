# When Someone Is Struck

> **The hour the change arrives.** To be Struck is to be changed with no leaf in anyone's hand, at a drowning or an oath or a death or some other threshold, before a town has had time to decide what sentence fits. Later, the town may acknowledge the person at a Tree, or settle a civic year with them, or walk them back to a door. Those are other days, and they are already written on [[Turning Tree]], [[The Fair Hand]], and [[The Old Ways]]. This page is only the hour itself, when the right sentence is still too early.

## At the pile

At [[Orentel]], under the pilings, on a tide that ought to have been an ordinary tide, the gang hauls up a person who went into the water as one of them and comes back up with a voice that does not fit the chest, or with a calm the morning did not have. The gang does not have a word for it yet, though they will.

What the hour is for is ordinary care. People get their shoulders under the person, get the water off them, and get a coat around them, and somebody counts the breaths. They do not walk the person to Tallo, which is the name the quay uses for its Tree, and they do not ask which story the new voice belongs to. If a book-hand opens a book over a person who cannot yet refuse a line, whoever is holding the head tells them to shut the book. Tomorrow is soon enough, if the person can refuse by then. If a civic settling is going to happen, it is an errand for daylight, not for this hour.

Before the coat is even fastened, someone on the gang will say the person was **Struck**, someone else will say the old rite reached them, and someone else will say a term opened in the storm. The person inside the coat is still coughing, so the argument can wait for the morning. The slips a hall may write afterward, about people who came in with no colour and would not agree on one account, are [[The Uncoloured Intake]]. Those slips are not this hour.

## If the door is a street

On the thaw-street in [[Maiethlir]], the family takes the person into a house, and they do not take them to the root of the Tree tonight. One sibling is already planning the walk for the morning, so the person can be acknowledged and not Given, because the walk is not a request that the Tree hand over a Condition. The other sibling says the Tree does not Strike, and that a walk in the morning is a kindness which can wait until the person is able to decline it. Both of them are in the family, and what the hour comes down to is the blanket and the shutters. The sibling who needs to say Maieth, the name the devout use, may say it over the blanket, and the person under the blanket is not required to say it.

## If the door is a vale

In [[Eolvaeth]] they get the person dry, and if the person can sit they take them to the pot in the gift-hall. If a door-keeper is in the fold, one of the people the older faith trusts with a door, that is who you go and fetch. If no door-keeper is there, the house does the same things it already does when the weather turns bad. Nobody names a colour over the steam. The spring's leaf is not a sign for this ([[Eolvaeth]]), and a warden who has arrived early, however kind the warden is being, is still early.

## If the door is the hill

It happens rarely on the hill, and once the hall has a name for what happened it treats the news as family news. [[Njunda]] feeds the person, nobody reads a test over the food, and nobody tells the loud story, the mainland telling of the Mother. How a becoming works on [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] is not a lesson the valley owes a guest ([[The Open Table]] · [[Yumboe]]). The place at the board stays set for whatever it was already set for.

## The same hour, other doors

If the change came by an oath said in a back room, the people there take the pen out of the person's hand until that person can hear the clause they are still saying yes to. If it came by a death that did not finish, they keep the person warm, and they do not name the person's Purpose tonight, the unfinished thing a Returned is still here to tend. A family that knows them can tell later, by a touch, whether that Purpose is being tended, because a Returned who is settling goes cool ([[Daily Life]]). Someone who was turned to stone and then came back, or someone who came out of the deep wild changed, is fed, and is not brought under a canopy tonight.

If the person is still inside their tenth year, the problem is a different one, and it is short. While the week is still open, people bring them to be welcomed. They do not stand them under the Tree as if the Tree were giving them a second life. The row for a child who was already Struck is on [[Turning Tree]].

## GM Notes
*(Stripped on player-safe export.)*

- **The hour is care, and the argument is postponed.** Do not confirm who, if anyone, meets a person at a threshold. Wide-Hand and Narrow-Hand remain an argument on [[The Leaf-Mother]], and this hour does not award the argument to either of them.
- **Do not name a household, a particular keeper of a door, or a new blessing.** Do not make the storm-wall into the reason this hour is rare on the hill. Rare is already the fact of the isle.
- **~5%.** What the hour should feel like is coughing, a coat, a pen taken out of a hand, and a morning that arrives too soon carrying a kindness. If a Tithe comes in with the change, that Tithe is the Condition's problem later, not a problem for this hour to solve. The world book stays untouched.
