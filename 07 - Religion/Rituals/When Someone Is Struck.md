# When Someone Is Struck

> **The hour itself.** Later, a town may acknowledge them at a Tree, settle a civic year, or walk them back to a door. Those are other days, and they are already on [[Turning Tree]], [[The Fair Hand]], and [[The Old Ways]]. This is the hour the change arrives, when nobody has a leaf and the right sentence is still too early.

## At the pile

[[Orentel]], under the pilings, on a tide that should have been ordinary. They haul a person up who went in as one of the gang and comes up with a voice that does not fit the chest, or with a calm the morning did not have. The gang does not have the word yet. They will.

The hour is shoulders, water, a coat. Count the breaths. Do not walk them to Tallo. Do not ask which story the voice belongs to. A book-hand who opens a book over a person who cannot yet refuse a line is told to shut it by whoever is holding the head. Tomorrow, if they can refuse. The civic settling, if there is one, is a daylight errand.

Someone will say **Struck** before the coat is fastened. Someone will say the old rite reached. Someone will say a term opened in a storm. The person in the coat is still coughing. The argument can have the morning. The Uncoloured slips a hall writes afterward are [[The Uncoloured Intake]]. They are not this hour.

## If the door is a street

On the thaw-street in [[Maiethlir]], they take the person into a house. They do not take them to the root tonight. One sibling is already planning the morning walk, to be acknowledged, not Given. Another says the Tree does not Strike, and that a morning walk is a kindness that can wait until the person can decline it. Both are in the family. The hour is the blanket and the shutters. Maieth can be said over the blanket by the sibling who needs to say it. It is not required of the person under it.

## If the door is a vale

In [[Eolvaeth]] they get the person dry and, if they can sit, to the gift-hall pot. A door-keeper, if one is in the fold, is who you fetch. If none is, the house does what it does for weather. Nobody names a colour over the steam. The spring's leaf is not a sign for this ([[Eolvaeth]]). A warden who is kind and early is still early.

## If the door is the hill

Rare, and the hall treats it as family news once there is a name for it. [[Njunda]] feeds them. Nobody reads a test. Nobody says the loud story. The how of a becoming on [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] is not a lesson the valley owes a guest ([[The Open Table]] · [[Yumboe]]). The place stays set for what it was already set for.

## The same hour, other doors

An oath in a back room: take the pen out of the hand until the person can hear the clause they are still saying yes to. A death that did not finish: keep them warm, and do not assign the unfinished work tonight. A family that knows them can already tell, later, by an embrace, whether that work is being tended ([[Daily Life]]). A person the ground held and then released, or a person who came out of the trees changed: feed them, and do not bring them to a canopy tonight.

The tenth year, if they are still inside it, is a different problem and a short one. Bring them to be welcomed if the week is still open. Do not stand them for a second life. The row for an already-Struck child is on [[Turning Tree]].

## GM Notes

- **The hour is care and a postponed argument.** Do not confirm who, if anyone, meets a threshold. Wide-Hand and Narrow-Hand stay an argument on [[The Leaf-Mother]]. Do not award it here.
- **Do not name a household, a door's keeper, or a new blessing.** Do not make the storm-wall a reason this hour is rare on the hill. Rare is already the isle's fact.
- **~5%.** Coughing, a coat, a pen taken away, a morning that comes too soon with a kindness. The Tithe, if one arrives with the change, is the Condition's later problem. World book untouched.
