# The Watching

> **The heartland heresy of [[Maiethorn]].** It is an older Tree-faith the [[The Leaf-Mother|Motherfaith]] grew over, and it is not a frontier dissent but a *centre* one. They keep the Tree and refuse the Mother-name, and they say the colours fall because the Tree *sees*, and not because a she *chooses*.

In everyday speech the name is **the Watching**. Their own name is **Haelthael** (hayl-THALE), from *hael* and *thael*, "the Tree kept whole." They mean the Tree before anyone split it into a Mother and her hands. The Motherfaith's soft name for them is **the First Watching**, and [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]]'s file-name is **the Grown-Over**.

## Shape of the faith (at a glance)

This is a witness-religion, and it is not a gift-religion, because the founding fact is that the Tree *looks*. A colour is a seeing made visible, and not a present wrapped in a leaf. To live well is to *name what was seen*, and not to thank a giver you cannot prove.

Their orthopraxy is older than the Motherfaith's. They stand at the same Leaf-Fall, and they will not say the Tender's blessing as the wardens say it. Practice binds them to the town, and the withheld word is the whole dissent.

They will not give the Tree a divine character: it is not a mother, not a bargain-partner, and not a land-spirit in a new dress; it is the Tree. Naming it *Maieth* is, to them, the overlay, a comfort the core invented when the seeing needed a face.

The mark of the faith is the name they keep for the wood. They keep **Thaeloren** ("the Tree that witnesses") and refuse **Hand of the Mother**. It is the same wood, and the title is the schism.

## Cosmology & doctrine (as believed)

They share the Motherfaith's picture of **the Before**, a beginning in which the world was Kind and Kind alone, people as they were born and nothing besides. They part company on what came next: the Tree, they say, was already there and already watching, the Giving came after that Watching, and the Mother-name came last, so that each layer is a story laid over a seeing.

"She chooses well" is a later sentence. Fittingness, they say, is what a witness produces: the Tree sees what you can carry and what the town will need, and the leaf matches the seeing, which is intent without a mother. To a Watcher, the skeptics of the Motherfaith are *almost* right, and still too quick to call the Tree a phenomenon with no mind. There is a mind in the seeing, and it does not owe you a lap.

Haelthael teaching remembers a cost, and holds that the Giving was not free: something was spent, someone was lessened, and the Tree, or whatever stands behind it, does not reach twice by chance, because *reaching costs*. They will not name a Mother who pretends otherwise, and what the cost *was* they will not agree on, as the schism below sets out, though they agree that the cost was real.

The Kept were seen: a hug and no colour is a seeing of wholeness, or a seeing that did not reach, and they argue this as fiercely as the Motherfaith does, without the consolation of a mother's lap. The hug is still a witness: it is not an oversight, and it is not, they insist, a slight dressed as love.

The Struck were seen at a harder door: a drowning, an oath, or a death is an older place of seeing, and the Tree is the ordinary one. The Watching does not need the Wide-Hand and Narrow-Hand split the Motherfaith keeps, the quarrel over how wide a giving hand reaches, because seeing was never confined to a grafted scion.

## Practice

**The Night Watch** is kept the night before [[The Reckoning of the Year|Turning-Week]]. Watchers keep a silent vigil at the town Tree, with no blessing spoken and no colour read. They keep watch on the Tree all night, as they believe it watches the town.

**The withheld blessing** is what they do at the Leaf-Fall itself. They attend, because orthopraxy still holds and their children still Turn, and when the warden says the Tender's line a Watcher household speaks its own, quietly:

> *"What falls, was seen. What is kept, was seen. The Tree does not reach twice by chance."*

The cadence is the same as the Motherfaith's blessing, and the Mother is not in it. Towns that have lived with both wordings for a long time barely notice the difference, and Threnmaieth notices.

**Name what you saw** is the devotion after a Turning. A Watcher does not say *tend what you're given*; they sit with the child and *describe* the fall, the colour, the way the leaf came, and what the year will ask, because devotion, for them, is accurate speech.

**Pre-Mother names** are what the liturgy will allow. In Haelthael liturgy the origin Tree is only **Thaeloren**, "First Hand" is tolerated as poetry, and "Hand of the Mother" is the overlay they will not say.

## Clergy & institutions

The working house is [[The Watchers]].

**Watchers** are not priests. They keep the Night Watch, they teach the withheld blessing, and, where a town will have it, they offer a second reading of a rare or mixed fall. That reading is not authoritative over the [[The Tree-Wardens|tree-warden]]; it is a *witness alongside*, and in Threnmaieth they are a known column on the roll.

The Watching has **no mother-church**, and no seat in the Awakening Tree's shadow, because that seat was grown over by the [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]]. Their oldest rooms are under older chapels, or in houses that never took a warden's blessing on the door. What Watchers do is keep the Night Watch and teach the withheld blessing, and they have no offices beyond that.

## The live schism (the faith arguing with itself)

1. **The Masked.** The Mother is a later face the Witness learned to wear, or a name people needed and the Tree allowed. The reading is syncretic, close to the Motherfaith, and common in mixed families. A Masked Watcher will stand for the Tender's blessing and add the withheld line under it.

2. **The Bare Tree.** There was never a she: the overlay is a comfort the core invented, and comfort is not the same as truth. This is the harder and smaller reading, and it is the one Threnmaieth files most carefully.

3. **What was spent?** Both branches agree the Giving cost something, and they do not agree *what*: a wound in the Tree, a year the watching failed, or a someone who became the wood. This is the question they will not close, and it is also the one the Motherfaith most wants left unasked.

## In-world text

The inscription was cut on a door-beam, then recut, and found when a warden-chapel on Maiethorn was re-floored. The new beam faces the Tree; this face was turned to the stone.

> *We kept the watch before the name. The wood was already looking. If a mother came after, she came to a work begun. Do not thank the latecomer for the seeing.*

It reads as heresy, stubborn and local, and it claims that the Tree is older than the Mother-name.

They are the keystone's keepers at the centre. They were placed on Maiethorn so that old memory sits next to the Awakening Tree, and not out on the frontier. They are the natural in-world source that could corroborate the keystone from an unexpected direction: not the skeptics, and not the devout, but the *heretics*. They have hold of the Before, of the cost, and, per [[The Other Hands]], of a household that was not yet named Mother. They miss that the seeing *is* hers.

Do not confirm their metaphysics: "what she is" stays open, as [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]] holds it. The Masked reading, that she is a later face, and the Bare Tree reading, that there was never a she, are both playable. Neither is the locked answer, and the inscription is a clue rather than a verdict.

The cost they remember is still open. That giving costs her is locked, and *what* was spent is the open nature of the limit. Story 6.1 showed the edge *moving with wood* and did not pick a cause, so let Watchers argue, and do not let a note decide.

Threnmaieth's tell is the filing itself: to file them as the Grown-Over is the census doing theology. A Long-Lived crown-archivist who still has a copy of the un-recut inscription is the kind of character this faith wants.
