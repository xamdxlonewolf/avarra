# Faiths of the Turning

> The [[The Leaf-Mother|Motherfaith]] is the dominant faith of the Turning lands and **not the only one**. This note is the map of how the others sit, rub, and sometimes share a rite, across the four [[The World Frame|continents]]. It is not a list of gods. It is five *lived faiths*, each answering a different experience the Trees (or their absence) actually produce.

## The five faiths at a glance

| Faith | Home | A Condition is… | Everyday name | Own name |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[[The Leaf-Mother]]** | everywhere the Trees stand; born on [[Maiethorn]] | a **gift chosen** | the Motherfaith | *Maieth* |
| **[[The Watching]]** | [[Maiethorn]]'s oldest districts | a **seeing**, later named a gift | the Watching | *Haelthael* |
| **[[The Fair Hand]]** | [[Strandoren]]'s ports and houses | a **bargain struck** | the Fair Hand | *Leddoren* |
| **[[The Old Ways]]** | [[Heskoren]]'s groves and doors | the **land making its own** | the Old Ways | *Vaeloren* |
| **[[The Open Table]]** | [[The Sundering Isle\|Kumbaan]] | a mainland hunger **they never had** | the Open Table | *Ndeyaan* |

The three mainland rivals have their homes on [[Maiethorn]], [[Strandoren]], and [[Heskoren]], so the friction is inside the settled world, among people who already live with Trees. [[The Open Table]] is the isolate: it grew up with no Tree and no Mother-name.

Smaller household rites, quay-charms, and Kind-customs, especially those of the [[Kitsune]], the [[Selkie]], and the [[Tengu]], exist and stay local, and they are not load-bearing, so the five faiths still stand when a household keeps a charm no other household has heard of. Kind-hearths and the hearth-registers that record them live in [[Kinds of the Turning]] and [[Naming People in the Turning]]. The people who do the daily work are [[The Tree-Wardens]] for the Motherfaith, [[The Watchers]], [[The Book-Hands]], [[The Door-Keepers]], [[The Table-Keepers]], and [[The Shore-Sitters]], who are the isle's own flavor of that work. A Long-Lived fringe, [[The Pourers]], keeps the cup they call [[The Unspent|the Poured God]]. They are not a sixth faith.

## The argument (what a becoming *is*)

Every faith in this world has to answer the same fact. Most people *become* something they were not born, and that becoming is what these faiths mean by a Condition, though they do not agree on the verb.

The Given are the people who become under a Tree, and even the name is already an argument. The Motherfaith says they were *chosen*, the Watching says they were *seen*, the Fair Hand says they were *signed*, and the Old Ways say the Tree is a *late guest* at a door the land already kept.

The Struck are the people who change later, at a threshold, and the change often comes with no Tree nearby. The Motherfaith is still arguing whether the Leaf-Mother reached that threshold, the Watching says the threshold was always a place of seeing, the Fair Hand hears a later contract struck in extremity, and the Old Ways call this the *oldest* becoming, the land's own children.

The Kept are the people who get the hug and no colour, and the same hug has four readings: a gift of wholeness, a person passed over, no bargain taken, or a year the land had no door for you. The Open Table does not offer a reading of that hug, because on their account a Condition is a mainland hunger they never had.

The Tithe is the upkeep a becoming still asks, the same upkeep under four ethics. The Motherfaith calls it the honest weight of a gift, the Watching calls it the cost someone once paid, the Fair Hand calls it the term of the contract, and the Old Ways call it the land's due.

No faith is a villain-theology. Each is a good-faith answer to a real seam: the fittingness of gifts, the thinning at the edge, the written terms the Bound carry, the Struck who change with no Tree nearby, and a whole isle that never Turned, that never brought its children under a Tree to become. The [[The Leaf-Mother|Motherfaith's own schism]] already taught the courtesy the others inherited, and they use it. Right practice binds, right belief is argued about, and dual practice is common, not scandalous.

## How they live together

**Orthopraxy is the peace.** Because the Motherfaith counts you by what you *do* at the Tree, a Watcher who brings their child, a Settler of the Fair Hand who stands as witness to the colour, and a Kin-speaker of the Old Ways who also walks the old doors can all belong at the same Leaf-Fall, the festival when the town brings its children to the Tree. Doctrine fights; the festival usually doesn't.

**Conflict is local, not crusading.** The fight stays inside a district or a port, and it does not become a march of one faith upon another.

On [[Maiethorn]] the fight is *at the centre*. [[The Watching]] and the Motherfaith share the same Trees and argue over the blessing spoken there. [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] files the Watchers on the [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]], the roll it keeps, as a known, counted dissent under the name the Grown-Over, and what follows from that filing is pressure, not a pogrom.

On [[Strandoren]] faith is worn light, so the fight is *for the Bound and the books*, the Bound being the people whose terms are already written down. [[The Fair Hand]] and the Motherfaith compete for the same oath-keepers in the same port. Lestrand prices both and polices neither.

On [[Heskoren]] the fight is *over the grove*. [[The Old Ways]] were there before the grafts, the Trees set into older ground, and the Motherfaith burns hottest where the gift is scarcest. A dying Tree has two claimants, and Vaethorn's piety makes the quarrel tender and sharp, not holy-war loud.

On [[The Sundering Isle]] the wall still holds. A [[Yumboe]] on the mainland is a guest at everyone else's rite; they keep table and find the noon-day faiths, the mainland rites kept in the day, *loud*. The quarrel is already in the port: a host counts a guest as family after two suppers, a clerk wants to know where the person was Turned, and a host will not spend a child's tenth-year week on a Tree. The rest of it is [[The Open Table#Two suppers, or a Turning-witness]].

**Syncretism is what actually happens, most years.** A household keeps two practices, and the hard edge is the point where the two stop agreeing.

| Pair | The common blend | The hard edge |
|---|---|---|
| Motherfaith + Watching | The household keeps the same Tree and inherits which of the two blessings it speaks. | Threnmaieth's roll wants one name for what you are. |
| Motherfaith + Fair Hand | The Leaf-Fall is a festival and a signing-watch, the Fair Hand standing witness to the terms, and the Tithe is paid as both tending and settling. | Gift-language and debt-language contend over the same Bound soul. |
| Motherfaith + Old Ways | People make a pilgrimage to a graft and still walk the old doors at the threshold. | When a Tree sickens, the quarrel is who tends it, the warden or the door-keeper. |
| Any mainland + Open Table | A shared supper is rare, and someone will say "no one is a stranger twice." | On the mainland a person belongs because a town watched them Turn, the Open Table says a person belongs after two suppers, and a child of the table who reaches Turning-Week in Eolthael is the hard case. |

A shared teaching-story, [[The Child at Four Doors]], is told in all five houses with a different last line. That is how the world argues without a council.

## GM Notes
*(Stripped on player-safe export. `reveals: [keystone-adjacent, the-other-hands]`.)*

The household is now filled, and that filling is still GM-only. Per [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]] she is real, benevolent, bounded, and costly, and per [[The Other Hands]] she is **first of a household**: she Gives, and lesser Hands Strike at restricted doors under her allowance. Do not confirm this in a player-facing note. The Open Table's staying-kin are **not** of that household, so do not absorb Kumbaan.

Each rival holds a true kernel and misses the whole. The Watching correctly remembers a *Before* and a *cost*, and a household before it was named Mother, and they miss that the seeing *is* hers. The Fair Hand is right about the Bound, because there *is* a Counterparty, and wrong that every becoming is a bargain, because the Mother's Gifts are still gifts. The Old Ways are right that the land-doors are real, and they miss that she authorized the guests. The Open Table is the cleanest experiment: no Gifts, rare Struck, and a faith that was never hers ([[Yumboe]], [[The Sundering Isle]], [[The Other Hands]]). Their claim, *we were never lacking*, is still a late beat.

Dissent is not automatic virtue. Rival faiths can still count heretics, price fading, or turn a thin reach into guilt.

If a campaign ever works toward the keystone, triangulate from the three old corners, the Watching, the Old Ways, and the Open Table, which sit pre-Mother, pre-graft, and never-grafted. The Fair Hand is the *modern* misreading, commerce wearing a theology, the bargain-reading of a becoming dressed up as the faith. Use all four, and don't let one speak for the whole reveal.

What this pass does not lock is the public picture of the powers. There is **no confirmed pantheon in player-facing text.** The Leaf-Mother remains a *belief* in public notes, the other faiths stay beliefs, and you should not dump a household roster onto these pages. **Faith names are 🔒 locked (2026-08-23):** *Haelthael*, *Leddoren*, *Vaeloren*, *Ndeyaan*, a user taste call. Texture around them stays polishable. The working people of the clergy are [[The Tree-Wardens]] (5.1), [[The Watchers]], [[The Book-Hands]], [[The Door-Keepers]], [[The Table-Keepers]], and [[The Shore-Sitters]] (isle flavor). The mainland shadow house is [[The Slide]], and it is not clergy. Tithe-infrastructure and the safety-guilds are [[Tithe-Infrastructure]] (5.3), and the names there are 🟡. Do not clone the Slide as those guilds.

This pass also does not lock the nature of her limit. The Watching *claims* to remember a cost, Story 6.1 showed the edge *moving with wood* and did not pick a cause, and that claim is still fuel, not an answer. See [[The Ages of the Turning]].
