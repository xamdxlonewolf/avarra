# The Uncoloured Intake

> **A gift-hall working copy.** Not a hymn. Not a census. Three slips and a covering note from a live-front hall that will feed anyone who arrived without a leaf. The clerk who wrote it can see a pot and a charter. They cannot see why the mouths will not sit under one line.

Found among last Hale-month's papers at a Heskoren gift-hall that still takes the neighbour's week. No Speaker signed it. The verso is a grain-count.

## Covering note

> *Three uncoloured this season who will take the pot. I have written Mother's catch on each, as the charter requires. The mouths will not keep still under that line. Feed them anyway. Do not send them to the grove until they ask. Narrow-Hand will call this a leak. The pot does not have a theology. If the Seat wants a colour on these people they may send wood. We have soup.*

## The slips

**Merrin, far shed.** *I did not wake in anyone's lap. Someone finished a page and set it down. I still have work. If you need a mother in it, you will have to write her yourselves. I will eat. I will not be blessed for eating.*

**Senn of the quay.** *I asked. I was answered. The face was not the one the last Bound in this hall described. The clause was. I prefer the clause. Do not bless it. If your book needs a giver, leave mine closed.*

**A drowning, unnamed.** *It was breath. Cold, then used. Not a hymn. If the Tree had wanted me it had ten years. It had a week. It did not use them. I came back with a voice. I did not come back with a colour.*

A fourth line, same hand, thinner ink:

> *One more who will only say the ground held, then let go. Same stamp. The ink looked tired. That is the weather, or I am.*

The stamp on all four is identical: *Mother's catch — uncoloured — fed.* Someone has under-scored *Mother's* on the drowning slip, then rubbed it. The paper still shows the pressure.

## How it travels

- A Wide-Hand warden keeps a copy so the hall can justify the pot.
- A Narrow-Hand preacher quotes the drowning slip and stops before the stamp.
- A Book-hand on a quay asks to see Senn's clause and is told the hall does not keep clauses, only soup.
- The fourth line is sometimes omitted. Halls that omit it say the clerk was tired. Halls that keep it do not agree what *thinner* means.

It is not taught to children. It is not [[The Child at Four Doors]]. It is paperwork that failed to settle.

## GM Notes

- **Household clue, not confirmation.** Four mouths, four rooms (book / term / breath / holding). The clerk *needs* one Mother or the charter will not feed them. That need is the blindness. Do not complete the table for the party — [[The Taken-In|Vaelorn]] is absent this season on purpose. A fifth slip that names green would turn this into a roster.
- **Do not name Hands in play from this paper.** The party may notice the rooms disagree. You do not award [[The Other Hands]]. Pair that naming late, or never, and never with [[The Spent Leaf]].
- **Rung 5 adjacent, not rung 6.** Struck-received, still testimony. The stamp is doctrine. The rubbed underscore is a person losing an argument with their own job.
- **Use it as a handout.** One slip in a pack. The covering note on a hall door. Senn's line quoted by a Bound who is tired of being blessed. Do not read all four in a row as a lecture.
