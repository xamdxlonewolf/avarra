# Maieth

> **The name a devout square says.** [[The Leaf-Mother]] is the faith: orthopraxy, the schism, the clergy, the texts. This note is the presence they mean by the name. It is what the faithful hold. A skeptic can live a whole life in the same square without granting it.

## Overview

They say she is a mother, and quiet: that she gives, that she does not thunder, and that she does not come when called. A gift, they say, has a weight, and tending the weight is the life. A hug, they say, is also hers. Whether a person who changed at a drowning, an oath, or a death was still met by her is the faith's live argument, kept on [[The Leaf-Mother]], not settled by a picture.

The faithful hear a mind in the way a fall fits. Skeptics hear a tree doing what this tree does. Both can bring a child. Both can be good members of the faith, because the faith counts the doing.

They give her no throne, and no sentence that answers on demand. They meet her, if they meet her, at the wood, in the turn of a life, in the fit of a thing. The quiet is, for them, a mother letting her children live. It is also why a skeptic can stay sincere.

## Worship & Iconography

There is no face the houses share. The picture is the wood. A pressed leaf in a chest is common, and it is a leaf. It does not speak. Some hearths keep no image at all.

She is not drawn as a drowning, a signature, or a wild. Those are other faiths' doors. The Motherfaith's own fight about them stays on the faith note. The cup some deathless people keep is [[The Unspent]]. A warden's sill is not where that cup sits.

What a year feels like when people act — the week, the burial, the guest, the hour someone changes — is [[What the Year Feels Like]]. The blessing spoken over a child stays on [[The Leaf-Mother]]. It is not copied here.
