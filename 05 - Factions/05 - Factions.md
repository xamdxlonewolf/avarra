# 05 - Factions

This shelf is where the governments, organizations, guilds, religious orders, militaries, and criminal powers are filed. These are the houses that keep a town's rule, its worship, its week, or its warrant.

They do not recruit by **Kind**, the ancestry a person is born with ([[Kinds of the Turning]]). They recruit by **Condition**, the becoming a person carries, or by the faith that person keeps, or by the office they hold. The orders and offices below share that rule.

## Contents

### Governments

The fifteen great powers are the ones worth a note of their own. Three of them, Lestrand, Threnmaieth, and Vaethorn, are worked out in [[Polity Archetypes]], where the spoken names are the Ledger Coast, the Tallied Crown, and the Waiting Lands. The other twelve each have their own note here, and one hub covers all fifteen. Nine of those twelve notes are short stubs, and three of them, [[Lirorn]], [[Netstrand]], and [[The Hinge Shore]], are first drafts. The Inner Close, the walled city, sits inside [[Orenbren]]. [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] is not a sixteenth mainland power.

- [[Powers of the Turning]] is the hub. It keeps the count, the axes, the network, and the two documents each power keeps.
- [[Maiethorn]] holds [[Maiethvael]], [[Orenbren]], [[Saelthael]], [[The Hinge Shore]], and [[Lirorn]], and Threnmaieth is counted with them.
- [[Strandoren]] holds [[Brenledd]], [[Leddvael]], [[Trenledd]], and [[Netstrand]], and Lestrand is counted with them.
- [[Heskoren]] holds [[Ornled]], [[Vaelhesk]], and [[Saelvaeth]], and Vaethorn is counted with them.
- [[The Reckoned Offices]] are Threnmaieth's named offices: the registrar, the Reckoned Speaker, the channel-clerk, and the warrant-hand.
- [[The Walled Book]] is the Kept-class remnant. It is the Inner Close inside Orenbren, and it is not a separate power.

### Religious orders

| House | The job, in one line | Who sends for them |
|---|---|---|
| [[The Tree-Wardens]] | They tend the wood, officiate the Leaf-Fall when a child stands to be Turned, Speak the colour that leaf gives, and carry grafts. | Every town with a Tree, a waiting town, or a scion that is sickening. |
| [[The Remainder]] | They log blight, unused cuttings, and swept leaves, and they keep the colourless scrap. | Someone brings a mixed-fall leftover, a dead scion in the wrap, or a relic rumour that needs a boring answer. |
| [[The Watchers]] | They sit the eve-vigil, and they describe a strange fall beside the warden, not over the warden. | A family that does not trust one speaker on a mixed leaf, or a town on Turning-Week eve. |
| [[The Book-Hands]] | They stamp signings, settle a Struck person's civic year, and recite a Bound's Terms without rewriting them. | A quay that needs a notary, or a Struck person who needs a hall and a warrant. |
| [[The Door-Keepers]] | They walk the old doors, speak at ancestor-meals, and name a Struck person kin at the door that made them. | Someone who needs the drowning-pool found, or a Struck person the town will not house. |
| [[The Table-Keepers]] | They open the hill-hall, lay the extra place, and the second night makes kin. | The valley on a bright night, or a rare Yumboe attic. |
| [[The Shore-Sitters]] | They sit the storm-beach, wait, and walk the living uphill. | A wreck comes in, or a year of empty sand passes. |
| [[The Pourers]] | They give years of memory into a cup they say they made, one year at a time. | A Long-Lived whose funerals have stacked, or a withdrawer the long-house will not seat. |

- [[The Tree-Wardens]] are tree-wardens in ordinary speech, and the liturgy calls them *Orenhael*.
- [[The Remainder]] are the Remainder, or remainder-clerks. They are a desk of the First Seat, and they keep [[The Spent Leaf]].
- [[The Watchers]] are Watchers in ordinary speech, and the liturgy calls them *Nethoren*. Their speaker is [[Laevila]].
- [[The Book-Hands]] are Book-hands in ordinary speech, and the liturgy calls them *Leddhael*. They do **not** rewrite Bound Terms.
- [[The Door-Keepers]] are door-keepers, or Kin-speakers, and the liturgy calls them *Vaelbren*.
- [[The Table-Keepers]] are table-keepers in ordinary speech, and their own name is *Njaalo*.
- [[The Shore-Sitters]] carry the isle's flavor, and their own name is *Njawaal*.
- [[The Pourers]] are the Pourers, or the Cup Circle, in ordinary speech. They say [[The Unspent|the Poured God]] keeps the cup. They are not a sixth faith. The seed of that note is [[Reimaethe]].

### Criminal

[[The Slide]], [[The Holding Desk]], and [[The Standing Trade]] live in `05 - Factions/Criminal`. They are the criminal layer. There is no fourth house beside them.

| House | The job, in one line | Who sends for them |
|---|---|---|
| [[The Slide]] | They rent *not falling*: one week of green, an outlet, or novelty. | Someone whose Tithe-queue is longer than their edge, the time left before the failure shows. |
| [[The Holding Desk]] | They refinance a Bound's Terms after the honest year has closed. | A Bound who cannot pay the Pledge, or a creditor who wants a walkable instrument. |
| [[The Standing Trade]] | They sell a witness who will say *I was there*. | An unvouched traveller, or a name that must cross a warrant-line. |

- [[The Slide]] are the Slide, or week-keepers, in ordinary speech, and the cant name is *Vaethledd*. The official guilds know about them and say nothing when their doors meet.
- [[The Holding Desk]] are hold-clerks in ordinary speech. They are not the Slide.
- [[The Standing Trade]] is the Standing Trade in ordinary speech. It is not the Slide.

### Care

[[The Protectors]] stay under Organizations. In ordinary speech they are the Protectors, or the Care, a public hospice that keeps the one Phoenix. They are not a street gang, and they are not a fourth criminal house.

### Tithe-infrastructure & safety-guilds

| House | The job, in one line | Who sends for them |
|---|---|---|
| [[Tithe-Infrastructure]] | Official furnishing means *enough*, and a gate. The lots and halls are doors into a safety-guild, not colleges. | A GM mapping the sector, or a wilt or a Cry that the official door caused. |
| [[The Greens-Keepers]] | They keep the lot so Taken-In do not run short of green, and they do not follow you home. | A Taken-In in a stone town, or a Book-hand whose term assumes a share. |
| [[The Hall-Keepers]] | They keep the hour, and they stamp the Voice-ticket. | A Far-Voiced who needs an outlet, or a child after sea-blue. |
| [[The Stillers]] | They stamp the strictest ticket, and they run the theatre and the yard on one Grey-clock. | A pale-stone child, or a street about to become a massacre. |
| [[The Element-Guilds]] | They keep four craft-doors so an Answered's element is used in earnest. | A lamp-amber child, or a hull that wants Tide. |
| [[The Intake]] | They catch a raw Struck person before the rope, and success is silence. | A runner, a family, or a square that already has a rope. |

- [[Tithe-Infrastructure]] is the sector hub. The greens and the halls are jurisdictions of this note.
- [[The Greens-Keepers]] and [[The Hall-Keepers]] are doors into a safety-guild, not colleges.
- [[The Stillers]], [[The Element-Guilds]], and [[The Intake]] are the ticket-yards and the shed. The Intake's speaker is [[Tesara]], in Orentel. The Hall-Keepers' Voice-ticket stays with the halls.
- [[Craft Fellowships]] are the mill-share, the hull-wrights, and the road-mend. The work lasts a season. It is not a body-licence.

## GM Notes

### Three opposition engines (keep distinct)

These three engines have to stay distinct, because each one works by a different mechanism.

| Engine | House | Mechanism | Seed (do not plot) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Religious self-consumption | [[The Pourers]] | Years are volunteered into a god they believe they created. | [[Reimaethe]] |
| Political classification | [[The Walled Book]] | The Book of Tithes is the instrument, and a Given heir loses the Closed name. | [[Hithaen]] |
| Worship used to hide extraction | [[The Protectors]] | The method is sanctuary, controlled deaths, harvested fire, and a hush. | [[Taeren]] · [[Rosire]] |

The Protectors row in the Care table leaves out the harvest and the hush on purpose. The Care's public job is the hospice and the one Phoenix.
