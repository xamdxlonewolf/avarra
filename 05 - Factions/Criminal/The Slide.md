# The Slide

The Slide is the illegal supply of Tithe needs that [[Economy and the Tithe]] describes. A Tithe is the upkeep a person owes for what they have become, and it has to be met or the failure shows in public. The Slide is not a fifth faith, not clergy, and not a copy of [[The Book-Hands]], the clerks who stamp an honest year. They do not help a person *pay* a Tithe. They step in when a person starts to fail, and they make the failing last.

Everyday speech calls them **the Slide**, and also **week-keepers.** In cant they are **the Vaethledd** — VAETH-ledd — built from [[The Old Tongue|vaeth]], "the waiting," and [[The Old Tongue|ledd]], "to reckon": the waiting, reckoned. The Waiting Lands say **the last pot**, the Ledger Coast says **week-rent**, and the Tallied Crown files nothing, because the roll already has a counted hall.

What they rent is *not falling*, sold as one week of green, or one outlet, or one new page, and they are never in a hurry to let you get free.

## Shape of the house (at a glance)

This is a practice, not a gang. There is no seat, no colour, no roster, and no cellar liturgy. You are of the Slide when someone comes back next week, and that return is the whole of the reputation. When a keeper dies, the book of weeks goes to whoever still has a pot, a room, or a key.

They have one job. They furnish *just enough* Tithe-supply that the person does not become a public hazard, and *never enough* that they recover. The product is the week, and the price of that week is remaining theirs.

They think they are in a rent story, so they do not call themselves monsters. They extend, they keep a book, and they will tell you they kept someone whole. That is what makes them neighbours: a kind face, a real key, and a measured withhold later.

They are not clergy, and they are not the official doors. They do not stamp books, Speak colours, walk old doors, or open a hill-hall. They do not run the town greenhouse, the counted ael-hall, or a licence-guild. Those doors are the ones that wait. On ordinary days the guilds pretend not to know these people. When an official gate closes on purpose, the pretence is over, because the guild and the Slide are then serving the same street.

## What they actually do

### If you meet one

They look like a landlord, or like a cousin who still has a pot after the commons ran dry, or like a junior clerk at a deathless house, a long-house kept by the [[Long-Lived]], who will extend a novelty subscription one more week. They may be a person with a spare room and a voice who will let you cry there, for a price that is never quite coin.

You would not call them a priest. You would find them if you asked *who still has green when the leaf-lot is closed.* A leaf-lot is a strip of living green let by the season, and when it is shut, someone still has a pot.

They are useful the way a week is useful. If you came for a stamp, a colour, or a false childhood, you have the wrong house.

### Who comes, and when

The people who come are already late. A [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] needs living green, and without it they wilt. A [[Far-Voiced]] needs somewhere for feeling to go, and without it a Cry breaks into the street. A [[Long-Lived]] needs something new, and without it they fade. The other needs are warmth, civic coin, a Purpose that does not pay, or a family trying to keep the square from seeing any of this. The week-keeper rents the missing piece and stops short of a cure.

| Who | Why they come | What the week-keeper does | What they will not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| A [[The Taken-In\|Taken-In]] cut off from land | The greens-queue is longer than the wilt. The people in that queue are City-Given who never left, harvest-hands who wintered, and lot-labor that cannot save the cart-fare home ([[Economy and the Tithe#Why Taken-In live in stone cities]]). | They rent a pot, a strip, or a night on living dirt, and they extend if the week cannot be paid. | They will not give a commons-share that lasts, and they will not walk anyone to a warden to be filed as housed. |
| A [[Far-Voiced]] who lost a stage | The posting is isolated, the labor is dull, there is nowhere to vent, and the Cry is close. | They give a room, an hour, and a door that shuts: one outlet, and the next week is the same week. | They will not find honest work. They will not let the Cry happen in the street, because that brings the watch and ruins the book. |
| A [[Long-Lived]] whose novelty has thinned | The endowment is late, and the fade has started. | They ration a page, a letter, or a gossip that is *new enough*. | They will not endow a library, and they will not be that person's deathless patron in public. |
| An [[The Unbound\|Unbound]] losing warmth | Company costs money, and the hole is getting loud. | They sit with them, let themselves be known a little, and charge for the company. | They will not be family. They will not sign a new Bargain, because that is another table. |
| A [[Bound]] behind on *civic* coin | The debt is rent, a guild fee, or a year that will not close. | They hold the debt, extend it, and use the hold. | **They will not rewrite Terms.** The hold is money, not a clause, and they will not sell a cellar-easing. |
| A [[Returned]] whose Purpose pays nothing | The work the dead want done is not a trade that pays. | They give a week of keep so the person can keep walking. | They will not furnish the Purpose. They will not send anyone to a plague-ward unless the ward pays *them*. |
| A family, before the town sees | A child or a parent is sliding, and the family will pay so the square does not have to watch it. | They take the week, take the family's coin, and keep the person *quiet*. | They will not tell the warden, and they will not make the slide a sermon. |

### An ordinary week (the job you can miss)

They **extend first.** That is the hook, and it is also what they believe about themselves. A person arrives already late. The week-keeper does not withhold on the first night. They open the lot, the room, or the page, they write *extended*, and they name a day.

The withhold comes later, and it is measured. It is never enough to make a public hazard, a wilt in the square, a Cry in the market, or a fade a neighbour can name, because that brings the watch, the guild, the counted hall, and the book dies. It is never enough to let the person stand up and walk to a commons that would keep them. The asset is a person who is always almost whole.

They keep a **book of weeks**, not a ledger of coin. Coin is how some weeks are paid. The thing they are counting is *how long you have not fallen, on their floor.*

A child of the house learns the work by carrying water to the pots and by being told not to let a guest sleep a second week for nothing. There is no Seat to confirm it.

### What they carry

They carry a key, a pot or a spare room, a stack of pages that are new enough, and a book of weeks. They carry no stamp, no colour-scrap, no Cutting-leave, and no false witness to sell in the square.

### What they refuse (the job's edge)

They will not pretend to be clergy. That means no colour, no book-stamp, no door-walk, and no extra place at a table that is not theirs. They will not rewrite a [[Bound]]'s Terms. They may hold a Bound's *rent*, and that hold is money. They will not steal a scion, a living cutting of wood, and they will not sell *I watched you Turn.* Those belong to other trades.

They prefer not to push someone over the scary edge. A corpse, a public Cry, or a wilt the town can see is a ruined week and a ruined name. Nothing stops a keeper who is losing the book from withholding too far, and a measured week can miss.

They will not run a mission, a cellar-church, or a Kumbaan export. There is almost no Tithe on the isle to own, so there is almost nothing there for a week-keeper to rent. They will not call the week a gift, and they will not call it a cruelty. They call it a week.

## Who may keep a week

**Not by Kind.** Kind is the ancestry a person is born with, and it does not decide this house. A human, a [[Kitsune]], a [[Selkie]], or a [[Tengu]] who has a pot and a book can be of the Slide. A [[Yumboe]] abroad who has started charging for the extra place has committed a different sin, the one [[The Table-Keepers]] argue, and that sin is not the Slide's.

**By practice first.** You are of the Slide when someone comes back next week and you still have the key. There is no apprenticeship for anyone to confirm. A cousin who extended once and then let the person recover was kind. They are not of this house.

**Condition leans, not Condition-gates.** A Condition is what a person became. It leans a keeper toward the week they already know how to furnish, and it does not bar anyone else.

| Act | Often | Why | Also, and welcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| A pot, a strip, a night of dirt | a [[The Taken-In\|Taken-In]] who kept a private lot | they already furnish green | a Kept person, who carries no Tithe of their own, with a window-box |
| A shut room, an hour of voice | a [[Far-Voiced]] with a spare hall | they already know what an outlet costs | a performer who lost the honest stage and kept the door |
| A page that is new enough | a [[Long-Lived]] clerk, a junior at a deathless house | novelty is already their weather | anyone who can still find a rumour the fade has not heard |
| The book of weeks itself | whoever can read a late face | the house is the count, not the crop | a Kept broker, unencumbered, who can move and can carry the book to the next town |

The feared-licensed, people who are feared and still carry a licence, are not barred. A [[The Stilled|Stilled]] who rents stillness-hours is rare and tightly watched. A Bound who holds civic debt is dealing in money, not in a Counterparty's Terms.

## How they live in the three corners (and off the map)

[[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]], which calls them the last pot, is home weather for the pious version. The commons ran out, and a cousin still has a pot. They will say they are tending a gift. The quiet obligation is the week: you are helped, and you are theirs. Subsidy already shades into conscription in the Warm, and this private week is that same shade.

[[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]], which calls the charge week-rent, treats the house as ordinary, posted, almost. A leaf-lot by the season is already the honest market ([[Naming in the Turning]]). The Slide is the lot that will still open after the season-fee failed, at a price that is the next season, and the person who opens it is a landlord, not a preacher.

[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] calls it the late week, because the crown already holds the Tithe and the Slide does not compete with the counted hall on ordinary days. They sell the week the hall is late, the extra green the roll does not cover, and the novelty the archive will not release. They look like a clerk. The crown's injustice is cold and precise, and this house is the leak that injustice cannot file without admitting the channel failed. The roll pretends not to know.

On [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] the Slide has no work, because the isle has no Tree and almost nobody on it carries a Given Tithe. A wreck walked uphill is a guest, not a debtor.

## The week that will not end (the house's injustice)

Whoever furnishes a provided-for Tithe holds power over the person who bears it. That is already the sharpest lever in [[Economy and the Tithe]]. The Slide is that lever with a face, and with a patience the official hall is not allowed.

The town is glad of the arrangement. A person who is *quietly* almost-falling is not a Cry in the market. The Warm can say the gift is still being tended. The Watchful can say the channel held. The quay can say the owing was marked and someone took them in.

The Motherfaith says she gave the need. The book of weeks is people's work, and nobody in a town expects the Tree to make the week fair.

The house has no queue of scions, no refinancing of a Bound's clauses, and no authority except the one the Tithe gives its keepers, which is one key and a measured week. The Road-hands already walk, and an attic-host already seats. Off-book brokerage of a Bound's clauses is [[The Holding Desk]]. Sold vouching, a witness who will say they were there, is [[The Standing Trade]]. They are not this house.

## Economy of the house

The week is the living. It is paid in coin, in labor, in a favor, or in a child sent to carry water, and the book counts weeks first.

They are not a charity-order, and they are not Tithe-infrastructure. A town greenhouse, a counted ael-hall, and an endowed long-house of books are [[Tithe-Infrastructure]], the official furnishing. A week-keeper may sit next to all three and take the overflow.

A junior at a long-house who rations novelty is how ossification feels at street level: not a century loan, a page at a time. The house does not *run* the bank.

They do not collect for a hall across the water. There isn't one.

## In-world text

*A week-slip, Lestrand leaf-lot, pinned inside a door that still opens after the season-fee has failed. The verso is the same slip, last week, the date scratched once.*

> *One week of the lot. Extended. Do not let the pot dry. If you cannot come, send a child. The week is the same week. We do not call this a gift. We do not call it a debt. Come before the leaves turn. If you have already turned, come anyway.*

The slip is a landlord's, practical and almost kind. The product is *not falling*, and the slip never calls the week a debt, which is how the book stays a book of weeks and never closes. "If you have already turned, come anyway" is the house's whole case: becoming did not save anyone from needing them. The child who carries water is learning the trade.

## GM Notes

**What this is.** This is the lived face of illicit Tithe-supply from [[Economy and the Tithe]]. Official Tithe-infrastructure is [[Tithe-Infrastructure]]. Do not clone this house as those guilds.

**Canon.** This is the mainland shadow house, chosen from [[Rogue House Options]]. The Bought Watch and the Quiet Cut stay unused. Tithe-need is the leverage. They recruit by practice, by desperation, and by Condition-lean, not by Kind. About 5% frightening: an ordinary neighbour with a real obstacle. They do not rewrite Bound Terms, steal scions, or sell a false Turning-witness. Those trades have other faces: [[The Holding Desk]], unused wood-theft, and [[The Standing Trade]].

**They do not know what story they are in.** Avoid the mustache-twirling slumlord, the Tithe-cult, and the devil-church, and keep the form as landlord and creditor. They are in a rent-and-credit story, they want continuity of the week rather than a victim they relish, and a corpse is a ruined asset. Play them as patient, useful, and sure they helped.

**Moral parallax.** The distances are causal, the sentence "I kept them from the Cry," and informational, the town being glad not to see. The engine is exchange, every week of green paid later by the same person, and the comfort mechanism is the word *extended*, which is how the town stays complicit. Do not make the week-keeper the only villain.

**Do not clone them as the official guilds.** Official Tithe-infrastructure and the licence-guilds are the public, posted, counted version of furnishing. This house is the overflow those guilds pretend not to know. The underdog among the Tithe houses is [[The Intake]], a shed rather than a week. Road-hands already walk.

**Do not absorb unused trades.** There is no false Turning-witness here, because that is [[The Standing Trade]], no stolen scion, and no rewrite of a Bound's clauses, because that rewrite is [[The Holding Desk]]. Do not reopen the Back Table under a new sign, and do not let the house take the Bought Watch or the Quiet Cut because it is convenient.

**Do not add.** There is no Kind-guild of landlords, a Stilled is not the face of the house, and the Slide is not Orledd's collection desk. There is no Kind-gate, no Kumbaan export, and no offices, and there is no Slide-boss unless a campaign is that city. A person who recovered was let go, and is no longer of the house.

**The official gate.** The old "pretend not to know" is no longer a lock. A public wilt or Cry caused by an official gate that closed on purpose is the worst case, and it can now meet the Slide at the table.

**Opposition can act.** They still do not want a corpse. A desperate keeper can squeeze. The preference against a public hazard is not a lock, and a miss is playable.

**Taste still open.** The cant name *Vaethledd* is open. Swap it or drop it, but not for *Orledd* and not for *Nethledd*. Everyday *the Slide* and *week-keepers* are settled.

**Hooks.** A week-slip the same date twice; a child who carries water and is learning; a Book-hand's *owing* that walks downstairs; a counted hall that is late on purpose; a Taken-In whose only dirt is a private lot; a junior at a long-house rationing pages; a Vaethorn cousin who still says *gift*; a Cry that almost happened and was *handled*; a PC who is already on the book.
