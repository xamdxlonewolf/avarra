# The Slide

The Slide is the illegal supply of Tithe needs that [[Economy and the Tithe]] describes. It is not a fifth faith, not clergy, and not a copy of [[The Book-Hands]]. They do not help a person *pay* a Tithe. They step in when a person starts to fail, and they make the failing last.

*(Everyday: **the Slide**, also **week-keepers.** Cant: **the Vaethledd** — VAETH-ledd — [[The Old Tongue|vaeth]] "the waiting" + [[The Old Tongue|ledd]] "to reckon": the waiting, reckoned. The Waiting Lands say **the last pot**; the Ledger Coast says **week-rent**. The Tallied Crown files nothing; the roll already has a counted hall.)*

**In one sentence:** they rent you *not falling* — one week of green, one outlet, one new page — and they are never in a hurry to let you get free.

## At the table

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | Continuity of the week. A person who is always almost whole. |
| **Have** | A key, a pot or a room or a page, a book of weeks. |
| **Fear** | A public wilt, a Cry in the market, a fade a neighbour can name — and an official door that closes on purpose so the week becomes the only door. |
| **Live conflict** | [[Tithe-Infrastructure]] closes. The week opens. Videm will say the channel held. A wilt two streets off is already theirs. |
| **Hooks** | A week-slip the same date twice; a child who carries water and is learning; a counted hall late on purpose; a PC already on the book. |

## Shape of the house (at a glance)

- **A practice, not a gang.** There is no seat, no colour, no roster, no cellar liturgy. You are of the Slide when someone comes back next week. Reputation. When they die, the book of weeks goes to whoever still has a pot, a room, or a key.
- **One job.** Furnish *just enough* Tithe-supply that the person does not become a public hazard, and *never enough* that they recover. The product is the week. The price is remaining theirs.
- **They think they are in a rent story.** They do not call themselves monsters. They extend. They keep a book. They will tell you they kept someone whole. That is what makes them neighbours: a kind face, a real key, and a measured withhold later.
- **Not clergy and not the official doors.** They do not stamp books, Speak colours, walk old doors, or open a hill-hall. They do not run the town greenhouse, the counted ael-hall, or a licence-guild. Those wait. On ordinary days the guilds pretend not to know these people. When an official gate closes on purpose, the pretence is over, because the guild and the Slide are then serving the same street.

## What they actually do

### If you meet one

They look like a landlord. A cousin who still has a pot after the commons ran dry. A junior clerk at a deathless house who will extend a novelty subscription one more week. A person with a spare room and a voice who will let you cry there, for a price that is never quite coin.

You would not call them a priest. You would find them if you asked *who still has green when the leaf-lot is closed.*

They are useful the way a week is useful. If you came for a stamp, a colour, or a false childhood, you have the wrong house.

### Who comes, and when

### An ordinary week (the job you can miss)

They **extend first.** That is the hook, and it is also what they believe about themselves. A person arrives already late. The week-keeper does not withhold on the first night. They open the lot, the room, the page. They write *extended*. They name a day.

The withhold comes later, and it is measured. Never enough to make a public hazard — a wilt in the square, a Cry in the market, a fade a neighbour can name — because that brings the watch, the guild, the counted hall, and the book dies. Never enough to let the person stand up and walk to a commons that would keep them. The asset is a person who is always almost whole.

They keep a **book of weeks**, not a ledger of coin. Coin is how some weeks are paid. The thing they are counting is *how long you have not fallen, on their floor.*

A child of the house learns the work by carrying water to the pots and by being told not to let a guest sleep a second week for nothing. There is no Seat to confirm it.

### What they carry

A key. A pot, a spare room, a stack of pages that are new enough. A book of weeks. No stamp. No colour-scrap. No Cutting-leave. No false witness to sell in the square.

### What they refuse (the job's edge)

- They will not pretend to be clergy. No colour, no book-stamp, no door-walk, no extra place.
- They will not rewrite a Bound's Terms. They may hold a Bound's *rent*. That is money.
- They will not steal a scion or sell *I watched you Turn.* Those belong to other trades.
- They prefer not to push someone over the scary edge. A corpse, a public Cry, a wilt the town can see — that is a ruined week and a ruined name. Nothing stops a keeper who is losing the book from withholding too far, and a measured week can miss.
- They will not run a mission, a cellar-church, or a Kumbaan export. There is almost no Tithe on the isle to own.
- They will not call the week a gift. They will also not call it a cruelty. They call it a week.

## Who may keep a week

**Not by Kind.** A human, a [[Kitsune]], a [[Selkie]], a [[Tengu]] who has a pot and a book can be of the Slide. A Yumboe abroad who has started charging for the extra place has committed a different sin, one that belongs to [[The Table-Keepers]]' argument and not to the Slide.

**By practice first.** You are of the Slide when someone comes back next week, and you still have the key. There is no apprenticeship confirmed by anyone. A cousin who extended once and then let the person recover was kind. They are not of this house.

**Condition leans, not Condition-gates.**

| Act | Often | Why | Also, and welcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| A pot, a strip, a night of dirt | a [[The Taken-In\|Taken-In]] who kept a private lot | they already furnish green | a Kept with a window-box and no Tithe of their own |
| A shut room, an hour of voice | a [[Far-Voiced]] with a spare hall | they already know what an outlet costs | a performer who lost the honest stage and kept the door |
| A page that is new enough | a [[Long-Lived]] clerk, a junior at a deathless house | novelty is already their weather | anyone who can still find a rumour the fade has not heard |
| The book of weeks itself | whoever can read a late face | the house is the count, not the crop | a Kept broker — unencumbered, can move, can carry the book to the next town |

The feared-licensed are not barred. A [[The Stilled\|Stilled]] who rents stillness-hours is rare and tightly watched. A Bound who holds civic debt is dealing in money, not in a Counterparty's Terms.

## How they live in the three corners (and off the map)

- **[[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] (the last pot).** Home weather for the pious version. The commons ran out. A cousin still has a pot. They will say they are tending a gift. The quiet obligation is the week: you are helped, and you are theirs. Subsidy already shades into conscription in the Warm; this is that shade, private.
- **[[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] (week-rent).** Ordinary. Posted, almost. A leaf-lot by the season is already the honest market ([[Naming in the Turning]]). The Slide is the lot that will still open after the season-fee failed, at a price that is the next season. No sermon. A landlord.
- **[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] (the late week).** The crown already holds Tithe. The Slide does not compete with the counted hall on ordinary days. They sell the week the hall is late, the extra green the roll does not cover, the novelty the archive will not release. They look like a clerk. The crown's injustice is cold and precise; this is the leak it cannot file without admitting the channel failed. The roll pretends not to know.
- **[[The Sundering Isle\|Kumbaan]].** The Slide has no work there, because the isle has no Tree and almost nobody on it carries a Given Tithe. A wreck walked uphill is a guest, not a debtor.

## The week that will not end (the house's injustice)

Whoever furnishes a provided-for Tithe holds power over its bearer. That is already the sharpest lever in [[Economy and the Tithe]]. The Slide is that lever with a face, and with a patience the official hall is not allowed.

The town is glad. A person who is *quietly* almost-falling is not a Cry in the market. The Warm can say the gift is still being tended. The Watchful can say the channel held. The quay can say the owing was marked and someone took them in.

The Motherfaith says she gave the need. The book of weeks is people's work, and nobody in a town expects the Tree to make the week fair.

The house has no queue of scions, no refinancing, and no authority except the one the Tithe gives its keepers: one key and a measured week. The Road-hands already walk, and an attic-host already seats.

Off-book Bound brokerage is [[The Holding Desk]]. Sold vouching is [[The Standing Trade]]. They are not this house.

## Economy of the house

- **The week is the living.** Paid in coin, in labor, in a favor, in a child sent to carry water. The book counts weeks first.
- **They are not a charity-order and not Tithe-infrastructure.** A town greenhouse, a counted ael-hall, an endowed long-house of books — those are [[Tithe-Infrastructure]]. A week-keeper may sit next to all three and take the overflow.
- **Deathless proximity.** A junior at a long-house who rations novelty is how ossification feels at street level: not a century loan, a page at a time. The house does not *run* the bank.
- **No collection for a hall across the water.** There isn't one.

## In-world text

*A week-slip, Lestrand leaf-lot, pinned inside a door that still opens after the season-fee has failed. The verso is the same slip, last week, the date scratched once.*

> *One week of the lot. Extended. Do not let the pot dry. If you cannot come, send a child. The week is the same week. We do not call this a gift. We do not call it a debt. Come before the leaves turn. If you have already turned, come anyway.*

The slip is a landlord's, practical and almost kind. The product is *not falling*, and the slip never calls the week a debt, which is how the book stays a book of weeks and never closes. "If you have already turned, come anyway" is the house's whole case: becoming did not save anyone from needing them. The child who carries water is learning the trade.

## GM Notes

- **What this is.** The lived face of illicit Tithe-supply from [[Economy and the Tithe]]. Official Tithe-infrastructure is [[Tithe-Infrastructure]]; do not clone this house as those guilds.
- **Canon.** This is the mainland shadow house, chosen from [[Rogue House Options]] (the Bought Watch and the Quiet Cut stay unused). Tithe-need is the leverage. They recruit by practice, desperation, and Condition-lean, not Kind. About 5% frightening: an ordinary neighbour with a real obstacle. They do not rewrite Bound Terms, steal scions, or sell a false Turning-witness. Those trades have other faces: [[The Holding Desk]], unused wood-theft, [[The Standing Trade]].
- **They do not know what story they are in.** Avoid the mustache-twirling slumlord, the Tithe-cult, and the devil-church. Form stays landlord/creditor. They are in a rent-and-credit story. They want *continuity of the week*, not a victim they relish. A corpse is a ruined asset. Play them as patient, useful, and sure they helped.
- **Moral parallax.** Distances: causal ("I kept them from the Cry") and informational (the town is glad not to see). Engine: exchange — every week of green is paid later by the same person. Comfort mechanism: the word *extended*. The town is complicit; do not make the week-keeper the only villain.
- **Do not clone them as the official guilds.** Official Tithe-infrastructure and the licence-guilds are the public, posted, counted version of *furnishing*. This house is the overflow those guilds pretend not to know. The underdog among the Tithe houses is [[The Intake]] (a shed, not a week). Road-hands already walk.
- **Do not absorb unused trades.** No false Turning-witness (that is [[The Standing Trade]]). No stolen scion. No Bound-clause rewrite (that is [[The Holding Desk]]). Do not reopen the Back Table under a new sign. Do not let the house take the Bought Watch or the Quiet Cut because it is convenient.
- **Do not add.** No Kind-guild of landlords. Do not make a Stilled the face of the house. Do not turn the Slide into Orledd's collection desk. No Kind-gate, no Kumbaan export, no offices. Do not give the house a Slide-boss unless a campaign is that city. A person who recovered was let go and is no longer of the house.
- **The official gate.** The old "pretend not to know" is no longer a lock. A public wilt or Cry caused by an official gate that closed on purpose is the worst case, and it can now meet the Slide at the table.
- **Opposition can act.** They still do not *want* a corpse. A desperate keeper can squeeze. The preference against a public hazard is not a lock; a miss is playable.
- **Taste still open.** The cant name *Vaethledd* is open: swap or drop it, but not *Orledd* and not *Nethledd*. Everyday *the Slide* and *week-keepers* are settled.
- **Hooks.** A week-slip the same date twice; a child who carries water and is learning; a Book-hand's *owing* that walks downstairs; a counted hall that is late on purpose; a Taken-In whose only dirt is a private lot; a junior at a long-house rationing pages; a Vaethorn cousin who still says *gift*; a Cry that almost happened and was *handled*; a PC who is already on the book.
