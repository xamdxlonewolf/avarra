# The Standing Trade

Citizenship in this world is [[Law and Citizenship|witnessed, not recorded]]. What travels ahead of you, when the town that watched you is far behind, is a **witness**: a person who will say they watched you Turn, which is the standing under a Tree where a child becomes what they become, or where a later threshold takes someone. The unvouched, people no living circle will stand for, will buy one.

Everyday speech calls the sale **the Standing Trade**. A buyer says **a standing**. A seller says **I was there**, and means they will be, later, in a square that was not theirs. [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]], the Ledger Coast, prices it. [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]], the Waiting Lands, calls it **false kin** when a Hands-folk hearth wants a sin. [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]], the Tallied Crown, does not need it on ordinary days, because the roll already stands, and wants it badly when a name is missing from that roll.

They are not [[The Slide]], and they do not rent a week of green or an hour or a page. They are not [[The Holding Desk]], and they do not write a Term, a clause on a Bound's contract. The product is a person who will stand and say they watched.

## If you meet one

They look like a useful cousin at a ford, or a factor's runner, or a pilgrim who happened to be in the square that Eolthael, the season when a town Turns its children. You would miss them if you came looking for a forger's press, because there is no press, only a person who will say they were there.

**Nomele of the Hinge** *(no-MEL-eh)* works the Old Crossing face, [[The Hinge Shore]] more often than [[Orentel]], because the Old Crossing has two shores and a standing has to travel between them. Nomele speaks the worn-down speech of the quay, in the conservative register. They do not sell paper. They sell who will walk with you to the next Tree.

## What they actually do

A person who Changed unwitnessed, or who left the town that watched them, or who was never there at ten, the year a child stands to be Turned, needs someone to **stand**. The three pillars already do this honestly: a [[Long-Lived]] carries the memory, a [[Bound]] binds the word so it holds, and a [[Far-Voiced]] carries a feeling that will not fake ([[Law and Citizenship]]). The trade sells a *corrupt* version of the same three.

| Kind of witness | What the buyer gets | Why it is catastrophic when it fails |
|---|---|---|
| A [[Long-Lived]] who will remember you | A childhood that will not fade from the witness | Blood-Read will give a true fragment. If the fragment is the *sale*, the standing dies, and so does every standing that witness has sold. |
| A [[Bound]] who will bind the word | A sentence that holds like a Term, as if it were a clause of a contract | The Counterparty, the other side of that Bound's own contract, did not ask for this. A bound lie is still a lie the town can use, until the next signing eats the witness. |
| A [[Far-Voiced]] who will make the feeling stick | The square *feels* that this person belongs | A Cry, that voice's feeling breaking loose, in the same square can splash the false feeling onto everyone in earshot. The watch then has a riot and a fraud. |

Honest vouching is priceless because it cannot be faked cheaply. That is why the corrupt one costs a year, a child sent to carry water, a hold on a Tithe, the upkeep a person owes for what they have become, or a name on someone else's book.

They will not sell a colour, the leaf a Tree gives a child, and they will not Speak one, which is a warden's naming. They will not write you onto the Threnhael, the Tallied Crown's roll. That is a registrar's work, and Menirein would rather list you than buy you ([[The Reckoned Offices]]).

## What they refuse

They will not stamp a civic year, the honest year's account, because [[The Book-Hands]] do that with a real table. They will not rewrite Terms, and they will not walk a Bound toward the Forfeit, the last signing the guilds name, because that walk belongs to [[The Holding Desk]]. They will not rent green or an hour, because that rent is [[The Slide]], and they will not steal a scion, a living cutting of wood. They will not stand on [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]], because the isle does not vouch by Tree.

A recant ruins the book. They still sell standings that will recant if the price is higher on the other side.

## How they reach a party

Rootless people are the customers the law already named. A party that cannot point at a town that watched them is the trade's ordinary hour. Nomele will offer a standing the way a ferryman offers a boat. The party can pay, refuse, or become the witness.

A town that already knows the child will smell the sale. A neighbour who *was* there still stands for free.

## In-world text

*A fee-slate, Old Crossing hinge, no childhood written on it. The verso is a list of squares, not names.*

> *I will stand at the next Tree that asks. I will not say which summer. I will not say which child. I will say I was there. If you have a Long-Lived who was actually there, do not hire me — I will not win that room. If you have no one, come before the watch sets the question. We do not sell a colour. We do not sell a hug. We sell a witness. If the witness recants, the fee does not.*

The writer sounds like a ferryman. "I will not say which child" is the whole fraud: an honest witness names the child, and this one names only the square. The sentences around that fraud are plain about the product. The slate will not sell a colour or a hug, the two things a Tree does when a child stands, because what it sells is a witness.

## GM Notes

They think they are in a travel-and-kin story, so avoid the stock forger, the fake paper, and the document-cult: there is no paper worth selling, and the product is a witness. What Nomele knows, what they want, and the role they fill all point the same way, which is that they want the crossing to work, not a setting to collapse.

The moral parallax sits at two distances. The social one is the sentence "they're not from here." The informational one is that the square is glad to have a witness. The engine is exchange: every standing is paid by someone the town will later trust. The comfort is the sentence *I was there.*

Opposition can act. A recant, a Blood-Read, or a sold standing used to hang a rival will do it. They will ruin a person to keep the book. They prefer not to, because a ruined witness cannot stand next week.

This trade is not the unused Bought Watch. That name stayed unused as a *Slide pick*, and this note is the later face for sold vouching, so do not rename the Slide, do not fold a false Turning into the Slide's book of weeks, and do not merge this trade with the Holding Desk. Do not make every gate a shakedown. Let a Settler who finds an unstamped standing have their case, leave the unused cut that steals a scion unused, and remember that a preference not to ruin people is not a lock.

Nomele of the Hinge comes from seed `20260831`, off the eroded list, mid-bottom. Not a median "Silas Crowe."

Seed, and do not plot, a witness who wants to recant and cannot afford the people who already used them.

The hooks already on the table are the fee-slate with squares and not names, a Blood-Read that shows the sale, three unvouched after one recant, a party hired to stand, and the Hinge Shore as the ordinary door.
