# The Standing Trade

Citizenship is [[Law and Citizenship|witnessed, not recorded]]. Proof at a distance is a **witness**, a person who will say they watched you. The unvouched will buy one.

Everyday: **the Standing Trade**. A buyer says **a standing**. A seller says **I was there** and means they will be, later, in a square that was not theirs. [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] prices it. [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] calls it **false kin** when a Hands-folk hearth wants a sin. [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] does not need it on ordinary days — the roll already stands — and wants it badly when a name is missing.

They are not [[The Slide]]. They do not rent a week. They are not [[The Holding Desk]]. They do not write a Term. The product is a person who will stand and say they watched.

|---|---|
| **Want** | A buyer who will not recant, and a witness who will not be Blood-Read in the wrong room. A standing that holds up over the years, not a forged paper. |
| **Have** | People who travel: a Far-Voiced who can make a feeling stick, a Bound who will bind the word, a Long-Lived who will remember a childhood that was not theirs. |
| **Fear** | An honest Long-Lived asked to Blood-Read the seller. A town that already has three living witnesses who were actually there. A recant in front of a watch. |

| **Hooks** | Nomele's fee-slate with no childhood on it; a Long-Lived who sold a memory and now cannot keep the real one separate; a party asked to stand for someone they did not watch; a recant that makes three people unvouched at once. |

## If you meet one

They look like a useful cousin at a ford. A factor's runner. A pilgrim who "happened to be in the square that Eolthael." You would miss them if you came looking for a forger's press. There is no press. There is a person who will say they were there.

**Nomele of the Hinge** *(no-MEL-eh)* works the Old Crossing face — [[The Hinge Shore]] more often than [[Orentel]], because the Old Crossing has two shores and a standing has to travel between them. Nomele speaks the worn-down speech of the quay, in the conservative register. They do not sell paper. They sell who will walk with you to the next Tree.

## What they actually do

A person who Changed unwitnessed, or who left the town that watched them, or who was never there at ten, needs someone to **stand**. The three pillars already do this honestly: Long-Lived memory, Bound binding-word, Far-Voiced unfakeable feeling ([[Law and Citizenship]]). The trade sells a *corrupt* version of the same three.

| Kind of witness | What the buyer gets | Why it is catastrophic when it fails |
|---|---|---|
| A [[Long-Lived]] who will remember you | A childhood that will not fade from the witness | Blood-Read will give a true fragment. If the fragment is the *sale*, the standing dies, and so does every standing that witness has sold. |
| A [[Bound]] who will bind the word | A sentence that holds like a Term | The Counterparty did not ask for this. A bound lie is still a lie the town can use, until the next signing eats the witness. |
| A [[Far-Voiced]] who will make the feeling stick | The square *feels* that this person belongs | A Cry in the same square can splash the false feeling onto everyone in earshot. The watch then has a riot and a fraud. |

Honest vouching is priceless because it cannot be faked cheaply. That is why the corrupt one costs a year, a child sent to carry water, a hold on a Tithe, or a name on someone else's book.

They will not sell a colour. They will not Speak. They will not write you onto the Threnhael — that is a registrar, and Menirein would rather list you than buy you ([[The Reckoned Offices]]).

## What they refuse

- They will not stamp a civic year. [[The Book-Hands]] do that with a real table.
- They will not rewrite Terms or walk a Bound toward the Forfeit. [[The Holding Desk]].
- They will not rent green or an hour. [[The Slide]].
- They will not steal a scion.
- They will not stand on [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]]. The isle does not vouch by Tree.

A recant ruins the book. They still sell standings that will recant if the price is higher on the other side.

## How they reach a party

Rootless people are the customers the law already named. A party that cannot point at a town that watched them is the trade's ordinary hour. Nomele will offer a standing the way a ferryman offers a boat. The party can pay, refuse, or become the witness.

A town that already knows the child will smell the sale. A neighbour who *was* there still stands for free.

## In-world text

*A fee-slate, Old Crossing hinge, no childhood written on it. The verso is a list of squares, not names.*

> *I will stand at the next Tree that asks. I will not say which summer. I will not say which child. I will say I was there. If you have a Long-Lived who was actually there, do not hire me — I will not win that room. If you have no one, come before the watch sets the question. We do not sell a colour. We do not sell a hug. We sell a witness. If the witness recants, the fee does not.*

The writer sounds like a ferryman. "I will not say which child" is the whole fraud: an honest witness names the child, and this one names only the square.

## GM Notes

- **They think they are in a travel-and-kin story.** Avoid the stock forgers, fake papers, and document-cult. There is no paper worth selling. The product is a witness. Knowledge/goal/role: Nomele wants the crossing to work, not a setting to collapse.
- **Moral parallax.** Distances: social ("they're not from here") and informational (the square is glad to have a witness). Engine: exchange — every standing is paid by someone the town will later trust. Comfort: the sentence *I was there.*
- **Opposition can act.** A recant, a Blood-Read, a sold standing used to hang a rival. They will ruin a person to keep the book. They prefer not to, because a ruined witness cannot stand next week.
- **Not the unused Bought Watch.** That name stayed unused as a *Slide pick*. This is the later face for sold vouching. Do not rename the Slide. Do not fold a false Turning into the Slide's book of weeks. Do not merge this trade with the Holding Desk. Do not make every gate a shakedown. Let a Settler who finds an unstamped standing have their case. The unused cut that steals a scion stays unused. A preference not to ruin people is not a lock.
- **Names.** Nomele of the Hinge — seed `20260831`, eroded list, mid-bottom. Not a median "Silas Crowe."
- **Seed, do not plot:** a witness who wants to recant and cannot afford the people who already used them.
- **Hooks.** The fee-slate with squares not names; a Blood-Read that shows the sale; three unvouched after one recant; a party hired to stand; the Hinge Shore as the ordinary door.
