# Movements

Three arguments were already in the world. Each one grew people who acted on it. One of those movements is past. Two are present. The pear-graft stayed a refusal to ride. The withdrawers stayed an argument about stopping. The disinherited stayed the people a colour takes off an heir-roll.

## The pear-graft (past)

During [[The Closing]], people of [[The Watching]] on the Near Mile grafted pears and would not start a war-year. Masked Watchers, who will say the Mother's blessing and add a withheld line, and Bare Tree Watchers, who will not say *she* at all, both held to that refusal. They went on arguing what the Giving cost. They did not ride with the Closed Seat, and they did not raise a host against it.

The riders went home in Cut-year 38. The refusal went with that war. People still point at an old pear beside a Hand and say the Watching would not ride. They do not call anyone out for it. A Watcher now does the work on [[The Watchers]]: a second reading beside a warden, a night sitting, a line withheld. That house is not a remainder of a company.

The question they still will not close, what was spent when the Giving came, stays in the faith, where the Watchers already argue it.

## The withdrawers (present)

[[The Pourers]] already argue about stopping. Some will give the next year. Some have asked to stop. The withdrawers are the people who asked.

They are [[Long-Lived]]. They have already given one or more years, a year at a time, into a cup the circle keeps. They are not people who turned down the invitation; a Long-Lived who never joined is still received everywhere, and they do not all want to stop for the same reason. They want the giving to end, or they want a thinner copy of their years back, and the circle has told them the god would weaken if they took it. The years already given stay in the cup either way. Only the papers come back. A long-house that keeps the circle will not seat them afterward. They are not a second circle. They do not pour anyone else's years. They do not stand the cup in a square. They do not say Mother over it.

[[Reimaethe]] is one of them, in a rise-loft in [[Orentel]], four years already given, wanting out before the fifth. The fifth year is still ahead of them. Neighbours still call the loft a hobby. The long-house calls it a person they will not seat.

When a circle meets, a withdrawer stands in the passage and does not go in. They will not say why they stand there instead of going home.

[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] wants minutes it can colour. The withdrawers do not keep minutes. The circle that refused the stop still has the book.

## The disinherited (present)

[[The Walled Book]] strikes a Closed heir off the heir-roll when the fall is a colour. A hug would have kept the name. The colour takes it off. The person is still a person. The Book already has turns of work written for what they have become. That rule is what makes the Closed houses a class. It is older than the Retreat, and the war made it a walled city.

The disinherited are Given people that rule made, who will not let the next town copy the Book's turns of work onto a slate. They eat where a hearth will have them. They do not ask to be let into the Close. They do not preach that the Kept should lose the Book. The Book's sentence stays in the Book. [[Orenbren]] may lodge them, because lodging is the compact outside the wall. They do not carry Orenbren's lodging as a banner. A town that writes the Book's turns of work onto its own slate has done what the Book already does. The disinherited do not keep a counter-book.

[[Hithaen]] is one of them, eating in [[Eolvaeth]]'s gift-hall, with a letter from behind the wall still unopened. They are not a leader of the others, and they are not Talnin, whose tenth year is still unfinished on the heir-roll ([[The Walled Book]]).

When two of them meet, they do not trade the old house-name. They will not say why.

## GM Notes

A later note must not explain the standing in the passage, or the refusal to trade the old house-name. It must not turn either habit into a power, a relic, or a rite.

The withdrawers want to stop for different reasons. Some found out about the inner circle's taking ([[The Pourers#The taking]]). Some helped with a taking without knowing what the inner circle was doing. Some believed in the practice until they saw what it did to someone. Do not give them one shared reason in player text.

The pear-graft stays past. It does not return as an army, a third branch of the Watching, or an answer to what the Giving cost. The withdrawers stay the stop side of a schism the Pourers already have. They do not get a new god, a second cup, or a finished plot for Reimaethe. The disinherited stay people the Book's rule made. They do not become a liberation front, a sixteenth power, or a rewrite of the heir-roll. Hithaen is not Talnin. The cutter stays unpicked. The pear-graft does not need a name for the knife.
