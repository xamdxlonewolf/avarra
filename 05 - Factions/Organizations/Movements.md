# Movements

Three arguments were already in the world. Each one grew people who acted on it. One of those movements is past. Two are present. The pear-graft stayed a refusal to ride. The withdrawers stayed an argument about stopping. The disinherited stayed the people a colour takes off an heir-roll.

## The pear-graft (past)

During [[The Closing]], people of [[The Watching]] on the Near Mile grafted pears and would not start a war-year. Masked Watchers, who will say the Mother's blessing and add a withheld line, and Bare Tree Watchers, who will not say *she* at all, both sat that refusal. They went on arguing what the Giving cost. They did not ride with the Closed Seat, and they did not raise a host against it.

The riders went home in C.Y. 38. The refusal went with that war. People still point at an old pear beside a Hand and say the Watching would not ride. They do not call anyone out for it. A Watcher now does the work on [[The Watchers]]: a second reading beside a warden, a night sitting, a line withheld. That house is not a remainder of a company.

The question they still will not close, what was spent when the Giving came, stays in the faith, where the Watchers already argue it.

## The withdrawers (present)

[[The Pourers]] already argue about stopping. Some will take the next pour. Some have asked to stop. The withdrawers are the people who asked.

They are [[Long-Lived]]. They have already given years into a cup the circle keeps. They want the pour to end, or they want a thinner return, and the circle has told them the cup would thin if they did. A long-house that keeps the circle will not seat them afterward. They are not a second circle. They do not pour anyone else's years. They do not stand the cup in a square. They do not say Mother over it.

[[Reimaethe]] is one of them, in a rise-loft in [[Orentel]], four years already poured, wanting out before the fifth. The fifth year is still ahead of them. Neighbours still call the loft a hobby. The long-house calls it a person they will not seat.

When a circle meets, a withdrawer stands in the passage and does not go in. They will not say why they stand there instead of going home.

[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] wants minutes it can colour. The withdrawers do not keep minutes. The circle that refused the stop still has the book.

## The disinherited (present)

[[The Walled Book]] strikes a Closed heir off the heir-roll when the fall is a colour. A hug would have kept the name. The colour takes it off. The person is still a person. The Book already has a labour-line for what they have become. That rule is the class. It is older than the Retreat, and the war made it a wall.

The disinherited are Given people that rule made, who will not let the next town copy the labour-line onto a slate. They eat where a hearth will have them. They do not ask to sit the Close. They do not preach that the Kept should lose the Book. The Book's sentence stays in the Book. [[Orenbren]] may lodge them, because lodging is the compact outside the wall. Lodging is not their flag. A town that writes the labour-line anyway has done what the Book already does. The disinherited do not keep a counter-book.

[[Hithaen]] is one of them, eating in [[Eolvaeth]]'s gift-hall, with a letter from behind the wall still unopened. They are not a leader of the others, and they are not Talnin, whose tenth year is still unfinished on the heir-roll ([[The Walled Book]]).

When two of them meet, they do not trade the old house-name. They will not say why.

## GM Notes

A later note must not explain the standing in the passage, or the refusal to trade the old house-name. It must not turn either habit into a power, a relic, or a rite.

The pear-graft stays past. It does not return as an army, a third branch of the Watching, or an answer to what the Giving cost. The withdrawers stay the stop side of a schism the Pourers already have. They do not get a new god, a second cup, or a finished plot for Reimaethe. The disinherited stay people the Book's rule made. They do not become a liberation front, a sixteenth power, or a rewrite of the heir-roll. Hithaen is not Talnin. The cutter stays unpicked. The pear-graft does not need a name for the knife.
