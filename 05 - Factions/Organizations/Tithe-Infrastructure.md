# Tithe-Infrastructure

Everyday speech is **the furnishing**, or **the greens and the halls**, and there is no liturgical umbrella over the sector. [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] says **the gift-works**, [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] says **the provision**, and [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] files **the counted channel**.

They keep people whole *enough*, in public, and they stop at the gate. A Tithe is the upkeep a Condition demands, the green or the hour or the new page a person has to have if the change is going to stay livable, and this sector is the civic way of meeting that upkeep until the share runs out.

[[The Greens-Keepers]] and [[The Hall-Keepers]] are parts of this sector, not separate houses with their own colleges. The ticket-yards, [[The Stillers]] and [[The Element-Guilds]], remain craft-guilds. [[The Intake]] remains the shed that does not face the street.

## Hours and gates

Official means **enough**, not a week. What the gate gives is a commons-share, a scheduled hour, or a library that is open. The product is a person who can walk away until tomorrow. [[The Slide]]'s product is a person who cannot. The town is glad of both, and the town now has to say so wherever an official gate and the Slide serve the same street.

**Furnishing** and **ticketing** are two jobs. A town may house both under one roof. They are still two jobs, the share of upkeep on one stool and the licence for a hazardous act on the other.

**The three tickets** are for danger to others only, and the craft issues them. A ticket names a **competence**, the practiced skill, and not a Condition, the change itself. The ticketable pool is large. Any person whose Condition can hurt others could be asked for a ticket if they practice. The roll of tickets actually issued is much smaller. There are three guilds, and no shared list. The fuller math is [[Law and Citizenship#The safety-licence — how hazard is regulated (and by whom)]].

[[The Intake]] is the shared shed of the three ticket-yards. It catches a raw Struck person, someone newly in the change and not yet trained, before anything happens in public, and its work has succeeded when nothing is heard of it afterwards.

There is no Furnishing Seat, no college set over the other towns. A town keeps its own lot and its own hall. A deathless house, a house of people who do not die of age, endows its own stacks. Nothing in this sector micromanages a far commons.

## The lot (the green)

The keepers open the gate, and a [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] person, someone the wild has taken partway into itself and who wilts without living ground, lies down on that ground until the tokens fill, the count of the upkeep coming back up, and then leaves. After that the bed is turned, and last year's commons-leaf is buried under a new bed. No one will explain this to a guest. A first-year who asks is told *that is how the lot remembers.*

When the queue is longer than the wilt, they still open for the share they have, and they close when the share is gone. They know a cousin still has a pot, and they do not say the cousin's name.

A bed-slate is plants. In Threnmaieth the crown already has another list, a list of persons. Videm, who holds the counted channel, wants the beds to match that channel. The lot will not invent a bed to close someone else's book.

## The hall (outlet and Voice-ticket)

Morning is the slate of hours, and evening is the house, feeling in earnest, on purpose, where a neighbour paid to hear it, so that a Far-Voiced Cry happens on the boards and not in the market. One seat is never swept, and the line people use for it is *That's the first Cry's seat.* No one will explain which Cry.

When a voice misses its hour, the keeper does not open the side door. They know a room that still shuts, and they do not say the room's name.

A Given sea-blue child, a Far-Voiced child the leaf named, is apprenticed here. A raw drowning, a person the voice took later and untrained, is handed to Intake. The show *is* some people's Tithe and other people's night out, the same hour doing both jobs.

## The long-house

A [[Long-Lived]] house that has endowed a library is already this sector. What that house furnishes is *something genuinely new*, and a deathless member of the house, someone who does not die of age, still sits the chair. The [[The Pourers|Pourers]] keep one such shelf, in Lestrand. Staff are often [[Law and Citizenship|Kept]], people the Tree hugged and left without a Condition. What the house will not furnish is a page at a time to a person who cannot walk in. They will not courier a rumour to a fade in a shut room, and a junior who extends a page has started a week.

## How they live in the three corners

- **Vaethorn (gift-works).** The works are commons-minded, and the purse is thin. A cousin's field stands in when the lot is short. The soft injustice is that you were helped, so you will spend the gift as the valley asks. The Slide here is a private pot after the share fails.
- **Lestrand (the provision).** The provision is posted, and it charges season-fees. You can be priced out of staying whole. The Slide is week-rent after the season-fee failed.
- **Threnmaieth (the counted channel).** The crown furnishes and the crown holds. Videm holds the channel. The Slide is the late week the channel will not admit failed.

**[[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]].** The sector has no greenhouse or ael-hall there.

## In-world text

*What follows is a closing-notice, nailed to a lot-gate and, in the same hand, to a hall door. The back of the sheet is last week's notice, not taken down.*

> *Share is enough. Hour is enough. After close we do not walk the rooms. After the lamps we do not open the side door. If you have already wilted, the lot is still the lot — come when we open. If you have already cried in the street, the hall is still the hall — come at open, not now. If you cannot wait, we do not know a pot. We do not know a room. The town is glad. File that as weather if you must. It is a door.*

The keepers do know a cousin's pot and a room that still shuts. They say they do not, so that neither the lot nor the hall is blamed for it. The town is glad of both, the official gate and the week that opens when the gate has shut.

## GM Notes

The bureaucratic voice is the point of how this sector understands itself. The people in it think in forms, in hours, and in the word *enough*, and they think they are in a wells-and-yards story.

Greens and halls were folded into this note. Recovered effort goes to named instruments and to lived shadow faces, not to two more identical slates. Keep the buried leaf and the unswept bench. Do not write matching rules for them, as if the two habits were one rite copied across.

On the Slide, the old instruction to pretend not to know is no longer a lock. The town is still glad. The two sides can now meet at the table. A public wilt or Cry caused by a closed official door is the escalation.

The distances are informational, what the gate will not say, and causal, the closing that makes the week. The engine is accumulation, because every closed gate feeds a week-book. The comfort words are *share*, *hour*, *ticket*, and *enough*.

This sector is not a fifth faith. It has no colour, and no blessing except Vaethorn's courtesy-words over a share. It is not a census. A bed-list is plants, a hall-slate is hours, and a guild-roll is practitioners of a dangerous art. The ticketable pool is not a supervised third of the world. The one kingdom that writes *persons and gifts* on the same paper is still only [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]]. This sector is not the Road-hands, and it is not the four houses' travelling edges.

Do not invent, on this page, a warmth-guild, a Purpose-desk, or wolf-police, and do not let a Road-hand become a greens-cart or export a greenhouse or an ael-hall to Kumbaan. Furnishing and ticketing are two jobs under one roof. The notice's "we do not know" is the one lie the sector tells, and the town's gladness is the other gate.

Do not clone the neighbouring shapes onto this one. Intake is the underdog, the Road-hands already walk, and the four houses keep their own injustices. Long-houses stay deathless patronage, a library endowed by people who do not die of age. There is no Kind-gate, no Kumbaan export, and no Furnishing pope.
