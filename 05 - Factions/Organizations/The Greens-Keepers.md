# The Greens-Keepers

Everyday speech is **greens-keepers**, or **lot-keepers**, and the liturgical name is *Saelhael*. This is a jurisdiction of [[Tithe-Infrastructure]], the civic work of greens and halls, and not a house with a college of its own. The lot, the gate, the bed-slate, and the cousin's pot they do not name live on that note, so this page is the door and that page is the keeping. Why [[The Taken-In]] stay in stone towns is written at [[Economy and the Tithe#Why Taken-In live in stone cities]]. The lot on this page is what keeps those people from wilting on a parlour floor.

They keep the lot, and they do not go looking for anyone who is not at the gate. A Taken-In person who has not come to that gate is not someone this house walks out to find.

## At the table

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | A living lot, because a wilt on the beds is a ruined commons. |
| **Have** | A key, a slate of rows, and last year's leaf buried under a new bed. |
| **Fear** | A queue longer than the wilt, and a crown channel that matches beds they do not have. |
| **Live conflict** | The gate closes while someone three streets off is wilting, and Videm's channel, the crown's count of the green, says the green held. |
| **Hooks** | A slate that says *we do not know a pot*, and a child who does; a Book-hand's share-term on a lot that is already full; a wilt the keepers will not walk to. |

The share they have on hand is enough, and then the gate closes. A private pot after the share fails is [[The Slide]], the week someone buys when the official gate has shut. A bed-slate is a list of plants. A person's name gets on it because that person is *on* a bed, not because the lot has started a roll of people, and in [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] the crown already has another list.

They recruit by practice, by who can keep a bed, and not by Kind, by what a person was born. They do not steal a scion, the living wood a Road-hand carries to plant a Tree. They do not export a lot to [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]].

## GM Notes

This door was folded in as a jurisdiction, not as a second copy of a faction. The buried leaf, and the sentence "we do not know a pot," stay playable on the sector note, where a guest is not told what the leaf is for. Do not grow a Green Seat in a later polish. A PC who digs up the buried leaf has been rude, and has not found how the world works.
