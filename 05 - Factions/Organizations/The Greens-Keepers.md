# The Greens-Keepers

> **What this is.** The lived face of **official green** from [[Economy and the Tithe]] — garden-commons, the greens, a leaf-lot. They furnish *enough* living land that a [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] can rest and walk away. They are not [[The Slide]]. Built with `economic-systems` and `moral-parallax` for [[Roadmap|Epic 5, Story 5.3]].

> **Canon status.** 🟡 **Proposed** (the liturgical name, whether a bed-slate is a plant-list or already too close to a roll). 🔒 **Locked upstream:** green is a provided-for Tithe; cities keep the Taken-In at social arm's length while employing them on the lots; the Slide rents the pot after the share fails. Recruits by practice / office / Condition-lean, **not Kind**.

*(Everyday: **greens-keepers**, or **lot-keepers.** Liturgical, 🟡: **the Saelhael** — SAYL-hayl — [[The Old Tongue|sael]] "sowing; the first green" + [[The Old Tongue|hael]] "whole": those who *keep you hale by putting you back on growing ground.* [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]]'s second word is **Mother's-green hands**; [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]]'s is **lot-keepers** (ordinary, like saying *gardener*); [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] files them as **the Tended**.)*

**In one sentence:** they keep the lot — and they will not follow you home.

## Shape of the house (at a glance)

- **A commons, not a church.** There is no Green Seat. A town has a lot, a strip along the wall, pots in a yard the size of a tannery. Vaethorn's "lot" is often a cousin's field held in common. The house is whoever waters it and says who sleeps on which bed tonight.
- **Enough, then the gate.** A share is a night on living dirt, a season on a bed, a commons-hour. The person should leave able to work. A person who cannot leave is not this house's product. That person is how you find the Slide.
- **They think they are in a gardening story.** Soil, blight, whose beans took. They do not call themselves wardens of a people. The ~5% dial stays at the gate — not at a wilt they walk in to watch.
- **A bed-slate is plants.** Rows, which bed is tired, which needs a year of beans. Names get onto it because a person is *on* a bed. The keepers will tell you it is horticulture. In Threnmaieth the crown already has another list.

## What they actually do

### If you meet one

Dirt under the nails. A key to a gate in a wall. A slate. You would miss them as clergy if you came looking for a lintel. You would find them if you asked *where a Taken-In sleeps when the rooms are stone.*

They are useful the way a well is useful. A stone town cannot keep its Taken-In without a lot. The faith, if any, is extra.

### Who comes, and when

Write the bed, not the person, if they write at all. | Will not follow them home with a pot. Will not open the gate after the share-hour because the wilt has started. |

### An ordinary week (the job you can miss)

They **open the gate.** That is the whole kindness. A Taken-In lies down on living ground. The tokens fill. They leave. The bed is turned. The next name — no, the next *row* — is watered.

They bury last year's commons-leaf under a new bed. No one will explain this to a guest. A first-year who asks is told *that is how the lot remembers.*

When the queue is longer than the wilt, they still open the gate for the share they have. They close it when the share is gone. They do not walk the three streets to the room where someone is going to seed. They know a cousin still has a pot. They do not say the cousin's name.

A child of the house learns the work by carrying water and by being told not to take the key home.

### What they carry

A key. A slate. A trowel. Last year's leaf in a twist of cloth, if they are the one who buries it. No ticket. No colour-scrap. No book of weeks.

### What they refuse (the job's edge)

- They will not follow you home. The lot is the job. The parlour floor is not.
- They will not rent a private pot after the share fails. That is [[The Slide]].
- They will not steal a scion to make a grove. That unused trade stays unused.
- They will not call the share a gift on Lestrand, or a debt anywhere they can help it. They call it a share.
- They will not run a Kumbaan export. There is land enough on the isle, and almost no Given Tithe to furnish.

## Who may keep a lot

**Not by Kind.** A human, a [[Kitsune]], a [[Tengu]] who will water and keep the gate can be of the house. A [[Yumboe]] abroad who starts charging for dirt has started a different sin; send them back to [[The Table-Keepers]].

**By practice first.** You are of the house when the lot is still alive in the dry month, and the town still sends its Taken-In to you.

**Condition leans, not Condition-gates.**

| Act | Often | Why | Also, and welcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Watering, blight, which bed is tired | a [[The Taken-In\|Taken-In]] who kept a commons | the Tithe and the lot are the same shape | a Kept with no Tithe of their own, who can sit the gate year-round |
| The slate | whoever can read a tired row | the house is horticulture first | a [[Long-Lived]] who remembers which bed failed twenty years ago |
| The gate at close | a Kept, or anyone who will lock it | someone has to say *enough* | a Wide-Hand Struck who still wants the lot to *acknowledge* |

The feared-licensed are not barred. A [[The Stilled|Stilled]] who stills a blight-spread is rare and tightly watched; do not make them the face. Do not turn the lot into an infirmary.

## How they live in the three corners (and off the map)

- **[[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] (Mother's-green hands).** Home weather for the pious version. The commons *is* a cousin's field. They will say they are tending a gift. The quiet obligation is the harvest: you were laid on the Mother's green, you will spend the green as the valley asks. When the field fails, the last pot is the Slide, and they will still call it tending.
- **[[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] (lot-keepers).** Posted. A leaf-lot by the season. Coin on the gate. A Taken-In who cannot pay the season is not this lot's problem until they wilt in the street — and then they are the watch's. The Slide is the lot that still opens.
- **[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] (the Tended).** The crown furnishes the tended green and can withhold it. Beds match the channel. A wilt off the lot is a leak the roll does not want to file. The keepers look like gardeners. The leash is the water.
- **[[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]].** No. Do not export a lot-keeper.

## The gate (the house's injustice)

Whoever furnishes a provided-for Tithe holds power over its bearer. The greens-keepers hold that power *and refuse to carry it past the wall.*

That refusal is their kindness (they will not own you) and their cruelty (they will not walk three streets). The town is glad. A person who wilts *quietly* off the lot is not a public hazard. The Warm can say the gift was offered. The quay can say the season was posted. The crown can say the channel held.

The Motherfaith says she gave the need for living land. The gate is people's work. A kind Tree would not open a free parlour-grove.

They have no scion-queue. They have no week that will not end. They have no catching-year. Do not clone those. This house's constraint is jurisdiction — analog dirt, one key, no authority except the lot.

## Economy of the house

- **The share is the living.** Civic stipend in kind (Vaethorn), season-fees (Lestrand), crown channel (Threnmaieth). Not a tax on Conditions.
- **They are not a charity-order and not the Slide.** A private pot after close is another house.
- **Tired beds are real.** A lot can be botanically full and still have a queue. That is not a lie they tell. It is also a choice about who sleeps on stone.

## In-world text

*A bed-slate, Lestrand leaf-lot, nailed inside the gate. The verso is last season's slate, the tired rows still marked. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *Beds 1–12: taken through the next moon. 13–16: beans, do not lie here. 17 is tired; it will take next year. Share is one night or one hour at open sky, then the gate. If you cannot come, send no one — a bed is not a letter. We do not walk the rooms. If you have already wilted, the lot is still the lot. Come when we open. If you cannot wait, we do not know a pot.*

On its face: competent gardening, a little cold, a commons keeping itself honest. Underneath: the gate confessing itself, and a blindness the keeper cannot afford — that "we do not know a pot" is the only lie on the slate. The child who carries water can name the cousin. The slate cannot.

## GM Notes
*(Stripped on player-safe export. Keyed to the keystone via `reveals: [keystone-adjacent]`.)*

- **They do not know what story they are in.** Defaults: welfare office, nice slumlord, druid-chapter. Rotate. Form stays gardener/commons (load-bearing). They want *a living lot*, not a client. A wilt on the lot is a ruined commons. A wilt off the lot is weather they have decided not to see.
- **Moral parallax.** Distances: spatial (the three streets) and informational ("we do not know a pot"). Engine: exchange — every share is a bed someone else waits for — and accumulation — every closed gate feeds the Slide. Comfort: the word *share*. R2: she did not build the gate.
- **The buried leaf is inscrutable.** Do not explain it. It is how the lot remembers. A PC who digs it up has committed a local rudeness, not found a cosmology.
- **Bed-slate vs Threnhael.** Proposed: the slate is plants. If a later taste call wants it already a quiet census, that is Threnmaieth leaking, not the world's baseline.
- **Do not clone the Slide, the Road-hands, or the scion-queue.** The injustice is the gate.
- **No Kind-gate. No Kumbaan export. No Green pope.**
- **Hooks.** A slate that says *we do not know a pot* and a child who does; a Book-hand's share-term on a full lot; a Vaethorn field that failed and a last pot that still says *gift*; a crown channel late on purpose; a Taken-In PC whose only dirt is the other side of a locked gate.
