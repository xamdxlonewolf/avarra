# Rogue House Options

> **What this is.** The Back Table is **out** (taste call 2026-08-23) — too much a night-clone of [[The Book-Hands]], too much fake Bound-easing. The shadow economy in [[Economy and the Tithe]] still exists; it does not yet have a lived face. **Pick one.** Do not take all three. Do not rebuild the Back Table under a new name.

> None of these is a fifth faith. Recruits by practice / desperation / Condition-lean, **not Kind**. ~5% scary: ordinary people, a real obstacle. Not a devil-church. Not a Kumbaan export.

## Why the Back Table failed

It was defined as *the job the Book-Hands refused*. Same ink, opposite hour. That made it a costume, not a house. The Bound-easing trade also reopened the clause you did not want clerks to own.

These three do **different jobs**.

---

## Option A — The Bought Watch

**In one sentence:** they sell *I watched you Turn* — a false childhood in a world where belonging is witnessed, not written down.

**If you meet one.** A neighbour with a good memory, or a Long-Lived who will say they remember your leaf. They do not stamp books. They stand in the square and *vouch*. The honest version of this is [[Law and Citizenship|standing-for]]. The Bought Watch sells the dishonest one.

**What they do.** Coach an unvouched Struck on a town's last Turning-Week; place a witness who will swear they were under the boughs; once in a century, a corrupt Long-Lived whose lie lasts longer than anyone who could contradict it.

**What they will not do.** Rewrite a Bound's Terms. Steal a scion. Run a cellar church.

**The injustice.** Every bought watch is a hole in citizenship-by-witness. The town thinks it *saw*. It was sold a seeing.

**Sinister lean.** Quiet. Social. The scar is a person who belongs and does not.

**Cant-name 🟡:** *Orenvaeth* (or-EN-vayth) — *oren* + *vaeth*, "the witnessing that was waited for." Everyday: **the Bought Watch.**

---

## Option B — The Quiet Cut

**In one sentence:** they steal, hide, or broker living wood — a scion that was meant for one town goes to another, or to none.

**If you meet one.** A carter on a Road-hand's route. A grove-hand who knows when a Cutting-leave is being carried. They do not tend a town Tree. They move *the next* Tree.

**What they do.** Jump the [[The Tree-Wardens|scion-queue]]. Sell a cut to a rich Lestrand college-town. Let a Vaethorn hamlet wait another twenty years. Once, bury a scion so a rival grove stays guest-only.

**What they will not do.** Speak a colour. Ease a Bound clause. Sell a false Turning-witness (that is Option A).

**The injustice.** The Seat already rations grafts. The Quiet Cut *sells the ration*. Confirming she is kind does not give Harrow's green the wood that went in a cart the other way.

**Sinister lean.** Material. Horticultural crime. The scar is a generation of borrowed Turnings.

**Cant-name 🟡:** *Thaelneth* (THALE-neth) — *thael* + *neth*, "the Tree taken by night." Everyday: **the Quiet Cut.**

---

## Option C — The Slide

**In one sentence:** they do not help you pay a Tithe — they *own the moment you start to fail*, and they make failure useful.

**If you meet one.** A landlord who rents the last green. A creditor who will extend one more week. A kind face who knows exactly how close you are to the scary edge and is not in a hurry to pull you back.

**What they do.** Keep Tithe-poor people *just* supplied — then withhold. Buy the debt of a fading Far-Voiced. Steer a Taken-In off the commons onto a private lot. They profit from the slide [[Economy and the Tithe]] already named. They are not a guild (that waits for 5.3). They are the people the guild would pretend not to know.

**What they will not do.** Pretend to be clergy. Rewrite Bound Terms as a rite. (They might *hold* a Bound's civic debt. That is money, not a clause.)

**The injustice.** Tithe-need as leverage, with a face. The Mother did not build this. R2 is the whole point.

**Sinister lean.** The most evil-leaning of the three, still mundane: rent, credit, a key to a room. Not a cult.

**Cant-name 🟡:** *Vaethledd* (VAYTH-led) — *vaeth* + *ledd*, "the waiting reckoned." Everyday: **the Slide.**

## How to pick

| | Job | Scar | Closest honest house | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A Bought Watch** | fake witness | a belonging that was never seen | Book-Hands / Law | feels like "identity theft" |
| **B Quiet Cut** | stolen grafts | a town that waits another generation | Tree-Wardens / Road-hands | feels like "heist the magic tree" |
| **C Slide** | own the Tithe-fail | a person used at the edge | (none yet; 5.3) | feels like "evil landlord"; may want to wait for 5.3 |

Pick **one**. If none of these, say what job you want that is not "night Book-Hands."
