# The Protectors

Everyday, when anyone speaks of them: **the Protectors**. The building the world is allowed to know: **the Care**. A hospice-endowment. Old money. A closed court. The story that travels is that the one [[Phoenix]] is being kept safe, and that is why no town has seen them in a generation.

Doctrine says there cannot be two. The Care still "holds the Phoenix." A white-fire fall Spoken far away is filed as a mis-Speaking or a hush. A far graft with no Speaker never enters the record.

They worship the one. They do not stand the worship in a square.

## At the table

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | The one kept safe, the stories kept pointed at an old name, and the Care's store unbroken. |
| **Have** | A closed hospice, a public clerk, a sanctuary that will not produce the Phoenix, and a generation of towns that have stopped looking. |
| **Fear** | A Spoken white-fire while the Care still "holds" the old name. The one dying in the inner court while a guest is waiting. A clerk who walks someone out. |
| **Live conflict** | Vimein will show a closed court and a kind sentence. A runner from a far warden is in the yard with a letter about a leaf that was never Spoken. |
| **Hooks** | A sanctuary that will not produce the one; a hush on a white-fire letter; a guest turned away from the inner court; a runner whose letter never comes back. |

## If you meet one (public)

**Vimein** *(vih-MAYN)* keeps the outer book of the Care. Conservative mouth, quiet sleeve. They will say the Phoenix is in care. They will not produce them. They will offer a viewing of the closed court, a hymn that does not name a Mother, and a request that you not carry a story back to a square that has finally gone quiet.

You would take them for a hospice clerk. You would be right about the building. You would be wrong about the job.

The wider world has not seen a Phoenix because the Care still holds the old name, and the squares have stopped looking. That is the public fact. How the Care stays warm is not.

## What a neighbour can see

- A hospice that endows its own heat and light.
- Letters from wardens that do not come back answered.
- A generation of squares that talk about the Phoenix in the past tense and the present tense in the same sentence.
- A child Spoken white-fire in a far town, filed as hush, while the Care still holds a name.

They will not stamp a ticket. They will not rent a week. They will not classify a Closed Heir. They will not pour five years into a cup.

## What they refuse (the job they admit)

- They will not parade the Phoenix. The fishbowl is what they say they saved the one from.
- They will not confirm a second Gift. Doctrine and the Care agree: there is one.
- They will not open the inner court to a relic-hunter or a Speaker who wants a colour-copy.
- They will not work a Kumbaan wreck. There is no white-fire on the sand.

## How they reach a party

The party is asked to carry a letter to the Care and is not let in. A town wants the Phoenix for a festival and is told the one is in care. A child with a white-fire fall needs a runner; the runner does not arrive. Vimein hires quiet people to keep a hush. A guest who got as far as the inner door comes out with a kind sentence and a missing hour.

## In-world text

*A visitor's slip, the Care, Vimein's hand. The inner court is not on the slip.*

> *The one is in care. The stories may rest. We do not produce a person for a square that has already decided what they mean. If you have come with a white-fire letter, leave it at the outer book. If you have come to worship, the hymn is said in the closed court and not in the street. If you have come to hunt, you will find a hospice. We keep the one. We keep the quiet. We do not keep a second name.*

On its face: a sanctuary doing kindness. Underneath: "we do not keep a second name" is how a new leaf is made into a hush.

## GM Notes

- **Worship used to hide coercive extraction.** Engine three of three. They remove each Phoenix from public life. They cause **controlled deaths**. They **harvest Phoenix Fire**. They take the memory of those deaths with the forced Rise. They turn the bank into institutional power — heat, light, a remnant that still walks, a hush that holds. They think they are in a relic-care story. The harvest is the job they cannot afford to see.
- **Use the locked engine. Do not rewrite it.** [[When the Fire Is Caught]]: one Gift; original self ends at the death they cannot Rise from; banked fire forces a Rise (memory of that death taken; Hope still scars) while the Gift lasts; after the Gift ends, the bank keeps a remnant, not a Phoenix; a new leaf may fall unseen because the remnant is still *called* the Phoenix. A PC Phoenix is never forced to Rise. That rule stays.
- **Not the other engines.** Not [[The Pourers]] (self-consumption into a made god). Not [[The Walled Book]] (class rule). Not [[The Remainder]] (yard-scrap, not fire). Do not store the Spent Leaf here. Do not pour years into the Care.
- **Cliché rotation.** Defaults: phoenix knights, fire cult, chosen-one bodyguard. Form stays hospice/sanctuary (load-bearing). Knowledge/goal/role: they want the one *kept*, the fire *tended so it does not burn the world*, the stories pointed at a safe name. They do not know they are farming a person. Play them as reverent and sure they helped.
- **Institutional power from the store.** Late-medieval, ~5%. The Care is warm when the street is not. A remnant can still take the air. A hush can move a Speaker to file "mis-Spoken." Do not give them a fire-nuke. Do not make the bank a second Gift.
- **Tone.** The public face is kindness. The harvest is the 5% underneath — opt-in for a table that wants it, not a hunt in the first hour. Controlled deaths are prepared, quiet, and terrible. Do not relish them.
- **Names.** Vimein — seed `20260830`, conservative list, mid-bottom. The Care is common-tongue. No new liturgy. Do not name a capital. Do not add a power.
- **The public sentence is "the one is in care."** They hide each Phoenix they take in. A remnant going to ash in front of a guest is the fear the outer book cannot write. Hope scars without memories, and fire that answers a child and an old name at once, stay GM hooks.
- **R.8 / Epic 10 seeds, do not plot:** [[Rosire]] walked someone out and now sits Tesara's shed. [[Taeren]] is a hush at a thin Tree. The child is real; the walker from the sanctuary is the remnant. Player-agency: [[A Hidden Phoenix]]. Epic 10 picks the opening. Do not write the hidden-Phoenix sheet here.
- **Clue order** stays on [[When the Fire Is Caught]]. Do not lead with a second bird.
- **Hooks.** The unproduced Phoenix; the hush-letter; Hope scars without memories; fire that answers two names; a guest's missing hour; Vimein hiring a hush.
