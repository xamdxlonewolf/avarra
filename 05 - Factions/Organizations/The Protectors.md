# The Protectors

When anyone speaks of them, the everyday name is **the Protectors**. The building the world is allowed to know is **the Care**. It is a hospice-endowment, old money behind a closed court, and the story that travels is that the one [[Phoenix]] is being kept safe there, which is why no town has seen them in a generation. In that story a Phoenix is the one person the tales have been about. The Care says it holds that person, and says there is only one.

They worship the one. They do not hold the worship in a square, where a town could come and look.

## If you meet one (public)

**Vimein** *(vih-MAYN)* keeps the outer book of the Care. Vimein speaks the conservative common speech, quietly, and says little. They will say the Phoenix is in care, and they will not produce them. They will offer a viewing of the closed court, a hymn that does not name a Mother, and a request that you not carry a story back to a square that has finally gone quiet.

You would take them for a hospice clerk, and the building is a hospice.

The wider world has not seen a Phoenix because the Care says it holds the old name, and the squares have stopped looking.

## What a neighbour can see

A neighbour can see a hospice with old money and a closed court, and a generation of squares that talk about the Phoenix in the past tense and the present tense in the same sentence, as if the one were both gone and still in the building.

They will not stamp a ticket, rent a week, classify a Closed Heir, or pour years into a cup. A Closed Heir is someone the [[The Walled Book]] has already classed. Pouring a year of memory into a cup is [[The Pourers]]' act, not this desk.

## What they refuse (the job they admit)

They will not parade the Phoenix. They say every square was staring at the one, and the Care ended that. They say there is only one Phoenix. They will not open the inner court to a relic-hunter, or to a Speaker who wants a colour-copy, a second writing of the leaf. They will not work a wreck on [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]]. There is no white-fire, the colour the Phoenix is known by, on that sand.

## How they reach a party

The party is asked to carry a letter to the Care, and is not let in. A town wants the Phoenix for a festival and is told the one is in care. A guest who got as far as the inner door is turned away with a kind sentence.

## In-world text

*What follows is a visitor's slip from the Care, in Vimein's hand. The inner court is not on the slip.*

> *The one is in care. The stories may rest. We do not produce a person for a square that has already decided what they mean. If you have come to worship, the hymn is said in the closed court and not in the street. If you have come to hunt, you will find a hospice. We keep the one. We keep the quiet.*

These are the Care's own words to a visitor. They say the Phoenix is in care, that nobody will be shown them, and that the Care would like the story left alone.

## GM Notes

The links that carry the engine are [[When the Fire Is Caught]], [[Taeren]], [[Rosire]], and [[11 - Secrets]].

Worship is what they use to hide a coercive extraction. The Protectors are the third of three groups that can look alike from the door. What they do with a Phoenix is remove each one from public life, cause controlled deaths, harvest Phoenix Fire, and take the memory of those deaths with the forced Rise. They turn the bank of that fire into institutional power, which shows up as heat, as light, as a remnant that still walks, and as a hush that holds. They think they are caring for a relic, and they cannot afford to see that they are harvesting a person.

What the player-facing body leaves out is the store. The Care keeps a store of fire taken from the dying, and the building stays warm when the street is not. It hushes a second white-fire on purpose. A white-fire fall Spoken far away is filed as a mis-Speaking or as a hush. A far graft with no Speaker never enters the record. A letter about such a leaf does not come back answered. The doctrine "there cannot be two" is how a new leaf is made into a hush. The Care fears a Spoken white-fire while it still "holds" the old name, the one dying in the inner court while a guest waits, and a remnant going to ash in front of a guest. A child Spoken white-fire in a far town needs a runner, and the runner does not arrive. Vimein hires quiet people to keep a hush. A guest who got to the inner door comes out with a kind sentence and a missing hour.

Use the locked engine. Do not rewrite it. [[When the Fire Is Caught]] already has it: one Gift; the original self ends at the death they cannot Rise from; banked fire forces a Rise, the memory of that death taken and the Hope still scarring, while the Gift lasts; after the Gift ends, the bank keeps a remnant, not a Phoenix; a new leaf may fall unseen because the remnant is still *called* the Phoenix. A PC Phoenix is never forced to Rise. That rule stays.

They are not [[The Pourers]], where a member gives their own memory into a cup. They are not [[The Walled Book]], which ranks people by Condition, and they are not [[The Remainder]], which is yard-scrap and not fire. Do not store the Spent Leaf here, and do not pour years into the Care.

Avoid the stock figures: Phoenix knights, a fire cult, a chosen-one bodyguard. The form stays a hospice and a sanctuary. They want the one *kept*, the fire *tended so it does not burn the world*, and the stories pointed at a safe name. They do not know they are farming a person. Play them as reverent, and sure they helped.

The power that comes off the store is institutional, late-medieval, and about 5% frightening. The Care is warm when the street is not. A remnant can still take the air. A hush can move a Speaker to file "mis-Spoken." Do not give them a fire-nuke. Do not make the bank a second Gift.

The public face is kindness. The harvest is the frightening part, and it is a small part of the whole, opt-in for a table that wants it and not a hunt in the first hour. Controlled deaths are prepared, quiet, and terrible. Do not relish them.

Vimein comes from seed `20260830`, the conservative list, mid-bottom. The Care is common-tongue. Do not write a new liturgy, do not name a capital, and do not add a power.

The public sentence is "the one is in care." Hope scars without memories, and fire that answers a child and an old name at once, stay GM hooks. They do not go into the player-facing paragraphs.

The opening, already picked, runs like this. [[Rosire]] walked someone out and is in Harrow's upper room this Hale-month, and [[Taeren]] is a Brenod hush at the neighbour's canopy. The child is real, and the walker from the sanctuary is the remnant. Player-agency lives on [[A Hidden Phoenix]], and the kit is [[The Opening]]. Do not invent a Care-fortress, do not write the hidden-Phoenix sheet here, and leave Vimein offstage for session one.

Clue order stays on [[When the Fire Is Caught]]. Do not lead with a second bird.

The hooks that belong under here, and not in the player table, are the unproduced Phoenix, the hush-letter, Hope scars without memories, fire that answers two names, a guest's missing hour, and Vimein hiring a hush.
