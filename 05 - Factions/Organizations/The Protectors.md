# The Protectors

Everyday, when anyone speaks of them: **the Protectors**. The building the world is allowed to know: **the Care**. A hospice-endowment. Old money. A closed court. The story that travels is that the one [[Phoenix]] is being kept safe, and that is why no town has seen them in a generation.

The Care says it holds the Phoenix, and says there is only one.

They worship the one. They do not hold the worship in a square.

## If you meet one (public)

**Vimein** *(vih-MAYN)* keeps the outer book of the Care. Vimein speaks the conservative common speech, quietly, and says little. They will say the Phoenix is in care. They will not produce them. They will offer a viewing of the closed court, a hymn that does not name a Mother, and a request that you not carry a story back to a square that has finally gone quiet.

You would take them for a hospice clerk, and the building is a hospice.

The wider world has not seen a Phoenix because the Care says it holds the old name, and the squares have stopped looking.

## What a neighbour can see

- A hospice with old money and a closed court.
- A generation of squares that talk about the Phoenix in the past tense and the present tense in the same sentence.

They will not stamp a ticket. They will not rent a week. They will not classify a Closed Heir. They will not pour years into a cup.

## What they refuse (the job they admit)

- They will not parade the Phoenix. They say every square was staring at the one, and the Care ended that.
- They say there is only one Phoenix.
- They will not open the inner court to a relic-hunter or a Speaker who wants a colour-copy.
- They will not work a Kumbaan wreck. There is no white-fire on the sand.

## How they reach a party

The party is asked to carry a letter to the Care and is not let in. A town wants the Phoenix for a festival and is told the one is in care. A guest who got as far as the inner door is turned away with a kind sentence.

## In-world text

*A visitor's slip, the Care, Vimein's hand. The inner court is not on the slip.*

> *The one is in care. The stories may rest. We do not produce a person for a square that has already decided what they mean. If you have come to worship, the hymn is said in the closed court and not in the street. If you have come to hunt, you will find a hospice. We keep the one. We keep the quiet.*

These are the Care's own words to a visitor. They say the Phoenix is in care, that nobody will be shown them, and that the Care would like the story left alone.

## GM Notes

- **Links.** [[When the Fire Is Caught]] — the engine · [[Taeren]] · [[Rosire]] · [[11 - Secrets]]
- **Worship used to hide coercive extraction.** The Protectors are the third of three groups that can look alike. They remove each Phoenix from public life. They cause **controlled deaths**. They **harvest Phoenix Fire**. They take the memory of those deaths with the forced Rise. They turn the bank into institutional power — heat, light, a remnant that still walks, a hush that holds. They think they are caring for a relic. They cannot afford to see that they are harvesting a person.
- **What the player body leaves out.** The Care keeps a store of fire taken from the dying, and it stays warm when the street is not. It hushes a second white-fire on purpose: a white-fire fall Spoken far away is filed as a mis-Speaking or a hush, a far graft with no Speaker never enters the record, and a letter about such a leaf does not come back answered. The doctrine "there cannot be two" is how a new leaf is made into a hush. The Care fears a Spoken white-fire while it still "holds" the old name, the one dying in the inner court while a guest waits, and a remnant going to ash in front of a guest. A child Spoken white-fire in a far town needs a runner, and the runner does not arrive. Vimein hires quiet people to keep a hush, and a guest who got to the inner door comes out with a kind sentence and a missing hour.
- **Use the locked engine. Do not rewrite it.** [[When the Fire Is Caught]]: one Gift; original self ends at the death they cannot Rise from; banked fire forces a Rise (memory of that death taken; Hope still scars) while the Gift lasts; after the Gift ends, the bank keeps a remnant, not a Phoenix; a new leaf may fall unseen because the remnant is still *called* the Phoenix. A PC Phoenix is never forced to Rise. That rule stays.
- **Not the other groups.** Not [[The Pourers]] (a member gives their own memory into a cup). Not [[The Walled Book]] (ranks people by Condition). Not [[The Remainder]] (yard-scrap, not fire). Do not store the Spent Leaf here. Do not pour years into the Care.
- **Stock figures to avoid.** Phoenix knights, a fire cult, a chosen-one bodyguard. The form stays hospice/sanctuary. They want the one *kept*, the fire *tended so it does not burn the world*, the stories pointed at a safe name. They do not know they are farming a person. Play them as reverent and sure they helped.
- **Institutional power from the store.** Late-medieval, about 5% frightening. The Care is warm when the street is not. A remnant can still take the air. A hush can move a Speaker to file "mis-Spoken." Do not give them a fire-nuke. Do not make the bank a second Gift.
- **Tone.** The public face is kindness. The harvest is the frightening part, and a small part of the whole — opt-in for a table that wants it, not a hunt in the first hour. Controlled deaths are prepared, quiet, and terrible. Do not relish them.
- **Names.** Vimein — seed `20260830`, conservative list, mid-bottom. The Care is common-tongue. No new liturgy. Do not name a capital. Do not add a power.
- **The public sentence is "the one is in care."** They hide each Phoenix they take in. Hope scars without memories, and fire that answers a child and an old name at once, stay GM hooks.
- **Opening, picked:** [[Rosire]] walked someone out and is in Harrow's upper room this Hale-month. [[Taeren]] is a Brenod hush at the neighbour's canopy. The child is real; the walker from the sanctuary is the remnant. Player-agency: [[A Hidden Phoenix]]. Kit: [[The Opening]]. Do not invent a Care-fortress. Do not write the hidden-Phoenix sheet here. Vimein stays offstage for session one.
- **Clue order** stays on [[When the Fire Is Caught]]. Do not lead with a second bird.
- **Hooks.** The unproduced Phoenix; the hush-letter; Hope scars without memories; fire that answers two names; a guest's missing hour; Vimein hiring a hush.
