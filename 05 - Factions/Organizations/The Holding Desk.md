# The Holding Desk

Everyday: **the Holding Desk**, also **hold-clerks**. [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] says it the way it says *extension*. [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] has no clean word and calls it **the second signing** when a preacher wants a sin. [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] files nothing; a private Term that is not the [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]] is already a problem, and this desk is that problem with a chair.

They are not [[The Slide]]. They do not rent green, an hour, or a page. They work in **clauses**.

## At the table

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | A Bound who can still sign next year. Continuity of the book, not a corpse and not a sermon. |
| **Have** | A downstairs room, a second stamp that looks almost honest, and a list of counterparties who will take a smaller Term to cover a larger one. |
| **Fear** | A Forfeit that becomes a public personhood case. A Book-hand who follows the customer down the stair. A Counterparty who will not accept the next signing. |
| **Live conflict** | [[The Book-Hands]] close a civic year upstairs; the desk opens a new Term downstairs. Same Bound. Same week. The stamp and the hold do not share a docket. |
| **Hooks** | A year marked *settled* and a person who still owes a clause; Mutelo's extension-slip dated twice; a sister who will vouch the Bound *as they are now* if the desk will let her see the Terms; a Forfeit the law has not named. |

## If you meet one

Ink. A stool that does not face the quay. They look like a junior Settler who lost the front room. You would find them if you asked *who still writes after the book is closed.*

**Mutelo of the Lower Slip** *(moo-TEL-o)* keeps the oldest chair on [[Orentel]]. Eroded mouth. They will tell you they kept a person walking. The book on the shelf is labelled *extensions*. The last page is not.

## What they actually do

A [[Bound]] finishes a civic year at an honest table. The year is payable. The **Terms** are not. Fear of the Pledge starts the next ask. The hold-clerk does not invent the fear. They name a smaller Term that covers the larger one for a season.

That is the pipeline. Each signing eats more of the person. The last signing is the [[Bound|Given-Over]] — the Forfeit, in guild speech. The law has never decided when the person on the stool stopped being one ([[Law and Citizenship]]).

| Who comes | What the desk does | The limit |
|---|---|---|
| A Bound behind on a Pledge | Write a covering Term. Name a day. Extend. | Will not recite the old Terms as if they were gone. The honest table still has them. |
| A family who will pay to keep a sibling "working" | Hold the person as an asset that still signs. | Will not call this kinship. Will not take them to a door-keeper to be named *kin*. |
| A creditor who wants a walkable instrument | Match a Counterparty who will accept the next signing. | Will not stamp a civic year. That is a Book-hand. |
| A Struck Bound, raw | Sometimes. If the ask is already live. | Will not sit [[The Intake]]. Wrong shed. The Whisper cannot command; this is still a table. |

They **extend first**. That is what they believe about themselves. The withhold is the next clause, measured, never quite the last one until it is.

## What they refuse

- They will not rent a Tithe-week. Dirt, a Cry, a page — [[The Slide]].
- They will not sell *I watched you Turn.* That mouth is [[The Standing Trade]].
- They will not steal a scion or Speak a colour.
- They will not rewrite a Term the Counterparty has already named. They add a Term. The distinction is how they sleep.
- They will not work a Kumbaan wreck. There is no Bound table on the sand.

A corpse is a ruined book. They still walk people toward the Forfeit. Those two facts sit in the same chair.

## How they reach a party

A Bound friend is being refinanced. A Book-hand's *owing* walks downstairs and does not come back up. The party is asked to witness a signing that is "only a season." A Given-Over case has no settled law and needs a mouth that is not the creditor's. Mutelo will hire runners. They pay in extensions.

## How they live with the other houses

| House | Everyday | The fight |
|---|---|---|
| [[The Book-Hands]] | Upstairs / downstairs. A Settler who marked *owing* has made a customer. | The same Bound, same week. The clerk who follows them down has started a case. |
| [[The Slide]] | Different product. A Bound may be on both books. | Money is not a clause. A week-keeper who starts writing Terms has changed houses. |
| [[The Standing Trade]] | A Forfeit who needs to cross a border may buy a mouth. | Do not merge the trades. A false childhood will not pay a Pledge. |
| [[The Tree-Wardens]] | Almost none. A quiet receiving-rite does not close a Term. | Do not let a Road-hand become a hold-cart. |
| [[The Reckoned Offices]] | A private Term Threnmaieth cannot file is a leak. | Menirein wants the name. The desk wants the name off any roll that is not theirs. |

**Lestrand** is home weather. **Vaethorn** calls it a sin and still uses it when a Bound would otherwise become a rope-story. **Threnmaieth** cannot file it without admitting the channel failed; the desk looks like a clerk.

## In-world text

*An extension-slip, Orentel lower slip, pinned under a stool. The verso is last season's slip, the date scratched once.*

> *Covering Term, one season. The upstairs year is closed. This is not that year. Come before the Pledge-day. If you cannot come, send no one — a clause is not a child. We do not call this a gift. We do not call it a sale. If you have already signed, come anyway. The next Term is the same Term until it is not.*

On its face: a clerk being practical. Underneath: the product is *not paying yet*, and the writer cannot afford the word *Forfeit*. "If you have already signed, come anyway" is the house's whole case. Becoming did not save you from needing them.

## GM Notes

- **They think they are in a refinancing story.** Defaults: devil's advocates, soul-sellers, a night-clone of the Book-Hands. Rotate. Form stays clerk/creditor. Knowledge, goal, role do not: they want the book to continue. A Given-Over is a failed extension they will still file as competence.
- **Moral parallax.** Distances: causal ("I kept them from the Pledge") and informational (the quay is glad not to see the stair). Engine: accumulation — each covering Term seems small. Comfort: the word *extension*. The town is complicit. R2: she did not build this desk. Orledd does not receive the spiral ([[Bound]]; [[The Other Hands]]).
- **Opposition can act.** They will push a person toward the Forfeit when the book requires it. They prefer a walking instrument. Preference is not a lock. A desperate hold-clerk will write the last Term.
- **Not the Slide. Not the unused Back Table.** The Back Table stayed unused as a *Slide pick*. This is a later face for the pipeline the economy already named.
- **Names.** Mutelo of the Lower Slip — seed `20260831`, eroded list, picked mid-bottom. Do not median-hop a "Cassian Vale."
- **R.8 seed, do not plot:** a Bound who wants the next Term refused, and a hold-clerk who will not let the book end.
- **Hooks.** The twice-dated slip; a sister at the stair; a personhood case with no rule; a Book-hand and a hold-clerk in one room; a Counterparty who starves on unread extras and wants *one* kept Term.
