# The Hall-Keepers

**Hall-keepers**, or **ael-hands**, in every register. This is a **jurisdiction of [[Tithe-Infrastructure]]**, not a house with its own college. They do two jobs: the outlet and the Voice-ticket. The hour, the unswept bench, and the room they do not name live on the sector note.

**In one sentence:** they sell you an hour in which you may feel in earnest — and they will not open after the lamps.

## At the table

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | A town that does not Cry in the market, and a bill that fills. |
| **Have** | A slate of hours, a Voice-ticket stool, one unswept bench. |
| **Fear** | A missed hour that becomes a street, and a counted hall late on purpose. |
| **Live conflict** | The lamps are out. A Far-Voiced person at the door still has a Cry unspent. Videm's channel says the hour held. The Slide's room still shuts. |
| **Hooks** | A seventh-day term on a full slate; a sweep-hour that is the only hour a PC can afford; the unswept bench after a Cry no one will name. |

A raw Struck Far-Voiced goes to [[The Intake]]. A shut room after the lamps is [[The Slide]]. A Voice-ticket names a competence, not a census — [[Law and Citizenship#The safety-licence — how hazard is regulated (and by whom)]]. They recruit by practice, not Kind. They do not run a mission-hall on [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]].
