# The Hall-Keepers

**Hall-keepers**, or **ael-hands**, is what people call them in every register, from the street to a docket. This is a jurisdiction of [[Tithe-Infrastructure]], not a house with a college of its own. They do two jobs, and the two jobs share a building. One is the outlet, an hour in which a [[Far-Voiced]] person may feel in earnest, on purpose, so the feeling does not break out as a Cry in the market. The other is the Voice-ticket, the licence for using that voice where other people can hear it. The hour, the unswept bench, and the room they do not name live on the sector note.

They sell you an hour in which you may feel in earnest, and they will not open after the lamps.

## At the table

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | A town that does not Cry in the market, and a bill that fills. |
| **Have** | A slate of hours, a Voice-ticket stool, and one unswept bench. |
| **Fear** | A missed hour that becomes a street, and a counted hall that is late on purpose. |
| **Live conflict** | The lamps are out. A Far-Voiced person at the door still has a Cry unspent. Videm's channel says the hour held. The Slide's room still shuts. |
| **Hooks** | A seventh-day term written on a slate that is already full; a sweep-hour that is the only hour a person can afford; the unswept bench after a Cry no one will name. |

A raw Struck Far-Voiced person, newly in the voice and not yet trained, goes to [[The Intake]]. A room that still shuts after the lamps is [[The Slide]], and it is not this hall's side door. A Voice-ticket names a competence, not a census. The rule for that licence is [[Law and Citizenship#The safety-licence — how hazard is regulated (and by whom)]]. They recruit by practice, by who can keep the hour, and not by Kind. They do not run a mission-hall on [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]].

## GM Notes

This door was folded in as a jurisdiction. The Far-Voiced are the seam Condition, the one that sits between a playhouse and a hazard, and you should not grow four offices on a playhouse to make the seam look like a college.

Do not explain which Cry the unswept bench belongs to. The bench stays, and the Cry it belongs to stays unnamed.

The old proposed liturgical form *Aeloren* is retired because it sat too close to *Vaeloren, Leddoren,* and *Eoloren* and added no useful distinction. A crossed-out roster may still show it, and no current hall uses it as a title. It is not waiting for a replacement, so do not refill the empty slot with another compound.
