# The Remainder

> **A desk, not a thirteenth house.** They sweep. They jar blight. They keep what the Leaf-Fall does not account for. Outsiders who have heard of them think they are janitors with a liturgical lintel. They are. They also keep [[The Spent Leaf]].

## Overview (public)

The First Seat authorizes cuttings. [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hands]] carry living earth. Someone has to take back what did not take, what rotted on the road, what the square swept up after the colours were Speaked.

That someone is **the Remainder**: a side-yard off the Motherwood, a shed, a kitchen scale, and two or three clerks who are not Speakers. They do not officiate a Leaf-Fall. They do not walk a circuit unless a Road-hand needs a box carried with a scion. Their charter, such as it is, is *uncounted tissue* — horticulture the college would rather not file on the same page as a colour.

They are of the [[The Tree-Wardens|Orenhael]] the way a yard is of a temple. They are not the Quiet Cut (that unused name was *stolen wood*; this desk does not steal). They are not a rival faith.

## Structure & Membership

- **Who.** Remainder-clerks. Often Kept; sometimes a skeptic who is good with jars; rarely a Road-hand on a winter rotation. Not a Kind-office. Not a Speaker track.
- **How many.** A handful at the origin. A borrowed shelf in a waiting town if a blight-cutting had to come home.
- **Who sends for them.** A warden with mixed-fall scraps; a Road-hand whose scion died in the wrap; the Seat when a relic rumour needs a boring answer.

## Goals & Methods

**Public job.** Keep the yard honest. Do not let blight ride the next Cutting-leave. Do not let a copper-green scrap get filed as a hug.

**The live fight inside the shed.** Some clerks want [[The Spent Leaf]] to stay a yard-fact — a colourless scrap, logged, locked, not Speaked. Some want it walked to a town that has waited a generation, so the queue cannot be called weather. Both think they are tending the wood.

Methods are small: a box that does not travel; a box that does; a clerk reassigned to blight; a Road-hand who "needed the earth not to go empty." No hunt. No relic-cult.

## How they look from the three corners

- **Vaethorn.** A waiting hearth that hears of a colourless leaf wants it as proof they were not unworthy — or as proof they were. The Remainder will not bring it to settle guilt.
- **Lestrand.** A viewing has a price if anyone will sell one. The Seat has not. Book-hands have written to the yard. The letters sit in the same cedar as the cloth.
- **Threnmaieth.** A scrap the roll cannot colour is a gap. A clerk who will not name it to the Threnhael is doing the Seat's old job: the Tree witnessed; a ledger is a second, lesser seeing. Pressure, not a raid.

## GM Notes

- **Positional, not chosen.** Competence trap: the people who empty carrying-earth are the people who would find a spend-remainder. Their involvement is the job, not a prophecy. Keep them excellent at jars and bad at politics.
- **Split want is the faction.** The audit asked for a group that wants proof found *or* suppressed. This desk is both. **Seat majority / orthodox remainder:** bury it. Orthopraxy-over-orthodoxy is the peace; proof would make skeptics a column and give Threnmaieth a fact to count. **Show-it minority (some clerks, some Road-hands):** walk the leaf. They want the scion-queue named as rationing a real reach, not as soil-science. Neither side is a dark court. ~5%: lose a box, refuse a leave, reassign a clerk. Do not invent assassins.
- **Not an opposition engine.** The three engines are [[The Pourers]], [[The Walled Book]], [[The Protectors]]. Do not grow this desk into any of those. Do not let them harvest fire or pour a god.
- **Firing pin.** The artifact and the tests: [[The Spent Leaf]]. What happens if the square ever believes it: [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real#If confirmation lands]]. The show-it clerks are right that the queue is people. They are wrong if they think the leaf will shame the Seat into justice.
- **Do not sort Kept with this office.** A remainder-clerk who offers to "tell you which hug was a miss" is breaking the locked ache. Shut that down in-world: the leaf will not, and the clerk who promised it was selling.
