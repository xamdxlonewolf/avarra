# The Remainder

The Remainder is a desk of the First Seat, not a separate house. They sweep up after the fall, they jar the blight so it does not travel, and they keep whatever the Leaf-Fall does not account for. Outsiders who have heard of them think they are janitors with a church title. They are janitors, and they also keep [[The Spent Leaf]].

## Overview (public)

The First Seat is the house that authorizes cuttings, and the [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hands]] are the people who carry the living earth. Someone still has to take back what did not take, what rotted on the road, and what the square swept up after the colours were Spoken, and that someone is **the Remainder**.

What you would actually find is a side-yard off the Motherwood, a shed, a kitchen scale, and two or three clerks who are not Speakers. A Speaker is the person who officiates a Leaf-Fall and Speaks a colour, and these clerks do not do that. They do not walk a circuit either, unless a Road-hand needs a box carried along with a scion. Their charter, such as it is, is *uncounted tissue*, the horticulture the college would rather not file on the same page as a colour.

They belong to the [[The Tree-Wardens|Orenhael]] the way a yard belongs to a temple. This desk does not steal wood, and it is not a rival faith.

## Structure & Membership

The people at the desk are remainder-clerks, and often they are Kept. Sometimes the clerk is a skeptic who is good with jars, and rarely it is a Road-hand on a winter rotation. The office is not reserved to one Kind, and it is not a track you enter in order to become a Speaker.

There are a handful of them at the origin, which is this yard. A waiting town gets a borrowed shelf only if a blight-cutting had to come home.

The people who send for them are a warden carrying mixed-fall scraps, a Road-hand whose scion died in the wrap, and the Seat itself, when a relic rumour needs a boring answer.

## Goals & Methods

The public job is to keep the yard honest. They keep blight off the next Cutting-leave, and they keep a copper-green scrap from being filed as a hug, so that a scrap which showed a colour is not written down as if no colour had come.

Inside the shed the fight is live, and it is about [[The Spent Leaf]]. Some clerks want it to stay a yard-fact, a colourless scrap that is logged, locked, and not Spoken. Some want it walked to a town that has waited a generation, so that the queue of towns waiting on wood cannot be called weather, as if the wait were only the land doing what land does. Both sides think they are tending the wood.

The methods stay small. A box may stay in the shed or it may travel, a clerk may be reassigned to the blight, and a Road-hand may say they needed the earth not to go empty. There is no hunt in any of that, and there is no relic-cult.

## How they look from the three corners

**Vaethorn.** A waiting hearth that hears of a colourless leaf wants it as proof the town was not unworthy, or as proof that it was. The Remainder will not bring the leaf in order to settle that guilt.

**Lestrand.** A viewing would have a price if anyone were willing to sell one, and the Seat has not set that price and has not sold the viewing. Book-hands have written to the yard, and the letters sit in the same cedar as the cloth.

**Threnmaieth.** A scrap the roll cannot colour is a gap in the record. A clerk who will not name that scrap to the Threnhael, the crown's roll, is doing the Seat's old job: the Tree witnessed the thing, and a ledger is a second, lesser seeing. What comes from the crown is pressure, not a raid.

## GM Notes

The clerks are in this position because of the job, not because anyone chose them for a prophecy. The people who empty the carrying-earth are the people who would find a spend-remainder, and that is a competence trap. Keep them excellent at jars and bad at politics.

The split want is the faction. This desk holds both wants at once, proof found or proof suppressed. The Seat's majority, the orthodox remainder, wants the leaf buried. Orthopraxy over orthodoxy is the peace they are keeping, because proof would make the skeptics into a column and would give Threnmaieth a fact to count. The show-it minority, some clerks and some Road-hands, wants the leaf walked. They want the scion-queue named as the rationing of a real reach, not as soil-science. Neither side is a dark court. Play the harm at ~5%: lose a box, refuse a leave, or reassign a clerk. Do not invent assassins.

This desk is not one of the three opposition groups, which are [[The Pourers]], [[The Walled Book]], and [[The Protectors]], and those three stay separate from each other as well as from this yard. Do not grow the Remainder into any of them, and do not let these clerks harvest fire or pour a god. The unused name *the Quiet Cut* meant stolen wood, and this desk does not steal.

If you need why the box matters, see [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real]]. The artifact and the tests live on [[The Spent Leaf]]. What happens if the square ever believes the leaf is on [[Is the Leaf-Mother Real#If confirmation lands]]. The show-it clerks are right that the queue is people. They are wrong if they think the leaf will shame the Seat into justice.

Do not sort the Kept with this office. A remainder-clerk who offers to tell you which hug was a miss is breaking the locked ache. Shut that down in the world of the scene. The leaf will not tell them, and the clerk who promised it was selling.
