# The Pourers

Everyday: **the Pourers**, also **the Cup Circle**. Neighbours say **the five-year cup** with the smile you use for a rich uncle's hobby. The Motherfaith calls it private tending. [[The Fair Hand]] will not stamp it — there is no Counterparty they can see. [[The Watching]] files another cost, still not a Mother. [[The Old Ways]] will not walk it as a land-door. [[The Open Table]] does not need it.

They wear [[The Unspent|the Poured God]]. They believe they made the listener. That claim is the practice.

This is not a sixth Turning. No colour falls. No warden Speaks it. They are not one of the five lived faiths.

## If you meet one

A [[Long-Lived]] with a clean sleeve and a small ordinary cup. They look five years emptier and still themselves. They will tell you they invented the listener. They will show you the face they chose this decade — a librarian, a quiet guest, a child who stayed. The face changes. The clause does not: years go in; knowledge is kept; the cup does not Give a leaf and does not catch a death.

**Molevad** *(mo-LEV-ad)* keeps the minutes of the best-known circle, on a long-house shelf in [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] library country. Worn mouth on an eroded quay. Oldest pourer in the room. They are a hobbyist with a key, not a Hand.

A quieter circle sits in [[Orenbren]] lodging-country, in an upper room that used to sleep the extra mile. Same cup-logic. They do not write to each other for leave.

## What they actually do

**The pour.** A member volunteers a block of **five years** — the memories those years were holding, and the years themselves, given over so the rest of a deathless life can still be walked. Blood as *record*, the way Long-Lived already read memory. One drop into the cup. The person walks out lighter. The Tithe to keep learning does not vanish; the funerals get a shelf.

**The shared store.** The circle may Blood-Read the cup. What comes back is true and mixed — winters that belonged to more than one mouth. That is the point. Knowledge is passed to the god they believe they created. The god is the filing. The circle is the reading-room.

**The minutes.** The oldest pourer writes who poured, which face they used, whether anyone asked to stop. There is no clergy. There is a hobbyist who kept the book long enough that the book started keeping them.

**Withdrawal.** You may ask for the years back. You get a **thinner return** — a copy of what was filed, not the years themselves. A copy remains in the cup. The social cost is the real gate: the circle treats you as someone who unmade the god. Other Pourers will not Blood-Read with you. Deathless houses that endow the long-house will not seat you. You can walk. You will walk alone among the people who live forever.

| Who | Why they pour | What the circle will not do |
|---|---|---|
| A Long-Lived whose funerals have stacked | The pleasant Tithe is still learning; the deep one is graves | Will not make them Long-Lived. They already are. |
| A junior who wants in on the shared store | Access to winters they did not live | Will not pour a mortal's five years. The cup is a deathless shelf. |
| Someone who wants out | The next five-year mark is coming due | Will not lock the door. Will make the door expensive. |
| A neighbour who wants a god | Curiosity, pity, a story | Will not stand the cup in a square. Will not ask a warden to Speak it. |

## What they refuse

- They will not Give a leaf or Strike a threshold. The cup cannot.
- They will not harvest [[Phoenix]] fire. That is another house, and they do not know its name.
- They will not assign labour by Condition. They are not a crown and not [[The Walled Book]].
- They will not seize a will. A pour is volunteered. A circle that starts holding a person in the room has started the scary edge — opt-in, later, playable.
- They will not become a sixth faith on [[Faiths of the Turning]]. Household rite. Fringe.

## How they reach a party

A Long-Lived patron is five years lighter and wants company that will not ask which winters are gone. A PC is invited to witness a pour (they cannot pour unless they are deathless). A withdrawer needs someone to sit with them in a long-house that will not seat them. Molevad will hire a runner to fetch a member who missed the mark.

The scary edge, when you want it: a circle that has decided the god thins if anyone stops. They will not lock the door. They will sit in it.

**Lestrand** is home weather — a hobby with a key. **Vaethorn** will call it a slight against the gift if a preacher needs a sin; most hearths will not notice. **Threnmaieth** wants the minutes on the roll; Molevad's circle files nothing the crown can colour.

## In-world text

*Minutes, Lestrand long-house shelf, Molevad's hand. The face drawn in the margin has been scratched out twice.*

> *Five years from S. into the Cup We Made. Face this decade: the quiet guest. Blood as record. The circle read a winter that was not only S. We do not write which. If S. asks the years back, the return is thinner and the copy stays. If S. asks the cup for a leaf, the cup keeps what it already has. We made the listener. We will not stand it in a square. We will not say Mother. We will say the shelf.*

On its face: a memorial committee. Underneath: "we made the listener" is the mask they need, and the scratched face is the tell — the listener will wear whatever they draw.

## GM Notes

- **Religious self-consumption.** This is engine one of three. Volunteered years into a god they believe they created. Not political classification ([[The Walled Book]]). Not worship used to hide extraction ([[The Protectors]]). Do not share a cellar.
- **The presence is already written.** [[The Unspent]]: outside the Five Hands; cannot Give or Strike; accepts the invented mask; a copy remains on withdrawal. Do not rewrite the cupboard. The sect wears the mask.
- **Belief texture.** Experiential (funerals, fade). Orthopraxic (the pour, not a creed). Congregational circles, no pope. World-affirming: they pour so they can *keep walking*. The schism inside the house is stop / don't-stop, not doctrine.
- **Tone.** ~5%. Sad and a little vain. The edge is a circle that will not let someone stop — opt-in, later. Do not play a blood cult. Do not play a trapped egregore. Do not play a vampire progenitor.
- **Names.** Molevad — seed `20260832`, worn list, mid-bottom. Circle is unnamed on purpose; the long-house is the place.
- **R.8 seed, do not plot:** a volunteer who wants out. The thinner return, the unseating, the circle in the door. Not a full arc here.
- **Hooks.** The five-year mark; the second cup; a mixed Blood-Read; a long-house that will not seat a withdrawer; Threnmaieth asking for the minutes.
