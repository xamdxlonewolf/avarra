# The Intake

> **What this is.** The shared **underdog office** of [[The Stillers]], [[The Hall-Keepers]], and [[The Element-Guilds]] — the mandatory guild-intake [[Law and Citizenship]] already named for dangerous Struck. Not clergy. Not a travelling circuit. Not [[The Slide]]. Built with `underdog-unit` and `moral-parallax` for [[Roadmap|Epic 5, Story 5.3]].

> **Canon status.** 🟡 **Proposed** (whether Intake is a reputation or a week you are assigned; the cant *catching-year*). 🔒 **Locked upstream:** dangerous Struck arrive raw; vouching-in is bound up with guild-intake; licences are competence, not a census. Recruits by practice / office / Condition-lean, **not Kind**. 🔒 **Do not clone:** Road-hands (they walk wood); the Slide (they own a week); recut lintel / novation / host-rights / the sentence that will not travel.

*(Everyday: **the Intake**, or **the raw-desk**, or **the shed.** Guild-cant, 🟡: **the catching-year.** There is no liturgical name. An office that the paying floor will not write on the wall does not get a holy one. [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] says **the late-catching**; [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] says **the emergency ticket**; [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] files **the unwitnessed warrant**.)*

**In one sentence:** they have a week to make a live hazard into a person the town will stand next to — and if they succeed, no one writes it down.

## Shape of the office (at a glance)

- **A door, not a road.** The Struck is brought *here*, or a runner arrives and a master is pulled off the paying floor. They are not a circuit. When they travel it is because the nearest ticket-house is three days and the rope is already in someone's hand — an accident of distance, not a route. [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hands]] already have the route.
- **Three ticket-houses, one shed-logic.** A raw Gaze, a raw throat, a raw Reach. Right house. Wrong house is how a Cry gets stilled quiet or an Ember is put on a bench to sing. The office is shared; the door is not.
- **They think they are in a first-aid story.** Catch. Steady. Hand to the year. They do not think they are in a redemption-play. The ~5% dial stays at the rope and the live Tithe — not at a monster-hunt.
- **Success is silence.** The guild leadership is **indifferent-to-hostile**. A quiet catching-year is a week the floor lost. A loud one is a charter hearing. No one converts. A grudging extra pair of hands is the most the yard will grant, and they will take them back.

## The underdog (so a table can drop a party here)

Built on the `underdog-unit` formula. Three or four constraints, not seven.

- **Mandate.** Take a raw Struck (Stilled / Far-Voiced / Answered) in hand *and* begin the apprenticeship at once, before the town meets them with a rope. The enemy is **time**, an already-live hazard, and a town that wants them *gone*. Mandate type: hot potato + new threat + a little cross-jurisdiction (guild charter vs town custom).
- **Constraints.**
  1. **Place:** the shed that does not face the street. Not the theatre, not the boards, not the paying shop.
  2. **Hands:** one master pulled off the floor, whoever will sit, maybe a Kept who has no Tithe of their own. The paying apprentices are not available.
  3. **Authority:** they can take the person *if the person will come*. They cannot overrule a frontier rope. They cannot stamp a ticket this week. The charter is at risk if the person harms someone *or* if they refuse the Struck and the town later says the guild left a hazard.
  4. **Weather:** leadership forgot them on a good day and will scapegoat them on a bad one. Support is **conditional** on silence.
- **Who ends up on it.** Not a Kind-unit. Not the floor's favourites. The master who can be spared (often the one who cannot). A Kept who will sit because they are *free* and the floor resents that. A Struck who remembers their own door. A junior ticket on probation. Formation is **assigned** or **inherited** — last year's leftovers — more often than recruited.
- **Victory.** The person is still alive, still here, and beginning to be *safe to stand next to*. The town pretends this was always going to happen. The guild does not apologize. Do not convert the institution. Do not give them a better shed because they earned it.

## What they actually do

### If you meet one

Tired. Chalk on a sleeve. A key to a door the slate does not name. You would miss them if you came in the front of the infirmary or the playhouse. You would find them if you asked *where the one who was stone yesterday went,* or if you were the runner.

They are useful the way a midwife is useful, except the birth is a later becoming and the town may prefer it not happen.

### Who comes, and when

| Who | Why they come | What Intake does | What they will not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| A raw [[The Stilled\|Stilled]] (they were stone) | Grey already filling; a rope in the square | Get them off the square. First drill. Reverse what can still be reversed. Sit the Gaze until it is a wound-hold, not a forever. | Will not stamp a ticket. Will not still the crowd as a lesson. |
| A raw [[Far-Voiced]] (they drowned) | Undertow already pulling; a Cry in the market | Get them into the hall's back room. First hour *with* a hall-keeper if one will sit. Vent on purpose before it vents them. | Will not open after the lamps as policy. Will not keep them half-full. |
| A raw [[The Answered\|Answered]] | An element let go later, untrained | Right door. Earnest use *now* that is not the crowded shop. | Will not put them on a hull the same day. Will not bench them. |
| A family, or a door-keeper, or a Book-hand | Someone must stand; someone wrote *owing* | Take the person. Ask who will [[Law and Citizenship\|vouch]] them *as what they are now*. | Will not sell a vouch. Will not call them Given. |
| The watch | The square wants them gone | Bargain for the shed instead of the rope. Sometimes lose. | Will not fight the town as a knightly order. They have a key, not a charter to command a street. |
| The paying floor | They want their master back | Finish the week or fail it. Return the master. | Will not promise this will not happen again. |

### An ordinary catching-year (it is not ordinary)

There is no ordinary week. There is a week.

1. Get them off the square, out of the water, away from the shop that is already wrong.
2. Name what is already true: *you carry a Gaze; you carry a voice; you carry a Reach.* Do not name a Mother unless the person does.
3. Find the first honest payment of the Tithe that is not a public hazard — a drill, an hour, a coal that is not the public forge.
4. Find someone who will stand for them *anew*. Intake is not a hiding-place from [[Law and Citizenship|the vouching seam]].
5. At week's end: still raw, beginning to be safe, handed to the real apprenticeship — or gone. Gone means the rope, the road, or a week-keeper who will not make them safe.

They write the catching-year on the **inside** of a ticket-blank, not on the face. The face stays empty until a real ticket is earned. A first-year who writes on the face is made to strike it.

### What they refuse (the job's edge)

- They will not stamp a ticket this week. Competence is not a week old.
- They will not become a circuit. If they must walk, it is toward *this* Struck, not along a route.
- They will not clone a Road-hand's cart, a Slide's book, or a Book-hand's novation.
- They will not rewrite Bound Terms. A raw Bound is a table's problem, not a shed's. The Whisper cannot command; they are not this office.
- They will not take a raw Taken-In, Returned, Two-Bodied, Long-Lived. No bystander ticket. Those doors have other houses.

## Who may sit the shed

**Not by Kind.**

**By practice first** — you sat a week and the person did not become a public story.

**Condition leans:**

## How they live in the three corners (and off the map)

- **[[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] (the late-catching).** The rope is real. The shed may be a barn. Soft injustice: once caught, the valley will spend them. The late-come are vouched graciously *and* put to work.
- **[[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] (the emergency ticket).** Posted, almost. A fee if anyone can pay it. The charter-risk is commercial. A raw Struck who cannot stamp a fee is still taken — the street-hazard demands it — and the house will remember the debt.
- **[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] (the unwitnessed warrant).** The crown wants the Struck *named* as they are caught. Intake's inside-writing is already a defiance. A quiet week looks like a leak. A loud week looks like incompetence. The darkest pressure: catch them for the channel.
- **[[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]].** A wreck walked uphill is a guest. Do not plant a shed on the sand.

## The week no one writes (the office's injustice)

The paying floor needs the master. The town needs the hazard gone. The Struck needs a year. Intake is given a week and a shed, and is blamed for all three.

If they succeed, the story becomes: *the guild trains its own.* If they fail, the story becomes: *the shed.* A kind Tree would not make the rope kind, and would not give them a better door. The becoming was unwitnessed. People built the square that prefers a rope.

They have no scion-queue, no gate-of-dirt, no scheduled hour as their *product*, no shop-you-cannot-leave (they want the person *able* to enter a shop). Do not clone those. This office's constraint is the week-and-the-silence — analog shed, borrowed key, authority that is a charter's fear.

## Economy of the office

- **Badly paid.** The parent house's leftover stipend. In kind: the shed, the food, the master's lost week.
- **Not Tithe-infrastructure.** They do not furnish a commons. They do not sell an hour. They catch.
- **Not the Slide.** A failed week may feed a week-keeper. That is a tragedy the floor will call weather.

## In-world text

*A catching-year slip, written on the inside of a blank ticket, stillers' shed, core town. The face is empty. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *Inside only. Gaze, raw. Came from stone on the third night. Stood for by the sister and by N. who will not give a family name. First drill held. No forever. No ticket. If the week ends quiet, burn this. If it does not, the charter already knows. We do not face the street. We do not write the wall. If they have already been a rope-story, this is still the shed — come if they will come. If they will not, we cannot make a town take them. The Mother does not reach twice by chance. Neither do we. That line is the wardens'. Strike it if you must. I have not.*

On its face: a competent catching, a little desperate, a college's shame in a folded paper. Underneath: the office stealing the Seat's sentence because it is the only heavy line the writer knows, and a blindness they cannot afford — that "burn this" is how success becomes the guild's ordinary story and the Struck's becoming stays unwitnessed *twice*. The empty face is the point. The sister stood. N. would not be named. Both are the vouching seam in one slip.

## GM Notes
*(Stripped on player-safe export. Keyed to the keystone via `reveals: [keystone-adjacent]`.)*

- **Underdog check.** Mandate + 3–4 constraints + outcast sitters + no institutional conversion. Do not add a sunset clause *and* hostile leadership *and* no tools *and* a cursed shed. The organic logic: the floor wants paying work; the charter wants no headlines; the town wants the hazard gone. Those three are enough.
- **They do not know what story they are in.** Defaults: monster squad, nice Slide, Road-hands in a different cart. Rotate. Form stays first-aid / catching (load-bearing). They want a quiet week. They do not want a client, a relic, or a route.
- **Moral parallax.** Distances: social (raw is *not our apprentice*) and informational (success is burned). Engine: exchange — every catching-week is a master the floor did not have — and inheritance — Given children got a childhood the Struck will never get. Comfort: the words *quiet* and *shed*. R2: she did not build the rope.
- **The inside writing is inscrutable-enough.** Practical (the face must stay empty) and a little shame. Do not make it a secret liturgy.
- **Do not take Bound / Taken-In / Returned / Two-Bodied / Long-Lived.** Wrong office. Bound is a table. Green is a lot. Purpose cannot be furnished. Two-Bodied need no ticket.
- **Travel is accidental, not a circuit.** A runner toward *this* person. If you find them on the road every session, you have cloned Thaelvaeth.
- **Taste still open.** Assigned vs inherited sitters; whether *catching-year* stays; whether "burn this" is custom or one master's habit.
- **No Kind-gate. No Kumbaan shed. No Intake-pope.**
- **Hooks.** A rope already in the hand; a runner three days out; a master the plague theatre cannot spare; a Book-hand's *owing* that walks into the shed; a door-keeper and Intake in the same week (kinship vs ticket); a slip that was not burned; a crown-clerk who wants the inside writing copied; a PC who is the raw throat.
