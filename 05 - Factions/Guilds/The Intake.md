# The Intake

The Intake is a shed, and it works to a plain rule. It gets a newly Struck person, someone the change took outside the childhood ceremony and who is not yet safe to stand next to, into a state where the town does not have to reach for a rope.

Everyday speech is **the Intake**, or **the shed**, and the cant, the in-house word, is **the catching-year**. Vaethorn calls it **the late-catching**, Lestrand calls it **the emergency ticket**, and Threnmaieth calls it **the unwitnessed warrant**, because the crown treats a change nobody witnessed as a thing that still needs a name. A person has a week. In that week the hazard is live and the town wants the person gone, and if the week works, the slip is burned.

## What the Intake has

The Intake has four things, and no more.

1. It has a place, the shed that does not face the street.
2. It has hands: one master the floor can spare, a Kept person who carries no Condition and will sit with the Struck, and last year's leftovers from the yard.
3. It has a narrow authority. They can take the person if the person will come, and they cannot overrule a rope or stamp a ticket this week.
4. It has a thin reputation. The guild leadership has forgotten them, so a quiet year is only a week the floor lost a master, and a loud year is a hearing.

The enemy is time. The Gaze, the Cry, or the Reach is already live, a Stilled look, a Far-Voiced outbreak, or an Answered element answering raw, and sending the person to the wrong house is how a Cry gets stilled quiet, as if a voice were a thing the yard could hold still.

## The week

They get the person off the square and name what is already true, and then they help the person make a first honest payment of the Tithe, the upkeep the change demands, that is not a public hazard. They find someone who will [[Law and Citizenship|vouch]] for the person *as what they are now*, which means someone who will stand up and say they know the person as they are now. At the end of the week the person is either still raw and beginning to be safe, or gone. Gone means the rope, the road, or a week-keeper, a person who will keep them by the week when the shed could not.

The slip is written on the **inside** of a ticket-blank, and the face of the blank is left empty. A first-year who writes on the face is made to strike it out.

| Who | Where they go | What the Intake will not do |
|---|---|---|
| Raw [[The Stilled\|Stilled]] | A first drill, and the hold does not outlast the scene. | Stamp a ticket, or still a crowd as a lesson. |
| Raw [[Far-Voiced]] | The back room of the hall, so the person can vent on purpose. | Open that room after the lamps as a policy. |
| Raw [[The Answered\|Answered]] | The right element door, for earnest use that is not the crowded shop. | Put them on a hull the same day. |
| Family, a door-keeper, or a Book-hand | The Intake takes the person and asks who will stand for them. | Sell a vouch, or call the person Given. |
| The watch | The Intake bargains with them for the shed, and sometimes loses. | Fight the town. |

The shed does not take a Bound person, because that contract is a table and not a body-ticket, or a Taken-In person, because that upkeep is a lot. It does not take a Returned person, who died and came back, a Two-Bodied person, who carries a second body, or a Long-Lived person, whose aging has stopped. It issues no ticket for a bystander, someone who was only standing nearby.

It is not a circuit. If its people walk, they walk toward one particular Struck person, because the [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hands]] already have the road.

## Who sits

The shed does not choose sitters by Kind. A person sits in the shed for a week so that the Struck person does not become a public story. The sitters are a ticket-holder for that hazard, there to cover the live Tithe; a Kept person, to sit; a Struck person who remembers the door; and a runner who can run.

## Other houses

The parent yards are [[The Stillers]], the hall, and [[The Element-Guilds]]. They pretend the shed is not on their wall, even in a week when they have lent it the master.

If the week fails, [[The Slide]] takes the overflow. The Intake wants the person safe enough to leave, and the Slide wants the person not fallen enough to stay.

[[The Book-Hands]] may send someone. A hall that is not there still counts as an *owing*, a debt written down even though the hall was never there.

[[The Door-Keepers]] may name the person kin the same week. Kinship is not a ticket. They are two separate courtesies, and either one can happen without the other.

[[The Standing Trade]] will sell a witness who says they watched the person. The Intake will not.

In Vaethorn the rope is real, and once the person is caught the valley will spend them. In Lestrand there is a fee if anyone can pay, and the street hazard still takes the person when nobody can. In Threnmaieth the crown wants the person named as they are caught, so writing on the inside of the blank is already a defiance. On Kumbaan a wreck is a guest, and there is no shed on the sand.

## The week no one writes

The floor needs the master back on the paying work, the town needs the hazard gone, and the Struck person needs a year while the shed has a week. If the Intake succeeds, the story told afterwards is that the guild trains its own. If it fails, the story is the shed.

## Slip (inside of a blank ticket)

*What follows is written on the inside of a blank ticket. The face of the blank is empty on purpose.*

> *Inside only. Gaze, raw. Third night. Stood for by the sister and by N. who will not give a family name. First drill held. The hold does not last. No ticket. If the week ends quiet, burn this. If it does not, the charter already knows. We do not face the street. If they will not come, we cannot make a town take them.*

The empty face of the blank is deliberate. When the slip is burned, the success becomes the guild's ordinary story, and the person's becoming was never written down in public.

## GM Notes

The guild is terse on purpose. It is the underdog among the Tithe houses: a mandate, four limits, and no institutional conversion. Do not convert the guild into a bigger one. Do not add a sunset clause and a cursed shed in the same breath, as if the smallness needed a curse to matter.

They want a quiet week. They do not want a client, a relic, or a route. The distance a table can feel is that success is burned, so the town never has to look at the week, and that a Given child got a childhood of training the Struck person will not get. Travel is accidental. If you find them on the road every session, you have cloned the Road-hands, who already walk.

Do not clone the Slide. The Intake wants the person safe enough to leave, and the Slide wants them not fallen enough to stay. There is no Kind-gate, no Kumbaan shed, and no Intake-pope. The cant name *the catching-year* is not settled.
