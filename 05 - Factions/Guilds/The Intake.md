# The Intake

Shed notice. Not a liturgy.

Everyday: **the Intake**, **the shed.** Cant: **the catching-year** 🟡. Vaethorn: **the late-catching.** Lestrand: **the emergency ticket.** Threnmaieth: **the unwitnessed warrant.**

A week. A live hazard. A town that wants them gone. If it works, burn the slip.

## At the table

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | Quiet. The person still here, beginning to be safe to stand next to. |
| **Have** | A borrowed shed, one master pulled off the floor, a key the slate does not name. |
| **Fear** | The rope. The charter hearing. A loud week. |
| **Live conflict** | The paying floor wants the master back. The watch wants the square clear. The Struck needs a year. They have a week. |
| **Hooks** | A rope already in the hand; a runner three days out; a slip that was not burned; a Book-hand's *owing* that walks into the shed. |

## Mandate (keep it to four)

1. **Place.** The shed that does not face the street.
2. **Hands.** One master the floor can spare. A Kept who will sit. Last year's leftovers.
3. **Authority.** They can take the person if the person will come. They cannot overrule a rope. They cannot stamp a ticket this week.
4. **Weather.** Leadership forgot them. A quiet year is a week the floor lost. A loud one is a hearing. Do not convert the guild.

Enemy: time. Already-live Gaze, throat, or Reach. Wrong house is how a Cry gets stilled quiet.

## The week

Get them off the square. Name what is already true. First honest payment of the Tithe that is not a public hazard. Find someone who will [[Law and Citizenship|vouch]] them *as what they are now*. Week's end: still raw, beginning to be safe — or gone. Gone means the rope, the road, or a week-keeper.

Write on the **inside** of a ticket-blank. Face stays empty. A first-year who writes on the face is made to strike it.

Not this shed: Bound (a table), Taken-In (a lot), Returned, Two-Bodied, Long-Lived. No bystander ticket.

Not a circuit. If they walk, it is toward *this* Struck. [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hands]] already have the route.

## Who sits

Not by Kind. By a week the person did not become a public story.

Ticket-holder of *that* hazard for the live Tithe. A Kept for the sitting. A Struck who remembers the door. A runner who can run.

## Other houses

Parent yards: [[The Stillers]] · the hall door · [[The Element-Guilds]]. They pretend the shed is not on the wall.

[[The Slide]] takes overflow if the week fails. Intake wants them *safe enough to leave.* The Slide wants them *not-fallen enough to stay.* Do not clone.

[[The Book-Hands]] may send them. A hall that is not here is still an *owing*.

[[The Door-Keepers]] may name them kin the same week. Kinship is not a ticket. Two courtesies.

[[The Standing Trade]] will sell a mouth. Intake will not.

Vaethorn: the rope is real; once caught, the valley will spend them. Lestrand: a fee if anyone can pay; the street-hazard still takes them. Threnmaieth: the crown wants them named as they are caught; inside-writing is already a defiance. Kumbaan: a wreck is a guest. No shed on the sand.

## The week no one writes

The floor needs the master. The town needs the hazard gone. The Struck needs a year. If they succeed, the story becomes *the guild trains its own.* If they fail, the story becomes *the shed.*

## Slip (inside of a blank ticket)

> *Inside only. Gaze, raw. Third night. Stood for by the sister and by N. who will not give a family name. First drill held. No forever. No ticket. If the week ends quiet, burn this. If it does not, the charter already knows. We do not face the street. If they will not come, we cannot make a town take them.*

The empty face is the point. "Burn this" is how success becomes the guild's ordinary story and the becoming stays unwitnessed twice.

## GM Notes

- **Terse on purpose.** Underdog formula kept: mandate + four constraints + no institutional conversion. Do not add a sunset clause *and* a cursed shed.
- They want a quiet week. They do not want a client, a relic, or a route.
- Moral parallax: success is burned; Given children got a childhood the Struck will not. R2: she did not build the rope.
- Travel is accidental. If you find them on the road every session, you have cloned the Road-hands.
- No Kind-gate. No Kumbaan shed. No Intake-pope.
