# The Intake

The Intake is a shed, and it works to a plain rule: it gets a newly Struck person safe to stand next to before the town reaches for a rope.

Everyday: **the Intake**, **the shed.** Cant: **the catching-year**. Vaethorn calls it **the late-catching.** Lestrand calls it **the emergency ticket.** Threnmaieth calls it **the unwitnessed warrant.**

A person has a week. The hazard is live, and the town wants the person gone. If the week works, the slip is burned.

## What the Intake has

The Intake has four things, and no more.

1. **A place.** The shed that does not face the street.
2. **Hands.** One master the floor can spare, a Kept person who will sit with the Struck, and last year's leftovers from the yard.
3. **Authority.** They can take the person if the person will come. They cannot overrule a rope. They cannot stamp a ticket this week.
4. **A thin reputation.** The guild leadership has forgotten them. A quiet year is a week the floor lost a master. A loud year is a hearing.

The enemy is time. The Gaze, the Cry, or the Reach is already live, and sending the person to the wrong house is how a Cry gets stilled quiet.

## The week

They get the person off the square. They name what is already true. They help the person make a first honest payment of the Tithe that is not a public hazard. They find someone who will [[Law and Citizenship|vouch]] for the person *as what they are now*. At the end of the week the person is either still raw and beginning to be safe, or gone. Gone means the rope, the road, or a week-keeper.

The slip is written on the **inside** of a ticket-blank, and the face of the blank is left empty. A first-year who writes on the face is made to strike it out.

| Who | Where they go | What the Intake will not do |
|---|---|---|
| Raw [[The Stilled\|Stilled]] | First drill. No promise of forever. | Stamp a ticket. Still a crowd as a lesson. |
| Raw [[Far-Voiced]] | The back room of the hall, to vent on purpose. | Open the room after the lamps as a policy. |
| Raw [[The Answered\|Answered]] | The right element door, for earnest use that is not the crowded shop. | Put them on a hull the same day. |
| Family, door-keeper, or Book-hand | The Intake takes the person and asks who will stand for them. | Sell a vouch. Call the person Given. |
| The watch | The Intake bargains with them for the shed, and sometimes loses. | Fight the town. |

The shed does not take a Bound (that is a table), a Taken-In (that is a lot), a Returned, a Two-Bodied, or a Long-Lived. It issues no ticket for a bystander.

It is not a circuit. If its people walk, they walk toward one particular Struck. The [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hands]] already have the road.

## Who sits

Not by Kind. A person sits in the shed for a week so that the person does not become a public story.

The sitters are a ticket-holder for that hazard, to cover the live Tithe; a Kept person to sit; a Struck person who remembers the door; and a runner who can run.

## Other houses

The parent yards are [[The Stillers]], the hall, and [[The Element-Guilds]]. They pretend the shed is not on their wall.

[[The Slide]] takes the overflow if the week fails. The Intake wants the person safe enough to leave. The Slide wants the person not fallen enough to stay.

[[The Book-Hands]] may send someone. A hall that is not there still counts as an *owing*.

[[The Door-Keepers]] may name the person kin the same week. Kinship is not a ticket. They are two separate courtesies.

[[The Standing Trade]] will sell a witness who says they watched the person. The Intake will not.

In Vaethorn the rope is real, and once the person is caught the valley will spend them. In Lestrand there is a fee if anyone can pay, and the street hazard still takes the person. In Threnmaieth the crown wants the person named as they are caught, so writing on the inside of the blank is already a defiance. On Kumbaan a wreck is a guest, and there is no shed on the sand.

## The week no one writes

The floor needs the master. The town needs the hazard gone. The Struck needs a year. If the Intake succeeds, the story told afterwards is *the guild trains its own.* If it fails, the story is *the shed.*

## Slip (inside of a blank ticket)

> *Inside only. Gaze, raw. Third night. Stood for by the sister and by N. who will not give a family name. First drill held. No forever. No ticket. If the week ends quiet, burn this. If it does not, the charter already knows. We do not face the street. If they will not come, we cannot make a town take them.*

The empty face of the blank is deliberate. When the slip is burned, the success becomes the guild's ordinary story, and the person's becoming was never written down in public.

## GM Notes

- **Terse on purpose.** The guild is the underdog among the Tithe houses: a mandate, four limits, and no institutional conversion. Do not convert the guild into a bigger one. Do not add a sunset clause *and* a cursed shed.
- They want a quiet week. They do not want a client, a relic, or a route.
- Moral parallax: success is burned; Given children got a childhood the Struck will not.
- Travel is accidental. If you find them on the road every session, you have cloned the Road-hands.
- Do not clone the Slide: the Intake wants the person safe enough to leave, the Slide wants them not fallen enough to stay.
- No Kind-gate. No Kumbaan shed. No Intake-pope.
- The cant name *the catching-year* is not settled.
