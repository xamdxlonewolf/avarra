# Vaelhesk

> **The Far Yield.** *(formal *Vaelhesk*, VAEL-hesk · [[The Old Tongue|vael]] in the older sense, "what the land yields" + *hesk-* "far/outer edge.")* The coinage is worn, the way frontier names wear. Devotion to the Motherfaith is middling. Devotion to the Old Ways, the ancestor-practice of doors and first meals, is high. Reach is low, and the government is light.

People say **the Far Yield** and **the Yield**, while charters and learned maps write *Vaelhesk*, and saying it that way keeps the spoken name clear of *Vaethorn*.

## Overview (public)

Vaelhesk is the pre-graft host-lands on [[Heskoren]]: old greens, doors kept for ancestors, and a first meal before anyone is allowed to leave with something of the place. The Mother's wood, the grafted Tree of the Motherfaith, is a **guest** on that older ground. A Cutting-leave, the Seat's permission to take living wood, is a claiming if it happens without the meal. [[The Door-Keepers]] walk here as Kin-speakers, people who speak for the kin the door is kept for. The land is the seat, not a college, and it does not need a college in order to be owed a meal.

The Struck, people who came into the change later than a child's standing at a Tree, and [[The Taken-In]], people whose life is the living green, are read here as the land's own children. A neighbour's week, the walk to a Motherfaith Tree somewhere else, can be refused on purpose, and what the refusal says is that the child is already the hill's. Vaethorn reads those same children as Kept-for-want, passed over because a gift was scarce. The disagreement is what decides whether a child travels.

**Site.** The country is Heskoren's old greens, and not only the live front where new grafts are still arriving. Its guest-grove is [[The First Bowl]], which folk call *Lonasir*, and the bowl is not a capital, because the land itself is the seat. There is no new city and no new name, and the villages and hamlets stay on the old greens. A traveler who asks for the seat is pointed at a door, and the First Bowl stays the guest-grove it already is.

**How it formed.** These were animist and ancestor polities, answering to the land and to the dead, and they predate the wave of grafts. When the grafts arrived, across Cut-year 200 to Cut-year 387 and still arriving, some districts became Vaethorn, and these districts kept the door. The state stays light because the land does not need a clerk in order to be owed a meal. [[The Thaw-Break]] moved guests off flooded ground and up to high doors. [[The Hinge Hush]] does not write Vaelhesk into its clauses, because a first meal is not a landing-due. Netstrand crews who skip the meal learn that, and they learn it without a treaty.

**What they remember.** The host-right is older than any Cut-year, and a waiting-gospel that came later called the refusal a deprivation. The chronicle of that argument is [[The Other Count]].

## Structure & Membership

Hearth-clusters, door-keepers, and the custom of the host-right are the membership. The land does not recruit by Kind, the ancestry a person is born with. The Taken-In are densest here as frontier labor and as kin. They are still not a flag.

## Goals & Methods

They feed the first meal, and they name a Struck person at the door that made them. A warden who arrives with a pot is a guest if they sat down for the meal, and a thief if they took the wood without sitting.

**Its injustice.** The harm is **host-rights used as a claim that can shun**: refuse the meal, take the wood, and you are not a neighbour. A Road-hand working to the Seat's clock, carrying out a sentence the Seat already wrote, can become a trespasser for doing it. The kindness has a threshold, the wrong side of that threshold is real, and the injustice stays local rather than turning into a holy war.

## GM Notes

Play the guest-grove with the Door-Keepers and with Nethiro at Vaelun. Leave the districts unfleshed, do not make a Taken-In nation, and do not clone a second Harrow or [[Harrow's Green]]. The land is the seat, so do not name a city in order to tidy the refusal of a capital, do not write a new place-note, and do not give the refusal a person. Villages and hamlets stay on the old greens, and a traveler who asks where the seat is gets pointed at a door.

**The Old Ways may be half-right for the wrong reasons.** The caution lives in [[Heskoren]] GM Notes, so do not confirm the half-rightness, and do not dissolve the host-rights when the keystone lands.

**Hearths, not nations.** The Taken-In are honoured here, and the Kinds around them are mixed.

**The vael/vaeth pair.** One name is the yield and the other is the waiting, two powers on the same continent that already oppose each other in the first syllable. A scholar may notice the pair. Vaethorn and Vaelhesk feel the split whether or not anyone at the table glosses it.

**Hooks.** A first meal can come before a Cutting-leave at [[The First Bowl]], and a neighbour's week can be refused. Do not sicken that guest-grove in order to merge it with another kind of town, because the sick-Tree is [[The Mill-hold]]. The three hamlets past the ford can hold an Old Ways hearth among Motherfaith ones.
