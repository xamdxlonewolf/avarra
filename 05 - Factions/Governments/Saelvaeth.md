# Saelvaeth

> **The Sown Waiting.** *(formal *Saelvaeth*, SAEL-vaeth · [[The Old Tongue|sael]] "sowing" + [[The Old Tongue|vaeth]] "the waiting.")* The name is worn the way frontier names wear. [[Harrow's Green]] sits in the march's orbit, and it does not sit as the capital.

People say **the Sown Waiting** and **March-folk**. Charters and learned maps write *Saelvaeth*. The shared *sael* with [[Saelthael|the Sown Tree]] stays visible in the writing. Speech keeps the two powers apart.

## Overview (public)

This is the live front of the Grafting on [[Heskoren]], the still-moving planting of Trees, from Cut-year 200 to Cut-year 387, and the front is still incomplete. Towns form where a graft has taken. Between those towns are waiting clusters who can see a neighbour's canopy and do not yet have one of their own. [[Harrow's Green]] is the luckiest kind of square in this march, a Hand that grew up, meaning a town Tree that took and can now do the week's work. [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] can see that green. They were written as remainder, the part left over, under the folk names *Brenod · Vaelun · Ornath*, and the Seat still does not use those names. The queue is visible from the ground, because people can see who is waiting. [[Ornsael]] on [[Saelthael]] is the same sowing on the origin continent.

Governance sits mid-low, because *someone* has to stand and answer for the sentence already passed on Harrow's Green. Wardens do that, Road-hands pass through with pots of living wood, and a march-voice speaks that did not exist when the first pots came. It is not a tall state, and it is enough state to be blamed.

**Site.** The march is Heskoren's grafted pockets on the moving edge. It is a live-front Tree-town, and it hosts the neighbour's week, the walk to a Tree that can do the work, out of necessity rather than devotion ([[The Years of Hands]]).

**Seat.** [[Natai]] is the town where the march-voice stands. Other towns where a graft has taken stay unnamed, and so do the hamlets and waiting clusters between them. [[Harrow's Green]] stays as it is and stays out of this seat, and [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] stay the three hamlets.

**How it formed.** The wave of planting reached here in this decade. Who is next for a scion, a cutting of living wood, depends on who can pay, whose soil will take it, and who is next in the chain on a list written in the Motherwood. The polity is that luck and that queue. [[The Grey Summer]] thinned the supply-line for a decade, and [[The Thaw-Break]] turned the neighbour's week into worse mud. [[The Hinge Hush]] wrote one mercy into the peace: this march may take a pot without paying a Vaethorn waiting-due. Vaethorn will preach that the march is unworthy of what it received. The history of the planting does not agree.

**What they remember.** The queue is the border. Harrow's luck is politics, not a reward for having waited more faithfully than the hamlets. The mercy in the clause does not move them up the scion-queue. The chronicle is [[The Other Count]].

## Structure & Membership

Town-wardens keep the squares, and Turnings are borrowed, a child's standing done where a Tree exists because the waiting cluster does not have one yet. Road-hands sleep here on the way to a town that was not chosen for the next pot. The march does not recruit by Kind, the ancestry a person is born with. [[The Old Ways]] are neighbours, which is [[Vaelhesk]], and they are not a museum of a dead practice. Vaethorn's heat sits on many hearths as gospel, and it does not always sit there as a flag.

## Goals & Methods

They keep the grafts that have taken alive, they host the neighbour's week, they argue for the next pot, and they survive being the sentence the Seat has already written.

**Its injustice.** The harm is **the queue as a border**, and Harrow's luck is politics rather than a blessing earned by waiting. The hamlets can see the canopy and send their children anyway, and spreading Trees did not make the march kind.

## GM Notes

Leave the districts unfleshed, and do not hand this march the next scion as a reward for anything it has done. [[Harrow's Green]] and [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] are the playable squares in the orbit, and they are still not a capital. The seat is [[Natai]], and Harrow's stays out of it.

**Do not make Harrow's the capital.** Harrow's stays a town in the orbit, the seat stays [[Natai]], and Saelvaeth is the march around both.

**Do not make Harrow's the good town that waited faithfully.** What happened there is horticulture plus a queue, and the fuller caution lives in [[Harrow's Green]] GM Notes.

**Pair with Saelthael.** One power is leftover walking on the origin continent, and the other is live-front planting, which is the same sowing running on two clocks.

**Fate-pressure** on the Seat, on the Road-hands, and on the waiting towns is noted, and it is not rolled into a result.

**Hooks.** A borrowed Turning can end with the witness walking home, a warden can want a stone moved, and a pot meant for someone else can sleep in Harrow's upper room. The seam at the ford can put Saelvaeth's luck, Vaelhesk's refusal, Vaethorn's guilt, and Ornled's slate into the same week.
