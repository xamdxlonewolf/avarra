# Leddvael

> **The Reckoned Gift.** *(formal *Leddvael*, LEDD-vael · [[The Old Tongue|ledd]] "to reckon" + [[The Old Tongue|vael]] "gift.")* Eroded drift. Little Motherfaith devotion; [[The Fair Hand]] is worn as civil law. **They do not rewrite Bound Terms.**

People say **the Reckoned Gift** and **Gift-clerks**. Charters and learned maps write *Leddvael*. The formal name preserves the doctrine: a gift is a bargain priced.

## Overview (public)

A signing-coast on [[Strandoren]] that took [[The Fair Hand]] from a dockside theology to a **civic rite**. Leaf-Fall is a festival *and* a signing-watch. Tithe is a term serviced. The [[Bound]] are model citizens here — not feared, *employed* as the shape of honesty. Mid governance: the book is a kind of statute without quite becoming a census of souls. They will not copy [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]]'s list. They will file a contract.

**Site.** A different stretch of indented coast than Lestrand — enough water to have its own houses. It is a salt quay with a signing-watch. Fair Hand clerks in the same wind as Motherfaith tree-tenders. The held prestige berth stays on the Ledger Coast.

**Seat.** [[Naenor]] is the port, slightly smaller than [[Orentel]], where the signing-watch sits. It competes with the Ledger Coast. It wins the term and loses the berth. Towns and villages along the rest of that coast stay unnamed. The [[The Book-Hands|Book-Hands]] still have no seat of their own, and they still do not rewrite Terms. Naenor is not a second Orentel.

**How it formed.** Fortune-and-contract practice thickening until a charter-coast found it cheaper to *establish* the Settling than to argue hymns per dock. After [[The Grey Summer]] the witnesses had died and a Bound's word still closed; the civic rite is that year made a seal. No prophet founded it. A habit became a statute.

**What they remember.** A Given child is a term opened. In the Grey Summer, honesty was in short supply, and the Bound were the people who kept it. Chronicle: [[The Other Count]].

## Structure & Membership

Charter-council plus book-tables. [[The Book-Hands]] stamp and recite; they have **no seat** and **do not rewrite Terms**. Recruits not by Kind. Gift-reading families live here; they are the minority that sounds devout and looks like a credit risk.

## Goals & Methods

Keep signings clear. Vouch the Struck briskly as a later contract. Treat Motherfaith gift-talk as unpriced language — allowed, not preferred.

**Its injustice.** **Bargain-theology as statute.** A Given child is a term opened. A household that insists the colour was a gift is a bad debtor in a signing-town. The Bound are safe and *over-read* — the world's contract-pillar asked to be the world's whole person. No villain-faith; a sincere ledger laid over real Conditions.

## GM Notes

- Do not flesh districts.
- Do not clone the [[The Book-Hands]] as a state church.
- Seat is [[Naenor]]. Do not move the signing-watch onto Orentel. Do not give the Book-Hands a seat. Do not census the rest of that coast.

- **The Fair Hand is not villain-theology** ([[Strandoren]] GM Notes). Leddvael is that reading made civic. Wrong about *who* (no bargain was struck); sincere; useful; crushing in the specific case.
- **Do not rewrite Bound Terms.** Book-hands' lock travels with the polity.
- **Theology knob.** Low *gift-devotion*, high rival practice. Do not invent a fourth axis.
- **Hooks.** A gift-reading family at a signing-watch; a Bound whose Terms are recited in the square as civic theatre; a Motherfaith warden and a Book-hand sharing a Leaf-Fall; someone vouched as a contract who wanted a blessing.
