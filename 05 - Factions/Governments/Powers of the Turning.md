# Powers of the Turning

> The fifteen great powers stand across the three large continents. Each one has a common spoken handle for play and, where the old form still survives, an Old-Tongue name, the liturgical form a charter, a rite, or a learned map will use.

> At the table, speak the common name and write the liturgical name. Say *the Night Shore* and *the Far Yield*. A factor's docket may write *Netstrand* and *Vaelhesk*. [[The Hinge Shore]] has no current liturgical form, and *Eolstrand* is the retired name of that slot.

## The count

**Fifteen** stand on the three large continents. **[[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] is not a sixteenth power.** It is an isolate with a table, a moon, and a storm-wall, a people at home on their own island, and it is not a flag on the Seat's list.

Beneath the fifteen the world still swarms with marches, free towns, petty Tree-seats where a town answers to its own Turning Tree, and wild that no polity has claimed. The figure "~15" names the powers worth naming at this table, and it is not a count of every place that exists.

| **[[Maiethorn]]** | full | **6** | The old heart, a clutch of kingdoms competing for the grove and the Old Crossing. |
| **[[Strandoren]]** | high | **5** | A constellation of coasts and compacts, with no single market wearing a crown. |
| **[[Heskoren]]** | thin | **4** | Fewer, poorer, and scattered, with a long wild between the Trees. |
| **[[The Sundering Isle]]** | near-none | **0** | The Isle is not on this list. |

## How a power is named

The lever is the same one [[Naming in the Turning]] uses: the name a polity gives itself shows the stance it has taken. The root sits in [[The Old Tongue]], and the sound then drifts in the direction of that polity's theology and its reach.

- **Conservative** speech, the pious speech of [[Maiethorn]], keeps *th* and the long vowels, which is why the Mother-names cluster in this country. [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] counts her, and [[Maiethvael]] gives in her name and will not count.
- **Worn** speech, on [[Heskoren]], is what ordinary talk does to a *th* that follows a vowel. The *th* wears down to *t*, and careful prayer puts the *th* back when the prayer is said. The frontier coinage *hesk-* is younger than the liturgy that still keeps the older sounds. The name written [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] is spoken *Wetorn*, and [[Ornled]] lets the old ending go.
- **Eroded** speech, on [[Strandoren]], takes *th* to *t* or drops it, and clips the ends of words. [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] is still the most drifted of the old three, and [[Trenledd]] is what happens when *thren* itself wears down to a roll.

Pronunciation on this page: **CAPS** mark the stress, `ae` is "ay", and `th` is the *th* of *thin*. New liturgical names are frozen. [[The Old Tongue]] owns the closed root families, and [[14 - Assets/Names/README|Naming Tools]] stores the generator that can reproduce a name and the gate that catches a collision.

## The fifteen at a glance

The table reads on three axes. **T** is theology, which here means Motherfaith gift-devotion, how far a power gives itself to the Mother's gift. **R** is reach, how far that power stretches. **G** is governance, how tightly the power is ruled. A filled mark, ●, is high, a half mark, ◐, is mid, and an empty mark, ○, is low. The three corners that were worked out first stay the reference points, and the other twelve are read between them. This is not a finished catalogue of the axes.

The three corners [[Polity Archetypes]] left unbuilt are on the map now. **Maiethvael** is devout, rich, and lightly governed, **Trenledd** keeps a surveillance that has no faith to sanctify it, and **Ornled** is the secular frontier [[Heskoren]] is required to have.

## How they sit (network, not a painted map)

**[[Maiethorn]]** holds six of the fifteen, on the old heart, the country of the grove.

The Tallied Crown sits coiled near the sacred centre because it wants everyone written onto the [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]], the roll it keeps and calls care. Its seat is [[Maiethlir]]. The Hand there, the town's Turning Tree, stands on the Core-thaw, the river that Maiethlir sits on, and that Hand is not the grove.

The Gift-Realm shares the Mother, the one the hymns address, and refuses the list. It sings the same hymns Threnmaieth sings, and it keeps the opposite paper. Its capital is [[Seinbrun]].

The Witness-Hearths are the lodging-country of the Near Mile, and the rule there is civic rather than clerical. The [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]] sits in the Motherwood beside them, and it is not their crown. Their capital is [[Rothallo]], still called the Inner Close, and it stands inside Orenbren. [[The Third Hearth]] is one lodging-hearth on that road. [[The Mill-hold]] is a Hands-town two days past Brenthael, and its Hand is unsound this year, so the Mill-hold is not a capital. [[Nelath]] is a spur that leaves the Mile and then stops, and it is neither a capital nor the seat.

The Sown Tree is the leeward east, still walking west after the core stopped. Its city is [[Larbril]], and [[Ornsael]] stays the smaller well-town.

[[The Hinge Shore]] faces [[Strandoren]] across [[Named Ground|the Old Crossing]], and it classifies the hull at [[Votaer]].

The Thaw-Land holds [[Named Ground|the Rain-Wall]], and its town is [[Tasain]], below the Shelf-gate. Fox-of-the-Snows hearths and [[Tengu]] hearths are densest on that snow. Tengu are the crow-born of the high country, and neither they nor the Fox-of-the-Snows have that snow as their country.

**[[Strandoren]]** holds five, sitting along coasts and compacts, with no single crown over them.

[[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] is the premier coast, and its seat is [[Orentel]], on the Salt Quay. The White Note keeps its house on the third quay, and that house is not the crown.

[[Brenledd]], the Hearth-League, sits its council at [[Raitin]], and the league stays a league of hearths rather than becoming one house.

[[Leddvael]], the Reckoned Gift, keeps the signing-watch at [[Naenor]], which is the watch it keeps on the signings.

[[Trenledd]], the Worn Count, keeps its roll at [[Lunbra]], on the Chart-run, and the person who governs Trenledd stays unnamed.

[[Netstrand]], the Night Shore, quotes the far crossing from [[Braetu]].

**[[Heskoren]]** holds four, with a long wild between the Trees.

[[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] preaches the waiting-gospel, the hot faith of a country where the gift is scarce, and its seat is [[Eolvaeth]], out on the pilgrim-edge. That seat is not [[Saelvaeth]]'s march.

[[Saelvaeth]], the Sown Waiting, stands its march-voice at [[Natai]], and [[Harrow's Green]] sits in the orbit of that voice while staying out of the seat.

[[Vaelhesk]], the Far Yield, keeps the land itself as the seat, and [[The First Bowl]], which folk call *Lonasir*, stays a guest-grove rather than a capital.

[[Ornled]], the Outer Ledger, keeps the secular frontier's slate at [[Sanbreo]].

[[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] sit on the seam where those four can all be heard. From the hamlets you can see Harrow's canopy, which is counted as Saelvaeth's luck, and you can still hear Vaethorn's guilt and Vaelhesk's refusal. Folk call the three *Brenod*, *Vaelun*, and *Ornath*, and the Seat still does not use those names.

## What they remember (the Other Count)

The fifteen are not fifteen ancient kingdoms. [[Maiethorn]]'s six inherit a grove-gate that fell, [[Strandoren]]'s five inherit a Salt Walk that outlived the gate and a fever that wrote new paper, and [[Heskoren]]'s four inherit a late cart and one landing-clause. The full years of those inheritances are on [[The Other Count]]. Two clocks still organize a life beside that count, the clock of how a person Turned and the clock of how far the wood has reached, and the inheritances in the table are why a border, a rate, or a grudge can be pointed at.

## What these names are not

- **Maiethorn's five seats are named.** They are [[Seinbrun]], [[Rothallo]], [[Larbril]], [[Votaer]], and [[Tasain]]. **Strandoren's four seats are named** as [[Raitin]], [[Naenor]], [[Lunbra]], and [[Braetu]]. **Heskoren's seats are named or refused:** [[Sanbreo]], the land of [[Vaelhesk]], where the land itself is the seat, and [[Natai]]. The three corner capitals stand apart from those lists, at [[Eolvaeth]], [[Orentel]], and [[Maiethlir]]. The small towns and squares a party can visit are not capitals. The Book-Hands still have no seat, the person who governs Trenledd stays unnamed, and Nidtol stays a hearth-name the compact recites rather than a city.
- **Hearths, not flags.** The born peoples are on [[Kinds of the Turning]], and none of the fifteen is one of those peoples under a crown. The Sown Tree is sand-country, and it is not a kingdom of the Fox-of-the-Snows. The Thaw-Land is a watershed, and it is not a Tengu empire. The Hinge Shore and the Night Shore are shores, and they are not Selkie thalassocracies, sea-realms ruled by the sea-born. The Far Yield is host-rights, the custom of giving a guest the first meal, and it is not a nation of the Taken-In, the people the wild has taken partway in.
- **Faiths are not extra flags.** [[The Watching]] stays a heartland heresy inside Maiethorn's districts. Leddvael is the one power that made a rival faith civic, and even there the Book-hands have no seat of their own.
- **Kumbaan is not on this list.** There is no mile-shrine on the Isle, no stone along a road to mark a day's walk.

## Two lists of the same fifteen

A clerk of the Seat and a Lestrand factor do not hand you the same map. The shared practice is what keeps the peace between them. The list each one is willing to write is what gives that speaker away.

> **From a Cut-year minute, First Seat (Cut-year 387).** *Threnmaieth names itself the Reckoning and would name us. Maiethvael will not be named to them and still gives the extra mile. Orenbren lodges what we do not house. Saelthael still walks, which the origin finds inconvenient. The Hinge Shore takes the Old Crossing. Lirorn takes the thaw. Across the water the factor's five: Lestrand, the Brenledd compact, Leddvael which files a Bound as a civic virtue, Trenledd which keeps a roll and will not call it a Threnhael, Netstrand which sells the west. On the far reach: Vaethorn waiting, Saelvaeth planting, Vaelhesk feeding a first meal we did not authorize, Ornled which will not say her name and will not keep a list either. Kumbaan is not a land we graft. We do not write it.*
>
> **From a house-year docket, a Lestrand factor.** *Price the Hinge Shore and do not argue hymns with the Gift-Realm. The Witness-Hearths' beds are a market that calls itself a duty. Avoid the Worn Count's roll unless you like being written. The Reckoned Gift will sign; that is cheaper than faith. The Hearth-League is many tickets. The Night Shore quotes the far crossing like a pilgrimage. Heskoren: the Waiting pays in blessing, the Outer Ledger in slate, the Far Yield in a meal you had better eat, the Sown Waiting in whatever the queue did this year. The Isle is a tale. We do not book it.*

Neither list is complete, and both are true of the speaker who wrote them.

## GM Notes

- **The Heskoren paragraph, as written for the map.** The seats below are the ones the map was written from, and a later pass has to leave them where they are. [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] is the waiting-gospel, and its seat is [[Eolvaeth]], the pilgrim-edge, which is not Saelvaeth's march. [[Saelvaeth]], the Sown Waiting, stands its march-voice at [[Natai]], and [[Harrow's Green]] sits in that orbit and stays out of the seat. [[Vaelhesk]], the Far Yield, keeps the land as the seat. [[Ornled]], the Outer Ledger, keeps the slate at [[Sanbreo]]. [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] sit on the seam, where they can see Harrow's canopy, counted as Saelvaeth's luck, and can still hear Vaethorn's guilt and Vaelhesk's refusal. Folk call the hamlets *Brenod*, *Vaelun*, and *Ornath*, and the Seat still does not use those names.
- **Do not rebuild.** The named-stub pass, the seats, the leftover squares, and the placement of the Inner Close are already done. *Eolstrand* is retired, and the slot is [[The Hinge Shore]]. The seed is `20260827`. Do not rebuild the era spine. Do not treat Ornthael as post-history, and do not narrate it as the modern age that began after history had finished. Do not add a sixteenth flag. Do not put a mile-shrine on Kumbaan.
- **Fifteen injustices, not one with hats.** Each of the fifteen has its own injustice, and they are not one injustice wearing fifteen names. The Gift-Realm is not the good foil to the Mother's Reckoning, because piety can still become a social tax. The Worn Count is colder for having no hymns, and it is not kinder for that lack. The Outer Ledger's lack of a congregation-net is exposure, and it is not freedom.
- **Reach-edge stays GM-side.** Saelthael's thin soil and Saelvaeth's young grafts should keep reading, in anything a player sees, as horticulture and as a queue. The bounded-reach cause does not migrate into a stub, and it does not migrate into the player text of this note.
- **Trenledd's etymology is a tell.** *Thren* worn down to *Tren* is the sacred count with the sacred worn off it, the same machinery Threnmaieth sanctifies, named for what the machinery does. A scholar-PC may notice. Do not gloss that wearing-down in player text as a clever pun.
- **Maiethvael / Threnmaieth** is the Motherland's argument carried in two names, the Mother's gift against the Mother's reckoning. The two names share *maieth*, and they keep opposite paper.
- **Saelthael / Saelvaeth** share *sael*, the sowing, on two continents. The sowing is still moving at the origin and at the front. Do not narrate one of them as history and the other as frontier flavour. The playable pair is [[Ornsael]], the well on the west road, and [[Harrow's Green]], the neighbour's canopy.
- **Kumbaan stays off the clerk's list on purpose.** A later campaign may reach the Isle. Do not promote Kumbaan to a great power in order to round a number.
- **Hooks.** Steal a Threnhael entry that would name Maiethvael, or refuse to. Lodge in Orenbren during Turning-Week, at [[The Third Hearth]], at Seine's bed, or on Brenthael's stone. Walk Saelthael's leftover west-road out of [[Ornsael]]. Sign in Leddvael under a gift-reading name. Get written into Trenledd. Eat Vaelhesk's first meal before a Cutting-leave. Sit the seam at the three hamlets, where Saelvaeth's luck, Vaelhesk's refusal, Vaethorn's guilt, and Ornled's slate can all be heard in one evening. Wait at [[Eolvaeth]] the year the Tree does not speak. Book a First-Hand year on [[Orentel]]'s first quay, next to a pot. Speak a colour at [[Maiethlir]], and do not copy it in the same breath.
