# The Walled Book

Everyday: **the Walled Book**. The class that keeps it: **the Closed Heirs**. The place: **[[Rothallo]]**, still called **the Inner Close** — first day's walk from the Motherwood, walled, the capital of [[Orenbren]] and not a sixteenth great power. Orenbren calls the arrangement a guest-compact: its inns lodge anyone outside the wall, and the Book rules inside it. [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] treats the Book as a rival roll, because the Book will not let Threnmaieth's clerks copy its pages.

This is **political class rule**, not a church. They do not Speak colours. They do not pour a god. They **classify**.

Folk still say there was an older empire of the untithed, and that it sat on the grove. They are right. Everyday historical name: **the Closed Seat.** The walls are what is left of it. How the Closed Seat broke — the copy, the uprooting, Hildal's Retreat — is [[The Closing]]. The Book is present tense.

## If you meet one

They look like lodging-gentry who never took a Tithe. They do not take the heavy turns of work inside the walls. Each one carries a key to a gate in the wall, and a person with a Condition may not carry that key. You would miss them as clergy. You would find them if you asked *who still decides what a Given owes besides the Tithe they already carry.*

**Delamem** *(del-AH-mem)* keeps the Book of Tithes. Delamem speaks the conservative common speech and will tell you the Book is housekeeping. The page that assigns labour is in the same hand as the page that assigns a hearth-tax.

**Talnin of the Inner Close** *(TAL-nin)* is a Closed Heir of the present generation. The name is on the heir-roll. The tenth year is not finished.

## What a visitor sees

A visitor to [[Rothallo]] does not need to read the Book to see the rank. It shows in four places.

- **Where a person sits.** At a meal in a Closed house, the Kept sit at the end of the table nearest the host. A guest who received a Condition is shown to the far end, past the last Kept guest. When the Book is read aloud in the Book's house, the Kept sit on the front benches and everyone else sits behind them. Nobody is turned away from the table. The host says where each guest sits, and the guest is expected to sit there.
- **Whose word is taken.** When two people disagree in front of a clerk of the Book, over a debt, a boundary, or a broken pot, and one of them is Kept and the other has a Condition, the clerk writes the Kept person's account as what happened. The other account is written underneath it as "also said." The person with a Condition can still win. To win, they need a second Kept person to say the same thing they said.
- **Who holds a key.** Only a Kept person may hold a key to a gate in the wall, to the Book's house, to a cistern, or to a granary. A person with a Condition holds the key to their own door and to their own stall, and no others.
- **What the school teaches.** The children of the Closed houses learn their letters at a school beside the Book's house. Other families inside the walls may send a child if they pay. The teacher, **Volnai** *(VOL-nai)*, tells the children that a hug is rare, and that a child the Tree hugged was kept back for something better than a colour. Volnai believes this and teaches it every year. The lesson is wrong about the count. See the next section.

A person with a Condition who lives inside the walls works for wages, keeps what they earn, owns their tools and their household goods, marries whom they choose, and can own a stall. The Book writes a licence for that stall. A Kept stall-keeper needs no licence. A person with a Condition can walk out of the gate on any day it is open and live somewhere else. Nobody owns them. They are not slaves. What they cannot have inside the walls is the heir's place, a gate key, a vote in the Close's compact, or the first word in front of a clerk.

## The lesson and the count

The Close teaches that a hug is rare. It is not. Most towns have Kept neighbours, and the shares are on [[Conditions]]. Most Kept people have never heard of the heir-roll.

What is actually rare is a family that has stayed without a Condition on purpose, generation after generation, and treats that record as a birthright. The Closed houses do it in two ways. An heir who receives a colour is struck from the heir-roll, so the roll only ever lists people who were hugged. An heir also does not swear the kind of oath that can Strike a person, and stays away from the other ways a person can be Struck where they can. After enough generations, every name on the roll belongs to a hugged person. A family like that is rare. Being hugged is common.

Delamem and the other clerks of the Book can read the count. The school does not teach it. If a visitor brings the count to the Close, a clerk will say the count is someone else's record and does not bind the Close, and the school teaches the same lesson the next year.

## The Book of Tithes

Not a hymn. A roster.

- **Taxes** — coin, board, and a cut of self-paying labour. An [[The Answered|Answered]] shop inside the walls pays a craft-due the Ember-yard on the quay does not. A [[Two-Bodied]] ranging-week is scheduled as *owed work* before it is a ranging.
- **Restrictions** — where a Conditioned person may hold a key, a stall, a vote in the Close's compact. The [[Conditions|Kept]] hold those. A Given who keeps a stall does it as a licensed exception, written.
- **Labour** — everyone who lives inside the walls owes the Close turns of work, the way a town expects each household to take a turn on the watch. The Book keeps the list of turns: green, hall, wall, road, and the beds outside the gate. A Kept household's turns are mostly the watch on the wall and the keeping of keys. A household with a Condition is given the heavier turns, and more of them, and the Book picks the turn by the Condition. The turns are not the Tithe. A person with a Condition owes the turns in addition to their Tithe. A person who does not want the turns can leave the city. The Book's list has no claim on anyone who lives outside the walls. The Book does not sell a person, lend a person to an employer, or hand a person over the way an owner hands over a worker. It cannot send for someone who has left.

A Closed Heir who is **Given** at Leaf-Fall **loses status**. The Gift is a disinheritance. The hug keeps the name on the heir-roll. The colour takes it off. The person is still a person. They are not a Closed Heir. The Book already has turns of work written for what they have become.

A Closed Heir who is **Struck** later, by a drowning, an oath, or a death, is treated the same way. The Book strikes the name from the heir-roll when the clerks learn of it, and writes the person's turns of work under the Condition they now carry.

This is the opposite of Threnmaieth's care-count. The [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]] writes everyone and keeps them. The Book **ranks** by whether you carry a Tithe, and will strike an heir for receiving a gift.

Orenbren's sleep-line at the gate records who slept in a bed. The Close's Book records who owes turns of work. Both lists are kept in Orenbren's country, and neither lets the other copy its page.

## What they refuse

- They will not call the Book a scripture. A preacher who calls it scripture is bringing the Motherfaith into it, or doing the Close a kindness it did not ask for.
- They will not Speak a colour or officiate a Leaf-Fall. A warden may come in for Turning-Week. The copy after the Speaking is *their* copy, not the Seat's and not the Threnhael's.
- They will not pour years into a cup. [[The Pourers]] are a deathless hobby. A Closed Heir who is Long-Lived may pour. The Book does not care.
- They will not hide a [[Phoenix]]. A white-fire fall inside the walls would be a problem for the Book to rank.
- They will not add a sixteenth flag. Rothallo is Orenbren's capital. People still say the Inner Close. The fifteen already include Orenbren.

## How they reach a party

A labour-roster names a PC. A Given heir wants the heir-line back and will pay in board. Delamem wants a colour-copy the warden has not released. Threnmaieth wants the Book; the Close wants the clerks off the gate. A Kept pilgrim who takes work inside the walls is put on the heavier turns, because the Book has no record of their hug.

The party can be hired by the Close, by Orenbren's lodging-guilds, or by a Closed child who just took a colour.

## Outside the walls

The Book stays inside Rothallo. The habit of ranking a hug above a colour does not. Three places show how far it reaches and where it stops.

- **[[Orenbren]], outside the wall.** The lodging-guilds do not ask a guest's colour, and the sleep-line at the gate does not record it. Near the gate it is different. An inn that wants the Closed houses' business keeps its best table for Kept guests on the nights a Closed house dines there, and moves a guest with a Condition to another table if the Closed house asks. The guild does not forbid this. In Turning-Week, when the Close will not lodge a Given pilgrim, the inns outside the wall take that pilgrim in. Some of them charge more for it.
- **[[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]], which rejects the ranking in public.** At [[Eolvaeth]], the gift-hall teaches that a colour is the Mother's gift and a hug is the Mother keeping a child as they are, and that neither makes a person better than the other. The gift-hall says out loud, in its Eolthael preaching, that the Close's teaching is wrong. A Given person who has left the Close can eat at the gift-hall without being asked for a house. That is why [[Hithaen]] walked there. The Waiting Lands have their own unkindness, the other way round: a hugged child there is sometimes pitied as passed over by the Mother. The two teachings disagree with each other. Neither one matches the count.
- **Brenthael, which copies the seating.** Brenthael is the town with its own Tree a day's walk past [[The Third Hearth]] on the Near Mile. At its Turning-Week supper in the square, Kept people sit on the benches nearest the Tree, and people with a Condition sit on the outer benches, even when that splits a family. A child who has just received a colour gets up from an inner bench and moves to an outer one before the food is served. Brenthael has no copy of the Book, no heir-roll, and no list of turns. Nobody in the town can say when the seating started. They keep it because it is how the supper has always been laid out.

## In-world text

*A leaf from the Book of Tithes, Inner Close, Delamem's hand. The line for heirs and the line for turns of work share a fold.*

> *Talnin, Closed, heir. If the fall is a colour, strike heir. Enter labour under the colour as the page already holds. The hug keeps the line. We do not file this as a slight. We file it as a house. Orenbren may lodge who they witness. They may not lodge who we rank. The Threnhael may count who they can see from the river. They may not copy this page. A gift is a gift. A gift is not a seat.*

This is a page from the Book of Tithes. It records an heir, says the Book will strike the name if the Tree gives a colour, and tells Orenbren and Threnmaieth that neither may copy it. Delamem writes that the Book does not mean the striking as a slight, and that is how a child learns that a leaf can take a name.

## GM Notes

- **Political classification.** The Walled Book ranks people. It is not the Pourers (a member gives their own memory) and not the Protectors (worship that hides taken fire). The injustice is the Book, not a hymn. Do not store fire in the Close; that belongs to a different group.
- **Not a sixteenth power.** [[Rothallo]] is Orenbren's capital, still called the Inner Close, still inside Orenbren. **Placement:** the Closed Seat sat the grove; Hildal's Retreat closed walls a day's walk from the Motherwood. A shore-flag cannot carry that remnant. Do not move the Book to [[The Hinge Shore]]. Do not move the walls out of Orenbren. Do not capture the First Seat. The wood is not crowned. Rothallo is not the college. Do not make this Threnmaieth's inner court — that collapses two lists into one.
- **Old empire, now written.** The Closed Seat sat the grove and decided who could walk. [[The First Cut]] broke the monopoly; [[The Closing]] is the war, the collapse, Hildal's Retreat. Do not name the cutter. Do not promote the Close to a sixteenth flag. Do not make Hildal a villain-king.
- **Distinct from Threnmaieth.** Sacred census writes everyone as care. The Book ranks the tithed and disinherits the Given heir. A campaign can steal both lists and they will not say the same thing about the same child.
- **Names.** Delamem, Talnin of the Inner Close — seed `20260830`, conservative list, mid-bottom. Inner Close / Walled Book / Closed Heirs / Book of Tithes are common-tongue. No new liturgy.
- **The teacher.** `python3 "14 - Assets/Names/generate_names.py" --seed 20261021 --register conservative --count 40`. **Volnai** is position 28. Given name only. No house is written for them. - **The count.** The lesson says a hug is rare. The figures stay on [[The Premise]] and are only quoted here. Do not write a second set of population numbers for Rothallo, for the Closed houses, or for the heir-roll. The Close knows the count and keeps the lesson anyway.
