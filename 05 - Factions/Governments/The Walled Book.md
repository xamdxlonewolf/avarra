# The Walled Book

People call the roster **the Walled Book**, the class that keeps it **the Closed Heirs**, and the place **the Inner Close**. The walled city a first day's walk from the Motherwood is **[[Rothallo]]**, the capital of [[Orenbren]]. Orenbren is already one of the fifteen great powers, so the Close is not a sixteenth flag of its own. Orenbren calls the arrangement a guest-compact. Its inns will lodge anyone outside the wall, and the Book rules inside it. [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] keeps the [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]], the roll that writes every person down and means to keep them, and it treats the Book as a rival roll, because the Book will not let Threnmaieth's clerks copy its pages.

The Closed Heirs are a political class, not a church. At Leaf-Fall, a child stands for the Tree, and the Tree either hugs the child, who stays one of the Kept, with no Condition and no Tithe, or lets a coloured leaf fall, which Gives a Condition and the Tithe that Condition carries from then on. A warden Speaks the hug or the colour aloud. The Closed Heirs do not Speak colours, and they do not pour a god. Pouring, in this country, is a Long-Lived person giving years of their own memory into a cup, which is the deathless hobby of [[The Pourers]], and a Closed Heir who is already Long-Lived may still pour as a private person. The Book does not take that cup up as its work. Its work is to classify people by whether they carry a Tithe, and then to write what the people inside the walls owe on top of that.

Folk still say there was an older empire of the untithed, people who carried no Tithe, and that it sat on the grove, and on that point the folk are right. The everyday historical name is **the Closed Seat,** and the walls of Rothallo are what is left of it. How the Closed Seat broke, the copy, the uprooting, and Hildal's Retreat, is [[The Closing]]. The Book is what that class is doing now.

## If you meet one

They look like lodging-gentry who never took a Tithe, the upkeep a Condition carries, and they do not take the heavy turns of work inside the walls. Each one carries a key to a gate in the wall, and a person with a Condition may not carry that key. You would miss them if you went looking for clergy, and you would find them if you asked who still decides what a Given owes besides the Tithe they already carry.

**Delamem** *(del-AH-mem)* keeps the Book of Tithes. Delamem speaks the conservative common speech and will tell you the Book is housekeeping, though the page that assigns labour is in the same hand as the page that assigns a hearth-tax.

**Talnin of the Inner Close** *(TAL-nin)* is a Closed Heir of the present generation. The name is on the heir-roll, and the tenth year is not finished, so the Tree has not Spoken this child's colour yet.

## What a visitor sees

A visitor to [[Rothallo]] does not need to read the Book to see the rank. It shows in four places.

- **Where a person sits.** At a meal in a Closed house, the Kept sit at the end of the table nearest the host, and a guest who received a Condition is shown to the far end, past the last Kept guest. When the Book is read aloud in the Book's house, the Kept sit on the front benches and everyone else sits behind them. Nobody is turned away from the table. The host says where each guest sits, and the guest is expected to sit there.
- **Whose word is taken.** When two people disagree in front of a clerk of the Book, over a debt, a boundary, or a broken pot, and one of them is Kept and the other has a Condition, the clerk writes the Kept person's account as what happened and writes the other account underneath it as "also said." The person with a Condition can still win, and to win they need a second Kept person to say the same thing they said.
- **Who holds a key.** Only a Kept person may hold a key to a gate in the wall, to the Book's house, to a cistern, or to a granary. A person with a Condition holds the key to their own door and to their own stall, and no others.
- **What the school teaches.** The children of the Closed houses learn their letters at a school beside the Book's house, and other families inside the walls may send a child if they pay. The teacher, **Volnai** *(VOL-nai)*, tells the children that a hug is rare, and that a child the Tree hugged was kept back for something better than a colour. Volnai believes this and teaches it every year. The lesson is wrong about the count, which the next section sets beside the teaching.

A person with a Condition who lives inside the walls works for wages, keeps what they earn, owns their tools and their household goods, marries whom they choose, and can own a stall. The Book writes a licence for that stall, and a Kept stall-keeper needs no licence. A person with a Condition can walk out of the gate on any day it is open and live somewhere else. Nobody owns them, and they are not slaves. What they cannot have inside the walls is the heir's place, a gate key, a vote in the Close's compact, or the first word in front of a clerk.

## The lesson and the count

The Close teaches that a hug is rare, and the teaching is wrong. Most towns have Kept neighbours, people the Tree hugged and left without a Condition, and most of those neighbours have never heard of the heir-roll. The shares of who is hugged and who carries a Condition are on [[Conditions]].

What is actually rare is a family that has stayed without a Condition on purpose, generation after generation, and treats that record as a birthright. The Closed houses manage it in two ways that reinforce each other. An heir who receives a colour is struck from the heir-roll, so the roll only ever lists people who were hugged. An heir also does not swear the kind of oath that can Strike a person, and stays away, where they can, from the other ways a person can be Struck. A Strike is a Condition put on a person later, by a drowning, an oath, or a death. After enough generations, every name on the roll belongs to a hugged person. A family that has kept that record is rare, and being hugged is common.

Delamem and the other clerks of the Book can read the count, and the school does not teach it. If a visitor brings the count to the Close, a clerk will say the count is someone else's record and does not bind the Close, and the school teaches the same lesson the next year.

## The Book of Tithes

The Book of Tithes is a roster rather than a hymn, and the roster does three kinds of work.

- **Taxes** — coin, board, and a cut of self-paying labour, work a person is paid to do on their own account. An [[The Answered|Answered]] shop inside the walls, a craft-house of a person an element answered and still answers, pays a craft-due the Ember-yard on the quay does not, and that yard is where the fire-answered keep their craft. A [[Two-Bodied]] ranging-week, the week a person spends in the animal body they also carry, is scheduled as owed work before it is allowed to count as a ranging.
- **Restrictions** — where a Conditioned person may hold a key, a stall, or a vote in the Close's compact. The [[Conditions|Kept]] hold those. A Given who keeps a stall does it as a licensed exception, and the exception is written down.
- **Labour** — everyone who lives inside the walls owes the Close turns of work, the way a town expects each household to take a turn on the watch. The Book keeps the list of turns: the green, the hall, the wall, the road, and the beds outside the gate. A Kept household's turns are mostly the watch on the wall and the keeping of keys. A household with a Condition is given the heavier turns, and more of them, and the Book picks the turn by the Condition. The turns are not the Tithe, so a person with a Condition owes them in addition to the Tithe they already carry, and a person who does not want the turns can leave the city. The Book's list has no claim on anyone who lives outside the walls. The Book does not sell a person, lend a person to an employer, or hand a person over the way an owner hands over a worker. It cannot send for someone who has left.

A Closed Heir who is **Given** at Leaf-Fall loses status. The Gift is a disinheritance: the hug keeps the name on the heir-roll, and the colour takes it off. The person is still a person, and is no longer a Closed Heir, and the Book already has turns of work written for what they have become.

A Closed Heir who is **Struck** later, by a drowning, an oath, or a death, is treated the same way. The Book strikes the name from the heir-roll when the clerks learn of it, and writes the person's turns of work under the Condition they now carry.

This ranking is the opposite of Threnmaieth's care-count. The [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]] writes everyone down in order to keep them, while the Book ranks by whether you carry a Tithe and will strike an heir for receiving a gift.

Orenbren's sleep-line at the gate records who slept in a bed, and the Close's Book records who owes turns of work. Both lists are kept in Orenbren's country, and neither lets the other copy its page.

## What they refuse

- They will not call the Book a scripture. A preacher who does is either bringing the Motherfaith, the faith kept for the Mother, into a roster, or doing the Close a kindness the Close did not ask for.
- They will not Speak a colour or officiate a Leaf-Fall. A warden may come in for Turning-Week, the festival week when the children stand and the Tree turns, and the copy made after that Speaking is the Close's copy, not the Seat's and not the Threnhael's.
- They will not pour years into a cup as the Book's own work. [[The Pourers]] are a deathless hobby, and a Closed Heir who is Long-Lived may still pour years of their own memory, which the Book does not count as its business.
- They will not hide a [[Phoenix]]. A white-fire fall inside the walls, the leaf that names a Phoenix, would be a problem for the Book to rank.
- They will not add a sixteenth flag. Rothallo is already Orenbren's capital, people still call it the Inner Close, and the fifteen great powers already include Orenbren.

## How they reach a party

The Book reaches a party on ordinary paper. A labour-roster names a PC, or a Given heir wants the heir-line back and will pay in board, or Delamem wants a colour-copy the warden has not released. Threnmaieth wants the Book, and the Close wants those clerks off the gate. A Kept pilgrim who takes work inside the walls is put on the heavier turns, because the Book has no record of their hug.

The party can be hired by the Close, by Orenbren's lodging-guilds, or by a Closed child who just took a colour.

## Outside the walls

The Book itself stays inside Rothallo, while the habit of ranking a hug above a colour keeps traveling, and three places show how far that habit reaches and where it stops.

- **[[Orenbren]], outside the wall.** The lodging-guilds do not ask a guest's colour, and the sleep-line at the gate does not record it. Near the gate it is different. An inn that wants the Closed houses' business keeps its best table for Kept guests on the nights a Closed house dines there, and moves a guest with a Condition to another table if the Closed house asks. The guild does not forbid this. In Turning-Week, when the Close will not lodge a Given pilgrim, the inns outside the wall take that pilgrim in, and some of them charge more for it.
- **[[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]], which rejects the ranking in public.** At [[Eolvaeth]], the gift-hall teaches that a colour is the Mother's gift and a hug is the Mother keeping a child as they are, and that neither makes a person better than the other. The gift-hall says this out loud in its Eolthael preaching, Eolthael being the high-solstice month, and it says the Close's teaching is wrong. A Given person who has left the Close can eat at the gift-hall without being asked for a house. That is why [[Hithaen]] walked there. The Waiting Lands have their own unkindness, pointed the other way: a hugged child there is sometimes pitied as passed over by the Mother. The two teachings disagree with each other, and neither one matches the count.
- **Brenthael, which copies the seating.** Brenthael is the town with its own Tree a day's walk past [[The Third Hearth]] on the Near Mile. At its Turning-Week supper in the square, Kept people sit on the benches nearest the Tree, and people with a Condition sit on the outer benches, even when that splits a family. A child who has just received a colour gets up from an inner bench and moves to an outer one before the food is served. Brenthael has no copy of the Book, no heir-roll, and no list of turns. Nobody in the town can say when the seating started. They keep it because it is how the supper has always been laid out.

## In-world text

*A leaf from the Book of Tithes, Inner Close, Delamem's hand. The line for heirs and the line for turns of work share a fold.*

> *Talnin, Closed, heir. If the fall is a colour, strike heir. Enter labour under the colour as the page already holds. The hug keeps the line. We do not file this as a slight. We file it as a house. Orenbren may lodge who they witness. They may not lodge who we rank. The Threnhael may count who they can see from the river. They may not copy this page. A gift is a gift. A gift is not a seat.*

This is a page from the Book of Tithes. It records an heir, says the Book will strike the name if the Tree gives a colour, and tells Orenbren and Threnmaieth that neither may copy it. Delamem writes that the Book does not mean the striking as a slight, and that is how a child learns that a leaf can take a name.

## GM Notes

- **Political classification.** The Walled Book ranks people. It is not the Pourers, whose member gives their own memory into a cup, and it is not the Protectors, whose worship hides taken fire. The injustice is the Book, not a hymn. Do not store fire in the Close. That belongs to a different group.
- **Not a sixteenth power.** [[Rothallo]] is Orenbren's capital, still called the Inner Close, and it stays inside Orenbren, so it is not a power of its own. **Placement:** the Closed Seat sat the grove, and Hildal's Retreat is what closed the walls a day's walk from the Motherwood. A shore-flag cannot carry that remnant. Do not move the Book to [[The Hinge Shore]]. Do not move the walls out of Orenbren. Do not capture the First Seat. The wood is not crowned. Rothallo is not the college. Do not make this Threnmaieth's inner court, because that would collapse two lists into one.
- **Old empire, now written.** The Closed Seat sat the grove and decided who could walk. [[The First Cut]] broke the monopoly, and [[The Closing]] is the war, the collapse, and Hildal's Retreat. Do not name the cutter. Do not promote the Close to a sixteenth flag. Do not make Hildal a villain-king.
- **Distinct from Threnmaieth.** A sacred census writes everyone as care. The Book ranks the tithed and disinherits the Given heir. A campaign can steal both lists, and the two lists will not say the same thing about the same child.
- **Names.** Delamem and Talnin of the Inner Close come from seed `20260830`, the conservative list, taken from the mid-bottom of that list. Inner Close, Walled Book, Closed Heirs, and Book of Tithes are common-tongue. Do not add a new liturgy.
- **The teacher.** The name was drawn with `python3 "14 - Assets/Names/generate_names.py" --seed 20261021 --register conservative --count 40`. **Volnai** is position 28. It is a given name only, and no house is written for them. Volnai is the one person who says the lesson aloud. They are not a pivot, and they do not get a plot. The named people of the Book stay Delamem, Talnin of the Inner Close, and [[Hithaen]].
- **The count.** The lesson says a hug is rare. The figures stay on [[The Premise]] and are only quoted here. Do not write a second set of population numbers for Rothallo, for the Closed houses, or for the heir-roll. The Close knows the count and keeps the lesson anyway.
- **Turns of work, not ownership.** The list of turns is a duty of living inside the walls. A person with a Condition can own a stall, keeps their wages, and can leave by the gate. The Book does not sell, lend, or hand over a person. Do not write it as slavery or as a slave class.
- **Outside the walls.** Three places only: Orenbren's inns near the gate, Vaethorn's gift-hall at Eolvaeth, and the supper benches at Brenthael. Do not write an answer for every power. Do not add a city. Brenthael keeps no copy of the Book. Do not explain who started its seating.

- **Hooks.** The colour that strikes an heir; clerks at the gate; a pilgrim ranked as labour; Orenbren full and the Close shut; a Bound whose wages the Book and the desk both claim in the same week.
