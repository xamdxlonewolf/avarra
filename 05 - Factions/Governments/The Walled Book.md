# The Walled Book

Everyday: **the Walled Book**. The class that keeps it: **the Closed Heirs**. The place: **the Inner Close** — first day's walk from the Motherwood, walled, lodged *inside* [[Orenbren]] and not counted as a sixteenth great power. Orenbren treats the walls as a guest-compact that never opened. [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] treats them as a roll that will not share ink.

This is **political class rule**, not a church. They do not Speak colours. They do not pour a god. They **classify**.

Folk still say there was an older empire of the untithed, and that it sat on the grove. The walls are what is left. How the empire broke is a later telling. The Book is present tense.

## If you meet one

They look like lodging-gentry who never took a Tithe. Clean hands. A key to a gate that is not a greens-gate. You would miss them as clergy. You would find them if you asked *who still decides what a Given owes besides the Tithe they already carry.*

**Delamem** *(del-AH-mem)* keeps the Book of Tithes. Conservative mouth. They will tell you the Book is housekeeping. The page that assigns labour is in the same hand as the page that assigns a hearth-tax.

**Talnin of the Inner Close** *(TAL-nin)* is a Closed Heir of the present generation. The name is on the heir-roll. The tenth year is not finished.

## The Book of Tithes

Not a hymn. A roster.

- **Taxes** — coin, board, and a cut of self-paying labour. An [[The Answered|Answered]] shop inside the walls pays a craft-due the Ember-yard on the quay does not. A [[Two-Bodied]] ranging-week is scheduled as *owed work* before it is a ranging.
- **Restrictions** — where a Conditioned person may hold a key, a stall, a vote in the Close's compact. The [[The Premise|Kept]] hold those. A Given who keeps a stall does it as a licensed exception, written.
- **Labour** — the Book assigns bodies to jobs the Close needs: green, hall, wall, road. The Tithe is not the assignment. The assignment sits *on top* of the Tithe.

A Closed Heir who is **Given** at Leaf-Fall **loses status**. The Gift is a disinheritance. The hug keeps the name on the heir-roll. The colour takes it off. The person is still a person. They are not a Closed Heir. The Book already has a labour-line for what they have become.

This is the opposite of Threnmaieth's care-count. The [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]] writes everyone and keeps them. The Book **ranks** by whether you carry a Tithe, and will strike an heir for receiving a gift.

Orenbren witnesses who slept. The Close witnesses who *owes*. They share a country. They do not share a page.

## What they refuse

- They will not call the Book a scripture. A preacher who does that is doing Motherfaith leaking in, or doing a kindness the Close does not want.
- They will not Speak a colour or officiate a Leaf-Fall. A warden may come in for Turning-Week. The copy after the Speaking is *their* copy, not the Seat's and not the Threnhael's.
- They will not pour years into a cup. [[The Pourers]] are a deathless hobby. A Closed Heir who is Long-Lived may pour. The Book does not care.
- They will not hide a [[Phoenix]]. White-fire inside the walls is a classification emergency, not a relic.
- They will not add a sixteenth flag. The Close is a town. The fifteen already include Orenbren.

## How they reach a party

A labour-roster names a PC. A Given heir wants the heir-line back and will pay in board. Delamem wants a colour-copy the warden has not released. Threnmaieth wants the Book; the Close wants the clerks off the gate. A pilgrim is charged as Conditioned labour because the Book does not know their hug.

The party can be hired by the Close, by Orenbren's lodging-guilds, or by a Closed child who just took a colour.

## In-world text

*A leaf from the Book of Tithes, Inner Close, Delamem's hand. The heir-line and the labour-line share a fold.*

> *Talnin, Closed, unmarked, heir. If the fall is a colour, strike heir. Enter labour under the colour as the page already holds. The hug keeps the line. We do not file this as a slight. We file it as a house. Orenbren may lodge who they witness. They may not lodge who we rank. The Threnhael may count who they can see from the river. They may not copy this page. A gift is a gift. A gift is not a seat.*

On its face: estate law. Underneath: "we do not file this as a slight" is the only kindness in the ink, and it is how a child learns that a leaf can take a name.

## GM Notes

- **Political classification.** Engine two of three. Not religious self-consumption ([[The Pourers]]). Not worship used to hide extraction ([[The Protectors]]). The injustice is the Book, not a hymn.
- **Not a sixteenth power.** Lives inside Orenbren as a walled town. Placement 🟡 pending the later geography pass (whether a weak shore-flag should carry this remnant instead). Do not promote the Close. Do not capture the First Seat. Do not make this Threnmaieth's inner court — that collapses two lists into one.
- **Old empire, light seed.** Folk memory: the untithed sat on the grove and decided who could walk to it. The First Cut is already the hinge in the wood. The war, the collapse, the retreat behind these walls — later history. Do not date a battle here. Do not name the cutter.
- **Distinct from Threnmaieth.** Sacred census writes everyone as care. The Book ranks the tithed and disinherits the Given heir. A campaign can steal both lists and they will not say the same thing about the same child.
- **Names.** Delamem, Talnin of the Inner Close — seed `20260830`, conservative list, mid-bottom. Inner Close / Walled Book / Closed Heirs / Book of Tithes are common-tongue. No new liturgy.
- **R.8 seed, do not plot:** [[Hithaen]] is a disinherited Given heir of the Closed, eating in [[Eolvaeth]]'s gift-hall. The labour-line is already written. Do not make them Talnin.
- **Hooks.** The colour that strikes an heir; clerks at the gate; a pilgrim ranked as labour; Orenbren full and the Close shut; a Bound eaten by Book and desk in the same week.
