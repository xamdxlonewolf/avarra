# Lirorn

> **The Thaw-Land.** *(formal *Lirorn*, LIR-orn · [[The Old Tongue|lir]] "thaw; water that runs again" + [[The Old Tongue|orn]] "land.")* They call the range **the Thaw-Wall**. Lowlanders say **[[Named Ground|the Rain-Wall]]**.

People say **the Thaw-Land** and **Thaw-folk**. Charters and learned maps write *Lirorn*.

## Overview (public)

Lirorn is [[Maiethorn]]'s highland spine: high snow, towns you reach by rope or by wing, and rivers that begin as thaw. Reach is still full, which here means the grafts take in the valleys and on the sheltered shelves, but the weather is the bigger fact, and the state is a **watershed holding**, organized around the water rather than around a cathedral square. The lowlands drink Lirorn's spring, and the ridges have noticed. [[Maiethlir]] drinks **[[Named Ground|the Core-thaw]]**, the melt that comes off this wall, and that thirst is a lever Lirorn can use.

[[Tengu]] ridge-towns and Fox-of-the-Snows hearths sit densest here ([[Kinds of the Turning]]). Every shelf is mixed Kinds, people of more than one birth. A lowlander arriving on foot is a guest who climbed.

**Site.** The holding is the Rain-Wall. Ridge-roads run where the walking-paths to the grove used to go. Stones stand in the high squares, and some roads now end at a local Tree. A table needs two passes. The **Noon Pass** is the water-line over the old ribbon-notch. The **Shelf-gate** is the road [[Narol of the Pass|Narol]] took after [[The Thaw-Break]].

**Seat.** [[Tasain]] is the walled town in a sheltered valley below the Shelf-gate, where the west road comes down and where the levy is taken, while the villages and hamlets stay up on the shelves and the Noon Pass stays a pass. [[Narol of the Pass]] stays the person already written, and no new person or place is added beside that name.

**How it formed.** The holding grew as march-holds on the snowmelt. Governance sits in the middle because water is a fact you can levy without taking a census of souls: you tax the thaw, you tax the pass, and you tax the high road. Theology cooled because of the height and the distance from the grove's daily pressure, not because anyone here stopped believing. In the year numbered Cut-year 233 the levy became visible as a water-line. The injustice did not start that year; that year gave it a name.

## Structure & Membership

Ridge-compacts, pass-tolls, and valley charters sit under the one holding. The holding does not recruit by Kind. Sky-couriers are an ordinary office, not a guild of one birth: they hear first, and the hearing has a price. [[Narol of the Pass]] is dead. The household that inherited Narol's toll still levies Break-rate, the toll named for the Thaw-Break, in a kind year and will not explain it to a lowlander.

## Goals & Methods

They keep the passes and the spring, and they mean to be paid for water the core calls a gift. They will not be named to the [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]], the roll that would count them as a people, though the keepers of that roll will name the passes if they can. After 233 they named the Shelf-gate. Lirorn charged them for the water anyway.

**Its injustice.** **The ridge pays in isolation for water the valley files as a gift.** When a shelf-town's graft sickens, that town is also a long way from a stiller, the medic who would be an ordinary sight in a valley. The state is large enough to levy, and not large enough to furnish every Tithe on a cliff, the upkeep a person on that cliff still needs. A toll that feels like geography feels like 233 if you were on the shelf when the water-line was drawn. The Core-thaw's loud week before Leaf-Fall, the festival when children stand at the Tree, belongs to Maiethlir, and Maiethlir does not explain it. It is not this holding's sermon.

## GM Notes

Leave the districts unfleshed, and do not make a Tengu empire or a Fox-of-the-Snows kingdom. The seat is [[Tasain]], so do not turn the Noon Pass into a town, do not give the gate a new person, and leave Narol dead, with the household that inherited the toll still unnamed.

**Not a watershed with a toll as the only sentence.** The job of the holding is last year's snow become this year's civic year. Maiethlir sits downstream of the thaw, Saelthael's west-road crosses the Shelf-gate, and closing either one is something the Motherland would feel.

**Hearths, not nations.** Tengu are densest on this snow, and the Snows-Fox are densest here too, which does not make either of them the country. The interesting PC is a human or a Selkie of the Thaw-Land, or a Tengu of [[The Hinge Shore]].

**Do not invent Kind-only orders.** A wing-post is a job a person holds, not an order closed to every other birth.

**Hooks.** A thaw can come late, children can rub a stone on the Noon Pass, and the household can levy Break-rate in a kind year. Threnmaieth tablets can turn up at the Shelf-gate, Vehaela can want an uncounted loft off thin snow, and a sky-courier's strip can say *this is not a row.*
