# Ornled

> **The Outer Ledger.** *(formal *Ornled*, ORN-led · [[The Old Tongue|orn]] "land" + [[The Old Tongue|ledd]] "to reckon.")* The name is worn down to eroded on purpose. This is the secular frontier, and there is no congregation to fall back on.

People say **the Outer Ledger** and **Outer folk**. Slates and learned maps write *Ornled*.

## Overview (public)

Ornled is thin-reach [[Heskoren]] country that never took the waiting-gospel. The grafts are as young as they are in [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]], and the people run the same way, heavy with the Kept and the Struck. A Kept person is one the Tree hugged and did not mark, and a Struck person is one who changed later, not at that Tree. What Ornled does not have is a congregation to fill the gap those young Trees leave. Tithe-provision, the upkeep people need, is barter, favor, and a slate on the wall. An unvouched Struck, someone who changed with nobody willing to stand for them, is a problem met with a rope, a deal, or a neighbour, not with a blessing. Custom outruns law, because there is almost no law here and almost no hymn.

They do not hate the Mother, and they do not call on her either. Vaethorn hears the missing *th* in Ornled's name as godlessness, and Ornled hears the *th* Vaethorn still pronounces as a luxury.

**Site.** The country is frontier pockets off the hottest pilgrim-roads. It looks like a waiting cluster, and it has no gospel to wait with. The same stones are here, and the same upper rooms where a walk used to pass, but the work in those rooms is necessity rather than devotion.

**Seat.** [[Sanbreo]] is the town on the brink of a small city where the slate is kept, and a beach-fee can be paid there when a hull arrives, while the villages and hamlets in the pockets around it stay unnamed. That town is the seat, and it remains a town rather than the city it is nearly large enough to be.

**How it formed.** Districts heavy with the Kept and the Struck watched the waiting-theology arrive with the grafts, and they declined to make scarcity into a problem of the soul. They make things up as they go, and that improvising has become the culture. The government is light because nobody has spare people enough to be a state. The Fair Hand is a rumour that comes off ships, not a civic rite, because they have few Bound to make a book of. [[The Thaw-Break]] gave one cluster a year they can point at: **Vathne**'s slate, after a waited pot drowned in someone else's thaw. The Hush writes them as *slate-shore*. What they owe a hull is a beach-fee, not a hymn. They were not asked.

**What they remember.** Vaethorn hears godlessness in the missing sound. Ornled hears Vaethorn's waiting-gospel as a luxury that only the well-supplied can afford. The chronicle is [[The Other Count]].

## Structure & Membership

Town custom, slates, and vouching stretched thin are what pass for membership. The pockets do not recruit by Kind, the ancestry a person is born with. [[The Intake]], the reception a newly Struck person needs, is an acute problem here and an unofficial one. A raw Struck gets a shed if they are lucky, and silence if the silence works.

## Goals & Methods

They try to stay whole with what is already in the pot. They owe no hymn in exchange for green. They trade with Netstrand when a ship comes, and they try not to become the neighbour that ship can bill.

**Its injustice.** Where Vaethorn's cruelty is a guilt laid on the person, Ornled's cruelty is **no net** at all. A person who cannot barter their Tithe slides, with no prayer under them and no guild beside them. The loneliest unvouched in the world are not in the waiting-lands; they are next door, where no one is waiting with them.

## GM Notes

[[Polity Archetypes]] and [[Heskoren]] require this corner, so do not drop it. Leave the districts unfleshed, do not write a Wild West, and do not write a noble-savage. The seat is [[Sanbreo]], and the town remains the seat without becoming a city. Do not name the pockets, leave Vathne as the slate, do not move Narol here, and add no new person.

**Required corner.** Theology, reach, and governance all sit low, which isolates theology against Vaethorn: the same reach, the same governance, and the faith burned out rather than burned up.

**Not freedom.** A light state, thin Trees, and no faith-net are exposure when they arrive together.

**Do not agree with Vaethorn** that Ornled is unworthy. What separates the two is distance and a choice of gospel: horticulture plus culture.

**Hooks.** An unvouched Struck can meet a slate, a Vaethorn congregation can try to convert a well, a Netstrand bill can come due, and a graft can take without anyone holding a feast or adding the graft to a count.
