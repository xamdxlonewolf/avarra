# Brenledd

> **The Hearth-League.** *(formal *Brenledd*, BREN-ledd · [[The Old Tongue|bren]] "hearth" + [[The Old Tongue|ledd]] "to reckon.")* The name has eroded the way names erode on this coast, the older sounds worn down to something shorter. What it names is a league of towns with little faith and a light government.

People say **the Hearth-League**, **the compact**, and **League-folk**. When a charter or a learned map has to be formal, it writes *Brenledd*.

## Overview (public)

On [[Strandoren]], Brenledd is a league of charter-towns — river-ports, lesser harbours, and inland markets — that compacted together so they could face the houses of [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] without becoming one house themselves. A charter is the writing a town actually lives by. Each town remains a hearth, with its own Tree in the square and its own vouching, which is simply someone of that square standing up to say a person belongs there. The compact is the arithmetic that makes the separate hearths worth joining: they share tickets, they share notes of credit, and they share a refusal to be priced one town at a time.

Faith is worn lightly here, the same as along the rest of this coast, and the Trees are mature. The league polices nothing, and what it means to keep, it writes into a charter.

**Site.** The towns sit behind and between the premier coast: salt towns, river towns, and some road-end Tree-towns that federated rather than fade into the hinterland of a deathless house, one of the great houses that does not die when its founders do.

**Seat.** [[Raitin]] is the throne-city, a river-city and one of the largest on this shore, which is where the council sits and where the shared notes clear. The shared list is seven hearths, Raitin among them, with six charter-towns sitting alongside that still have no names, while the villages and hamlets stay off the list. People still recite Nidtol, and that recited hearth is not the throne-city. The league means to stay a league of hearths rather than become one house, and Raitin is not a second [[Orentel]].

**How it formed.** The towns got tired of being the other party on a Lestrand note. After [[The Grey Summer]], in the year numbered Cut-year 174, **Nidtol**'s hearth piled the first shared refusal: berth-fees charged in a summer when there were no berths, and notes called in early because a house was frightened. No king founded the league. A set of charters began acting together, and that shared acting is what the league is. [[The Hinge Hush]] added a side-paper later: hinge-tolls will not price them one hearth at a time either.

**What they remember.** Lestrand still offers to simplify them, which would turn the many hearths into one party at the table. The chronicle of that offer, and of the refusal, is [[The Other Count]].

## Structure & Membership

A council of hearths runs the compact, and you are of *your* town first. The league does not recruit by Kind, the ancestry a person is born with. Someone vouched in one Brenled square, with a person there willing to stand for them, may still be a stranger in the next, because the compact moves coin better than it moves standing.

## Goals & Methods

They want many tickets to stay cheaper than dealing with one house. The towns underwrite each other, and they stay a league in the seasons when Lestrand would rather negotiate with a single spokesman.

**Its injustice.** The harm is **falling between charters**: you can be vouched in one town and unknown in the next, and Tithe-provision, the upkeep a town furnishes for the people who need it, stays a town matter the compact will not touch. Welfare stays thin inside a rich network, and a person fades in the gaps the league calls local, because no single charter has been written to catch them.

## GM Notes

Leave the districts unfleshed, keep the seat at [[Raitin]], and keep the league a league of hearths rather than one house. Do not name the six charter-towns, do not give them a habit or a person, and do not turn Nidtol into the throne-city or write a note for that recited hearth. Do not clone [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] under a second name.

**A league, and a throne-city.** [[Raitin]] is where the council sits, and it is one hearth on a list of seven. That is not the league turned into one house, and it is not a second [[Orentel]].

**Same low-G corner as Lestrand, different structure.** Brenledd shares Lestrand's low-governance corner, since both are secular, Tree-rich, and lightly governed. Lestrand is the premier coast and Brenledd is an arithmetic of towns, and that split is the proof the knobs are not hats.

**Hooks.** A note can clear in three hearths and die in the fourth, someone can sell vouching across the lines of the compact, a town can want out, and Lestrand can offer again to "simplify" the league.
