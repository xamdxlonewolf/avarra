# Brenledd

> **The Hearth-League.** *(formal *Brenledd*, BREN-ledd · [[The Old Tongue|bren]] "hearth" + [[The Old Tongue|ledd]] "to reckon.")* Eroded drift. A league of towns with little faith and a light government.

People say **the Hearth-League**, **the compact**, and **League-folk**. Charters and learned maps write *Brenledd*.

## Overview (public)

A **league of charter-towns** on [[Strandoren]] — river-ports, lesser harbours, inland markets — that compact to face [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]]'s houses without becoming one house. Each town is a hearth with its own Tree and its own vouching. The compact is the arithmetic: shared tickets, shared notes, shared refusal to be priced one at a time.

Faith worn light, same as the coast. Trees mature. Nothing is policed; everything is *chartered*.

**Site.** The towns behind and between the premier coast: salt towns, river towns, and some road-end Tree-towns that federated instead of fading into a deathless house's hinterland.

**Seat.** [[Raitin]] is the throne-city, a river-city and one of the largest on this shore. The council sits there. The shared notes clear there. The shared list is seven hearths. Raitin is one. Six charter-towns sit with it. They have no names yet. Villages and hamlets are not on the list. People still recite Nidtol. Nidtol is not the throne-city. The league stays a league of hearths. It does not become one house. Raitin is not a second [[Orentel]].

**How it formed.** Towns that got tired of being the other party on a Lestrand note. After [[The Grey Summer]], in Cut-year 174, **Nidtol**'s hearth piled the first shared refusal — berth-fees in a summer with no berths, notes called early because a house was frightened. No king founded it. A set of charters began acting together. [[The Hinge Hush]] added a side-paper: hinge-tolls will not price them one hearth at a time either.

**What they remember.** Lestrand still offers to simplify them. Chronicle: [[The Other Count]].

## Structure & Membership

A council of hearths. You are of *your* town first. Recruits not by Kind. A person vouched in one Brenled square may still be a stranger in the next — the compact moves coin better than it moves standing.

## Goals & Methods

Keep many tickets cheaper than one house. Underwrite each other. Stay a league when Lestrand would rather negotiate with a single spokesman.

**Its injustice.** **Falling between charters.** Vouched here, unknown there; Tithe-provision a town matter the compact will not touch. Thin welfare in a rich network. The fade happens in the gaps the league calls local.
