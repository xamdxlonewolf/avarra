# Brenledd

> **The Hearth-League.** *(formal *Brenledd*, BREN-ledd · [[The Old Tongue|bren]] "hearth" + [[The Old Tongue|ledd]] "to reckon.")* Eroded drift. Theology low · reach high · governance low.

People say **the Hearth-League**, **the compact**, and **League-folk**. Charters and learned maps write *Brenledd*.

## Overview (public)

A **league of charter-towns** on [[Strandoren]] — river-ports, lesser harbours, inland markets — that compact to face [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]]'s houses without becoming one house. Each town is a hearth with its own Tree and its own vouching. The compact is the arithmetic: shared tickets, shared notes, shared refusal to be priced one at a time.

Faith worn light, same as the coast. Trees mature. Nothing is policed; everything is *chartered*.

**Site.** The constellation behind and between the premier coast. Leftover: salt and river towns; some road-end Tree-towns that federated instead of fading into a deathless house's hinterland.

**How it formed.** Towns that got tired of being the other party on a Lestrand note. After [[The Grey Summer]], in C.Y. 174, **Nidtol**'s hearth piled the first shared refusal — berth-fees in a summer with no berths, notes called early because a house was frightened. Network emergence, not a founding king: the compact is a pile of charters that learned to speak together. [[The Hinge Hush]] added a side-paper: hinge-tolls will not price them one hearth at a time either. Layered, ugly, and alive.

**What they remember.** Lestrand still offers to simplify them. The fade happens in the gaps the league calls local. Chronicle: [[The Other Count]].

## Structure & Membership

A council of hearths. You are of *your* town first. Recruits not by Kind. A person vouched in one Brenled square may still be a stranger in the next — the compact moves coin better than it moves standing.

## Goals & Methods

Keep many tickets cheaper than one house. Underwrite each other. Stay a league when Lestrand would rather negotiate with a single throat.

**Its injustice.** **Falling between charters.** Vouched here, unknown there; Tithe-provision a town matter the compact will not touch. Thin welfare in a rich network. The fade happens in the gaps the league calls local.
