# The Reckoned Offices

People call these four jobs **the Reckoned Offices**, or simply **the tablet offices**. They are not a church, and they are not a sixteenth power, because the fifteen great powers are already named and these jobs sit inside one of them. They are four jobs the Tallied Crown, [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]], already had, and what a party can do now is go and find them by name.

[[Maiethlir]] still has **Rithim** *(RITH-im)* as town Speaker, the person who Speaks at the town's Tree and names the colour that fell, or the hug, which is no colour at all. These offices speak for the roll, the [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]], which is the Crown's list of persons. They are not a replacement for the hearth, the neighbours who stood and watched the child. The gap between the Speaking and the copy is where a party can stand.

## The four offices

They recruit by the office and by the practice of the work, not by Kind, the ancestry a person is born into. Long-Lived memory is common at the tablet, and a Long-Lived person does not age and does not forget what they have witnessed, so here that memory is bent to the census rather than left free to check it. That bending is already how this corner of the Crown works.

### Menirein — registrar

**Menirein** *(men-EE-rayn)* keeps the Threnhael's local book, and what they want from it is a roll that is complete. An unlisted child is, to them, a child the Mother cannot be thanked for, and a child the crown cannot keep hale. They will knock on a door, including a party's door, because they believe they are doing care.

They do not Speak the fall themselves. Once they have heard it, they copy it, and if Rithim will not let the copy happen in the same breath as the Speaking, Menirein will wait in the square until the water quiets, and then write what they heard.

Their good-faith agenda is *no one unmarked*, and the harm comes from that same wish.

### Tarvae — Reckoned Speaker

**Tarvae** *(TAR-vay)* is the crown's preferred Speaker when a fall is mixed or argumentative, a Leaf-Fall where the colour can be read two ways or the square is already arguing. They want the Speaking and the filing to stay one act. A colour Spoken and not written is, to them, a seeing that can be stolen. They will stand beside the town-warden, and they will not always wait for Rithim to finish a breath.

They are still a Speaker, and they still name the fall truly when they can. The roll is the second seeing, the lesser one, and they refuse to call it lesser.

Their good-faith agenda is one name, spoken and filed in one breath, and the fight with Rithim is the one that is live. Tarvae does not lie about colours, so the dispute is about when the copy is allowed.

### Videm — channel-clerk

**Videm** *(VEE-dem)* runs the counted hall and keeps the slate that matches the tended green. They want the channel to hold, so that the late week never comes, the week the channel will not admit that someone went without. A Far-Voiced cut off from the counted hour is one leak: that person's voice carries feeling, and without the hour the feeling breaks into a Cry that everyone nearby has to hear. A Taken-In wilted off the lot is another leak, a person failing for want of green, and a novelty the archive will not release is a third leak of the same kind. Videm will work late to stop a leak, and will also mark a name *late* so the leak can be blamed on the person who carries it.

Their good-faith agenda is no Cry in the market and no wilt in the street, and the injustice is that staying whole runs through them.

They are not a separate faction of the hall. The hall is [[Tithe-Infrastructure]], the civic greens and halls that keep people whole enough, held on a crown leash. Videm is the hand on that leash.

### Sirtal — warrant-hand

**Sirtal** *(SEER-tal)* writes the warrant that withholds the share. The warrant can close the green, the counted hour, a novelty, or a stiller's bed, the bed where a stiller works, a stiller being a person whose gaze can hold a wound or a patient still. Sirtal wants the roll and the channel to match before that share opens. A name that will not sit still does not get it, whether the name was bought from someone paid to claim they watched, is missing from the book, belongs to the Closed of the Inner Close, or is unvouched, with nobody who will stand and say they were there. Sirtal believes a share given to an unlisted person is how the realm sickens.

Their good-faith agenda is that the channel opens only for a name the offices already have, and the injustice is that same withhold dressed up as care.

They will sit a gate to keep the share shut, and they will sit a party's gate in the same way.

## What they refuse

- They will not capture the [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]]. Nearness is the pressure they use, and the college of the tree-wardens stays in the wood.
- They will not copy the Book of Tithes without a fight, and the lists stay two. [[The Walled Book]] ranks people by the Tithe they carry, while these offices count people and mean the count as care.
- They will not stamp a Bound's Terms. A Bound is a person who swore a contract, and the Terms of that contract are another table's paper.
- They will not sell a standing, a person paid to say they watched you Turn in a town that did not see it. [[The Standing Trade]] is a leak they want written on the roll, and it is not a service they will hire.
- They will not export a Threnhael, this roll of persons, to [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]].

## How they reach a party

Menirein wants you listed on the roll, Tarvae wants your colour copied in the breath it was Spoken, Videm wants you on the slate that opens the channel, and Sirtal wants you kept still until the first three have you. A campaign can meet one of them without the other three, because they do not move as a squad. When they do move together, someone is about to lose a channel.

## In-world text

*Four docket-marks from the same Eolthael, the high-solstice month, written in the tablet-hall by four hands.*

> *Menirein: the child is unmarked. I will wait in the square.*
> *Tarvae: the fall was storm-grey. File it in the breath. A delayed copy is a stolen seeing.*
> *Videm: the counted hour matches the name. If the name is late, the hour is late. The hall held.*
> *Sirtal: no green until the name sits. A share to an unlisted bed is how a channel fails.*
> *Rithim (verso, not a Reckoned mark): I Spoke. I did not copy. The water was loud. The Tree was enough.*

The four marks are competent, and they are four different cares that cannot all be met in the same hour. Rithim's verso is the gap the offices exist to close.

## GM Notes

- **Antagonists can stay good-faith and still knock.** That was the point of giving them names. Do not make them a dark tetrarchy, and do not make them a squad shaped like a party. Each one can arrive alone, still believing the errand is care.
- **Do not capture the First Seat.** Do not replace Rithim, and do not make Tarvae a villain. Tarvae is the pressure in the square. Rithim is the incomplete refusal already written on [[Maiethlir]], the Speaker who sometimes will not copy.
- **Not an opposition group.** These offices are the Tallied Crown's own instruments. The three opposition groups are [[The Pourers]], [[The Walled Book]], and [[The Protectors]]. Threnmaieth's injustice stays the sacred census, the roll kept as care. Do not let the Walled Book become Threnmaieth's inner court.
- **Names.** Menirein, Tarvae, Videm, and Sirtal come from seed `20260830`, the conservative list, taken from the mid-bottom of that list. Their initials stay distinct, M, T, V, and S, against Rithim. The office-names are common tongue. Do not add a new liturgy.
- **Instruments, not pivots.** These four are instruments of the roll. They are not positional pivots, and they do not grow full arcs on this page. A later pass over the capital's cast may pick one of them up. It does not have to.
- **Hooks.** An unmarked child. A copy delayed in the square. A counted hour marked late. A green withheld. Clerks sent into Maiethvael. Clerks at the Close. Tarvae and Rithim standing in one square.
