# The Hinge Shore

> **The Motherland's Old-Crossing ports.** Devotion is middling, reach is high, and the government is light. They decide whether a hull is pilgrim, pot, person, or cargo before anyone across the water prices it.

**The Hinge Shore** is both the spoken and written name; its people are **Hinge-folk**. Some old dockets write *Eolstrand*, a form no current authority uses.

## Overview (public)

[[Maiethorn]]'s western ports on **[[Named Ground|the Old Crossing]]** — the oldest trade route in the world. Ancient, rich, less frantic than the Ledger Coast. Trees still healthy because this is still the Motherland. Faith worn *thinner* than [[Maiethvael]] because a dock teaches categories.

[[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] **prices** the crossing. The Hinge Shore **sorts** it. A child, a pot of living earth, a Given-Over, a recanter, a necessity-family on a neighbour's week — the quay-bell can ring the same note for all five. A clerk writes which docket. That writing is the job. [[The Standing Trade]] works this face more often than [[Orentel]] because a standing has to travel.

[[Selkie]] families are common on these quays, as they are on any old water. That is a hearth, not a thalassocracy.

**Site.** Old Crossing face. It has salt-adjacent Old-World quays, on the other shore of the Salt Walk: people embarked from Strandoren and landed here. The **Hush-rate** is charged as old custom ([[The Hinge Hush]]). They will not call it a victory.

**Seat.** [[Votaer]] is the port, one of the largest, because the crossing is why the people are there. Hulls are classified on those quays. The Hush-rate is charged there. It faces [[Orentel]] and is not a second Orentel. Towns and villages along the rest of the shore stay unnamed.

**How it formed.** Port-kings and charter-harbours grown from the crossing. The tongue is conservative because the grove is at their backs, and devotion is middling because Lestrand is in front of them. [[The Grey Summer]] taught the pious shore how to step around a fade *without putting the blessing down.* The cooler Lestrand way of doing things began here as a matter of weather, not as a conversion.

## Structure & Membership

Guild-coast, light state, old houses. Recruits not by Kind. [[Far-Voiced]] on every quay as infrastructure, same as Strandoren, with more hymn left in the voice. Taerso spoke the Hush for the hinge-houses; the name is a desk, not a throne.

## Goals & Methods

Keep the Old Crossing the world's highway. Classify the hull before anyone prices it. Broker pilgrimage-traffic and cargo without becoming either Threnmaieth's counted port or Lestrand's priced one. Charge the Hush-rate. Say the blessing if a customer wants one.

**Its injustice.** **Category as fate.** Lestrand will let you fade and feel it owed you nothing. The Hinge Shore will *bless you as it files you*, and a Tithe-poor household fails in sight of the pilgrimage-road with a hymn still in the air. They learned the step-around from looking across the water in a grey summer. They kept the hymn so they would not have to call the step a conversion.

## GM Notes

- *Eolstrand* was a proposed compound and is retired, because *Eoloren, Eolthael,* and *Eolvaeth* already carry the hinge-root across an era, a month, and a city. Old dockets may keep the form as a dateable scribal fashion.
- Do not flesh districts.
- Seat is [[Votaer]]. Do not make it a second Orentel.
- Do not make a Selkie nation.
- Do not let this flag carry [[Rothallo]], still called the Inner Close.
- The three corner seats stay where they are. Votaer is this shore's port.

- **Not Lestrand-lite.** Same high reach, cooler faith — on the *conservative* shore. The independent-axes proof is the *job*: classify, do not price. Do not let mid-hymns read as thin Trees.
- **Not a Selkie flag.** Coasts are hearths.
- **Not the remnant.** The Closed Seat sat the grove. The Retreat closed walls a day from the Motherwood. Do not move the Book here to tidy a stub.
- **Hooks.** A cargo that is also a pilgrim; a Hush-rate on a neighbour's week; a Lestrand note protested in a Motherland court; a Far-Voiced dock-witness who will not sell their feeling; Nomele of the Hinge selling a standing; dock-bells that still pause in Hale-month, then go back to cargo.
