# The Hinge Shore

> **The Motherland's Old-Crossing ports.** Devotion is middling, reach is high, and the government is light. They decide whether a hull is a pilgrim, a pot, a person, or cargo before anyone across the water prices it.

**The Hinge Shore** is the name people say and the name the maps write, and its people are **Hinge-folk**. Some old dockets write *Eolstrand*, a form no current authority uses.

## Overview (public)

These are [[Maiethorn]]'s western ports on **[[Named Ground|the Old Crossing]]**, the oldest trade route in the world. The shore is ancient and rich, and less frantic than the Ledger Coast, the spoken name of the power whose charters write Lestrand. The Trees are still healthy, because this is still the Motherland. Faith is worn *thinner* here than in [[Maiethvael]], because a dock's work is to sort one arrival from another.

[[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] **prices** the crossing, and the Hinge Shore **sorts** it. A child, a pot of living earth, a Given-Over, a recanter, and a necessity-family on a neighbour's week can all make the quay-bell ring the same note. The pot is soil and living wood for a graft. A Given-Over is a person bargained onward until the contract holds them. A recanter is someone taking back a standing, the claim that a witness watched them. A necessity-family on the neighbour's week is walking to a Tree that can do the work, because the one at home cannot. The bell does not tell those five apart. A clerk writes which docket they belong on, and that writing is the job. [[The Standing Trade]] works this face more often than [[Orentel]], because a standing has to travel and this is the water it has to cross.

[[Selkie]] families are common on these quays, as they are on any old water. That is a hearth of families, not a sea-realm with the Selkie as its flag.

**Site.** The shore is the Old Crossing's face: salt-adjacent Old-World quays, on the other side of the Salt Walk from the coast people embarked on. People left [[Strandoren]] and landed here, and the **Hush-rate**, the charge left behind by [[The Hinge Hush]], is collected as old custom. They will not call it a victory.

**Seat.** [[Votaer]] is the port, one of the largest, because the crossing is why the people are there. Hulls are classified on those quays, and the Hush-rate is charged there. The port faces [[Orentel]] without becoming a second Orentel, and the towns and villages along the rest of the shore stay unnamed.

**How it formed.** Port-kings and charter-harbours grew up on the crossing. The tongue stayed conservative because the grove is at their backs, and the devotion stayed middling because Lestrand is in front of them. [[The Grey Summer]] taught this pious shore how to step around a fade, a household failing for want of what it needs, without putting the blessing down. The cooler way of doing things that Lestrand is known for began here as the weather of that summer, not as a conversion.

## Structure & Membership

The shore is a guild-coast with a light state and old houses. It does not recruit by Kind, the ancestry a person is born with. The [[Far-Voiced]], people whose feeling comes through in the voice and who are used as witnesses, stand on every quay as infrastructure, the same as on Strandoren, with more hymn left in the voice. Taerso spoke the Hush for the hinge-houses, and the name is a desk, not a throne.

## Goals & Methods

They keep the Old Crossing as the world's highway by classifying a hull before anyone prices it, and they broker pilgrimage-traffic and cargo without becoming [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]]'s counted port or Lestrand's priced one. They charge the Hush-rate, and they say the blessing if a customer wants one.

**Its injustice.** The harm is **category as fate**. Lestrand will let you fade, and it will feel that it owed you nothing. The Hinge Shore will bless you in the same hour that it files you, and a Tithe-poor household, one that cannot pay the upkeep it needs, fails in sight of the pilgrimage-road with a hymn still in the air. They learned that step-around by looking across the water in a grey summer. They kept the hymn so they would not have to call the step a conversion.

## GM Notes

*Eolstrand* was a proposed compound, and it is retired, because *Eoloren*, *Eolthael*, and *Eolvaeth* already carry the hinge-root across an era, a month, and a city. Old dockets may keep the form as a scribal fashion that a reader can date.

Leave the districts unfleshed, keep the seat at [[Votaer]], and do not make that port a second Orentel or a Selkie nation. Do not let this flag carry [[Rothallo]], which people still call the Inner Close. The three corner seats stay where they already are, and Votaer is this shore's port.

**Not Lestrand-lite.** The reach is the same high reach and the faith is cooler, and both of those sit on the conservative shore. The proof that the axes are independent is the job itself: this shore classifies a hull, and it does not price one. Do not read the mid-hymns as if the Trees were thin.

**Not a Selkie flag.** The coasts are hearths, families on the water rather than a people turned into a realm.

**Not the remnant.** The Closed Seat sat the grove, and the Retreat closed its walls a day from the Motherwood, so do not move the Book onto this shore in order to tidy a stub.

**Hooks.** A cargo can also be a pilgrim, a Hush-rate can fall on a neighbour's week, and a Lestrand note can be protested in a Motherland court. A Far-Voiced dock-witness can refuse to sell their feeling, Nomele of the Hinge can sell a standing, and the dock-bells can still pause in Hale-month before they go back to cargo.
