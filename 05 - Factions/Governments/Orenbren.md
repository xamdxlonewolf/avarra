# Orenbren

> **The Witness-Hearths.** *(formal *Orenbren*, OREN-bren · [[The Old Tongue|oren]] "to witness" + [[The Old Tongue|bren]] "hearth.")* The drift of the name is conservative, the old sounds kept. **It does not own [[Turning Tree|Thaeloren]] or the [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]].**

People say **the Witness-Hearths** and **Hearth-folk**. Charters and learned maps write *Orenbren*. It began as a string of inns and became a country, not a single house.

## Overview (public)

Orenbren is the civic mass of the **Near Mile**, the approach to the grove: lodging-squares, upper rooms, and old road-houses that stayed hearths after the walk stopped being required in the core. Pilgrims still come. Some are on [[The Years of Hands|the extra mile]], the walk to the grove that devotion still makes when a local Tree would have done. Some are buying the First-Hand year, the prestige season spent near that same grove. Orenbren **houses** them. The power in that housing is not a crown set on the grove. It is a hold on the beds within a week's walk of it. Orenbren does not sell the Coast's product. The berth is [[Orentel]]'s hold, the paper is [[The White Note House]], the hull may be [[Netstrand]], and what Orenbren takes is the sleep.

The [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]] sits in the Motherwood beside this power and remains a college, not a throne. [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] would like the lodging-rolls. Orenbren witnesses who slept, and it does not witness what colour they are.

Inside the lodging-country, and not as a sixteenth flag, sits **[[Rothallo]]**, the walled capital, still called the [[The Walled Book|Inner Close]]. The Closed Heirs still rank the Conditioned, the people who carry a Condition, by a Book of Tithes. Orenbren calls the arrangement a guest-compact. Its inns lodge anyone outside the wall, and the Book rules inside it. The Close records who *owes*, Orenbren records who slept, and neither copies the other's records. Smaller towns and villages around the walls stay unnamed. The First Seat stays in the wood, the wood is not crowned, and Rothallo is not the college.

**Site.** This is [[Maiethorn]]'s Sacred Core approach-country, an origin pilgrimage-town repeated along the road. A Tree stands at the centre of each square. The road used to go further, and now it ends in a festival season.

**How it formed.** These were walking-era hearths that federated around the one-week door, the single week in which a child had to be present or miss being Given. In [[The Closing]] they would not starve a pilgrim-season in order to save the grove, and that refusal is the lodging-country's first weather. When the Years of Hands made the origin-walk optional, the beds did not empty, and the work those beds did was simply a different job. The lodging-network learned it could levy, charter, and speak as one without becoming clergy. The guest-compact with the Inner Close starts at Hildal's Retreat: beds outside the wall, and the Book inside it.

**What they remember.** They housed the war by keeping the extra beds open. They did not sit the grove then, and they do not sit it now. Threnmaieth wants the lodging-rolls, the Close ranks who owes, and the chronicle of both arguments is [[The Other Count]].

## Structure & Membership

Orenbren is many hearth-towns, not a single inn, run by town-wardens and lodging-guilds. It does not recruit by Kind, the ancestry a person is born with. The capital is [[Rothallo]], still called the Inner Close, one day from the wood. Deathless houses exist here as *patrons of beds*, great houses that do not die with their founders and that pay to keep rooms. [[The Third Hearth]] is the fleshed square of that type, and it is not this power's capital. The neighbour Hands-town is **Brenthael**. Further out the Mile, [[The Mill-hold]] is a Hands-town whose Hand, the local Tree, is unsound this year. It is not a capital, and it is not Brenthael. [[Nelath]] is a road-end on a spur that leaves the Mile a day short of the Third Hearth and stops. It is not a capital, and it is not the seat.

## Goals & Methods

They keep the extra mile possible, and they charge for it in coin, in duty, or in hymn. Which of the three a given town uses is the local argument. They refuse to be named as the First Seat's civic arm, and they refuse to be named as Threnmaieth's counted inn.

**Its injustice.** The harm is **hospitality as rent**, devotion priced as board. A pilgrim who cannot pay the First-Hand year still needs the hug, the moment under the Tree they came for, and the upper room has learned what a bed is worth in Eolthael, the month when the children stand. The kindness comes with a slate.

## GM Notes

Leave the districts unfleshed. The seat is [[Rothallo]], Inner Close stays an alias for it, and the alias stays inside Orenbren. The three corner seats stay [[Eolvaeth]], [[Orentel]], and [[Maiethlir]], and Rothallo is this power's capital without joining that trio.

**Do not capture the First Seat.** The clergy's college is in the wood, and the civic lodging is in the towns. [[Maiethlir]] is Threnmaieth's counted river-seat, which makes it neither this lodging-country nor the grove, and that gap has to hold.

**Do not clone a deathless house as the state.** [[The Third Hearth]] is already fleshed, [[The White Note House]] stays a seed, Orenbren remains many hearths, and Brenthael is a neighbour square rather than a capital.

**Rothallo is the capital, and it is not a sixteenth power.** Inner Close stays the alias. **Placement:** they retreated here because this is where the sitting was, so do not move the Book to the Hinge Shore, do not move the walls out of Orenbren, and do not capture the First Seat. The wood is not crowned, and Rothallo is not the college.

**Prestige-walk chain.** Orenbren is where the beds are at the end of the walk, and it is not a fourth seller of the berth.

**Hooks.** Turning-Week can fill the rooms, a house can keep a Held bed it will not let, and Threnmaieth can ask who slept. A Road-hand can use an upper room as if the walk had not ended, a First-Hand-year student can come for the prestige and find a duty waiting, and a term can clear on the quay while the bed does not. The Close can be shut while the beds outside are full, and [[The Mill-hold]] can send a cohort *in* to Brenthael while Meirim still wants the inn's stone down.

**Do not make the Mill-hold a capital.** It is a sick-Tree square, and Brenthael's Hand stays sound.
