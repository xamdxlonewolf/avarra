# Saelthael

> **The Sown Tree.** *(formal *Saelthael*, SAEL-thael · [[The Old Tongue|sael]] "sowing" + [[The Old Tongue|thael]] "Tree.")* The drift of the name is conservative, the old sounds kept. Devotion is strong, the reach is thinner because the country runs on wells rather than woods, and the government is light.

People say **the Sown Tree** and **Sown-folk**. Charters and learned maps write *Saelthael*. The shared *sael* with [[Saelvaeth|the Sown Waiting]] remains visible in the writing. Speech keeps the two powers apart.

## Overview (public)

Saelthael is [[Maiethorn|the Rain-Shadow]]: the country leeward and east of [[Named Ground|the Rain-Wall]], dry hills and sand, with towns set at wells and at whatever graft will take. The seasonal water is **[[Named Ground|the Well-wash]]**, on a continent that is otherwise full-reach, though this side of it is **thinner wood**. The core stopped walking once its own Trees were enough, and Saelthael did not. Children still go west in the neighbour's week, the walk to a Tree that can do the work when theirs cannot, or they go further than a week, because a well-Tree is not a certainty.

[[Kitsune|Fox of the Sands]] hearths are densest here. That density is the terrain, not a flag ([[Kinds of the Turning]]). A town with *no* fox-born would be the thing worth asking about.

**Site.** This is Maiethorn's dry east, a Rain-Shadow walk-hold on the west-road, which the core stopped walking. The hold is the proof that Ornthael, the present years in which a town Tree is supposed to be enough, is unfinished even on the origin continent. The fleshed square is [[Ornsael]], the sown-ground, a well-town that is still walking west.

**Seat.** [[Larbril]] is the city, a medium one, where the west road meets [[Named Ground|the Well-wash]], short of the pass. Ornsael stays the smaller well-town, farther into the dry. [[The Dry Stair]] is not the reason for the city.

**How it formed.** Tree-seats formed around wells and around grafts that took slowly. From Cut-year 0 to 80 the work was slower here, and the cause was dirt, not a different gospel. When the origin-squares became Hands-country, places where a local Tree could do the week's work, the sand-country kept the habit of going to a healthier canopy. That habit grew into a polity of the people who still sow. [[The Thaw-Break]] cut the west-road for two summers, so some children went later and some missed the door, and the Given-door, the week in which a child can still be Given, did not loosen.

**What they remember.** The core calls their waiting a matter of soil. [[The Dry Stair]] is the local dare that is *not* a Tree. They climb it so they know the difference, and then they walk west. The chronicle is [[The Other Count]].

## Structure & Membership

Custom is light: town-wardens keep it, and the wells are a civic fact, something the town has to manage whether or not anyone is devout that week. The realm does not recruit by Kind, the ancestry a person is born with. [[The Taken-In]] are honoured here as they are on any thirsty land, the backbone of the work and not a nation of their own.

## Goals & Methods

They keep grafts alive in thin soil, they keep the west-road open, and they refuse the core's story that waiting here is a spiritual failing.

**Its injustice.** The motherland treats their waiting as a **local soil-problem**, which is true as far as it goes, and also as a slight against the origin. Plenty sits next door. The pilgrimage has become a leftover they cannot retire. Vaethorn's guilt, the teaching that want is a failing of the soul, has been imported onto sand that never asked for it.

## GM Notes

Leave the districts unfleshed, and do not make a Fox kingdom. [[Ornsael]] is the playable square. It stays the well-town, and it is not the seat, which remains [[Larbril]]. Do not rename Ornsael into the city, and do not make the Dry Stair the city's reason.

**Not a Kitsune nation.** The Sands-Fox are the densest hearth here, the Kinds are mixed, and ears do not go on the flag.

**Pair with Saelvaeth.** The two share a root and amount to two clocks' worth of the same planting, so do not narrate Saelthael as history and Saelvaeth as frontier flavour.

**Keystone:** a thin take in dry soil stays horticulture in the text a player reads.

**Hooks.** A west-road cohort can leave [[Ornsael]], a well-graft can sicken, and a core pilgrim can be shocked to find the neighbour's week still happening *on Maiethorn*. A Sands-Fox warden can be tired of being asked whether this is a fox-country. Pair that week with [[Harrow's Green]] so both clocks are visible in one campaign.
