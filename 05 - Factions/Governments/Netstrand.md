# Netstrand

> **The Night Shore.** *(formal *Netstrand*, NET-strand · eroded [[The Old Tongue|neth]] "night; the long dark" + *strand* "shore.")*

People say **the Night Shore** and **Night-folk**. Charters and learned maps write *Netstrand*. They keep watch for ships that do not arrive, and one unlit berth still has no house attached to it.

## Overview (public)

[[Strandoren]]'s west and south face is **[[Named Ground|the West Water]]**, the open ocean toward [[Heskoren]] and, far beyond that, the storm-wall. The bold houses run the frontier trade. What they sell is **hulls and west-water berths**: crews, capacity, and a crossing quoted the way other shores quote a pilgrimage. They do **not** sell the First-Hand year, the origin-winter, as a finished product. That year is assembled on the Old Crossing, and the work is split: Netstrand charters the ship, [[The White Note House]] writes the terms, and [[Orentel]] holds the berth. Almost none of the people of this shore have crossed to [[The Sundering Isle]]. The people who claim they have are the tellers of Yumboe stories.

Faith on this shore is mixed. Motherfaith is worn light, the Fair Hand is ordinary on a signing-quay, and there are quay-charms nobody writes down. The Trees along this wealthy edge are still mature.

**Site.** The coast faces the open ocean. It has a salt quay, and it holds empty berths in Eolthael, the month when the children stand, for the Heskoren run and for hulls chartered *in* to Orentel's first quay. The holds smell of earth because graft-pots are going out. A neighbour's week, the walk to someone else's Tree when the one at home cannot do the work, gets billed here as a crossing. The far crossing is this shore's prestige, and the origin-winter is not.

**Seat.** [[Braetu]] is the harbour city on this face, smaller than [[Orentel]] because the far run is thinner than the Old Crossing. A far crossing is quoted there, and a lamp can be kept on a name. The unlit berth stays one berth in that city, with no house attached, and the dark book stays a dark book. [[Selkie]] stay common on the quays and off the flag, the towns and villages along the rest of the shore stay unnamed, and Braetu is not a second Orentel.

**How it formed.** The west-houses grew out of Salt Walk traffic and the later carrying to Heskoren, which [[The First Cut]] set in motion by sea, the coasts first and then the far water. This is a shore that faces the wave's supply-line, not the grove. After [[The Grey Summer]] the west trade thinned for a decade. After [[The Hinge Hush]] they kept the **west-water clause**: Netstrand quotes the far crossing, Saelvaeth may take a pot without a waiting-due, Vaethorn may bless a landing and may not levy it, and Ornled is a beach-fee.

Vaethorn waits for wood the way a devout shore waits on a gift, and the Night Shore waits for hulls the way a house waits on a fare. From the quay the two waits can look alike. Vaethorn is waiting in faith, and the Night Shore is waiting for money.

## Structure & Membership

Merchant houses, berth-guilds, and a light charter-council run the shore. It does not recruit by Kind, the ancestry a person is born with. [[Selkie]] are common here without becoming the flag, sky-couriers come from [[Lirorn]], and Tengu families keep the clock-towers, all of it as jobs rather than as a district of their own.

They keep a **dark book** of quoted crossings that never returned, and a Held bed for hulls, which is a berth held back and not let onward. Houses pay to keep a lamp on a name. The book is not a Threnhael, the sacred roll of persons, and it is not a White Note. It is the only record the shore keeps of its lost ships.

## Goals & Methods

They quote the far crossing, keep the Heskoren run profitable, and charter hulls to the Old Crossing when inland coin wants an origin-winter. They sell tales of the Isle without having to survive the crossing those tales describe. They do not let Trenledd write the berths, and they do not claim the First-Hand year is theirs to crown.

**Its injustice.** The harm is **the crossing as inventory**. Prestige is priced, necessity is billed, and a neighbour's week owed to a waiting cluster becomes a line on a docket. Kindness is for the people who can book. The origin-year they do not own still uses their wood. A lamp kept on a name that will not come back is charged as *watch*, not as grief.

## GM Notes

Leave the districts unfleshed. Do not make this the explorer-nation, do not book Kumbaan, and do not re-book the First-Hand year as something this shore owns. The seat is [[Braetu]], so do not attach a house to the unlit berth, do not move the White Note, do not make Braetu a second Orentel, keep the Selkie off the flag, and do not census the rest of the shore.

**Night is a job, not a badge.** They light lamps for the ships that quoted a crossing last year, and that wait mirrors Vaethorn's without collapsing into it, because one wait is gospel and the other is inventory.

One berth stays unlit, and no house is attached to it. It might be a night-watch, or the dark of the storm-wall, or a name the dark book will not take. Pick among those in play, or never pick, and do not solve the berth with a Watching custom that does not belong this far west.

**Staging ground for Isle expeditions** stays on Strandoren, with Netstrand as the natural berth. Hold open whether a claim of having crossed is true. A taking on Kumbaan is still forbidden.

**Prestige-walk chain.** Netstrand supplies the berths and the hulls, the White Note supplies the terms, Orentel supplies the holds, and Orenbren supplies the destination beds without becoming a fourth seller of the year.

**Hooks.** A pot can sit in a hold, and a hull can be chartered to Orentel whose passengers thought the berth *was* the hug. A Yumboe story can be a lie, and a Yumboe story can be not a lie. A Saelvaeth town can wait on a ship that quoted them last year, a lamp can burn on a name, and the unlit berth can stay unlit.
