# Dangers of the Turning

> **What this is.** This is a small Daggerheart layer for the road, the rolls you make on the Long Mile, in the wild of Heskoren, and on the named waters, which is the country between settlements. A settlement already has the things that can enter one of its scenes, a cohort, a crane, a well-mouth, and this page is for the ground where none of those notes are open. The distances, and how long the walks take, are on [[Named Ground#Travel times]].

> **The monsters are already people.** A Condition is a neighbour with a job and a life, so this layer does not add a second vampire, a cursed wolf-kin, or a hungry dead who is not already a [[Returned]], and the beasts and the weather stay what they already are. What threatens a traveler out here is people who missed their week, the animals on the road, and the weather itself, which is a real obstacle under ordinary travel and not a menagerie of extra monsters.

## When to open this

Open this page when the party is between mile-shrines, when the next shrine is a day later than it should have been, or when a Salt Walk, one of the named walks, or a berth on the Chart-run does not arrive. Open it for inland Heskoren, off the live-front road, when a Two-Bodied, a person who already carries that Condition, says they are ranging and you need something in the country to range against, and when the danger you want is not a hearing and not a guild.

A city street does not need this page, so you use the line on the settlement's own note that says what enters a scene.

## Battle points (SRD)

You start from a budget of `[(3 × PCs) + 2]`. A Minion group costs 1, and a Support/Social adversary costs 1. A Horde, a Ranged adversary, a Skulk, or a Standard each costs 2. A Leader costs 3, a Bruiser costs 4, and a Solo costs 5. At Tier 1 the benchmark Difficulty is 11, the damage thresholds are 7/12, and the attack bonus is +1. At Tier 2 the Difficulty is 14, the thresholds are 10/20, and the attack bonus is +2.

## Adversaries

| Name | Tier / type | Where it lives | What it wants |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Gap-Cat]] | T1 Skulk | Rain-Wall shelves, Heskoren ridge, mile-gaps | A quiet kill, and then the high rock back |
| [[Chart-Crew]] | T1 Horde | Chart-run, Night Shore, West Water wrecks | The hull that did not arrive, or yours if that one is gone |
| [[Bed-Keeper]] | T1 Leader | Empty road-houses on the Long Mile | The bed occupied, and you staying in it |
| [[Spent-Week Walker]] | T2 Standard | Long Mile, west-road, live-front tracks | To finish a tenth-year week that has already closed |
| [[Colourless Host]] | T2 Support | Dead pots, unsound grafts, waiting ground | To Give a Condition the way a Tree would, which it cannot do |

None of these stat blocks are Conditions. A walker may carry a Condition the way any person might, and the card they carry is not this block. The Spent-Week Walker and the Colourless Host are Tier 2 blocks, so in a Tier 1 scene you use the Tier 1 Difficulty, thresholds, and attack bonus from the benchmarks above in place of the numbers on their own notes.

## Environments

You use one of these as the scene, and you drop a single row from the adversary table into it. An impulse is what the place does when you spend Fear, the currency the GM uses to make a scene move, or when someone fails a roll to travel.

### Mile-gap — Tier 1 Traversal

This is the day's walk between two mile-stones, and the next shrine depends on the weather, rather than on a promise that the stone will be there when you need it.

The place wants to stretch the day, hide the next stone, offer a false bed, and make a Tithe expensive.

**Difficulty:** 12

**Potential adversaries:** [[Gap-Cat]] · [[Spent-Week Walker]] · [[Bed-Keeper]]

| Feature | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **The next stone is a rumour** | Passive | Travel rolls to find the next mile-shrine are Difficulty 12, and on a failure with Fear the day ends in the gap, so mark a Stress or spend the night here. |
| **Tithe at a distance** | Passive | A [[The Taken-In\|Taken-In]] who cannot touch living green, or a [[Long-Lived]] with nothing new, feels the gap. Their next long rest runs the card's check as usual, and the gap adds no extra tick. A [[Far-Voiced]] is not on this feature, because that clock is triggered by a scene, and everyone else is only tired. |
| **A bed that is not a shrine** | Action | Spend a Fear to put an occupied road-house in Very Far range, with a fire in a window and a voice that knows the walk-custom. It may be a [[Bed-Keeper]], or it may be empty and still have a fire. |
| **Weather on the stones** | Action | Spend a Fear and the stones take hail, a wash-out, or a night with no moon. Everyone marks a Stress, or takes 1d6+2 physical damage from the cold and from going over. A [[Two-Bodied]] in a form that can take the weather may mark an Armor Slot as usual. |

### Thaw-gate — Tier 1 Traversal

This is [[Named Ground|Shelf-gate]] or [[Named Ground|Noon Pass]] in a thaw-week, when the snow that fell last year is the road you are walking this year.

The place wants to close the pass, take a cart, bring back the memory of [[The Thaw-Break]], and make you wait there with people you did not choose.

**Difficulty:** 13

**Potential adversaries:** [[Gap-Cat]] · a local watch holding the bench until morning

| Feature | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **The cut is a lake** | Passive | Crossing without a guide is Difficulty 13. On a failure, the character is swept to Close range of the next rock and takes 1d8+2 physical damage, or they lose a pack-animal or a cart, and the GM chooses which. One roll does not do both. |
| **Two summers** | Passive | Anyone who lived the Thaw-Break, in Cut-year 233, has Advantage on the next travel roll here if they say what they remember. Anyone who did not live it has to trust someone who did. |
| **The pass takes a day** | Action | Spend a Fear to hold the party until the next thaw-bell. A watch, a guest-right, or a cohort sharing the rock becomes the scene, and someone asks the usual three things: name, last hearth, and who stands. |
| **Undercut** | Reaction | When a PC succeeds on a crossing roll, you may spend a Fear to collapse the path behind them. Allies still on the far side must roll again, or find another cut. |

### Empty road-house — Tier 1 Exploration

This is a [[The Walking Years|brenhael]] *(BREN-hael)*, a road-house those walking years left on the mile, and it kept the upper room after the walk ended, with someone still making the bed.

The place wants to seat you, ask which walk you are on, refuse to let the door be the end of the scene, and charge a Tithe in the form of hospitality.

**Difficulty:** 11

**Potential adversaries:** [[Bed-Keeper]] · [[Spent-Week Walker]] (as guest, or as the keeper's unfinished week)

| Feature | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Guest-right is a week** | Passive | If you eat, a host stands for the stay. Leaving before morning without a word is a slight the next shrine will hear about, and the watch-custom on [[Law and Citizenship]] still holds. |
| **The slate remembers** | Passive | A Long-Lived, or anyone who searches with care at Difficulty 11, finds names of people who should have gone home. Some of those people are still walking, and some of them are the keeper. |
| **Stay** | Action | Spend a Fear and the keeper offers a second night, a bed held for you, or a story that needs a listener. A PC who refuses marks a Stress, or makes a Presence roll at Difficulty 11 to leave cleanly. On a failure, the house takes offence, or a walker at the door takes the house's side. |
| **The upper room** | Action | Spend a Fear to reveal the room that was never let, a pack still tied and a week-slate stuck on Eolthael *(ay-ohl-THALE)*, the month the Leaf-Fall belongs to. Spotlight a [[Spent-Week Walker]], or give the Bed-Keeper Relentless (2) until that room is closed. |

### Night-berth — Tier 1 Exploration

This is a quay on the Chart-run, or on the West Water, at first dark, where a hull that does not arrive is counted as local weather.

The place wants to wait, to strip what the sea gives up, to sell a stand, and not to file a hill.

**Difficulty:** 12

**Potential adversaries:** [[Chart-Crew]] · a Night-Shore clerk who will not stamp a missing name

| Feature | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **The slate has empty lines** | Passive | A Presence or Knowledge roll at Difficulty 12 reads which berths are late. On a failure with Fear, you are asked to stand for a name you do not know. A company-vouch covers the passage, and it does not cover a hull that is already dead. |
| **What the water brought** | Action | Spend a Fear to wash a wreck, a crate, or a body into Close range of the quay, and the Chart-Crew move to take it. A Far-Voiced who Cries here without a hall-hour, the scheduled hour a ticket would have allowed, has a problem with that ticket, and the Cry is not a rescue. |
| **Coasting weather** | Action | Spend a Fear and the next day's sail is cancelled, or the last sail is simply missing. Travel on the table in [[Named Ground]] becomes "not a promise." Anyone who needed that hull in order to keep a walk-custom is now unvouched at this quay. |
| **We were here first** | Reaction | When a PC claims wreck-right or a berth, mark a Stress on a Chart-Crew, if one is present, and they contest the claim as a standard attack. If you would rather not roll damage yet, start a social scene at Difficulty 13 instead. |

A Colourless Host is a Support, so what it does is make the place worse, and you pair it with walkers or with a cat rather than with a second Host.

## What this layer is not

This layer is not a bestiary of Conditions, and when Two-Bodied characters range, they range against Gap-Cats rather than against a people the table would call werewolves. It is not the depth of an adventure site, which [[The Low Wall]], [[The Seeing-Ring]], and [[The Dry Stair]] already keep on their own notes. It is not a sixteenth power, a graft brought over from Kumbaan, or a haunted Tree, and dead wood on this page stays dead wood. It is also not permission to play the Leaf-Fall as something eerie, because the scare that belongs on the road is the weather, the hospitality a house will not let end, and the people who missed their week.

## GM Notes

- **Small on purpose.** There are five adversaries and four environments, and that size is the point. If a scene seems to need a sixth adversary, reskin a Gap-Cat or a Chart-Crew before you invent a new taxonomy to hold it.
- **The cliché pass already happened.** These are not wolves standing in for werewolves, they are not quay-pirates sailing under a flag, and they are not the undead. A Bed-Keeper wants the beds occupied, a Chart-Crew wants the hull that grief has already priced, a walker wants a week the wood will not reopen, and the Host wants to Give and cannot, which is the 5% and not a third Tree-outcome.
- **Do not confirm the Leaf-Mother.** A Colourless Host is horticulture that went wrong, or a pot that never took. Skeptics and believers can both be loud about what that means. The player-facing text does not settle the question.
- **The settlement layer stays where Story R.11 put it.** Do not restock Eolvaeth's cohort, or the well at Ornsael, as if they were these blocks. If the danger is a cohort someone sent, that notice belongs to Vaethod, and it is not a Spent-Week Walker, unless the cohort missed their week and kept walking anyway.
- **The battle-point math is the SRD's.** A Bed-Keeper, a Leader at 3 points, plus a Chart-Crew, a Horde at 2, plus two Gap-Cats, Skulks at 2 each, comes to 9 points. That is a light fight for four player characters, whose budget is 14. Most scenes on the road want one feature from the environment and one adversary, and then they want to get on with the travel.
