# Dangers of the Turning

> **What this is.** A small Daggerheart layer for the road: what you can *roll* on the Long Mile, in Heskoren's wild, and on the named waters. Settlements already have things that enter a scene (a cohort, a crane, a well-mouth). This page is the country between those doors. Distances: [[Named Ground#Travel times]].

> **The monsters are already people.** Conditions are neighbours. Do not stock this layer with a second vampire, a cursed wolf-kin, or a hungry dead that is not a [[Returned]]. Beasts are beasts. Weather is weather. The leftover walk is people who did not go home. The ~5% is a real obstacle under ordinary travel, not a menagerie.

## When to open this

- The party is between mile-shrines, or the next shrine is a day late.
- A Salt Walk or Chart-run berth does not arrive.
- Heskoren inland, off the live-front road.
- A Two-Bodied says they are ranging and you need something to range *against*.
- You want teeth that are not a hearing or a guild.

You do not need this for a city street. Use the settlement's "enters a scene" line.

## Battle points (SRD)

Start from `[(3 × PCs) + 2]`. Spend: Minion group 1 · Support/Social 1 · Horde/Ranged/Skulk/Standard 2 · Leader 3 · Bruiser 4 · Solo 5. Benchmarks: Tier 1 Diff 11, thresholds 7/12, ATK +1; Tier 2 Diff 14, 10/20, ATK +2.

## Adversaries

| Name | Tier / type | Where it lives | What it wants |
|---|---|---|---|

| [[Chart-Crew]] | T1 Horde | Chart-run, Night Shore, West Water wrecks | The hull that did not arrive — or yours |
| [[Bed-Keeper]] | T1 Leader | Empty road-houses on the Long Mile | The bed occupied. You staying. |
| [[Spent-Week Walker]] | T2 Standard | Long Mile, west-road, live-front tracks | To finish a week that already closed |

None of these are Conditions. A walker may *be* Conditioned the way anyone is. The card they carry is not this stat block.

## Environments

Use these as the scene. Drop one adversary table-row into them. Impulses are what the place does when you spend Fear or when someone fails a travel roll.

### Mile-gap — Tier 1 Traversal

The day's walk between two stones. The next shrine is weather, not a promise.

**Impulses:** stretch the day · hide the next stone · offer a false bed · make a Tithe expensive

**Difficulty:** 12

**Potential adversaries:** [[Gap-Cat]] · [[Spent-Week Walker]] · [[Bed-Keeper]]

| Feature | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **The next stone is a rumour** | Passive | Travel rolls to find the next mile-shrine are Difficulty 12. On a failure with Fear, the day ends in the gap. Mark a Stress or spend the night here. |
| **Tithe at a distance** | Passive | A [[The Taken-In\|Taken-In]] who cannot touch living green, a [[Long-Lived]] with nothing new, a [[Far-Voiced]] with no outlet: their rest clock ticks as if the gap were a long rest they did not meet. Others are only tired. |
| **A bed that is not a shrine** | Action | Spend a Fear to put an occupied road-house in Very Far range — fire in a window, a voice that knows the walk-custom. It may be a [[Bed-Keeper]]. It may be empty and still have a fire. |
| **Weather on the stones** | Action | Spend a Fear: hail, a wash-out, or a night with no moon. Everyone marks a Stress or takes 1d6+2 physical from cold and going over. A [[Two-Bodied]] in a form that can take weather may reduce it by their armor thresholds as usual. |

### Thaw-gate — Tier 1 Traversal

[[Named Ground|Shelf-gate]] or [[Named Ground|Noon Pass]] in a thaw-week. Last year's snow as this year's road.

**Impulses:** close the pass · take a cart · remember [[The Thaw-Break]] · make you wait with people you did not choose

**Difficulty:** 13

**Potential adversaries:** [[Gap-Cat]] · a local watch holding the bench until morning

| Feature | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **The cut is a lake** | Passive | Crossing without a guide is Difficulty 13. Failure: swept to Close range of the next rock and 1d8+2 physical, or lose a pack-animal / cart (GM's call, not both on one roll). |
| **Two summers** | Passive | Anyone who lived the Thaw-Break (C.Y. 233) has Advantage on the next travel roll here if they say what they remember. Anyone who did not has to trust a mouth. |
| **The pass takes a day** | Action | Spend a Fear to hold the party until the next thaw-bell. A watch, a guest-right, or a cohort sharing the rock becomes the scene. Name, last hearth, who stands. |
| **Undercut** | Reaction | When a PC succeeds on a crossing roll, you may spend a Fear to collapse the path behind them. Allies still on the far side must roll again or find another cut. |

### Empty road-house — Tier 1 Exploration

A [[The Walking Years\|brenhael]] that kept the upper room after the walk ended. Someone still makes the bed.

**Impulses:** seat you · ask which walk · not let the door be the end · charge a Tithe in hospitality

**Difficulty:** 11

**Potential adversaries:** [[Bed-Keeper]] · [[Spent-Week Walker]] (as guest, or as the keeper's unfinished week)

| Feature | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Guest-right is a week** | Passive | If you eat, a host stands for the stay. Leaving before morning without a word is a slight the next shrine will hear. The watch-custom on [[Law and Citizenship]] still holds. |
| **The slate remembers** | Passive | A Long-Lived or a careful search (Difficulty 11) finds names that should have gone home. Some are still walking. Some are the keeper. |
| **Stay** | Action | Spend a Fear: the keeper offers a second night, a held bed, a story that needs a listener. A PC who refuses marks a Stress or makes a Presence roll (Difficulty 11) to leave clean. Failure: the house takes offence, or a walker at the door takes their side. |
| **The upper room** | Action | Spend a Fear to reveal the room that was never let: a pack still tied, a week-slate stuck on Eolthael. Spotlight a [[Spent-Week Walker]] or give the Bed-Keeper Relentless (2) until the room is closed. |

### Night-berth — Tier 1 Exploration

A Chart-run or West Water quay at first dark. Hulls that do not arrive are a local weather.

**Impulses:** wait · strip what the sea gives · sell a stand · not file a hill

**Difficulty:** 12

**Potential adversaries:** [[Chart-Crew]] · a Night-Shore clerk who will not stamp a missing name

| Feature | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **The slate has empty lines** | Passive | Presence or Knowledge (Difficulty 12) to read which berths are late. A failure with Fear: you are asked to stand for a name you do not know. Company-vouch covers *passage*, not a dead hull. |
| **What the water brought** | Action | Spend a Fear to wash a wreck, a crate, or a body into Close range of the quay. The Chart-Crew move to take it. A Far-Voiced who Cries here without a hall-hour is a ticket problem, not a rescue. |
| **Coasting weather** | Action | Spend a Fear: the next day's sail is cancelled or the last one is missing. Travel table on [[Named Ground]] becomes "not a promise." Anyone who needed that hull for a walk-custom is now unvouched at this quay. |
| **We were here first** | Reaction | When a PC claims wreck-right or a berth, mark a Stress on a Chart-Crew (if present) to have them contest it as a standard attack, or start a social scene at Difficulty 13 if you would rather not roll damage yet. |

A Colourless Host is a Support: it makes the *place* worse. Pair it with walkers or a cat, not with a second Host.

## What this layer is not

- A Condition bestiary. Two-Bodied range against Gap-Cats, not against "werewolves."
- Adventure-site depth. [[The Low Wall]] · [[The Seeing-Ring]] · [[The Dry Stair]] already have entrances.
- A sixteenth power, a Kumbaan graft, or a haunted Tree. Dead wood is dead wood.
- Permission to make the Leaf-Fall eerie. The scary edge on the road is weather, leftover hospitality, and people who missed a week.

## GM Notes

- **Small on purpose.** Five adversaries, four environments. If you need a sixth, reskin a Gap-Cat or a Chart-Crew before you invent a taxonomy.
- **Cliche pass.** Not wolves-as-werewolves, not quay-pirates with flags, not undead. Bed-Keepers want occupancy. Chart-Crews want the hull that grief already priced. Walkers want a week the wood will not reopen. The Host wants to Give and cannot — that is the 5%, and it is not a third Tree-outcome.
- **Do not confirm her.** A Colourless Host is horticulture gone wrong, or a pot that never took. Skeptics and believers can both be loud. The player body does not settle it.
- **Settlement layer stays R.11.** Do not restock Eolvaeth's cohort or Ornsael's well as these blocks. If a sent cohort is the danger, that is Vaethod's notice, not a Spent-Week Walker — unless the cohort *missed* their week and kept walking.
- **Battle-point math is the SRD's.** A Bed-Keeper (Leader, 3) plus a Chart-Crew (Horde, 2) plus two Gap-Cats (Skulk, 2 each) is a full table for four PCs. Most travel scenes want one environment feature and one adversary.
