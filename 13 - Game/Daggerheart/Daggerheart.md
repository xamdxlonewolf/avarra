# Daggerheart

The System Reference Document stays the published Daggerheart rules, and this vault does not copy those rules onto its own pages. When a session needs a ruling that belongs to The Turning, you go to [[At the Table]] for the order of play, to [[Kind Heritage]] for the ancestry a person is born with, to [[Dangers of the Turning]] for the road between towns, and to [[A Hidden Phoenix]] for a Phoenix the table is keeping out of the public legend. A domain card is already a job someone in a town is paid to do, and those jobs are named on [[How the Work Is Done]]. The Condition cards, the transformations a person acquires, stay in `09 - Creatures/Conditions`, where the cards themselves are written.

What you roll is the book's text, and you will not find a second copy of it on this page.
