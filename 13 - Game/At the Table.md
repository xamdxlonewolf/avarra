# At the Table

> **What this is.** These are the session procedures for The Turning, gathered so a GM can open one page instead of hunting six notes. You will find character creation, the deal the Kept live with, what to do when someone is Struck during play, how a Kind and a Condition sit on the same sheet, advancement, travel, the way a city witnesses a person, crime, and the Leaf-Fall cases that do not fit the usual week. The fiction stays on the notes that own it, and the law stays on [[Law and Citizenship]]. This page is the order you do those things in.

> **Tone.** The people of this world are neighbours and colleagues, and most of what a session spends its time on is ordinary life, with a real danger underneath it. A Tithe, the upkeep a Condition card asks of the person who carries it, never seizes the player character.

## Character creation (order)

You run this once, in this order, and you do not invent a second way to build a character beside it.

1. **Kind.** Choose what the character was born, which the book calls an ancestry. That can be a stock ancestry from Daggerheart, one of the four written for this world ([[Kitsune]], [[Selkie]], [[Tengu]], or [[Yumboe]]), or a Mixed Ancestry in the way the System Reference Document already allows. The rulings for those choices are on [[Kind Heritage]]. A Yumboe needs the GM's leave, and a Yumboe character is always the full Kind.
2. **Community.** Take a Community the way the SRD has it, the card for the sort of place that raised the character. A [[Kind Heritage|Hearth-Mark]], the place-benefit Kind Heritage gives to some stock characters, is not a Community, and it does not stand in for one.
3. **Class, Experiences, equipment.** Take class, equipment, and Experiences as the SRD has them. Every character gets two Experiences, the phrases on the sheet that say what they have lived. A Kept character, defined in the next step, does not get a third Experience as consolation for having no Condition card. The tradeoff is written under [The Kept deal](#The%20Kept%20deal).
4. **Turning.** Pick one of the four, and write it on the sheet. The Leaf-Fall is the week a child stands under the town's Turning Tree in their tenth year. What the Tree did then, or did not do, is the Turning.
   - **Given** means a coloured leaf fell on you when you were ten, and you take one Given-path or Both-path card from [[Conditions]].
   - **Kept** means the leaves closed around you in a hug and no colour fell, so you take no Condition card. What that empty card is worth, and what it refuses to be, is [The Kept deal](#The%20Kept%20deal).
   - **Struck** means a later door, one of the thresholds the roster already names, happened in the backstory. You take a Struck-path or Both-path card. Someone vouched for you after that change, which means someone the town will believe stood up and said what you had become, and you say who that was.
   - **UnTurned** means you never stood under the Tree, so there was no hug and no colour, and you are not Kept. People will call you Kept, and the law should not, though you can still be Struck later.
5. **Special seats (whole table, not one player).** Some characters are a conversation the whole table has, not a private pick.
   - [[Phoenix]] is a spotlight Condition. The other players consent to a campaign that will keep arriving at that fire, or the Phoenix is run as an NPC.
   - **Hidden Phoenix** is a different conversation from the public legend, and it uses [[A Hidden Phoenix]].
   - [[Yumboe]] needs the GM's leave, and the character is the full Kind only.
   - [[The Unbound]] is not a starting pick. You reach it through [[Bound]], either in play or arranged at session zero as a Bound whose term has already ended.

Kind and Condition **stack**, so the ancestry you were born and the Condition you carry are both on the sheet. A Selkie can be Given [[Two-Bodied]], a stock Human can be Struck [[Returned]], and a character of mixed heritage Turns, or is Kept, or is Struck, the same as anyone else.

They stack even when the fiction seems to rhyme. An Infernis, a stock ancestry whose features are already on the sheet from birth, who swears a contract is [[Bound]] the way anyone else who swears one would be. The blood they were born with stays Kind, and the contract is the Condition. The two do not merge, they do not cancel, and the character does not become Given-Over by default. Given-Over is the world's name for a Bound person who signs contract after contract, and it is a title rather than a Condition this page hands out. The Infernis features stay the ancestry's, and the Granted Power, the power the contract itself gives, stays the contract's. Bound is something you acquire, and it is never something you are born.

## The Kept deal

The Kept are a deliberate tradeoff, and they are not a consolation Condition invented to make up for a missing card. They have no boon and no Tithe, so the card is empty and there is no clock on it to tick.

- They are the **default legal person**. A town has witnessed them, they are not in a guild, and they do not carry a licence. The rights that follow from that are on [[Law and Citizenship#Rights of the Kept]].
- At every **long rest** they take their downtime with no Tithe checklist to work through. Everyone who carries a Condition may be ticking the clocks on [[Conditions#Tithe clocks at a long rest]]. That free evening, while other people are still paying upkeep, is the advantage at the table.
- They can still be **Struck** later. Kept describes the life they have now, and it is not a promise that the life will stay empty.

What they do not get is a tenth Condition, a bonus Experience, a Hearth-Mark they did not already qualify for, or a "Kept feature" that is a Tithe dressed up as a gift. If a table later wants a house rule, that house rule is theirs. This note does not give one.

An UnTurned life looks similar from the outside, because there is no Tithe and no guild. The difference is that a Kept person's hug was witnessed, and an UnTurned person never stood. Do not file an UnTurned neighbour as Kept just to be kind.

## Struck in play

A Kept or UnTurned player character can be Struck if the fiction crosses a door the roster already names. Dying can make them [[Returned]], swearing a contract can make them [[Bound]], a drowning they survive can make them [[Far-Voiced]], the edge of a gaze can make them [[The Stilled]], and a taking by the green can make them [[The Taken-In]]. [[Long-Lived]], [[Two-Bodied]], [[The Answered]], and [[Phoenix]] are Given only, so no later door can Strike them into being.

The player's consent sits in front of the card. They agree to take it, and the GM does not assign a becoming as punishment for a failed roll. A Tithe never seizes the player character, and becoming is not a moment where the player loses control of them.

When the door does open, and the player has taken the card, you do four things.

1. Add the Condition card, and the Kind stays what it was.
2. They are **vouched anew** at the next desk that will take them, which means someone stands for them so the town can know what they became. A hazardous door goes to [[The Intake]].
3. They were not Given, so there is no leaf for this, and there is no second Leaf-Fall to send them back to.
4. They do not stack a second Condition on the first. The Tree does not put one becoming on top of another, and a later door does not either.

A Bound whose term ends becomes [[The Unbound]], as that card describes, and that is the only becoming that follows on from another.

## Advancement

A Condition card does not level, so the card you took at level 1 is the same card at level 10, and it does not grow a new rank while class features, domain cards, Experiences, weapon tier, Proficiency, and Hope scars change around it. Hope scars are the marks that cross a Hope slot off the sheet, and the full ruling is [[Conditions#Advancement]].

A few things look like the card is growing, and they are not a new level of the Condition. A Two-Bodied character can gain an Experience, a Bound term can move, and a Returned or a Phoenix counts down Hope slots, while attacks the card already grants can roll more Proficiency dice as that Proficiency rises. The card underneath those changes is the card you started with.

## Travel

You move on a spoken **word**, not on a paper a state issued you. When a watch stops a stranger, they ask your name, your last hearth, and who stands for you, and two of those three will do. If you can give them none, you sit until morning, or they walk you to the next gate.

The customs that count as that word are a road-word, a named person who will stand for you; a company-vouch, the party standing for one another so you can pass; a guild-mark, a craft mark that travels; a house-note, paper from a house that will answer for you; a guest-right, a host who took you in and stands for the stay; and a walk-custom, the word the road itself gives someone who is on a named walk. The table of what each one is, and what it is not, is [[Law and Citizenship#How a traveler stays vouched]].

Kumbaan is not on this grammar, so a crossing onto that ground works the way [[The Sundering Isle#How a crossing works]] describes, and the distances between places are on [[Named Ground#Travel times]].

Sold vouching, people paying for a word, exists and is not this custom, and the face of that trade is [[The Standing Trade]].

## City witnessing

A village remembers the child who stood, while a city is too large for that memory, so it remembers a **hearth**, and the legal sentence is "I am of the third quay," rather than "I am of Orentel."

Turning-Week is still **one week**, the children stand by hearth in named slots, and the warden Speaks the colour or the hug without writing a roll of gifts. How a hearth stands, and how Turning-Week runs in a city, is [[Law and Citizenship#Turning in a city — the hearth stood]] and [[Turning Tree#Turning-Week in a city]].

Threnmaieth writes the stand down, and that writing is the aberration, because the other towns do not keep the roll.

## Crime

There is no crime of *being* a Condition, because the Inviolate Will, the doctrine that a Condition cannot seize another person's will, already sorts the charge into a negligent Tithe-lapse, which is letting the upkeep slip, or a willful weaponizing, which is turning the Condition on someone on purpose.

The charge walks a ladder from a neighbour's word, to the watch, who hold someone until morning, then to a guild-hearing, and then to an open hearing. Exile from the hearth is the civic death, the end of belonging to that place, and the full ladder is [[Law and Citizenship#Harm, the watch, and the hearing]].

A ticket names a hazardous *use*, a Gaze, a Cry, or an element in a yard, and it does not name the Condition. Bound is overseen as a table, and it is not a fourth licence carried on the body.

## Leaf-Fall edge cases

Open [[Turning Tree#Questions a warden gets asked]] and read the row that matches the family in front of you. You do not invent a third thing the Tree can do, and you do not invent a second-chance year.

When you cannot open that table, hold to this. The door that Gives a Condition is open for **one week** in the tenth year, and a completed standing, whether a colour fell or the leaves hugged, under sound wood, happens **once**. A miss, a refusal, or a week spent under unsound wood makes the child **unTurned**, not Kept, and Struck remains the mercy that can still come later. You may correct a Speaking, the warden's sentence about what the leaf did, and you may not rerun the fall itself. [[The Wrong Green]] is the year people cite when a Speaking was put right and the child was not stood again. White-fire, the leaf that would name a Phoenix, is sent to the First Seat, and a town-warden does not guess that reading.

## Domain cards, a Speaking, a made thing

When the question is a job someone is paid to do, open [[How the Work Is Done]] and use the SRD card as it was printed, without adding a second roll on top of the card. A Condition clock ticks only from its own card, and a domain card does not tick it.

A Spoken colour is the sentence the warden says at the Leaf-Fall, naming the colour or the hug, and it is not a spellcast. Hope, the currency a player spends to push a moment, does not buy a colour, and the edge cases stay in the section above, with the week and the standing that happens once.

If a scene has a thing that keeps working with no person in it, open [[A Made Thing]]. Name the maker, and name the problem that thing is still causing. You do not give it a Kind, a Condition, or a domain.

[[The Cart-Ox]], [[The Terrace Goat]], and [[The Path Dog]] are livelihoods people keep, and they are not adversaries you drop into a fight. A [[Two-Bodied]] other self is a person, and a door, together with the return through it, is [[Kin at the Door]].

## GM Notes

- **This note compiles; it does not reopen.** The door is one week, a completed standing happens once, an unTurned person is not Kept, Condition cards do not scale with level, a ticket is not a census, and Kumbaan law is not grafted onto this page. If a procedure here and the note that owns the subject disagree, the home note wins, and this page is the one that is wrong.
- **The Kept tradeoff is the lock.** Do not add a Kept Experience, or a feature you describe as "wanted," in play, to make someone feel they skipped a card. The Kept card stays empty, with no boon and no Tithe, and that emptiness is the design. A house rule can exist. It stays optional, and it stays off this page.
- **Struck-in-play is opt-in.** A failed death move can offer [[Returned]], and it does not assign Returned. The same is true of a drowning and [[Far-Voiced]], and of a contract and [[Bound]], and in each case the player takes the card or they do not.
- **Phoenix consent stays on the public card.** The agency rules for a hidden Phoenix are [[A Hidden Phoenix]]. The opening kit, when you are using one, is [[The Opening]].
- **Scaffolding stays in this section.** Story numbers, the instruction not to rebuild a ruling, and the audit trail do not belong on the player procedure above.
