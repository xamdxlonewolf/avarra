# At the Table

> **What this is.** The session-facing procedures for **The Turning**. Character creation, the Kept deal, Struck-in-play, stacking, advancement, travel, city witnessing, crime, and Leaf-Fall edge cases — in one place a GM can open without hunting six notes. Fiction and law stay on their home notes. This page is the order of operations.

> **Tone.** Neighbours and colleagues. A real obstacle underneath. A Tithe never seizes the PC. ~5% scary.

## Character creation (order)

Run this once, in this order. Do not invent a second engine.

1. **Kind.** Stock Daggerheart ancestry, one of the four customs ([[Kitsune]] · [[Selkie]] · [[Tengu]] · [[Yumboe]]), or Mixed Ancestry as the SRD allows. Rulings: [[Kind Heritage]]. [[Yumboe]] needs GM leave and is always the full Kind.
2. **Community.** As the SRD. A [[Kind Heritage|Hearth-Mark]] is not a Community.
3. **Class, Experiences, equipment.** As the SRD. Two Experiences. A Kept PC does not get a third as consolation (see [The Kept deal](#The%20Kept%20deal)).
4. **Turning.** Pick one, and write it on the sheet:
   - **Given** — a colour fell at ten. Take one Given-path or Both-path card from [[Conditions]].
   - **Kept** — the leaves hugged. No Condition card. See [The Kept deal](#The%20Kept%20deal).
   - **Struck** — a later door already happened in backstory. Take a Struck-path or Both-path card. You were vouched anew; say who stood.
   - **UnTurned** — you never stood. No hug, no colour. Not Kept. You can still be Struck later. Folk will call you Kept; the law should not.
5. **Special seats (whole table, not one player).**
   - [[Phoenix]] — spotlight Condition; other players consent. Or run it as an NPC.
   - **Hidden Phoenix** — a different conversation. [[A Hidden Phoenix]].
   - [[Yumboe]] — GM leave. Full Kind only.
   - [[The Unbound]] — not a starting pick. You reach it through [[Bound]], in play or arranged at session zero as a Bound whose term already ended.

Kind and Condition **stack**. A Selkie can be Given Two-Bodied. A stock Human can be Struck Returned. Mixed heritage Turns, or is Kept, or is Struck, like anyone else.

They stack even when the fiction rhymes. An **Infernis** who swears a contract is [[Bound]] like anyone else. The born blood is Kind; the contract is Condition. They do not merge, cancel, or become Given-Over by default. Infernis features stay the ancestry's; the Granted Power stays the contract's. The card already says Bound is acquired, never born.

## The Kept deal

**Deliberate tradeoff, not a consolation Condition.** The Kept have no boon and no Tithe. That is the card: the empty clock.

What they *do* have:

- They are the **default legal person** — witnessed, unguilded, unlicensed ([[Law and Citizenship#Rights of the Kept]]).
- At every **long rest** they take their downtime without a Tithe checklist. Everyone else may be ticking [[Conditions#Tithe clocks at a long rest]]. That free move is the table advantage.
- They can still be **Struck** later. Kept describes a present, not a promise.

What they do *not* get: a tenth Condition, a bonus Experience, a Hearth-Mark they did not already qualify for, or a "Kept feature" that is a Tithe in costume. If a table later wants a house rule, that is a house rule. This note does not give one.

UnTurned lives look similar (no Tithe, no guild). Only the hug was witnessed. Do not file them as Kept to be kind.

## Struck in play

A Kept or UnTurned PC can be Struck if the fiction crosses a door the roster already names: death ([[Returned]]), a sworn contract ([[Bound]]), a survivable drowning / gaze-edge / green-taking ([[Far-Voiced]] · [[The Stilled]] · [[The Taken-In]]). Given-only fates (Long-Lived, Two-Bodied, Answered, Phoenix) cannot be Struck.

**Consent.** The player agrees to take the card. The GM does not assign a becoming to punish a failed roll. A Tithe never seizes the PC; becoming is not a lose-control event.

When it fires:

1. Add the Condition card. Kind stays.
2. They are **vouched anew** at the next desk that will take them. Hazardous doors go to [[The Intake]].
3. They were not Given. There is no leaf and no second Leaf-Fall.
4. They do not stack a second Condition. The Tree does not stack; a later door does not either.

A Bound whose term ends becomes [[The Unbound]] per that card. That is the only downstream becoming.

## Advancement

**Condition cards do not level.** The card at 1 is the card at 10. Class features, domain cards, Experiences, weapon tier, Proficiency, and Hope scars change around it. Full ruling: [[Conditions#Advancement]].

What looks like growth and is not level-scaling: a Two-Bodied Experience; a Bound Term that moves; a Returned or Phoenix Hope-slot countdown; Proficiency dice on attacks the card already has.

## Travel

You move on a **word**, not a paper-state. A watch asks **name, last hearth, who stands.** Two will do. None, and you sit until morning or you are walked to the next gate.

Customs that count: road-word · company-vouch · guild-mark · house-note · guest-right · walk-custom. Table: [[Law and Citizenship#How a traveler stays vouched]].

Kumbaan is not on this grammar. Crossing: [[The Sundering Isle#How a crossing works]]. Distances: [[Named Ground#Travel times]].

Sold vouching exists. It is not this custom. Face: [[The Standing Trade]].

## City witnessing

A village remembers a child. A city remembers a **hearth**. The legal sentence is "I am of the third quay," not "I am of Orentel."

Turning-Week is still **one week**. Stands are by hearth, in named slots. The warden Speaks colour or hug. They do not write a roll of gifts. Procedure: [[Law and Citizenship#Turning in a city — the hearth stood]] · [[Turning Tree#Turning-Week in a city]].

Threnmaieth writes the stand down. That is the aberration.

## Crime

There is no crime of *being* a Condition. The Inviolate Will already sorts the charge: negligent Tithe-lapse versus willful weaponizing.

Ladder: **neighbour-word → watch (hold till morning) → guild-hearing → open hearing.** Exile from the hearth is the civic death. Full ladder: [[Law and Citizenship#Harm, the watch, and the hearing]].

Tickets name a hazardous *use* (Gaze, Cry, element in a yard), not a Condition. Bound is a table, not a fourth body-licence.

## Leaf-Fall edge cases

Open [[Turning Tree#Questions a warden gets asked]] and read the row. Do not invent a third Tree-outcome or a second-chance year.

The load-bearing sentences, for when you cannot open the table:

- The Given-door is **one week** in the tenth year.
- A completed standing (colour or hug, sound wood) is **once**.
- A miss, refusal, or week spent under unsound wood makes a child **unTurned**, not Kept.
- Struck remains the later mercy.
- You may correct a Speaking. You may not rerun a fall. [[The Wrong Green]] is the cited year.
- White-fire is sent to the First Seat. A town-warden does not guess Phoenix.

## GM Notes

- **This note compiles; it does not reopen.** One-week door, once-only standing, unTurned ≠ Kept, no level scaling, tickets ≠ census, no Kumbaan graft. If a procedure here and a home note disagree, the home note wins and this page is wrong.
- **Kept tradeoff is the lock.** Do not add a Kept Experience or a "wanted" feature in play to make someone feel they skipped a card. The empty clock is the design. Optional house rules stay optional and stay off this page.
- **Struck-in-play is opt-in.** A failed death move can *offer* Returned. It does not assign it. Same for a drowning and Far-Voiced, a contract and Bound. The player takes the card or they don't.
- **Phoenix consent stays on the public card.** Hidden-Phoenix agency is [[A Hidden Phoenix]]. Opening kit: [[The Opening]].
- **Scaffolding stays here.** Story numbers, "do not rebuild," and the audit trail do not belong on player procedure.
