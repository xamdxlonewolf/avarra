# 13 - Game

This folder is the part of the vault you open when it is time to play. The notes elsewhere say what The Turning is, who lives in it, and how a town holds a Leaf-Fall, the week a child stands under the Turning Tree. The notes here say the order a session runs in, and they leave the published Daggerheart rules in the book where those rules already live.

## Contents

- [[At the Table]] is the note a GM keeps beside the sheet. It walks a new character from Kind, the ancestry they were born, through the deal the Kept make by carrying no Condition, a Struck becoming during play, travel, the way a city witnesses a hearth, crime, the Leaf-Fall cases that do not fit the usual week, a domain card, and a made thing.
- [[How the Work Is Done]] is where the crafts live, the jobs a town already pays someone to do. That hub sits in `06 - Magic`, and this folder only points at it.
- [[Kind Heritage]] sets the four custom Kinds, the ancestries written for this world, beside Daggerheart's stock ancestries. It names the Hearth-Mark, allows Mixed Ancestry the way the rules already allow it, and keeps one surprise keyword on a single Kind.
- [[A Hidden Phoenix]] is for a player who carries the Phoenix and is not the public legend the towns already have an opinion about.
- [[Dangers of the Turning]] is the country between settlements, the adversaries on the road and the travel environments you open when the next shrine is not a sure thing.
- [[Conditions]] holds the Transformation cards, the becomings a person acquires rather than being born into. That hub sits in `09 - Creatures`.
- [[Rules]], [[Mechanics]], [[Encounters]], [[Tables]], and [[Daggerheart]] are the shelves. The procedure itself is written on [[At the Table]], [[Kind Heritage]], [[Dangers of the Turning]], and [[A Hidden Phoenix]], and the rules text you roll against is the Daggerheart System Reference Document, which this vault does not copy.
