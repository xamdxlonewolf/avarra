# Rules

Table procedure for a session of The Turning is written in four notes, and this page is only the way into them.

You begin with [[At the Table]], which is the session itself: how a character is made, what the Kept live with when the Tree gave them no Condition, what you do when someone is Struck during play, and how travel, a city, a crime, and a Leaf-Fall are handled while people are sitting there. When the question is Kind, the ancestry a person was born with, including the four ancestries written for this world and a mix of parents, you open [[Kind Heritage]]. When the question is the country between towns, [[Dangers of the Turning]] is the roll you make on the road. When a player is carrying the Phoenix and the table wants that kept out of the public legend everyone already knows, [[A Hidden Phoenix]] is the page that says how the player stays in charge of it.
