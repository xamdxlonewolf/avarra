# How the Work Is Done

People hire a craft. They do not enroll in a school of magic.

A Daggerheart domain card is a job the town already pays someone to do. The nine domains below are those jobs. The cards stay on the SRD. This note does not copy them. A class on a sheet is how the table plays.

[[Conditions]] stay in `09 - Creatures/Conditions`. A Condition is a becoming, with a Tithe. A domain card does not Turn anyone. If the same person has both, each one does what its own card says. The Tithe ticks only from the Condition card. The domain card still does what the SRD printed.

A licence is still a licence. [[The Stillers]] stamp a Gaze. [[The Hall-Keepers]] stamp a Voice-ticket. [[The Element-Guilds]] stamp an element in a yard. A domain card does not print any of those papers. [[Craft Fellowships]] still keep a mill, a hull, and a road, and they still do not stamp a body.

## The nine jobs

| Domain | The job |
|---|---|
| **Arcana** | Shaping heat, water, stone, air, or a light a workshop already charges for. The same family of work as an [[The Answered\|Answered]] coax. The card is the craft. The Condition is the element that answered a person for life. |
| **Blade** | A weapon in the hand. A watch holding someone until morning. A pruning knife. A kitchen knife. A cohort walking children to a sound Hand. |
| **Bone** | Where to stand, who is tired, which bank will go. Reading a fight the way a road-mend reads a ford. |
| **Codex** | Paper, minutes, a copy, a count. A Book-hand's table. Reading a page is not Speaking a colour. |
| **Grace** | A guest-meal, a factor's terms, a voice that asks. The hazardous Cry is the Hall-Keepers' ticket. This job is the ask. |
| **Midnight** | A shut door, a night's work, a trade that does not want a square. [[The Slide]] keeps its own note. |
| **Sage** | Ground, herd, weather, the bark of a young graft. A greens-keeper's eye. [[The Taken-In]] remain their own card. |
| **Splendor** | The room after harm. Wash, light, a pot, the table once a stiller's gaze is done. Phoenix fire stays on [[Phoenix]]. |
| **Valor** | Standing a gap. A flood-wall. A child walked to a sound Hand. [[The Watch and the Cohort]] is the civic hold and the send. |

An Answered who also carries an Arcana card has a Tithe and a craft. Idle still means what [[The Answered]] already says. A Kept person with the same card has the craft, and no element that lets go in a crowded room. The town can tell those two people apart by the work, and by whether the workshop has a plan for a day off.

## A Spoken colour

At Leaf-Fall a warden says the colour, or says that the child was held. The square hears what the leaf did. That sentence is the Speaking.

It is not a spell. It does not use a Spellcast trait. Hope does not buy a colour. A warden cannot Speak a person into a Condition who is not standing under a sound Tree in the week the door is open. A misspoken colour is corrected the way [[The Wrong Green]] already allows. The week is not recast.

The colours, the hug, and the questions a warden gets asked live on [[Turning Tree]].

## GM Notes

A later note must not turn a domain into a college, a Kind, or a second Tithe. It must not make a Spoken colour into a spell, a Hope spend, or a way to rerun a fall.

If a card and a Condition seem to answer the same moment, keep both texts. Do not merge the clocks. Do not reprint SRD abilities into this note.
