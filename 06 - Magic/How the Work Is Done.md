# How the Work Is Done

When the work needs doing, people hire a craft, and they do not enroll in a school of magic to get it.

A Daggerheart domain card is a job the town already pays someone to do, and the nine domains below are those jobs. The cards stay on the SRD, and this note does not copy them, because a class on a sheet is how the table plays those jobs.

[[Conditions]] stay in `09 - Creatures/Conditions`. A Condition is a becoming, a change a person comes to carry, and it brings a Tithe, the upkeep that comes with that change. A domain card does not Turn anyone, which means it does not make a person into a Condition. If the same person has both, each one does what its own card says. The Tithe ticks only from the Condition card, and the domain card still does what the SRD printed.

A licence is still a licence: [[The Stillers]] stamp a Gaze, [[The Hall-Keepers]] stamp a Voice-ticket, and [[The Element-Guilds]] stamp an element in a yard, and none of those papers comes off a domain card. [[Craft Fellowships]] still keep a mill, a hull, and a road, and they still do not stamp a body.

## The nine jobs

| Domain | The job |
|---|---|
| **Arcana** | The job is shaping heat, water, stone, air, or a light a workshop already charges for, the same family of work as an [[The Answered\|Answered]] coax. The card is the craft, and the Condition is the element that answered a person for life. |
| **Blade** | The job is a weapon in the hand, a watch holding someone until morning, a pruning knife or a kitchen knife, or a cohort walking children to a sound Hand, meaning a sound Tree. |
| **Bone** | The job is knowing where to stand, who is tired, and which bank will go, which is reading a fight the way a road-mend reads a ford. |
| **Codex** | The job is paper, minutes, a copy, and a count, the work of a Book-hand's table, and reading a page is not Speaking a colour. |
| **Grace** | The job is a guest-meal, a factor's terms, or a voice that asks. The hazardous Cry stays on the Hall-Keepers' ticket, and this job is the ask, not that Cry. |
| **Midnight** | The job is a shut door, a night's work, or a trade that does not want a square. [[The Slide]] keeps its own note for that trade. |
| **Sage** | The job is ground, herd, weather, and the bark of a young graft, which is a greens-keeper's eye. [[The Taken-In]] remain their own card. |
| **Splendor** | The job is the room after harm, the wash and the light and the pot and the table once a stiller's gaze is done. Phoenix fire stays on [[Phoenix]]. |
| **Valor** | The job is standing a gap, whether that is a flood-wall or a child walked to a sound Hand. [[The Watch and the Cohort]] is the civic hold, and the watch that sends a cohort. |

An [[The Answered|Answered]] who also carries an Arcana card has a Tithe and a craft, and what it means for that person to be idle is already said on [[The Answered]]. A Kept person with the same card has the craft, and no element that lets go in a crowded room. The town can tell those two people apart by the work, and by whether the workshop has a plan for a day off.

## A Spoken colour

At Leaf-Fall, the week the door is open and a child can stand under a Tree, a warden says the colour, or says that the child was held. The square hears what the leaf did, and that sentence is the Speaking.

The Speaking is not a spell, and it does not use a Spellcast trait. Hope does not buy a colour, and a warden cannot Speak a person into a Condition who is not standing under a sound Tree in the week the door is open. When a colour is misspoken, it is corrected the way [[The Wrong Green]] already allows, and the week is not recast.

The colours, the hug, and the questions a warden gets asked live on [[Turning Tree]].

## GM Notes

A later note must not turn a domain into a college, into a Kind a person is born as, or into a second Tithe. It must not make a Spoken colour into a spell, a Hope spend, or a way to rerun a fall.

If a card and a Condition seem to answer the same moment, keep both texts, do not merge the clocks, and do not reprint SRD abilities into this note.
