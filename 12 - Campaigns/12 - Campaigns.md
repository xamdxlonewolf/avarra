# 12 - Campaigns

This folder is **what happened**, kept apart from the world that goes on underneath the table. A campaign breaks down into arcs, then chapters, then sessions, and the same folder holds the player characters and the people the campaign itself brought in. The world notes stay the source of truth. What you write here is the record of what the players did to that world.
