# The Opening

> **GM kit, not a novel.** Five key moments, not a plot. Mystery comes first, there is one 5% beat of wrongness, and the wonder belongs to an isolated fall that has already happened. The Leaf-Mother is not this session's reveal. What the players get to decide stays on [[A Hidden Phoenix]].

## Where it sits

You have **one existing square, [[Harrow's Green]]**. It is a live front, which means the square is already in the middle of its day, and the scene is that day rather than a seat you built for someone to fill. The square's own job is still the necessity in front of it: room for the crowd, water on the stone, and a neighbour's week that can be walked home. Session one happens *during that day*, not in place of it.

The isolated fall is last Eolthael's neighbour's week, at a Tree the square already had, so you are not planting a new one for the session. [[Taeren]] is of Brenod, not of Harrow's. They Turned at the canopy the hamlets can see, Haelin did not Speak the white-fire, and the witness never walked that colour home. That is why the child is still in the square when the Hale-month visitors arrive.

[[Rosire]] is of [[Orentel]]. They are not on Tesara's water this week. They walked the Long Mile with someone who burns, and they took an upper-room bed Haelin will not always name. The cistern-notice already has its line, that a pot is not a pilgrim and a pilgrim is not a pot, and these two are the arrival that notice will not say.

The Care stays off the map. You do not build a hospice into a fortress, and Vimein is not in the square. If the house reaches the table at all, it is a letter in a pack or a disappointment at the door, not a hunt.

## Which seats are on-screen

There is **one Gift**, one living Phoenix seat. This opening needs what looks like two, so the seats divide like this.

| Seat | Who | What they are |
|---|---|---|
| **The new Gift** | [[Taeren]] *or* a player character occupying that seat. Not both. | The unseen leaf, a fall the record does not have. They can still Rise. They have few Hope scars or none, and the stories are not about them. |
| **The escaped Phoenix** | The walker. An NPC, and unnamed. | A remnant whose Gift has already ended, so they cannot Rise. Leftover fire that still walks, burns, and can take the air. Not a Phoenix for Leaf-Fall, for the law, or for the roster. |
| **The inside helper** | [[Rosire]] | They opened a door that was not on the slate. They want the child kept out of the story, and they want themselves forgotten. |

If the table wants a **walker who is a player character and can still Rise**, that player character *is* the Gift, and there is no new leaf beside them. That shape is a legal other opening, on [[A Hidden Phoenix]]. It is **not this kit**. Do not put Taeren on-screen as a second Gift so you can keep both shapes at once. Do not decide, off the page, what the fire *means*, just so a reunion comes out tidy.

[[Reimaethe]] and [[Hithaen]] stay in their own squares. Do not grow the four seeds into a crew that arrives together. The six pivots stay pointed at this front, and they do not arrive.

## Opening situation

It is Hale-month, the square is full of dust and extra visitors, and the stone is still the place people drink for the day while also standing in the way of the crowd that Eolthael will need. Haelin's cistern-notice is up. Tora of Brenod is here because the next column still drinks at that stone, and because last year's Brenod child was seen under the canopy and has not been walked home.

Under the boughs there is heat that does not belong to the season, and a hymn that uses a name the child does not own. Haelin Speaks slowly. The hearth still keeps Nathdo's letter ([[The Wrong Green]]), from the year this square said a colour and then had to say a second word over the first. White-fire would ask them to make that kind of history again, and they will not do it in a square that already had to say a second word.

Upstairs, the guest has more crossed lives than they can tell, and a ticket-hand brings water and does not talk about travel. With them is a closed travelling-lamp that should have gone cold.

The square talks about the Phoenix in two tenses inside the same sentence. People will tell you the one is in care, and nobody here has seen them. Leaf-Fall comes inside Eolthael, and Eolthael begins next week. Someone will ask if the one is coming, and Haelin has a Tree and does not have a legend.

That is the situation. It does not tell the party what to want.

## What the table can see

Keep this list in front of you. If a fact is not on it, do not volunteer that fact.

- A live-front square in the middle of a necessity-day, with ropes on the stone, water, the dust of Hale-month, and the hamlets visible down the slope.
- Civic talk in which the Phoenix *should* exist, the one is in care, and no town has seen them in a generation.
- A Brenod child, or the player character in that seat, whose hands hold heat, who asks ordinary questions, and who has been told not to be a story.
- Haelin will not name the colour from last Eolthael. Tora needs someone who was there to walk the witnessing home. Those two wants meet in the square, and the meeting is not yet a Phoenix plot.
- An upper-room guest who burns the way a story burns and cannot account for their scars. The wings stay furled, and furled like that they pass for an ordinary guest, until they don't.
- Rosire's flinch when a room has been cleared and laid with sand, grey under the nails, water in their hands, and no talk of where they travelled.
- A closed lamp that leans toward the new Gift and still knows an old name.
- The public sentence, if anyone is carrying it: *the one is in care*, from [[The Protectors]]' outer book. Not the harvest. Not the house named as if they were harvesters.

## What stays player choice

[[A Hidden Phoenix]] already owns these choices. Do not settle them inside your narration.

- Whether anyone takes the unseen-leaf seat, or Taeren holds it as an NPC.
- Whether a fragment is taken, left, or rewritten in its meaning and not in its fact. A prepared room existed. What it *means* that someone walked out of it belongs to the player.
- Whether they want the missing deaths back.
- What they believe they are, whether that is the one, a leftover, a fraud, or a story other people need.
- Whether they use the public name, and whether they fly.
- Whether they walk back toward the house that kept the walker, whether they make Taeren into a story, and what they do with Rosire: gratitude, rage, or nothing.
- Whether a Phoenix player character Rises, if a death move comes. Never force that Rise.
- What they want from this square that has nothing to do with fire: wages for moving the stone, a walk home with the witness, a bed, or bread.

The other players committed to a campaign that will arrive at fire. They did not commit to the answers.

Stored fire can show **facts**. It can show a count, a layout, a name the fire still answers, and that a death was arranged. It cannot assign a self, a forgiveness, a purpose for the Gift, a second Gift, or her, the Leaf-Mother.

## Five key moments (not a plot)

The primary genre is **mystery**, and the secondary is **wonder**. You get one **wrongness** glimpse at ~5%, and no more. Do not run a thriller hunt, a fishbowl parade of the public legend, or a cosmology lecture.

Play the moments in any order the table reaches. The order below is the recommended one, and you do not skip a moment. Do not open on the lamp, and do not open on two burning people in a single shot, because that is leading with a second bird.

### 1. Two tenses in the square

**Mystery — question inception.** The place where a person should be, and is not.

Someone asks, in the ordinary talk of the square, whether the one is coming for Leaf-Fall, and a neighbour answers that the Phoenix is in care. Another says they have never seen one, and their parent did not either, while the stories keep being told in the present tense, as if the person were still expected. Haelin's answer is the one they actually have: they have a Tree, and they do not have a legend.

**Feel:** Curiosity, not dread. The square is not hiding a secret from the party; it has stopped looking.

**What changes:** The table notices that the world *knows* a Phoenix should exist, and that the world has stopped seeing one.

**Bridge:** The same people will turn around and argue about the stone. Let the necessity-day keep running while that absence sits in the talk.

### 2. White that nobody Spoke

**Wonder — initial encounter.** The isolated fall, met after it has already happened.

Last Eolthael, a Brenod child stood at Harrow's Tree and something white showed. Haelin did not Speak it, so the colour was never said aloud, and a colour that is not civic cannot be walked home as a witnessing. That is why the child is still here, with heat in the hands they do not show in a gift-hall, asking whether there is bread and why that hymn uses a name they do not own.

If a **player character occupies this seat**, the moment is theirs, and you do not run Taeren as an NPC beside them. Describe the canopy, the hamlets in one sightline, and how small the square is against what the stories said a Phoenix-fall would be. Linger on that, and then stop.

**Feel:** The awe does not fit the job going on in the street. You are not in a festival, and you are not in a capital crowd. What you have is dust, a leftover stone, and a warden who will not make Nathdo's cousin-mistake in the loud colour.

**What changes:** The table has seen a fire the record does not have.

**Do not:** confirm the Leaf-Mother. If a neighbour says *she Gave*, that is a neighbour talking, and you do not settle it. Do not flash back the standing, the child under the Tree, unless the player asks to remember it.

### 3. Scars that do not match the lives

**Mystery — second question.** Hope scars without the memories of the deaths that made them.

The walker is in the upper room, with more crossed lives than they can put in a row. They were told they were kept, and what they remember of that is sand, not the deaths that made the marks. They cannot Rise, so if a death move is ever set in front of them that option is already gone. Do not stage a death this session just to prove the engine.

Rosire brings the water up and does not explain the guest.

**Feel:** The question now has a person standing in it, and you keep the room quiet, because this is the energy of looking and not of a confrontation.

**What changes:** The table is holding two burning facts that will not sit in one story, a child the hymns are not about, and a legend who cannot account for their count.

### 4. The lamp that answers two people

**Wrongness glimpse (~5%) / mystery — pattern.** Harvested fire, answering the new Gift.

Rosire kept a closed travelling-lamp, and they will not call it a relic. The competence is the ordinary kind: you do not leave a fire from a prepared room sitting unattended. The coal ought to be cold, and it is not. When the new Gift comes onto the stair, the lamp leans toward them. When the walker speaks an old name, out of habit and not as a title they are claiming, the lamp answers that name as well.

**Feel:** The warmth is real, and under it something was taken. You hold the moment still, because this is not a climax and not a fight, and then you linger once and let someone pour tea.

**Narrate:** the lamp, the lean, and the two names. Do **not** narrate harvest, bank, remnant, the Protectors, or "this is not a real Phoenix." Give them the facts, and do not hand over the meaning.

**What changes:** The fire has answered a child and an old name in the same hour. The table is allowed to start seeing a pattern. You do not finish the pattern for them.

### 5. The door that was not on the slate

**Mystery — progressive, not crystallized.** Rosire on-screen as a person, not as a confession cut into a scene.

The room has been cleared and sand has been laid in it, and Rosire flinches and will not sit. A visitor's slip from a hospice is there, the hospice that will not produce the one, and the words are already written on [[The Protectors]]. What Rosire will say is that someone walked, that they walked the other way, and that this is all the travel they have. They want the child not to become a story, and they want not to be sent for.

**Feel:** Pressure, without anyone being hunted. A door that opened has arrived in the square. What the party does with the person who opened it is theirs.

**What changes:** The helper is a choice the table can make, not a device that hands out clues. Session one can end with the lamp still lit, the stone still unmoved, the witness still unwalked, and nobody named as the one.

**Do not:** fire clue 5, the beat where the remnant goes to ash or a Tree decides between two names. Do not have Vimein arrive. If you need the house to be felt, a runner is a disappointment and a job offer that is not a job, which is Rosire's own situation, not a raid.

## Tone

**Anchor:** a live-front square getting through a necessity-day, with the fire kept at the edge of the week.

| Dial | This session |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Neighbours and colleagues, ~5% scary, and only on purpose. |
| Humor | The dry voice Haelin uses on a notice, integrated into the day. No slapstick in the upper room. If the table jokes through the lamp, let the lamp wait, and do not chase the joke. |
| Consequence | Medium. A hush can leave a child with nobody to vouch for them at breakfast, and a story can travel, while nobody is hunted in the first hour. |
| Wonder / horror | Wonder first, then a single beat of wrongness, with mystery running under the whole session. |
| Description | Two or three sentences for the square and the stone. Linger on the canopy in moment 2 and on the lamp in moment 4. Do not purple the ropes. |
| Energy | The square sits at a medium energy. Talk of the witness starts lower and builds. Wonder goes slow. The lamp is still, not loud. After the lamp, decompress on water, bread, and the stone still sitting where it was. |

**Transitions.** Move people by the stairs, the cistern, and the dust, and by the time of day. Do not cut from Tora's water straight across to harvested fire. After moment 4, give the room a quiet beat before anyone asks what the lean *means*.

**What this session is not.** It is not cosmic horror, not a parade for a chosen one, not a heist against the Care, not a proof of the Leaf-Mother, and not a fight put there to set the tone.

## Voices

Each speaker has more than one job, so do not let all of them explain the Phoenix. If you cover the tags below, you should still know who was speaking.

### Haelin *(HAY-lin)* — town-warden

- **Want they will say:** They want the stone moved before Eolthael, and they want the water still on it while that happens.
- **Avoid:** They will not name a colour they are not sure of, and they will not use the word *Phoenix* as if it were a plan.
- **Hidden:** They saw white last Turning-Week and will not Speak it. Nathdo's letter is in the hearth.
- **Rhythm:** Short, the way a cistern-notice is short. They finish the sentence when the matter is civic, and they fall into fragments when someone asks them to be a legend.

*If asked whether the one is coming:* "The one is in care. That is a hospice sentence. I have a Tree. I do not have a legend."

*If pressed on last Eolthael:* "I can tell a colour. I will not always say. This square already said a colour once and had to say it again."

### Tora *(TOR-ah)* of Brenod

- **Want they will say:** They want someone who was *there* to walk the witnessing home, and they want the water to stay on the stone.
- **Avoid:** They will not pick up Harrow's talk of luck, and they will not call the child a story.
- **Hidden:** Nothing about the fire. They are not in that plot. Haelin's hush is starving a kitchen, and that is already enough for them.
- **Rhythm:** Longer than Haelin once the subject is the ford. What you hear is duty, not a gospel.

*If the witness is stuck:* "If nobody walks it back, Brenod has a child who was seen at a canopy and is weather at breakfast. I didn't climb here for the view."

### Rosire *(ro-SEER-eh)*

- **Want they will say:** Water, the floor under them, and a place off the slate.
- **Avoid:** Travel, the question of where they sat before, and the name of the house.
- **Hidden:** The door they opened, the lamp, and a child who must not become a story.
- **Rhythm:** Fragments. They deflect into the job in their hands, and they are bad at the question *where did you sit before*.

*If asked who the guest is:* "Someone walked. I walked the other way. That's all the travel I have."

*If the room has been cleared:* They do not go in. They bring the water to the threshold. "I don't sit rooms that have been laid with sand."

### Taeren *(TAY-ren)* — only if no PC holds the seat

- **Want they will say:** Bread, the week over, and not to be made into a story.
- **Avoid:** They will not claim the public name, and they will not explain the white.
- **Hidden:** Nothing they could put a name to. There is heat in the hands, and they have been told to keep it there.
- **Rhythm:** Ordinary questions, from someone young. They are not being cryptic.

*If a hymn uses the old name:* "Why does it use a name I don't own."

### The walker — unnamed remnant

- **Want they will say:** Not to be produced for a square to look at, a window, and water.
- **Avoid:** The prepared room, any telling that would finish their life into a story, and a speech in which they call themselves the one. They may still *answer* to that name, the way a habit answers.
- **Hidden:** They do not have a hidden fact they can retrieve. The deaths that are missing stay missing.
- **Rhythm:** Gaps. What they give you is sensory and in the present tense, and a name leaks out of them when the lamp leans.

*If asked how many times:* "I have more winters than I can put in a row. They said I was kept. I don't remember being kept. I remember sand."

Do not hand them a byname this session to make the guest-book look tidy. Haelin's notice already has a place for people they will not always say.

## How the party is here (pick one; do not stack a crew)

The square is already doing its day. The party is extra visitors, not a fellowship anyone summoned.

1. **Hands on the stone.** Haelin needs ropes and backs, and the pay is bread and a bed upstairs.
2. **A witness for Brenod.** Tora will take anyone who stood under the canopy and will walk that witnessing home. They will not take a sermon in place of the walk.
3. **Hale-month beds.** The upper room opens for the neighbour's week and for the overflow that dust-season brings, and the party was vouched for enough to sleep.
4. **A letter that was not for you.** It may carry a hush-colour, a pot-chain, or a Road-hand's ask. You carried it as far as the live front, and nobody hired you to find a Phoenix.

Do not open with Vaethod sending the party out as a plot. Someone Vaethod sent from [[Eolvaeth]] can be *one* of these four ways in, if a player character already has that tie. Vaethod does not arrive, [[Tesara]] does not arrive, and the White Note does not file this square.

Road-word is enough to be standing here. What the road asks is a name, the last hearth, and who stands for you, and two of those three will do ([[At the Table]]).

## Stop lines

- **Vimein does not come.** The Care does not grow a keep on the rise.
- **The lamp is not a combat.** If someone opens it, what it shows are facts: a count, a room, and two names. It does not lecture, and it does not confirm her.
- **No remnant-to-ash.** Clue 5 stays for later.
- **No second Given-door.** Haelin may Speak a correction of a colour, and they may not stand the child again.
- **No fishbowl tax on the new Gift** unless they fly or Kindle. Flying is taking the air, and Kindle is the other way they stop passing for ordinary. Wings that stay furled still pass, and the walker passes the same way until they take the air.
- **Do not name the Protectors as harvesters** in anything the players hear. The public sentence is still that the one is in care.
- **Do not use this hour to prove the Leaf-Mother.** Neighbours may argue it. You do not settle the argument.

## In the warden-hearth (if they look)

*Unsent, in Haelin's hand, kept in the drawer and not on the cistern-wall.*

> *Last Eolthael. Neighbour's week. Colour not named. Child of Brenod still in the square. I can tell. I have not said. This square already said a colour once.*
>
> *The one is in care. I will not write a second name to thicken a week.*

On its face, a careful warden. Underneath, the hush is a person still waiting to be walked home.
