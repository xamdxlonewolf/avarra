# The Opening

> **GM kit, not a novel.** Five key moments, not a plot. Mystery first. One 5% beat. Wonder at an isolated fall. The Leaf-Mother is not this session's reveal. Agency stays on [[A Hidden Phoenix]].

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sits** | [[Harrow's Green]], Hale-month, C.Y. 387. Next week is Eolthael ([[The Reckoning of the Year]]). |
| **On-screen** | The walker · [[Rosire]] · the new Gift ([[Taeren]] *or* a PC in that seat) · Haelin · Tora of Brenod |
| **Offstage** | [[Reimaethe]] · [[Hithaen]] · Vimein · Tesara's week · the Care as a place you can walk into |
| **Do not** | Invent a Protector fortress. Lead with a second bird. Confirm her. Name the cutter. Date the Tree. Coin liturgy. Add a sixteenth power. Resume the old Epic 8 roster. |

## Where it sits

**One existing square: [[Harrow's Green]].** Live front, not a seat. The leftover job is still necessity — room for the crowd, water on the stone, a neighbour's week that can walk home. Session one happens *during that day*, not instead of it.

The isolated fall is last Eolthael's neighbour's week, not a new Tree. [[Taeren]] is of Brenod, not of Harrow's. They Turned at the canopy the hamlets can see. Haelin did not Speak white-fire. The witness never walked home. That is why the child is still in the square when Hale-month extra mouths arrive.

[[Rosire]] is of [[Orentel]]. They are not on Tesara's water this week. They walked the Long Mile with someone who burns, and they took an upper-room bed Haelin will not always name. A pot is not a pilgrim. A pilgrim is not a pot. These two are the thing the notice will not say.

The Care stays off the map. No hospice-fortress. No Vimein in the square. If the house reaches, it is a letter in a pack or a disappointment at the door — not a hunt.

## Which seats are on-screen

There is **one Gift**. This opening needs an apparent two, so:

| Seat | Who | What they are |
|---|---|---|
| **The new Gift** | [[Taeren]] *or* a PC occupying that seat. Not both. | Unseen leaf. Can still Rise. Few or no Hope scars. The stories are not about them. |
| **The escaped Phoenix** | The walker. NPC. Unnamed. | Remnant. Gift already ended. Cannot Rise. Leftover fire that still walks, burns, and can take the air. Not a Phoenix for Leaf-Fall, law, or the roster. |
| **The inside helper** | [[Rosire]] | Opened a door that was not on the slate. Wants the child gone as a story and themselves forgotten. |

If the table wants a **walker-PC who can still Rise**, that PC *is* the Gift and there is no new leaf. That is a legal other opening ([[A Hidden Phoenix]]). It is **not this kit**. Do not put Taeren on-screen as a second Gift to keep both shapes. Do not decide off-page what the fire *means* so you can tidy a reunion.

[[Reimaethe]] and [[Hithaen]] stay in their squares. Do not grow four seeds into a crew. The six pivots stay pointed at this front; they do not arrive.

## Opening situation

Hale-month. Dust. Extra mouths. The stone is still the day's drink and still in the way of the crowd that Eolthael will need. Haelin's cistern-notice is up. Tora of Brenod is here because the next column still drinks there, and because last year's Brenod child was seen under the canopy and has not been walked home.

Under the boughs: heat that is not the season. A hymn that uses a name the child does not own. Haelin Speaks slowly. The hearth still keeps Nathdo's letter ([[The Wrong Green]]). White-fire is the loud cousin of that wound. They will not make history in a square that already had to say a second word.

Upstairs: a guest with more crossed lives than they can tell, and a ticket-hand who brings water and does not talk about travel. A closed travelling-lamp that should have gone cold.

The square talks about the Phoenix in two tenses in the same sentence. The one is in care. Nobody here has seen them. Leaf-Fall is next month. Someone will ask if the one is coming. Haelin has a Tree. They do not have a legend.

That is the situation. It does not tell the party what to want.

## What the table can see

Keep this list in front of you. If it is not on this list, do not volunteer it.

- A live-front square doing a necessity-day: ropes, water, Hale-dust, hamlets visible down the slope.
- Civic talk: the Phoenix *should* exist; the one is in care; no town has seen them in a generation.
- A Brenod child (or the PC in that seat) whose hands hold heat, who asks ordinary questions, who has been told not to be a story.
- Haelin will not name last Eolthael's colour. Tora needs a mouth that will walk a witnessing home. Those two wants collide in the square without being a Phoenix plot.
- An upper-room guest who burns like a story and cannot account for their scars. Wings furled. They pass, until they don't.
- Rosire's flinch at a room that has been cleared and laid with sand. Grey under the nails. Water. No travel-talk.
- A closed lamp that leans toward the new Gift, and still knows an old name.
- The public sentence, if anyone carries it: *the one is in care* ([[The Protectors]]' outer book). Not the harvest. Not the house's name as harvesters.

## What stays player choice

[[A Hidden Phoenix]] already owns these. Do not decide them in narration.

- Whether anyone takes the unseen-leaf seat, or Taeren holds it as an NPC.
- Whether a fragment is taken, left, or rewritten in meaning (not in fact). A prepared room existed. What it *means* that someone walked out is theirs.
- Whether they want the missing deaths back.
- What they believe they are — the one, a leftover, a fraud, a story other people need.
- Whether they use the public name. Whether they fly.
- Whether they walk back toward the house that kept the walker. Whether they make Taeren a story. What they do with Rosire: gratitude, rage, nothing.
- Whether a Phoenix PC Rises, if a death move comes. Never force it.
- What they want from this square that is not fire: wages on the stone, a witness-walk, a bed, bread.

The other players committed to a campaign that will arrive at fire. They did not commit to the answers.

Stored fire can show **facts** (a count, a layout, a name the fire still answers, that a death was arranged). It cannot assign a self, a forgiveness, a Gift's purpose, a second Gift, or her.

## Five key moments (not a plot)

Primary genre: **mystery**. Secondary: **wonder**. One **wrongness** glimpse at ~5%. Do not run a thriller hunt, a fishbowl parade, or a cosmology lecture.

Play them in any order the table reaches. Recommended order below. Do not skip. Do not open on the lamp or on two burning people in one shot — that is leading with a second bird.

### 1. Two tenses in the square

**Mystery — question inception.** The hole where a person should be.

Someone asks, as civic talk, whether the one is coming for Leaf-Fall. A neighbour says the Phoenix is in care. Another says they have never seen one, and their parent didn't either, and the stories are still in the present tense. Haelin: they have a Tree. They do not have a legend.

**Feel:** curiosity, not dread. The square is not hiding a secret from the party; it has stopped looking.

**What changes:** the table notices the world *knows* a Phoenix should exist and has stopped seeing one.

**Bridge:** the same mouths will argue the stone. Let the necessity-day keep running while the hole sits there.

### 2. White that nobody Spoke

**Wonder — initial encounter.** The isolated fall, met in the leftover of it.

Last Eolthael, a Brenod child stood at Harrow's Tree. Something white. Haelin did not Speak it. The child is still here because a colour that is not civic cannot be walked home as a witnessing. Heat in the hands they do not show in a gift-hall. They ask: is there bread. Why does that hymn use a name I do not own.

If a **PC occupies this seat**, this moment is theirs. Do not run Taeren as an NPC beside them. Describe the canopy, the hamlets in one sightline, the smallness of the square against what the stories said a Phoenix-fall would be. Linger. Then stop.

**Feel:** awe that does not fit the job on the street. Not a festival. Not a capital crowd. Dust, a leftover stone, a warden who will not make Nathdo's cousin-mistake in the loud colour.

**What changes:** the table has seen fire the record does not have.

**Do not:** confirm the Leaf-Mother. If a neighbour says *she Gave*, that is a neighbour talking. The GM does not settle it. Do not flash back the standing unless the player asks to remember it.

### 3. Scars that do not match the lives

**Mystery — second question.** Hope scars without memories.

The walker is in the upper room. More crossed lives than they can put in a row. They were told they were kept. They remember sand. They do not remember the deaths that made the marks. They cannot Rise — if a death move is ever in front of them, that option is gone. Do not stage that death this session to prove the engine.

Rosire brings water. They do not explain the guest.

**Feel:** the puzzle gets a body. Quiet. Investigation energy, not a confrontation.

**What changes:** the table has two burning facts that do not fit one story — a child the hymns are not about, and a legend who cannot account for their count.

### 4. The lamp that knows two mouths

**Wrongness glimpse (~5%) / mystery — pattern.** Harvested fire answering the new Gift.

Rosire kept a closed travelling-lamp. They will not call it a relic. Competence: you do not leave a prepared-room fire unattended. The coal should be cold. It is not. When the new Gift is on the stair, the lamp leans. When the walker speaks an old name — habit, not a title they claim — the lamp answers that name too.

**Feel:** warmth with a theft under it. Still. Not a climax. Not combat. One linger, then let someone pour tea.

**Narrate:** the lamp, the lean, the two names. Do **not** narrate harvest, bank, remnant, Protectors, or "this is not a real Phoenix." Facts, not meaning.

**What changes:** fire has answered a child and an old name at once. The table may pattern. You do not complete the pattern for them.

### 5. The door that was not on the slate

**Mystery — progressive, not crystallized.** Rosire on-screen as a person, not a confession cutscene.

A cleared room. Sand. They flinch. A visitor's slip from a hospice that will not produce the one (text already on [[The Protectors]]). They will say: someone walked; I walked the other way; that is all the travel I have. They want the child not to become a story. They want not to be sent for.

**Feel:** pressure without a hunt. A door that opened is in the square. What the party does with the person who opened it is theirs.

**What changes:** the helper is a choice, not a clue-dispenser. Session one can end with the lamp still lit, the stone still unmoved, the witness still unwalked, and no one named as the one.

**Do not:** fire clue 5 (remnant to ash / Tree arbitrating two names). Do not have Vimein arrive. A runner, if you need the house to breathe, is a disappointment and a job offer that is not a job — Rosire's own note, not a raid.

## Tone

**Anchor:** a live-front square doing a necessity-day; fire at the edge of the week.

| Dial | This session |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Neighbours and colleagues. ~5% scary, and only on purpose. |
| Humor | Integrated dry (Haelin's notice-voice). Not slapstick in the upper room. If the table jokes through the lamp, let the lamp wait; do not chase. |
| Consequence | Medium. A hush can leave a child unvouched at breakfast. A story can travel. No one is hunted in hour one. |
| Wonder / horror | Wonder first. One wrongness beat. Mystery throughout. |
| Description | Two or three sentences for the square and the stone. Linger on the canopy (moment 2) and the lamp (moment 4). Do not purple the ropes. |
| Energy | Square: medium. Witness-talk: low-medium, building. Wonder: slow. Lamp: still, not loud. After the lamp: decompress — water, bread, the stone still there. |

**Transitions.** Stairs, cistern, dust. Time of day. Do not smash-cut from Tora's water to harvested fire. After moment 4, give a quiet beat before anyone asks what it *means*.

**What this session is not.** Cosmic horror. A chosen-one parade. A heist on the Care. A Leaf-Mother proof. A fight to establish tone.

## Voices

Each mouth does more than one job. Do not let them all explain the Phoenix. Cover the tags: you should still know who spoke.

### Haelin *(HAY-lin)* — town-warden

- **Want they will say:** the stone moved before Eolthael; water still on it.
- **Avoid:** naming a colour they are not sure of. The word *Phoenix* as a plan.
- **Hidden:** they saw white last Turning-Week and will not Speak it. Nathdo's letter is in the hearth.
- **Rhythm:** short. Cistern-notice. Complete when civic; fragments when asked to be a legend.

*If asked whether the one is coming:* "The one is in care. That is a hospice sentence. I have a Tree. I do not have a legend."

*If pressed on last Eolthael:* "I can tell a colour. I will not always say. This square already said a colour once and had to say it again."

### Tora *(TOR-ah)* of Brenod

- **Want they will say:** a mouth that was *there* walks the witnessing home. Water stays on the stone.
- **Avoid:** Harrow's luck-talk. Calling the child a story.
- **Hidden:** none about fire. They are not in that plot. Haelin's hush is starving a kitchen, and that is enough.
- **Rhythm:** longer than Haelin when the subject is the ford. Duty, not gospel.

*If the witness is stuck:* "If nobody walks it back, Brenod has a child who was seen at a canopy and is weather at breakfast. I didn't climb here for the view."

### Rosire *(ro-SEER-eh)*

- **Want they will say:** water. The floor. To be left off the slate.
- **Avoid:** travel. Where they sat before. The house's name.
- **Hidden:** the opened door; the lamp; a child who must not become a story.
- **Rhythm:** fragments. Deflect into the job. Bad at *where did you sit before*.

*If asked who the guest is:* "Someone walked. I walked the other way. That's all the travel I have."

*If the room has been cleared:* they do not go in. Water from the threshold. "I don't sit rooms that have been laid with sand."

### Taeren *(TAY-ren)* — only if no PC holds the seat

- **Want they will say:** bread. The week over. Not to be a story.
- **Avoid:** claiming the public name. Explaining white.
- **Hidden:** none they could name. Heat they have been told to keep in their hands.
- **Rhythm:** ordinary questions. Young. Not cryptic.

*If a hymn uses the old name:* "Why does it use a name I don't own."

### The walker — unnamed remnant

- **Want they will say:** not to be produced for a square. A window. Water.
- **Avoid:** a prepared room. Completing a life-story. Calling themselves the one as a speech. They may *answer* to it as habit.
- **Hidden:** they do not have one they can retrieve. The missing deaths are missing.
- **Rhythm:** gaps. Present-tense sensory. A name leaking when the lamp leans.

*If asked how many times:* "I have more winters than I can put in a row. They said I was kept. I don't remember being kept. I remember sand."

Do not give them a byname this session to make the guest-book tidy. Haelin's notice already has a slot for people they will not always say.

## How the party is here (pick one; do not stack a crew)

The square is already doing a day. The party is extra mouths, not a summoned fellowship.

1. **Hands on the stone.** Haelin needs ropes and backs. Wages in bread and a bed upstairs.
2. **A mouth for Brenod.** Tora will take anyone who stood under the canopy and will walk home. They will not take a sermon.
3. **Hale-month beds.** The upper room opens for the neighbour's week and for dust-season overflow. You were vouched enough to sleep.
4. **A letter that was not for you.** A hush-colour, a pot-chain, a Road-hand's ask. You carried it to the live front. You were not hired to find a Phoenix.

Do not start with Vaethod sending the party as a plot. A send from [[Eolvaeth]] can be *one* of these if a PC already has that tie. Vaethod does not arrive. [[Tesara]] does not arrive. The White Note does not file this square.

Road-word is enough to be here: name, last hearth, who stands. Two will do ([[At the Table]]).

## Stop lines

- **Vimein does not come.** The Care does not sprout a keep on the rise.
- **The lamp is not a combat.** If someone opens it, it shows facts (count, room, two names). It does not lecture. It does not confirm her.
- **No remnant-to-ash.** Clue 5 stays later.
- **No second Given-door.** Haelin may Speak a correction. They may not stand the child again.
- **No fishbowl tax on the new Gift** unless they fly or Kindle. Furled passes. The walker passes until they take the air.
- **Do not name the Protectors as harvesters** in player-facing narration. The public sentence is still care.
- **Do not use this hour to prove the Leaf-Mother.** Neighbours may argue. You do not settle it.

## In the warden-hearth (if they look)

*Unsent. Haelin's hand. Drawer, not the cistern-wall.*

> *Last Eolthael. Neighbour's week. Colour not named. Child of Brenod still in the square. I can tell. I have not said. This square already said a colour once.*
>
> *The one is in care. I will not write a second name to thicken a week.*

On its face: a careful warden. Underneath: the hush is a person still waiting to be walked home.
