# The Offered Fragment

> **GM kit, not a novel.** Still the live front. Hale-month going over into Eolthael, C.Y. 387. Four key moments. One fragment, if they are in the room for it. The house breathes as a sentence the square already owns. It does not arrive.

## Where it sits

[[The Walk Home]] may have ended on the stone, on the track, or in Brenod. Start from that, not from a reset. If they never walked, Tora's breakfast is still unresolved and can stay unresolved. This kit does not require the walk.

The week is late Hale-month, or the first dull days of Eolthael. Leaf-Fall is not this session. The neighbour's week was last year. Do not stage a Turning to force a colour into the record.

## Which seats are on-screen

**One Gift.** The same seat as session one. Taeren or a PC. Not both. The walker is not a Phoenix for the roster. Apparent two, if both are in the upper room, is leftover fire next to a Gift. Do not explain that sentence to the table. Let them have the two heats and no lecture.

## Opening situation

Someone has swept the upper room. The sand Rosire will not sit in is a habit, not a new clue you narrate from nothing: if they already saw the flinch, the room is only cleaner. If they never went up, the flinch is still available, and it is still not the opening image.

Downstairs, Haelin's day is ropes or the lack of them. A neighbour repeats the hospice sentence because someone asked again whether the one is coming. The sentence is the one already on [[The Protectors]]' slip: the one is in care; they do not produce a person for a square. No new letter. No one is hired. Vimein does not come to say it.

The lamp is in the room. It is not the first thing you describe.

## What the table can see

- A square between dust and the next month. Fewer extra mouths. The stone still there.
- Rosire with water, grey under the nails, bad at the question *where did you sit before*.
- The walker, if they are in the room: wings furled, scars they cannot list, a window they prefer to a door.
- The closed lamp. It leans only if the Gift is near and you have earned the quiet. It is not a parade.
- The slip's sentence, in a neighbour's mouth or in the drawer if they already found it. Not a new policy.
- One fragment on offer: a count, a layout, a name the fire still answers, or a face on a scar. Facts. The player chooses.

## What stays player choice

- Whether they go up to the room at all. The day can be the square.
- Take, leave, or rewrite the meaning. A refused fragment does not return louder because they were brave enough to refuse.
- Whether they believe the useful lie: that the walker is the real one and the child is a fraud. A neighbour may say it. You do not.
- What they do with Rosire. Gratitude, anger, a job, nothing.
- Whether anyone opens the lamp. Opening it shows a count, a room, two names. It does not show a mother, a forgiveness, or a second Gift.

## Four key moments (not a plot)

Primary: **mystery**. One **wrongness** glimpse only inside moment 3, and only if they are there. Do not run it as horror. Do not crystallize a solution.

### 1. The sentence, downstairs

**Mystery — false resolution, offered by a neighbour and not taken by you.**

Someone has an answer that would end the week. The one is in care. The child is a story people are being unkind about. The guest upstairs is sick, or a pilgrim, or none of their business. Haelin will agree with the part that keeps the square from making history. They will not agree to produce anyone.

**Feel:** the relief of a closed question, and the draft under it. Curiosity, not dread.

**What changes:** the table hears the lie the town can live on. They do not have to puncture it.

**Bridge:** stairs, if they take them. Water, if they don't. Do not smash-cut to two fires.

### 2. Rosire at the threshold

**Relationship, kept small.** They want to be forgotten and they want the child not to become a story. Both can be said as work.

They will bring water to the doorway. They will not sit a room laid with sand. If asked who opened a door, the line from session one still holds, shorter if they have already said it: someone walked; they walked the other way.

**Feel:** a person, not a confession cutscene.

**What changes:** the helper remains a choice. If the table is kind, Rosire does not become grateful on a schedule. If the table is angry, they flinch and keep the water coming.

*If asked whether to send them back:* "Don't. I don't have the key. I have the floor, and I'd like to keep it."

### 3. What the fire still knows

**Wrongness glimpse (~5%), only here.** Pattern, not a lecture.

If they are near the lamp, or the walker says an old name by habit, the coal answers. One fragment. A count of burns in a room. A door that was not on a slate. A name that is not the one the child uses. The Gift's player, or the walker if the fragment is the walker's body and no PC is in that seat, chooses take, leave, or meaning.

The walker does not remember the deaths that made the marks. Do not fill the murders in because the lamp made a number.

**Feel:** warmth with a theft under it. Still. Then tea, or the window.

**What changes:** a fact is in the room. The pattern is the table's.

**Do not:** say remnant, bank, harvest, or Protectors-as-harvesters. Do not confirm her. Do not let the fire tell them who they are. If it seems to, that is a keeper's sentence riding a coal, and they may refuse it.

### 4. Two heats, and no verdict

**Mystery — still progressive.** The child and the walker can be in the same hour without a reveal.

Haelin, if fetched, will talk about the stone. Tora, if still in the square, will talk about breakfast. Neither will rank the two burning people. A neighbour might. Let the ranking stay ugly and unofficial.

**Feel:** the puzzle has a shape and no last piece. That is the session.

**What changes:** nothing is crowned. The lamp can stay lit. The walker stays unnamed. The Gift is still one.

**Do not:** fire the beat where the remnant goes to ash, or where a Tree arbitrates two names. Clue 5 stays unfired. This is not the close.

## Tone

**Anchor:** an upper room at the end of a dusty month. The square is still downstairs.

| Humor | Rosire's deflection can be dry. Do not joke the sand into a bit if the table has gone quiet. |
| Consequence | Medium. A story can leave with a neighbour. No arrest. No raid. |
| Wonder / horror | Wonder is last session's slope. This one is the quiet wrongness, once. |
| Description | The room in three sentences: sand, window, lamp. Linger on the lean. Do not describe the Care. |
| Energy | Downstairs: medium. Threshold: low. Lamp: still. After: decompress on purpose. |

**Transitions.** A broom. A stair. Time of day. After moment 3, someone pours something. Do not go from the coal to a moral.

**What this session is not.** A heist on the hospice. A second opening. A trial of Rosire. Cosmic horror.

## Voices

### Rosire *(ro-SEER-eh)*

- **Want they will say:** the floor. Not to be sent for. The child left out of songs.
- **Avoid:** the house's name. Travel. A full account.
- **Rhythm:** fragments. A job in the hand.

*If they are thanked:* "Don't. Water's the job. The rest I already walked away from."

*If they are blamed:* "I opened a door that wasn't on the slate. I didn't open a square. You can be angry. I'm still not sitting in that room."

### The walker — unnamed

- **Want they will say:** the window. Not to be produced.
- **Avoid:** finishing a life. They may answer to an old name as habit when the lamp leans.
- **Rhythm:** present-tense. Gaps where a death would be.

*If asked the count:* "More than I can put down. They said kept. I have sand. That's the list."

### Haelin

- **Want they will say:** the square's day.
- **Avoid:** ranking guests. The word Phoenix as a plan for tonight.

*If asked to come up:* "I have a Tree. I don't have a legend. If your lamp is a civic problem, bring it down. If it isn't, drink water."

### Taeren — only if no PC holds the seat

*If the old name is in the room:* "That isn't mine. Why does the fire know it."

They want bread and the week over. They are not cryptic.

## Stop lines

- **Vimein does not come.** No one with a new name comes from the Care.
- **No fortress.** The endowment stays a sentence.
- **No remnant-to-ash.** No Tree deciding between two names.
- **No second Gift.** No fishbowl tax unless they fly or Kindle.
- **Do not use this hour to prove the Leaf-Mother.**
