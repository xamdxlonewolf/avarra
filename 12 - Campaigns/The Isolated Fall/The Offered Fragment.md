# The Offered Fragment

> **GM kit, not a novel.** You are still on the live front, the square in the middle of its own week, with Hale-month going over into Eolthael in Cut-year 387. There are four key moments, and one fragment if they are actually in the room for it. The house is already present as the sentence the square owns, that the one is in care, and the house itself does not arrive.

## Where it sits

[[The Walk Home]] may have ended on the stone, on the track, or in Brenod. Start from whichever of those the table actually reached, and do not reset them to a clean morning. If they never walked, Tora's breakfast is still unresolved, and it is allowed to stay unresolved. This kit does not require the walk.

The week is late Hale-month, or the first dull days of Eolthael. Leaf-Fall is not this session, and the neighbour's week was last year, so do not stage a Turning to force a colour into the record.

## Which seats are on-screen

**One Gift.** It is the same seat as session one, held by Taeren or by a player character and not by both, and the walker is not a Phoenix you add to the roster. If both of them are in the upper room, what looks like two is leftover fire standing next to a Gift. Do not explain that sentence to the table, and let them have the two heats and no lecture.

## Opening situation

Someone has swept the upper room. The sand Rosire will not sit in is a habit, not a new clue you narrate out of nothing. If they already saw the flinch, then what you have now is only a cleaner room. If they never went up, the flinch is still available to you, and it is still not the image you open on.

Downstairs, Haelin's day is ropes, or the lack of ropes if the stone has not moved. A neighbour repeats the hospice sentence, because someone has asked again whether the one is coming. The sentence is the one already on [[The Protectors]]' slip: the one is in care, and they do not produce a person for a square. There is no new letter, nobody has been hired to say it, and Vimein does not come to say it.

The lamp is in the room, and it is not the first thing you describe.

## What the table can see

- A square caught between the dust and the next month, with fewer extra visitors than Hale-month brought, and the stone still where it was.
- Rosire, carrying water, grey under the nails, and bad at the question *where did you sit before*.
- The walker, if they are in the room: wings furled, scars they cannot list, and a window they prefer to a door.
- The lamp, still closed. It leans only if the Gift is near and you have earned the quiet. It is not a parade.
- The slip's sentence, said by a neighbour, or found in the drawer if they already found it. It is not a new policy.
- One fragment on offer, which may be a count, a layout, a name the fire still answers, or a face that has appeared on a scar. These are facts. The player is the one who chooses.

## What stays player choice

- Whether they go up to the room at all. The day is allowed to be the square and nothing else.
- Whether they take the fragment, leave it, or rewrite the meaning. A fragment they refuse does not come back louder because they were brave enough to refuse.
- Whether they believe the useful lie, that the walker is the real one and the child is a fraud. A neighbour may say that lie. You do not.
- What they do with Rosire. Gratitude, anger, a job, or nothing are all available, and none of them is scheduled.
- Whether anyone opens the lamp. Opening it shows a count, a room, and two names. It does not show a mother, a forgiveness, or a second Gift.

## Four key moments (not a plot)

The primary genre is **mystery**. You get one **wrongness** glimpse, only inside moment 3 and only if they are there for it, and you do not run that glimpse as horror or crystallize a solution.

### 1. The sentence, downstairs

**Mystery — false resolution, offered by a neighbour and not taken by you.**

Someone downstairs has an answer that would close the week, if you let it. They will say the one is in care, that the child is a story people are being unkind about, and that the guest upstairs is sick, or a pilgrim, or none of their business. Haelin will agree with the part of that answer that keeps the square from making history. They will not agree to produce anyone.

**Feel:** The relief of a question that sounds closed, and the unease that is still moving under the relief. You want curiosity, not dread.

**What changes:** The table hears the lie the town is able to live on. They do not have to puncture it.

**Bridge:** The stairs, if they take them, and the water, if they don't. Do not cut straight from this talk to the two fires.

### 2. Rosire at the threshold

**Relationship, kept small.** They want to be forgotten, and they want the child not to become a story. Both of those wants can be said as work they are doing.

They will bring water as far as the doorway, and they will not sit in a room that has been laid with sand. If someone asks who opened a door, the line from session one still holds, shorter if they have already said it: someone walked, and they walked the other way.

**Feel:** You are playing a person, not a confession cut into a scene.

**What changes:** The helper remains a choice. If the table is kind, Rosire does not become grateful on a schedule. If the table is angry, they flinch and keep the water coming.

*If asked whether to send them back:* "Don't. I don't have the key. I have the floor, and I'd like to keep it."

### 3. What the fire still knows

**Wrongness glimpse (~5%), only here.** You are showing a pattern, not delivering a lecture.

If they are near the lamp, or if the walker says an old name out of habit, the coal answers. What answers is one fragment. It may be a count of burns in a room, a door that was not on a slate, or a name that is not the one the child uses. The Gift's player chooses whether to take it, leave it, or decide its meaning. If the fragment is on the walker's body and no player character sits in the Gift's seat, the walker is the one who chooses.

The walker does not remember the deaths that made the marks. Do not fill those murders in just because the lamp produced a number.

**Feel:** Warmth, with a theft underneath it. Hold the moment still, and then let it go to tea, or to the window.

**What changes:** A fact is now in the room. The pattern that fact belongs to is the table's to make.

**Do not:** say remnant, bank, harvest, or the Protectors as harvesters, and do not confirm her. Do not let the fire tell them who they are. If it seems to, what they are hearing is a keeper's sentence, and they may refuse it.

### 4. Two heats, and no verdict

**Mystery — still progressive.** The child and the walker can share an hour without anyone revealing what they are.

Haelin, if someone fetches them, will talk about the stone, and Tora, if they are still in the square, will talk about breakfast. Neither of them will rank the two people who burn. A neighbour might, and you let that ranking stay ugly and unofficial.

**Feel:** The puzzle has a shape and no last piece. That is the session.

**What changes:** Nothing is crowned, and nobody is named the one. The lamp can stay lit, the walker stays unnamed, and the Gift is still one.

**Do not:** fire the beat where the remnant goes to ash, or where a Tree arbitrates between two names. Clue 5 stays unfired. This is not the close.

## Tone

**Anchor:** an upper room at the end of a dusty month, with the square still going on downstairs.

| Dial | This session |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Colleagues, and a guest. The ~5% arrives only at the lamp, and only if they go to it. |
| Humor | Rosire's deflection can be dry. Do not turn the sand into a joke if the table has gone quiet. |
| Consequence | Medium. A story can leave the square in a neighbour's mouth. There is no arrest, and there is no raid. |
| Wonder / horror | The wonder was last session's slope. This session is the quiet wrongness, and you get it once. |
| Description | Give the room in three sentences: the sand, the window, and the lamp. Linger on the lean. Do not describe the Care. |
| Energy | Downstairs stays medium. The threshold drops. The lamp is still. Afterward, decompress on purpose. |

**Transitions.** A broom, a stair, and the time of day will carry you. After moment 3, someone pours something, and you do not walk them from the coal straight into a moral.

**What this session is not.** It is not a heist on the hospice, not a second opening, not a trial of Rosire, and not cosmic horror.

## Voices

### Rosire *(ro-SEER-eh)*

- **Want they will say:** The floor, not to be sent for, and the child left out of songs.
- **Avoid:** The name of the house, any account of the travel, and a full telling of what happened.
- **Rhythm:** Fragments, with a job kept in the hand.

*If they are thanked:* "Don't. Water's the job. The rest I already walked away from."

*If they are blamed:* "I opened a door that wasn't on the slate. I didn't open a square. You can be angry. I'm still not sitting in that room."

### The walker — unnamed

- **Want they will say:** The window, and not to be produced for anyone.
- **Avoid:** Finishing a life into a story. They may answer to an old name as a habit, in the moment when the lamp leans.
- **Rhythm:** Present tense, with gaps where a death would otherwise be told.

*If asked the count:* "More than I can put down. They said kept. I have sand. That's the list."

### Haelin

- **Want they will say:** The square's ordinary day.
- **Avoid:** Ranking the guests, and the word Phoenix used as a plan for tonight.

*If asked to come up:* "I have a Tree. I don't have a legend. If your lamp is a civic problem, bring it down. If it isn't, drink water."

### Taeren — only if no PC holds the seat

*If the old name is in the room:* "That isn't mine. Why does the fire know it."

They want bread, and they want the week to be over. They are not being cryptic.

## Stop lines

- **Vimein does not come.** Nobody carrying a new name comes from the Care.
- **No fortress.** The endowment stays a sentence. You do not build it into a place the table can walk into.
- **No remnant-to-ash.** No Tree decides between two names.
- **No second Gift.** No fishbowl tax, the recognition the public legend lives under, unless they fly or Kindle.
- **Do not use this hour to prove the Leaf-Mother.**
