# The Walk Home

> **GM kit, not a novel.** These are the days after [[The Opening]], still Hale-month of Cut-year 387. You have four key moments and not a plot. The square still has its own day to get through. A walk to Brenod is a choice the table can make, and one fragment may be offered along the way. Offering none is legal.

## Where it sits

Session one has already happened. The stone is still in the square, and the witness has still not walked home, unless the table walked it in the last hour and you are only catching them up on the road. Eolthael still begins next week, and Leaf-Fall, the rite at the Tree, comes inside that month. Do not play the solstice.

[[Harrow's Green]] stays a square in [[Saelvaeth]]'s orbit, and it is not the seat of the march. If the party wants a door they can blame, that door is [[Natai]], which is not this week, and you do not send them there to use a new town.

The ford is down the slope, and Brenod is the first kitchen past it. Vaelun and Ornath lie further along the same water. Their people stay on [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]]. Do not collect those hamlets into a crew, and do not walk anyone out to [[The First Bowl]] or to [[Sanbreo]] just to give the day a destination.

## Which seats are on-screen

There is **one Gift**. [[Taeren]] holds the unseen-leaf seat, the fall whose colour was not Spoken, or a player character holds it, and not both. The walker is leftover fire, and they stay unnamed. If they come down the stair, they are a person who wants a window and water, not a second bird and not the one for Leaf-Fall, for the law, or for the roster.

If the table never took the unseen-leaf seat and Taeren is the child, the child can walk. If a player character holds the seat, the moment when the canopy comes into sight is theirs. Do not run Taeren beside them.

## Opening situation

The dust is still up, and the cistern-notice is still posted. Haelin still wants the stone moved before the crowd comes, and the stone is still the place the day drinks. Tora is still here, or is about to leave, because a colour that was not Spoken cannot be breakfast in Brenod until someone who was *there* walks it home.

The upper room is as session one left it, with the lamp closed, and you do not start there.

That is the situation. The party may stay on the ropes, they may walk, or they may do neither of those and simply eat.

## What the table can see

- The same necessity-day as before: ropes, the dust of Hale-month, water on the stone, and the hamlets down the slope when the light is kind enough to show them.
- Tora's ask, said as a kitchen matter and not as a quest. A child was seen at a canopy, and a breakfast is coming that will not know what to call them.
- The track down to the ford, which is a cart-track and not a pilgrimage.
- If they go: Brenod's door, bread, and a neighbour who was not at the Tree and wants the week to be ordinary.
- One offer, and only if someone is quiet enough to meet it. Sand on a cuff, or the shape of a room when the walker looks back at the canopy. A fact, and not a speech.
- The public sentence, if it comes up at all: *the one is in care.* Not the harvest.

## What stays player choice

- Whether anyone walks the witnessing home. Staying on the stone is a finished day.
- Whether the walker comes down, and whether anyone asks them to.
- Whether the offered fragment is taken, left, or kept as a fact whose meaning is theirs. A prepared room can stay a pressure in the wrist, a thing the body still holds, and it does not have to become a biography.
- Whether the child is talked about on the road. Tora will not call them a story. A party can.
- What they want that is not fire: wages, a bed, the stone moved or left where it is, bread in Brenod.

Do not decide the missing deaths for them. Do not decide that the walk means they have chosen a side.

## Four key moments (not a plot)

The primary genre is still **mystery**, the same unanswered question from session one, now with a road running through it. The secondary is **wonder** at how small the canopy is once you are on the slope. There is no wrongness beat unless they lean on the fragment. If they do, linger once, and then let the next thing be bread. Do not open the session on that moment.

Play the moments in any order the table reaches. Do not skip the square in order to start on the hill.

### 1. The stone is still the drink

**Mystery — the question is still civic.** Someone has to move water before they can move a legend.

Haelin points at the stone with the notice rather than with a name. A neighbour asks who is walking back, and another says the child can stay, because Hale-month still has beds. Tora does not argue the theology. They argue breakfast.

**Feel:** The week is simply continuing. Curiosity can sit in the square, and it does not have to lead anywhere.

**What changes:** The table sees that the hush, the colour nobody Spoke, has a kitchen attached to it, and that the kitchen is allowed to win.

**Bridge:** Dust, a rope, and the track, visible when someone turns around. Do not cut away to the lamp.

### 2. The canopy from the slope

**Wonder — scale, the other way.** The stories said a fall would fill a crowd. From the track, the green is one tree and a wedge of roofs.

If a **player character holds the Gift**, this sightline is theirs. The hamlets sit in the same look, and the heat is in the hands if they are hiding it. Linger on how small the canopy is, and then the ford, which is a ford.

If **Taeren** is the child, they ask an ordinary question, which is how far it is to breakfast. They do not explain the white.

**Feel:** Awe that does not fit the job of walking downhill. Not a festival, and not a capital.

**What changes:** The isolated fall becomes a distance you can see, not a sermon someone preaches.

**Do not:** confirm the Leaf-Mother. If a neighbour on the track says *she Gave*, that is a neighbour talking. Do not put the hamlets' cup ([[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]]) on the rock, since this is not the night before the neighbour's week, and do not explain why the hamlets' cup is ever there.

### 3. Sand, if they meet it

**Mystery — a fragment, offered.** There is one fragment, and they can leave it where it is.

On the walk, or at the door of the upper room if they never left the square, the walker or the Gift meets a fact the body kept. It may be sand where a cuff was, or it may be the layout of a room that is not this square, arriving as a picture and not as a voice. [[A Hidden Phoenix]] already owns the choice. If they take it, they may name what came back, and if they leave it, the fragment does not get louder for having been refused. They may rewrite the meaning, and they may not rewrite the room if the room is already established.

**Feel:** Quiet. A question that now has a body, and not a confrontation, and not a fight.

**What changes:** Something changes only if they take the fragment. If they leave it, the day is unchanged, and that unchanged day is a result.

**Do not:** narrate a harvest, call anyone a remnant, or say who the deaths were. Do not stage a death to prove the walker cannot Rise. Stored fire, if the lamp is opened later, shows facts, and it does not assign a self. Do not open the session on this moment.

### 4. Brenod's door, or the square again

**Mystery — progressive, not crystallized.** A witnessing is a sentence a kitchen can use, and it is not a verdict on who the child is.

If they walk in, Tora puts bread down. They want the colour said the way a neighbour says the weather, and then they want the child to be a child at the table. A Brenod neighbour who was not under the boughs may ask if the one is coming. Tora will not let that question become the child's name.

If they do not walk, Haelin pays the ropes in bread. The witness is still unwalked, and Brenod's breakfast will have to treat the week as weather, because nobody carried the colour home. That is allowed to stand.

**Feel:** Pressure, without a hunt. If Rosire is in the room, the helper is still a person with a flinch, not a clue you are owed.

**What changes:** The table has chosen whether a kitchen hears the week. Session two can end on a road, in a doorway, or back on the stone. Nobody is named as the one.

**Do not:** fire clue 5. Do not have Vimein, [[Reimaethe]], or [[Hithaen]] arrive. Do not give the walker a name so that Brenod's book looks tidy.

## Tone

**Anchor:** a necessity-day that might turn into a walk, with the fire kept at the edge.

| Dial | This session |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Neighbours, with the scary edge at ~5% and only if they take the fragment. |
| Humor | Dry, and it lives on the notice and on breakfast. No slapstick on the slope. If the table jokes through the sand, let the sand wait. |
| Consequence | Medium. A witness nobody walked home leaves a child with no one to vouch for them at a table. A witness who is walked lets a story travel as far as one kitchen. There is no hunt. |
| Wonder / horror | Wonder belongs on the slope, and mystery belongs in the ask. Horror is not this kit. |
| Description | Two sentences for the track. Linger once, on the green seen from above. Do not purple the ford. |
| Energy | The square is medium. The slope is slow. The door starts low, and then there is bread. |

**Transitions.** You move by the dust, then the track, then a door or a return to the square. After the fragment comes water, and you do not cut from the sand straight into a hymn.

**What this session is not.** It is not a chase, not a map of the march, not a second version of session one, and not a proof of the Leaf-Mother.

## Voices

Cover the tags. Do not let every speaker explain the Phoenix.

### Haelin *(HAY-lin)*

- **Want they will say:** The stone, moved before the next crowd, with the water still on it.
- **Avoid:** The colour, and any sending of the party as if they were the warden's own hand.
- **Rhythm:** Short, in the voice of the notice.

*If asked whether to walk:* "Brenod can feed a child without my sentence. I can tell a colour. I am moving a stone."

### Tora *(TOR-ah)* of Brenod

- **Want they will say:** Someone who was there, walking the colour home, and then breakfast.
- **Avoid:** Harrow's luck, and the public name applied to the child.
- **Rhythm:** Longer once they are on the road. Duty is what you hear. They count hills, not hymns.

*On the track:* "If we don't walk it, I still have to say something at the table. I'd rather say you were there than say the week was weather."

*If they call the child the one:* "Don't. Brenod has bread. It doesn't have a legend, and I'm not bringing one down this hill."

### The walker — unnamed, if they are on the road

- **Want they will say:** Water, and not to be shown to a kitchen.
- **Avoid:** A count of the lives, and any title.
- **Rhythm:** Gaps. They answer a question with the thing that is in front of them.

*If asked to come:* "I'll stand where the window was. I don't know their table."

Do not give them a byname because the road feels long.

### Taeren — only if no PC holds the seat

- **Want they will say:** Bread, and not to be talked about on the way down.
- **Rhythm:** Young, and ordinary. A question, not a riddle.

*If the slope makes the tree look small:* "Is that all it is from here."

## Stop lines

- **The Care does not appear on the hill.** There is no keep, and there is no Vimein.
- **No second bird.** The lamp stays shut unless they go back and open it, and even then you give them facts and then tea.
- **No remnant-to-ash.**
- **No second Given-door.** Haelin does not stand the child again.
- **Do not name the Protectors as harvesters.**
- **Do not settle the Leaf-Mother.**
