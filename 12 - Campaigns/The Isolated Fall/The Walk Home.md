# The Walk Home

> **GM kit, not a novel.** The days after [[The Opening]]. Still Hale-month, C.Y. 387. Four key moments, not a plot. The square still has a day. A walk to Brenod is a choice. One fragment may be offered. Zero is legal.

## Where it sits

Session one already happened. The stone is still in the square. The witness has still not walked home, unless the table walked it in the last hour and you are only catching the road. Next week is still Eolthael. Leaf-Fall is still a month out. Do not play the solstice.

[[Harrow's Green]] stays a square in [[Saelvaeth]]'s orbit. It is not the march's seat. If the party wants a door that can be blamed, that door is [[Natai]], and it is not this week. Do not send them there to use a new town.

The ford is down the slope. Brenod is the first kitchen past it. Vaelun and Ornath are further on the same water. Their mouths stay on [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]]. Do not collect them into a crew. Do not walk anyone to [[The First Bowl]] or to [[Sanbreo]] to give the day a destination.

## Which seats are on-screen

There is **one Gift**. [[Taeren]] holds the unseen-leaf seat, or a PC does. Not both. The walker is leftover fire. They stay unnamed. If they come down the stair, they are a person who wants a window and water. They are not a second bird, and they are not the one for Leaf-Fall, law, or the roster.

If the table never took the unseen-leaf seat and Taeren is the child, the child can walk. If a PC holds the seat, the moment at the canopy-in-sight is theirs. Do not run Taeren beside them.

## Opening situation

Dust. The cistern-notice is still up. Haelin still wants the stone moved before the crowd, and the stone is still the day's drink. Tora is still here, or is about to go, because a colour that was not Spoken cannot be breakfast in Brenod until a mouth that was *there* walks it.

The upper room is as session one left it. The lamp is closed. Do not start there.

That is the situation. The party may stay on the ropes. They may walk. They may do neither and eat.

## What the table can see

- The same necessity-day: ropes, Hale-dust, water on the stone, hamlets down the slope when the light is kind.
- Tora's ask, said as a kitchen and not as a quest. A child seen at a canopy. A breakfast that will not know what to call them.
- The track to the ford. It is a cart-track. It is not a pilgrimage.
- If they go: Brenod's door, bread, a neighbour who was not at the Tree and wants the week ordinary.
- One offer, and only if someone is quiet enough to meet it: sand on a cuff, or the shape of a room when the walker looks back at the canopy. A fact. Not a speech.
- The public sentence, if it comes up: *the one is in care.* Not the harvest.

## What stays player choice

- Whether anyone walks the witnessing home. Staying on the stone is a finished day.
- Whether the walker comes down. Whether anyone asks them to.
- Whether the offered fragment is taken, left, or kept as a fact whose meaning is theirs. A prepared room can stay a pressure in the wrist. It does not have to become a biography.
- Whether the child is talked about on the road. Tora will not call them a story. A party can.
- What they want that is not fire: wages, a bed, the stone moved or left, bread in Brenod.

Do not decide the missing deaths. Do not decide that the walk means they have chosen a side.

## Four key moments (not a plot)

Primary genre: **mystery**, still the hole from session one, now with a road in it. Secondary: **wonder** at how small the canopy is from the slope. No wrongness beat unless they lean on the fragment. If they do, one linger, then bread. Do not open on it.

Play them in any order the table reaches. Do not skip the square in order to start on the hill.

### 1. The stone is still the drink

**Mystery — the question is still civic.** Someone has to move water before they move a legend.

Haelin points at the stone with the notice, not with a name. A neighbour asks who is walking back. Another says the child can stay; Hale-month has beds. Tora does not argue the theology. They argue breakfast.

**Feel:** the week continuing. Curiosity can sit. It does not have to lead.

**What changes:** the table sees that the hush has a kitchen attached, and the kitchen is allowed to win.

**Bridge:** dust, a rope, the track visible when someone turns. Do not cut to the lamp.

### 2. The canopy from the slope

**Wonder — scale, the other way.** The stories said a fall would be a crowd. From the track, the green is one tree and a wedge of roofs.

If a **PC holds the Gift**, this sightline is theirs. Hamlets in the same look. Heat in the hands, if they are hiding it. Linger. Then the ford, which is a ford.

If **Taeren** is the child, they ask an ordinary question: how far is breakfast. They do not explain white.

**Feel:** awe that does not fit the job. Not a festival. Not a capital.

**What changes:** the isolated fall is a distance, not a sermon.

**Do not:** confirm the Leaf-Mother. If a neighbour on the track says *she Gave*, that is a neighbour. Do not put the cup on the rock. This is not the night before the neighbour's week. Do not explain why a cup is ever there.

### 3. Sand, if they meet it

**Mystery — a fragment, offered.** One. They can leave it.

On the walk, or at the upper-room door if they never left: the walker, or the Gift, meets a fact the body kept. Sand where a cuff was. Or the layout of a room that is not this square, arrived as a picture and not as a voice. [[A Hidden Phoenix]] already owns the choice. Take it, and they may name what came back. Leave it, and it does not get louder. Rewrite the meaning, not the room, if the room is already established.

**Feel:** quiet. A puzzle with a body. Not a confrontation. Not combat.

**What changes:** only if they take it. If they leave it, the day is unchanged, and that is a result.

**Do not:** narrate harvest, remnant, or who the deaths were. Do not stage a death to prove the walker cannot Rise. Stored fire, if the lamp is opened later, shows facts. It does not assign a self. Do not open the session on this moment.

### 4. Brenod's door, or the square again

**Mystery — progressive, not crystallized.** A witnessing is a sentence a kitchen can use. It is not a verdict.

If they walk in: Tora puts bread down. They want the colour said the way a neighbour says weather, and then they want the child to be a child at the table. A Brenod mouth who was not under the boughs may ask if the one is coming. Tora will not let that become the child's name.

If they do not walk: Haelin pays the ropes in bread. The witness is still unwalked. Brenod will have weather at breakfast. That is allowed to stand.

**Feel:** pressure without a hunt. The helper, if Rosire is in the room, is still a person with a flinch, not a clue.

**What changes:** the table has chosen whether a kitchen hears the week. Session two can end on a road, in a doorway, or on the stone. No one is named as the one.

**Do not:** fire clue 5. Do not have Vimein, [[Reimaethe]], or [[Hithaen]] arrive. Do not give the walker a name to make Brenod's book tidy.

## Tone

**Anchor:** a necessity-day that might become a walk. Fire stays at the edge.

| Consequence | Medium. An unwalked witness leaves a child unvouched at a table. A walked one lets a story travel one kitchen. No hunt. |

**Transitions.** Dust, then the track, then a door or a return. After the fragment, water. Do not smash-cut from sand to a hymn.

**What this session is not.** A chase. A map of the march. A second session-one. A Leaf-Mother proof.

## Voices

Cover the tags. Do not let every mouth explain the Phoenix.

### Haelin *(HAY-lin)*

- **Want they will say:** the stone, before the next crowd. Water still on it.
- **Avoid:** the colour. Sending the party as if they were a warden's hand.
- **Rhythm:** short. The notice.

*If asked whether to walk:* "Brenod can feed a child without my sentence. I can tell a colour. I am moving a stone."

### Tora *(TOR-ah)* of Brenod

- **Want they will say:** a mouth that was there, walking. Then breakfast.
- **Avoid:** Harrow's luck. The public name for the child.
- **Rhythm:** longer on the road. Duty. They count hills, not hymns.

*On the track:* "If we don't walk it, I still have to say something at the table. I'd rather say you were there than say the week was weather."

*If they call the child the one:* "Don't. Brenod has bread. It doesn't have a legend, and I'm not bringing one down this hill."

### The walker — unnamed, if they are on the road

- **Want they will say:** water. Not to be shown to a kitchen.
- **Avoid:** a count of lives. A title.
- **Rhythm:** gaps. They answer a question with the thing in front of them.

*If asked to come:* "I'll stand where the window was. I don't know their table."

Do not give them a byname because the road feels long.

### Taeren — only if no PC holds the seat

- **Want they will say:** bread. Not to be talked about on the way down.
- **Rhythm:** young. Ordinary. A question, not a riddle.

*If the slope makes the tree look small:* "Is that all it is from here."
