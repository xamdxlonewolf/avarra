# The Week Still Open

> **GM kit, not a novel.** This is early Eolthael in Cut-year 387, the month after Hale. Leaf-Fall is still ahead of you, and it is not this session. You have four key moments. The fragment you offer by default is none. The campaign does not close.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sits** | [[Harrow's Green]], a square doing the work of the next month. Not a solstice, not a capital, and not the Care. |
| **On-screen** | Haelin, whoever is still in the upper room, Tora if the walk home is still unpaid, and the new Gift ([[Taeren]] or a player character). |
| **Offstage** | [[Reimaethe]], [[Hithaen]], Vimein, the six pivots, a second Gift, and an ending. |
| **Do not** | Play Leaf-Fall. Fire the moment the remnant goes to ash. Lead with a second bird. Name the walker. Confirm her, the Leaf-Mother. Invent a fortress. Bring the old Epic 8 roster in to fill the month. |

## Where it sits

[[The Opening]] was Hale-month, and [[The Walk Home]] and [[The Offered Fragment]] used the days after that opening. This kit is the first of Eolthael, with fewer visitors from the dust-season, the same stone, and the same hush. High Solstice is not on this page. Do not skip ahead to it on the grounds that the fire has been patient.

If the table has already walked the witness home, Tora may be gone. If they have not, the kitchen is still waiting, and that wait is allowed to be boring. Boring is a civic fact. Do not punish it with a raid.

## Which seats are on-screen

**One Gift** is still the rule, the same as before, with Taeren or a player character holding the seat and not both. The walker, if they are still upstairs, stays unnamed and cannot Rise, and you do not prove that with a death. If the walker has left the square because the table sent them away or because they walked, do not drag them back to keep the mystery staffed. The absence is a result, and Rosire follows the same rule. A person who was forgotten on purpose may stay forgotten.

## Opening situation

The first mornings of Eolthael are cooler, and the cistern-notice is still only a notice. Haelin has not moved the stone, or else has moved it, and either way that angers the road-drink, the people who still take their water at that stone. Leaf-Fall is what the square is afraid of having no room for, and it is not tonight.

Someone in the square is telling the Phoenix story in two tenses again. That telling may now include a child, or a guest, or neither, depending on what the party actually fed it. You do not add a verse.

Nobody new arrives from a far power, and the six pivots stay pointed at this front without coming to check the work.

## What the table can see

- A town between two months, with the gardens in use, the stone either moved or still in the way, and the upper room either occupied or stripped.
- The public sentence, unchanged from the weeks before: the one is in care.
- Whatever the party has already made true. A witness who was walked home, a fragment that was refused, a lamp that leaned, or a story a neighbour is now repeating.
- The Gift, still able to Rise, and still not a parade unless they fly or Kindle.
- The road down to the ford, still a cart-track. [[Natai]] is not visible from here, and it is not the errand.

## What stays player choice

- Whether they stay through Eolthael or leave the square. Leaving is not a failure of the kit.
- Whether they make the child into a story before Leaf-Fall. Haelin would rather they did not. The table may do it anyway.
- Whether they go toward the house that kept the walker. This kit does not take them there. The door, if they want it later, is not a fortress you build during this session.
- Whether a fragment is asked for. If they ask, you offer one. If they do not ask, you offer zero. Do not offer a louder fragment because the campaign has already had three sessions.
- What the scars mean. That meaning is still theirs.
- Whether a Phoenix player character Rises, if a death move comes out of play. Never force it. The walker has no such move available.

## Four key moments (not a plot)

The primary genre is **mystery**, and by now it lives as gossip and as a date on the calendar. The secondary is **wonder**, and only if they look at the canopy again and find that it is still just a tree. Do not build a new set-piece of wrongness. If the lamp is already known, do not restage it for the people who missed it. Catch them up in a sentence from someone who was there.

### 1. The month turns without a festival

**Drama, kept civic.** The pressure from outside is a date, not a villain.

Haelin is counting beds against a Leaf-Fall that has not arrived. A neighbour wants the guest gone before a crowd comes. Another wants the child walked home, or has already heard the story from Brenod and is improving it in the retelling. The square is doing what it did in Hale-month, with less dust in the air.

**Feel:** What you are playing is time passing. The absence is still an absence, and nobody is required to solve it before breakfast.

**What changes:** The table feels the solstice coming as a problem of room. You do not start the rite.

### 2. What travelled

**Mystery — pattern, as other people say it.**

Repeat, as gossip, only what the party actually did: a colour that was walked home, a guest who burns, a lamp if they showed the lamp. If they showed nothing, the gossip is the old two tenses and the child who asks for bread. Do not invent a witness who saw the harvest.

**Feel:** The pleasure of being talked about, and the risk that sits in the same talk. Curiosity, a little heat, and not dread.

**What changes:** They learn that the square keeps stories the way it keeps water: badly, and in public.

*Haelin, if the story has grown:* "I asked for a stone moved. I have a song. Fix your own verse."

### 3. The window

**Mystery — the walker, or their absence.**

If the walker is still here, they want the window and they do not want a crowd. They will not finish a life into a story just because the month changed. An old name may leak out if someone is unkind enough to wait for it. The leak is habit, not a title they are claiming this morning.

If they are gone, the room has a blanket folded, or it does not, and Rosire will not narrate the departure into a clue. "They wanted water. They didn't want a square." That can be the whole of it.

**Feel:** Quiet. A person who is not being used as the end of a chapter.

**What changes:** The remnant has still not gone to ash, the store is not spent on-screen, and Clue 5 does not fire just because Eolthael arrived.

### 4. Stop in the open

**Not a crystallization.** Say what is still unchosen, and then let the table pick the next thing they want. Do not pick it for them.

Still open, unless they closed it in play:

- who the walker is
- whether the missing deaths come back
- whether the child is a story
- whether anyone goes toward the house
- whether the stone moves
- whether Leaf-Fall will Speak anything
- the Leaf-Mother
- the remnant going to ash

**Feel:** A week the table is allowed to leave, which you do not sharpen into a cliffhanger. What you end on is bread, the notice, and the tree.

**What changes:** The campaign is still going, this kit is not the close, and the endings stay unwritten until a later ask.

**Do not:** deliver a last image of two fires, of a leaf falling, or of a keep on the rise, and do not name a cutter, date the Tree, or add a power.

## Tone

**Anchor:** early Eolthael, in a square that has already had its strange week.

| Dial | This session |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Neighbours. The 5% of wrongness stays out of the scene unless they open it themselves. |
| Humor | The voice of the notice. Gossip can be funny until the joke is about the child, and then you let it be ugly and you let it be short. |
| Consequence | Medium, and mostly social. A verse can stick to a person. A bed can be refused. There is no army. |
| Wonder / horror | Little wonder, no set-piece of horror, and the mystery living as talk. |
| Description | The morning in two sentences. Do not describe the lamp again unless it is in someone's hand. |
| Energy | Medium, and then low. End on a chore, not a sting. |

**Transitions.** Move them with a cooler morning, with a sentence someone retells, and with a window. Do not cut from the gossip to a cosmology.

**What this session is not.** It is not the finale, not an episode of the solstice, not a reunion of every seed, and not a tour of the seats Epic S named.

## Voices

Use the people who are still in the square. Do not import a new person to deliver the theme.

### Haelin

- **Want they will say:** Room for the coming crowd, water, and a stone that remains a civic object.
- **Avoid:** Any plan that has been given the name Phoenix.
- **Rhythm:** Shorter than last month. They are tired of the verse people are singing.

*If asked what Eolthael changes:* "The dust drops. The Tree doesn't. Leaf-Fall is not this week, and I am not spending this week to make it exciting."

### Tora — only if the witness is still unwalked

*If they are still waiting:* "Brenod ate. The child is still a weather report. I can wait. I don't like it."

They are not a herald of the next plot.

### Rosire — if they stayed

*If asked what next:* "The floor, until someone makes it a story. Then I won't be on it."

### The walker — unnamed, if they stayed

*At the window:* "It's a month. I have been in months. I don't have them in a row."

### Taeren — only if no PC holds the seat

*If Leaf-Fall is mentioned:* "Do I have to stand there again. I wanted breakfast."

## Stop lines

- **Do not play the solstice.**
- **Do not fire clue 5.**
- **Do not confirm the Leaf-Mother.**
- **Do not build the Care onto the rise.**
- **Do not name the walker.**
- **Do not seat a second Gift** in order to keep both shapes alive through Eolthael.
- **Reimaethe and Hithaen stay offstage.**
