# The Week Still Open

> **GM kit, not a novel.** Early Eolthael, C.Y. 387. The month after Hale. Leaf-Fall is still ahead, and it is not this session. Four key moments. The default fragment is none. The campaign does not close.

## Where it sits

[[The Opening]] was Hale-month. [[The Walk Home]] and [[The Offered Fragment]] used the days after. This kit is the first of Eolthael: fewer dust-mouths, the same stone, the same hush. High Solstice is not on this page. Do not skip to it because the fire has been patient.

If the table has already walked the witness, Tora may be gone. If they have not, the kitchen is still waiting, and that wait is allowed to be boring. Boring is a civic fact. Do not punish it with a raid.

## Which seats are on-screen

**One Gift.** Same rule. Taeren or a PC. The walker, if they are still upstairs, stays unnamed and cannot Rise. Do not prove that with a death. If the walker has left the square because the table sent them or they walked, do not drag them back to keep the mystery staffed. The absence is a result. Rosire the same. A person who was forgotten on purpose may stay forgotten.

## Opening situation

Eolthael's first mornings are cooler. The cistern-notice is still a notice. Haelin has not moved the stone, or has, and the road-drink is angry either way. Leaf-Fall is what the square is afraid of having no room for. It is not tonight.

Someone in the square is telling the Phoenix in two tenses again. The telling may now include a child, or a guest, or neither, depending on what the party fed. You do not add a verse.

No new mouth arrives from a far power. The six pivots stay pointed at this front. They do not come to check the work.

## What the table can see

- A town between months. Gardens. The stone, moved or not. The upper room, occupied or stripped.
- The public sentence, unchanged: the one is in care.
- Whatever the party already made true: a walked witness, a refused fragment, a lamp that leaned, a story a neighbour is repeating.
- The Gift, still able to Rise, still not a parade unless they fly or Kindle.
- The road down to the ford, still a cart-track. [[Natai]] is not visible from here and is not the errand.

## What stays player choice

- Whether they stay through Eolthael or leave the square. Leaving is not a failure of the kit.
- Whether they make the child a story before Leaf-Fall. Haelin would rather they did not. The table may.
- Whether they go toward the house that kept the walker. The kit does not take them there. The door, if they want it later, is not a fortress you build this session.
- Whether a fragment is asked for. If they ask, one. If they do not ask, zero. Do not offer a louder one because the campaign has had three sessions.
- What the scars mean. Still theirs.
- Whether a Phoenix PC Rises, if a death move comes from play. Never force it. The walker has no such move.

## Four key moments (not a plot)

Primary: **mystery**, now living as gossip and a calendar. Secondary: **wonder** only if they look at the canopy again and it is still just a tree. No new wrongness set-piece. If the lamp is already known, do not restage it for the people who missed it. Catch them up in a sentence from someone who was there.

### 1. The month turns without a festival

**Drama, kept civic.** The external pressure is a date, not a villain.

Haelin counts beds against a Leaf-Fall that is not here yet. A neighbour wants the guest gone before a crowd. Another wants the child walked, or already has the story from Brenod and is improving it. The square is doing what it did in Hale, with less dust.

**Feel:** time. The hole is still a hole. Nobody is required to solve it before breakfast.

**What changes:** the table feels the solstice coming as a room problem. You do not start the rite.

### 2. What travelled

**Mystery — pattern, in other people's mouths.**

Repeat back, as gossip, only what the party actually did. A walked colour. A guest who burns. A lamp, if they showed it. If they showed nothing, the gossip is the old two tenses and the child who asks for bread. Do not invent a witness who saw the harvest.

**Feel:** the pleasure and the risk of being talked about. Curiosity. A little heat. Not dread.

**What changes:** they learn the square keeps stories the way it keeps water: badly, and in public.

*Haelin, if the story has grown:* "I asked for a stone moved. I have a song. Fix your own verse."

### 3. The window

**Mystery — the walker, or their absence.**

If they are still here: they want the window and not a crowd. They will not complete a life because the month changed. An old name may leak if someone is unkind enough to wait for it. It is habit. It is not a title they claim this morning.

If they are gone: the room has a blanket folded or not, and Rosire will not narrate the departure into a clue. "They wanted water. They didn't want a square." That can be all.

**Feel:** quiet. A person who is not a chapter ending.

**What changes:** the remnant is still not ash. The store is not spent on-screen. Clue 5 does not fire because Eolthael arrived.

### 4. Stop in the open

**Not a crystallization.** Say what is still unchosen, then let the table pick the next want. Do not pick it for them.

Still open, unless they closed it in play:

- who the walker is
- whether the missing deaths come back
- whether the child is a story
- whether anyone goes toward the house
- whether the stone moves
- whether Leaf-Fall will Speak anything
- the Leaf-Mother
- the remnant going to ash

**Feel:** a week that can be left. Not a cliffhanger you sharpen. Bread. The notice. The tree.

**What changes:** the campaign is still going. This kit is not the close. Endings stay unwritten until a later ask.

**Do not:** deliver a last image of two fires, a falling leaf, or a keep on the rise. Do not name a cutter. Do not date the Tree. Do not add a power.

## Tone

**Anchor:** early Eolthael in a square that already had its strange week.

| Dial | This session |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Neighbours. The 5% stays in the drawer unless they open it. |
| Humor | Notice-voice. Gossip can be funny until it is about the child; then let it be ugly and short. |
| Consequence | Medium, and mostly social. A verse can stick. A bed can be refused. No army. |
| Wonder / horror | Little wonder. No horror set-piece. Mystery as talk. |
| Description | The morning in two sentences. Do not re-describe the lamp unless it is in a hand. |
| Energy | Medium, then low. End on a chore, not a sting. |

**Transitions.** A cooler morning. A retold sentence. A window. Do not cut from gossip to cosmology.

**What this session is not.** The finale. A solstice episode. A reunion of every seed. A map tour of the seats Epic S named.

## Voices

Use the mouths still in the square. Do not import a new one to deliver theme.

### Haelin

- **Want they will say:** room, water, a stone that is a civic object.
- **Avoid:** a plan called Phoenix.
- **Rhythm:** shorter than last month. They are tired of the verse.

*If asked what Eolthael changes:* "The dust drops. The Tree doesn't. Leaf-Fall is not this week, and I am not spending this week to make it exciting."

### Tora — only if the witness is still unwalked

*If they are still waiting:* "Brenod ate. The child is still a weather report. I can wait. I don't like it."

They are not a herald of the next plot.

### Rosire — if they stayed

*If asked what next:* "The floor, until someone makes it a story. Then I won't be on it."

### The walker — unnamed, if they stayed

*At the window:* "It's a month. I have been in months. I don't have them in a row."

### Taeren — only if no PC holds the seat

*If Leaf-Fall is mentioned:* "Do I have to stand there again. I wanted breakfast."

## Stop lines

- **Do not play the solstice.**
- **Do not fire clue 5.**
- **Do not confirm the Leaf-Mother.**
- **Do not build the Care onto the rise.**
- **Do not name the walker.**
- **Do not seat a second Gift** to keep both shapes in Eolthael.
- **Reimaethe and Hithaen stay offstage.**
