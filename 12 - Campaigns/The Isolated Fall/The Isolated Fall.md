# The Isolated Fall

> **What this is.** The first campaign on this vault. Play starts in a square that is already standing, and the kit for session one is [[The Opening]]. The world notes stay the source of truth. This folder records what the table does to them.

## Premise

The world still talks about **the Phoenix**, and it has not seen one in a generation, so the sentence people will give you in public is that the one is in care. Last Turning-Week, at the neighbour's canopy, something white happened and was not Spoken, which means nobody said the white aloud as the colour of that fall. Someone who burns the way a legend burns walked out of a room that had been prepared for them, and the lamp they kept still carries a fire that answers to more than one person.

There is still **one Gift**. If the room seems to hold two, the extra heat is leftover fire standing next to that Gift, not a second Phoenix. [[When the Fire Is Caught]] is the page that runs that distinction, and [[A Hidden Phoenix]] is where the players' choices are kept.

## Party

The party stays empty until session zero. When you make the characters, follow the order already written in [[At the Table]].

The whole table shares one special seat. If anyone takes the hidden Phoenix, they sit in the **unseen-leaf** seat at [[Harrow's Green]], which is [[Taeren]]'s seat and not a second card you lay beside them. The walker you put on-screen is leftover fire, not a player character written on that sheet. The fishbowl Phoenix, the public legend, is a different campaign, so do not run both taxes.

## Later sessions

Session one stays [[The Opening]]. The kits after it are key moments rather than a plot, and they are not the campaign's close.

| | |
|---|---|
| **After the opening** | [[The Walk Home]], still Hale-month. The stone, or the track to Brenod. One fragment may be offered. |
| **The room** | [[The Offered Fragment]]: the slip's sentence, Rosire at the threshold, and the lamp only if they go to it. |
| **The next month** | [[The Week Still Open]], early Eolthael. Leaf-Fall is not this kit, and the threads stay open. |

One Gift runs through all of it. [[Taeren]] holds that seat, or a player character does, and not both. The walker stays unnamed, [[Reimaethe]] and [[Hithaen]] stay offstage, and there is no Leaf-Mother reveal. The remnant-to-ash confirm stays unfired. You do not build the Protectors a fortress, and you do not lead with a second bird.

## Not written

The campaign's close is not written here. Endings stay undecomposed, left whole rather than broken into scenes you could run. Do not resume the old Epic 8 roster to fill a court. Do not name the cutter, date the Tree, coin liturgy, or add a sixteenth power.
