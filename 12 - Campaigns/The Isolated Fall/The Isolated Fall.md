# The Isolated Fall

> **What this is.** The first campaign on this vault. Play starts at an existing square. The kit for session one is [[The Opening]]. The world notes stay the source of truth; this folder records what the table does to them.

## Premise

A world that still talks about **the Phoenix** has not seen one in a generation. The public sentence is that the one is in care. Last Turning-Week, at the neighbour's canopy, something white happened and was not Spoken. Someone who burns like a legend walked out of a prepared room. The lamp they kept still knows more than one mouth.

There is still **one Gift**. Apparent two is leftover fire standing next to it. Engine: [[When the Fire Is Caught]]. Player agency: [[A Hidden Phoenix]].

## Party

Empty until session zero. Creation order: [[At the Table]].

Special seat, whole table: if anyone takes the hidden Phoenix, they sit in the **unseen-leaf** seat at [[Harrow's Green]] — [[Taeren]]'s seat, not a second card. The walker on-screen is leftover fire, not a PC on that sheet. Fishbowl Phoenix (the public legend) is a different campaign; do not run both taxes.
