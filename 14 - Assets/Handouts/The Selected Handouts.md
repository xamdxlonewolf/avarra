# The Selected Handouts

The unlabeled Prototype 3 sheets are the handouts. They stay in `14 - Assets/Maps`, on [[The Atlas Sheets]]. This folder does not hold a second set.

Labeled overlays are table aids. They are not a second set of handouts. Prototype 3 masters stay in `14 - Assets/Maps/prototype3/`. Prototypes 1 and 2, and the Heskoren label-trial image, are under `99 - Archive/Atlas/`. The comparison is [[Atlas Prototype Review]].

## GM Notes

Do not copy the sheets into this folder. Do not ask an image model to write. Do not draw a new sheet from this note.
