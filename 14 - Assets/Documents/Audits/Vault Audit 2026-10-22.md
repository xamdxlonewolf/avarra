# Vault Audit 2026-10-22

> **What this is.** The report for [[Roadmap#Epic V — Read the whole vault|Epic V]]. Eight readers each read one group of folders all the way through. A script checked front-matter, links, retired phrases, and percentages first. Nothing in the vault was changed by the read. The world book was not read and was not touched. The user reads this report in Story V.3 and says which findings to fix, which to leave, and which are wrong. The fixes are Story V.4.

## The short answer

The world holds together. The engine, the Condition cards, the population count on [[The Premise]], the campaign kits, the Library pages, and the history are sound, and no note names the cutter, dates the Tree, names the Pourers' founder, or adds a power.

What is wrong falls into five kinds, in order of how much it matters:

1. **Facts that break a lock or disagree with another note.** 44 high findings, most of them in a few repeated mistakes. The worst is that four notes say nearly every child in Maiethorn is Given, which is the Walled Book's false lesson written as fact.
2. **GM secrets in player text.** The Leaf-Mother's limit, her household, and the Protectors' fire are hinted at or stated outright in about 25 player bodies.
3. **Design scaffolding in player text.** 🔒 and 🟡, story numbers, skill names, "Do not…" orders to the GM, "Leftover job on this street", "Clock 1 / Clock 2", "~5% dial", and the "X cannot afford to see…" essay paragraphs. Contradictions entry C-01 says this was cleaned up. It was cleaned up in some notes and not in most.
4. **The old voice.** A person is called a "mouth" about 290 times. "Lintel", "sitting", "throat", and "seen seeing" stand in for plain facts. The newer notes are clean. The older notes in Epics 2, 3, 6, and 7 still use these words.
5. **Holes and stale trackers.** Newer seats have no place in [[Named Ground]]. The [[Reveal Index]] is missing about 47 notes. Seven links are broken. The Roadmap and the Build Plan still say "next" for work that is done.

## 1. Facts that break a lock or disagree with another note

These are logged on [[Contradictions]] as C-10 to C-24, with the note that should win.

- **The Kept are counted as rare.** [[Maiethorn]] (lines 26, 42, 64), [[The World Frame]] (58), [[The Reckoning of the Year]] (93), and [[Maiethvael]] (42) say nearly every child is Given, or that almost no child is Kept. The Premise says about two children in five are hugged. That rarity is what [[The Walled Book]] teaches, and the Library primer is meant to be wrong about it. A reference note must not say it as fact. [[The Leaf-Mother]] (59) says "~25% Given no colour", which mixes the share Kept for life with the share hugged at the ceremony. The Premise wins.
- **Kept and UnTurned are confused.** [[Heskoren]] (26) defines the Kept as children who reached no Tree. [[Named Ground]] (42) says a late storm makes a child Kept. [[Turning Tree]] and [[At the Table]] say a child who misses the Tree is UnTurned. Those two notes win.
- **Kumbaan is "near-Treeless".** [[The World Frame]] (52, 61) and [[The Sundering Isle]] (18, 40) still say "near-Treeless" and "no healthy Turning Trees". [[Climate of Kumbaan]] and C-06 say there is no Tree and no graft ever crossed. C-06 is marked resolved, but these lines were missed.
- **Only one crown keeps a list of persons.** [[Law and Citizenship]] (28), [[Polity Archetypes]] (94), and [[Naming in the Turning]] (59) say so. [[Trenledd]] keeps a roll of persons, and [[How a Place Speaks]] names the list-lands. Trenledd and How a Place Speaks win.
- **A second population table.** [[Economy and the Tithe]] (34–37) copies all nine Condition shares and works out its own totals. The Premise is the only census (Story R.1). Replace the table with a link.
- **Long-Lived children.** Economy and the Tithe (122) says they do not inherit the Condition. The Build Plan lock says none are guaranteed to inherit, and [[Daily Life]] agrees. The lock wins.
- **Selkie and drowning.** [[Selkie]] (35) says death by drowning Strikes the Far-Voiced. [[Far-Voiced]] says the threshold is surviving a drowning; dying is the Returned threshold. Far-Voiced wins.
- **The Stilled petrify.** Law and Citizenship (102) speaks of "you petrified a citizen". [[The Stilled]] and Daily Life say the gaze does not petrify and does not harm. The Stilled wins.
- **Narol is alive.** [[Lirorn]] (38) says Narol of the Pass still levies. [[Narol of the Pass]] and [[Tasain]] say Narol is dead and the household still levies. Narol's note wins.
- **Sanbreo's coast.** [[Sanbreo]] (36) puts the town on the West Water coast. [[The Known Map]] (28) puts Ornled on the west, by the last capes. This needs your answer.
- **Two-Bodied has no Struck path.** The Far-Voiced caption (64) calls Two-Bodied a mixed Given and Struck Condition. [[Two-Bodied]] says Given only. Two-Bodied wins.
- **Reimaethe's gift.** [[Reimaethe]] (22, 26) gives years to the cup one at a time and wants to stop before the fifth. [[The Pourers]], [[The Unspent]], and [[Long-Lived]] say a member is asked once and gives five years at one time. This needs your answer.
- **Methei is kept at the beds.** [[Methei]] (21) says the beds stop at dark "if they are allowed to stop". The Walled Book (53, 127) says a person with a Condition can walk out, and the turns of work are owed only by people who live inside the walls. The Walled Book wins.
- **The Yumboe as a nation.** [[Yumboe]] (25) calls them a whole living nation. The lock is that Kinds are not nations, and [[Kinds of the Turning]] says the Yumboe are rare because they live far away. Kinds of the Turning wins.
- **Vaelun's guest-grove.** [[The Watchers]] (36, 147), [[The Door-Keepers]] (126), [[05 - Factions]] (22), and [[The Tree-Wardens]] (178) call the guest-grove Vaelun's. [[The First Bowl]] and [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] say the guest-grove is the First Bowl. The First Bowl wins.

## 2. GM secrets in player text

None of these says the Leaf-Mother is real in so many words. Many say there is a hidden answer, which is nearly as bad.

- **Her limit and her household.** [[Heskoren]] (40) and [[The World Frame]] (28) say distance is not the true cause of thin reach. [[The Ages of the Turning]] (17, 90, 117) speaks of her reach and her limit as fact and calls the grafts "keystone evidence of a hand". [[The Other Count]] (76) says the same about her limit. [[The Open Table]] (20) says she has a household. The narrator's closing paragraphs on [[The Watchers]] (185), [[The Door-Keepers]] (158), and [[The Tree-Wardens]] (162) do the same.
- **The Other Hands.** [[The Unspent]] (41) names two of the Hands in the player body and says "not a sixth", which tells players there are five. Its Links section (59) sits after GM Notes at the same heading level, so it survives export. [[The Grey Summer]] (45) says "No Other Hand."
- **The keystone by name.** [[10 - Items]] (19) calls the spent leaf "the keystone's firing pin". About ten other player lines in `01 - World` say "keystone" or "see GM Notes".
- **The Protectors' fire.** [[The Protectors]] (lines 22, 30, 32, 42, 46, 49, 62, 70) shows across its player body that the Care keeps a store of fire taken from the dying, and that it hushes a second fire on purpose. [[05 - Factions]] (79, 83, 117), [[The Pourers]] (80, "another house does that"), [[The Walled Book]] (99, "Do not store fire in the Close"), [[People of the Turning]] (142, "Hidden second fire"), and [[Rosire]] (26, 39) point at the same thing.
- **Links after the GM wall.** In a dozen notes, `## Links` comes after `## GM Notes` at the same level, so export keeps it. Several of those Links lines carry 🟡, story numbers, or "(GM)" links.
- **Missing `reveals` tags.** Most of the notes above have `reveals: []` or no key at all, so the export filter cannot catch them.

## 3. Design scaffolding in player text

C-01 (P2.1) moved this behind the GM wall in history, faction, settlement, and MOC notes. Much of it is still there.

- **Lock labels and story numbers.** About 80 🔒 and 🟡, about 105 story or epic numbers, "Canon status" blocks, and "Built with `systemic-worldbuilding` for Epic 3" lines. They are heaviest in the old continent notes, [[The Old Tongue]], [[Naming People in the Turning]], [[Kinds of the Turning]], and the faith notes.
- **Orders to the GM in the player body.** The Walking Years has 10 "Do not…" lines, The Years of Hands 12, and The First Cut 11. Fourteen settlements do the same ("Do not make all three the town's theme", "Use that.").
- **Essay paragraphs after in-world texts.** The "X cannot afford to see…" codas appear in 14 history notes and 12 settlements. The "On its face… Underneath…" paragraphs appear in 16 faction notes.
- **Template labels.** "Leftover job on this street" is in 21 settlements, "Clock 1 / Clock 2" in about 50 player lines, "No Epic-8 pivot" in 4, the "~5% dial" in about 15 places, "Unlock:" in 7 notes, and the axis header plus "Leftover:" on all twelve power stubs.
- **Talk about the vault instead of the world.** The newer Items notes say "already says so", "stays unwritten", and "the playable version". Each custom ancestry opens with a "**The fresh angle:**" paragraph. The four Epic 2 notes talk about "grammars", "knobs", and their own revision history.

## 4. The old voice

- **"Mouth" for a person** about 290 times in player bodies, plus "throat". It is in nearly every settlement, faction, and people note. The Standing Trade alone has about 12.
- **Pictures for facts.** "The lintel" for the Closed Seat's gate. "The sitting." "Seen seeing." "A send." "Before the town uses a rope." "The thorn." "Answers two mouths." "The queue to stop being called weather."
- **"C.Y."** about 120 times. It is short for Cut-year and not wrong, but a reader who has not seen it spelled out cannot read it.
- **A riddle.** "A kind Tree would not…" appears in four notes as a closing line.

## 5. Holes and stale trackers

- **Placement.** Seinbrun ("a day's argument"), Naenor, Raitin, Natai, Tasain, Larbril, and the Seeing-Ring give no direction or travel time a table can use, and Named Ground has no row for them.
- **Broken links.** Seven, listed in `mechanical-scan.txt`.
- **The Reveal Index** leaves out about 47 notes that carry a reveal tag, including [[A History of the Turning]] and [[The Library Pages]].
- **Resolved entries with leftovers.** C-01 (scaffolding), C-04 (house-name markers), and C-07 (Rothallo called a town) each still have hits in the notes they name.
- **Player cards point at a GM note.** All ten Condition cards, the Conditions hub, and Turning Tree say "share from [[The Premise]]". The Premise is `visibility: gm`, so a player copy has nowhere to send the reader.
- **Trackers.** The Roadmap spine row for Epic H says "next H.4". The Epic 2 and Epic 8 Progress lines say "Next: P2.1". The Build Plan says Epic H is the last open epic. The Build Plan's "Active next" section has grown to about 40 long entries, one of them over 1,500 words, though its header says it is not a second Roadmap. [[Conventions]] points at empty `00 - Core/Canon` and `Cosmology` folders. [[Condition Audit]] and [[Epic 2 Audit Guide]] still describe old branches and PRs. The two earlier audit notes have status lines that later work closed.
- **Names that sound alike in one place.** Brimaen, Breolnir, Breillai, and Brormei among the Maiethorn seats. Sorim and Sorel in Orentel. Valen and Valein in the six-pivot table.
- **One habit used by several powers.** A bread heel on the threshold (Seinbrun, Raitin, Rothallo). An empty bowl at the end of the table (Seinbrun, Raitin). A chair turned away (Orentel, Naenor, Lunbra). Each is fine alone. Together they make different powers read as one culture.

## What is strong and should be protected

- **[[The Premise]]'s population section** is the single census, shows its arithmetic, and says Unbound is inside Bound. All ten Condition cards and the hub match it exactly.
- **[[Conventions]]**, the Plain prose and Player-safe export sections. They are the rules this audit used.
- **[[A History of the Turning]]** and **[[The Library Pages]]** are the baseline voice. The six Library pages keep every lock, and each page's deliberate error is recorded rather than fixed.
- **The climate notes**, **[[How the Work Is Done]]**, and **[[At the Table]]** are plain and playable, and they keep UnTurned and Kept apart.
- **The four Kind rule blocks** (Kitsune, Selkie, Tengu, Yumboe) match [[Kind Heritage]]. Do not trim them.
- **The campaign kits** keep every lock and have clear stop lines.
- **The GM Truth notes** keep each secret off player text and agree on the one-Gift rule and the Protectors' harvest.
- **The S.1–S.3 seats**, Raillath, Denlad, Tunral, and the White Note House's "How you get a term" table are models of usable procedure.
- **The newer creature and ritual notes** ([[The Guest-Meal]], [[A Made Thing]], [[The Cart-Ox]], [[Kin at the Door]]) are in the target voice.
- **[[The Pourers]], [[The Walled Book]], and [[The Protectors]]** are never merged or confused in any note.

## Questions for the user (Story V.3)

1. **Reimaethe's gift.** Does a Pourer give five years at one time, as the Pourers note says, or a year at a time, as Reimaethe's note says? The proposal is five at one time, and Reimaethe has given once and is being asked again.
2. **Maiethorn.** Is any regional drift allowed, so that Maiethorn has somewhat more Given than the world mix? Or does every region match the world count? The proposal is "somewhat more Given than the world mix, and still about two in five hugged at the Tree".
3. **Sanbreo.** Which coast is Sanbreo on? The proposal is to follow [[The Known Map]].
4. **"C.Y."** Spell it as "Cut-year" in player text everywhere, or spell it out once per note?
5. **"Mouth".** Sweep every use in player text, including the in-world speech, or only the narrator's uses?
6. **The shares on player cards.** Give The Premise's population section a player-visible home (a short player note, or a player section on [[Conditions]]), or let the cards keep their numbers and drop the link?
7. **Repeated habits.** Vary the bread heel, the empty bowl, and the turned chair, or leave them as a shared custom across the mainland?
8. **The Build Plan.** Trim "Active next" to a few lines per epic and leave the history to the Roadmap?
9. **The world book.** After V.4, rebuild it, or leave it as it stands?

1. **Facts and locks.** C-10 to C-24.
2. **Leaks and `reveals` tags.** Section 2, and move Links lists that sit after the GM wall.
3. **Scaffolding.** Section 3: move it behind the GM wall or delete it.
4. **The old voice.** Section 4, by your answers to questions 4 and 5.
5. **Holes.** Named Ground placement rows, the broken links, the Reveal Index, the C-01, C-04, and C-07 leftovers, and the cards' link to The Premise.
6. **Trackers.** The Roadmap spine and "Next" lines, the Build Plan, Conventions' folder tree, and the old audit notes.
7. **The rest.** The remaining medium and low findings, one folder at a time.
8. **The world book.** Your decision.
