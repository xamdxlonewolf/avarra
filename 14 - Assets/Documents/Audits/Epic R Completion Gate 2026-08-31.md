# Epic R Completion Gate (2026-08-31)

> **What this is.** The six-box close of [[Roadmap#Epic R: Editorial repair and table readiness|Epic R]]. Source findings: [[Editorial Audit 2026-08-29]]. Stories R.1–R.13 are the work. This note is the map, the fresh scans, the table test, and the residuals that pass-two owns. **No canon engines were rebuilt. The world book was not touched.**

**Verdict:** Epic R **closes**. Every non-green audit finding maps to a completed story or a recorded decision. Protected strengths stand. Keystone confirmation is off the player body. Arithmetic, front-matter vocabulary, and Daggerheart terms pass. A table can create a PC, travel, meet a conflict, and face a danger without inventing procedure. Remaining production scaffolding in many player-facing *working* notes is a **known residual**, owned by [[#Pass-two residual P2.1|P2.1]], not a silent close.

## 5 · Table test

Walked without inventing procedure.

1. **Create a Kept PC.** [[At the Table]]: Kind → Community → class → Turning = Kept. Empty clock. No consolation Experience. Default legal person. **Create a Conditioned PC:** pick Given or Struck; take one card from [[Conditions]]; Unbound is not a start pick; Phoenix needs other-four consent; Infernis + Bound stacks.
2. **Travel to a city.** Watch asks name / last hearth / who stands ([[Law and Citizenship#How a traveler stays vouched]]). Distances on [[Named Ground#Travel times]]. Kumbaan crossing is a different grammar ([[The Sundering Isle]]).
3. **Meet an active conflict.** Seats have "At the table" headers. Example: [[Harrow's Green]] stone-moving day (Haelin / Tora). Capitals: Vaethod's send, Tesara's stamp, Rithim's withheld line. Faction fights: Watchers/Door-Keepers, Book-Hands/Holding Desk, Tithe-Infrastructure/Slide.
4. **Face a runnable danger.** Settlement "enters a scene" lines, or [[Dangers of the Turning]] + an adversary (Gap-Cat, Chart-Crew, Bed-Keeper, Spent-Week Walker, Colourless Host). Leaf-Fall edges: open the warden table; do not invent a third Tree-outcome.

Missing procedure that would have failed this test: Infernis + Bound. Written this gate.
