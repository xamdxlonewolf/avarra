# Findings: 00 - Core (Concept, Meta; Templates excluded)

Notes read: 9. `00 - Core.md`, `Concept/Build Plan.md`, `Concept/The Premise.md`, `Meta/Conventions.md`, `Meta/Flight in this World.md`, `Meta/The Wardens Ear.md`, `Meta/Epic 2 Audit Guide.md`, `Meta/Condition Audit.md` were read all the way through. `Meta/Roadmap.md` was read to the scope in the task: the spine table, every epic Status line, every unchecked box, the Progress section against a box count per epic and per story, the Links section, and every "next" claim. The recorded-decision blockquotes of finished stories were not voice-audited.

Every note in the group is `visibility: gm`, so no finding here is a player-facing leak. Box count check: every epic's checked boxes match its Progress tally (R 97/97, P2 15/15, 0 7/7, 1 16/16, 2 16/16, 3 5/5, 4 11/11, 5 22/22, 6 27/27, 7 20/20, 8 5/5, 9 4/4, 10 9/9, A 65/65, L 50/50, S 21/21, M 42/42, B 26/26, C 13/13, H 35/35). Epic V has 1 of 13 boxes checked and no Progress line. The twelve unchecked boxes (V.1 ×8, V.2 ×3, V.3 ×1) are real open work, not stale.

## Patterns
- Stale "next" and "not opened" lines from earlier passes were left behind when later work finished (about 12 lines). Examples: Roadmap spine row H says "next H.4"; Roadmap Epic 8 Status and the Epic 2 and Epic 8 Progress lines say "Next: Pass two · P2.1"; Build Plan says "Epic H is the last open epic" after Epic V was opened.
- Epic V is missing from the places a fresh agent reads first (3 places). It has no row in the Roadmap spine table, no line under Roadmap Progress, and no paragraph in Build Plan "Active next".
- Finished audit notes still use the retired word "Locked" and still describe old branch and PR state (about 15 lines across 2 notes). Examples: `Condition Audit.md` (the quick-reference table says "Locked." ten times) and `Epic 2 Audit Guide.md` (draft PR #7, an unpushed local master, and "Story 2.3 not yet built").
- The Build Plan has grown into a second Roadmap. Its "Active next" section is about 40 paragraph-length entries, and one entry (line 39, Epic S) runs over 1,500 words. The note's own header says it is "not a second Roadmap". Insider shorthand sits inside it ("dated shelf", "mouth", "XOR", "Care-fortress", "Leftovers 1 / 3 / 7 / 9").
- The hub notes describe the vault folders in ways that no longer match it (3 notes). Examples: `Conventions.md` says canon lives in `00 - Core/Canon, Cosmology`, but those folders are empty; its folder tree leaves out `Concept`; `00 - Core.md` lists only half the Meta notes.

## Strong — protect
- The population section in `The Premise.md` (lines 49–79) is the single census, shows its arithmetic, and says outright that Unbound is inside Bound's share. Other notes in this group quote it rather than keep their own table.
- The **Plain prose** and **Player-safe export** sections of `Conventions.md` are clear, ordered, and give examples of the right and wrong sentence. They are the best part of the group.
- The Roadmap tallies are honest. Every Progress count matches the boxes actually checked.
- The Build Plan's **Locked**, **Rejected**, and **Do not** lists agree with every locked decision in the brief (Rothallo inside Orenbren, no sixteenth power, Kumbaan never grafted, the cutter unnamed, the Tree undated, the limit open, the founder unnamed, the three groups kept separate).
- The "Left where it is" section of `The Wardens Ear.md` names the real holes plainly and says they wait for a later ask.
