# Findings: Group 2 — World, Magic, Items, Game

Notes read: 49 (all notes under `01 - World/`, `06 - Magic/`, `10 - Items/`, `13 - Game/`; none left unfinished). Condition cards in `09 - Creatures/Conditions/` were read to check the rules text in `13 - Game`.

## Patterns
- **Old continent notes still carry design scaffolding in the player body (6 notes).** These are canon-status blocks with 🟡/🔒 tags, "Built with `systemic-worldbuilding` for Epic 3", a "fresh angle" essay paragraph, "named-stubs", and Roadmap or Story numbers in the Links. Examples: `Maiethorn.md`, `Strandoren.md`, `Heskoren.md`, `The Sundering Isle.md`, `The Reckoning of the Year.md`, `The World Frame.md`. Later notes in the same folder (the climate notes, `Planes.md`, the phenomena) do not have this problem.
- **Player bodies point at the keystone (about 10 places).** Examples include "see GM Notes", "keystone edge", "quiet keystone tell", "a scholar-PC's thread straight toward the keystone", and "the keystone's firing pin". Two of them say outright that distance is not the real cause of thin reach. Found in `Heskoren.md`, `The World Frame.md`, `Maiethorn.md`, `The Reckoning of the Year.md`, `The Sundering Isle.md`, and `10 - Items.md`.
- **The Kept are miscounted or misdefined (6 places).** `Maiethorn.md`, `The World Frame.md`, and `The Reckoning of the Year.md` say nearly every Maiethorn child is Given and that a hug is rare. That is the Walled Book's false lesson, now stated as fact. `Heskoren.md` and `Named Ground.md` treat a child who missed the Tree as Kept, but `Turning Tree` and `At the Table` call that child UnTurned.
- **The "near-Treeless" hedge on Kumbaan was never removed (2 notes, 4 lines).** `The World Frame.md` and `The Sundering Isle.md` still say "near-Treeless" and "no healthy Trees". `Climate of Kumbaan.md` and C-06 both say player geography now says "no Tree".
- **The newer Items notes talk about the vault instead of the world (5 notes).** They use lines like "already says so", "stays unwritten", "does not finish that sentence", and "the playable version". Examples: `The Other Chip.md`, `The Ribboned Knife.md`, `The One Knife.md`. The fire and leaf objects use insider pictures instead of facts ("answers two mouths", "need one Phoenix in the sentence", "the queue to stop being called weather"): `The Closed Lamp.md`, `The Spent Leaf.md`.
- **Two habits from the banned list recur (about 12 places): calling a person a "mouth", and the label "playable square".** Examples: `Named Ground.md`, `Dangers of the Turning.md`, `A Hidden Phoenix.md`, `The Sundering Isle.md`, `The Known Map.md`, `Ecology of the Turning.md`.

## Strong — protect
- `Climate of the Turning.md` and the four regional climate notes are plain and playable: weekly weather tables, a d6 weather turn, and Tithe-by-weather tables that match the cards. Each "The one thing the weather does not explain" section says what people see and that nobody settles it. That is the unexplained-habit rule done right.
- `How the Work Is Done.md` maps the nine SRD domains to jobs people pay for. It says the Tithe ticks only from the Condition card, and that a Spoken colour is not a spell and Hope cannot buy one. It is consistent with the cards and with `At the Table`.
- `At the Table.md` keeps UnTurned and Kept apart, matches the Conditions hub on long-rest clocks and on cards not levelling, and says the home note wins when they disagree.
- The single-purpose pointer notes (`Schools`, `Systems`, `Traditions`, `Artifacts`, `Weapons`, `Armor`, `Equipment`, `Materials`, `Rules`, `Encounters`, `Daggerheart`) and `What a Place Needed.md` are short. Each records its decision in a sentence, and the GM wall says what not to add.
