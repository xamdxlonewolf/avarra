# Findings: Group 5 — 04 - Settlements

Notes read: 34 (all finished). Cities 10, Towns 8, Villages 6, Sites 5, Regions 2, Continents 1, Settlement Seeds, section MOC.

## Patterns
- **Designer template labels in player bodies.** "Leftover job on this street" appears in 21 notes. "Clock 1 / Clock 2" paragraphs appear in 10. "No Epic-8 pivot/cast" appears in 4. These labels come from `Settlement Seeds`, and a reader who has not opened that note cannot decode them. Some of the lines that follow the label are plain. Others hide a fact. Examples: `Rothallo.md` ("This wall keeps a rank."), `Natai.md` ("The job that sits here is the blame."), `Sanbreo.md` ("The job that stayed is a line on a wall.").
- **People called "mouths."** Nearly every note uses "one mouth / two mouths / three mouths" in the People section or the At-the-table rows, about 26 notes in all. The worst cases are `The White Note House.md` ("the writing-mouth"), `Rothallo.md` ("the Book's mouths"), and `Nelath.md`, `Ornsael.md`, and `The Mill-hold.md` ("Conservative mouth").
- **Design instructions left in player text.** 14 notes give the GM orders in the player body. Examples: "Do not make all three the town's theme" (`Orentel.md`, `Eolvaeth.md`, `Nelath.md`), "Do not explain the cup" (`The Three Hamlets Past the Ford.md`), "That blindness is load-bearing" (`The First Bowl.md`), "Use that.", "Do not put the number in a player-facing sentence as if it were geology" (`Maiethlir.md`).
- **Design-essay codas after in-world texts.** The "X cannot afford to see that…" paragraphs appear in 12 notes. They analyse injustice in abstract terms ("the only refusal the roll still has to metabolize") instead of saying plainly what the page shows. Examples: `Maiethlir.md`, `Orentel.md`, `The Low Wall.md`.
- **Placement holes for the newer seats.** Several seats give no direction or travel time a table can use, and `Named Ground` has no row for them. The affected seats are Seinbrun ("a day's argument"), Naenor, Raitin, Natai, Tasain, Larbril, and the Seeing-Ring.
- **Habits reuse one template across powers.** Examples: a bread heel on the threshold, sill, or wall-stone (Seinbrun, Raitin, Rothallo); an empty bowl or cup at the end of the table (Seinbrun, Raitin); a chair turned away (Orentel, Naenor, Lunbra). Each habit is fine alone. Together they make different powers read as one culture.

## Strong — protect
- Most distances in this group agree with `Named Ground`. Rothallo is one day from the wood. Thaeloren is three days from the Third Hearth, Brenthael one more, Raillath one more, and the Mill-hold one more. Nelath is two days to the junction plus one day on the spur. Votaer is 8–12 days inland and 3–5 days' sail. Lunbra is 4–8 days up the Chart-run. Braetu is 8–14 days coasting, then 2–4 weeks over the West Water. Denlad, Tunral, and the First Bowl also match. Every seat sits in the power that `Powers of the Turning` gives it, Rothallo stays inside Orenbren, and Kumbaan has no graft. Do not move any of these.
- The S.1–S.3 seats (`Votaer.md`, `Braetu.md`, `Lunbra.md`, `Larbril.md`, `Seinbrun.md`) have plain "Ways in" and water/food/fuel blocks. Their "will not say why" habits follow the Conventions rule exactly.
- `Raillath.md`, `Denlad.md`, and `Tunral.md` are short, plain villages that each do one job a table can use. `The White Note House.md` has a "How you get a term" table that is a model of usable procedure.
- `Nelath.md` and `The Mill-hold.md` have clear site sections that tie physical causes to the crisis (a sound Hand at a stopped spur; a choked culvert against a full mill-race).
