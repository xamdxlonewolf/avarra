# Findings: Group 3 — 02 - History and 11 - Secrets

Notes read: 29 (all finished). 02 - History: 17 notes. 11 - Secrets: 12 notes.

## Patterns
- Writer's instructions sit in player bodies as "Do not…" lines and lock labels. The Walking Years has 10, The Years of Hands 12, and The First Cut 11. The Ages of the Turning, The Other Count, and Before the Walk have the same habit ("Do not slide it", "What this epic does not lock", "Liturgical 🔒"). Contradictions entry C-01 says this was cleaned up, but it was not.
- Every era, event, and timeline note except A History of the Turning still uses the old in-world-document scaffolding in the player body. Each has a "`oblique-worldbuilding`" tag line and a "X cannot afford to see…" gloss, 14 notes in all. The First Cut has six "cannot afford to see" lines (five are "What this mouth cannot afford to see"). The Closing, The Hinge Hush, and The Two Papers have two each.
- People are called "mouths", and insider shorthand stands in for facts. The Years of Hands uses "mouth(s)" 9 times and The First Cut 7. Other shorthand: "the lintel" for the Closed Seat's gate (The Closing 4, The Walking Years 2, The Years of Hands 2), "seen seeing" and "the one seeing" (The Closing, The First Cut, The Other Count), "the sitting", "Clock 1/Clock 2" (The Years of Hands 9, The Ages of the Turning 6, The First Cut 5), and "step around a fade".
- Three history player bodies take the Leaf-Mother as real. They speak of "her reach" or "her limit" as fact: The Ages of the Turning (lines 17, 90, 117), The Other Count (line 76), and The Walking Years (line 26).
- Production text survives export because it sits under `## Links`, after the GM wall. The Walking Years, The Years of Hands, and Before the Walk keep 🟡 markers and story numbers there. Four event notes end with "[[Roadmap]] (Story R.9)".
- The Secrets indexes have fallen behind the vault. The Reveal Index leaves out about 47 notes that carry a reveal tag, including both Epic H history notes. Three "resolved" Contradictions entries (C-01, C-04, C-07) still have leftovers in the notes they name.

## Strong — protect
- `02 - History/A History of the Turning.md` is the baseline voice: ordinary sentences, who did what, and the dates match the source notes. Its GM Notes keep the founder unnamed and the Unspent's age off the player page. Do not roughen it.
- `02 - History/The Library Pages.md` says for each page where it is kept, who works there, what that person says, and what a reader is meant to understand. It also states plainly where each page is wrong. Its GM list "Three truths no page reveals" is exactly the right wall.
- `11 - Secrets/Mysteries/A Buyer for the Knife.md` and `The Name-Stone Bed.md` are short and playable, with a clear "Where to stop". Keep that shape.
- The GM Truth notes (`Is the Leaf-Mother Real`, `The Other Hands`, `When the Fire Is Caught`) keep each secret off player text and record what confirmation does not include. The one-Gift rule and the Protectors' harvest stay GM-only and are consistent across all three.
