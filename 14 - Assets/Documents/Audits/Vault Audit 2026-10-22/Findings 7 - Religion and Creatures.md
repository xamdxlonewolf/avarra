# Findings: Group 7 — 07 - Religion and 09 - Creatures

Notes read: 43 (all read to the end; none unfinished)

## Patterns
- **Designer scaffolding left in faith player bodies (about 25 hits).** "Built with `belief-systems` for Story 1.4", "Canon status 🔒/🟡" blockquotes, "Full faction (Epic 5, Story 5.2). Names 🟡", skill names in backticks, and "(Story 5.2)" link tags sit outside GM Notes. Examples: `07 - Religion/Faiths/The Watching.md`, `07 - Religion/Faiths/The Old Ways.md`, `07 - Religion/Faiths/Faiths of the Turning.md`.
- **Player text that tells the reader a hidden truth exists (12 hits).** The narrator says "keystone", "her household", "the GM keystone truth this faith points at", or links `[[The Other Hands]] (GM)` in a Links list that survives export. No player body says outright that the Leaf-Mother is real, but several say there is a GM answer, and one names her household. Examples: `07 - Religion/Faiths/The Open Table.md`, `07 - Religion/Faiths/The Leaf-Mother.md`, `07 - Religion/The Uncoloured Intake.md`.
- **Old-voice shorthand in player text (about 20 hits).** People are called "mouths". "Lintel", "sitting", "a send", "of a door", "a raw throat", and "before the town uses a rope" stand in for plain facts. Examples: `07 - Religion/Faiths/The Open Table.md`, `09 - Creatures/Conditions/Far-Voiced.md`, `07 - Religion/Rituals/When the Town Buries.md`.
- **Player cards send readers to a GM-only note (all ten cards, the hub, and Turning Tree).** "Share from [[The Premise]]" and "The engine lives on [[The Premise]]" point at a `visibility: gm` note, so an exported player copy has no source for the shares or the engine. Examples: `09 - Creatures/Conditions/Conditions.md`, `09 - Creatures/Conditions/The Answered.md`, `07 - Religion/Turning Tree.md`.
- **Condition cross-references that misstate another card (5 hits).** Link captions call a Condition "the other" of a kind when there are three or four, give Two-Bodied a Struck path, or call a finished Returned "Kept". Examples: `09 - Creatures/Conditions/Far-Voiced.md`, `09 - Creatures/Conditions/Returned.md`, `09 - Creatures/Conditions/The Stilled.md`.
- **The design dial written into player prose (6 hits).** "~5%-scary", "the ~5% dial holds", and "keeps the setting ~5%-scary" appear outside GM Notes. Examples: `07 - Religion/Faiths/The Leaf-Mother.md`, `09 - Creatures/Conditions/Two-Bodied.md`, `09 - Creatures/Conditions/Conditions.md`.

## Strong — protect
- **Shares on all ten Condition cards and the Conditions hub match The Premise exactly.** That covers 2.5, 18, 10, <0.1, 7, 5 (3.5 + 1.5), 12 (11/1), 7 (6/1), and 13 (12/1). The "eleven parts in twelve", "six in seven", and "twelve in thirteen" splits on the Both-path cards are correct. The leaf colours on every card match the Turning Tree palette. No card mechanic contradicts another card or the hub, and no Tithe seizes a PC.
- **The four paradox-fables are clean on leak, contradiction, and lock.** No cutter is named, no Tree date is given, and the Leaf-Mother is not confirmed. The GM walls forbid the closing line. Leave their voice alone.
- **`07 - Religion/Rituals/The Guest-Meal.md`, `09 - Creatures/Constructs/A Made Thing.md`, `09 - Creatures/Beasts/The Cart-Ox.md`, and `09 - Creatures/Spirits/Kin at the Door.md` are in the target voice.** They say who does what in ordinary sentences, and unexplained habits are left unexplained ("They will not say why."). Use them as models.
- **The Unspent, The Pourers, and Long-Lived agree on the Pourers.** All three say members are asked once, give five years, and get their papers back when they leave. The founder is not named, and "older than the Pourers" stays under GM Notes. Keep that agreement when fixing the leak below.
