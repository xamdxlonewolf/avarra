# Kind and Condition

Every person in the Turning carries two layers, and both are true at once.

You are born with the shape of a body and a bloodline. That is **Kind**. Human, Elf, Drakona, Kitsune, Selkie, Tengu, and the rest. A Kind is ordinary. Nobody blinks at it. You arrived in that ancestry.

**Condition** is what you become. Something happens, and a vampire, a werewolf, or their kin is changed. That is a becoming. Roughly three in four people acquire a Condition over a lifetime. One in four never do. People manage a Condition through law, guild, medicine, and work attached to it. Society is built around what people with a Condition can do, and around helping them live with the cost.

## How a becoming arrives

There are two paths, and which path a person took is the main division in society.

Children gather with their families around a **Turning Tree** for the **Leaf-Fall**. That is a child's tenth-year solstice. A living cutting of the origin Tree stands in every town. That is a **scion**. Leaves fall and wrap around each ten-year-old. A colour falls, and a Condition is **Given**; the leaf-colour names which one. It is ceremonial, expected, proper. A Given child grows up already knowing what they will become, and is trained and guilded for it. No colour, but the leaves hug the child. That child is one of **the Kept**. The Tree keeps them as they are. That hug is a blessing in its own right. The Kept are wanted and special.

Later, some people are **Struck**. Some Conditions cannot be Given to a child. A ten-year-old cannot die and walk back. A child cannot consent to a contract. These arrive only when a person crosses a threshold later in life, and the threshold they cross decides what they become. No leaf, no ceremony. Onlookers call it tragic, miraculous, or a little suspect. Some of the Kept are Struck later.

The sorting is simple. Conditions that need no act from the child are Given only: the Long-Lived (vampire), the Two-Bodied (werewolf), the Answered (djinn), and the Phoenix. Things a child physically cannot have done are Struck only: the Returned (death), the Bound (a sworn pact). A near-death a person can survive can be foreseen by the Tree or walked into by accident, and those Conditions come both ways: the Far-Voiced (siren), the Stilled (gorgon), and the Taken-In (leshy). A Given Far-Voiced (blue leaf at ten, guild-trained) and a Struck Far-Voiced (drowned at thirty) are the same becoming and very different people.

Of the whole population, over a lifetime: about **60 percent Given** at the Tree, about **15 percent Struck** later, about **25 percent Kept for life**. The Given and the Struck together are the three-quarters who carry a Condition. At the ceremony itself, about 40 percent of children receive no colour; about a third of those will still be Struck someday.

## The Tithe

Every Condition carries a **Tithe**. You meet that upkeep to live well with the becoming. You pay it to stay whole. Meet it, and you are a valued citizen. Neighbours, colleagues, and family treat you as one. Fall behind, and a small, real edge shows: hunger, restlessness, a Cry, a limb locking stone-still, an element that lets go in a crowded room. The infrastructure that helps people pay their Tithes is part of why society organizes around people with a Condition.

A Tithe is satisfied by ordinary work and rest. A person can walk toward the dangerous edge, or circumstances can press them toward it. It does not seize the will. A person with a Condition remains themselves. The cost is paid in body, memory, Stress, standing, and time, never in stolen control.

Standing differs from one Condition to another. Some Conditions are common and ordinary. Some are rare and looked up to. Some are rare and looked down on. Some are feared. One is legendary.

## Nine to choose, ten in the world

Nine Conditions can be Given or Struck. The tenth is **the Unbound**. Common speech calls it the Hollow. It comes later. A Bound enters it when a term-limited contract ends without another being signed. You become Unbound only through Bound.

The world uses **the Given-Over** as a title for a Bound who signs contract after contract until the being on the other side effectively holds them. The contract-guilds call it **the Forfeit**. A player character does not take this fate.

### Rules

Conditions are Daggerheart **Transformation cards**. Each card has a flavor line and features, at minimum a boon and a labeled Tithe, hooked to Hope, Stress, Hit Points, Fear, and Experiences. One transformation per player character. It sits in the loadout like a heritage card and does not count against domain-card limits. Feature count varies. Conditions that take the table's focus, and the complicated ones, carry more features than the common cards. What is constant is a clean boon and a labeled Tithe on every card.
