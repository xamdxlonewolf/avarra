# Kind and Condition

Every person in the Turning carries two layers, and they stack.

**Kind** is what you are born: the shape of a body and a bloodline. Human, Elf, Drakona, Kitsune, Selkie, Tengu, and the rest. Kind is mundane. Nobody blinks at it. It is simply the ancestry you arrived in.

**Condition** is what you become. Vampire, werewolf, and their kin are not species in this world. They are becomings: something happens, and a person is changed. Roughly three in four people acquire a Condition over a lifetime. One in four never do. A Condition is a managed state with law, guild, medicine, and work attached to it. Society is built around what the Conditioned can do, and around helping them live with the cost.

## How a becoming arrives

There are two paths, and the split is the central social fault line of the world.

**Given**, at Leaf-Fall. In their tenth year, on the solstice, children gather with their families around a Turning Tree. A scion (a living cutting of the origin Tree) stands in every town. Leaves fall and wrap around each ten-year-old. A colour falls, and a Condition is Given; the leaf-colour names which one. It is ceremonial, expected, proper. A Given child grows up already knowing what they will become, and is trained and guilded for it. No colour, but the leaves hug the child: they are one of **the Kept**. The Tree keeps them as they are. That hug is a blessing in its own right. The Kept are wanted and special, not a failure and not a lack.

**Struck**, later. Some Conditions cannot be Given to a child. A ten-year-old cannot die and walk back. A child cannot consent to a contract. These arrive only when a person crosses a threshold later in life: the door you go through decides what you become. No leaf, no ceremony. Onlookers call it tragic, miraculous, or a little suspect. The Kept are not a closed club. A slice of them still turn.

The sorting is simple. Innate fates are Given only: the Long-Lived, the Two-Bodied, the Answered, the Phoenix. Things a child physically cannot have done are Struck only: the Returned (death), the Bound (a sworn pact). Survivable near-death doors can be foreseen by the Tree or walked through by accident, and those Conditions come both ways: the Far-Voiced, the Stilled, the Taken-In. A Given Far-Voiced (blue leaf at ten, guild-trained) and a Struck Far-Voiced (drowned at thirty) are the same becoming and very different people.

Of the whole population, over a lifetime: about **60 percent Given** at the Tree, about **15 percent Struck** later, about **25 percent Kept for life**. The Given and the Struck together are the three-quarters who carry a Condition. At the ceremony itself, about 40 percent of children receive no colour; about a third of those will still be Struck someday.

## The Tithe

Every Condition carries a **Tithe**: an upkeep that must be met to live well with the becoming. This is not a tax paid to a temple. It is the cost of staying whole. Meet it, and you are a valued citizen: neighbour, colleague, family. Fall behind, and a small, real edge shows: hunger, restlessness, a Cry, a limb locking stone-still, an element that lets go in a crowded room. The infrastructure that helps people pay their Tithes is part of why society organizes around the Conditioned.

A Tithe is satisfied by ordinary work and rest. The dangerous edge is something a person can walk toward, or something a story can press. It does not seize the will. A Conditioned person remains themselves. The cost is paid in body, memory, Stress, standing, and time, never in stolen control.

Standing is not one thing. Some Conditions are common and ordinary. Some are rare and looked up to. Some are rare and looked down on. Some are feared. One is legendary.

## Nine to choose, ten in the world

Nine Conditions can be Given or Struck. A tenth, **the Unbound**, called the Hollow in the common tongue, is not a starting becoming. It is the downstream state a Bound enters when a term-limited contract ends without another being signed. You become Unbound only through Bound.

**The Given-Over** is a title, not a Condition. It is the world's name for a Bound who signs contract after contract until the being on the other side effectively holds them. Among the contract-guilds the term of art is **the Forfeit**. It is an NPC-tier fate.

### Rules

Conditions are Daggerheart **Transformation cards**: a flavor line and features, at minimum a boon and a labeled Tithe, hooked to Hope, Stress, Hit Points, Fear, and Experiences. One transformation per player character. It sits in the loadout like a heritage card and does not count against domain-card limits. Feature count varies; spotlight and complex Conditions carry more than the workhorse cards. What is constant is a clean boon and a labeled Tithe on every card.
