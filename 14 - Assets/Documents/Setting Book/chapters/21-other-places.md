# Other Places

Places the world already implied, now seated: three pre-Walk leftovers a party can enter, a Kumbaan hall a session can sit down in, and three squares the road already needed. The twelve seats are in The Fifteen Powers and the Gazetteer. Do not make Harrow's Green or Ornsael a capital to tidy the map. Do not capture the First Seat. One type is still only a type.

## The Low Wall

Orenbren lodging-country, first days from the Motherwood, not Rothallo. People still say the Inner Close for that city. This fold is not it. Shoulder-high stone; three gaps used as gates; a blank name-stone; a garden-end against an inn's back wall. Different quarry from Rothallo. Same wall is a claim, not a fact. The site is found, not founded, and undated.

**Enter** by the cart-gap, the person-gap Rothallo still assigns labour walks through, the socket-gap an Old Ways first-meal uses, a crawl under the name-stone, or the garden-end loft.

**Pressure.** The Book wants a chip that files as Rothallo's quarry. Watchers block a chisel. The lodging-guild wants climbers off before Eolthael.

**Discovery.** A wrong quarry chip, still under the fill put down in Hale-month of C.Y. 381. The chip is the other quarry. It has no year on it. It does not match Rothallo's stone. A pot-ring. A knife-ribbon left in error: a Near-Mile kitchen knife on a ribbon marks a Closing summer in a house, not this wall. No date for the wall. No date for the Tree. No first enemy. No solve.

**Actors.** Delamem of the Close; Thuda at the inn; a Watcher at the panel; an Orenbren guild runner.

## The Seeing-Ring

A Watching hill on Maiethorn, not the grove. Uncounted ring below; later shrine-work above, roof removed. A ring-walk clockwise; four out-looks: origin smear, Old Crossing weather, two facing nothing. Found, not founded. Undated.

**Enter** by the hill path, mismatched shrine steps, an under-ring crawl, or Tithsael's night.

**Pressure.** A clerk with a chisel. A warden who wants the roof back. In Eolthael someone ties a cloth ribbon on the lintel-socket. By Leddorn it is gone. Tithsael takes a colour-ribbon off. A hug-ribbon stays. The cloth does not Speak. They will not say why the ribbon was a colour.

**Discovery.** A dual pecked mark, a clay pot-ring. No confirmation she stood here. No Closed Seat gate. The ribbon does not explain the two gaps that face nothing.

**Actor.** Tithsael keeps the night.

## The Dry Stair

Saelthael Rain-Shadow, a rise above a well-town that is **not** Ornsael. Worn steps to a door-stone and a dry basin (wrong pecking for a cistern); a warden's dead slip still in the upper basin. Found, not founded. Undated.

**Enter** by the flight (skip the broken step), the door-stone threshold, or a crawl to a second dry basin.

**Pressure.** The warden wants another pot. A clerk wants riser-scratches. A missed child needs overnight at the slab.

**Discovery.** A salt-white ring, mineral or brush-ash. Not her first Hand. Not a Fox temple. A later warden planted a slip in the upper basin. The slip died. What is left is a thumb of dead wood in a crack at the lip. It does not green. It is not a scion. People say the stair refused a mother. The rock was dry, and the slip died of that.

Pair with Ornsael: a water crisis in a living well-town versus a climb that Gives nothing.

## Ndenjoo

A hill-hall on Kumbaan. Own-name *nden-JOH*; sailors say **the Feeding Hill**. One of the Paps: a green hill honeycombed underneath, pasture and standing-stones on the turf, the life below. The valley comes here on the bright nights. There is no Tree. There is a table.

**Njunda** opens it because it was their grandmother's, and because it still fits the crowd. Reputation, not office. **Soonke** sits the storm-beach below. They do not swim out. A living wreck they walk uphill. First night as guest starts at the hall, not on the sand. **Saalo** of the valley wants a crossing *out*, to find Waandi, who left two moons ago. **Nolas** of a Netstrand hull sat twice, kin inside the hall, and will not go back to a ship.

There is no leftover job of the walk. Kumbaan is off the clocks. The leftover is the wall: people who arrived and did not leave; people who want to leave and cannot book a thinning.

Life is under the hill. You walk into a Pap and come out at a board. The extra place is set slightly back, every meal that matters. To omit it is the one impiety. Daylight is a Sabbath of sleep. A mainlander loud at noon is a problem the hall can feel in its teeth. On one night of the moon sailors call Yoltal, the heel of the loaf is eaten first. Ndenjoo will not say why. A guest who asks is given more bread.

**Crossing, short enough to start.** You do not book Ndenjoo. You are on the sand or you are not. Soonke decides *alive*. Njunda decides *kin*. The second sitting is how you belong on the isle. It does not travel back to a mainland watch. A road-word still works on a Strandoren quay *before* you sail. Here the question is not asked. Sitters do not send anyone back.

Session wants: an extra place laid; Nolas gone as kin, not as a slight; a wreck off the hill; a word of Waandi. None of them are "prove what the Trees are." A warden guest is still a guest. There is no Tree to tend.

What can enter a scene: a wreck walked uphill tonight; the hands stop; noon-noise; a hull rumour.

*Said to the extra place before the hands arrive, a bright night. A second line added in Njunda's mouth after Nolas sat twice.*

> *We have counted you. Sit. If you have a name we have not learned it yet; if you have a colour we have no tree to read it.*
>
> *If you have sat twice, you are of the table. The table is not a ship. Sleep when the moon is down. The sand does not owe you a second hull.*

## The Mill-hold

The sick-Tree town. Everyday *the Mill-hold*; travellers who only saw the drain say *the culvert*. Orenbren lodging-country, two days past Brenthael on the Near Mile, six days out from the grove in the count the road already uses. Not Rothallo. Not Brenthael. A few hundred hearths around one mill-race and one canopy.

For a generation they Turned at home. This Liren the culvert that keeps spring-water off the roots choked. The mill-race stayed full. The Hand stood in it. Root blight is waterlogged soil, crowded roots, a drain someone assumed someone else cleared. It is this year. Unsound wood spends the week. The children walk in, toward Brenthael, because a warden who stands them under wood that cannot Turn has made them unTurned. Next summer is not a repair.

**Talen** *(TAH-len)* is town-warden. They will not stand a child under unsound wood, and they want the mill to still turn through Harvest. The same water is bread. **Milsun** *(MIL-sun)*, Taken-In, keeps the drain and a planting above the race. **Thurrei** *(thur-RAY)* is the Road-hand, here for the wood, not for the children. The neighbour-slate is chalked on the mill-door: this year's ten-year-olds, and which Brenthael mouth has room.

Mill-foam on the drain-lip keeps last Eolthael's colour a day longer than the water does. Milsun will not say why. Talen moves the foam with a stick. Thurrei wipes it off a pot-wrap and does not write it down.

A miller still takes a share of grain. The mill-share is not the mouth this square turns on. When the race is drawn down, they set the stones and leave one measure of last year's grain on the lip until the water comes back. They will not say why.

## The First Bowl

The guest-grove. An old green that kept host-rights before any leave. A young Hand stands in it now, cut from Harrow's Green after Harrow's took. Everyday *the First Bowl*. Folk hearth-name *Lonasir* *(lo-NAH-seer)*. The Seat still writes *a later planting in the Far Yield* and does not use the hearth-name.

Not Vaelhesk's capital. The land is the seat. There is no city here to mark. Not Vaelun. Not a second Harrow. Not a sick Tree. A day's walk, sometimes two, from Vaelun, away from Harrow's, deeper into the Yield. You do not pass Harrow's square to reach it unless you are carrying wood from there.

The Mother's wood is a guest. A Cutting-leave without the meal is a claiming. **Delvor** *(DEL-vor)* of Lonasir asks about the bowl before they ask about a leave. **Vilraet** *(vil-RAYT)* is town-warden of the guest and wants the week Spoken so a marriage can be witnessed here. **Brudu** *(BROO-doo)*, Taken-In, keeps the old planting and wants the morning water before the guest's pot. The question of whether this is Taken-In country is the slight.

The first-meal bread is always a little sour. Delvor says that is how you know the grove sat down. After the last person has gone in, a rye-straw is laid on the door-path's first stone and left until first light. The First Bowl will not say why.

## Nelath

The road-end. *(NEL-ath.)* Mile-folk say **the spur** and mean the road, then use the town's name once they are in it. A Hands-town in Orenbren lodging-country: one square at the end of a side-day of the Near Mile. The junction is a day short of the Third Hearth. From the junction the spur is one day, and then the canopy. You do not pass Thilim. You do not pass the culvert. Not Harrow's Green. Not Brenthael. Not the Mill-hold. Not Rothallo, and not the First Seat.

The main Mile still goes on. This track leaves it and does not rejoin. When Hands made the origin-walk optional, this town brought the next stone in. Beyond the boughs there is a scar: a ditch, thorns, and the width of a road that is no longer a road. There is no ford. There is no neighbour's canopy.

**Raetoth** *(RAY-toth)* is town-warden. They Speak the fall at home and want the stone left in the square and the scar left closed. **Lithsur** *(LITH-sur)* wants the stone back on the scar so a cut name is a day's mark again, and will bring a rope in Eolthael. The stone is not the day's drink. The cistern is the drink. A notch on the stone still holds a little rain after the square has dried. A name is cut into the lintel over the upper room. Nelath will not say why the notch stays wet, or whose name that is.

The road-mend stops at the boughs. They do not cut the thorns back to make the spur go on.
