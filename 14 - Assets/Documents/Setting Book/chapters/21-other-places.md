# Other Places

> **Not yet written.** This chapter is reserved for places the world has already implied but not yet seated: leftover settlement types with no named square, and the unnamed seats of the twelve stub powers. Do not make Harrow's Green or Ornsael a capital to tidy the map. Do not capture the First Seat.

## What belongs here

Leftover types listed in the Gazetteer that still have no named square:

- A **sick-Tree** town (civic crisis; neighbour's week as the whole year)
- A **guest-grove** (Old Ways door and a graft in the same green)
- A **lead road-end** (the road that used to go further, and now doesn't)

And, when a stub power needs a place rather than a name:

- Seats of the twelve (currently unnamed on purpose)
- White Note inland houses beyond Mataero's letting-slate
- Kumbaan places, when the Open Table wants ground rather than a table
