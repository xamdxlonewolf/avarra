# Playing in the Turning

How to sit down in this world. Fiction and law stay in their home chapters. This page is the order of operations.

**Tone.** Neighbours and colleagues. A real obstacle underneath. A Tithe never seizes the player character. About five percent scary.

## Making a character

Run this once, in this order. Do not invent a second engine.

1. **Kind.** Stock Daggerheart ancestry, one of the four customs (Kitsune, Selkie, Tengu, Yumboe), or Mixed Ancestry as the SRD allows. Rulings sit in *The Kinds*. A Yumboe needs the GM's leave and is always the full Kind.
2. **Community.** As the SRD. A Hearth-Mark is not a Community.
3. **Class, Experiences, equipment.** As the SRD. Two Experiences. A Kept character does not get a third as consolation.
4. **Turning.** Pick one, and write it on the sheet:
   - **Given** — a colour fell at ten. Take one Given-path or Both-path card from *The Conditions*.
   - **Kept** — the leaves hugged. No Condition card. See below.
   - **Struck** — a later door already happened in backstory. Take a Struck-path or Both-path card. You were vouched anew; say who stood.
   - **UnTurned** — you never stood. No hug, no colour. Not Kept. You can still be Struck later. Folk will call you Kept; the law should not.
5. **Special seats (whole table, not one player).**
   - **Phoenix** — spotlight Condition; the other players consent. Or run it as an NPC.
   - **Hidden Phoenix** — a different conversation. Book X.
   - **Yumboe** — GM leave. Full Kind only.
   - **the Unbound** — not a starting pick. You reach it through Bound, in play or arranged at session zero as a Bound whose term already ended.

Kind and Condition **stack**. A Selkie can be Given Two-Bodied. A stock Human can be Struck Returned. Mixed heritage Turns, or is Kept, or is Struck, like anyone else.

They stack even when the fiction rhymes. An **Infernis** who swears a contract is Bound like anyone else. The born blood is Kind; the contract is Condition. They do not merge, cancel, or become Given-Over by default.

### The Kept deal

**Deliberate tradeoff, not a consolation Condition.** The Kept have no boon and no Tithe. That is the card: the empty clock.

What they *do* have: they are the default legal person — witnessed, unguilded, unlicensed. At every long rest they take their downtime without a Tithe checklist. Everyone else may be ticking a clock. That free move is the table advantage. They can still be Struck later. Kept describes a present, not a promise.

What they do *not* get: a tenth Condition, a bonus Experience, a Hearth-Mark they did not already qualify for, or a "Kept feature" that is a Tithe in costume.

UnTurned lives look similar (no Tithe, no guild). Only the hug was witnessed. Do not file them as Kept to be kind.

### Struck in play

A Kept or UnTurned character can be Struck if the fiction crosses a door the roster already names: death (Returned), a sworn contract (Bound), a survivable drowning / gaze-edge / green-taking (Far-Voiced, the Stilled, the Taken-In). Given-only fates (Long-Lived, Two-Bodied, Answered, Phoenix) cannot be Struck.

**Consent.** The player agrees to take the card. The GM does not assign a becoming to punish a failed roll. A Tithe never seizes the character; becoming is not a lose-control event.

When it fires: add the Condition card; Kind stays; they are vouched anew at the next desk that will take them (hazardous doors go to the Intake); they were not Given; there is no leaf and no second Leaf-Fall; they do not stack a second Condition.

A Bound whose term ends becomes the Unbound per that card. That is the only downstream becoming.

### Advancement

Condition cards do not level. The card at 1 is the card at 10. Class features, domain cards, Experiences, weapon tier, Proficiency, and Hope scars change around it. Full ruling in *The Conditions*.

### Travel

You move on a **word**, not a paper-state. A watch asks **name, last hearth, who stands.** Two will do. None, and you sit until morning or you are walked to the next gate.

Customs that count: road-word, company-vouch, guild-mark, house-note, guest-right, walk-custom. Distances: *Climate, Ecology, and Travel*. Kumbaan is not on this grammar; crossing sits in *Other Places* under Ndenjoo and in *The Four Continents*. Sold vouching exists. It is not this custom.

### City witnessing

A village remembers a child. A city remembers a **hearth**. The legal sentence is "I am of the third quay," not "I am of Orentel."

Turning-Week is still **one week**. Stands are by hearth, in named slots. The warden Speaks colour or hug. They do not write a roll of gifts. Threnmaieth writes the stand down. That is the aberration.

### Crime

There is no crime of *being* a Condition. The Inviolate Will already sorts the charge: negligent Tithe-lapse versus willful weaponizing.

Ladder: **neighbour-word → watch (hold till morning) → guild-hearing → open hearing.** Exile from the hearth is the civic death. Tickets name a hazardous *use* (Gaze, Cry, element in a yard), not a Condition. Bound is a table, not a fourth body-licence.

### Leaf-Fall edge cases

The load-bearing sentences, for when you cannot open *The Turning Tree*:

- The Given-door is **one week** in the tenth year.
- A completed standing (colour or hug, sound wood) is **once**.
- A miss, refusal, or week spent under unsound wood makes a child **unTurned**, not Kept.
- Struck remains the later mercy.
- You may correct a Speaking. You may not rerun a fall.
- White-fire is sent to the First Seat. A town-warden does not guess Phoenix.

## Starting situations

One door each. Not a tour. The leftover job on the street is the hook.

**Eolvaeth.** A send-year. Vaethod will not invent a colour. Saelid's stall needs winter mouths. A gift-hall stranger with no list. The wait as destination.

**Orentel.** Empty Eolthael berths. A White Note term that is not a hull and not a hold. Tesara's shed that does not face the street. A fade two streets off the third quay.

**Maiethlir.** A thaw that is loud the week before Leaf-Fall. A Speaker who will not say the line. A recut chapel. A channel that can be shut.

**Harrow's Green.** Hale-month, C.Y. 387. The stone needs moving so the crowd can stand. Water still on it. A neighbour's week that can walk home. Extra mouths. A live front, not a capital.

**Ornsael.** The well is dropping. The share is chalked on the lip. Core pilgrims expecting a holiday. A wet knot below last year's water-line.

**the Three Hamlets.** Send, refuse, or take the other road. The canopy is a dark on the far slope. Same week as Harrow's.

**the Third Hearth.** Seine's bed is made. Brenthael wants the stone down. Mouths at dark. A name that is also a well.

**Ndenjoo.** A wreck walked uphill. The extra place already set. Noon-noise. No Tree.

## Tone at the table

Neighbours, colleagues, family. The Tithe is the edge. The Leaf-Fall is warm. Dread does not live in the Tree. A wrongness beat, when you need one, is weather, leftover hospitality, a week that already closed, or a person who missed the door — not an eerie Hand.

Do not stock a second vampire, a cursed wolf-kin, or a hungry dead that is not a Returned. The monsters are already people.

## Sample frames

Short shapes that use two clocks and do not treat the Years of Hands as post-history.

**A live Grafting town.** Harrow's Green, or the hamlets looking at its canopy. Necessity on the street. A pot that may or may not arrive. Someone still sending; someone who thinks the wave is over lives two weeks away and cannot see the square.

**A quay with a White Note.** Orentel. Prestige as a product that takes three desks (a Night-Shore hull, a White Note term, an Orentel hold). Necessity in the loft. Ledan asks which count you want.

**An origin-town under a roll.** Maiethlir. Extra mile layered, not replaced. A Speaker who will not copy. A Watcher who will describe. The First Seat in the wood beside, able to refuse.

**A hill-hall beyond the wall.** Ndenjoo. Hospitality as the rite. The storm-wall is the climate. Session wants are kin and wreck, not a proof.

A campaign opening that uses a remnant-walker, an inside helper, and an isolated white-fire fall is a GM kit. Book X.

## Dangers on the road

Settlements already have things that enter a scene (a cohort, a crane, a well-mouth). This is the country between those doors.

Start battle points from the SRD: `[(3 × PCs) + 2]`. Spend: Minion group 1 · Support/Social 1 · Horde/Ranged/Skulk/Standard 2 · Leader 3 · Bruiser 4 · Solo 5. Benchmarks: Tier 1 Difficulty 11, thresholds 7/12, ATK +1; Tier 2 Difficulty 14, 10/20, ATK +2.

| Name | Tier / type | Where it lives | What it wants |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gap-Cat** | T1 Skulk | Rain-Wall shelves, Heskoren ridge, mile-gaps | A quiet kill and the high rock back |
| **Chart-Crew** | T1 Horde | Chart-run, Night Shore, West Water wrecks | The hull that did not arrive — or yours |
| **Bed-Keeper** | T1 Leader | Empty road-houses on the Long Mile | The bed occupied. You staying. |
| **Spent-Week Walker** | T2 Standard | Long Mile, west-road, live-front tracks | To finish a week that already closed |
| **Colourless Host** | T2 Support | Dead pots, unsound grafts, waiting ground | To Give. It cannot. |

None of these are Conditions. A walker may *be* Conditioned the way anyone is.

**Mile-gap** (T1 Traversal, Difficulty 12). The day's walk between two stones. Impulses: stretch the day, hide the next stone, offer a false bed, make a Tithe expensive. Travel rolls to find the next shrine are Difficulty 12; failure with Fear ends the day in the gap.

**Thaw-gate** (T1 Traversal, Difficulty 13). Shelf-gate or Noon Pass in a thaw-week. Crossing without a guide is Difficulty 13. Failure: swept, or a cart lost — not both on one roll.

**Empty road-house** (T1 Exploration, Difficulty 11). A road-house that kept the upper room after the walk ended. Guest-right is a week. Leaving before morning without a word is a slight the next shrine will hear.

**Night-berth** (T1 Exploration, Difficulty 12). A Chart-run or West Water quay at first dark. Hulls that do not arrive are local weather.

Pairing: Long Mile inland — mile-gap and a Gap-Cat. Long Mile leftover — empty road-house and a Bed-Keeper. Rain-Wall — thaw-gate; weather is enough. Chart-run — night-berth and a Chart-Crew. Dead pot on the live front — a Colourless Host as Support, not a second Host. Near Mile — use the town's "enters a scene." Storm-wall — not this layer.

A Colourless Host is horticulture gone wrong, or a pot that never took. Skeptics and believers can both be loud. The book does not settle it.
