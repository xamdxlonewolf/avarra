# Playing in the Turning

Follow this page, in this order, when you make a character and when you start play. Stories and laws live in their own chapters. This page is the sequence at the table.

**Tone.** Play neighbours and colleagues. A real obstacle sits under the ordinary day. A Tithe never takes the player character over. Keep the frightening part rare.

## Making a character

Run this once, in this order.

1. **Kind.** Take a stock Daggerheart ancestry, one of the four ancestries written for this world (Kitsune, Selkie, Tengu, Yumboe), or Mixed Ancestry as the SRD allows. Rulings sit in *The Kinds*. A Yumboe needs the GM's leave, and the character is the full Kind.
2. **Community.** Take a Community as the SRD defines it. A Hearth-Mark records Kind. It does not fill the Community line.
3. **Class, Experiences, equipment.** Take these as the SRD defines them. Write two Experiences. A Kept character has those same two.
4. **Turning.** Pick one, and write it on the sheet:
   - **Given** — a colour fell at ten. Take one Given-path or Both-path card from *The Conditions*.
   - **Kept** — the leaves hugged. No Condition card. See below.
   - **Struck** — a later threshold already happened in the backstory. You died and came back, you swore a contract, or you survived a drowning, the edge of a gaze, or a taking by the green. Take a Struck-path or Both-path card. Someone stood for you again. Say who.
   - **UnTurned** — you never stood under a Turning Tree. No hug, no colour. You are not Kept. You can still be Struck later. Neighbours will call you Kept. Write the law as UnTurned.
5. **Choices for the whole table.**
   - **Phoenix** — this Condition takes the table's attention. Every other player agrees before anyone plays one. The GM may run a Phoenix as a person in the world instead.
   - **Hidden Phoenix** — have that talk with the GM apart from the table. It is not a choice you announce with the others.
   - **Yumboe** — the GM's leave. Full Kind only.
   - **the Unbound** — you do not start here. A Bound character becomes Unbound when the term ends, in play, or because the table agrees at the start that the term already ended.

Kind and Condition stay two things on one character. A Selkie can be Given Two-Bodied. A stock Human can be Struck Returned. Mixed heritage is Given, Kept, or Struck the same way anyone else is.

They stay two things when the stories sound alike. An **Infernis** who swears a contract is Bound like anyone else. The born blood is Kind. The contract is Condition. They do not merge, cancel, or become Given-Over by default.

### The Kept deal

The Kept have no boon and no Tithe. A Tithe clock is the upkeep a Condition card asks a character to mark, usually at a long rest. The Kept clock is empty on purpose. That emptiness is the tradeoff.

What they have is a legal standing and a free rest. The law treats them as the ordinary person: the hug was witnessed, they owe no guild, and they hold no Condition licence. At every long rest they take their downtime with no Tithe checklist. Everyone else may be marking a Tithe clock. That free rest is the advantage at the table. They can still be Struck later. Kept is the present. It is not a promise to stay that way.

They do not receive a tenth Condition, a bonus Experience, a Hearth-Mark they did not already qualify for, or a Kept ability of their own. An ability that is really a Tithe belongs on a Condition card.

An UnTurned life looks similar: no Tithe, no guild. Only the hug was witnessed. Write an UnTurned character as UnTurned.

### Struck in play

A Kept or UnTurned character can be Struck when the story crosses a threshold this book already names: death (Returned), a sworn contract (Bound), or a survivable drowning, the edge of a gaze, or a taking by the green (Far-Voiced, the Stilled, the Taken-In). Long-Lived, Two-Bodied, Answered, and Phoenix are Given only. Those four cannot be Struck.

The player agrees to take the card. The GM does not hand out a becoming to punish a failed roll. A Tithe never takes the character over. Becoming is not a moment where the player loses the character.

When the player takes the card, add the Condition. Kind stays. Someone stands for them again at the next desk that will take them. A hazardous becoming goes to the Intake. Mark them Struck. They were not Given. There is no leaf and no second Leaf-Fall. They do not take a second Condition.

A Bound character whose term ends becomes the Unbound, as that card says. No other Condition turns into a further Condition.

### Advancement

Condition cards do not level. The card at 1 is the card at 10. Class features, domain cards, Experiences, weapon tier, Proficiency, and Hope scars change around it. The full ruling is in *The Conditions*.

### Travel

You move on someone's word. A paper that names what you are does not replace the answer. A watch asks **name, last hearth, who stands.** Two of the three will do. If you have none, you sit until morning, or you are walked to the next gate.

These customs answer the watch: a road-word, a company's vouch, a guild-mark, a house-note, guest-right, and the custom of a walk. Distances are in *Climate, Ecology, and Travel*. Kumbaan, the Sundering Isle, does not use this road-word. The crossing is in *Other Places*, under Ndenjoo, and in *The Four Continents*. People do sell a vouch. That sale is a separate trade. It does not answer this watch.

### City witnessing

A village remembers a child. A city remembers a **hearth**. When the watch asks, the legal answer is "I am of the third quay."

Turning-Week is **one week** in a city too. People stand by hearth, in named slots. The warden says the colour or the hug aloud. They do not write a list of gifts. Threnmaieth writes the stand down. Other towns leave it spoken. Treat the writing as Threnmaieth's exception.

### Crime

There is no crime of *being* a Condition. The Inviolate Will already sorts the charge into a Tithe that lapsed through neglect, or a power used on purpose as a weapon.

The charge goes up this ladder: a neighbour's word, then the watch holds the person until morning, then a guild hearing, then an open hearing. Exile from the hearth is the penalty the town treats as civic death. A ticket names a hazardous use — the Gaze, the Cry, or an element let loose in a yard. It does not name the Condition. A Bound hearing sits at a table and covers the Terms of the contract. The three licences on the body stay the Stilled, the Far-Voiced, and the Answered. Bound is not a fourth.

### Leaf-Fall edge cases

Use these when you cannot open *The Turning Tree*:

- The Given-door is **one week** in the tenth year.
- A completed standing (colour or hug, sound wood) is **once**.
- A miss, a refusal, or a week spent under unsound wood makes a child **unTurned**. It does not make them Kept.
- Struck remains the later mercy.
- You may correct a Speaking. You may not rerun a fall.
- White-fire is sent to the First Seat. A town-warden does not name it Phoenix.

## Starting situations

Start in one of these places. Stay with the one job the old walk left on that street. After a town has its own Tree, that job is one of three. **Devotion:** people keep a walk they no longer need, either as an extra mile or as a wait at a Tree that may not speak. **Prestige:** a household pays for a winter, or a berth, for a walk they do not need. **Necessity:** the local Tree is missing, young, or sick, or the week cannot hold the mouths, so people use a neighbour's standing, water, or bed. Give the place one of those jobs. A guest may bring a second into the same week. The second is not the place's theme.

**Eolvaeth.** People come here to wait, and the wait is the job. Vaethod, the town-warden, will not invent a colour. Saelid's stall has to feed the winter mouths. A stranger in the gift-hall has no list to stand on. Some years the wood says send the children on. The gospel in the vale says stay.

**Orentel.** The quay sells a winter to households who can pay for a walk they do not need, and that sale is the job. The Eolthael berths are empty because they are held. A White Note term is the paper. A Night-Shore hull is the ship. An Orentel hold is the berth. A prestige winter takes all three, at three desks. Tesara works the stillers' shed, and the shed does not face the street. Two streets off the third quay, a person too poor to meet a Tithe fades while the city steps around them.

**Maiethlir.** Start the week before Leaf-Fall, when the thaw is loud. Rithim, the Speaker, will not give the clerk the line. The wardens have recut a chapel, and a Watching room is under it. The crown keeps a counted channel for a Far-Voiced hour and for the city's green. That channel can be shut. The extra mile is still walked here. The roll writes on top of that walk, and the walk remains.

**Harrow's Green.** Hale-month, Cut-year 387, the 387th summer counted from the First Cut. The job is a neighbour's week that can still be walked home. The stone has to move so the crowd can stand, and the water is still on it. The upper room has extra mouths. Children here Turn under this Tree. The hamlets can see the canopy and are still sending theirs.

**Ornsael.** The well is dropping, and the job is water. The share is chalked on the lip. Pilgrims from the core arrive expecting a holiday walk. A wet knot sits below last year's water-line.

**the Three Hamlets.** Play the same week as Harrow's Green. The canopy is a dark on the far slope. Brenod sends the child. Vaelun may refuse the walk. Ornath may take the other road. The job is the neighbour's week.

**the Third Hearth.** Thilim has made Seine's bed and left it made. The extra mile is optional, and the house still keeps the bed. That is the job. Meirim, Brenthael's warden, wants the stone taken down. Mouths will be here at dark. Seine's name is on the lintel. The stone at the road is the day's water.

**Ndenjoo.** Play the hall's hospitality, and play the storm-wall as the weather. A wreck has walked uphill. The extra place is already set. A guest is loud at noon, when the hall sleeps. There is no Tree. Seat the wreck, keep kin who will not leave, and let someone want a crossing out. Leave what the Trees are unproved.

## Tone at the table

Play neighbours, colleagues, and family. The Tithe is where the danger sits. Leaf-Fall is a warm week. Keep dread out of the Tree. When you need one moment of wrongness, use the weather, a host still keeping a guest from a week that ended, a week that already closed, or a person who missed the standing.

The Conditions are already the people in the world. Do not add a second vampire, a cursed wolf-kin, or a hungry dead who is not a Returned.

## Sample frames

Use one of these for a run of sessions. In each mainland frame, some people Turn under the Tree in town, and this year someone is still walking because living wood has not reached every hearth. Play both as the present. The Years of Hands are the years a town Tree is the ordinary way to Turn. They are underway.

**A town where the planting is still arriving.** Harrow's Green, or the hamlets looking at its canopy. The job on the street is a neighbour's week. A pot of living wood may arrive, and it may not. Someone here is still sending this year's children. Someone who thinks the planting is finished lives two weeks away and cannot see this canopy.

**A quay that sells a winter.** Orentel. A household that wants the prestige of a First-Hand year goes to three desks: a Night-Shore hull, a term from the White Note, and a hold at Orentel. One desk does not finish the winter. Necessity still sleeps in the loft. Ledan, at the White Note desk, asks which count the debt should wear: the house's own year, a Cut-year, or a third date a court invented. Say which.

**An origin town with a roll.** Maiethlir. The extra mile is still walked, and the clerks write on top of it. Rithim, the Speaker, will not copy the clerk's line. A Watcher will describe what they saw. The First Seat is in the wood beside the city, and can refuse what the city asks.

**The hall on the isle.** Ndenjoo. Hospitality is the rite: an extra place, a second night, a guest who can sleep the day. The storm-wall is the weather. Play kin and a wreck. Do not spend the session proving what the Trees are.

A campaign opening with a remnant-walker, a helper on the inside, and a white-fire fall kept apart from the town belongs to the GM. It is not one of the starts above.

## Dangers on the road

Settlements already have things that enter a scene: a cohort, a crane, a well-mouth. Use this section for the country between those towns.

Start battle points from the SRD: `[(3 × PCs) + 2]`. Spend: Minion group 1 · Support/Social 1 · Horde/Ranged/Skulk/Standard 2 · Leader 3 · Bruiser 4 · Solo 5. Benchmarks: Tier 1 Difficulty 11, thresholds 7/12, ATK +1; Tier 2 Difficulty 14, 10/20, ATK +2.

| Name | Tier / type | Where it lives | What it wants |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gap-Cat** | T1 Skulk | Rain-Wall shelves, Heskoren ridge, mile-gaps | A quiet kill and the high rock back |
| **Chart-Crew** | T1 Horde | Chart-run, Night Shore, West Water wrecks | The hull that did not arrive — or yours |
| **Bed-Keeper** | T1 Leader | Empty road-houses on the Long Mile | The bed occupied. You staying. |
| **Spent-Week Walker** | T2 Standard | Long Mile, west-road, live-front tracks | To finish a week that already closed |
| **Colourless Host** | T2 Support | Dead pots, unsound grafts, waiting ground | To Give. It cannot. |

None of these are Conditions. A walker may *be* Conditioned the way anyone is.

**Mile-gap** (T1 Traversal, Difficulty 12). The day's walk between two stones. The place will stretch the day, hide the next stone, offer a false bed, or make a Tithe expensive. Travel rolls to find the next shrine are Difficulty 12. Failure with Fear ends the day in the gap.

**Thaw-gate** (T1 Traversal, Difficulty 13). A shelf-gate or the Noon Pass in a thaw-week. Crossing without a guide is Difficulty 13. On a failure, someone is swept, or a cart is lost. One roll does not do both.

**Empty road-house** (T1 Exploration, Difficulty 11). A road-house that kept the upper room after the walk ended. Guest-right is a week. Leaving before morning without a word is a slight the next shrine will hear.

**Night-berth** (T1 Exploration, Difficulty 12). A Chart-run or West Water quay at first dark. Hulls that do not arrive are local weather.

Pair a place with one danger. On the Long Mile inland, use a mile-gap and a Gap-Cat. On the Long Mile where a house still keeps a bed from a week that ended, use an empty road-house and a Bed-Keeper. On the Rain-Wall, use a thaw-gate, and let the weather be enough. On the Chart-run, use a night-berth and a Chart-Crew. At a dead pot on the live front, use one Colourless Host as Support. Do not add a second Host. Near a town on the Near Mile, use what that town already sends into a scene. The storm-wall belongs with the isle, in *Other Places*. It is not one of these road dangers.

A Colourless Host is a planting gone wrong, or a pot that never took. Skeptics and believers can both be loud. This book does not decide between them.
