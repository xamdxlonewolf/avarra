# Playing in the Turning

> **Not yet written.** This chapter is reserved for the table: how to make a character in this world, where a campaign can start, and how the room should feel. The world exists; this is how you sit down in it.

## What belongs here

- Making a character: Kind + Condition (or Kept) + place, against Daggerheart's class and community
- What "the ticket" and a Tithe look like at session zero
- Starting situations tied to places that already exist (a seat, a square, a leftover job)
- Tone: integrated, lived-in, about five percent scary
- Sample frames: a live Grafting town; a quay with a White Note; an origin-town under a roll

## Making a character

> **Not yet written.** Heritage (Kind), transformation (Condition), community, class. The Given / Struck / Kept choice. Guild tickets for the hazardous few. Hearth-name and byname.

## Starting situations

> **Not yet written.** One door each from Eolvaeth, Orentel, Maiethlir, Harrow's Green, Ornsael, the hamlets, the Third Hearth — not a tour. The leftover job on the street is the hook.

## Tone at the table

> **Not yet written.** Neighbours, colleagues, family. The Tithe is the edge. The Leaf-Fall is warm. Dread does not live in the Tree.

## Sample frames

> **Not yet written.** Short campaign shapes that use two clocks and do not treat the Years of Hands as post-history.
