# Maps

East is the old heart. West is the wave's front, then the wall. Names live in *Climate, Ecology, and Travel*. The idea the picture is of lives in *The Four Continents*. How to *draw* a map lives among production aids, not in this book. Where a painted roof and a note disagree, the note is the place.

Reading **west to east** (left to right on the drawing) is the reach-gradient run backward: storm-wall, thin, high, full.

```
Kumbaan  ~storm-wall~  HESKOREN  ==West Water==  STRANDOREN  ==Old Crossing==  MAIETHORN
 (none)                 thin                      high                         full
```

Coastlines are schematic, not a survey. Flip the compass if a later campaign wants the gradient on another axis; the **relative** positions stay. West is left on the sheets that carry names.

## World map

The labelled schematic is placement only, light on terrain. Four landmasses. The Old Crossing, the West Water, the Rain-Wall, four rivers, the Noon Pass, and the Shelf-gate. The fifteen are regions, not surveyed borders.

**Rothallo** is the mark inside Orenbren, one day's walk from the wood. People still say the Inner Close. It is the same place, not a sixteenth colour. The painted square on the regional sheets is still small. The city is the capital. No sheet puts a star on a capital. Maiethlir and Orentel stay the marks they were. They are not given a second dot.

Capitals, large cities, and important towns are marked where the notes already place them. **Vaelhesk** is the land, not a dot. Harrow's Green and Ornsael are not capitals. The First Seat is not a capital, and it is not marked. The Tree is not dated. There is no graft on Kumbaan.

The three pre-Walk leftovers are small marks: the Low Wall, the Seeing-Ring, the Dry Stair.

Painted sheets are terrain. Extra isles on a generator sheet are not places. Add the names from this chapter. Do not copy an island the schematic does not show.

**Natai** sits west of Harrow's Green, between Sanbreo and the square, and not on the ford. **Larbril** is where the west road meets the Well-wash, short of the pass, west of Ornsael's well. **Ornsael** is farther into the dry, at a well, not the next roof and not the coast.

## Continent maps

**Maiethorn**, east. Mother-core west of the Rain-Wall. Thaeloren is the sole exceptional Tree, a canopy-ring in the old growth. Rothallo is the walled mark one day out, still called the Inner Close, and it is the capital. Seinbrun stands off the Core-thaw. Maiethlir sits where that river slows. The Third Hearth is a road-house three days out, not a city. Lirorn is the range: the Rain-Wall is the name of the divide, and *the Thaw-Wall* is only Lirorn's handle for it. The Noon Pass is the older high notch. The Shelf-gate is the lower road. Tasain is in the valley below that gate, on the wet side. Saelthael is the dry east. Larbril is the meeting of the west road and the Well-wash. Ornsael is the well-town farther along that road into the dry. The Hinge Shore is the Old Crossing face. Votaer is the port on that shore.

**Strandoren**, middle. Orentel is a plain dot at the eastern estuary. It is not a star. The Chart-run crosses the interior to that estuary. Lunbra is on the run, upriver of the salt. Raitin is the river behind the premier coast, not the Chart-run. Trenledd and Netstrand stay area labels, not borders. Naenor is on the south coast, on its own water, not the Chart-run and not the West Water. Braetu is the harbour on the Night Shore. The six charter-towns are not marked.

**Heskoren**, west of the West Water. West is the last capes, then Ornled, then the storm-wall side. The sea on the Strandoren side of the sheet is the West Water. Eolvaeth is the pilgrim-town in the waiting vale. It cannot see Harrow's. Sanbreo is the slate-shore town, the mark that names Ornled's seat. Harrow's Green is a plain grove-town on the live-front rise. Natai is the march west of that square, between Sanbreo and Harrow's, not on the square and not on the ford. The hamlets sit on different ground past the Rise-water crossing: Brenod, Vaelun, Ornath. Vaelhesk is old greens. It has no settlement dot.

**Kumbaan**, behind the storm-wall. One isle. The name sits on the hill-country with no point marker. The storm-wall is the cloud, the current, and the reef, not a border and not a door. No graft, no city, no harbour, no safe channel, and no Tree. Ndenjoo is a hill-hall in the text. It is not a flag on the sheet, and nothing on the isle is a graft.

## The overlays

Each painted region keeps the names the ground already had. A pointer is a plain mark. It is not a new town.

**The world sheet** runs the same west-to-east line: Kumbaan, the storm-wall, Heskoren, the West Water, Strandoren, the Old Crossing, Maiethorn, the Rain-Wall. Seas sit in the water, not across a shore. Vaelhesk is not a dot on this sheet either. Maiethlir and Orentel are marked once here, because the world sheet did not already have them.

**The Old Crossing.** Orentel on the Strandoren estuary. The Hinge Shore is area-type on the Maiethorn coast, and Votaer is the port on that coast. The Hush-rate is a crossing-charge, not a line across the water.

**The Sacred Core.** Thaeloren's canopy-ring. No separate mark for the First Seat. Rothallo is the walled square one day out. Seinbrun is off the Core-thaw. The Third Hearth is three days out. Maiethlir is the counted city on the other road.

**The Rain-Wall.** The range is the Rain-Wall. The Noon Pass and the Shelf-gate are notches, not towns. Tasain is the town in the valley below the Shelf-gate. No Kind-nation fills the snow.

**The Rain-Shadow.** Climate-type on the dry east. Larbril on the west road, short of the pass. Ornsael on the well, farther into the dry. The Well-wash is seasonal water, not a border. The Dry Stair is a site on a different rise. The well-town at its shoulder stays unnamed.

**The Chart-run.** The river to Orentel's estuary. The first quay is the old landing. The White Note is one desk-house on the third quay, north side, not a crown. Lunbra is upriver of the salt. Other filed towns stay unnamed.

**The West Water.** The open ocean, in the blue. The Night Shore is area-type on the west-and-south face. Braetu is the harbour. The unlit berth is not marked on this sheet.

**The live front.** Harrow's is a grove-town on the rise, not a capital. The Rise-water runs to the ford. Brenod, Vaelun, and Ornath are three small hearths past the crossing. Natai is not this square.

**The waiting vale.** Eolvaeth is a pilgrim-town at the gift-hall. The spring is a pool, not a mile-shrine. Harrow's canopy is not drawn here.

Rothallo's gate is the same square gatehouse as the city: beds outside, the Book inside. Larbril's Well-wash is a silt-line, not a road. Vaelhesk has no sheet.

The three walks as history, and as present roads:

- **Near Mile** — inland stones toward Thaeloren. Some now end at a town Tree. Nelath's spur is one that stops.
- **Salt Walk** — Old Crossing, then the Near Mile. Hush-rate on the hinge. Votaer sorts the hull. Orentel prices the berth.
- **Long Mile** — West Water, then Heskoren mud. Empty road-houses still make the bed. Braetu quotes the far run.

Travel times sit in the chapter before this one. A day's walk is a mile-shrine.
