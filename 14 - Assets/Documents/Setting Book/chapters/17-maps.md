# Maps

East is the old heart. West is the wave's front, then the wall. Names live in *Climate, Ecology, and Travel*. The idea the picture is of lives in *The Four Continents*. How to *draw* a map lives among production aids, not in this book.

Reading **west to east** (left to right on the drawing) is the reach-gradient run backward: storm-wall, thin, high, full.

```
Kumbaan  ~storm-wall~  HESKOREN  ==West Water==  STRANDOREN  ==Old Crossing==  MAIETHORN
 (none)                 thin                      high                         full
```

Coastlines are schematic, not a survey. Flip the compass if a later campaign wants the gradient on another axis; the **relative** positions stay.

## World map

The labelled schematic. Placement only, light on terrain. The Inner Close is a mark *inside* Orenbren, not a sixteenth colour. There is no graft on Kumbaan. Harrow's Green and Ornsael are not capitals. The First Seat is not a capital. The Tree is not dated.

Painted atlas sheets exist as production aids. They are terrain feel, not a second gazetteer. Extra isles on a generator sheet are not canon. Add the names after; do not copy islands the schematic does not show.

## Continent maps

The fifteen are regions, not surveyed borders. Sit them from the schematic and from *The Fifteen Powers*.

**Maiethorn**, east: Mother-core west of the Rain-Wall; Lirorn on the range; Saelthael in the dry east; the Hinge Shore on the Old Crossing; Threnmaieth and Maiethvael arguing paper near the grove; Orenbren lodging the Near Mile, Inner Close a day's walk from the wood.

**Strandoren**, middle: Lestrand and Orentel on the Crossing face; Brenledd the compact behind; Leddvael a different stretch of signing-water; Trenledd on the wealthy Chart-run; Netstrand on the West Water.

**Heskoren**, west of the West Water: Eolvaeth in Vaethorn's vale; Harrow's Green on Saelvaeth's live front; the hamlets on the Rise-water; Vaelhesk on old greens; Ornled off the hottest pilgrim-roads.

**Kumbaan**, behind the storm-wall: one isle, no graft, Ndenjoo as a hill-hall, not a flag.

## Square and seat maps

Walkable squares already have streets in the Gazetteer. This is only how they sit on the schematic.

| Place | What to find on the drawing |
|---|---|
| **Harrow's Green** | Saelvaeth live front; Rise-water running to the ford |
| **the Three Hamlets** | the ford; canopy visible from the water in clear weather |
| **the Third Hearth / Brenthael** | Near Mile, three days out from Thaeloren |
| **Ornsael** | Rain-Shadow east, west-road over the Shelf-gate |
| **Eolvaeth** | Vaethorn vale; cannot see Harrow's |
| **Orentel** | Strandoren Crossing face; Chart-run to the estuary |
| **Maiethlir** | Core-thaw, a day's living-earth from the Motherwood, not the grove |
| **Ndenjoo** | Kumbaan; no Tree |

The three walks as history, and as present roads:

- **Near Mile** — inland stones toward Thaeloren. Some now end at a town Tree.
- **Salt Walk** — Old Crossing, then the Near Mile. Hush-rate on the hinge.
- **Long Mile** — West Water, then Heskoren mud. Empty road-houses still make the bed.

Travel times sit in the chapter before this one. A day's walk is a mile-shrine.
