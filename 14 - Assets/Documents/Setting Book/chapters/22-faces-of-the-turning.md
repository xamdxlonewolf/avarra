# Faces of the Turning

Offices that already had to exist, made into people. The job is why they matter. None of them were chosen. A party can meet two in a week and never meet the rest. Recruits not by Kind.

## Offices already on the ground

These mouths were furniture first. They are people now. Do not rebuild the seats or squares to give them a stage.

## Positional pivots

### Vaethod — Eolvaeth

**Vaethod** *(VAYTH-od)* is town-warden of Eolvaeth: the mouth who decides whether the hinge happened, whether the Speaking week is over, and some years whether a cohort walks toward Saelvaeth's luck. They will not invent a colour that did not fall. What they want is not the Tree but **Saelid**'s baker's stall on the camp-streets — winter mouths buy bread, and a send-year takes those mouths away. They tell themselves that speaking truly means they have not chosen. They have sent people toward Haelin's live front without meeting them.

### Rithim — Maiethlir

**Rithim** *(RITH-im)* is Speaker of Maiethlir — the mouth that names the leaf-colour while clerks wait to copy it. What they want is **Valein**, Kept and unmarked in the same household, to stay off the tablet; the verso already knows how to write *unmarked*. They believe delaying the copy is the same as refusing. On the same morning Laevila describes what Rithim will not say into a roll. The First Seat sits in the wood beside them, not as a master they report to weekly.

### Mataero — Orentel

**Mataero** *(mah-TAY-ro)* lets the inland loft of the White Note House — twelve beds, the north bed not to let — and files all of it as occupancy. They did not walk. What they want is **Sorel** off a century-note their mother still carries from a walk Sorel cannot point to; they think a clean loft-slate will buy that. They believe the house is a bank that happens to have rooms. A household Vaethod sent may already owe a bed here. Reimaethe pays a rise-room Mataero writes *let*.

### Thilim — the Third Hearth

**Thilim** *(THIL-im)* keeps the Third Hearth, the third lodging out from the Motherwood on the Near Mile — Long-Lived, inland, notes smaller than the White Note's, shrine larger. They walked the mile as the inn until the stretch was optional and kept making the bed anyway. What they want is the stone's water still feeding the pot when it is dark; Seine's bed is still made. They hold that mouths at dark matter more than paper. Brenthael's warden wants the stone moved. Thilim will not move a name that is also a well.

### Laevila — Maiethlir

**Laevila** *(lay-VEE-lah)* bakes on the old extra-mile, keeps the room under the chapel the wardens recut, and holds the only copy of the beam's hidden face. Long-Lived; they walked as a child under Thaeloren before this river had to count them. What they want is **Senithi**'s name good for a marriage: the hidden face still has the grandmother under her own name, and the roll wrote the household wrong. They tell themselves that if they only describe, they have not chosen sides. Rithim Speaks in the same square. They eat after Night Watch and do not agree what that means.

### Tesara — Orentel

**Tesara** *(teh-SAH-rah)* sits the stillers' shed that does not face the street — a key the slate does not name, success measured in silence. Struck years back, they remember the door from their own catching-week. What they want is **Valen** stamped with a real ticket, not a burned slip. They believe a quiet week means they saved someone. The paying floor wants the master back. Mataero will need a loft if Valen lives without a ticket.

## Named wants in the three seats

Wants sit on people. An institution can furnish or refuse. It cannot *want*.

**Eolvaeth.** Vaethod wants Saelid's stall to keep its winter mouths. **Nedaen** *(neh-DAYN)*, Taken-In, wants the gift-hall pot to feed their child through Ledger-month. **Heiral** *(HAY-ral)*, Kept, wants a marriage witnessed without being pitied as Mother-kept. **Daron** *(DAR-on)*, Returned, wants a specific corpse named so a grain-debt dies with it.

**Orentel.** Mataero wants Sorel off the century-paper. **Sorim** wants berths 1–4 held through Hale-month. Tesara wants Valen stamped. **Lasole** *(lah-SO-leh)* rents a leaf-lot and wants the credit extended one season. **Didel** *(dih-DEL)* of Hallowquay, Selkie, wants a kin-berth for a skin-kin wedding in Eolthael; Sorim has held 1–4 for a First-Hand year.

**Maiethlir.** Rithim wants Valein unfiled. Laevila wants Senithi's name read off the hidden face of a recut beam, once, in a room a clerk will accept as a witness. **Vehaela** *(veh-HAY-lah)* came down from Lirorn's thin snow with two children and wants an uncounted loft for one winter. **Baerith** *(BAY-rith)*, Far-Voiced, sings the counted hour and wants the crown to stop cutting green on the street where their sister keeps a pot.

How the squares touch: paper, beds, pots, and one week in Eolthael. A household Vaethod sent sometimes eats now on a White Note necessity-note. Sorim's berth 5 is earth; the pot is going toward a luckier Hand; Haelin will sleep the Road-hand if the wood arrives. Rithim Speaks; Laevila describes the other colour in the same square. Tesara's week, if it is not burned, becomes a person who later needs a loft or a berth. Thilim still holds a far-cape name the White Note does not. They do not travel to deliver the mismatch.

**Haelin** *(HAY-lin)* wants the stone moved before Leaf-Fall so the crowd can stand, and water still on it until then. They will not name a colour they are not sure of. **Tora** *(TOR-ah)* of Brenod is here because last year's Brenod child was seen under the canopy and has not been walked home.

**Reimaethe** *(RYE-may-theh)* is a Long-Lived on the rise in Orentel who poured four years into a cup and wants to stop before the fifth. Neighbours call it a rich uncle's hobby. They pay a rise-loft on time. Mataero keeps writing *let* and not *why*.

**Hithaen** *(hith-AYN)* arrived in Eolvaeth's gift-hall this Hale-month with no list behind them. Given; they used to be heir to a house that counts the Kept as the line that may rule. The leaf took that. They want a life that is not a wall-pass. Vaethod has not decided if this mouth came to wait or to be counted.

**Taeren** *(TAY-ren)* is of Brenod, not of Harrow's. Last Eolthael they stood at the neighbour's canopy at Harrow's Green; something white happened, and because a colour that is not civic cannot be walked home as a witnessing, they are still in the square when Hale-month extra mouths arrive. Heat in the hands they do not show. Ordinary questions.

**Rosire** *(ro-SEER-eh)* is of Orentel, infirmary-tired. They used to bring water at Tesara's shed; this Hale-month they are not on that floor — an upper room at Harrow's Green. They do not talk about travel. They want the person they walked inland with not to become a story, and themselves not sent for.

Ledan still writes the quay-desk. They are a mouth, not a seventh pivot. The four later seeds stay seeds. Do not grow them into a crew.
