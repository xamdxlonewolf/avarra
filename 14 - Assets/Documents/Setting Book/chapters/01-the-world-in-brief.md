# The World in Brief

Vampires, werewolves, and their kin exist in the Turning, and they are part of society — accepted, employed, and needed. They are not a persecuted underclass and they are not a secret. Each is a becoming, not a species, and each is a neighbour with a job and a cost.

Every person carries two layers, and they stack.

**Kind** is what you are born: Human, Elf, Kitsune, Selkie, Tengu, Yumboe, and the rest. Kind is mundane. Nobody blinks at a fox-tail in a market or wings on a ridge. You are raised by a place, not by your ears. There are no Kind-nations.

**Condition** is what you become. Roughly three in four people acquire one over a lifetime. A Condition is a managed state with law, guild, medicine, and work attached to it. Society is built around what the Conditioned can do, and around helping them live with the cost.

Two paths lead to a becoming. At a child's tenth-year solstice, families gather at a **Turning Tree**. A coloured leaf falls, and a Condition is **Given**. The leaves hug with no colour, and the child is one of **the Kept** — kept whole, wanted in their own right, not a failure. Later, some people cross a threshold alone — a drowning, an oath, a death — and are **Struck**, with no leaf and no ceremony. Over a lifetime the split runs about sixty Given, fifteen Struck, twenty-five Kept for life.

Every Condition carries a **Tithe**: an upkeep that must be met to live well with it. Meet it, and you are a valued citizen. Fall behind, and a small, real edge shows. The will stays one's own. No Condition can compel another person.

Some hold that the **Leaf-Mother** blessed the world with these gifts, and that the Trees are her hands. Skeptics say it is simply what the Trees do. Right practice binds; right belief is argued about. The Motherfaith is one faith among several.

There is one origin Tree — the **Awakening Tree**, Thaeloren, on Maiethorn — and every town Tree is a living graft of it. The map of the world is a gradient of that wood's reach: sacred-dense origin, mercantile middle, thin frontier, and a storm-walled isle beyond the Trees entirely.

The surface of life is late-medieval: chartered towns, guild halls, markets, letters of credit, mills, universities. No printing press. No gunpowder. The one advancement beyond that surface comes from Condition-labor, paid for by Tithes, concentrated where those Conditions live.

It is the **Years of Hands**, Cut-year **387**. Town Trees stand in most squares. The walk to the origin is no longer required — except where the wood is still travelling, or has failed. On Heskoren the Grafting is not finished. Kumbaan has never Turned.

This book is the world as it stands.
