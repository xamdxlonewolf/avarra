# The World in Brief

Vampires, werewolves, and their kin exist in the Turning. Society accepts them, employs them, and needs them. They live openly, and the people around them know what they are. Each has changed, and is a neighbour with a job and a cost. That is a becoming.

Every person carries two layers, and both are true at once.

**Kind** is what you are born: Human, Elf, Kitsune, Selkie, Tengu, Yumboe, and the rest. A Kind is ordinary. Nobody blinks at a fox-tail in a market or wings on a ridge. The place you live in raises you. A Kind does not keep a country of its own.

**Condition** is what you become. Roughly three in four people acquire one over a lifetime. People manage a Condition through law, guild, medicine, and work attached to it. Society is built around what people with a Condition can do, and around helping them live with the cost.

Two paths lead to a becoming. At a child's tenth-year solstice, families gather at their town's **Turning Tree**. A coloured leaf falls, and a Condition is **Given**. If the leaves hug with no colour, the child is kept whole, and wanted in their own right. That child is one of **the Kept**. Later, some people cross a threshold alone (a drowning, an oath, a death) and are **Struck**, with no leaf and no ceremony. Over a lifetime the split runs about sixty Given, fifteen Struck, twenty-five Kept for life.

Every Condition carries a **Tithe**. You meet that upkeep to live well with the becoming. Meet it, and you are a valued citizen. Fall behind, and a small, real edge shows. The will stays one's own. No Condition can compel another person.

Some hold that the **Leaf-Mother** blessed the world with these gifts, and that the Trees are her hands. Skeptics say it is simply what the Trees do. The practice is binding. Belief is argued about. The Motherfaith holds her. It is one faith among several.

There is one origin Tree, the **Awakening Tree**, called Thaeloren, on Maiethorn. Someone takes a cutting of the Awakening Tree and plants it in new ground. That is a living graft, and every town Tree is one. The map of the world follows that wood's **reach**. That is how far the grafts have spread. The origin is sacred and dense. The middle lives by trade. The frontier is thin. A storm-walled isle lies beyond the Trees entirely.

Ordinary life is late-medieval. Towns are chartered, and people keep guild halls, markets, letters of credit, mills, and universities. No printing press. No gunpowder. The one step past that life comes from Condition-labor, paid for by Tithes, concentrated where those Conditions live.

It is the **Years of Hands**, **Cut-year 387**. That is the age of the town Trees. Years are numbered from the First Cut. That number is a Cut-year. Town Trees stand in most squares. The walk to the origin is no longer required, except where the wood is still travelling, or has failed. On Heskoren the planting is not finished. Kumbaan has never Turned. It is the storm-walled isle.

This book is the world as it stands.
