# The World in Brief

Vampires, werewolves, and their kin exist in the Turning. They are part of society: accepted, employed, and needed. They live openly, and the people around them know what they are. Each is a becoming, a person who has changed, and a neighbour with a job and a cost.

Every person carries two layers, and both are true at once.

**Kind** is what you are born: Human, Elf, Kitsune, Selkie, Tengu, Yumboe, and the rest. A Kind is ordinary. Nobody blinks at a fox-tail in a market or wings on a ridge. The place you live in raises you. A Kind does not keep a country of its own.

**Condition** is what you become. Roughly three in four people acquire one over a lifetime. A Condition is a managed state with law, guild, medicine, and work attached to it. Society is built around what people with a Condition can do, and around helping them live with the cost.

Two paths lead to a becoming. At a child's tenth-year solstice, families gather at a **Turning Tree**, the tree a town keeps for that day. A coloured leaf falls, and a Condition is **Given**. If the leaves hug with no colour, the child is one of **the Kept**: kept whole, and wanted in their own right. Later, some people cross a threshold alone (a drowning, an oath, a death) and are **Struck**, with no leaf and no ceremony. Over a lifetime the split runs about sixty Given, fifteen Struck, twenty-five Kept for life.

Every Condition carries a **Tithe**, an upkeep that must be met to live well with the becoming. Meet it, and you are a valued citizen. Fall behind, and a small, real edge shows. The will stays one's own. No Condition can compel another person.

Some hold that the **Leaf-Mother** blessed the world with these gifts, and that the Trees are her hands. Skeptics say it is simply what the Trees do. The practice is binding. Belief is argued about. The Motherfaith, the faith that holds her, is one faith among several.

There is one origin Tree, the **Awakening Tree**, called Thaeloren, on Maiethorn. Every town Tree is a living graft of it: a cutting of that same wood, planted in new ground. The map of the world follows that wood's **reach**, how far the grafts have spread. The origin is sacred and dense. The middle lives by trade. The frontier is thin. A storm-walled isle lies beyond the Trees entirely.

Ordinary life is late-medieval: chartered towns, guild halls, markets, letters of credit, mills, universities. No printing press. No gunpowder. The one step past that life comes from Condition-labor, paid for by Tithes, concentrated where those Conditions live.

It is the **Years of Hands**, the age of the town Trees, **Cut-year 387**. A Cut-year is a year numbered from the First Cut. Town Trees stand in most squares. The walk to the origin is no longer required, except where the wood is still travelling, or has failed. On Heskoren, the outer land, the Grafting — that planting of cuttings — is not finished. Kumbaan, that storm-walled isle, has never Turned.

This book is the world as it stands.
