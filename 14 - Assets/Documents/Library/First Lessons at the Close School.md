*Lessons for the first year, the school beside the Book's house, Rothallo. Copied out by Volnai for the children to say back.*

---

**The first lesson. The Tree.**

*What stands inside the Close?*
A Hand of the Mother stands inside the Close, within the wall.

*What does the Hand do in Turning-Week?*
It lets a coloured leaf fall on a child, or it gathers its leaves around the child and hugs them.

*What does a colour mean?*
A colour means the child is Given a Condition. The child will carry its Tithe for the rest of their life.

*What does a hug mean?*
A hug means the child is kept as they are.

*Which happens more often?*
The colour. Most children who stand under a Tree are given one.

*Which is rare?*
The hug. A hug is rare.

*Why is the hug rare?*
Because the Mother keeps back only a few children, and she keeps them back for something better than a colour.

---

**The second lesson. The table.**

*Where does a Kept guest sit?*
At the end of the table nearest the host.

*Where does a guest with a colour sit?*
At the far end, past the last Kept guest.

*Is that unkind?*
No. Every guest is fed the same food. The host says where each guest sits, and a good guest sits there.

---

**The third lesson. The keys.**

*Who holds the key to a gate in the wall?*
A Kept person.

*Why?*
A person with a colour already carries a Tithe. We do not ask them to carry the city's keys as well.

*Who holds the key to a neighbour's own door?*
The neighbour. Every person holds the key to their own door and their own stall.

---

**The fourth lesson. The roll.**

*What happens if a child of a Closed house is given a colour?*
The clerk strikes the child's name from the heir-roll.

*Is the child sent away?*
No. The child is still a person. They may stay in the city and work, and they may go out of the gate if they choose.

*What do we say to that child?*
We say: a gift is a gift. A gift is not a seat.

---

**The fifth lesson. Kindness.**

*How do we treat a neighbour who carries a colour?*
Kindly. Their Tithe is hard, and they did not choose it.

*Why does the Book give them the heavier turns of work?*
Because their colours make them strong, and the Book gives each household the work it is fit for.

*Why do Kept households keep the watch on the wall?*
Because someone who carries no Tithe can stand a whole night without needing anything.

---

**Say it back together.**

*Most are given. Few are kept.*
*A colour is a gift. A hug is rare.*
*The Tree knows whom to keep.*
