*A scene from* The Heir's Seat, *in the common-speech copy kept on the long-house shelf in Lestrand. The copy is played from as it stands, in a yard or a hall, with a table, a tall chair, and a lantern. The writer is not named.*

---

**PEOPLE IN THIS SCENE**

THE GRANDPARENT, head of a Closed house.
A CLERK OF THE BOOK.
THE HUGGED COUSIN, ten years old.
THE GIVEN COUSIN, ten years old.

*A hall in a Closed house in Rothallo, the night after Turning-Week. A long table. One tall chair at its head. The CLERK sits at a side desk with the heir-roll open. The GRANDPARENT stands by the tall chair. The two COUSINS come in from the square. The GIVEN COUSIN has a storm-grey leaf pinned to their shirt. The HUGGED COUSIN still has green leaves caught in their hair.*

GRANDPARENT: Sit.

*Both COUSINS go toward the tall chair. Both stop.*

CLERK: The roll has one name for that chair. This morning it had two.

GIVEN COUSIN: I know.

HUGGED COUSIN: They can have it. I don't want it.

CLERK: It is not yours to give. *(Dips the pen.)*

GRANDPARENT: Let them eat first.

CLERK: The Book does not wait for supper.

*The CLERK strikes a line. The pen is loud.*

GIVEN COUSIN: Is that me?

CLERK: That was your name on the heir-roll. *(Turns the page.)* Your name is written again here.

GIVEN COUSIN: What page is that?

CLERK: The page for your colour. It says which turns of work you will owe.

HUGGED COUSIN: They're ten.

CLERK: The page does not say tonight. It says what will be owed.

GIVEN COUSIN *(to the GRANDPARENT)*: Where do I sit?

*The GRANDPARENT does not answer for a long time.*

GRANDPARENT: At the far end. Past the last Kept guest.

*The GIVEN COUSIN walks the whole length of the table and sits at the far end. The HUGGED COUSIN stays standing beside the tall chair.*

HUGGED COUSIN: Then I'll sit at the far end too.

GRANDPARENT: You will sit where the host tells you.

HUGGED COUSIN: It's my chair now. *(Sits in the tall chair.)* So I'm the host. I say everyone sits at the far end.

CLERK: The heir is not the host until the house is theirs. *(Writes.)* "Also said."

GIVEN COUSIN: It's all right.

HUGGED COUSIN: It isn't.

GIVEN COUSIN: The leaf was grey. Do you know what grey is? I'll run on the hills on my ranging-week. I can walk out of the gate any morning it's open. You have to stay here and hold the key.

HUGGED COUSIN: I didn't ask to be hugged.

GIVEN COUSIN: I didn't ask for the leaf.

*Silence. The GRANDPARENT takes a gate key on a cord from their own neck and puts it over the HUGGED COUSIN's head.*

GRANDPARENT: The Tree kept you back for something better.

HUGGED COUSIN *(looking down the table at their cousin)*: Better than what?

*Nobody answers. The CLERK blots the page. The lantern is put out.*
