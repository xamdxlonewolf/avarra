*A walking song that the school beside the Book's house teaches for the day's road from Rothallo to the wood. Written out on the back of an old sleep-line sheet at the gate, for pilgrims who ask for the words.*

---

**The Day-Road Count**

At the first green the leaves came down,
red leaf, grey leaf, falling down.
Count them on your fingers, count them on the road:
a colour at the first green, and nobody hugged.

At the second green the leaves came down,
copper leaf, grey leaf, falling down.
Count them on your fingers, count them on the road:
a colour at the second green, and nobody hugged.

At the river crossing the leaves came down,
every colour falling down.
Count them on your fingers, count them on the road:
a colour at the crossing, and nobody hugged.

Under the wood the leaves came down,
all the colours in the world came down.
Count them on your fingers, count them till you're done:
a colour for every child, and a hug for none.

Back at the wall the leaves came round,
green leaves holding, not falling down.
Shut your hand and hold it, hold it all the road:
the Close keeps the hug, and the hug keeps the Close.
