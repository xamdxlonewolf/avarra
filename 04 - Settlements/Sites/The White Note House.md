# The White Note House

> **A road-house that became a desk, and is still a house you can walk.** It is a [[Economy and the Tithe|deathless house]] of the trusted note, a house a [[Long-Lived]] keeps, so the paper does not die when a mortal clerk would. **[[Ledan]]** writes at the quay-desk. [[Mataero]] *(mah-TAY-ro)* lets the loft and is still junior to Ledan. People say *the White Note*. Lestrand, the coast that counts a house by its success on paper, still says *the bed-house* and means the bank. The founding is about Cut-year 80, about that numbered year in the clerks' count, not a sharper date than "about." The present house-year is **307**, which is the same summer as Cut-year 387. The conversion query headed "the 200th summer" is **archival, from Cut-year 280**, and it is not the count the desk is in now. The house stands on [[Orentel]]'s third quay, on the north side, with the inland loft in the rise behind the salt. The desk is not the crown.

## Overview

The fortune here is the same fortune as [[The Third Hearth]], come later and to a quay. In Brenvaeth, the years when Turning was a walk you could not miss, the house was beds, porridge, and credit for that walk. When the walk stopped being required in the core, the old centre of the country, the Long-Lived still remembered every debtor, and the paper outlived the pilgrimage-season. The desk sat down on [[Orentel]]'s third quay because that is where notes move now. The inland upper room is occupancy. Twelve beds stand in the rise behind the salt, and their window looks at a Tree that still Turns for free, the leaf given with no price set on it.

What the house sells now is century instruments, notes written to be trusted across a long run of summers, and the **terms** on a First-Hand year, which is credit for a First-Hand year you do not need, a booked stay at the origin bought because the buyer can. It also sells loft-terms to wool through Hale-month, the crowded month, and it still writes the old kind of note for a necessity-family, people who have to eat now and pay on the home-walk. Same paper, two jobs.

The house does **not** hold the berth, and it does not own the hull. A Coast origin-winter, a winter booked at the origin from this coast, is one product with three desks. [[Netstrand]] supplies the ship, this house writes the term, and [[Orentel|Sorim]] holds the first-quay berth. [[Orenbren]] takes the sleep at the far end, the beds by the grove. Ledan will convert the year from one count to another. They will not tell the crane which berth is empty.

**The north bed** is how a bank proves it used to be an inn. Empty is not a shrine, Mataero says, because empty is a bed they are not charging for. Ledan says the house is the house it always was. The founding-book has a name in it, and Thilim, inland, still holds a far-cape name the White Note does not.

## How you walk it

There are two doors and one house. You can use both in a morning if the rise is not wet.

### The quay-desk (third quay, north side)

The street door opens on the salt, and there is no chapel-front. Inside, the counting-room smells of ink and of the tide. Ledan's stool faces the door, not the water, because they have seen the water. A second stool is there for a junior who is not Mataero, Mataero having the inland key, and a chest under the desk holds the **founding-book**. The Held names in it are credit-tokens, names the house still extends credit against, and they are not read aloud when a term is being written.

An hour at the desk goes like this. A customer comes in with a walk, a wool-season, a grandchild's debt, or a court-slip, and Ledan asks which count the customer wants written: the house-year, the house's own count of summers, a Cut-year, the clerks' numbered year, or a third date that a Lestrand court invented last generation. Ledan can convert among them, and will make you say which one you want. If the paper is a First-Hand year, the term is written here and the berth is **not**. Ledan will send you to Sorim for the hold, and will not walk you there. If the paper is a necessity-note, the verso still says *eat now* and the recto says a house-year, and Mataero may already have given the family a bed.

A smell of earth out of season means a pot, not a child, is the passenger on the next term. The clerk files that pot as cargo, and at the next berth over a First-Hand year is not cargo.

### The inland loft (the rise behind the salt)

The way up from the third quay is a walk, not a secret stair. There are twelve beds, eight let and four open, and the north bed is one of those four. Three of the open beds are for Eolthael, the Turning month, and **the north bed is not to let.** The window beside that bed looks at the Tree on the rise. Mataero's slate hangs on the inland door, and it reads *let / open / not to let.*

An hour in the loft can hold all of this at once. Wool, a First-Hand household, a necessity-family, and a Road-hand carrying a pot can all want a bed in the same week. Mataero files who is in which bed, and will not copy the north bed onto a term. If you ask whose name the north bed is, you will be told it is house custom. If you ask Ledan, they will want that name spoken once, aloud, and they will not speak it first.

Lestrand will only count this house as a success. Orentel's charter-council, the body that actually governs the quay, will only count the house as one ticket among many.

## A day's work (Cut-year 387, a usable afternoon)

**Morning at the desk.** Ledan converts a Hale-month loft-term into a Cut-year because a court on the rise asked for that count. Mataero brings the occupancy numbers and does not sit. A necessity-family from a thin year that [[Vaethod]] sent is already in a bed, and their grandchild cannot point to the ford named on the verso.

**Afternoon.** One of these can walk in without a scene having to break for it.

- A **due note**. The century-instrument on [[Sorel]], the long note still carried in Sorel's name, is not due this hour, but someone else's note is. The family does not remember the walk, and Ledan does.
- A **court-clerk** who wants the founding-book opened in the room can have Ledan convert a year, and cannot have the Held names read out as a performance.
- A **loft fight**, two lets claimed against one bed, with Mataero's slate either wrong or right. Wool and a neighbour's week, the name for people going on to a neighbour's Tree, do not share a mattress just because they share a job-name.
- **The north bed occupied.** The person in it may be a guest, a thief, a junior's mistake, or a name that came back. The house is then a house again, in public.

**Evening.** Ledan stays at the desk until the tide-bell, while Mataero walks the rise and checks the north bed. Empty is the custom, and occupied is a session, because the room has become a scene the moment someone is in that bed.

## How you get a term

| You brought | Ledan writes | You still need |
|---|---|---|
| Coin, a house-year, a walk you do not need | a First-Hand term, credit for a stay at the origin you could have skipped | Sorim's hold, and a Netstrand hull if you want the water |
| Hunger, a last hearth, someone who will pay on the home-walk | a necessity-note, eat now and pay on the way home | a loft-bed if Mataero has one, and guest-right, the right to be lodged, if they do not |
| A court-slip, a Cut-year, a demand to convert | the same debt, written in the count they asked for | the court, because the house does not sit the case |
| A question about the north bed | nothing | to leave the desk, or to say the founding-name if you have it and are someone who may say it |

You do not need a clergy to get a term, and you do not need a colour, a leaf's gift. A [[Long-Lived]] memory of a ford is why the note holds. A grandchild who cannot point to the ford is why the note is cruel.

## People

- **[[Ledan]]** is the clerk at the desk, the same person who wrote the Cut-year 280 query. Ledan wants the founding-name, the name in the founding-book, spoken once aloud over the north bed, so they can stop being the only memory of it.
- **[[Mataero]]** keeps occupancy, and wants [[Sorel]] off the century-note.
- **[[Sorel]]** is still on the paper, and still alive, and is not a clerk of this house.
- **[[Sorim]]** is not of this house. Sorim holds the first quay, and will not convert house-years into any other count.
- **[[Thilim]]** is not of this house. Thilim is the other fate, the hearth that went on making the bed.

## In-world text

*A term, half-written, on the quay-desk, on a Ledger-month afternoon in Cut-year 387. The hand is Ledan's. The verso is blank because the customer has not said which count to use.*

> *House-year 307, or Cut-year 387, or the court's third date — say one. I will not invent the hold. I will not invent the hull. I will write the winter.*
>
> *Loft: ask the junior. North bed: not a line on this paper.*
>
> *If you came about a ford your grandparent walked, I remember it. If you came to hear the name in the founding-book, you did not bring someone who may say it. Sit or go. The tide-bell is not a liturgy.*

Ledan is in the middle of a conversion, waiting for the customer to choose a count. If Mataero sees the blank verso, they will hear a junior's chance in it, a moment when the younger clerk might be the one who writes. Sorim will not be in the room unless the term needs a berth, and this house cannot give it one.

## GM Notes

Play the place as rooms and as an afternoon, not as a ledger that only points at other notes. The prestige-walk chain stays in this order: Netstrand hulls, then White Note terms, then Orentel holds. Orenbren houses the origin-winter, the sleep at the far end. Do not let "we sell the First-Hand year" mean that all three desks are this house.

The road-past works as credit no mortal court can audit. No mortal court can check a Long-Lived's memory of a ford two hundred summers back. That is why the note holds, and it is also why a grandchild can owe a walk they cannot point to on a map.

Ledan is Long-Lived. The Cut-year 280 query is the same clerk Mataero is still junior to. Do not invent a second Ledan and call it a title, unless play wants the house-name reused. The deathless reading, one person still at the desk, is the one to keep.

Mataero's blindness is the document of the junior. Mataero has to believe the house is a bank that happens to have rooms. What Mataero needs is for the conversion to count as competence, the lens Mataero uses is occupancy, and the blindness is that house custom *is* the credit. Do not have Ledan explain that, unless play wants the older clerk to say it.

Do not kill the Held name to tidy the ledger, and do not move Thilim into this house. The two fates stay two doors.

Do not make this house into Orentel's government. The Salt Quay is a charter-council, and the White Note is a desk.

The hooks are a due note this afternoon, the north bed found occupied, Sorel's paper, a First-Hand year and a neighbour's week in the same loft, a Bound signing-watch treated as prestige, a pot-term that smells of earth, a court that wants a Cut-year, and a session paired with [[The Third Hearth]] so the two fates share a week.
