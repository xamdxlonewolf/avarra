# The Low Wall

> **A wall that does not know its enemy.** In [[Orenbren]] lodging-country, off the present Near Mile and not inside the [[The Walled Book|Inner Close]], a low run of stone walks a fold of ground and stops. Everyday name: **the Low Wall.** Folk: **the Wall Against No One.** The Close will tell you it is theirs. It is a different colour. Chronicle leftovers: [[The Other Count]].

> **Canon status.** 🟡 **Pre-Walk physical survival (Story R.9).** 🔒 **Do not date it.** 🔒 **Do not date the Tree from it.** 🔒 **Not the Inner Close.** Adventure-depth (entrances, pressures, current actors as a site-run) waits on [[Roadmap|Story R.11]]. This note is enough to walk, argue, and dig.

## Overview

The wall is shoulder-high in most places, higher in a dip, broken in three gaps people use as gates. It does not enclose a town. It does not face the grove. It faces *out*, if "out" still means anything — toward a fold that is now lodging-fields and an old road-house's back garden.

No one living built it. [[Before the Walk]] has no start, and this stone is of that kind of old: present, stubborn, and uncounted. [[The Watching]] will fight any Cut-year a clerk tries to scratch into it. [[The Old Ways]] will leave a first-meal at a gap and not call it a guest-Tree. Motherfaith wardens want a ribbon off it because children already climb it like a mile-shrine, and it is not a mile-shrine.

[[The Closing]] made a *different* wall. Hildal's Retreat is dressed stone, taller, still lived. People who say the Low Wall is the Close's outer ring are doing a claim. Scratch the lichen: the Close is one quarry. This is another.

## Features

- **The three gaps.** Folk use them as gates. One gap has a socket that might have held a post. One has been widened for a cart. One is only wide enough for a person, and the Close's labour-roster sometimes "assigns" a walk through it as owed work, which is how a Book learns it cannot own an uncounted stone.
- **The inward step.** On the field side, the footing is built as if someone expected to stand *behind* the wall and look out. There is no walkway left. There is a habit of standing there anyway.
- **The blank name-stone.** Mid-run, a slab with a panel that held letters. The letters are gone. Not worn — taken off. Watchers say that is correct. A Closed Heir says the panel was theirs. A lodging-guild says a later pilgrim stole a luck-name.
- **The garden end.** The wall dives into the back of a hearth that still lets beds. Thilim's type, not Thilim's house. The innkeep waters the last course as if it were a pot-ring. It is not.

## Archaeology and play (lean)

You can walk it in an afternoon. You can sleep in the garden-end loft. You can be asked to fetch a child off the high dip. You can be hired by the Close to "confirm" it is theirs, or by a Watcher to stop a clerk from dating it, or by the innkeep to pull a labour-gang out of the person-gap.

What you might find: a different quarry's chip in the name-stone's bed; a pot-ring that is not motherwood; a knife-ribbon someone left because they thought this was the nameless knife's first house (it is not). What you must not be handed: a year that starts the Tree, a first enemy with a flag, a gospel in the panel.

Recognizable: a ruined wall, a climb, an inn's back garden. Inferrable: two quarries, a claim, a Book that assigns a walk. Inscrutable: who it was against, and whether "against" is even the verb.

## In-world text

*A lodging-guild complaint, unsigned, nailed to the garden-end for a summer and then taken in. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *The Close sent three Conditioned to walk the person-gap as owed work. They are not our beds and they are not the Close's wall. The children will climb whether we complain. If a Speaker asks, it is not a shrine. If Delamem asks, it is not a page. If a Watcher asks, we did not scratch a year. Pull your labour off our garden. The stone was here when the beds were full, and it will be here when the notes are.*

The innkeep cannot afford to see that "when the beds were full" is already a house-year. Delamem cannot afford to see that a gap is not a row. Both can stand on the inward step.

## GM Notes

- **Uncounted on purpose.** This is the 20% inscrutable of the Before, made walkable. Resist a first king, a first war, a first enemy-nation. A forgotten flood-bank, a forgotten raid, a forgotten *watching* — play may lean; the note does not pick.
- **Keystone-adjacent, not confirming.** An old wall near the grove is compatible with "people organized around a Tree that was already there" and with "people organize around anything tall." Do not use it to decide what she is.
- **Not the adventure-site pass.** R.11 may add an entrance under the name-stone, a pressure (Close vs Watcher vs guild), a discovery. Do not steal that pass by solving the wall here.
- **Hooks.** Confirm-or-refuse the Close's claim; get a child down; a labour-walk through the person-gap; a Watcher and a clerk at the blank panel; a knife-ribbon left in error.
