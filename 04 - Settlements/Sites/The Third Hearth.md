# The Third Hearth

> **A road-house that stayed a hearth.** The third hearth on the [[The Walking Years|Near Mile]], kept by **Thilim** *(THIL-im)* — [[Long-Lived]], inland, keeping this house since before [[The First Cut]], as later clerks would say. Seine's bed is still made. The notes are smaller than the [[The White Note House|White Note]]'s. The shrine is larger. Seeded in [[The Walking Years]] (the slate) and landed as present in [[The Years of Hands]]. Epic 7 may give it a mile-mark and a neighbour-town; do not flesh a village around it here.

> **Canon status.** 🟡 **Named stub.** Everyday *the third hearth*; cant *brenhael* like any road-house. Do not move Thilim to the quay to tidy the two fates. Do not kill Thilim to close the Held bed. Do not invent a second banking-order out of this inn.

## Overview

An old Near-Mile house on [[Maiethorn]]: beds, porridge, a dry room, a green-lot, a shed. In Brenvaeth it sold the walk you could not miss twice. In Ornthael it still opens for Eolthael — devotion's extra mile, a necessity-family on a Rain-Shadow west-road, the rare prestige household that did not book the White Note's winter. The rest of the year it is local: merchants who know the ford, a Road-hand watering at the stone, empty beds that look like failure from a quay and like memory from the lintel.

**The Held:** Seine of the far cape, tenth summer, not come. The mother said next year. Next year was last year — and has been, for a length of time Thilim will not convert into Cut-years. The bed is made. If Seine comes, Thilim will not have to remember they made it for nothing.

Lestrand calls this a bed-house that failed to become a bank. Thilim asks which mouths will be here at dark. Both are telling the truth they have.

## Features

- **The upper room** — still too many beds; not converted. Opens in Hale-month. Quiet in Ledger-month.
- **Seine's bed** — Held. Not a shrine Thilim will admit. A shrine everyone else can see.
- **The slate** — still kept. Verso still does not match recto.
- **A stone** — a day's walk toward the origin, or toward a town Tree that ended the road; Thilim uses it for water and will not join the argument about pulling it down.
- **Green-lot and shed** — still a pot and a shed, not [[Tithe-Infrastructure]]. Do not clone greens-keepers onto the yard.

## GM Notes

- **Two fates of the same origin.** The White Note collected. The Third Hearth kept making the bed. Play both. Do not have Thilim "lose" because the paper went to the coast.
- **Thilim's four jobs** are the same four [[The Walking Years|Long-Lived *I walked* jobs]], inland and tired. Blood-Read will give a true fragment. It will not tell you which job they are doing when they make the bed.
- **Mataero cannot see this house except as a warning.** That blindness is the White Note's. Do not correct the junior in Thilim's voice unless play wants the collision.
- **Hooks.** Seine's name on a lintel; a Hands-town that wants the stone down; a First-Hand year and a neighbour's week in the same loft; a note that outlived a family; Thilim asked to convert a house-year to a Cut-year for a court; a cutting in the pot that is still not a Tree.
