# The Seeing-Ring

> **A high place that was over-built, then politely emptied.** On a hill in [[Maiethorn]]'s old Watching country stands a ring of stones older than any name a clerk will be allowed to write. Someone later put a shrine on it. Someone after that took the shrine's roof off and left the ring. Everyday name: **the Seeing-Ring.** Folk: **the High Stones.** Watchers keep a night there and will not call it a temple. Chronicle leftovers: [[The Other Count]].

> **Canon status.** 🟡 **Pre-Walk physical survival (Story R.9).** 🔒 **Do not date the lower ring.** 🔒 **Do not date the Tree from it.** 🔒 **The upper work is overlay — Hands-era or late Walking, argued.** 🟡 **Tithsael** *(tith-SAYL)* keeps the present night. Adventure-depth added Story R.11.

## Overview

The hill is not Thaeloren. It does not want to be. You can see a smear of Motherwood weather from the top on a clear Eolthael, which is why later mouths built a shrine: if the First Hand is *there*, a seeing-place *here* can be talked into hers.

The lower work is a ring — not quite a circle, not quite a wall — of stones set to make a person turn as they walk. You look *out*. That is the whole argument [[The Watching]] needs. The Tree was already seeing. A mother-name came after. Each layer is a story laid over a seeing.

The upper work is cut from a kinder quarry: steps that do not match the ring's tread, a socket for a lintel, a Tender's mark pecked over a withheld pattern. The roof is gone. The mark is still there if you know to rub the lichen. Wardens call the emptying *respect*. Watchers call it *finally*.

## Features

- **The ring-walk.** Clockwise is local habit. A Watcher will not tell you why. A warden will tell you it is Turning-Week in stone, which is overlay so confident it sounds like folklore.
- **The out-looks.** Four wider gaps. One faces the smear of the origin. One faces the Old Crossing weather. Two face nothing a present map cares about. The last two are the inscrutable fifth.
- **The emptied shrine.** Steps, lintel-socket, no roof. A ribbon sometimes appears on the socket in Eolthael and is gone by Leddorn. Tithsael takes it off if it is a colour-ribbon. A hug-ribbon they will leave, which is how a Watcher does a kindness the Seat will not understand.
- **The pecked mark.** Tender's blessing over a withheld geometry. You can feel both if you put a thumb on them. Dual-practice in a fingertip.
- **The night.** Tithsael keeps it. Not a service. A seeing. People come with a cough, a question, or a child who was not Held and was not Given and wants a hill that does not owe them a name.

## How you enter

- **The hill path.** Ordinary. You climb in a morning. Clockwise is local habit once you are in the ring.
- **The emptied shrine steps.** Later tread. They do not match the ring. A warden will call this the door. A Watcher will call it overlay.
- **Under-ring crawl.** Between the lower stones and the hill, on the sea-gap side: a squeeze a small person can do, a mainlander can do with a scraped back. It is a drainage-gap or a seeing-gap. It goes a body-length and opens on one out-look from underneath. There is no temple-crypt.
- **The night.** Tithsael lets you sit if you did not bring a colour-ribbon and did not bring a year.

## Pressures (while you are there)

- **A clerk** wants a Cut-year on the ring. Tithsael will not let the chisel touch. A party can stand with either mouth.
- **A warden** wants a roof back on the socket — *respect*, they say. Watchers call that a congregation starting.
- **A colour-ribbon** appears on the socket in Eolthael. Tithsael takes it off. A hug-ribbon they will leave.
- **Maiethvael** will try to furnish the night without a list. Furnishing a night is already a bill.
- **Weather.** The origin-smear only shows on a clear Eolthael. The other two out-looks show themselves in any weather, which is worse for a pilgrim who wanted a meaning.

## Discoveries (still no year)

You might find: a second withheld cut under the Tender's mark; a pot-ring that is ordinary clay; a view of the origin that makes a pilgrim weep and a Watcher shrug; under-ring, a scratch that is not a letter; lichen that grows only on the overlay quarry.

You must not be handed: confirmation she stood here, a date for the lower ring, a first sermon, "it was a Closed Seat gate" (that hunger belongs to [[The Low Wall]]).

Recognizable: a stone circle, a ruined shrine, a night-watch. Inferrable: two builds, an overlay, a faith that will not start history at a knife. Inscrutable: the two out-looks that face nothing, and what "seeing" meant before it meant a mother.

This site serves the keystone *as pattern*: reach and seeing are older than the name. Skeptics still win the mundane reading (a high place, later piety). Both readings share the same hill.

## Archaeology and play

You can climb it in a morning. You can keep the night. You can be asked to rub the pecked mark and say which hand was first (do not; the note does not). You can be hired to stop a clerk from writing a Cut-year on the ring, or to put a roof back on, or to take a colour-ribbon off the socket.

Pair with [[Maiethlir]] if the party already knows a Speaker who will not say the line — same hymn-country, different hill. Do not make Tithsael Rithim's cousin to tidy a map.

## In-world text

*Tithsael's night-slate, one line a week, kept under a stone that is not in the ring. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *Ribbon off. Clerk came with a year; I would not let the chisel touch. A child asked if the hug lives here. I said the hill does not Give and does not Keep. The child said that was a better door than a slight. I did not correct them. The origin was a smear. The sea-gap was weather. The other two were themselves. If you are a Speaker, this is not a colour. If you are Limrae's child, this is not a row. If you are Hildal's child, this is not a seat. I am going to sleep on the inward step of a different stone. I am not taking the hill with me.*

Tithsael cannot afford to see that "a better door than a slight" is already a theology. The clerk cannot afford to see that a refused year is a year. The child can hold the hill without a paper.
