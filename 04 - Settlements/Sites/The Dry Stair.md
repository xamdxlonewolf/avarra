# The Dry Stair

> **A stair that does not climb to a Tree.** In [[Saelthael]]'s dry east, a rise above a well-town holds a flight of steps cut into the rock. They climb to a door-stone and a basin that has not held water in any summer a living mouth will swear to. Everyday name: **the Dry Stair.** Folk: **the stair that does not climb to a Tree** — said as a joke and as a warning. Chronicle leftovers: [[The Other Count]].

> **Canon status.** 🟡 **Pre-Walk physical survival (Story R.9).** 🔒 **Do not date it.** 🔒 **Do not make it the Tree's original temple, a first Fox-shrine, or a Kind-nation ruin.** 🔒 **Not Ornsael's hill.** Adventure-depth added Story R.11.

## Overview

The Rain-Shadow still walks west after the core stopped. This stair does not. It climbs *up*, locally, to nothing a Hands-child is trained to want.

The steps are worn in the middle. People have gone up for a long time. At the top: a slab with a threshold-cut, standing like a door with no wall, and a basin pecked into the rock behind it. The basin is dry. Taken-In who know wells say the rock was never a good cistern — the pecking is wrong for holding. Old Ways mouths on a visiting week leave a first-meal at the threshold and do not call the slab a guest. Children dare the last step and come down louder than they went up.

A later warden tried to plant a slip in the basin "so the stair would have a point." The slip died. Dead wood is not a Tree. The Seat did not send a second. Saelthael already knows about dirt.

## Features

- **The worn flight.** Countable steps; the count changes if you ask three children. One step is broken. People skip it and call the skip luck, or a slight, or nothing.
- **The door-stone.** Threshold-cut, no wall, no lintel. You can stand in it and be on both sides. [[The Door-Keepers]] who have come this far west will not officiate. They will eat if a bowl is there.
- **The dry basin.** Wrong pecking for a well. Right size for a pot, which is why the warden tried. A ring of salt-white in the pecking that is not motherwood-ash and not a colour.
- **The well-town below.** Not Ornsael. A lesser walk-hold that still sends west. The stair is the town's dare and its shame: visitors ask where the Tree is, and the town points west, and then someone laughs at the stair.
- **Names in the risers.** A few steps have a scratch that might be a person. Watchers would not date them. A Threnmaieth clerk would want them copied. The town rubs them with a thumb and does not read them aloud.

## How you enter

- **The worn flight.** Quarter-hour. Skip the broken step or don't. The count changes if you ask three children.
- **The door-stone.** You can stand in it and be on both sides. No wall. No lintel. Door-Keepers who have come this far west will eat if a bowl is there. They will not officiate.
- **Behind the basin.** A crawl where the pecking is wrong: a slit a hand can disappear into, then a shoulder. It opens on a **second basin**, lower in the rise, also dry. Same salt-white. No Tree-pit.
- **The well-town below.** Not [[Ornsael]]. A lesser walk-hold that still sends west. You enter the Stair from their shame: visitors ask where the Tree is, the town points west, someone laughs, then someone dares you.

## Pressures (while you are there)

- **A warden with a pot** wants the stair to have a point. The last slip died. They have another.
- **A clerk** wants the riser-scratches copied. The town rubs them with a thumb. A party can stand with either.
- **A missed child** — unTurned, not Kept. The town will not call them Kept. Someone will ask you to sit the door-stone overnight so the child has a door that is not a slight and not a Tree.
- **Fox-of-the-Sands hearths** want no ears on the slab. Outsiders will try.
- **Night.** The last step is louder going down. Wind in the basin that is not water.

## Discoveries (still no year)

You might find: the salt-white is mineral, or ash of ordinary brush, or something the town does not have a word for; the second basin; a west-road name scratched over an older scratch; pecking-tool marks that are the wrong angle for a cistern on purpose; a dead slip's fibres still in the upper basin.

You must not be handed: this was her first Hand, this was a Fox temple, this was a Closed Seat well-gate, a year, "it was a Tree all along."

Recognizable: stairs to a ruin, a dry fountain, a dare. Inferrable: a failed planting, a door-habit without a grove, a town that walks west because *this* is not a Tree. Inscrutable: what the basin was for, and why the pecking is wrong on purpose.

## Archaeology and play

You can climb it in a quarter-hour. You can dare the last step. You can be hired to carry a slip that will die, or to stop a clerk from copying the risers, or to sit the door-stone overnight because a child missed Eolthael and the town will not call them Kept.

Pair with [[Ornsael]]'s west-road so both clocks are in one week and the leftovers are not the same hill. Ornsael's crisis is a falling drink. This Stair Gives nothing.

## In-world text

*A west-road child's count, said going up, copied by a Road-hand who should have been going the other way. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *One, the broken. Two, the thumb-name. Three, the laugh. I am not to say the last number because the last step is the dare. At the top there is a door that does not keep you in. There is a bowl that does not keep water. We go west for the week. We come here so we know the difference. If a warden plants again I will water it, and I will not call the death a slight. If a clerk writes a year I will rub it. If you are from the core, this is not your mile-shrine. We climb those too. They have ribbons. This has a dare.*

The child cannot afford to see that "so we know the difference" is already a theology of thin soil. The Road-hand cannot afford to see that they copied a dare as if it were a mile. Both can skip the broken step.

## GM Notes

- **Not a Tree-ruin.** The whole point is the climb that refuses to be a Hand. A reveal that "it was a Tree all along" breaks the leftover and the Rain-Shadow's joke.
- **Not a Kind-nation.** Sands-Fox densest in the country; this hill is a town's dare. Do not put ears on the door-stone.
- **Keystone-adjacent as absence.** A place that never held a Hand, next to a country that still walks to one, is compatible with bounded reach *and* with dirt. Skeptics win the mundane reading easily here. Do not overplay the tell.
- **R.11 added the crawl, the pressures, the actor table.** Still not a Tree-ruin.
- **Hooks.** Stop a second slip; sit the door with a missed child; rub a year off a riser; an Old Ways meal at a slab that is not a guest; Ornled slate vs Saelthael joke; pair with [[Ornsael]]'s west-road so both clocks are in one week.
