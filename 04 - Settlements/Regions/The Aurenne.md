# The Aurenne

> The imperial core: the great river-basin where the Empire began, and the seat from which it still governs. If the Empire has a *there* that everywhere else measures itself against, this is it.

## Overview

The Aurenne is the deep, well-watered lowland at the heart of the continent — the Empire's cradle, breadbasket, and capital. It is the oldest-settled, most prosperous, and least *visibly* governed part of the realm: the machinery is so mature here that it has faded into the background of everyday life. People do not feel ruled; they feel *at home in a place that works.*

The **capital** (proposed: **Vaurehm**, at the head of navigation on the river Vaur) sits where the trunk river becomes a road — the knot where rolls, relay-post, high courts, and grain-fleets all tie together. It is a city of archives and granaries more than palaces: the Empire's power here looks like ledgers and warehouses, not walls.

## Geography & Climate

Broad temperate lowland, deep soils, long growing season, reliable rain off the interior heights. The **Vaur** runs its length, navigable and canal-laced (see [[Climate & Terrain]]). This is the surplus that feeds everywhere else — and the reason a bad harvest anywhere in the Empire is survivable.

## Settlements & Sites

- **Vaurehm** (proposed) — the capital; archives, granaries, high courts, the relay hub.
- Dense river-towns and market-towns strung along the Vaur and its canals — comfortable, old, unfortified.
- The **Zero Stone** is *said* to lie somewhere in the Aurenne — the marker every imperial road is measured from. No one can currently point to it. (See [[Climate & Terrain]].)

> Nothing here needs defending, and it shows: the heartland's towns pulled down their walls generations ago and built granaries on the footings. Safety is so assumed it has become architecture.
