# The Down-Bank

> **The blank between two places a traveler already crosses.** It lies downstream of [[Maiethlir]], on [[Named Ground|the Core-thaw]], before [[The Hinge Shore]] asks what a hull is. It is not a power, not a seat, and not a city. Maiethvael's people live along the first day below the Down Gate. The seat of that people is [[Seinbrun]], and Seinbrun is not on this bank. This note does not list the people.

## What you are in

The counted conduits stop within a day of the city. The orchards do not stop with them, and the river is still the Core-thaw. Clerks in Maiethlir call the spring rise snowmelt. On this bank nobody is filing it, so the water is just the river, louder in Liren, ordinary in Hale-month, a road when it is a road and a wait when it is a flood.

[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]]'s roll thins here. A hearth will witness that you slept, and it will not copy a colour onto a tablet. If you are still inside that first day, the Gift-Realm refuses the list: the hymns stay the same, and the paper is the opposite of a roll. After that day you are in a stretch that is not the roll, not the gift-seat, and not the quay. The Hinge Shore's question, which is what the hull is, has not started, because you are not at a quay yet. What you have instead is days of bank, gardens, and inns that know the Mile's reputation and are not the Mile.

The Near Mile is a different road. [[Nelath]]'s spur leaves that road, and you do not reach the spur by following this water.

## What a person notices

What you can recognize without being told is a river road, orchards, a bed, mud in Liren, and dust in a dry Gift-month. What you can work out once you have walked it is that two paperwork habits have both stopped, and that the city's loud week is behind you. The hinge is still ahead. In fair weather it is several days yet, because the walk from the grove to the quay takes eight to twelve days, and the city has already taken the first one or two ([[Named Ground#Travel times]]).

Posts along the bank have a notch that is not a mile-shrine and not a ribbon. People touch the notches in passing. The crown did not put them there, and the hinge did not put them there. Nobody on the bank says what the notch is for.

No Tree named in this note is a seat. Where the bank has Hands, those Hands Turn at their own hearths. That is ordinary Motherland reach, not a new capital and not the First Seat.

## What can enter a scene

- **A bed without a copy.** Someone asks to be written down. The hearth writes that they slept, and will not write a colour.
- **Liren mud.** The Shelf-gate is another road. This bank still softens in Liren, and a cart shares a dry patch with a stranger who is going the other way.
- **The first quay-question, too early.** A traveler rehearses a hull-class for an innkeeper who has no hull. The innkeeper hears a person who has not arrived yet.
