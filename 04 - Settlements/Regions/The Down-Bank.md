# The Down-Bank

> **The blank between two places a traveler already crosses.** Downstream of [[Maiethlir]], on [[Named Ground|the Core-thaw]], before [[The Hinge Shore]] asks what a hull is. Not a power. Not a seat. Not a city. The first day below the Down Gate can still be [[Maiethvael]]'s argument. This note does not name that seat and does not list those people.

## What you are in

The counted conduits stop within a day of the city. Orchards do not. The river is still the Core-thaw. Clerks in Maiethlir call the spring rise snowmelt. On this bank nobody is filing it, so the water is just the river, louder in Liren, ordinary in Hale-month, a road when it is a road and a wait when it is a flood.

[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]]'s roll thins here. A hearth will witness that you slept. It will not copy a colour onto a tablet. The Gift-Realm's refusal of the list, if you are still inside that first day, is the same hymns and the opposite paper. After that day you are in a stretch neither roll nor gift-seat nor quay. The Hinge Shore's question — what the hull is — has not started, because you are not at a quay yet. Days of bank, gardens, and inns that know the Mile's reputation and are not the Mile.

The Near Mile is a different road. [[Nelath]]'s spur leaves that one. You do not reach the spur by following this water.

## What a person notices

Recognizable: a river road, orchards, a bed, mud in Liren, dust in a dry Gift-month. Inferrable: two paperwork habits have both stopped, and the city's loud week is behind you. The hinge is still ahead, several days in fair weather — the grove-to-quay walk is eight to twelve days, and the city has already taken the first one or two ([[Named Ground#Travel times]]).

**Inscrutable, on purpose:** posts along the bank with a notch that is not a mile-shrine and not a ribbon. People touch them in passing. The crown did not put them there. The hinge did not put them there. The bank will not say what the notch is for. Do not promote the notches into a second shrine-road.

No Tree in this note is a seat. Hands along the bank Turn at their own hearths when they have them. That is ordinary Motherland reach, not a new capital and not the First Seat.

## What can enter a scene

- **A bed without a copy.** Someone asks to be written. The hearth writes that they slept and will not write a colour.
- **Liren mud.** The Shelf-gate is another road. This bank still softens, and a cart shares a dry patch with a stranger going the other way.
- **The first quay-question, too early.** A traveler rehearses a hull-class for an innkeeper who has no hull. The innkeeper hears a person who has not arrived.
