# Settlement Seeds

> **What this is.** These are the kinds of place that [[The Years of Hands]] left on the ground. They are not cities. Each town below already has a past, so none of them feels designed last week. The powers are named in [[Powers of the Turning]]. Kinds are hearths, not nations ([[Kinds of the Turning]]), so no town has a Kind-quarter.

## What every Turning-place already inherits

[[Daily Life]] already gives every Turning-place the same grammar, and this note does not change it. The Tree stands at the centre, or the neighbour's Tree does, or the place is waiting for one. Where a polity can afford it, Tithe-provision is a civic utility, dangerous trades sit on the edges, and homes accommodate becomings. The edge of the reach is what writes the map.

[[The Years of Hands]] lay a second set of leftovers on that grammar: a road that used to go further, a stone that used to be a day's walk, an upper room that used to be a pilgrim-dorm, and a house that used to be an inn. That reuse is how Ornthael comes to look like a present.

Kumbaan has no towns of this kind. Its places have a different past, the storm-wall and the moons and the Open Table, and not a mile-shrine. It has no Tree, and it is not a mainland power. Its one hall is [[Ndenjoo]].

## Towns, villages, and sites

- [[Harrow's Green]] is a Tree-town on [[Heskoren]], next to the live front.
- [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] are three hamlets that can see Harrow's Green and have no Tree of their own. Their hearth-names are Brenod, Vaelun, and Ornath.
- [[The Third Hearth]] is the third hearth on the Near Mile, and a neighbour of Brenthael.
- [[The White Note House]] is the house on [[Orentel]]'s third quay where a First-Hand year is written down.
- [[Ornsael]] is a walk-hold in the dry hills of [[Saelthael]].
- [[The Mill-hold]] is a Tree-town on [[Orenbren]]'s Near Mile, and its Tree is sick.
- [[The First Bowl]] is a guest-grove in [[Vaelhesk]]. The folk name is *Lonasir*.
- [[Nelath]] is a Tree-town at the end of a spur road off the Near Mile.

## Capitals

- [[Eolvaeth]] is the capital of [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]]. A poor land's capital is a congregation that waits.
- [[Orentel]] is the capital of [[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]]. A rich coast's capital is berths and a desk, not a throne.
- [[Maiethlir]] is the capital of [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]]. The census sits on the pilgrim road, and the First Seat stays beside it.

### How to use this catalog

1. **Pick a type, then a square.** Geography comes first, from [[The World Frame]], the leftover comes second, and the polity-face comes third, from [[Powers of the Turning]].
2. **Keep the two clocks visible.** A road-end Tree-town that never mentions the stone is only a backdrop. A waiting cluster written as frontier flavour is a stacked age.
3. **Give a street one leftover job, not all of them.** Devotion, prestige, and necessity can share an inn. They should not all be the town's theme.
4. **Name squares from the leftover types, and check them against [[Powers of the Turning]].** The types in this catalog are seated, [[Nelath]] closed the road-end lead, and a further type should not be invented to fill a folder.

### The lead is seated

[[Nelath]] is the road-end Tree-town. Do not seat that lead again. Do not relabel [[Harrow's Green]], Brenthael, or [[The Mill-hold]] so that one of them becomes Nelath. The guest-grove is seated at [[The First Bowl]]. Do not clone a second Harrow onto Vaelun.

### Production notes

- Do not invent Kind-quarters. Do not clone the Road-hands, or [[The Slide]], into a district.
- **Seeds are a blast-radius brake.** Each pass added squares or seats without rebuilding the seats already placed.
- **Write the job, not the postcard.** A pretty road-end town whose leftover is only picturesque hides the graves. A waiting cluster whose only note is that the people are poor and faithful agrees with Vaethorn guilt.
- **Keystone.** Thin grafts, sick grafts, and few grafts stay the player-facing cause. Do not let a seed-note assert the mind.
- **Kumbaan stays off this list.** [[Ndenjoo]] is a hall with a table at the centre. It is not a mile-shrine type, and it is not a mainland power. Do not add a Tree to complete the catalogue.
