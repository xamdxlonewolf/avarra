# The First Bowl

> **A guest-grove.** An old green that kept host-rights before any leave. A young Hand stands in it now — cut from [[Harrow's Green]], acting on the origin's behalf, married after the live front had a canopy of its own. Everyday *the First Bowl* (what you do before you cut, tend, or Speak). Folk hearth-name *Lonasir* *(lo-NAH-seer)*. [[The Tree-Wardens|The Seat]] still writes *a later planting in the Far Yield.* It does not use the hearth-name. That blindness is load-bearing.

> Not [[Vaelhesk]]'s capital. The land is the seat. Not Vaelun. Not a second Harrow. Not a sick Tree.

## Overview

A vale-pocket on [[Heskoren]], in [[Vaelhesk|the Far Yield]]: old green, ancestor-doors, first meals. The Mother's wood is a **guest**. A Cutting-leave without the meal is a claiming. [[The Door-Keepers]] walk here as Kin-speakers; they did not carry the scion. They do not confirm colours. They argue *host*.

The cut came from Harrow's after Harrow's took — a healthy Hand acting on the origin's behalf, not a first Tree for [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]]. The hamlets can see that sentence from the ford even when they cannot see this vale. Wood went *past* them into Yield-land. Brenod calls it the queue. Vaelun calls it a guest that should have sat at their green. Ornath has looked at the other road. None of those kitchens is this grove.

**Leftover job on this street: host-rights.** The meal. Devotion is a misreading visitors bring (they look for a chapel). Prestige does not book a first bowl. A Road-hand's necessity leaked through once, in a pot; do not make the walk's three jobs the grove's theme.

## Site

A day's walk, sometimes two, from Vaelun *away* from Harrow's — deeper into the Yield, not back along the Rise-water. The ford's three kitchens share a complaint. This vale shares a bowl. You do not pass Harrow's square to reach it unless you are carrying wood from there.

Sheltered vale weather: rye, oats, cabbage, hay, apples on the warm slope, pigs on woodland mast under local rights. Short kind season. One brilliant dry day, then rain. [[Climate of Heskoren]] already said a clear day is when everything moves at once. Leaf-Fall and a hinge-walk can land on the same dry hours.

Water is a vale-beck and a planting-ditch. Not a named river. Not Ornsael's well. The fight over a morning bucket is *host vs guest in the same green*, not a town emptying itself for drink.

Dispersed around the green, not nucleated into a street-grid. You walk from hearth to grove. You do not walk *through* a town. No walls. The boundary is where the old green's courtesy ends and the moor does not.

## The square (not a district grid)

- **The old green** — Taken-In ground, ancestor-doors, a first meal older than a Cut-year. Brudu's planting. Mixed Kinds. Not a Kind-quarter. Not a leshy village.
- **The guest** — a young [[Turning Tree|Tree]] at the green's heart, as [[Daily Life]] requires of any Turning-place that has one. Spoken a few years. Not mature like Harrow's. Not unsound. Leaves take colour. Vilraet Speaks when the bowl has gone around — or tries to.
- **The bowl** — wood, darkened, set on a stump that was never a mile-shrine. Two chalk-marks on the rim some years: *sat* / *cut*. Most years only one mark is honest.
- **The door-path** — water, then hill, then stone, in that order. A guest who reverses it is corrected without a sermon. The path is not a procession-route for Leaf-Fall; the crowd wants the boughs. The two routes cross in the mud.
- **The warden-hearth** — new, relatively. Practice-first. A lintel that faces the guest. Vilraet lives here. A pot may sleep in the loft when a Road-hand is passing through with someone else's wood.
- **The ranging** — at the vale-edge. Two-Bodied work the same woods the doors walk. No Kind-flag.
- **The far shed** — downwind. Returned work is a different door from the ancestor-meal. Delvor will say so if you sit in the empty place.

Homes accommodate becomings. A window that opens. A room kept green. Threshold courtesy. Struck named *kin* at the door that made them, then — if the town will house them — vouched. The exposed edge is the Struck the town will not house. Delvor has a name to give and not always a pillar.

## The leftover, visible

**Clock 1:** the green was a door before it was a square. Children were already the land's. A neighbour's week to a Motherfaith Tree can be refused on purpose.

**Clock 2:** a guest-Hand Speaks some years. Some ten-year-olds stand here. Some still walk to Harrow's. Some do not walk. The Harrow-sentence is a luck this vale did not ask to inherit; the wood arrived anyway.

Vaethorn heat sits on many tongues as gospel. This square lives in Vaelhesk custom. A waiting-gospel that calls refusal deprivation is the enemy of the meal. History does not agree with it.

**Inscrutable, on purpose:** the first-meal bread is always a little sour. Delvor says that is how you know the grove sat down. Vilraet will not eat the heel. Brudu puts a pinch of hill in the bowl that is not this hill, and will not say which door it came from.

Do not clone the hamlets' cup. The cup is a send/don't-send. The bowl is a sit/don't-sit. Same water-country, different question.

## People a visitor must find

No Epic-8 cast. Two claimants, one tender:

- **Delvor** *(DEL-vor)* of Lonasir — Kin-speaker. Asks about the bowl before they ask about a leave. Wants a particular Struck housed this season — kin-named at the grove-door, not walked to a Tree to be filed as late. May refuse a neighbour's week on purpose for a child they believe is already the hill's. Will tend the guest as host. Will not carry a scion.
- **Vilraet** *(vil-RAYT)* — town-warden of the guest. Practice-first, not Kind. Wants the week Spoken so a marriage in the vale can be witnessed this Eolthael without a walk to Harrow's. Did not sit, or sat late, the year the wood arrived. Delvor has not forgotten. The leave in the hearth does not mention the meal.
- **Brudu** *(BROO-doo)* — [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]]. The old planting. Wants the morning water before the guest's pot. Will not follow you home. Home is the green that was here first. Tired of being asked if this is Taken-In country. The question is the slight.
- **Nethiro** *(neth-EE-ro)* of [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford|Vaelun]] — Door-Keeper's cousin. Walks here when the bowl is set. Vaelun's leftover is still host-rights at a kitchen that *refused* the neighbour's week; this grove is the later planting that kitchen points at. Do not make Nethiro this square's warden.
- **A Road-hand passing through** — sometimes. They planted, or their circuit did. They are told to eat first. If they do not, they are a thief for a season. Do not clone them as a chapter-house.

The Seat's leave still says *a later planting in the Far Yield.* Clerks who learn *Lonasir* have learned more than the minute wanted them to. Use that.

## What can enter a scene

- **The bowl set out.** Before Leaf-Fall, or before a pot is opened, or before Vilraet Speaks. If it goes around, they sit. If it does not — the child still stands, or does not, according to the mouth, not the leave.
- **A pot that has not sat.** Earth-smell. A Road-hand on a clock. Delvor at the stump. Treat a warden with a pot as a guest or as a thief, depending on whether they sat down.
- **A child waiting in the rain** while two honest claims take the dry hour. Colour and kin-name both want to go first.
- **A hinge-walk colliding with the crowd.** Water, hill, stone. The Leaf-Fall wants the boughs. Someone reverses the order and is corrected without a sermon.
- **A Struck the vale will not house**, standing where Delvor named them kin. Courtesy without a pillar. [[Law and Citizenship|Standing-for]] is still civic.

## Two marks on one rim

*Said at the stump, not written. A Delvor mouth, a Vilraet mouth. Same bowl. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *Sit. Then Speak. The wood is a guest. I will not let a colour go first in a house that fed it.*
>
> *The week is one week. If the bowl lasts the rain I still have a fall to name. I have a Tree. I did not invent it.*

The argument does not resolve into a proverb. At dawn somebody still decides whether to lift the bowl, wake the child, and take the colour or the door-path first.

A third line, if Brudu is there, is only: *The planting drinks at first light. The guest can wait until the bowl.*

## GM Notes

- **This is the guest-grove.** Old Ways planting; first meal vs Cutting-leave; host-rights. Play with [[The Door-Keepers]] and with Nethiro at Vaelun. Do not clone a second Harrow. Do not make Vaelun this square. Do not sicken the guest to merge types; the sick-Tree is [[The Mill-hold]].
- **Both love the wood.** Neighbours, not pagans-vs-church. Door-Keepers do not carry scions. Vilraet is not a missionary. The injustice is host-rights as a claim that can shun — kindness with a threshold. Keep it local and real. Not holy war.
- **Not the hamlets.** Formula = two settings of one bowl, not three kitchens. Inscrutable = sour bread / pinch of another hill, not the cup. The cup stays at the ford.
- **Not Ornsael.** A morning bucket is manners in one green, not a west-road of thirst.
- **Wood that went past.** Harrow's cut, after Harrow's took. The hamlets' slight is structural. Do not tidy it by giving Brenod this guest. Do not make the First Bowl a Saelvaeth square; it sits in Vaelhesk.
- **Keystone:** thin/sick/few grafts stay horticulture in player text. A guest that took is still a graft of the one Tree; do not let Delvor or Vilraet assert the mind. Confirmation stays late. Household doors stay in GM walls on the faith notes.
- **One leftover job.** Host-rights. A Road-hand's pot is texture, not a second theme.
- **Names.** Seed `20260901`, algorithm v1, worn person-register; pick from the middle: Delvor (pos. 6), Lonasir (pos. 8, hearth), Brudu (pos. 10), Vilraet (pos. 16). Everyday *the First Bowl*; no new liturgy. Seat blindness on the hearth-name is load-bearing.
- **Hooks.** A Road-hand told to eat first; a marriage that needs a local Speaking; Brudu's morning water; Nethiro arriving with a Vaelun child who will not walk to Harrow's; a Struck named kin and unhoused; pairing with [[The Mill-hold]] so a meal-fight and a blight-slate are not the same week.
