# The First Bowl

> **A guest-grove.** An old green kept host-rights here before any leave was written, and the right is to feed a guest. A young Hand stands in the green now, cut from [[Harrow's Green]], acting on the origin's behalf, and married into the ground after the live front had a canopy of its own. Everyday speech calls the place *the First Bowl*, which is what you do before you cut, tend, or Speak. The folk hearth-name is *Lonasir* *(lo-NAH-seer)*. [[The Tree-Wardens|The Seat]] still writes *a later planting in the Far Yield*, and it does not use the hearth-name.

> This grove is not [[Vaelhesk]]'s capital, because the land is the seat, and it is not Vaelun, not a second Harrow, and not a sick Tree.

## Overview

The First Bowl is a vale-pocket on [[Heskoren]], in [[Vaelhesk|the Far Yield]], and what the pocket still has is an old green, ancestor-doors, and first meals. The Mother's wood stands here as a **guest**. A Cutting-leave, the Seat's paper for a cut, is a claiming if it arrives without the meal. [[The Door-Keepers]] walk here as Kin-speakers. They did not carry the scion, they do not confirm colours, and the thing they argue is who the host is.

The cut came from Harrow's after Harrow's took. What stands here now is a healthy Hand, acting on the origin's behalf, and it was not a first Tree for [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]]. The hamlets can see that from the ford even when they cannot see this vale. The wood went *past* them into Yield-land. Brenod calls it the queue. Vaelun calls it a guest that should have sat at their green. Ornath has looked at the other road. None of those kitchens is this grove.

The village's daily work is host-rights, and the right is the meal. Devotion is a misreading visitors bring, because they come looking for a chapel. Nobody books a first bowl. A Road-hand's necessity leaked through once, and it leaked through in a pot.

## Site

The vale is a day's walk from Vaelun, sometimes two, and the walk goes *away* from Harrow's, deeper into the Yield rather than back along the Rise-water. The ford's three kitchens share a complaint. This vale shares a bowl. You do not pass Harrow's square to reach it unless you are carrying wood from there.

The weather in the vale is sheltered. People grow rye, oats, cabbage, and hay, with apples on the warm slope and pigs on woodland mast under local rights. The kind season is short: one brilliant dry day, and then rain. [[Climate of Heskoren]] already said that a clear day is when everything moves at once. Leaf-Fall, the week the children stand under the Hand, and a hinge-walk can land on the same dry hours.

Water is a vale-beck and a planting-ditch. It is not a named river, and it is not Ornsael's well. The fight over a morning bucket is host against guest in the same green, not a town emptying itself for drink.

The houses sit dispersed around the green, not nucleated into a street-grid. You walk from hearth to grove, and you do not walk *through* a town. There are no walls, and the boundary is where the old green ends and the moor begins.

## The square (not a district grid)

- **The old green** is [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] ground, with ancestor-doors and a first meal older than a Cut-year. It is Brudu's planting, the hearths are mixed Kinds, and the green is not a Kind-quarter and not a leshy village.
- **The guest** is a young [[Turning Tree|Tree]] at the green's heart, as [[Daily Life]] requires of any Turning-place that has one. It has been Spoken for a few years. It is not mature the way Harrow's is, and it is not unsound, and the leaves take colour. Vilraet Speaks when the bowl has gone around, or tries to.
- **The bowl** is wood, darkened by use, set on a stump that was never a mile-shrine. Some years the rim carries two chalk-marks, *sat* and *cut*. Most years only one of those marks is honest.
- **The door-path** goes water, then hill, then stone, in that order, and a guest who reverses it is corrected without a sermon. The path is not a procession-route for Leaf-Fall. The crowd wants the boughs, and the two routes cross in the mud.
- **The warden-hearth** is new, relatively, and it is practice-first, with its door facing the guest, which is where Vilraet lives. A pot may sleep in the loft when a Road-hand is passing through with someone else's wood.
- **The ranging** is at the vale-edge, where Two-Bodied work the same woods the doors walk. There is no Kind-flag.
- **The far shed** is downwind. Returned work uses a different door from the ancestor-meal, and Delvor will say so if you sit in the empty place.

The homes accommodate becomings. A window opens, a room is kept green, and the courtesy is at the threshold. A Struck person is named *kin* at the door that made them, and then, if the town will house them, someone vouches. The exposed edge is the Struck the town will not house. Delvor has a name to give, and not always a pillar.

## What the village still does

The green was a door before it was a square, and the children were already the land's. A household can refuse a neighbour's week at a Motherfaith Tree on purpose. The guest-Hand Speaks in some years. Some of the ten-year-olds stand here, some still walk to Harrow's, and some do not walk. The Harrow-sentence is a luck this vale did not ask to inherit, and the wood arrived anyway.

Vaethorn's gospel is spoken on many hearths, and this square lives in Vaelhesk custom. A waiting-gospel that calls refusal deprivation is the enemy of the meal. History does not agree with that gospel.

The first-meal bread is always a little sour, and Delvor says that is how you know the grove sat down. Vilraet will not eat the heel. Brudu puts a pinch from another hill in the bowl, and will not say which door it came from.

The hamlets' cup asks whether to send, and this bowl asks whether to sit. It is the same water-country, and it is a different question. After the last person has gone in, a rye-straw is laid on the door-path's first stone and left until first light. The First Bowl will not say why.

## People a visitor must find

Two claimants share the green with one tender.

- **Delvor** *(DEL-vor)* of Lonasir is the Kin-speaker. They ask about the bowl before they ask about a leave. This season they want a particular Struck housed, kin-named at the grove-door rather than walked to a Tree to be filed as late. They may refuse a neighbour's week on purpose for a child they believe is already the hill's. They will tend the guest as host, and they will not carry a scion.
- **Vilraet** *(vil-RAYT)* is town-warden of the guest. The practice comes first, and the office is not a Kind. They want the week Spoken so a marriage in the vale can be witnessed this Eolthael without a walk to Harrow's. The year the wood arrived, Vilraet did not sit, or sat late, and Delvor has not forgotten. The leave in the hearth does not mention the meal.
- **Brudu** *(BROO-doo)* is [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]], and the old planting. They want the morning water before the guest's pot. They will not follow you home, because home is the green that was here first. They are tired of being asked if this is Taken-In country. The question is the slight.
- **Nethiro** *(neth-EE-ro)* of [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford|Vaelun]] is a Door-Keeper's cousin, and walks here when the bowl is set. Vaelun's work is still host-rights at a kitchen that *refused* the neighbour's week, and this grove is the later planting that kitchen points at.
- **A Road-hand passing through** comes sometimes, and either that hand planted or their circuit did. They are told to eat first, and if they do not, they are a thief for a season.

The Seat's leave still says *a later planting in the Far Yield.* Clerks who learn *Lonasir* have learned more than the minute wanted them to.

## What can enter a scene

- **The bowl set out.** It may be before Leaf-Fall, or before a pot is opened, or before Vilraet Speaks. If the bowl goes around, they sit. If it does not, the child still stands, or does not, according to the hearth and not according to the leave.
- **A pot that has not sat.** There is an earth-smell, a Road-hand on a clock, and Delvor at the stump. A warden with a pot is a guest or a thief, depending on whether they sat down.
- **A child waiting in the rain** while two honest claims take the dry hour. The colour and the kin-name both want to go first.
- **A hinge-walk colliding with the crowd.** The door-path is water, then hill, then stone, and the Leaf-Fall wants the boughs. Someone reverses the order and is corrected without a sermon.
- **A Struck the vale will not house.** That person stands where Delvor named them kin, with the courtesy and without a pillar, and [[Law and Citizenship|Standing-for]] is still civic.

## Two marks on one rim

*Said at the stump, not written. One voice is Delvor's, one voice is Vilraet's, and the bowl is the same.*

> *Sit. Then Speak. The wood is a guest. I will not let a colour go first in a house that fed it.*
>
> *The week is one week. If the bowl lasts the rain I still have a fall to name. I have a Tree. I did not invent it.*

The three voices disagree, and nobody settles it. At dawn someone still decides whether to lift the bowl, wake the child, and take the colour or the door-path first.

A third line, if Brudu is there, is only: *The planting drinks at first light. The guest can wait until the bowl.*

## GM Notes

- **This is the guest-grove.** The planting belongs to the Old Ways, the fight is the first meal against a Cutting-leave, and the work is host-rights. Play it with [[The Door-Keepers]] and with Nethiro at Vaelun, and do not clone a second Harrow, make Vaelun into this square, or sicken the guest in order to merge types. The sick-Tree is [[The Mill-hold]].
- **Both love the wood.** They are neighbours, not pagans set against a church. Door-Keepers do not carry scions, and Vilraet is not a missionary. The injustice is host-rights used as a claim that can shun, which is kindness with a threshold. Keep it local and real, and do not turn it into a holy war.
- **Not the hamlets.** This square is two settings of one bowl, not three kitchens. The sour bread is already explained in the player text, and it does not count as the habit. The pinch from another hill stays as written. The habit is the rye-straw on the door-stone. A later note must not explain it, and must not turn it into a power, a relic, or a rite. The cup stays at the ford, and a later note must not explain the cup. No new person. Delvor, Vilraet, and Brudu stay.
- **Not Ornsael.** A morning bucket is manners in one green, not a west-road of thirst.
- **Wood that went past.** The cut was Harrow's, after Harrow's took, and the hamlets' slight is structural. Do not tidy that slight by giving Brenod this guest, and do not make the First Bowl a Saelvaeth square. It sits in Vaelhesk.
- **Keystone:** thin, sick, or few grafts stay horticulture in the player text. A guest that took is still a graft of the one Tree. Do not let Delvor or Vilraet assert the mind. Confirmation stays late, and household doors stay in the GM walls on the faith notes.
- **The village's daily work** is host-rights. A Road-hand's pot is texture, not a second theme. Do not make the walk's three jobs the grove's theme, do not clone the hamlets' cup, do not make Nethiro this square's warden, and do not clone the Road-hand as a chapter-house.

Everyday speech says *the First Bowl*, and there is no new liturgy. The Seat's blindness to the hearth-name is load-bearing.
