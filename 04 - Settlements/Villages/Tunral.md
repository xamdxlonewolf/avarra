# Tunral

> **A kitchen on the live-front road.** *(TUN-ral.)* About a week short of [[Harrow's Green]], on the way a traveler already walks between the frontier coast and that canopy. You cannot see the green from here. Not the three hamlets. Not [[Eolvaeth]]. Not a march-seat.

> Mouth: *Huval*. A neighbour says the given name. A stranger hears *Huval of Tunral*. No house-name. [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] is a different road and does not get a person-list here.

## Overview

[[Saelvaeth]]'s march is a line of grafts and the gaps between them. [[Harrow's Green]] is a Hand that grew up. The hamlets can see it from the ford. Tunral is further back, one kitchen and a mile-post, where the road toward that luck is still a road and not yet a view. There is no Tree here. A slip was not planted to give the post a point. Dead wood is not a Hand, and this village will not audition for one.

People sleep here because the next dry ground is not guaranteed. **Huval** *(HOO-val)* feeds the night. Worn speech. They will point toward Harrow's and will not draw the canopy on the post. They have not seen it this month. The bog, or a flood, or a simple fold of hill, is enough. Weather on the Sundered upland already does this. The village did not invent a marvel.

**This street's job is a night on a road that already exists.** Not the neighbour's week (that is Harrow's, for kitchens that can walk home). Not the wait as a destination (that is Eolvaeth, and Eolvaeth cannot see Harrow's and does not want to). Not a guest-grove. One kitchen is not a cluster.

## The street

The post, the kitchen, a yard that takes a cart if the cart is small. The road comes from the coastward country and goes on toward the rise. No square, because there is no canopy to square. No stone pulled in from a further day. The post is a post. Huval sets a bowl outside at dusk on the side away from Harrow's and will not say why. It is not a first meal and not a shrine. A [[The Door-Keepers|Door-Keeper]] who tried to officiate would be in the wrong valley. [[The First Bowl]] is the other direction from the hamlets, not this road.

Food is oats, a smoked fish if the coastward week was kind, and the pot. No stiller. A [[Two-Bodied]] on the road is a traveler, not a quarter. Mixed Kinds in the sense that a frontier road is mixed. Do not found a people here.

**Inscrutable, on purpose:** the bowl on the wrong side of the kitchen. Huval will not gloss it. A pilgrim who calls it the Mother's portion has already preached. A clerk who calls it a due has already levied.

## What can enter a scene

- **A child who is ten.** The green is about a week if the bog allows. It may not allow. Huval will not invent a colour and will not send them back to a gospel-town to be consoled.
- **A clear day.** Someone climbs the yard-wall. There is still no canopy. The argument about whether Harrow's Spoke this week has no evidence in this yard.
- **The bowl.** Dusk. The side away from the road's hope. A guest eats from the kitchen and asks about the other bowl. Huval puts supper in their hands instead.

## In-world text

*Said over the pot, not chalked. Huval, Hale-month. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *Supper is the kitchen. The green is a week if the bog allows. I have not seen it this month. I will not draw it on the post.*
>
> *If you are ten, you are ten here too. I am not a warden and this is not a Tree. Eat. The other bowl is not for you. Do not ask me whose it is.*

Huval cannot afford to see that a week of bog is the march's queue in one yard. The child cannot afford to see a kitchen as the place the gift failed. The post stays blank.
