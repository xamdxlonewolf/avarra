# The Three Hamlets Past the Ford

> **A waiting cluster.** The Seat's [[The Tree-Wardens|Cutting-leave]] has already filed these hearths as a remainder: *the wood will take at Harrow's green. It will not take this year at the three hamlets past the ford — the soil is thin there.* They can see [[Harrow's Green]] from the water. They send this year's ten-year-olds to the neighbour's week, or further if Harrow's year is too full, or still the Long Mile if the neighbour is a stick in a pot. The folk hearth-names sit in worn [[The Old Tongue|Maiethren]]. **The Seat still does not use them.**

> There are three hearths here, not one village, and none of them is a capital.

## Before you cross

Three hearths sit past a ford on [[Heskoren]], close enough to share a complaint and far enough apart that a scion cut for "the hamlets" would still start a fight about *which* green received it. The reach is thin as weather. This year, for this cohort, the hamlets are Kept-heavy and Struck-heavy: a child may stay Kept at the ford while a sibling walks, and a Kin-speaker may stand for a Struck person the square will not. That thin year is not a re-enactment of [[The Walking Years]]. This is the present.

They have learned not to say *queue.* They can recite the horticulture-sentence, the one that names the thin soil. Folk-right cutting is a kitchen-knife kept in a drawer, not a rebellion with a flag. Most slips die. The ones that take are how the First Seat learns that it does not own the Tree's tissue, only the authorization to cut it.

[[The Old Ways]] hearths among them may **refuse the neighbour's week** on purpose, because their children are already the land's. Motherfaith neighbours read those children as Kept-for-want. The disagreement changes who walks, who stands witness afterward, and who will share a kitchen on the return. History does not agree with [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] guilt.

They sit on a seam. [[Saelvaeth]] luck is the canopy they can see, [[Vaelhesk]] refusal is in the kitchen, Vaethorn's gospel is on the tongue, and [[Ornled]]'s slate is what they would meet if they walked the other way.

The hamlets are making the week themselves. Devotion is a misreading that visitors bring with them, and nobody with money stops at the ford.

## What the ford tells you

The ford is the Seat's geography, the place from which you can see Harrow's green, water a mile-shrine would have marked if anyone had set a stone on the waiting side. No one set that stone. The absence is what is left. Necessity still drinks there, and children still ask how far it is.

**[[Named Ground|The Rise-water]]** comes down from Harrow's rise. In clear weather the canopy is a dark against the far slope. In rain it is a rumour you have to take on trust. The three greens sit on slightly different ground: a wetter shelf, an old taken plot, and a thinner rise past the last hedge. A scion planted "for the hamlets" would still be a fight about which of those grounds is soil.

Food is gardens, a shared mill-path, and ranging that is simply the woods. There is no venting-hall and no lot. Tithe-provision is the yard and the neighbour. A Condition that is lapsing here is a family problem before it is a guild's.

The houses are dispersed, not nucleated, so you walk from hearth to hearth and you do not walk *through* a town. The cluster is a complaint with three kitchens.

## Ask three kitchens

The names are worn drift, short compounds with the stress on the root, and everyday speech comes first. There is no liturgy in them. Naming the hearths in the old holy shape would pick a Seat-hearth, and a Seat-hearth would pick a scion.

### Brenod *(BREN-od)*

Brenod is the sending hearth. Motherfaith here is a practice. They walk the child to Harrow's, they want someone who was *there* to walk back and stand for the fall, and they can recite the thin-soil sentence without using the word *queue.* The kitchen-knife sits in this drawer more often than in the others, which does not mean they have used it. It means they have looked at it.

Brenod's part in all this is the neighbour's week treated as a *duty.* A visitor from the core will hear faith. Brenod hears a date in Eolthael.

### Vaelun *(VAY-lun)*

Vaelun is the yield-hearth. The older word *vael* is what the land keeps, and it is not *vaeth*, the waiting. [[The Old Ways]] sit this kitchen. They may refuse the neighbour's week on purpose, because their children are already the land's. A first meal is not a courtesy-card. A later cut from Harrow's, acting on the origin's behalf, still wants the bowl if this ground is an old green.

Vaelun's work is host-rights, not a second Harrow. [[The Door-Keepers]] play here. The later planting they point at is [[The First Bowl]], a day's walk into the Yield, sometimes two, and not this kitchen. Motherfaith neighbours in Brenod will call Vaelun's Kept children passed-over. Vaelun will not correct them in the Mother's language.

### Ornath *(OR-nath)*

Ornath is the furthest past the ford. The soil is thinnest here as a feeling, not as a locked horticulture. Ornath can see Harrow's when the weather is kind, and Ornath can walk the *other* way, toward [[Sanbreo]], where [[Ornled]] keeps the slate, if the congregation-net fails. They have not gone. The road is still a temptation that looks like sense: no gospel, a ledger, and the same thin Trees.

Ornath's situation is the wait with an exit that is not a Tree. Some people there still wait. Some have stopped saying the Mother's name, and have not started keeping a list either.

A pot in a yard, a young failure, or nothing yet: any of the three greens can hold the sick stick for a night. The fight about *which* green would receive a scion is the cluster's civic life. Naming the hearths did not end that fight.

## The week at the ford

Harrow's has a Tree, and the square down the slope is Hands. This year's ten-year-olds still walk. The cluster is Kept-heavy and Struck-heavy as weather, not as a closed age. A household can split on a shorter road than that: one child goes to Harrow's, one stays Kept at the ford, and a sibling will not walk the same side of the road.

There is no stone on the waiting side. There is a **cup**, and the night before the neighbour's week someone leaves it on the ford-rock. If it fills, they send, and if it does not, the tellings do not agree. The child still goes, or does not, according to the kitchen and not according to the cup. Brenod calls the cup weather, Vaelun calls it the land answering, and Ornath has started leaving it empty on purpose, or has not.

## People a visitor must find

Three kitchens, and a person in each.

- **Tora** *(TOR-ah)* of Brenod is the one who sends. They walk a neighbour-fall home when they can. They are at [[Harrow's Green]] when the stone is to be moved, because the next column still drinks there.
- **Nethiro** *(neth-EE-ro)* of Vaelun is a Door-Keeper's cousin. They ask about the bowl before they ask about a leave, and they may refuse the neighbour's week on purpose. They walk to [[The First Bowl]] when the bowl is set.
- **Baelon** *(BAY-lon)* of Ornath has not taken the slate-road, and has looked at it. The cup this kitchen leaves empty on purpose, or does not, is Ornath's.

There are also families who walk one child and not another, a Kin-speaker who will stand for a Struck the square will not, and someone who owns a knife they will not call nameless.

The Seat's leave still says *the three hamlets past the ford.* Clerks who learn Brenod, Vaelun, and Ornath have learned more than the minute wanted them to.

## What can enter a scene

- **The cup on the ford-rock.** It is the night before the week. If the cup fills, they send. If it does not, the child still goes, or does not, according to the kitchen.
- **A ford in flood.** The canopy is a rumour, and the week is still a date. Someone has to decide without seeing Harrow's.
- **A folk-right slip that took.** Which green receives it is still undecided, and the fight about *which* is the civic life.
- **Tora at Harrow's, needing a witness** who will walk back. If Haelin will not, the child is seen at the canopy and unvouched at breakfast.

## Three answers at the water

*Said at the ford, not written, in a Brenod voice, a Vaelun voice, and an Ornath voice, over the same water.*

> *We can see it. That is not the same as having it.*
>
> *The land had them before the cart came. I will not walk a child to a neighbour to be named a Waited.*
>
> *If the cup stays dry I still have to decide. The slate the other way does not ask about soil.*

The voices disagree, and nobody settles it. At dawn someone still decides whether to lift the cup, wake the child, and take the Harrow road.

## GM Notes

- Vaelun's work is host-rights, not a second Harrow, so do not clone the live-front Tree-town onto an old plot. [[The Door-Keepers]] play here. Motherfaith neighbours in Brenod will call Vaelun's Kept children passed-over, and Vaelun will not correct them in the Mother's language.

- **Do not decide which green has the sick stick until a session needs it.** The Seat's refusal to use the hearth-names is on purpose, so keep that refusal. Do not make Ornath the "secular hamlet" as a hat, do not explain the cup, and use the clerks' learning of the three names as a hook.
- **They are not living in the past.** A hamlet that sends this year's ten-year-olds to Harrow's is doing the present. Narrating the cluster as a Brenvaeth re-enactment is the stacked-ages error.
- **Cause.** They wait because the wood is slow, the road is long, and the list was written in the Motherwood, not because they were unworthy.
- **Fate-pressure, noted and not rolled.** The pressure is precarious, a single dependency on Harrow's week or on a pot. Do not wipe the cluster to make the queue feel real. The pressure is the play.
- **A seam, not a planet of hats.** All four voices can sit in one week: Saelvaeth's luck, Vaelhesk's refusal, Vaethorn's guilt, and Ornled's slate. Do not assign one polity to each hamlet as a costume. Brenod leans toward sending, Vaelun leans toward refusal, and Ornath leans toward the other road. Those are leans, not flags. Wood that went past them to [[The First Bowl]] is a slight, not a fourth kitchen.
- **Hearths, not Kind-quarters.** Taken-In ground in Vaelun is an old plot, not a leshy village. All three hearths are mixed Kinds.
- **Habit.** There is one habit among the three, the cup on the ford-rock, and the count is full. Do not explain the cup, and do not add a person.
- **Hooks.** A borrowed Turning whose Harrow-witness will not walk back; a folk-right slip that took; a first meal a Road-hand ate or refused; a Kept child asked what they lacked; an unvouched Struck and a congregation deciding; the stone in Harrow's square climbed by a hamlet child; the cup; a clerk who writes Brenod on a leave and starts a fight about which green; Ornath walking toward [[Sanbreo]].
- **Opening.** [[Taeren]] is a Brenod person this Hale-month, not a fourth kitchen-speaker, and Tora still wants the walk. Do not make Tora a Phoenix plotter. The kit is [[The Opening]].
