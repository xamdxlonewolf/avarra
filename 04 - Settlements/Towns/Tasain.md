# Tasain

> **Lirorn's walled town.** *(tah-SAYN.)* Below the Shelf-gate, where the west road comes down and the levy is taken. People stay the winter here. It is not a great city. It is not a second [[Maiethlir]].

## Overview

Tasain is a walled town in a sheltered valley of [[Lirorn]], below [[Named Ground|the Shelf-gate]]. The west road comes down through the valley. The levy is taken at the town gate. People who cannot keep the high road through a winter sleep here.

Villages and hamlets stay on the shelves and at the passes. This note does not name them. [[Named Ground|the Noon Pass]] is the old high notch. A water-line sits on its stone. It stays a pass. It is not a town, and it is not this one.

The Shelf-gate above the valley is the road [[Narol of the Pass]] took after [[The Thaw-Break]], when the old stone sat under the water. Narol is dead. The house still levies. The person does not. Break-rate is still asked in a kind year. A lowlander is not given a sermon with the coin.

[[Tengu]] hearths and Fox-of-the-Snows hearths are densest on the high snow. Mixed Kinds live in the valley. The office at the gate is the levy. A sky-courier who hears a thaw first is doing a job. The hearing is not a people.

A Hand stands in the valley, where a graft can take in shelter. The levy is reckoned on how much snow fell the winter before.

The town's daily work is taking the levy. Water the core will call a gift is taken here as a due. Devotion can still walk west once the gate has opened.

## The gate

The **town gate** is the wall in the valley, where a cart comes down off the pass and can wait. The Shelf-gate is the notch above. They are not the same gate. The Noon Pass is a third thing, higher and older, and it is not a seat.

[[Saelthael]]'s west road crosses by this pass. [[Larbril]] is the city where that road meets the Well-wash, on the dry side, short of the climb if you are walking west. Tasain is where you are when the road has come down into shelter and someone asks for the year's due.

## The work

The work is the **levy**, taken at the gate.

The levy is reckoned on last winter's snow. The household levies the thaw, the pass, and the high road. After Cut-year 233 the argument was which notch. The household that inherited Narol's toll still collects, and it carries no house-name. [[Maiethlir]] drinks the [[Named Ground|Core-thaw]] downstream and files a loud week as snowmelt. That filing is not a receipt for this gate.

A traveller at the gate meets a collector of that household. The collector has no name in this note and says the toll is Narol's. [[Narol of the Pass]] remains the name on the taking.

## One tension

The valley takes the levy in shelter. The shelves above pay in isolation for water the lowland files as a gift. A shelf whose graft sickens is a long way from a stiller. The town is mid-state enough to charge, and not a cathedral that furnishes every Tithe on a cliff. A toll that feels like weather feels like Cut-year 233 to anyone who was on the shelf when the stone went under.

## Habits

In a kind year, before the levy is taken, a bowl of shelf-snow is left on the gate-stone until it is water. Tasain will not say why.

## People

No new named person. The household levies. Narol does not.

- **[[Narol of the Pass]]** — dead. The name is still said at the toll, as Break-rate. Of the Pass. There is no house-name to put first. Lirorn would not be written as a people.
