# The Mill-hold

> The Mill-hold is a Hands-town whose Hand is standing in water. Everyday speech says *the Mill-hold*. Travellers who only saw the drain say *the culvert*. It is not [[Orenbren]]'s capital, and it is not Brenthael. The three corner seats stay [[Eolvaeth]] · [[Orentel]] · [[Maiethlir]]. The hold sits two days further out the Near Mile than Brenthael's boughs. This Eolthael the wood is unsound, and the children walk *in*, toward Brenthael.

## Overview

West of the Rain-Wall, on [[Maiethorn]]'s four-season core, a mill-town grew around a graft that took beside a race, so the town has bread and a Hand in the same water. For a generation they Turned at home. The Near Mile goes on past Brenthael and past this hold. Children here were told the road went to the Motherwood, and most Hands-children did not keep walking.

The culvert choked this Liren, the drain that keeps spring-water off the roots. The mill-race stayed full, and the Hand stood in it. [[Ecology of the Turning|Root blight]] is ordinary horticulture: waterlogged soil, crowded roots, and a drain someone assumed someone else cleared. It is not a curse, and it is not a sermon. It is this year.

[[Turning Tree|Unsound wood]] spends the week. A warden who lets a cohort stand under a Tree they know cannot Turn has made those children **unTurned**, and next summer is not a repair. The civic crisis is wearing a child's summer, which is the only summer the Given-door will open.

The town's daily work is meeting need, so the neighbour's week returns, the week of walking to someone else's Tree. Devotion belongs to people whose Hand still Speaks. Nobody books a blight.

## Site

The Near Mile, counted from the grove, puts [[The Third Hearth]] at three days, Brenthael one day further, and **the Mill-hold two days past Brenthael**. Six days out, still lodging-country, and still not a throne. [[Named Ground#Travel times|Fair weather]] is a promise the culvert did not keep.

A side-cut of living water feeds the race, and the same cut was supposed to take flood off the square. Settlement nucleated around the mill and the canopy together, organic rather than a grid. The oldest houses face the Tree. The mill-house and the race sit on the lower side, where the water wants to be. No walls stand here. The boundary is the last mill-share and the ranging.

Water for people is not the crisis. There is drink, and there is too much of it in the wrong place. Food is mill-grain, terrace gardens, and pigs on mast-rights older than the lodging-guild. Fuel is coppice the [[Two-Bodied]] also range. A travelling [[The Stilled|stiller]] is a Near-Mile luck, not a resident of the hold.

## The square (not a district grid)

- **The Tree at the centre** — mature enough that grandparents Turned under it, and young enough, for the Motherland, that someone still remembers the year the road stopped being required. The leaves this Hale-month are late, and a little colourless at the edges. Talen will not Speak a fall here. That is the job.
- **The culvert** — brick and timber under the square, older than the mill-house's present roof. Climbing-scuff marks the lip, from children who have heard a stone-story. There are shovels, and a smell of wet bark. Mill-foam holds on the lip a day after the race is drawn down.
- **The mill-race** — the town's bread and its name. Opening the culvert to save the Hand lowers the race. Harvest is not theoretical.
- **The neighbour-slate** — chalked on the mill-door, not on a warden-lath. It carries the names of this year's ten-year-olds, and which Brenthael household has room. The verso is Meirim's marks, when they arrive. The recto is Talen's.
- **The upper room** — used to sleep the extra mile when people were walking in toward the grove. This Hale-month it sleeps the necessity-column going the same way, for a different reason. It is not a merchant-loft yet.
- **The greens** — potted, and hauled up off the wet. There is no lush lot. The Taken-In here are neighbours. Milsun's planting sits above the race on purpose.
- **The ranging** — at the edge, not a quarter. Mixed Kinds work it, and no Kind-flag hangs on the mill.
- **The far shed** — Returned work, downwind of the drink and the grain. There is no infirmary.

Homes accommodate becomings the way [[Daily Life]] already said, so a house has room for what someone in it is becoming. Mixed Kinds live here. A Fox of the Wilds at the ranging is unremarkable.

Tithe-provision, what a person needs in order to stay whole, is core-warm and suddenly thin. The greens still work. The Hand does not. You can be too poor to walk a child two days and still be a Hands-town in every way but this week's.

## What the square still does

This is Maiethorn, Hands-country, where the town Turned its children at home and most of them did not keep walking past the boughs. The Mile still goes on. This year the wood is sick, so the children walk to Brenthael, and a Road-hand is here with earth rather than with a colour. A Hand can fail. That is weather, not the end of the age.

The Seat will call thin soil, the mill will call a race, and Talen will call a date in Eolthael. All three are looking at the same square.

Mill-foam on the drain-lip keeps last Eolthael's colour a day longer than the water does. Milsun will not say why. Talen moves the foam with a stick, and Thurrei wipes it off a pot-wrap and does not write it down.

## People & Powers

The talk in the square is about the **drain and the slate**.

- **Talen** *(TAH-len)* — town-warden. Speaks the conservative way, not a Kind, so the office is a practice and not a people Talen was born. Talen is practice-first, will not stand a child under unsound wood, and wants the mill to keep turning through Harvest. The same water that has to come off the roots is the town's bread. The document of this square is the **neighbour-slate**, not a notice about a stone.
- **Milsun** *(MIL-sun)* — [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]], on the drain and the planting. Milsun has been saying the culvert would choke since last Tally, and wants the mill-family to stop taking the Tree's turn as if plenty were a hymn. A sister's child is on this week's slate. Milsun will not follow you home. Home is wet if the Hand dies, and dry if the race does.
- **Thurrei** *(thur-RAY)* — [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hand]], here for the wood. Thurrei brings living earth and the watering-words, and a report to the Seat if the scion sickens, and will not cut a second without leave. The queue that would replace this Hand is the same queue that has not yet given [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] a first Tree. Thurrei knows that sentence and will not say *queue*. What Thurrei wants is one night when no one asks about the hamlets.
- **Meirim** of Brenthael — the neighbour-host. Meirim wants the Third Hearth's stone down for an extra-mile crowd, and now has a necessity-column walking in from the other side. Same square, two crowds. Meirim is not this town's warden.
- **The square that used to watch children Turn** — this year the witness has to walk grove-ward and come back. Vouching-at-a-distance on the Motherland is the tell that Ornthael is unfinished even here, the years when Turning at home was supposed to be enough.
- **Orenbren lodging-custom** — hospitality kept as rent in Eolthael. A Hands-town that sends its children inward looks, from a bed-guild, like failure. Talen hears a date.

A miller still takes a share of the grain ([[Craft Fellowships]]). The square does not turn on that miller unless you ask whose race was kept full.

## What can enter a scene

What follows is not the weather told in a paragraph. It is a thing that arrives while someone is already talking.

- **A bough coming down.** The wood is wet, the Hale-month crowd is under it, and someone is already standing there.
- **The culvert at first light.** Milsun digs, the race drops, and a mill-hand is already there with a count of the turns the mill lost.
- **A slate with one name too many.** Brenthael's verso says the beds are full, and a child is still ten this week. The Given-door is still one week.
- **A child already in the square at dawn**, dressed for a fall Talen will not Speak. Unsound wood does not become a hug because someone is hopeful.
- **Thurrei's pot in the upper room.** Earth-smell. A second cut would be a fight with the leave, not a kindness.

Talen is doing a week, and will not file a blight. Milsun will read the slate as a slight on the drain. Thurrei will already have walked the roots.

## GM Notes

- **This is the sick-Tree square.** A Hand can fail, and the necessity-walk returns. Do not narrate Ornthael as over because one culvert choked. The civic crisis is a child's summer being spent. Do not make the Tree cursed, do not make it a dying faith, and do not make it a last-tree-of-a-people. The horticulture is a choked culvert, root blight, unsound wood, and one week.
- **Not Ornsael.** The physical problem here is the mill-race against the roots, plenty of drink and the wrong water at the Hand. The institutional answer is a neighbour-slate. The cast is the drain and the slate. The mystery is foam that holds last year's colour. Ornsael keeps the well, the share, the wet knot, and the west-road.
- **Not Brenthael.** Brenthael's Hand is sound, and Meirim hosts. Do not sicken the Third Hearth's neighbour in order to tidy a map. The lead road-end is [[Nelath]], a spur that stops. A road ending at the boughs is texture here, because the Mile still goes grove-ward. Do not relabel this hold into that town.
- **Not the Grey Summer again.** Grafts went unwatered in Cut-year 171 because the tenders died. This year is a drain. The people are alive, and they are arguing.
- **Queue parallax.** A replacement scion for a sick Hands-Tree and a first Tree for the hamlets are the same carrying-earth. Do not let Thurrei solve that, and do not convert the Seat.
- **Keystone:** thin, sick, or few grafts stay the player-facing cause. Do not let the foam assert the mind. Do not date the Tree. Do not pick a cutter.
- **The town's daily work.** The work is necessity. Do not make necessity, devotion, and prestige all the town's theme. Pair the square with [[Colourless Host]] only if someone insists on standing a circle under a fallen colourless bough. The living Hand is not that adversary. Dead wood is not a Tree. An extra-mile ribbon in the upper room is texture, not a second theme.
- **Fate-pressure (noted, not rolled):** the hold is precarious, a single neighbour's week. Do not wipe the town to make the blight feel real.
- **Names.** Seed `20260901`, algorithm v1, conservative person-register; pick from the middle: Milsun (pos. 8), Talen (pos. 13), Thurrei (pos. 14). Common-tongue place-name, and no new liturgy.
- **Hooks.** A child dressed for a fall, Brenthael too full, Milsun's kin on the slate, Thurrei asked for a second cut, mill-foam on the lip, a session paired with [[Harrow's Green]] so both squares are visible and the two scarcities are not the same, and a session paired with [[The First Bowl]] so a replacement-queue and a guest-meal are not the same fight.
