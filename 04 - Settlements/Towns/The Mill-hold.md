# The Mill-hold

> **A Hands-town whose Hand is standing in water.** Everyday *the Mill-hold*; travellers who only saw the drain say *the culvert*. Not [[Orenbren]]'s capital. Not Brenthael. The three corner seats stay [[Eolvaeth]] · [[Orentel]] · [[Maiethlir]]. Two days further out the Near Mile than Brenthael's boughs. This Eolthael the wood is unsound. The children walk *in*.

## Overview

West of the Wall, on [[Maiethorn]]'s four-season core, a mill-town grew around a graft that took beside a race. Bread and a Hand in the same water. For a generation they Turned at home. The pilgrim-way ended at their boughs the way it ends at Brenthael's: children were told the road went to the Motherwood, and most Hands-children did not keep walking.

This Liren the culvert that keeps spring-water off the roots choked. The mill-race stayed full. The Hand stood in it. [[Ecology of the Turning|Root blight]] is ordinary horticulture: waterlogged soil, crowded roots, a drain someone assumed someone else cleared. It is not a curse and not a sermon. It is this year.

[[Turning Tree|Unsound wood]] spends the week. A warden who lets a cohort stand under a Tree they know cannot Turn has made those children **unTurned**. Next summer is not a repair. The civic crisis is wearing a child's summer, which is the only summer the Given-door will open.

**Leftover job on this street: necessity.** The neighbour's week *returns*. Devotion belongs to people whose Hand still Speaks. Prestige does not book a blight. Do not make all three the town's theme.

## Site

The Near Mile counted from the grove: [[The Third Hearth]] at three days, Brenthael one day further, **the Mill-hold two days past Brenthael**. Six days out. Still lodging-country. Still not a throne. [[Named Ground#Travel times|Fair weather]] is a promise the culvert did not keep.

A side-cut of living water feeds the race; the same cut was supposed to take flood off the square. Settlement nucleated around mill and canopy together — organic, not a grid. Oldest lintels face the Tree; the mill-house and the race sit on the lower side, where water wants to be. No walls. The boundary is the last mill-share and the ranging.

Water for people is not the crisis. There is drink. There is too much of it *in the wrong place*. Food is mill-grain, terrace gardens, pigs on mast-rights older than the lodging-guild. Fuel is coppice the [[Two-Bodied]] also range. A travelling [[The Stilled|stiller]] is a Near-Mile luck, not a resident.

## The square (not a district grid)

- **The Tree at the centre** — mature enough that grandparents Turned under it. Young enough, for the Motherland, that someone still remembers the year the road stopped being required. Leaves this Hale-month are late and a little colourless at the edges. Talen will not Speak a fall here. That is the job.
- **The culvert** — brick and timber under the square, older than the mill-house's present roof. Climbing-scuff on the lip from children who have heard a stone-story. Shovels. A smell of wet bark. Mill-foam holds on the lip a day after the race is drawn down.
- **The mill-race** — Clock 1 as bread. The town's name. Opening the culvert to save the Hand lowers the race. Harvest is not theoretical.
- **The neighbour-slate** — chalked on the mill-door, not on a warden-lath. Names of this year's ten-year-olds, and which Brenthael mouth has room. Verso is Meirim's marks, when they arrive. Recto is Talen's.
- **The upper room** — used to sleep the extra mile going *in*. This Hale-month it sleeps the necessity-column going the same way, for a different reason. Not a merchant-loft yet. Not a third theme.
- **The greens** — potted, hauled off the wet. No lush lot. The Taken-In here are neighbours. Milsun's planting sits above the race on purpose.
- **The ranging** — at the edge, not a quarter. Mixed Kinds. No Kind-flag on the mill.
- **The far shed** — Returned work downwind of drink and grain. No infirmary.

Homes accommodate becomings the way [[Daily Life]] already said. Mixed Kinds. A Fox of the Wilds at the ranging is unremarkable. Do not grow Kind-quarters.

Tithe-provision is core-warm and suddenly thin: the greens still work; the Hand does not. You can be too poor to walk a child two days and still be a Hands-town in every mouth but this week's.

## The leftover, visible

**Clock 1:** this is Maiethorn. Hands-country. They Turned at home. The road ended at the boughs.

**Clock 2:** the wood is sick; the children walk to Brenthael; a Road-hand is here with earth, not with a colour. Hands can un-Hands. That is weather, not a fall of the age. Do not narrate Ornthael as over because one culvert choked.

The Seat will call thin soil. The mill will call a race. Talen will call a date in Eolthael. All three are looking at the same square.

**Inscrutable, on purpose:** mill-foam on the drain-lip holds last Eolthael's colour a day longer than the water. Milsun will not explain. Talen moves the foam with a stick. Thurrei wipes it off a pot-wrap and does not write it.

## People & Powers

No Epic-8 pivot lives here. The square talks as a **drain and a slate**:

- **Talen** *(TAH-len)* — town-warden. Conservative mouth, not Kind. Practice-first. Will not stand a child under unsound wood. Wants the mill to still turn through Harvest; the same water they must let off the roots is bread. The document of this square is the **neighbour-slate**, not a notice about a stone.
- **Milsun** *(MIL-sun)* — [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]]. Drain and planting. Has been saying the culvert would choke since last Tally. Wants the mill-family to stop taking the Tree's turn as if plenty were a hymn. Has a sister's child on this week's slate. Will not follow you home. Home is wet if the Hand dies and dry if the race does.
- **Thurrei** *(thur-RAY)* — [[The Tree-Wardens|Road-hand]]. Here for the wood. Living earth, watering-words, a report to the Seat if the scion sickens. Will not cut a second without leave. The queue that would *replace* this Hand is the same queue that has not yet given [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] a first Tree. Thurrei knows that sentence and will not say *queue*. They want one night no one asks them about the hamlets.
- **Meirim** of Brenthael — the neighbour-host. Wants the Third Hearth's stone down for an extra-mile crowd, and now has a necessity-column walking *in* from the other side. Same square, two leftover jobs. They are not this town's warden.
- **The square that used to watch children Turn** — this year the witness has to walk grove-ward and back. Vouching-at-a-distance on the Motherland is the tell that Ornthael is unfinished even here.
- **Orenbren lodging-custom** — hospitality as rent in Eolthael. A Hands-town sending children *in* looks, from a bed-guild, like failure. Talen hears a date.

A miller still takes a share of grain. They are not the mouth this square turns on unless you ask whose race was kept full.

## What can enter a scene

Not weather in a paragraph. A thing that arrives while someone is talking.

- **A bough coming down.** Wet wood. Hale-month crowd. Someone is already under it.
- **The culvert at first light.** Milsun digs. The race drops. A mill-hand is already there with a count of lost turns.
- **A slate with one name too many.** Brenthael's verso says full. A child is still ten this week. The Given-door is still one week.
- **A child already in the square at dawn**, dressed for a fall Talen will not Speak. Unsound wood does not become a hug because someone is hopeful.
- **Thurrei's pot in the upper room.** Earth-smell. A second cut would be a fight with the leave, not a kindness.

Pair with [[Colourless Host]] only if someone insists on standing a circle under a fallen colourless bough. The living Hand is not that adversary. Dead wood is not a Tree.

## GM Notes

- **This is the sick-Tree square.** Hands un-Hands. Necessity-walk *returns*. Civic crisis wearing a child's summer. Do not make it a cursed Tree, a dying faith, or a last-tree-of-a-people. Horticulture: a choked culvert, root blight, unsound wood, one week.
- **Not Ornsael.** Physical problem = mill-race vs roots (plenty of drink, wrong water at the Hand). Institutional response = a neighbour-slate. Cast = drain-and-slate. Mystery = foam that holds last year's colour. Ornsael keeps the well, the share, the wet knot, the west-road.
- **Not Brenthael.** Brenthael's Hand is sound. Meirim hosts. Do not sicken the Third Hearth's neighbour to tidy a map. The lead road-end type stays unused; road-ending-at-boughs is Clock 1 texture here, not a second seated type.
- **Not the Grey Summer again.** Grafts went unwatered in C.Y. 171 because tenders died. This is a drain. People are alive and arguing.
- **Queue parallax.** A replacement scion for a sick Hands-Tree and a first Tree for the hamlets are the same carrying-earth. Do not let Thurrei solve that. Do not convert the Seat.
- **Keystone:** thin/sick/few grafts stay the player-facing cause. Do not let the foam assert the mind. Do not date the Tree. Do not pick a cutter.
- **One leftover job.** Necessity. An extra-mile ribbon in the upper room is texture, not a second theme.
- **Fate-pressure (noted, not rolled):** precarious — single neighbour's week. Do not wipe the town to make blight feel real.
- **Names.** Seed `20260901`, algorithm v1, conservative person-register; pick from the middle: Milsun (pos. 8), Talen (pos. 13), Thurrei (pos. 14). Common-tongue place-name; no new liturgy.
- **Hooks.** A child dressed for a fall; Brenthael too full; Milsun's kin on the slate; Thurrei asked for a second cut; mill-foam; pairing a session with [[Harrow's Green]] so both clocks are visible and the leftovers are not the same scarcity; pairing with [[The First Bowl]] so a replacement-queue and a guest-meal are not the same fight.
