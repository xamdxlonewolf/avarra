# Sanbreo

> Sanbreo *(san-BREE-oh.)* is Ornled's town, the place where the slate is kept and where a beach-fee can be paid when a hull arrives. People say **the Outer Ledger** and **Outer folk**, while slates and learned maps write *Ornled*. The town is the seat, standing on the brink of a small city, and it is still a town.

## Overview

Sanbreo is a town on [[Heskoren]]'s slate-shore, the west coast, in [[Ornled]]. Its beach faces west, toward the last capes and the storm-wall beyond them. The frontier coast that faces [[Named Ground|the West Water]] is on the east side of Heskoren, so a hull that wants Sanbreo's beach has to come round the capes. This town is the pocket that kept the slate, and the beach where a hull can pay.

The roofs have filled the pocket until the town stands on the brink of a small city. A walk that used to pass through here left stones and upper rooms, and people still sleep in them. The brink did not grow a congregation, and it did not grow a counted hall. Custom outruns law, because there is almost no law and almost no hymn.

Villages and hamlets in the pockets around Sanbreo stay unnamed, and they are not this seat. They share the same thin grafts, the same barter, and the same absence.

[[Netstrand]] can quote a far crossing at [[Braetu]]. The fee here is a beach, not that quote. [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] may bless a landing, and may not levy it. Ornled was written into the Hush as a beach-fee, and they were not asked. They take the fee anyway, because a hull that wants the beach is a hull, and a hymn is not what they sell.

The town's daily work is the slate. It records the beach-fees, and it records the town's refusal to wait with a hymn. The work is necessity, not devotion, and it uses the same stones the old walk left behind.

## The gate

The **shore gate** is where a pocket-road meets the beach. A hull that wants the beach comes to it, and a neighbour from a pocket comes to it, and the pockets stay unnamed. The last capes lie further out, toward the storm-wall. This note does not name them. [[Eolvaeth]] is a vale behind the frontier coast, in the waiting-gospel, and it is not this gate.

## The work

The work is the **slate**, kept on the wall inside the gate.

The first line is still the sentence **Vathne** cut, after the Thaw-Break, when a graft pot the town had waited for drowned in a flood that came from someone else's thaw. The line says the town will not wait with a hymn for a stick that drowned. The wood of the wall is newer than the words, because someone recopied the line onto it. Vathne cut that line, and Vathne is not a person you meet here. The line is what the town keeps.

When a hull arrives, the beach-fee is cut on the same slate. Barter, favor, and the wall are the Tithe, what a person puts up so they do not slide. An unvouched Struck, someone with no witness to stand for them, is met with a rope, a deal, or a neighbour. There is no blessing queued behind the fee.

A hull deals with whoever is on the shore gate that day, and that person cuts the fee on the slate. This note does not name them.

## One tension

A hull can pay, and a neighbour who cannot barter a Tithe still slides. The roofs have filled the pocket until the town stands on the brink of a small city, and the roofs did not become a net. Vaethorn hears godlessness in the missing hymn, and the wall hears a luxury that died in a cart. The loneliest unvouched on this coast are next door to a gospel, in a town where no one is waiting with them.

## Habits

When a hull has paid, the coin stays on the slate's sill until the hull is gone. Sanbreo will not say why.

## People

No new named person stands on this gate. The wall keeps the sentence, and whoever is on the shore gate cuts the fee. A neighbour does not introduce a keeper of the slate.

Vathne stays the line on the slate. There is no house to put first, and the Outer Ledger does not keep a person-list.
