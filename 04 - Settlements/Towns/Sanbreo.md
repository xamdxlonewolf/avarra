# Sanbreo

> **Ornled's town.** *(san-BREE-oh.)* People say **the Outer Ledger** and **Outer folk**. Slates and learned maps write *Ornled*. This is where the slate is kept. A beach-fee can be paid here when a hull arrives. The town is the seat. It stands on the brink of a small city, and it is still a town.

## Overview

Sanbreo is a town on [[Heskoren]]'s slate-shore, in [[Ornled]]. The frontier coast faces [[Named Ground|the West Water]]. Beyond the last capes is the storm-wall. This town is the pocket that kept the slate, and the beach where a hull can pay.

It is on the brink of a small city. The roofs have filled the pocket. A walk that used to pass left stones and upper rooms, and people still sleep in them. The brink did not grow a congregation or a counted hall. Custom outruns law because there is almost no law and almost no hymn.

Villages and hamlets in the pockets around Sanbreo stay unnamed. They are not this seat. They share the same thin grafts, the same barter, and the same absence.

[[Netstrand]] can quote a far crossing at [[Braetu]]. The fee here is a beach, not that quote. [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] may bless a landing and may not levy it. Ornled was written into the Hush as a beach-fee. They were not asked. They take the fee anyway, because a hull that wants the beach is a hull, and a hymn is not what they sell.

**Leftover job on this street: the slate.** Necessity, not devotion. The same stones a walk used. The job that stayed is a line on a wall.

## The gate

The **shore gate** is where a pocket-road meets the beach. A hull that wants the beach comes to it. A neighbour from a pocket comes to it. The pockets stay unnamed.

The last capes lie further out, toward the storm-wall. This note does not name them. [[Eolvaeth]] is a vale behind the frontier coast, in the waiting-gospel. It is not this gate.

## The work

The work is the **slate**, kept on the wall inside the gate.

The first line is still the sentence **Vathne** cut, after a waited pot drowned in someone else's thaw: the town will not wait with a hymn for a stick that drowned. The wood of the wall is newer than the words. Someone recopied the line onto it. Vathne is that line. Vathne is not a mouth in the room.

When a hull arrives, the beach-fee is cut on the same slate. Barter, favor, and the wall are the Tithe. An unvouched Struck is met with a rope, a deal, or a neighbour. There is no blessing queued behind the fee.

No new person is seated here.

## One tension

A hull can pay, and a neighbour who cannot barter a Tithe still slides. The roofs have filled the pocket until the town stands on the brink of a small city. The roofs did not become a net. Vaethorn hears godlessness in the missing hymn. The wall hears a luxury that died in a cart. The loneliest unvouched on this coast are next door to a gospel, in a town where no one is waiting with them.

## Habits

When a hull has paid, the coin stays on the slate's sill until the hull is gone. Sanbreo will not say why.

## People

No new person. The wall keeps the sentence. A neighbour does not introduce a keeper of it.

Vathne stays the line on the slate. There is no house to put first. The Outer Ledger does not keep a person-list.
