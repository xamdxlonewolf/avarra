# Natai

> Natai *(NAH-tye.)* is Saelvaeth's town, the place where the march-voice stands. People say **the Sown Waiting** and **March-folk**, while charters and learned maps write *Saelvaeth*. [[Harrow's Green]] stays in the orbit of that march, and it is not this seat.

## Overview

Natai is a town on [[Heskoren]]'s moving edge, in [[Saelvaeth]]. It lies west of [[Harrow's Green]] on the live-front roads, between Harrow's Green and [[Sanbreo]], and not on the ford. This note does not record the days to either place. Towns on this march form where a graft has taken, a Tree set in the ground that caught, and one has taken here. The canopy is a town's Hand, young enough that the neighbours still argue the queue of which town gets a pot, and it is not the luck at [[Harrow's Green]].

Other towns where a graft has taken stay unnamed, and so do the hamlets and waiting clusters between them. They can see a neighbour's canopy, and this note does not name them.

[[Harrow's Green]] stays the live-front square it already is, with the neighbour's canopy, Haelin's stone, and the upper room. It is not the march's seat, and it is not a capital. [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] stay the three hamlets, so Brenod, Vaelun, and Ornath stay their kitchens, and the ford stays the ford.

[[The Hinge Hush|The Hush]] lets this march take a pot without a Vaethorn waiting-due. The due that remains is local. Someone has to stand where the queue can be shouted at, and that is why this town has the office. Natai's office takes the complaints about the queue for grafts, which towns get a pot and when. The neighbour's week of need still happens at Harrow's. What sits here is the blame.

## The gate

The **town gate** stands on the road between grafts. A pot comes in by it, and a complaint comes in by it, and people come to it from a waiting cluster that this note leaves unnamed. The gate is not Harrow's square, and it is not the ford. The voice stands at the gate.

## The work

The work is the **march-voice**, the office that answers for the queue.

[[Dumu]] stands it. A neighbour says Dumu, and a stranger hears Dumu of Natai. There is no house to put first, because the Sown Waiting does not keep a person-list. The office did not exist when the first pots came. The Seat's ruling about who gets a pot has no one to answer for it, and the office is that person. Dumu hears the complaint and answers for the queue to whoever is angry. Dumu cannot decide where a pot goes, and cannot move the wood into the next pocket.

Haelin keeps Harrow's square, Tora of Brenod keeps the walk from the ford, and Dumu keeps this gate.

## One tension

The town can be blamed for a queue it did not write. Harrow's has the canopy the hamlets can see, and the hamlets send this year's children anyway. Natai has the person to blame. Dumu can answer, and cannot move the wood into the next pocket. The town has enough state to be blamed, and not enough state to be a kingdom of the luck.

## Habits

While a complaint is said, [[Dumu]] leaves the gate-latch on the post, and puts it back in the catch when the speaker turns. They will not say why.

## People

One person stands here, not a court, not Harrow's warden, and not a hamlet kitchen.

- **[[Dumu]]** — stands the march-voice, and can be blamed for the queue.
