## Overview

Natai is a town on [[Heskoren]]'s moving edge, in [[Saelvaeth]]. It lies west of [[Harrow's Green]] on the live-front roads, between Harrow's Green and [[Sanbreo]], and not on the ford. This note does not record the days to either. Towns on this march form where a graft has taken. One has taken here. The canopy is a town's Hand, young enough that the neighbours still argue the queue, and it is not the luck at [[Harrow's Green]].

Other towns where a graft has taken stay unnamed. Hamlets and waiting clusters between them stay unnamed. They can see a neighbour's canopy. This note does not name them.

[[Harrow's Green]] stays the live-front square it already is: the neighbour's canopy, Haelin's stone, the upper room. It is not the march's seat, and it is not a capital. [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] stay the three hamlets. Brenod, Vaelun, and Ornath stay their kitchens. The ford stays the ford.

The Hush lets this march take a pot without a Vaethorn waiting-due. The due that remains is local. Someone has to stand where the queue can be shouted at, and that is why this town has the office.

Natai's office takes complaints about the queue for grafts: which towns get a pot, and when. The neighbour's week of need still happens at Harrow's. What sits here is the blame.

## The gate

The **town gate** is on the road between grafts. A pot comes in by it. A complaint comes in by it. People come from a waiting cluster. The cluster stays unnamed.

The gate is not Harrow's square, and it is not the ford. The voice stands at the gate.

## The work

The work is the **march-voice**, the office that answers for the queue.

[[Dumu]] stands it. A neighbour says Dumu. A stranger hears Dumu of Natai. There is no house to put first. The Sown Waiting does not keep a person-list. The office did not exist when the first pots came. The Seat's ruling about who gets a pot has no one to answer for it, and the office is that person. Dumu hears the complaint and answers for the queue to whoever is angry. Dumu cannot decide where a pot goes and cannot move the wood into the next pocket.

Haelin keeps Harrow's square. Tora of Brenod keeps the walk from the ford. Dumu keeps this gate.

## One tension

The town can be blamed for a queue it did not write. Harrow's has the canopy the hamlets can see. The hamlets send this year's children anyway. Natai has the person to blame. Dumu can answer, and cannot move the wood into the next pocket. Enough state to be blamed. Not enough state to be a kingdom of the luck.

## Habits

While a complaint is said, [[Dumu]] leaves the gate-latch on the post, and puts it back in the catch when the speaker turns. They will not say why.

## People

One person. Not a court. Not Harrow's warden. Not a hamlet kitchen.

- **[[Dumu]]** — stands the march-voice, and can be blamed for the queue.
