# Harrow's Green

> **A live-front Tree-town.** The luckiest kind of [[Heskoren]] square: a scion that arrived in the C.Y. 200s, spoke a generation later, and has now Turned local children for a few generations. The neighbour's canopy. The Harrow-sentence — *we Turned at Harrow's Tree before we had our own* — is how new towns talk everywhere ([[Turning Tree]]); here it is the decade. Seeded in [[The Years of Hands]] for [[Roadmap|Epic 7]]. Do not flesh districts here.

> **Canon status.** 🟡 **Named stub.** The name was already a Seat sentence ([[The Tree-Wardens|Cutting-leave]]). Everyday *Harrow's green*; liturgical unknown on purpose. Do not rename to pick a hearth. Do not make it a capital. Do not give it the next scion as a reward.

## Overview

A grafted pocket on the Sundered Reach. Settlement clustered where the wood took, as [[Heskoren]] already says civilization here *is* a map of surviving Trees. Harrow's is old enough, for the front, to be a town: a canopy at the centre, a square that used to be a road, borrowed Turnings inbound from [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] every Eolthael.

It is not the end of the wave. It is a Hand that grew up. The three hamlets can see the green from the ford. The Seat has already written that the wood will take *here* and not, this year, *there.* Harrow's lives inside that sentence whether it asked to or not.

[[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] heat sits on many hearths as gospel, not always as flag — this square lives in [[Saelvaeth]]'s orbit (the live-front march), not as its capital. [[The Old Ways]] are neighbours, not a museum. A first meal at a later planting is a live argument, not a courtesy-card.

## Districts & Sites

*Epic 7.* Until then, only what the residues require:

- **The Tree at the centre** — mature enough to Turn; young enough that grandparents remember the borrowed years.
- **A stone in the square** — last mile-shrine of the road that used to go further; climbing-scuff; water the Road-hands still use.
- **An upper room** — still opens for the neighbour's week; not yet a merchant-loft.

## People & Powers

No named cast (Epic 8). Offices that must exist: a town-warden (practice-first, not Kind); a square that watches children Turn; Road-hands who sleep here on the way to a town that was not chosen. The three hamlets' children are seasonal, not a district.

## GM Notes

- **Do not make Harrow's the "good town" that waited faithfully.** They were a paying-enough, soil-enough, chain-enough next on a list written in the Motherwood. Luck is horticulture plus the queue. Vaethorn will preach otherwise.
- **The Harrow-sentence is a tell.** When an NPC says it as nostalgia, they are Maiethorn. When they say it as this year, they are the front. When they say it as a slight (*they Turned at Harrow's because they had no Hand*), they are a hamlet.
- **Host-rights.** A later cut from Harrow's, acting on the origin's behalf, still wants the meal if the ground is an old green. Do not skip [[The Door-Keepers]] to make the town simpler.
- **Hooks.** The hamlets can see the canopy; a borrowed Turning whose witness has to walk home; a stone the warden wants moved before Leaf-Fall; a Road-hand sleeping here with a pot for someone else; a folk-right slip in a hamlet that took, and Harrow's warden asked to Speak it.
