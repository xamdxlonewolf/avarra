# Nelath

> **The road-end Tree-town.** *(NEL-ath.)* The walk stopped here. The square is the last mile. Mile-folk say **the spur** and mean the road, then use the town's name once they are in it. Not a capital of [[Orenbren]]. Not the [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]]. The three corner seats stay [[Eolvaeth]] · [[Orentel]] · [[Maiethlir]].

> Warden: *Raetoth*. Neighbour: *Lithsur*. This is not [[Harrow's Green]], not Brenthael, and not [[The Mill-hold]].

## Overview

West of the Rain-Wall, in [[Orenbren]] lodging-country, a graft took where a side-day of the Near Mile used to end. The main Mile still goes on to [[The Third Hearth]], Brenthael, and [[The Mill-hold]]. This track leaves it and does not rejoin. When Hands made the origin-walk optional, this town did not leave the feeling in a sentence. They brought the next stone in. The boughs closed what had been the day after.

[[The Years of Hands]] already says that many Hands-roads "end" at a canopy while a cart-track continues to a ford, a mill, or a neighbour. That sentence is true at Harrow's, and it is true at the Mill-hold, whose children can still walk grove-ward. It is not true here. Beyond these boughs there is no ford. There is a scar: a ditch, thorns, and the width of a road that is no longer a road.

The town's daily work is being the place where the walk stopped. The square is the last mile. A pilgrim may leave a ribbon. A family in need, if one comes, turns around and uses the Mile. Nobody books a spur that cannot be walked through.

## Site

The junction is on the Near Mile, **a day short of [[The Third Hearth]]** — about two days from [[Turning Tree|Thaeloren]] in fair weather ([[Named Ground#Travel times]]). From the junction the spur is **one day**, and then the canopy. You do not pass Thilim. You do not pass Meirim. You do not pass the culvert. [[The Low Wall]] is a different fold, closer to the wood, and has no Hand.

The graft was planted beside the old day-mark because living earth and a child want a drink. The drink for people is a **cistern** by the warden-hearth. The stone is not the well. Settlement nucleated around the canopy and then stopped, because there was nowhere further to put a house. Organic. The oldest houses face the Tree. The spur comes in on one side and becomes the square. No walls. The boundary is the last garden and the scar.

Food is the spur's gardens, a pig on a mast-right, and whatever a Mile-cart leaves when someone is willing to turn around. Fuel is coppice. A travelling [[The Stilled|stiller]] is a Near-Mile luck, not a resident. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] keep the green around the Hand. They are neighbours. They are not a nation.

## The square (not a district grid)

- **The Tree at the centre** — a mature Hand. Grandparents Turned under it. The road's requirement ended in living memory of the deathless and in stories for everyone else. The wood is sound. This is not a sick year. Leaf-Fall is held in the square because the square is the mile.
- **The stone** — the mile-shrine that used to stand a day further, now in the square. Flat top, climbing-scuff, a ribbon some years, a name cut in it. **Raetoth** *(RAY-toth)* will not read the name aloud. **Lithsur** *(LITH-sur)* will, and wants the stone back on the scar so the name is a day's mark and not a festival climb. It is not the day's drink. The cistern is the drink. A notch on the stone still holds a little rain after the square has dried. Raetoth calls that weather. Lithsur calls it the day.
- **The scar** — beyond the boughs. Thorn, ditch, and a line you can follow for a stone's throw before it closes. No cart-track. No neighbour's canopy. People who try it come back scratched and thirsty. The water-notch is on the stone, and the stone is here.
- **The upper room** — too many beds for the present trade. It slept the day that was pulled in. It is let, and it is not a bank. A name is cut over its door that no one reads aloud and no lodging-slate has copied. A Road-hand still asks which bed is the next day. Raetoth will not answer that question with a bed.
- **The cistern** — the town drinks at home. Not a mile-shrine. Not Seine's pot.
- **The greens** — around the Hand, enough, not a ticketed lot. They will not follow you home.
- **The ranging** — at the edge, not a quarter. Mixed Kinds. No Kind-flag.
- **The far shed** — Returned work downwind of the cistern. Not an infirmary.

Homes accommodate becomings the way [[Daily Life]] already said. Mixed Kinds live here.

Tithe-provision is core-warm and local. You can be too poor to stay whole here. The stopped road will not furnish you, and the Mile's beds are a day back at the junction plus the walk you already left.

## What the square still does

Children Turn at Nelath. The walk stopped, the square is the last mile, and the road ends at the boughs. Someone still walks the scar and will not call it closed. A Road-hand still treats the upper room as if a pot were going further. No Tree failed this year, and the wood is sound.

The Seat will call the requirement over. Raetoth will call the mile the square. Lithsur will call a name that used to be a day. All three are looking at one stone.

The rain-notch stays wet, and a name is cut over the door of the upper room. Nelath will not say why the notch stays wet, or whose name that is. A clerk who copies either one has already misunderstood the town.

## People & Powers

Two people are named here. The talk in the square is about the **stone and the scar**.

- **Raetoth** — town-warden. Speaks the conservative way, not a Kind. Practice-first. Speaks the fall at home. Wants the stone left in the square and the scar left closed. A neighbour says *Raetoth*. A stranger hears *Raetoth of Nelath*. There is no house-name. Orenbren witnesses who slept. It does not list a family.
- **Lithsur** — neighbour. Given name. Wants the stone back on the scar so the cut name is a day's walk again. Will bring a rope in Eolthael and will not call it a riot. Will not take a house-word to win the argument.
- **The upper room** — let. The name over its door is not a person you can hire.
- **A Road-hand passing the junction** — sometimes turns down the spur with earth or with nothing, sleeps upstairs, and asks for a road. They are not a chapter here.
- **Orenbren** — a compact of hearths. This is one of them. It is not the compact's crown. The [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]] is in the Motherwood, beside the country, not in this square.

Hugs are less common on a sound Motherland Hand than elsewhere, but not rare, and a missed week still hurts. A family that needs a neighbour's Tree walks back to the Mile. That walk is a repair, not this town's brand.

## What can enter a scene

- **A rope on the stone.** Eolthael crowd. Lithsur has the rope. Raetoth has the square. The Hand is still going to Speak. The argument does not get a second week.
- **Someone coming out of the scar.** Thorns, no water, a story that the ditch went further than it does. The cistern is the drink. The stone is not.
- **A Road-hand in the upper room** asking which bed is the next day. The name over the door has an answer nobody will read. The scar does not.
- **A child on the stone** asking how far it is to the Tree. They are already under it. The climb is not a map.

## In-world text

*Chalk on the cistern, Hale-month, Cut-year 387. Raetoth's hand. The name on the stone is not copied.*

> *The mile is the square. The scar is not a road. I will not put the stone back to make a day this town ended.*
>
> *Lithsur may say the name. They may not take the stone in order to say it. A rope is not a mile.*
>
> *Drink here. The notch on the stone is not the drink. The upper room has beds. The name over the door is not a slate. If you came for the Mile, you left it yesterday.*

The chalk shows that Raetoth will keep the stone in the square, and that Lithsur may say the name but may not take the stone to do it. Closing the road did not unsay the name. The Hand does not decide between them.
