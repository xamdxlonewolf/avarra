# Nelath

> Nelath *(NEL-ath.)* is the road-end Tree-town, the place where the walk stopped, so the square is the last mile. Mile-folk say **the spur** and mean the road, then use the town's name once they are in it. It is not a capital of [[Orenbren]], and it is not the [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]]. The three corner seats stay [[Eolvaeth]] · [[Orentel]] · [[Maiethlir]].

> The warden is *Raetoth*, and the neighbour who argues with that warden is *Lithsur*. This town is not [[Harrow's Green]], not Brenthael, and not [[The Mill-hold]].

## Overview

West of the Rain-Wall, in [[Orenbren]] lodging-country, a graft took where a side-day of the Near Mile used to end. The main Mile still goes on to [[The Third Hearth]], Brenthael, and [[The Mill-hold]]. This track leaves that road and does not rejoin it. When Hands made the origin-walk optional, this town did not leave the feeling in a sentence. They brought the next stone in. The boughs closed what had been the day after.

[[The Years of Hands]] already says that many Hands-roads "end" at a canopy while a cart-track continues to a ford, a mill, or a neighbour. That sentence is true at Harrow's, and it is true at the Mill-hold, whose children can still walk grove-ward. It is not true here. Beyond these boughs there is no ford. There is a scar: a ditch, thorns, and the width of a road that is no longer a road.

The town's daily work is being the place where the walk stopped, so the square is the last mile. A pilgrim may leave a ribbon. A family in need, if one comes, turns around and uses the Mile. Nobody books a spur that cannot be walked through.

## Site

The junction is on the Near Mile, **a day short of [[The Third Hearth]]** — about two days from [[Turning Tree|Thaeloren]] in fair weather ([[Named Ground#Travel times]]). From the junction the spur is **one day**, and then the canopy. You do not pass Thilim, or Meirim, or the culvert. [[The Low Wall]] is a different fold, closer to the wood, and has no Hand.

The graft was planted beside the old day-mark because living earth and a child want a drink. The drink for people is a **cistern** by the warden-hearth, and the stone is not the well. Settlement nucleated around the canopy and then stopped, because there was nowhere further to put a house, and the growth stayed organic. The oldest houses face the Tree, the spur comes in on one side and becomes the square, and no walls stand here. The boundary is the last garden and the scar.

Food is the spur's gardens, a pig on a mast-right, and whatever a Mile-cart leaves when someone is willing to turn around. Fuel is coppice. A travelling [[The Stilled|stiller]] is a Near-Mile luck, not a resident, and the [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] who keep the green around the Hand are neighbours, not a nation.

## The square (not a district grid)

- **The Tree at the centre** — a mature Hand, old enough that grandparents Turned under it. The road's requirement ended in the living memory of the deathless, and in stories for everyone else. The wood is sound. This is not a sick year. Leaf-Fall, the week the Tree speaks for the children, is held in the square because the square is the mile.
- **The stone** — the mile-shrine that used to stand a day further on, now set in the square. It has a flat top, climbing-scuff, a ribbon some years, and a name cut in it. **Raetoth** *(RAY-toth)* will not read the name aloud. **Lithsur** *(LITH-sur)* will, and wants the stone back on the scar so the name is a day's mark again and not a festival climb. The stone is not the day's drink. The cistern is the drink. A notch on the stone still holds a little rain after the square has dried. Raetoth calls that weather, and Lithsur calls it the day.
- **The scar** — what lies beyond the boughs. Thorn, ditch, and a line you can follow for a stone's throw before it closes. There is no cart-track and no neighbour's canopy. People who try it come back scratched and thirsty. The water-notch is on the stone, and the stone is here.
- **The upper room** — too many beds for the trade the town has now. It slept the day that was pulled in, the old next day's lodging brought back into the square. It is let, and it is not a bank. A name is cut over its door that no one reads aloud, and no lodging-slate has copied it. A Road-hand still asks which bed is the next day. Raetoth will not answer that question with a bed.
- **The cistern** — the town drinks at home. It is not a mile-shrine, and it is not Seine's pot.
- **The greens** — around the Hand, enough to live from, and not a ticketed lot. They will not follow you home.
- **The ranging** — at the edge, not a quarter of its own. Mixed Kinds live and work it, and no Kind-flag is hung there.
- **The far shed** — Returned work, downwind of the cistern. It is not an infirmary.

Homes accommodate becomings the way [[Daily Life]] already said, so the house has room for the change a person is already undergoing. Mixed Kinds live here.

Tithe-provision, what a person needs in order to stay whole, is core-warm and local. You can be too poor to stay whole here. The stopped road will not furnish you, and the Mile's beds are a day back at the junction plus the walk you already left.

## What the square still does

Children Turn at Nelath. The walk stopped, the square is the last mile, and the road ends at the boughs. Someone still walks the scar and will not call it closed. A Road-hand still treats the upper room as if a pot were going further. No Tree failed this year, and the wood is sound.

The Seat will call the requirement over, Raetoth will call the mile the square, and Lithsur will call a name that used to be a day. All three are looking at one stone.

The rain-notch stays wet, and a name is cut over the door of the upper room. Nelath will not say why the notch stays wet, or whose name that is. A clerk who copies either one has already misunderstood the town.

## People & Powers

Two people are named here. The talk in the square is about the **stone and the scar**.

- **Raetoth** — town-warden. Speaks the conservative way, not a Kind, so the office is practice-first and not a people anyone was born. Raetoth speaks the fall at home, and wants the stone left in the square and the scar left closed. A neighbour says *Raetoth*, a stranger hears *Raetoth of Nelath*, and there is no house-name. What Orenbren witnesses is who slept, and it does not list a family.
- **Lithsur** — neighbour, and a given name with no house behind it. Lithsur wants the stone back on the scar so the cut name is a day's walk again, will bring a rope in Eolthael, and will not call that rope a riot. Lithsur will not take a house-word to win the argument.
- **The upper room** — let to whoever needs the bed. The name over its door is not a person you can hire.
- **A Road-hand passing the junction** — sometimes turns down the spur with earth or with nothing, sleeps upstairs, and asks for a road. They are not a chapter of this town.
- **Orenbren** — a compact of hearths. This town is one of them, and it is not the compact's crown. The [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]] is in the Motherwood, beside the country, and not in this square.

Hugs are less common on a sound Motherland Hand than elsewhere, but not rare, and a missed week still hurts. A family that needs a neighbour's Tree walks back to the Mile. That walk is a repair, not this town's brand.

## What can enter a scene

- **A rope on the stone.** The Eolthael crowd is in the square, Lithsur has the rope, and Raetoth has the square. The Hand is still going to Speak. The argument does not get a second week.
- **Someone coming out of the scar.** They come back through thorns, with no water, and with a story that the ditch went further than it does. The cistern is the drink. The stone is not.
- **A Road-hand in the upper room** asks which bed is the next day. The name over the door has an answer nobody will read, and the scar does not have one either.
- **A child on the stone** asks how far it is to the Tree. They are already under it. The climb is not a map.

## In-world text

*Chalk on the cistern in Hale-month, Cut-year 387, in Raetoth's hand. The name on the stone is not copied.*

> *The mile is the square. The scar is not a road. I will not put the stone back to make a day this town ended.*
>
> *Lithsur may say the name. They may not take the stone in order to say it. A rope is not a mile.*
>
> *Drink here. The notch on the stone is not the drink. The upper room has beds. The name over the door is not a slate. If you came for the Mile, you left it yesterday.*

The chalk shows that Raetoth will keep the stone in the square, and that Lithsur may say the name and may not take the stone in order to say it. Closing the road did not unsay the name. The Hand does not decide between them.

## GM Notes

- **This seats the lead road-end.** The walk stopped, the square is the last mile, the stone is in the square, the road ends at the boughs, and the upper room is the pulled-in day. [[Settlement Seeds]] can stop waiting on this one.
- **Not Harrow's Green.** Harrow's job is the neighbour's week. Its stone is still the road's drink, and Haelin wants that stone moved for the crowd. Beyond those boughs a cart-track goes to the ford. Do not relabel that green into this town.
- **Not Brenthael.** Meirim wants the Third Hearth's stone down for an extra-mile crowd. Brenthael's Hand is sound and the Mile goes through. Nelath's stone is the spur's own next day, already pulled in.
- **Not the Mill-hold.** That hold has sick wood, a neighbour-slate, and children walking in. "The road ended at the boughs" there is texture while the Mile continues grove-ward. Here the spur stops. Do not sicken Nelath to make it interesting, and do not move the culvert here.
- **Not a capital.** Orenbren's capital is [[Rothallo]], still called the Inner Close. This town is not it. The First Seat stays in the wood. Do not capture either.
- **Not a deathless inn.** There is no Held bed and no century-note. The upper room is the pulled-in day, not a second White Note and not Seine's doorway.
- **The town's daily work.** The work is the stopped walk. Do not make devotion, necessity, and prestige all the town's theme. Do not invent a deathless clerk to own the upper room, and do not narrate a blight to make the ending feel earned. A ribbon is texture, a Road-hand upstairs is texture, and a necessity-family belongs back on the Mile.
- **Postcard check.** The scar is thorns and a dry ditch, not a green lane. The stone is a waymark and a grave. Do not launder it into a view.
- **Keystone:** a sound Hand in the core stays horticulture. Do not let the rain-notch assert a mind. Do not date the Tree. Do not name a cutter.

- **Hooks.** A rope on the stone, the scar walked and regretted, the unread name over the door, a child who thinks the climb is the mile, and a week paired with the Third Hearth so a through-road and a stopped spur share the week and are not the same stone.
