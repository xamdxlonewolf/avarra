# Ornsael

> **A Rain-Shadow walk-hold.** *(orn-SAYL · [[The Old Tongue|orn]] "land, ground" + [[The Old Tongue|sael]] "sowing" = "the sown-ground.")* Conservative drift: *th* is not in the name, and the holy sound is still in the mouths — they are the Motherland's dry east, still walking west after the core stopped. The new square for [[Roadmap|Story 7.2]] (`settlement-design` at square scale). Proves [[The Years of Hands|Ornthael]] is unfinished even at the origin-continent. Clock 2 is not "the frontier."

> **Canon status.** 🟡 **Playable square.** Seed `20260828` (middle of the list; not *Saelthael*, not *Saelorn*, not *Saelhael*, not *Ornthael*). Everyday *Ornsael*; core travellers will try *the west-road well* and be corrected. **Not [[Saelthael]]'s capital** (the three corner seats are [[Eolvaeth]] · [[Orentel]] · [[Maiethlir]]). Not a Fox kingdom. Do not clone Road-hands or [[The Slide]] as a district.

## Overview

East of [[Maiethorn]]'s central range, on the leeward side away from the inner sea: dry hills, sand-country, towns at wells and at whatever graft will take. Ornsael is one of those wells. A Tree stands beside it — young for the Motherland, old enough to have Turned local children some years and not a certainty this one. The core stopped walking. Ornsael did not. Children still go west in the neighbour's week, or further, because a well-Tree is not a certainty.

[[Saelthael]] is the polity of people who still sow. Ornsael is a square of that habit, not a throne. Plenty sits on the other side of the range. Pilgrimage here is a leftover they cannot retire. Core pilgrims who arrive in Eolthael expecting a holiday extra mile find a necessity-column already on the west-road, and are shocked. That shock is the design. They thought waiting was a Heskoren feeling.

[[Kitsune|Fox of the Sands]] hearths are densest here. That is terrain, not a flag ([[Kinds of the Turning]]). A town with *no* fox-born would be the thing worth asking about. Mixed Kinds drink the same well.

**Leftover job on this street: necessity.** The west-road is the neighbour's week. Devotion belongs to people who already have a healthy canopy. Prestige does not book a dry hill.

## Site

The well is why anyone stopped. The range takes the rain; this side keeps the dust. Settlement is nucleated around water the way Harrow's is nucleated around wood — same grammar, different scarcity. The graft was married to the well on purpose: living earth wants a drink in sand-country as much as a child does.

The **west-road** is the main street. It used to be how you reached a healthier canopy, then how you reached the origin, then how you reached the core's Hands-towns after those towns stopped coming east. It is still how this year's ten-year-olds go, when the well-Tree is thin or the fall is missed. Beyond the last house the road is a pale cut toward the range-pass. East of the well it peters into tracks and Taken-In plantings that look like stubbornness.

Water is the well, a few cisterns, dew-catch on the north walls. Food is terrace-gardens, goats, whatever the west-road pays when it still pays. Fuel is dung and what the range-trade carries over. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] are backbone here — thirsty land honours green — and they are not a nation.

No walls. The boundary is where the well's courtesy ends and the dust does not.

## The square (not a district grid)

- **The Tree at the well** — civic and sacred heart, as [[Daily Life]] requires, even when the wood is a maybe. Some years it Turns. Some years the colour is late, or thin, or the warden will not call a fall that isn't. A missed Eolthael still shuts the Given-door. Struck remains the later mercy. There is still no second-chance year.
- **The west-road** — Clock 2 as a street. Mile-markers toward the pass; one stone still in the well-square because the town grew around a day's water, not around a finished Hand. Climbing-scuff. Ribbons pointed *west*, which core children find backwards.
- **The well-market** — stalls in the morning shade. Fox-born densest in the crowd, not gated. A hearth-neighbourhood in the sense [[Kinds of the Turning]] allowed: a fox-market without the town *being* a Kind. Stock ancestries, a Tengu on a pass-errand, a Selkie who married in from Eolstrand and is dry and funny about it. Do not put ears on a flag.
- **The greens** — potted, hauled, argued over. No lush lot. The greens-keepers' liturgy does not run a chapter here; a cousin of the craft might winter. They will not follow you home, and home is dust if they did.
- **The ranging** — dry hills, night-work, Two-Bodied who know the difference between a sand-fox and a Fox. At the edge, not a quarter.
- **The far shed** — Returned work downwind of the well, because you do not put the dead on the drink. No infirmary. A travelling stiller is a pass-story.

Homes bend to becomings: north-side cool rooms, green pots on lintels, shutters that open for a Far-Voiced hour because there is no hall to book. Thin infrastructure, high theology, low governance — Saelthael's combination at eye level.

## The leftover, visible

**Clock 1:** this is Maiethorn. The Motherland. Hands-country in the core. Conservative mouths. The extra mile is supposed to be optional.

**Clock 2:** the well-Tree is not a certainty; children still go west; the road that used to go further still does. Same *sael* as [[Saelvaeth]] on the live front — sowing on two continents. Do not narrate Ornsael as history and Harrow's as frontier flavour. Both are this year.

Hands can un-Hands. A well-graft that sickens puts a Motherland square back on the neighbour's week. That is weather, not a fall of the age. The Seat will call thin soil. The town will call this year. Vaethorn-guilt imported to sand that never asked for it is the injustice: waiting read as unworthiness on the continent that invented plenty.

**Inscrutable, on purpose:** children put a pinch of well-sand in the west-facing shoe before they walk. Core pilgrims find sand in their own shoes and do not know who did it. Ornsael will not explain. The pass takes the rest.

## People & Powers

No Epic-8 cast. Offices that must exist:

- **Town-warden** — practice-first, not Kind. Tired of being asked if this is fox-country. May be Sands-Fox; may not. Either way the question is the slight. Speaks the fall when there is one; will not write a colour that didn't happen, even if a core clerk wants the year tidy.
- **The square that watches, and the west-road that watches instead** — some years the witness is local. Some years it has to walk home from a healthier canopy over the pass. Vouching-at-a-distance on the Motherland is the tell that Ornthael is unfinished.
- **Road-hands** — rare; the circuit prefers paying nexts. A pot for Ornsael is a fight with the queue, not a chapter-house. Do not clone them here.
- **Saelthael-folk custom** — light, town-wardens, wells as civic fact. No list. [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] would like one. Plenty next door is [[Maiethvael]]'s problem, not this well's.

## In-world text

*A west-road ribbon-count, kept on a lath by the well, Eolthael through Hale-month. No clerk's name. The verso is sand-scored. `oblique-worldbuilding`.*

> *Ribbons west: this week. Ribbons at the well: last fall, if you can call it. The core's extra mile came through on the fourth day and asked where the holiday was. We gave them water. They gave us a hymn. The children already had sand in the shoe.*
>
> *If the Tree speaks we stay. If it does not we walk. I will not say which year we are in. I know which pass.*
>
> *A man from the Reckoning asked to name the well on a roll. We named the well Ornsael, which it already was. He wrote something else. The sand does not keep his hand.*

The lath cannot afford to see that the core's shock is the setting working, or that "which year we are in" is the two clocks asked of a well. Threnmaieth will file a west-road as piety or as a gap. It is a child who needs a canopy this week.

## GM Notes

- **Not a Kitsune nation.** Sands-Fox densest. Mixed Kinds. A fox-market is a neighbourhood, not a quarter-with-a-gate. The moment the government is *for* a Kind, the two-layer model has started to rot.
- **Pair with [[Saelvaeth]] / [[Harrow's Green]].** Same sowing-root, two continents, both present tense. A party that has only seen Heskoren waiting should meet this well and have to recalibrate. A party that has only seen the Sacred Core should meet the west-road and lose the stacked-age.
- **One leftover job.** Necessity. A core pilgrim's hymn is friction, not a second theme.
- **Keystone:** thin take in dry soil stays horticulture in player text. Do not let the well assert the mind. Do not import Vaethorn's sermon as GM voice.
- **R2:** plenty next door does not make the leftover kind. Confirming she is kind does not retire the west-road.
- **Do not make Ornsael the capital of Saelthael.** Named square. Seat unnamed.
- **Hooks.** A west-road cohort; a well-graft that sickens; a core pilgrim shocked to find the neighbour's week *on Maiethorn*; a Sands-Fox warden asked if this is fox-country; sand in a shoe; Threnmaieth naming the well on a roll the town will not use; a Taken-In planting that is the only green for a day's walk; a missed fall and no second-chance year; pairing a session with Harrow's so both clocks are visible in one campaign week.
