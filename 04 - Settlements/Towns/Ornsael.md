# Ornsael

> **A Rain-Shadow walk-hold whose drink is leaving.** *(ORN-sael · [[The Old Tongue|orn]] "land, ground" + [[The Old Tongue|sael]] "sowing" = "the sown-ground.")* Conservative drift. Everyday *Ornsael*; core travellers still say *the west-road well* and are corrected — the well is why anyone stopped, and it is not why they stay this year. Not [[Saelthael]]'s capital. The three corner seats are [[Eolvaeth]] · [[Orentel]] · [[Maiethlir]].

## Overview

East of [[Maiethorn]]'s [[Named Ground|Rain-Wall]], on the leeward side away from the Old Crossing: dry hills, sand-country, towns at wells. Ornsael is one of those wells. A [[Turning Tree|Tree]] stands beside it — young for the Motherland, old enough to have Turned local children for a generation. **It Speaks most years.** A missed Eolthael is weather, not the town's crisis. The crisis is the drink.

[[Saelthael]] is the polity of people who still sow. Ornsael is a square of that habit, not a throne. Plenty sits on the other side of the range. Pilgrimage here used to be a leftover they could not retire because the wood was thin. The wood grew up. The well did not. Children still go west in the neighbour's week — not to hunt a colour, but because the Hale-month crowd needs water this well cannot host. Core pilgrims who arrive in Eolthael expecting a holiday extra mile find a necessity-column already on the west-road, and are shocked. They thought waiting was a Heskoren feeling. They were not wrong about waiting. They were wrong about *what* is waited for.

[[Kitsune|Fox of the Sands]] hearths are densest here. That is terrain, not a flag ([[Kinds of the Turning]]). A town with *no* fox-born would be the thing worth asking about. Mixed Kinds drink the same share.

The town's daily work is meeting need. The west-road is the neighbour's week as *water*. Devotion belongs to people who already have a healthy canopy *and* a wet well. Nobody books a dry hill.

## Site

The well is why anyone stopped. The range takes the rain; this side keeps the dust. Settlement is nucleated around water. The graft was married to the well on purpose: living earth wants a drink in sand-country as much as a child does. The Hand took, and now the well is what is failing.

The **west-road** is the main street. It used to be how you reached a healthier canopy, then how you reached the origin, then how you reached the core's Hands-towns after those towns stopped coming east. It is still how this year's extra people go, when the share will not stretch through Hale-month. Beyond the last house the road is a pale cut toward the range-pass. East of the well it peters into tracks and Taken-In plantings that look like stubbornness, and now look like a second drink.

Water is the well, a few cisterns, dew-catch on the north walls. The cisterns are the civic argument: who filled them, who may draw, whether a planting may have a bucket before a child. Food is terrace-gardens, goats, whatever the west-road pays when it still pays. Fuel is dung and what the range-trade carries over. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] are backbone here — thirsty land honours green — and they are not a nation. Their pots also need water.

No walls. The boundary is where the well's water no longer reaches and the dust does not.

## The square (not a district grid)

- **The Tree at the well** — civic and sacred heart, as [[Daily Life]] requires. A reasonable Hand. Most Eolthaels it Turns. The town-warden Speaks when there is a colour and does not invent one when there is not; that is ordinary work, not the town's plot. A missed week still shuts the Given-door. Struck remains the later mercy. There is still no second-chance year.
- **The well** — the day's drink and the year's fear. A rope with knots at old water-lines. The share is chalked on the well-lip, not on a warden-lath. Climbing-scuff on the lip from children who have heard a stone-story.
- **The west-road** — the main street. Mile-markers toward the pass; one stone still in the well-square because the town grew around a day's water. Ribbons pointed *west*, which core children find backwards. The column that leaves at dusk is people the share refused.
- **The well-market** — stalls in the morning shade. Fox-born densest in the crowd, not gated. A hearth-neighbourhood in the sense [[Kinds of the Turning]] allowed: a fox-market without the town *being* a Kind. Stock ancestries, a Tengu on a pass-errand, a Selkie who married in from [[The Hinge Shore]] and is dry and funny about it.
- **The greens** — potted, hauled, argued over. No lush lot. Lesna's planting is the only green for a day's walk some weeks, and it drinks. The greens-keepers' liturgy does not run a chapter here.
- **The ranging** — dry hills, night-work, Two-Bodied who know the difference between a sand-fox and a Fox. At the edge, not a quarter.
- **The far shed** — Returned work downwind of the well, because you do not put the dead on the drink. No infirmary. A travelling stiller is a pass-story.

Homes bend to becomings: north-side cool rooms, green pots beside the doors, shutters that open for a Far-Voiced hour because there is no hall to book. Thin infrastructure, high theology, low governance — Saelthael's combination at eye level.

## What the square still does

This is Maiethorn, the Motherland, Hands-country in the core. The extra mile is supposed to be optional, and this canopy Turns at home. The well is dropping. Children and Hale-month guests still go west, and the road that used to go further still does. The word *sael* is the same as in [[Saelvaeth]] on the live front: sowing, on two continents. If the wood sickened, a Hand could fail. It has not. The drink has.

Vaethorn-guilt imported to sand that never asked for it is still an injustice, and it is the wrong sermon for this square. Waiting read as unworthiness was about a colour. This year the town empties itself for water. A core hymn about thin soil will be heard as a man who has never drawn a bucket.

The well-rope is knotted at last year's water-line. This year's knot is wet even when the water stands below it. Ornsael will not say why. Theisva moves the knot. Lesna says the planting tastes the same when it is wet. Bovaer puts sand in a west-facing shoe and does not call that a rite.

## People & Powers

Three people keep the well's argument:

- **Theisva** *(THAYS-vah)* — well-keeper. Speaks the conservative way, not a Kind. Keeps the share. Will cut a guest's bucket before a child's, and a planting's before a guest's, and will not pretend the chalk is a hymn. Tired of being asked if this is fox-country. May be Sands-Fox; may not. Either way the question is the slight.
- **Lesna** *(LES-nah)* — [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]]. The planting east of the well. Wants one more bucket than the share allows, because the green is also the town's food. Will not follow you home. Home is dust if the planting dies.
- **Bovaer** *(bo-VAIR)* — west-road guide. Still sends. Some years the Tree has Spoken and they send anyway, because the drink is the limit. Core pilgrims hear faith. Bovaer hears a date and a water-mark.
- **The square that watches, and the west-road that watches instead** — some years the witness is local. Some years a person has to walk home from a wetter town over the pass, not because the wood failed, because the week could not be hosted. Vouching-at-a-distance on the Motherland is still the tell that Ornthael is unfinished.
- **Road-hands** — rare; the circuit prefers paying nexts. A pot for Ornsael is a fight with the queue, not a chapter-house.
- **Saelthael-folk custom** — light, wells as civic fact. The well-share *is* the town-law. No list. [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] would like one. Plenty next door is [[Maiethvael]]'s problem, not this well's.

A town-warden still Speaks the fall. They are not who this square turns on.

## What can enter a scene

Not weather in a paragraph. A thing that arrives while someone is talking.

- **The well-mouth at first light.** Theisva draws. The mark is lower. A crowd is already there with jars. Someone will take a second bucket. Someone will be seen doing it.
- **A cistern-fight.** Lesna's planting got a night-bucket. A camp-street child did not. The planting is also breakfast.
- **A west-road leaving at dusk.** Bovaer's column: ten-year-olds, Hale-month guests, a core pilgrim who came for a hymn and is being sent on. The Given-door is still one week. A dry well is not a second-chance year.
- **A collapse.** The lip shears. The rope goes. For an hour the town has a hole and no share.
- **Range-weather.** Night-work comes home with a person who needs water now. The share was written at dawn.

## In-world text

*Well-share marks, chalked on the lip, Hale-month, Cut-year 387. Theisva's hand. The Tree is not mentioned.*

> *Dawn-mark: below last knot. Child-jars first. Guest-jars after the planting's one bucket, and I will hear about the planting.*
>
> *West-road at dusk if the noon-mark does not rise. Bovaer has the names. I have the rope.*
>
> *A man from the Reckoning asked to name the well on a roll. We named the well Ornsael, which it already was. He wrote a colour-year. The sand does not keep his hand. The knot is wet. I did not wet it.*

Theisva is keeping the water. They will not file a year. Lesna will read the marks as a slight on green. Bovaer will already be on the road.

## GM Notes

- **This is the clone-break.** [[Eolvaeth]] keeps the maybe-Tree, the spring, the warden who will not invent a colour, the wet leaf. Ornsael does not. Physical problem = the well. Institutional response = a share. Cast = a gang. Mystery = a wet knot below the water. Do not slide back into "warden won't call a false fall."
- **Not a Kitsune nation.** Sands-Fox densest. Mixed Kinds. A fox-market is a neighbourhood, not a quarter-with-a-gate.
- **Necessity as water, without a maybe-Tree.** The Hand Speaks most years. If you need a thin fall, it is weather, not the plot. If you need a Hand that fails, that square is [[The Mill-hold]].
- **Not the Thaw-Break's silted well-town.** That cistern is another leeward well-town ([[The Thaw-Break]]). Some locals date the first low mark after 233. The rope does not. Do not confirm.
- **Not a capital of Saelthael.** The seat is [[Larbril]].
- **Keystone:** thin take in dry soil stays horticulture in player text. Do not let the well assert the mind. Do not import Vaethorn's sermon as GM voice.
- **The west-road remains necessary.** Plenty next door does not shorten it.
- **Hooks.** A west-road column the Tree did not require; a night-bucket; a wet knot; Threnmaieth naming the well on a roll the town will not use; a Taken-In planting that is the only green and the other drink; pairing a session with [[Harrow's Green]] so both squares are visible and the two scarcities are not the same. Pair with [[The Dry Stair]] if you want a climb that Gives nothing on the same dry east; the Stair is not this hill. Do not narrate Ornsael as history and Harrow's as frontier flavour. Both are this year.
