# 04 - Settlements

This section gathers the places people live, and the places they dig: continents, regions, cities, towns, villages, and sites.

The continent notes live in `01 - World`. The powers live in `05 - Factions/Governments/`, on [[Powers of the Turning]]. What this section keeps is the squares themselves, the cities and towns and villages and sites.

## Contents

### Seeds
- [[Settlement Seeds]] — the kinds of place the Years of Hands left behind

### Towns and villages
- [[Harrow's Green]] — a live-front Tree-town, with a stone-moving day, and Tora of Brenod
- [[The Three Hamlets Past the Ford]] — a waiting cluster, Brenod, Vaelun, and Ornath, three kitchens with names
- [[The Third Hearth]] — a Near-Mile hearth, Seine's bed, and Meirim, who wants the stone
- [[Ornsael]] — a Rain-Shadow walk-hold where the well is dropping, and not a copy of a town that is only maybe a Tree
- [[The Mill-hold]] — a Hands-town with a sick Tree, a neighbour-slate, and a shovel
- [[The First Bowl]] — a guest-grove, folk name *Lonasir*, two settings of one bowl

### Capitals
- [[Eolvaeth]] — Vaethorn's pilgrim-edge, a town, where the conflict walks in
- [[Orentel]] — Lestrand's salt-quay, a crane and a hold, with the Drop as the hill between them
- [[Maiethlir]] — Threnmaieth's counted river-seat, where the Loft Row is the tension

### Maiethorn's other seats
- [[Seinbrun]] — Maiethvael's capital, the furnished hall
- [[Rothallo]] — Orenbren's capital, still called the Inner Close
- [[Larbril]] — Saelthael's city, where the west road meets the Well-wash
- [[Votaer]] — the Hinge Shore's port, the quay where a hull is classified
- [[Tasain]] — Lirorn's walled town below the Shelf-gate

### Strandoren's other seats
- [[Raitin]] — Brenledd's throne-city, the council hall, seven hearths, while the six towns stay unnamed
- [[Naenor]] — Leddvael's port, the signing-watch
- [[Lunbra]] — Trenledd's city on the Chart-run, the roll-room
- [[Braetu]] — the Night Shore's harbour, the quote-desk

### Heskoren's other seats
- [[Sanbreo]] — Ornled's town, the slate, and a beach-fee when a hull arrives. The pockets stay unnamed
- [[Vaelhesk]] — no town-note, because the land is the seat. [[The First Bowl]] stays a guest-grove
- [[Natai]] — Saelvaeth's town, the march-voice. [[Harrow's Green]] stays out of that seat

### The spur, and three halts
- [[Nelath]] — a road-end Tree-town, where the walk stopped and the square is the last mile
- [[Raillath]] — a Near Mile halt between Brenthael and the Mill-hold
- [[Denlad]] — a Salt Walk inlet, a day's sail short of Orentel
- [[Tunral]] — a kitchen on the live-front road, with no sightline to Harrow's
