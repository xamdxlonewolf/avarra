# Seinbrun

> **Maiethvael's capital.** *(SAYN-brun.)* People say **the Gift-Realm**. Charters write *Maiethvael*. This is the city that furnishes. It does not keep a list.

## Overview

Seinbrun is a large city in the warm core of [[Maiethorn]], the capital of [[Maiethvael]]. Smaller towns and villages sit around it. This note does not name them.

The city furnishes greens, a hall, and medicine by congregation and guild. Citizenship is witnessed the way the Motherland witnesses it. There is no [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]] here. Counting persons is treated as a failure of trust. The hymns are the same ones [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] sings. The paper is not.

It is not [[Maiethlir]]. It is not [[The Down-Bank]]. A traveler can spend the first day below Maiethlir's Down Gate among Gift-folk and still not have arrived. That day is the argument. The bank goes on toward [[The Hinge Shore]]. Seinbrun stands off that water.

[[Orenbren]] houses the people who are still walking toward the grove. Seinbrun can see that season. It is not an inn on the Near Mile, and it is not coiled on the wood. The [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]] stays in the Motherwood. This city does not crown it.

A Hand stands beside the furnished hall. Leaf-Fall is kept there. The colour is Spoken. Nobody copies it onto a roll.

**Leftover job on this street: hospitality.** The extra mile arrives as a meal and a bed, not as a line. Devotion is the hymn. Necessity is a neighbour who has already left it.

## Ways in

- **From [[Maiethlir]].** Leave the counted river. The Down Gate opens onto [[The Down-Bank]], and the bank is not this seat. Gift-folk use that first day. The city is off the water, a day's argument from the tallied seat, where the hall is.
- **From the Near Mile.** The pilgrimage season is visible from here. The beds on that road belong to Orenbren. Seinbrun's door is a hall, not an upper room.
- **From the grove.** The wood is in sight of the season. It is not inside the city. A pilgrim who asks for the college is pointed at the clearings.

Water for the city is wells and the rain of the warm core, not the Core-thaw's conduits. Food is the core's plenty, tended as a green. Fuel is coppice and what the congregation hauls. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] work the tended lots. They are not a nation.

The hall's court and the older house-walls are the inside a crowd can point at. The weather the core still plans for is a flood.

## The work

The work happens in the **furnished hall**, with the green beside it.

Congregation and guild tend what a person was Given: the pot, a bed when the hall has one, medicine, the commons. There is no crown channel and no tablet-hall. To be inside the hymn is to be inside the net. [[Vuthbraen]] keeps the hall. [[Raermu]] keeps the basket. A neighbour says those names. A stranger hears *of Seinbrun*. There is no house to put first. Maiethvael will not write one.

## One tension

The warmth is real, and so is the bill. A household that leaves the congregation loses the green, the hall, and the medicine together. There is no crown to appeal to. The [[Law and Citizenship|Kept]] are pitied here, in plenty, and the pity measures what they missed.

[[Breillai]] lives on the outside of that net. The hall can still set a bowl. The net stays where the hymn is.

## Habits

The furnished hall sets an empty bowl at the end of the table, away from the pot, and clears it with the others. Seinbrun will not say why.

On the day a household leaves the hymn, a neighbour stacks their green pots in the lane, still planted. They will not say why.

[[Raermu]] opens the medicine basket's latch before knocking. They will not say why.

After a hug at Leaf-Fall, the child's bowl is turned so the chip in the rim faces the door. The hall will not say why.

The Kept drink first from a new cask. The next cup comes from the old one. The city will not say why.

A lamp in the hall window is lit through the afternoon and put out while the pilgrimage road is still full. They will not say why.

[[Vuthbraen]] tears the guest's bread and sets the first piece on the threshold. They will not say why.

## People

Three mouths. Not a court. Not a roll.

- **[[Vuthbraen]]** — keeps the furnished hall. A neighbour says Vuthbraen. A stranger hears Vuthbraen of Seinbrun.
- **[[Breillai]]** — left the hymn. The green went with it. Still in the city.
- **[[Raermu]]** — carries the medicine the guild furnishes.

[[Maiethlir]]'s clerks, [[Orenbren]]'s beds, and the college in the wood are other powers. They are not a staff for this hall.
