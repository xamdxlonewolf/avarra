# Larbril

> **Saelthael's city.** *(LAR-bril.)* People say **the Sown Tree**. This is where the west road meets the Well-wash, short of the pass. It is not a great capital. It is not [[Ornsael]].

## Overview

Larbril is a medium city in [[Saelthael]], on the dry side of [[Named Ground|the Rain-Wall]]. The west road meets [[Named Ground|the Well-wash]] here, short of the pass. Walking west, the [[Named Ground|Shelf-gate]] is still ahead. Walking east, you have already come down, and the dry is in front of you.

[[Ornsael]] stays the smaller well-town, farther into the dry. Its drink is a well. Larbril's drink, when it has one, is the wash. [[The Dry Stair]] is another rise. People climb it so they know a dare from a Tree. It is not why this city is here.

The wash is a river in a kind Liren and a silt-line in a cruel one. The city stopped at the meeting because travelers already wanted water and the walk west, and because the core stopped walking while this country did not.

A Hand stands at the meeting. It Speaks most years. A missed week is weather. The civic fact is the wash. [[Kitsune|Fox of the Sands]] hearths are dense in this country. That is terrain. The city's mouth is not an ear on a flag. Mixed Kinds use the road.

**Leftover job on this street: the walk west.** Necessity is a column that still has to go. Devotion belongs to people whose well and canopy are both sound.

## Ways in

- **The west road.** Down from the Shelf-gate, or up toward it. [[Tasain]] is the walled town in the valley on the other side of that pass, where the levy is taken. This city is the meeting on the dry side.
- **The Well-wash.** A ford when the water is a river. A pale line in the road when it is not. Towns at wells sit farther out, Ornsael among them. This note does not name the rest.
- **Not the Dry Stair.** The Stair is a site on a different rise. Travelers can leave from here to climb it. The climb is not the city's reason.

No wall around the meeting. The boundary is where the road stops being a street and becomes the climb, or the dust. Food is what the wash year allows, plus what a west-bound cart pays. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] plant where a bucket can be argued. They are not a nation.

## The work

The work happens at **the meeting**, where the road crosses the wash.

[[Brormei]] says what the wash is this week, and whether a column goes west toward the pass. A neighbour says Brormei. A stranger hears Brormei of Larbril. Saelthael does not keep a person-list. There is no house to put first.

Ornsael's well-share is Ornsael's. Larbril does not chalk a rope for them. The pass-levy is [[Lirorn]]'s, taken at Tasain and at the Shelf-gate. This city keeps the road and the wash in one place.

## One tension

The city stands on water that is not a certainty. In a cruel Liren the carts still come, and the wash is a line in the dirt. The core will call that thin soil, and a slight. Larbril still has to say whether the week goes west. Plenty on the far side of the range does not shorten the road.

## Habits

When the Well-wash is a silt-line, [[Brormei]] still wets the meeting-stone from a jar. They will not say why.

A cart bound west is held while the driver sets a pebble on the dry line. Larbril will not say why.

## People

One new mouth. Not a court. [[Ornsael]] keeps Theisva, Lesna, and Bovaer. They are not moved here.

- **[[Brormei]]** — stands at the meeting. Given name. Of Larbril if you are not from the road.
