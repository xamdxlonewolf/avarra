# Larbril

> **Saelthael's city.** *(LAR-bril.)* People say **the Sown Tree**. This is where the west road meets the Well-wash, short of the pass. It is not a great capital, and it is not [[Ornsael]].

## Overview

Larbril is a medium city in [[Saelthael]], on the dry side of [[Named Ground|the Rain-Wall]], where the west road meets [[Named Ground|the Well-wash]], short of the pass. Walking west, the [[Named Ground|Shelf-gate]] is still ahead of you. Walking east, you have already come down, and the dry country is what stands in front of you.

[[Ornsael]] stays the smaller well-town, farther into the dry, and its drink is a well. Larbril's drink, when the city has one, is the wash. [[The Dry Stair]] is another rise, and people climb it so they will know a dare from a Tree. The climb is not why this city is here.

The wash is a river in a kind Liren and a silt-line in a cruel one, a ford in the one case and a pale line in the road in the other. The city stopped there because travelers already wanted water and the walk west, and because the core stopped walking while this country did not.

A Hand stands at the meeting and Speaks most years, and a missed week is called weather. The civic fact is the wash. [[Kitsune|Fox of the Sands]] hearths are dense in this country, and that density is terrain: Brormei is not chosen for being Fox-born, Larbril is not a Fox nation, and mixed Kinds use the road.

The city's daily work is the walk west, and the decision of whether a column goes on. Necessity is a column that still has to go, and devotion belongs to people whose well and canopy are both sound.

## Ways in

- **The west road.** The road comes down from the Shelf-gate, or goes up toward it. [[Tasain]] is the walled town in the valley on the other side of that pass, where the levy is taken. This city is the meeting on the dry side, short of the Shelf-gate, and this note does not record the days to the pass or to Tasain. [[Ornsael]] is farther into the dry, not the next roof, and the days to it are not recorded either.
- **The Well-wash.** The crossing is a ford when the water is a river, and a pale line in the road when it is not. Towns at wells sit farther out, Ornsael among them, and this note does not name the rest.
- **Not the Dry Stair.** The Stair is a site on a different rise. Travelers can leave from here to climb it, and the climb is not the city's reason.

No wall stands around the meeting. The boundary is the place where the road stops being a street and becomes the climb, or the dust. Food is what the wash year allows, plus what a west-bound cart pays. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] plant where a bucket can be argued. They are not a nation.

## The work

The work happens at **the meeting**, where the road crosses the wash.

[[Brormei]] says what the wash is this week, and whether a column goes west toward the pass. A neighbour says Brormei, and a stranger hears Brormei of Larbril. Saelthael does not keep a person-list, and there is no house to put first.

Ornsael's well-share is Ornsael's, and Larbril does not chalk a rope for them. The pass-levy is [[Lirorn]]'s, taken at Tasain and at the Shelf-gate. This city keeps the road and the wash in one place.

## One tension

The city stands on water that is not a certainty. In a cruel Liren the carts still come, the wash is a line in the dirt, and the core will call that thin soil, and a slight. Larbril still has to say whether the week goes west. Plenty on the far side of the range does not shorten the road.

## Habits

When the Well-wash is a silt-line, [[Brormei]] still wets the meeting-stone from a jar. They will not say why.

A cart bound west is held while the driver sets a pebble on the dry line. Larbril will not say why.

## People

The note names one person, not a court. [[Ornsael]] keeps Theisva, Lesna, and Bovaer, and they are not moved here.

- **[[Brormei]]** — stands at the meeting. A neighbour uses the given name. A stranger who is not from the road hears Brormei of Larbril.
