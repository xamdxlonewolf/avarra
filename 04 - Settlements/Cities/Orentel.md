# Orentel

> **The Ledger Coast's seat: a salt quay, not a desk wearing a crown.** *(OREN-tel · eroded [[The Old Tongue|oren]] + [[The Old Tongue|thael]] = "the witnessing-Tree," *th* worn to *t*.)* Coast-folk say **the Salt Quay** and mean the city. Notes and berths meet here; no house sits a throne. [[The White Note House]] is on the third quay, north side — placed, not crowned.

> The factor people ask for is *Sorim*. [[The White Note House|Ledan]] is not the government.

## Overview

Orentel stands on an Old Crossing estuary on [[Strandoren]], facing [[Maiethorn]] across the oldest water in the world, on the shore where the Salt Walk used to land. When that walk stopped being required on this coast, the berths did not empty, and they changed jobs. A Tree-town grew a quay, then a second quay, then a run of warehouses along the tide, until the old square was the smaller half of a salt-city.

[[Polity Archetypes|Lestrand]] is guild self-rule carried as far as it goes, and Orentel is the square that proves it: a charter-council of houses and craft-guilds, no crown, and no [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]]. Citizenship-by-witness runs smoothly because reach is high, with a mature Hand and the city's own children Turned at home. The [[Law and Citizenship|three pillars]] are the infrastructure of that witnessing, and an unvouched person is a transaction that cannot clear.

The city's daily work is prestige, the First-Hand year held as a product and sold to households who can pay to walk a walk they do not need. The factor assigns the empty Eolthael berths on the first quay. The White Note writes the **terms**, a [[Netstrand]] hull may be the wood in the water, and [[Orenbren]] takes the sleep at the origin. Three desks, one commodity. Necessity still eats in the lofts, and [[The White Note House|Mataero]] files that necessity as occupancy.

## Site

People stopped here because the estuary gave them deep water, a sheltered sound, and a tide that writes the working day. The graft took on the **rise above the first quay** in Cut-years 40 to 89, a wealthy coast and a paying next, and the oldest houses still face that Tree. The road used to come inland from the berth toward a healthier origin-canopy. Then this canopy grew up, and then the berth became the point.

Water is the tide, the cisterns on the rise, and the paid wells in the warehouse-leap. Food is the [[Named Ground|Chart-run]] plain, the catch off the Old Crossing, and whatever the hinge pays. Fuel is drift, peat, and what the factor-houses book. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] rent **leaf-lots** by the season, and they are not a nation. They are here because they were Given here, because grain-hands winter, and because the lot is a job, not because the city is a forest ([[Economy and the Tithe#Why Taken-In live in stone cities]]). [[Selkie]] hearths are densest on the water, a lot of shoreline and not a gate ([[Kinds of the Turning]]). Citizenship-by-witness in a city this size is **by hearth**: the quay-gang stood, not the twenty thousand ([[Law and Citizenship#Turning in a city — the hearth stood]]).

No walls here matter. The boundary is the last crane and the ranging-yard inland, and low governance looks like the council chartering the guild and never the person.

**Hallowquay** is a lesser inner quay, not the capital. A fox-born broker might still be called *of Hallowquay*. That byname was already in the world. The seat is Orentel.

## Layout

There is enough here to walk, and enough for a later map. The estuary faces east, toward the Old Crossing, and the Chart-run comes in from the west.

- **The Crossing-mouth** — the sail in from [[The Hinge Shore]], three to five days in fair weather, into the estuary. [[Denlad]] is a side inlet about a day's sail short of this mouth, on the way in, and not a berth in the mouth itself.
- **The Chart mouth** — inland boats and the river-plain. [[Lunbra]], where [[Trenledd]] keeps the roll, is four to eight days upriver. The food on the quay is this water's plain as much as it is the catch.
- **The Rise** — above the first quay, where the graft took. The oldest houses face the Tree, and the square still Turns local children for free.
- **The Drop** — the street from the Rise down to the first quay. The district's tension is this one hill: free colour at the top, held berths at the bottom. A family can stand on the Drop and see both prices in the same glance. [[Sorim]]'s slate starts at the bottom. The Tree-tenders' free starts at the top. Neither of them owns the street.
- **The First Quay** — the Salt Walk's old landing. Berths 1–4 are held in Eolthael. Berth 5 is earth.
- **The Third, north side** — [[The White Note House]]. It is a desk, not the council, and not the crown.
- **Hallowquay** — the lesser inner quay already named above. The name is a byname, not a second city.
- **The inland yard** — the ranging ground behind the Rise, where the city's boundary is the last hired beast and not a wall.

Leaf-lots and the ticket-infirmaries sit downwind, off the Drop's seaward end. They are not the tension. The tension is the hill between a free Hand and a priced berth.

## The square (not a district grid)

The old town on the rise gathers around the Tree. The quays run in a line along the tide. The warehouses jumped to the next stretch of bank wherever a fire or a silted berth made that stretch cheaper than rebuilding, and the interesting streets are the edges where the rise meets the salt.

- **The Tree at the centre** — still the civic heart, as [[Daily Life]] requires, even when the money has gone downhill. The Hand is mature. Leaf-Fall is a civic and commercial milestone, because a family's colour is watched for the guild it opens, and the day is not a warm festival. The square still Turns local children for free, and the quay cannot price that without noticing itself.
- **The first quay** — the Salt Walk's old landing. Empty Eolthael berths are **held** as prestige, which is Sorim's occupancy, and wool through Hale-month covers a shrine. There is a smell of earth out of season when a pot, and not a child, is the passenger. A west-water hull at an Old Crossing hold is a Netstrand charter, not a second leftover.
- **The third quay, north side** — [[The White Note House]], which is Ledan's desk, with Mataero's loft in the rise behind it, twelve beds, the north bed not to let, and century instruments in the house. The house is a desk, not a council, and Lestrand will only count the house as success.
- **The release-house** — the [[Naming in the Turning|ael-hall]] used as a playhouse and an outlet, and ticketed. It is superb if you can pay. The queue outside it is the have-and-have-not gap at eye level.
- **The leaf-lots** — rented green, not the Mother's common. They will not follow you home, and home is a loft if they did.
- **The beast-yard** — hired ranging at the inland edge, not a wild-leave. Two-Bodied haulage becomes a trade dynasty if the match was priced that way.
- **The ticket-infirmaries** — [[The Stillers]] on the downwind edge. The care is excellent, and it is for those who can reach it. The queue is longest here too.
- **The far shed** — Returned work past the last crane, kept on the same logic as a tannery.

Homes accommodate becomings, mixed Kinds live here, and a Sands-Fox at a desk is unremarkable if they have warranting.

## What the street still does

The canopy here Turns children at home, and the Salt Walk is optional. Empty Eolthael berths are still shaped like a landing, pots still take a child's space, and a First-Hand year can still be booked. A Hand can fail. If Orentel's Tree sickened, this quay would be the neighbour's week. It has not sickened. The council does not say the age is over. It reports occupancy.

The city sells a walk people do not need. That is how a rich coast gets a capital without a crown. When a hymn lands here, the release-house schedules it. Necessity in the loft is Mataero's concern, and it is not the city's brand.

Salt sits on the Tree's roots in Eolthael. People say it is the tide, and the tide does not reach the rise. Orentel will not say why the salt is there. A Hall-keeper will schedule around it. A Book-hand will not put it in a Term.

## Habits

The charter-council turns one chair to the wall before a meeting of the council, and turns it back when the room is empty. Orentel will not say why.

A cook on the Drop sets the pot on the rise-side of the step and ladles toward the quay. They will not say why.

On the first quay, a crane-crew taps the hook once against the beam before a lift. They will not say why.

The release-house leaves the queue's last bench unroped after the doors have shut. They will not say why.

A leaf-lot renter knocks twice on the gate-post and does not knock a third time. They will not say why.

The ticket-infirmary hangs a dry cloth on the downwind nail on a coughing morning, and leaves it after the queue has gone. They will not say why.

At Hallowquay the first basket of the morning is set with the lid underneath. The quay will not say why.

At the inland yard, the hired beasts are led out and led back in before the first job. The yard will not say why.

## People & Powers

The cast is on [[People of the Turning]]. [[Sorim]], [[Mataero]], and [[Tesara]] are people now, and not only the offices they hold.

**Named wants on this square (carried by people):**

- [[Mataero]] wants [[Sorel]] off the century-note
- [[Sorim]] wants berths 1–4 to stay held, with the hold-fee as the house-share
- [[Tesara]] wants [[Valen]] stamped before the floor takes the master back
- [[Lasole]] wants leaf-lot credit for one more season
- [[Didel]] of Hallowquay wants a kin-berth for a wedding, not a First-Hand year

[[Reimaethe]] lets a rise-room. [[Rosire]] works the ticket-infirmary downwind of the shed.

Offices here:

- **The charter-council** — houses and craft-guilds. The council watches the guild and never the individual. [[Sorim]] is a factor who can be asked who chartered the empty berths this Eolthael, and the answer will come back as occupancy. Sorim is not Ledan.
- **Tree-tenders** — Lestrand's word for the wardens, practice-first and not Kind. They Speak the fall as a civic fact, and a colour that opens a guild is still a colour.
- **[[The White Note House]]** — Ledan writes, and [[Mataero]] lets. The north bed is house custom.
- **[[The Book-Hands]]** — ordinary on this quay. A signing-watch here is prestige, if you book it. They do not rewrite Bound Terms, and they do not sit a seat.
- **[[The Intake]]** — [[Tesara]] sits the stillers' shed. Success, for that shed, is silence.
- **Road-hands / graft-carts** — rare on this quay. A pot on an Eolthael berth is cargo to the clerk.

The Tithe-poor fade while the city steps around them, and that is the injustice. Nothing is policed. Everything is priced.

## What can enter a scene

- **A crane failing on berth 5.** The failure is earth, a pot, and a family who wanted berths 1–4. The hold was not invented for them. The wood can still drop.
- **Tide in the downwind shed.** Tesara's catching-year does not pause for the weather. The queue at the ticket-infirmary gets wet, and it gets loud.
- **A hold-fight on berths 1–4.** Didel's kin-wedding is set against Sorim's house-share, on the same morning a First-Hand year is boarding.
- **Salt on the Tree's roots in Eolthael.** The tide does not reach the rise. A Hall-keeper will schedule around it.
- **An alley the council does not charter.** What moves there is sold vouching and smuggled green. [[The Standing Trade]] is there if you need a face.

## In-world text

*A berth-slate, first quay, Eolthael through Hale-month, Cut-year 387. Sorim's hand. The Tree is not mentioned.*

> *Eolthael berths 1–4: held. Do not load wool. A First-Hand year is not cargo. I have explained this to the crane.*
>
> *Berth 5: earth. A pot is cargo. I have explained this to the family who wanted 5 for a child. They could not hold 1–4. I did not invent the hold.*
>
> *The rise still Turns for free. That is a Tree-tender matter. The quay is a hold matter. If you need both in one week you are at the wrong desk.*
>
> *White Note, third north: not a berth. Do not send me their conversions. I do not keep house-years.*

The slate shows that Sorim says the hold was not Sorim's doing, and that the family without a berth is still without one. It does not mention the salt on the roots. Ledan reads a neighbour who cannot convert. Mataero reads occupancy. Their readings call for different action on the same berth.

## GM Notes

- Homes accommodate becomings. Mixed Kinds live here, and a Sands-Fox at a desk is unremarkable if they have warranting. Do not grow Kind-quarters. Do not clone the Slide as a district. The shadow economy, which is smuggled green, sold vouching, and off-book Terms, lives in the alleys the guilds pretend not to charter.
- **[[The Intake]]** — [[Tesara]] sits the stillers' shed, and success there is silence. Do not clone [[The Slide]].
- **Road-hands / graft-carts** — these are rare, and a pot on an Eolthael berth is cargo to the clerk. Do not clone them as a chapter-house.

- **Layout.** The parts to keep are the Crossing-mouth, the Chart mouth, the Rise, the Drop, the First Quay, the Third on the north side, Hallowquay, and the inland yard. The Drop is the one district tension. [[Denlad]] is a cove, not a fifth berth. The city sheet is drawn later, Story A.14 (2026-10-02), on [[The Atlas Sheets]]. Regional sheets were not relabeled.
- **Do not make the White Note the crown.** The desk is one house on the third quay, and the council is many tickets. A party that only meets Ledan has not met Orentel.
- **The city's daily work.** The daily work is prestige, the First-Hand year held as a product **the city holds**. Do not make prestige, necessity, and devotion all the city's theme. Do not let the desk be the whole job, and do not let Netstrand crown the year. Necessity in the loft is texture, not a second theme. Devotion that books a release-house is friction.
- **Prestige-walk chain.** The chain runs from Netstrand berths and hulls, to White Note terms, to the berth Orentel holds. Orenbren houses the year at the origin. Do not let Ledan, Sorim, and a Night-Shore factor each "invent" the same winter.
- **Two clocks.** The first: this canopy Turns at home, the Salt Walk is optional, and the bed-house collected. The second: empty Eolthael berths are still shaped like a landing, pots still take a child's space, a First-Hand year can be booked as a winter, and a Hand can fail.
- **Thilim.** Do not move Thilim to the White Note House. The two fates are two doors.
- **The salt.** Do not explain the salt on the roots.
- **Do not clone 7.2's stone or sand.** Empty berths and earth-holds are this street's leftover. Salt on the roots is not a ribbon.
- **Hallowquay stays a lesser quay.** The example in [[Naming People in the Turning]] is a neighbourhood byname, not a retcon of the capital.
- **Habits.** Nine, and the count is full. The salt on the Tree's roots was already here. The eight in the Habits section are the ones added. A later note must not explain any of the nine, and must not turn them into a power, a relic, or a rite. Do not add a person from this pass. Do not rename the city. Do not move the White Note.
- **Keystone:** dense Given stay horticulture-and-trade in player text. Do not let Sorim assert the mind.
- **The hold is not care.** A rich city stepping around a fade is the Coast's injustice at eye level.
- **Hooks.** A First-Hand year and a pot booked into adjacent berths; Ledan's unanswered conversion; Mataero's north bed; a Tithe-poor family in the rise-lofts; sold vouching in an alley the council does not charter; a Bound signing-watch as prestige; salt on the roots; a colour that opened a guild and a marriage priced as a dynasty; pairing a session with [[The Third Hearth]] so the two fates of the road-house share a week.
