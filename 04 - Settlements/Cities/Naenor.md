# Naenor

> **Leddvael's port.** *(NAY-nor.)* People say **the Reckoned Gift**, and the charters write *Leddvael*. This is where the signing-watch sits. The port is slightly smaller than [[Orentel]], and it is not a second Orentel.

## Overview

Naenor is a port city on [[Strandoren]], down the coast south-west of Orentel and south of Raitin on the atlas. This note does not record the days between them. The city has enough water to keep its own houses, the towns and villages along the rest of that coast stay unnamed, and the port itself is large and slightly smaller than Orentel.

[[Leddvael]] took [[The Fair Hand]] from a dockside theology and made it a civic rite, so Leaf-Fall in Naenor is a festival and a signing-watch at once. A Tithe is a term serviced. The [[Bound]] are employed here to witness a term, because a Bound's word still closed in [[The Grey Summer]], when the other witnesses had died. The city will file a contract. It will not copy [[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]]'s list, and it will not become a census of souls.

The port competes with the Ledger Coast, and it wins the signing. A term can close on this quay while Orentel is still pricing the hold. What Naenor loses is the berth, the [[The White Note House|White Note]], and the scale of the deathless houses on the Old Crossing. A First-Hand year is not this city's product. The empty prestige berths are Orentel's, Naenor has working berths, and the work the city is for is the watch.

The [[The Book-Hands|Book-Hands]] stamp and recite on this quay, as they do on other signing-quays. They have no seat of their own here, and they do not rewrite Terms.

A Hand stands off the signing-square, which Motherfaith tree-tenders and Fair Hand clerks share. Gift-reading families live in the city. They are the minority that sounds devout, and the statute reads them as a credit risk.

The city's daily work is the signing. Devotion, if it lands, is a hymn the festival will schedule, and necessity is a household that still calls the colour a gift.

## Ways in

- **Along this coast.** Other harbours and coves sit on the same stretch. This note does not name them. They are not a second signing-watch, and they are not Orentel.
- **Toward the Ledger Coast.** Coasting brings a hull into the argument, a term set against a price. The Old Crossing and Orentel's estuary are that other water, and this port does not face [[The Hinge Shore]].
- **Not the Chart-run, and not the West Water.** Inland filing is [[Lunbra]], the far crossing is [[Braetu]], and Naenor is the stretch between those jobs. It keeps its own.

Water for the city is rain, casks, and a paid well off the salt. Food is the coast's catch and the grain a signed hull actually lands. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] keep greens above the quay, and they are not a nation. A wall, where the town kept one, is for a surge. The statute is not a wall.

## The work

The work happens at the **signing-watch**, on the book-table in the square.

[[Derdil]] stands the watch. A neighbour says Derdil, and a stranger hears Derdil of Naenor. There is no house to put first, because the Reckoned Gift does not keep a person-list. Book-Hands also work on this quay, and they have no seat. They are not Derdil, and they are not a seat.

## One tension

The city wins the term and loses the berth. A Bound's word can close a contract on this quay, and the hold that would make the contract a life is still priced on the Ledger Coast. Inside the win, a household that calls the colour a gift is filed as a bad debtor. The Bound are safe here, and they are over-read: the contract-pillar asked to be the whole person. No villain-faith. A sincere ledger, laid on a real Condition.

## Habits

[[Derdil]] stamps the wax, then warms the stamp in a closed hand before the next page. They will not say why.

The watch reads a term aloud and turns the page with the left hand only. Naenor will not say why.

At Leaf-Fall the square sets the Bound witness's chair with its back to the Tree. The square will not say why.
