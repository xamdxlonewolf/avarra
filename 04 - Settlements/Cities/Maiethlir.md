# Maiethlir

> **The Tallied Crown's seat: an origin pilgrimage-town under a roll, not the grove.** *(MAI-eth-lir · [[The Old Tongue|maieth]] "Mother" + [[The Old Tongue|lir]] "thaw.")* This is where the crown started counting a hearth that was already holy — not where it acquired [[Turning Tree|Thaeloren]]. The [[The Tree-Wardens|First Seat]] sits in the Motherwood *beside* this country and is not its crown.

> The speaker on the square is *Rithim*. The First Seat is not this city's. This is not [[The Third Hearth]], and it is not Brenthael.

## Overview

Maiethlir is a thaw-river town on [[Maiethorn]], in the Sacred Core and not in the grove. Snowmelt comes down from [[Lirorn]]'s [[Named Ground|Rain-Wall]], and **[[Named Ground|the Core-thaw]]** carries that melt toward the Old Crossing. Maiethlir is an origin-town of Cut-years 0 to 40, one or two days' walk from the wood, and it sat down where the water slowed enough to hold a Hand, the city's Tree, on a *different road* than [[Orenbren]]'s Near Mile. Orenbren lodges the approach. This river counted the people who stayed.

The extra mile used to come through with the thaw. When the Hands made the origin-walk optional, the lodging emptied toward Orenbren's beds, and the rooms did not stay empty. Clerks filled them, and a pilgrimage-street learned house-names on a tablet.

[[Polity Archetypes|Threnmaieth]] coils near the sacred centre and wants everyone on the [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]], the roll the hall keeps of names, colours, and marriages of heritable Conditions once they have been noticed. Maiethlir is the square that proves the coil without owning the Tree: the colour that falls is a hymn *and* a line. [[Maiethvael]] shares the Mother and refuses the list, the same hymns on the opposite paper. Its people live downriver, along the first day below the Down Gate.

The street's older work is devotion: the extra mile is kept as a civic season, and the roll sits on top of it. A family from thin up-range soil still sleeps in an uncounted loft, and a counted winter can be booked for a price. The Threnhael sits on that season and does not replace it.

## Site

The Core-thaw is why anyone stopped. There was a ford, a mill, and a Hand planted beside the water, because the first carrying followed living earth and a day's drink. The Motherwood is a dark on one horizon. You can walk to the grove, and most Hands-children do not. The college is in those clearings, and it is not this square.

Water is the river, the conduits the crown maintains, and the cisterns in the old pilgrim-streets. Food is the Core's plenty. Fuel is the wood's edge, which the Seat will not let a crown cut without a leave. Proximity is pressure, not ownership. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] work the **tended green** the crown furnishes, and they are not a nation.

A wall exists, and it is old. It was for floods and for a generation of argument, not for a siege the Core no longer expects. The interesting streets are inside it, where the old extra-mile meets the tablet-hall.

## Layout

There is enough city here to walk, and enough for a later map, and it is not a grid. The Core-thaw runs west, toward the Old Crossing. The city sits where that water slowed.

- **The Grove Bank** — the way from [[Turning Tree|Thaeloren]], one or two days on the river road, which is not the Near Mile. You arrive with the Motherwood as a dark behind you. The First Seat is in that wood, and it is not inside this wall.
- **The Down Gate** — downstream and west, onto [[The Down-Bank]]. The counted conduits thin within a day. The Hinge Shore's quay is still several days of bank, orchard, and mud beyond that. [[Maiethvael]]'s people live along the first day down. Their capital, [[Seinbrun]], is more than a day from Maiethlir, off the water, and this gate does not lead to it. This note does not record how much more.
- **The Wall Path** — upstream, toward [[Lirorn]] and the snow that feeds the thaw. [[Vehaela]]'s thin-snow winters come this way. It is not the Noon Pass. It is the path the river's source already implies.
- **The Slow Water** — the ford and the mill-reach inside the wall, where a Hand could be planted because the carrying had stopped being a current. The Tree stands on this water.
- **The Tree** — the civic heart, inside the old flood-wall, and not the First Hand. Leaf-Fall is kept in this square.
- **The Loft Row** — the old extra-mile, one street from the Tree to the tablet-hall. The upper rooms that slept pilgrims are clerk-dorms now. This is where the district's tension lives: a hymn on the square end, a copy at the hall end, and beds between them that the roll has not all found. [[Rithim]] Speaks at the Tree. The clerk waits on the hall side of the same street. They are not two cities.

The tended green, the counted hall, the stiller, and the far shed stay where the square already put them. They are not a second map.

## The square (not a district grid)

The streets ring the canopy and then run out along the river and the old extra-mile. The order is the order a counted city grows into, an accretion that learned its numbers, not a grid drawn last week.

- **The Tree at the centre** — a mature Hand, not the First, and the civic and sacred heart, as [[Daily Life]] requires. Leaf-Fall is a joyous holy day that is also a state registration. The colour is Spoken, and a clerk waits to copy it. **Rithim** *(RITH-im)* Speaks, and they do not always let the copy happen in the same breath.
- **The tablet-hall** — the Threnhael's local office, beside the Tree and not the Tree. What it notices are names, colours, and marriages of heritable Conditions. Outsiders hear the work called "the Counting." The clerks say they keep the realm hale.
- **The counted hall** — the [[Naming in the Turning|ael-hall]] used as outlet and as attendance. A Far-Voiced hour is furnished through the crown's channel, and to be cut off is framed as your own lapse.
- **The old extra-mile** — still a street. The upper rooms that used to sleep pilgrims are clerk-dorms. A Held bed would offend the roll, and the roll has not found all of them. The Watching keep a room under a chapel the wardens recut ([[The Watchers|the Grown-Over]]).
- **The tended green** — commons the crown furnishes. They will not follow you home, and home is on file if they did.
- **The ranging-roll** — Two-Bodied leave at the edge is noticed, and that notice is not a Kind-flag.
- **The crown-stiller** — excellent medicine, set downwind and reached through the channel. The Bound are watched past their hazard, and the watching is dressed as safety.
- **The far shed** — Returned work, kept past the last conduit and filed there.

Homes here accommodate becomings, and the accommodation is on the tablet. Mixed Kinds live in the city, and a Fox of the Snows, down from Lirorn, is a guest with a name.

The clerks will tell you a number, and it changes after every Eolthael.

## What the street still does

The canopy here Turns children at home. The Near Mile is Orenbren's work, the extra mile is a civic season in Maiethlir, and the Motherwood is a walk, not a requirement. The thaw road still brings a family in need when the up-range soil is thin, an uncounted loft still smells of Hale-month, and a Hand can fail. The roll would like those summers filed.

People still come because the hinge is holy. The Threnhael is a tablet beside the Tree, not a record put there instead of it. That is how a pious core got a capital without taking the grove. The same layer has learned how to cut off a household's green.

The Core-thaw runs louder the week before Leaf-Fall. Clerks file that rise as snowmelt, and the watchers do not. Maiethlir will not say why. Rithim will Speak over it. A clerk will wait for the water to quiet before copying.

The counted hall serves the Far-Voiced hour from cups that have already been used that morning. Maiethlir will not say why.

Clerks tie a loose thread around the tablet-stylus on the day a marriage is copied, and cut it off before a colour. They will not say why.

## People & Powers

The cast is on [[People of the Turning]]. [[Rithim]] and [[Laevila]] are people now, and not only the offices they hold.

**Named wants on this square (carried by people):**

- [[Rithim]] wants [[Valein]] to stay unmarked on the tablet
- [[Laevila]] wants [[Senithi]]'s name good for a marriage
- [[Vehaela]] wants an uncounted loft for one winter off [[Lirorn]]'s thin snow
- [[Baerith]] wants the crown to stop cutting green on their sister's street

Offices here:

- **Speaker / town-warden ([[Rithim]])** — Reckoned Hands pressure, practice-first, not Kind. Rithim Speaks the fall truly, and the copy is a second, lesser witnessing. Some summers they let the copy happen in the same breath, and some summers they do not. The crown has not replaced them. The Seat has not recalled them.
- **The tablet-clerks** — Long-Lived among them, their memory bent to the census rather than left free to check it. The named instruments of the roll live on [[The Reckoned Offices]] (Menirein, Tarvae, Videm, Sirtal). They are not a replacement for this hearth. Rithim is still the town Speaker.
- **The First Seat** — in the Motherwood, beside the city, a college and not a throne. Some Speakers cooperate with the roll, because they believe they are keeping the realm hale. Some refuse. A pilgrim who asks Rithim where the college is will be pointed at the wood, not at the tablet-hall.
- **Orenbren lodging** — on the Near Mile, the other road. The lodging witnesses who slept, not what colour fell. Threnmaieth would like those lodging-rolls.
- **Watchers in the oldest district** — a second reading kept alongside the roll, not set over it. They are filed as the Grown-Over. [[Laevila]] keeps the old room. They have no seat.

The Kept are pitied *and* noted as unmarked. A household whose bloodline and upkeep are both on file cannot easily refuse the crown anything.

## What can enter a scene

- **Thaw-flood in the tablet-hall.** The river runs louder the week before Leaf-Fall, the clerks file the rise as snowmelt, and this year the water comes over the step. Rithim will Speak over it. A clerk will wait for the water to quiet before copying, or will not.
- **A street shove over cut green.** The shove starts at Baerith's sister's pot, with the crown's channel framed as a lapse. The people in it are neighbours, not a riot-district.
- **An unfiled loft giving way.** The loft may be Vehaela's winter, or another winter like it, in the old extra-mile timber. The roll has not found all of those rooms. Gravity has.
- **A colour Spoken and not copied in the same breath.** This is already the city's own document. Let it happen in the square while the river is loud.

## In-world text

*A Threnhael copy-slip, tablet-hall, Eolthael, Cut-year 387. The Speaker's name is on the recto. The clerk's hand is on the verso. The Tree is named as hale.*

> **Recto, Rithim.** *Spoken: storm-grey. Witnessed in the square. The hug was not this child's. I have said the colour. I have not said the line. The river was loud. I will not wait for it.*
>
> **Verso, clerk.** *Copied: Two-Bodied, named to the Threnhael, household already on file. Speaker delayed the copy until after the hymn. Delay noted. River-noise noted as snowmelt. The unmarked in the same household noted. Provision continues through the counted channel.*
>
> *The wood was asked whether the Speaker is a Reckoned Hand. The wood did not answer in this hall.*

The slip shows that Rithim said the colour and refused to give the line, and that the clerk filed the loud river as weather. The clerk calls the delay weather. Rithim calls it the hymn coming first. The First Seat, asked later, will say only that the Tree witnessed.

## GM Notes

- Homes accommodate becomings, and the accommodation is on the tablet. Mixed Kinds live here. A Fox of the Snows down from Lirorn is a guest with a name. Do not grow Kind-quarters. Do not clone the Slide; the late week here is *unfiled*, which is the local crime.

- **Layout.** The walkable parts are the Grove Bank, the Down Gate, the Wall Path, the Slow Water, and the Loft Row, and the Loft Row is the one district tension. Do not turn the Down Gate into Maiethvael's seat. The city sheet is drawn later, Story A.14 (2026-10-02), on [[The Atlas Sheets]]. Regional sheets were not relabeled.
- **Do not capture the First Seat in the first sentence, or the fifth.** Proximity is pressure. A First Seat that *is* the Threnhael would be a different and darker setting. Keep the college able to refuse the roll, and keep Rithim as the local version of that refusal, still incomplete.
- **Do not own Thaeloren.** This is a Hand beside a thaw-river. The grove is a dark on the horizon. Orenbren lodges the other road. Brenthael is a neighbour of an inn, not this capital.
- **The street's older work.** Devotion and the extra mile are layered here. Do not make devotion, necessity, and prestige all the city's theme. The census is the layer, not the postcard. Necessity from up-range is friction. Prestige that books a counted winter is the First-Hand year wearing a halo.
- **Do not clone 7.2's stone, cups, or sand.** What this street has are tablets, a loud thaw, and clerk-dorms in the old lofts. The unfiled Held bed is a rumour the roll has not found. Use that rumour, and do not rebuild Seine's lintel here.
- **Two clocks.** The first: this canopy Turns at home, the Near Mile is Orenbren's job, the extra mile is a civic season, and the Motherwood is a walk, not a requirement. The second: the thaw road still brings a family in need when the up-range soil is thin, an uncounted loft still smells of Hale-month, and a Hand can fail. Do not narrate Ornthael as post-history.
- **The roll's number.** Do not put the number in a player-facing sentence as if it were geology.
- **The gap around Rithim.** The crown has not replaced Rithim, and the Seat has not recalled them. Keep that gap open.
- **Brenthael.** Do not make Brenthael this city. Do not grow Kind-quarters.
- **Maiethvael is the argument next door.** The two share *maieth* and keep the opposite paper. Do not make them the kind foil, piety treated as a social tax in plenty.
- **Keystone:** dense Given stay horticulture in player text. Do not let the tablet assert the mind. The loud river is not her voice unless a later reveal earns it.
- **The counted channel remains coercive.** Devotion sanctifies the surveillance, and the surveillance enforces the devotion. There is no clean villain.
- **Habits.** Three, and the count is full. The loud thaw was already here. The used cups and the thread on the stylus are the two added. A later note must not explain any of the three, and must not turn them into a power, a relic, or a rite. Do not add a person from this pass.
- **Hooks.** A colour Spoken and not copied in the same breath; steal, forge, or refuse a Threnhael line; a Grown-Over room under a recut chapel; a Kept child pitied and filed; the crown withholding green; a Long-Lived archivist who remembers an overwrite; a pilgrim who thought Maiethlir *was* the Seat; a necessity-family from Lirorn's thin snow; pairing a session with [[The Third Hearth]] so lodging and counting have to share a week.
