# Votaer

> **The Hinge Shore's port.** *(VOH-tayr.)* Votaer is one of the largest cities on the Motherland coast, because the crossing is why the people are there. It faces [[Orentel]], and it is not a second Orentel.

## Overview

Votaer is the port where [[The Hinge Shore]] meets [[Named Ground|the Old Crossing]]. Hulls are classified on these quays, and the Hush-rate is charged here. The towns and villages along the rest of the shore stay unnamed.

[[Orentel]] prices the crossing, and Votaer sorts it. A child, a pot of living earth, a Given-Over, a recanter, or a family on a neighbour's week can make the bell sound the same, and a clerk writes which docket that hull belongs to. That writing is the job. The [[The White Note House|White Note]] is not on this quay, and the Chart-run is not this water. The city is ancient and rich, and less frantic than the Ledger Coast, because the grove is still at its back.

A fair-weather hull reaches Orentel in three to five days. From [[Turning Tree|Thaeloren]] the inland way is the [[Named Ground|Core-thaw]] and then [[The Down-Bank]], eight to twelve days in fair weather. The bank is the stretch before anyone asks what a hull is. The question is asked here.

The trees on this shore are healthy, and this is still the Motherland. Faith is worn thinner than in [[Maiethvael]], because a dock teaches categories. A Hand stands back from the working quay, and the quay's work is the hull, not the canopy.

[[Selkie]] families work these quays, as they do on any old water, and the docket does not belong to a hearth.

The city's daily work is deciding the class of each hull. Pilgrimage and cargo share one bell. The rate is old custom from [[The Hinge Hush]], and they will not call it a victory.

## Ways in

- **From [[Orentel]].** You come by the Crossing itself, and you arrive already inside an argument about price. This quay has not written that price. It has written what you are.
- **From inland.** The road in is the Core-thaw, then Maiethlir if you stopped, then the Down-Bank. Inns on the bank know the Mile's reputation and are not the Mile, and none of them has a hull. The first person who can class one is on this water.
- **Along the shore.** Other harbours exist on this coast. This note does not name them. They are not a second port for the Hush, and they are not a second Orentel.

Water for the city is rain and the casks a quay keeps. Food is the shore's catch and what the bank's gardens send down. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] keep greens off the salt, and they are not a nation. A wall, where it exists, is for a surge and for the old argument about a fade, not for a siege the Crossing still thinks it can sail around.

## The work

The work happens on the **classification quay**.

[[Tumair]] writes the hull, pilgrim or pot or person or cargo, and [[Lertho]] charges the Hush-rate on the same water. A neighbour says the given name, and a stranger hears *of Votaer*. The quay does not file a family, and there is no house to put first. [[Taerso]] spoke the Hush for the hinge-houses, and Taerso is dead. Taerso spoke the Hush in Cut-year 304, the rate is remembered as Taerso's, and Taerso is not a living clerk and not either of these two.

[[The Standing Trade]] uses this face more often than Orentel, because a standing has to travel and a hinge has two sides. That trade is not the city's office.

## One tension

The blessing and the docket happen in the same breath. A Tithe-poor household can fail in sight of the road to the grove while a hymn is still in the air. Across the water, Lestrand will price what this quay has already sorted. Votaer will not become that price, and it will not put the blessing down in order to look unlike it.

## Habits

[[Tumair]] chalks the hull's class under the waterline, where the next tide takes the mark. They will not say why.

[[Lertho]] counts the Hush-rate aloud, sets the last coin in the box, and takes it out once before it stays. They will not say why.

A cook-fire on the quay is put out when the bell rings for a sail that is not in sight. Votaer will not say why.

A dry net hangs on the blessing-rail on days when no one asks for a hymn. The quay will not say why.

## People

The note names two people, not a court, and not a hearth-flag.

- **[[Tumair]]** — writes the class of the hull.
- **[[Lertho]]** — charges the Hush-rate.
