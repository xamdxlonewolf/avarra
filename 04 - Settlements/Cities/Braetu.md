# Braetu

> **The Night Shore's harbour.** *(BRAY-too.)* People say **the Night Shore**, and the charters write *Netstrand*. This is the quay where a far crossing is quoted, and where a house can pay to keep a lamp on a name. It is smaller than [[Orentel]], and it is not a second Orentel.

## Overview

Braetu is a harbour city on the west and south face of [[Strandoren]], on the [[Named Ground|West Water]]. It is smaller than Orentel because the far run is thinner than the Old Crossing, and the towns and villages along the rest of that shore stay unnamed. People say the Night Shore, and the charters write [[Netstrand]], which sells the hulls and the west-water berths. A crossing is quoted here the way another shore talks about a pilgrimage.

The city does not sell the First-Hand year, the origin-winter, as a finished thing, and that winter is not this city's to crown. The year is assembled on the old water: a Night Shore hull is chartered here, the [[The White Note House|White Note]] writes the terms, and Orentel holds the berth. Braetu is the first of those three desks, and it is not the other two.

The far run, toward the Heskoren frontier, takes two to four weeks in fair weather. Beyond the last capes is the storm-wall, and almost nobody has crossed it. A story that says otherwise is a story, and [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] is not booked from this quay.

The harbour keeps a **dark book** of the quoted crossings that did not come back, and houses pay to keep a lamp on a name in it. A lamp on a name that will not come back is charged as watch. The book is not a [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]], and it is not a White Note. It is how this shore remembers that it is a night shore. One berth in the harbour stays unlit, with no house attached to it, and the city does not add one.

[[Selkie]] families are common on this water, as they are wherever the shore is long, and they are not the flag. The civic name is not a Kind-name. Sky-couriers out of [[Lirorn]], and Tengu families on the clock-towers, are jobs in the harbour, and they are not a district of it.

Faith is mixed and worn light, and quay-charms stay off the charter. A Hand stands back from the working water, the trees along this edge are mature, and the harbour's worry is a hull past the last forecast anyone trusted.

## Ways in

- **From [[Orentel]].** Coasting to these west quays takes eight to fourteen days. You arrive with a hull to charter, or with a name to keep lit, and you do not arrive in a hold.
- **From the Heskoren frontier.** The West Water takes two to four weeks. Along that run [[Saelvaeth]] may take a pot without a waiting-due, [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] may bless and may not levy, and [[Ornled]] is a beach-fee. The quote for the run is written here.
- **Along the rest of the shore.** Other coves keep boats, and this note does not name them. They are not a second dark book, and they are not a second Orentel.

Water for the city is rain, casks, and a well above the surge line. Food is fish, sheep, oats, and whatever a hull was carrying if it tied up. Peat and driftwood are the fuel when the coal-boat is late. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] keep greens off the salt meadow, and they are not a nation. A berth that breaks in a second wind is weather, and it is not a ward.

## The work

The work happens at the **quote-desk**, where the dark book is kept and where a lamp can be bought for a name.

[[Mursur]] writes the far crossing and takes the fee for the lamp. A neighbour says Mursur, and a stranger hears Mursur of Braetu, because there is no house to put first and the Night Shore does not keep a person-list. Mursur is not a house on the unlit berth, and nobody is.

## One tension

A name can be kept lit, and the lighting is a charge. In the same harbour, one berth stays dark, with no house attached and no one to bill. The city will not put a house on that berth to make the book tidy, and it will not pretend the far quote is grief. Watch is what the lamp is sold as. The thinner run is why the city is smaller than Orentel, and the quote is still said in the voice of a pilgrimage.

## Habits

[[Mursur]] lights a name's lamp with a spill from the cook-fire, not from another lamp. They will not say why.

The far figure is written, blotted, and written again the same. Mursur will not say why.

People leaving the harbour touch the unlit berth's ring, and do not hang a line on it. Braetu will not say why.
