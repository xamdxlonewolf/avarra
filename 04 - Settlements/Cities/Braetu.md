# Braetu

> **The Night Shore's harbour.** *(BRAY-too.)* People say **the Night Shore**. Charters write *Netstrand*. This is where a far crossing is quoted, and where a lamp can be kept on a name. It is smaller than [[Orentel]]. It is not a second Orentel.

## Overview

Braetu is a harbour city on [[Strandoren]]'s west and south face, on the [[Named Ground|West Water]]. It is smaller than Orentel, because the far run is thinner than the Old Crossing. Towns and villages along the rest of the shore stay unnamed.

[[Netstrand]] sells hulls and west-water berths. A crossing is quoted here the way another shore talks about a pilgrimage. The city does not sell the First-Hand year as a finished thing. That year is assembled on the old water: a Night Shore hull is chartered, the [[The White Note House|White Note]] writes the terms, and Orentel holds the berth. Braetu is the first of those three desks. It is not the other two.

The far run, toward the Heskoren frontier, is two to four weeks in fair weather. Beyond the last capes is the storm-wall. Almost nobody has crossed it. A story that says otherwise is a story. [[The Sundering Isle|Kumbaan]] is not booked from this quay.

They keep a **dark book**: quoted crossings that did not come back. Houses pay to keep a lamp on a name. The book is not a [[Naming in the Turning|Threnhael]] and not a White Note. It is how this shore remembers that it is a night shore. One berth in the harbour stays unlit. No house is attached to it. The city does not add one.

[[Selkie]] families are common on this water, as they are wherever the shore is long. They are not the flag. The civic name is not a Kind-name. Sky-couriers out of [[Lirorn]], and Tengu families on the clock-towers, are jobs. They are not a district.

Faith is mixed and worn light. Quay-charms stay off the charter. A Hand stands back from the working water. Trees along this edge are mature. The harbour's worry is a hull past the last forecast anyone trusted.

**Leftover job on this street: the quoted far.** The origin-winter is not this city's to crown. A lamp on a name that will not come back is charged as watch.

## Ways in

- **From [[Orentel]].** Coasting, eight to fourteen days, to these west quays. You arrive with a hull to charter, or with a name to keep lit. You do not arrive in a hold.
- **From the Heskoren frontier.** The West Water, two to four weeks. [[Saelvaeth]] may take a pot without a waiting-due. [[Polity Archetypes|Vaethorn]] may bless and may not levy. [[Ornled]] is a beach-fee. The quote for that run is written here.
- **Along the rest of the shore.** Other coves keep boats. This note does not name them. They are not a second dark book, and they are not a second Orentel.

Water for the city is rain, casks, and a well above the surge line. Food is fish, sheep, oats, and whatever a hull was carrying if it tied up. Peat and driftwood are the fuel when the coal-boat is late. The [[The Taken-In|Taken-In]] keep greens off the salt meadow. They are not a nation. A berth that breaks in a second wind is weather. It is not a ward.

## The work

The work happens at the **quote-desk**, where the dark book is kept and a lamp can be bought for a name.

[[Mursur]] writes the far crossing and takes the fee for the lamp. A neighbour says Mursur. A stranger hears Mursur of Braetu. There is no house to put first. The Night Shore does not keep a person-list. Mursur is not a house on the unlit berth. Nobody is.

## One tension

A name can be kept lit, and the lighting is a charge. In the same harbour, one berth stays dark, with no house attached and no one to bill. The city will not put a house on it to make the book tidy. It will also not pretend the far quote is grief. Watch is what the lamp is sold as. The thinner run is why the city is smaller than Orentel, and the quote is still said in the voice of a pilgrimage.

## Habits

[[Mursur]] lights a name's lamp with a spill from the cook-fire, not from another lamp. They will not say why.

The far figure is written, blotted, and written again the same. Mursur will not say why.

People leaving the harbour touch the unlit berth's ring, and do not hang a line on it. Braetu will not say why.

## People

One mouth. Not a court. Not a house on the dark berth.
