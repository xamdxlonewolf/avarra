# 00 - Core

This folder is the spine of the vault, and every note in it is `visibility: gm`. **Canon** is what is actually true, **Cosmology** is how reality works, **Themes** is what the setting explores, and **Meta** is how the vault itself is organized.

## Contents
- **Concept** is [[The Premise]], the integrated-"monsters" two-layer world, together with [[Build Plan]], the fast brief. *(GM)*
- **Meta** is [[Conventions]], the schema, the front-matter, and the player-export strip rule; [[Roadmap]], the tracker from Epic to Story to Task; [[The Wardens Ear]], the spoken study for the pitch; [[Condition Audit]], the balance review of the ten Conditions; [[Flight in this World]], who flies and what each flier cannot do; and [[Epic 2 Audit Guide]], the history of the society audit. The templates live in `00 - Core/Meta/Templates/`.
- **Canon, Cosmology, Themes** are an unused shelf. Nothing has decided an essay for any of the three, so the folders stay empty.
