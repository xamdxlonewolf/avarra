# Flight in this World

> **Why this note exists.** Flight recurs across the roster — one Kind and three Conditions can leave the ground. The [[Condition Audit]] flagged this (#12) to make sure the recurrence is **deliberate tiering, not accident**. It is. This note is the canonical statement of *who flies, how rare that is, and what each flier cannot do* — so the lanes stay clean when new content is written.

## The one-line principle

**The sky belongs to the sky-folk.** Guaranteed, hide-in-a-crowd *personal* flight is a [[Tengu]] thing — a whole ancestry built around it. Every other flier in the world is either **not a person while aloft**, **singular and self-marking**, or **a rare, visibly-marked bargain**. Nobody else quietly gets to be a flying person.

## The tiers

| Flier | Who / how rare | What kind of flight | The lane-line — what they *can't* do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[[Tengu]]** (Kind) | a whole ancestry; the sky-folk | **At-will, person-flight** — hands, mind, voice, tools, all aloft. Faerie sets the SRD precedent for at-will flight on a Kind. | Nothing missing — this is *the* flight fantasy. Everyone else is defined against it. |
| **[[Two-Bodied]]** (avian) | common Condition (~18%), but a bird is one animal-choice among many | **Real animal flight** — a hawk genuinely flies. | **No hands, no person's mind, no speech, no tools** while aloft. A superb scout/courier; *not* a person in the air. Out-*animals* a Tengu, can't out-*person* one. |
| **[[Phoenix]]** | exactly **one alive in the world** (<0.1%) | **Flaming flight** — wings of living fire. | Can't hide it — the moment they fly, everyone watching knows *what* they are. Singular and self-marking; not a build anyone can plan for. |
| **[[Bound]]** (contracted) | slice of ~5% who choose Bound, and only if they contract *for* flight — uncommon | **A rare, marked bargain** — true flight is a GM's call, priced with heavier Terms + a Pledge. | The wings (or stranger tell) are **visible and can't be furled** — a flying Bound reads as plainly marked, like a Phoenix. Most are steered to gliding/slow-fall instead. Deliberately *not* one of Bound's ordinary hide-in-a-crowd boons. |

## Why it's fine at this density

- Only **one** source — the Tengu — gives *guaranteed, unremarked, person*-flight. That's the scarce thing, and it's fenced to a single ancestry.
- The others each pay a legibility cost that keeps flight *meaning something*: the shifter isn't a person aloft, the Phoenix and the marked Bound are visibly Other, and both Phoenix and flight-Bound are rare besides.
- Gliding / saving slow-fall is the common, unremarkable version (Bound's default, an avian shifter's everyday use). **Sustained personal flight** is the rare, marked, or ancestry-locked version. That split is the whole guardrail.

## Rule of thumb for new content

Before giving anything flight, ask: *is this person-flight?* If yes, it must be either **Tengu-adjacent** (sky-folk), **singular/legendary** (Phoenix-tier), or **visibly marked and costly** (Bound-tier). If it's a common, hidden, at-will flying person that isn't a Tengu, it's off-lane — pull it back to gliding, or mark it.
