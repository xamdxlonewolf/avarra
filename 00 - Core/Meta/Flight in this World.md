# Flight in this World

> **Why this note exists.** Flight recurs across the roster, where one Kind and three Conditions can leave the ground: the [[Tengu]], an avian [[Two-Bodied]], the [[Phoenix]], and a [[Bound]] who contracted for flight. The [[Condition Audit]] flagged the recurrence as #12, to make sure it was **deliberate tiering, not an accident**, and it is deliberate. This note is the canonical statement of *who flies, how rare that is, and what each flier cannot do*, so the lanes stay clean when new content is written.

## The one-line principle

**The sky belongs to the sky-folk,** and the sky-folk are the [[Tengu]]. Guaranteed, hide-in-a-crowd *personal* flight is a Tengu thing, a whole ancestry built so that a person stays a person in the air, hands, mind, voice, and tools included. Every other flier in the world is either **not a person while aloft**, **singular and self-marking**, or **a rare, visibly-marked bargain**. Nobody else quietly gets to be a flying person.

## The tiers

| Flier | Who / how rare | What kind of flight | The lane-line — what they *can't* do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[[Tengu]]** (Kind) | A whole ancestry, the sky-folk. | **At-will, person-flight.** Hands, mind, voice, and tools all stay available while they are aloft. Faerie sets the SRD precedent for at-will flight on a Kind. | Nothing is missing, because this is *the* flight fantasy, and everyone else is defined against it. |
| **[[Two-Bodied]]** (avian) | The commonest Condition, though a bird is only one animal-choice among many. | **Real animal flight.** A hawk genuinely flies. | While aloft they have **no hands, no person's mind, no speech, and no tools**. They are a superb scout and courier, and they are *not* a person in the air: they out-*animals* a Tengu, and they can't out-*person* one. |
| **[[Phoenix]]** | Exactly **one** is alive in the world. | **Flaming flight,** carried on wings of living fire. | They can't hide it, because the moment they fly, everyone watching knows *what* they are, so the flight is singular and self-marking, and it is not a build anyone can plan for. |
| **[[Bound]]** (contracted) | A few contract for flight, and even those are uncommon. | **A rare, marked bargain.** True flight is a GM's call, priced with heavier Terms + a Pledge. | The wings, or some stranger tell, are **visible and can't be furled**, so a flying Bound reads as plainly marked, like a Phoenix. Most are steered to gliding or a slow-fall instead. This is deliberately *not* one of Bound's ordinary hide-in-a-crowd boons. |

## Why it's fine at this density

Only **one** source, the Tengu, gives *guaranteed, unremarked, person*-flight. That is the scarce thing, and it is fenced to a single ancestry.

The others each pay a legibility cost, which is what keeps flight meaning something. The Two-Bodied shifter is not a person while aloft, the Phoenix and the marked Bound are visibly Other, and both the Phoenix and a flight-Bound are rare besides.

Gliding and a saving slow-fall are the common, unremarkable version of leaving the ground, which is Bound's default and an avian shifter's everyday use. Sustained personal flight is the other version, rare or marked or locked to an ancestry, and that split is the whole guardrail.

## Rule of thumb for new content

Before giving anything flight, ask whether it is *person-flight*, the kind where hands, mind, voice, and tools stay with the person who is aloft. If it is, it must be either **Tengu-adjacent** (the sky-folk), **singular and legendary** (Phoenix-tier), or **visibly marked and costly** (Bound-tier). A common, hidden, at-will flying person that isn't a Tengu is off-lane, so pull that flight back to gliding, or mark it.
